What depends on the corner, and what depends on the slope

An edge limit is one of the polygon's corners. Writing u for the target's offset from that corner's level and r' for the corner's own radius, the ring modulus k² = 4rr'/a² and its complement k'² = (u² + (r' − r)²)/a² are functions of the corner alone — and everything built on them is too. The slope b₁ does not appear until the plane denominator.

The rung named the first four rows. The fifth was found while splitting: the target's offset from the edge's extended line is a cross product of its offsets to the two endpoints, so it — and r₁, B² and both slope blocks with it — is the same at both limits and was being formed twice per edge as well. Only the last row is genuinely per (edge, limit).

The moment machinery moved onto the corner with the stack it operates on: the contractions, the partial-fraction split between a denominator's two factors, and the two pole routes (exact weight for a near root, the family's own moments for a far one). _Edge reaches through it with the numerators its slope contributes, which is what keeps the arithmetic identical.

The term that telescopes, measured before it was dropped

The arsinh β₁ contribution is a function of the corner alone in all three components — the flux weights it by 4ur, the radial field by 4r, and the vertical field's weight is constant so its term is the residual quadrature itself. A section sums each edge as its lower limit less its upper, so around a closed chain every corner carries it twice with opposite signs. It survives only where a horizontal edge is dropped (paper eq 7a — its integrand vanishes identically), which breaks the chain at that edge's two ends.

Both directions of that were measured on the gate target sets before the term was removed, because a cancellation that is asserted rather than measured is a guess:

So this is a cancellation of one to three decades, not a term that was negligible anyway; and carried per limit it was leaving up to 7e−15 of the answer behind as round-off — three orders inside the recorded envelopes, which is exactly why the accuracy tables did not move when it went. The rectangle is the counter-case and is asserted the other way: its chain breaks at all four corners (+1, −1, +1, −1) and the surviving sum is 6% of BZ and 105% of the flux, so a formulation that dropped the term wherever it dropped the quadrature would be wrong by that much.

A pure regrouping, and how that is known

Every entry of SECTION_ACCURACY (4 sections × 2 bands), ASPECT_ACCURACY (5 radii) and FIELD_ASPECT_ACCURACY (5) is asserted in BOTH directions — an improvement fails the test as loudly as a regression — and all eighteen hold unchanged, with the whole 95-test acceptance gate passing untouched. That is the rung's own prediction and it is what makes the saving free: the arithmetic per limit is bit-for-bit what it was, only formed once instead of twice, and the one arrangement that did change (the corner term, from a sum of rounded per-edge differences to nothing at all) changed it by less than the envelope's third significant figure.

That also means no accuracy test in the module can see whether the sharing is in effect — it is the same arithmetic either way. So the structure is pinned directly, by counting:

And one more identity the sharing rests on: pack_section hands each edge its own copy of the two corners it spans, so a test asserts those copies are bit-identical. If they ever disagreed in the last bit the sharing would be an approximation and the term that cancels exactly would stop cancelling — and no tolerance in the module is anywhere near one ulp of a corner coordinate, so nothing else would notice.

Peak allocation moves the other way, 10.6 → 12.1 kB/pair, because up to three corner parts are live at once where one edge limit used to be. Three, not one per corner: a corner is released as soon as the later of its two edges has been evaluated, and only the first corner is held across the whole loop because the last edge closes on it. Verified by instrumenting the constructor — it is three for a four-, six- and nine-corner section alike. The residual quadrature's (pairs × nodes) blocks dominate that figure either way.

The rectangle row is the honest shape of the saving. Two of its four edges are horizontal and dropped, so each of its corners belongs to exactly ONE live edge and there is nothing to share; and its chain is broken at every corner, so the first residual survives everywhere. What is left for it is one more object and the release bookkeeping, a few per cent either way across runs. A hexagonal plasma cell is the opposite case — every corner shared and the chain closed — and it halves both counts.

What this changes downstream

The adoption rung's cost case gets substantially stronger. The closed form is now 4.9× faster than the 858 µs/pair 16×48 boundary quadrature it would replace, where the figure that rung was written against was 2.6×, while remaining one to two orders more accurate — and accurate ON the contour, where a quadrature has nothing to converge to and the shipped rule sits at 2.6e−04. Against the banded scheme's 13.1 µs/pair whole-column figure it is 13.5×, so the architecture that rung proposes — closed form for the near band, banded for mid and far — is unchanged in shape and cheaper in the band that matters.

The remaining cost is now two sizing constants rather than anything structural, and both change VALUES where this rung changed none: the residual quadrature's 128 nodes (73 of the 100 floor, and the measurement that set it predates the analytic logarithm removal) and the moment stack's length (carried forty orders past the harmonics the numerators reach, for pole families that are skipped in the common case).
