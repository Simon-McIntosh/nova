Coil conductor fidelity closeout: the reference mesh and all twelve feeders

Landed 2026-07-29 · implementation 9ea1a269 · parent: coil-conductor-fidelity

Verdict

Use dcoil=0.06 m for reference inductance comparisons. It resolves the three-coil comparison with 1,096 elements in 45.3 s on a sun_debug CPU node. The final 0.08→0.06 m refinement changes PF1 by 3.33e-06 H and each CS module by 2.95e-05 H; the shipped 0.25 m mesh is too coarse for effects at the few-1e-04 H scale.

The no-section target route must reject fewer than 134 targets for an inductance reference run. Its error is 1.32e-01 H at two targets and 5.19e-03 H at 60; the existing 5e-03 H gate first holds at about 134 targets (2.2e-03 H), then falls to 1.2e-03 H at 265 and 3.1e-04 H at 816. This is a recommendation, not an API change: the followup explicitly required agreement before enforcing it.

The feeder rung across the fleet

Every feeder was solved twice: a solid cable space and the same section with its 10 mm cooling channel. The terminal step is 2 × M(pack, feeder) + L(feeder). Block reciprocity was at worst 9.4e-16. The cooling channel changes the terminal step by only 0.02–0.53 µH; the dominant variation is pack–feeder mutual coupling.

Circuit boundary: explicitly undetermined

The public ITER feeder record describes the feeders as the systems connecting coils to terminal boxes, but neither those pages, the IDS metadata, nor the available machine-description records define whether the reference inductance table is pack-only or terminal-to-terminal. The circuit boundary is therefore undetermined, not inferred.
