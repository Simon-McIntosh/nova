The verdict, plainly

The finite section does not close the +7.302e-04 H residual. With a solid 35 mm cable space it closes +2.0e-06 H (0.3 %). With the cooling channel in — which is the conductor as built — it opens the residual by 5 %, to +7.670e-04 H (whole pack, 32 corners; +7.700e-04 carried to 64). To close +7.30e-04 by section alone the cable space would have to be 19.4 mm instead of 35, at stage 1's measured dL/dln(a) = −1.235e-03 H per e-fold. The granularity-scale remainder is not the conductor's section.

What does move it is conductor no rung of either study ever had in: the feeder run. 36.19 m of conductor (68 elements, sweeping 0.0722 turns, out to r = 18.3 m) in series with the pack is worth +3.335e-04 H — 46 % of the residual: twice its mutual with the pack (+1.572e-04, block reciprocity 1.7e-16) plus its own self (+1.907e-05, which needs the resolved section — the filament estimate of that term is negative). Stated conditionally, because the data cannot say which the machine table means: if the table is terminal-to-terminal the residual falls to +4.365e-04; if it is pack-only, this is a fact about the hardware and not about the gap. Either way the section is not the explanation.

The channel, and its validation

The cooling channel is modelled as coupled counter-flowing sections — outer at +J, 10 mm channel at −J, one circuit — worth −4.178e-05 H over the pack (−5.88e-03 relative), identical at 16, 32 and 64 corners. Superposition gates: per segment, Σ factor × footprint / area = 1.000000000000 (Iouter + Icore = I), and the linked pair against the annulus integrated cell by cell (16/32/64 solid quadrilateral cells, no cancellation between members) agrees to 3.3e-16 / 0 / 1.1e-16.

Corner-count convergence, and the validated carry

On a 25-turn subset: 16→32 = −1.204e-06 (−1.67e-03 relative); 32→64 = −3.009e-07 (−4.18e-04). Quadratic, ratio 4.0, identical for the annulus. Length-scaled to the pack: −1.192e-05 and −2.98e-06. The scaling is validated against two whole-pack runs rather than asserted: it predicted disc-32 as 0.007110843 where the pack measured 0.007110842, and annulus-32 as 0.007069066 where the pack measured 0.007069066 — nine decimals. (The direct 64-corner whole-pack hollow run would have cost 137 min, over the 1-hour debug wall.) Quadrature: intervals 2→4 = −1.74e-07, 4→8 = −1.26e-07 on 25 turns, so intervals=4 carries ≤1.3e-06 H over the pack. The turn-split seam — pairs handed to the filament across a turn boundary, where the sections separate along the conductor rather than across it — was measured both ways over 12 boundaries: +1.69e-08 H over the whole pack, five orders below the residual.

Two things the study had to discover

  1. A centreline A·dl sum is not an inductance. It owes the gap between the section's geometric mean distance from itself and its mean log distance from its own centroid — μ₀/8π per metre for a solid disc, 3.09e-04 H over this coil's 6180 m of cable, 42 % of the residual. Omit it and you appear to close half the gap for free. It was computed from the section's own corners by an axisymmetric kernel sharing no code with the swept elements, and lands on μ₀/8π to ten digits (5.0000006e-08 vs 4.9999999993e-08 H/m); annulus 3.4489e-08.
  2. The frame's section of record is a swept footprint, not a cross-section. Winding.insert fills the poly column with the poloidal footprint of the swept volume; for an axisymmetric coil that is the section, but a winding pack's elements advance across the pack as they sweep: 77 % of PF1's pack length sits on elements whose ends move further in (r, z) than their section is wide (worst footprint a 170 mm smear); the worst column reaches the kernel with 244 corners carrying 7.6× the conducting area — first order on a self term; 41 straight joggles have a disconnected footprint (MultiPolygon → AttributeError in PolyGeom.points); a footprint straddling z = 0 silently loses corners to the shapely union (32→23; centroid off by 0.1 section radii); and winding.insert refuses a feeder run outright (IndexError in Polygon.translate for a segment that travels without sweeping). Change needed (not made — outside this study's scope): the thickened elements need the section handed to them as a section, distinct from the swept footprint. The study drove polygon_arc_greens / polygon_beam_greens directly, with the framed route kept side by side on the coaxial ring where the two agree to 1e-10.
