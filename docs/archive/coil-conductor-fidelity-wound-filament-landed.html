The two method findings that make the count defensible

  • Feeders cannot be separated with a box on the model outline. The as-built packs carry an outer cross-over layer at a far coarser pitch than the winding (PF1 ~140 mm/turn against 49 mm), reaching 171 mm beyond the outline — an outline box silently deletes it: PF3 loses 0.90 turns, CS3U 41.5. The working criterion is path-intrinsic: the poloidal extent of the elements that sweep more than 0.3 turn toroidally (feeders sweep thousandths), inflated one jacket width, plus path contiguity. Stable to ±0.03 turns over thresholds 0.1–0.4 and inflations 49–100 mm.
  • The sweep is read off each arc's own circle, not its three stored points. Some arcs run ~200° between stored points with the intermediate point splitting them 85/15, within 2 % of np.unwrap's half-turn limit — where a whole turn would be lost silently. Resampled on the arc geometry, the counts are identical to 4 decimals at 3, 8, 24 and 64 samples per arc.

The solenoid modules — a geometry-revision conflict, not a winding effect. The centreline file's CS3U pack sits 202 mm above and 19 mm inside the outline the shipped gate carries, worth −2.14e-02 on the self term — forty times the +5.01e-04 the continuum was missing — and the as-built modules sit 75 mm further apart (CS3U–CS2U mutual +2.35e-02 low). Which geometry the machine was actually built to has to be reconciled upstream before a winding effect can be read off a solenoid module; together with the CS section factor-of-two, the 111004/1 entry now carries two independent provenance questions.
