The arc rows were returning nan, not losing digits

The brief called this transcription. It was not. Six arrangements landed, each measured end to end on the rows it feeds:

  • The Jacobian pair is the amplitude's own sine and cosine, so a scipy inversion disappears — and the inversion it replaces returned nan for every target within 1e-08 radii of the source ring, the complete first kind it inverts being infinite there.
  • dn as cos²θ + k'² sin²θ, a sum of positives, where the printed radical returns no digits at all on the arc's own end plane. It divides the toroidal field row, multiplies the radial potential row, and enters the composite second kind.
  • The first and second kinds off one complement-native descent: on the closed arc against the axisymmetric kernel the flux goes 1.9e-05 (infinite forty nanometres in) → 7.8e-16, and both field rows nan → 1.9e-15.
  • The third kind on its pole and complement: 8.2e-04 → 3.3e-16, where the characteristic-and-parameter route also fails the general entry point's own assertion once the parameter rounds to one.
  • The pole gap as (1 − n) − k'² and the denominator as cos²θ + (1 − n) sin²θ: 4.0e-04 → 4.3e-10, and it removes a negative denominator from under a logarithm.
  • The vertical row's second-kind weight, worth 7.1e-09 on the closed arc.

A seventh cancellation was found unprompted: the far root's logarithm returned exactly zero past a pole of 1e20; its difference times its sum is k² times the denominator identically, so the ratio is now a quotient of positives.

Stated limitation, measured rather than hidden: the amplitude is assembled as (π − ψ)/2, so an end row is only as good as the assembled angle and the first kind holds 2.5e-12 within 1e-08 radians of the end plane and 1e-08 radii of the ring. An amplitude landing on a right angle is exempt and taken exactly, which covers both integration limits. Cost: 2.4× and 2.2× per element on the host.

A compiled kernel disagreed with its own host path

Found while taking the baseline, not by the brief. _elementary opened on β − α, and those two are exactly equal when Carlson's pole argument equals two of the other three — which the incomplete third kind reaches whenever the pole and the complement are both small. The subtraction kept four digits on the host and six fewer once XLA contracted its multiply-adds: 1.2e-09 against the extended-precision integral where numpy held 1.3e-15. Eager jax.numpy was bit-identical to numpy, so the cause was the fused arithmetic and nothing else.

Two exact statements remove it. The difference factorises as (√p − √x)(√p − √y)(√p − √z) whose conjugate is their sum — a sum of positives — so the difference is the product of the three gaps over that sum, and the root is the product of the gaps' own square roots. And the duplication shifts all four arguments by the same amount, so the gaps quarter exactly and are carried rather than re-formed; the subtracted gap is already 2.6e-04 wrong by the second trip.

Both paths now hold 2.2e-15. The compiled lane got faster — 891 → 592 ns/element — while numpy rose 1655 → 2119. The CUDA backend never contracted this term and is unchanged at 1.7e-15.

A vanishing gap is held before its square root, since gaps formed as products reach exactly zero where the subtraction never did and sqrt has an infinite derivative there. The confluence is reachable only below a pole of one: above it the reflection is in force, and a modulus complement never exceeds one, so the two cannot meet. Driven there anyway — with a complement above one, which no ring produces — the reflected arrangement divides by pole − complement and returns nan for the value as well. Recorded at the test rather than guarded for.

The box-section gate was masking a kernel error, not measuring discretisation

The plan asserted this comparison was discretisation-dominated. That was wrong. Three 120° arcs sweep a true ring, so the comparison carries no discretisation term at all.

An independent panelled Gauss–Legendre integral of the filament-ring Green's functions over the square annulus settles which side moved: the axisymmetric element reproduces that reference to 7.4e-12 at every target, including the twelve inside the cavity, so the whole departure belongs to the thickened arc — whose error grows toward its own section face: 8.8e-10 at six section widths, 2.5e-06 at 0.0075 m, 1.3e-04 at 0.0005 m. The bands now separate and pin that two-sided.

Why naive refinement made it worse (4% and 30% on Ay): raising minimum_arc_nodes past the point count switches the path from arcs to chords — eighteen chords sit at 2.2e-02 and thirty-six at 7.4e-03, second-order in the chord count, against three arcs at 8.8e-10.

Sensitivity demonstrated rather than asserted: rows are contracted from the float64 operators because the reduced field arrays are single precision and floor any comparison at 1e-06, and scaling an axisymmetric row by a relative 1e-09, or a swept row by 1e-08, now fails the gate.

The diagonal: the removed model was not a fudge, and the oracle could not arbitrate

OffsetFilaments was removed and the ring element's diagonal now comes from the finite-width section. But the measurement that shaped the work overturned its own brief: for a square section 0.5·dx·turnturn is the section's geometric mean distance, so the removed machinery was a leading-order self-inductance model, not an arbitrary nudge. A single integral at the target point is a different quantity, +7.1% on the psi self term, and would have been a regression. The resolution shipped is the double integral — the source's exact finite-section kernel area-averaged over the target element's own section.

A brute-force 4-D quadrature oracle was then built and refined. It establishes, and these stand:

  • the shipped fan rule reproduces the uniform-current double integral to 6.5e-07 against the Richardson limit;
  • the tiling identity closes — with both bands opened, the sub-element sum reproduces the continuum to 1.4e-06;
  • no aspect-driven loss through aspect 2.89;
  • band 2.0 costs 1.46×/1.57× on a plasma grid, not the 7× a stale docstring claimed, and takes the undiscretised pair from 3.397e-02 to 3.596e-03 of the continuum, unchanged at band 4.0.

What it does not establish is that the machine reference is wrong. Every one of those evaluations assumes a uniform current density over the gross section. A real CS module is 554 turns of cable-in-conduit conductor — cable space inside a steel jacket, insulation between — so the uniform-current integral is exact for a conductor nobody built. And the sign supports the winding: concentrating current into discrete sub-conductors raises self-inductance, and the reference sits above the continuum on every self term.
