The reduction, and the two choices that matter

Current along the local z axis leaves one potential component, whose axial integral is elementary; the divergence theorem takes the remaining section integral to the polygon's boundary twice — once through arsinh itself for the field, once through a radial field for the potential. Two derivation choices carry the quality of the result:

  • The interior delta is cancelled by the constant of integration. The potential's radial field has a free constant; the naive choice carries a 1/ρ singularity whose flux is an interior-point indicator — discontinuous where the answer is smooth. Choosing w·ρ/(2(r+|w|)) instead leaves the field vanishing at the origin, so ONE expression covers a target outside the section, on its boundary and inside the conductor, with no inside test anywhere.
  • The edge arctangents are combined before they are evaluated. Each edge's pair carries a 1/|d| pole on the edge's extended line; the difference identity (valid unconditionally here — the arguments share a sign) turns the pole into a factor of d, so the row is regular where a field grid aligned with the section lands by construction.

The prism is finite where Beam is not: Beam's theta ratios divide by corner offsets, and a target aligned axially with a section corner zeroes two of them at once — Beam returns a non-finite value there, the contour sum a finite one. Pinned by test_the_prism_is_finite_where_beam_is_not. (Beam itself is untouched — the peer relationship is a locked decision.)

A subtlety the frame forced: the poly column is a POLOIDAL projection of the section, and for a straight segment that projection is not the identity it is for an arc — a chord's section plane is tilted by half the chord's own turn, and reading the column raw would come back narrow by that cosine. The element inverts the frame's own projection map to recover the authored section; the swept-hexagon test pins it end-to-end through winding.insert.

Why the element matters at all — the routing overstatement

Every non-rectangular straight winding previously routed to Beam's bounding box while dividing by the section's true area: measured through the frame, a hexagon overstates by 4/3 and a disc by 4/π. Both are pinned by test now, and the cost argument against fixing the routing is gone — through a coilset the two elements are indistinguishable (56.3 vs 55.8 µs/pair; the assembly dominates), so the cheap route is worth keeping only where it is also exact. The routing flip itself stays with the arc plan's queued work, which owns the same defect for curved segments.
