Pin 1 — the gate never had the headroom its annotation claimed

The 1.6e-12 annotation never described the committed test. Run at the very commit that wrote it, the comparison measures 9.46e-12 against the 1e-11 gate — 6 % of headroom from birth, not 6×; the annotations were measured on an earlier in-session draft and never refreshed. What later crossed the gate is the Carlson-gap commit (0939d3e5): across that step the corner set, the area column and the vs-ideal value are bit-identical, so the mover is the kernel — both sides of the framed-vs-bare comparison at once, from different arguments (Aφ +20 %, Br −8 %, three rows unmoved to five figures). The ring-diagonal and prism merges are innocent; the poly column and the frame route did not move; the two sides integrate the same 8 corners bit-for-bit.

The gate is a property of the corner set's round-off floor. The reduction differences neighbouring corners' contributions, so cancellation grows with corner count and obliqueness — kernel residue measured by an analytically invariant translation: rectangle 1.9e-13, hexagon 9.7e-13, this 8-corner footprint (with a 6.3e-6-gap sliver pair worth ~4× of it) 2.4e-12, a 64-gon disc 8.3e-12. The framed comparison samples that floor twice plus the rotation round trip. Recalibrated: worst_overall 1e-11 → 5e-11 (measured 1.13e-11, ~4.4× headroom, the module's convention), worst_by_row left at 1e-10, and the vs-ideal gate tightened 1e-04 → 1e-05 (measured 1.7e-06 — the old 4.7e-05 annotation was the footprint's excess area, which divides out of a per-ampere comparison). A len(corners) == 8 assert keeps the floor's basis a checked fact. A warning recorded for future gates: a 1e-11 framed-vs-bare gate is a 4–6-corner number — a disc comparison would land near 3–6e-11 and should never inherit it.

Pin 2 — the host sensitivity was the XLA backend, and no input can rebuild the stray

Not libm, not an ulp. The login node compiles JAX through the GPU backend; a sun_debug node fails cuInit and falls back to LLVM CPU. Same kernel, same arguments: the subtracted route holds 1.16e-15 on the GPU backend (the authored lower bound had no premise there — hence the failure) and strays 1.201e-09 on the CPU backend. Every transcendental measured correctly rounded on both; host numpy bit-identical across nodes. The mechanism: the degenerate Carlson form is second order in its root, and the root − difference under the logarithm cancels the log's first-order term — so even a root overstated by eight decades leaves the value at round-off provided root and difference stay mutually consistent. Carried, they are; subtracted, they are two separately-rounded derivations with no correct digits, and a compiler free to recompute one side lets the eight decades through. Observing the loss destroys it — returning the intermediate drops the stray 1.2e-09 → 1.3e-15 (HLO shows sqrt rewritten into a multiply). A host-independent constructed stray in the assembled value therefore does not exist.

The pin moved onto the gap arithmetic, provable from round-to-nearest alone: the gaps are products the caller holds exactly; subtracting them quantises by ulp(cos²φ) — supplied 4.6e-17 vs subtracted 1.1e-03 at pole 1e-14, and exactly zero at a denormal partner where the product is non-zero. The gaps are spied off incomplete_pole as it forms them, so the pin sees the wiring. The assembled comparison stays, bounded from above only, with the backend span recorded at the assertion. Revert-guard demonstrated both ways: withdrawing the gap supply and re-forming the gaps by subtraction each trip the new assertions (source restored byte-identically; the landed commit touches only the test file).
