Neither printed option was right, and the third route is exact

The decision this rung locks offered two routes and the partial-range rung had already measured both out. Carlson's symmetric forms taken as printed are F + (n/3) sin³φ R_J with n = 1 − p, and for a root just past the NEAR end of the range — which is where the range starts, so it is reached at every corner rather than occasionally — n is hugely negative and the two terms are of opposite sign and nearly equal. Their sum falls as 1/√p while each stays of order F, so the arrangement throws away half the decades of the pole: measured against the extended-precision integral, 6.3e-11 at p = 1e12, which is a section a millionth as thick as it is wide. Extending the complete descent was the other option and it does not carry over at all: Bartky's rearrangement is what makes cel one fixed-trip descent over eighteen decades of pole and there is no amplitude anywhere in it.

The third route the analysis pointed at — take the elementary growth out exactly and leave a well-conditioned remainder — turns out to be an exact identity rather than an approximation, and that is what shipped. Reflecting the pole onto its partner k'²/p gives

Pi(phi, p) = [k'^2 (p - 1)/(p (p - k'^2))] Pi(phi, k'^2/p)
           + [k^2/(p - k'^2)] F(phi, k)
           + [mu/(p - k'^2)] arctan(mu sin phi cos phi/Delta),
    mu^2 = (p - 1)(p - k'^2)/p

and for p > 1 every one of those three terms is positive, so no cancellation is left anywhere. The arctangent is precisely the arctan(√p tan φ)/√p the analysis identified as carrying the whole growth — it reduces to it exactly at k = 0 — the first kind's term carries the predicted k²/(p − 1) factor that is small where the direct route is worst, and the partner pole is BELOW one, which is the orientation in which the symmetric forms are themselves a sum of positives. So one evaluation covers both orientations: the pole handed to the duplication is the smaller of p and k'²/p, and the three coefficients collapse to (1, 0) where no reflection is wanted, the arctangent's weight vanishing with mu. Nothing selects on the result.

Two arrangements that had to be measured, not reasoned

The degenerate form's logarithm. R_C's two arguments COINCIDE whenever the pole argument equals one of the other three, which the arc reaches at a vanishing amplitude exactly and approaches wherever an edge end is level with the target. Written the direct way, log((σ + root)/τ), the ratio is then one to within its own rounding and the assembled third kind is 4.3e-09 out — six decades outside the tolerance the rest of the sweep holds, and it looks for all the world like an error in the algorithm. log1p of root + |τ − σ| is the same value with no cancellation, and the accompanying test runs both so the claim is a measurement.

The third kind carries its weight into the accumulation. R_J alone passes 1e308 at a near pole on the source ring — it is inf at a reflected pole of 1e-318 — while the integral it belongs to is 1.6e-09. The weight it is destined for is small by the same amount, so folding it in term by term, and dividing it by the root before it multiplies the transcendental, is what keeps the whole evaluation inside the exponent range. It costs one multiply per trip.

The one corner, and it is not this routine's

The reflection needs k'²/p, and below the smallest normal double that partner carries fewer bits than a double does: 2.3e-13 at a partner of 1e-312, 3.8e-07 at 1e-317. Geometrically it takes a section a millionth as thick as it is wide AND a target within 1e-150 ring spans of the source, at once — for a metre-scale ring, a separation a hundred and thirty decades below the Planck length. The boundary is asserted in both directions, so a regression and an unexplained improvement both fail.

Two findings came out of the checking and neither was sought. First, scipy.special.elliprj returns nan where this routine returns 3.5e+202 — at x = 0, a complement of 1e-300 and a pole 1e-200 past the range — and the same algorithm run in np.longdouble puts the value where the fixed-trip evaluation does, to 1.3e-16. Second, at a unit pole the symmetric route and the shipped amplitude-carrying descent compute the same integral, and in the arc-end-edge corner the descent's own contract documents as degraded the descent is 5.3e-06 out where the symmetric route is 2.5e-16. That corner is the descent's and not the integral's, which is worth knowing but is not this rung's to act on.

What is next
