Conclusions, in the order they matter

  1. The ITER field coil's "unexplained" self-inductance gap is mostly its as-built geometry, and is not a turn count. PF1's centreline measures 248.926 ± 0.03 turns (method validated on the six CS modules' undisputed 554, reproduced to +0.03), ruling out the 249.36 the gap would require. The attribution ladder — everything converged, nothing tuned — closes 82 % of the +4.14e-03 H gap: measured turn count +1.62e-03, as-built outline and turn positions +2.71e-03, the true 3-D path −9.25e-04, leaving +7.30e-04 H, which is the granularity scale the earlier winding study measured (+6.7e-04) and exactly what the finite-section stage now tests. The PF1–CS2U mutual agrees to 1.5e-06. Record.
  2. The solenoid reference and the solenoid centreline file disagree about where the coil is. The machine table's CS values match the SHIPPED outline revision (granularity-scale gap of +5.0e-04), while the 111004/1 centreline packs sit 202 mm higher and 19 mm inside — on the OLDER itergeom revision (1.687, 5.464, 0.740 × 2.093; confirmed independently by the 14-layer histogram) — worth −2.14e-02 H, forty times the winding effect. Until this provenance conflict is resolved upstream, no solenoid winding effect is readable from the centreline file.
  3. The CS entry's cross-section stores exactly half its radius. The nine-point section convention is a circle of radius hypot(delta_r, delta_z) in a possibly tilted plane — exact to 10⁻¹⁴ mm on all 21 conductors across three files. PF stores 17.500 mm (Ø35 cable space, correct); CS stores exactly 8.150 mm where the documented cable space is Ø32.6 mm (radius 16.30): a diameter→radius halving applied to an already-radius value. For modelling, the CS section is Ø32.6 mm. Record.
  4. nova's element grid is complete on the polygon column. The prism (PolyBeam) couples a straight segment through an arbitrary polygon section in closed form — arsinh and arctan only, one expression covering inside, boundary and outside with no inside test, finite where the box element is non-finite, round-off everywhere (7e-15 envelope), Beam parity 1.0e-14, cost 1.39/1.80 µs/pair. Polygon-profile straight segments now route to it, closing the 4/3-hexagon / 4/π-disc overstatement. Record.
