§1 — Why this exists, and what is already settled

The ring element spreads a source element's current at constant density over its whole section polygon — no jacket, no insulation, no cooling channel, no turn structure. An nturn count scales the result and never subdivides the area. That assumption was nowhere stated until now, and in its absence a four-dimensional quadrature oracle built alongside the element read as an arbiter of the real conductor. It is not one: it shares the assumption by construction.

The assumption is not neutral in sign. Concentrating the same current into discrete sub-conductors inside one outline shrinks each conductor's own geometric mean distance and raises the self inductance, so smearing over the gross outline is the lowest self inductance an outline can carry. A wound coil sits above it — and the machine description sits above the element on all three self terms, which is the direction granularity predicts.

This plan owns that question. It is not a kernel-repair task: the kernels are repaired and merged (see nova-3d-native). It is a modelling question with real decisions in it, and it was split out because a research question wearing a bugfix's clothing is what made the parent plan feel endless.

§4 — The field coil anomaly

Rather than extrapolate a logarithm, the conductor sweep was pushed to absurdity to find the diameter at which each coil's gap actually closes. These are builds, not fits:

coilgap [H]diameter the gap requiresis that a conductor?
CS3U / CS2U+4.7795e-04 30.1 mm annulus over a 10 mm channel, or 40.5 mm solid disc Yes — a 30 mm cable space inside a 49 mm jacket at a 51 mm pitch is an ITER cable-in-conduit conductor.
PF1+4.0988e-03 about 2.5 mm solid disc No — 0.14 % of the site cell. A wire, not a conductor, and nothing that could be wound into a poloidal field coil.

The two required diameters differ by more than an order of magnitude, so no single conductor model reaches both.

The gap restated as a turn count — the strongest lead

A pack's inductance goes as the square of its turn count, so a discrepancy can be restated as turns, which is the scale any competing explanation has to be judged on. The derivative was measured, by rebuilding the converged continuum at a perturbed turn count rather than assuming quadratic scaling:

coildL/dN [H/turn]local exponentgap as turns
PF15.673e-032.005+0.723
CS3U2.877e-032.005+0.166
CS2U2.877e-032.005+0.166

The exponent comes out at 2.005, so the restatement is sound. A solenoid module's discrepancy is a sixth of a turn; the field coil's is three quarters of one, and PF1 carries a fractional 248.64 turns. That a coil sits within a single turn of its description — and that 248.64 + 0.723 is 249.36 — is worth weighing against the turn count itself before anything more elaborate is attempted.

Counted and solved ✓ landed 2026-07-29: PF1's as-built centreline measures 248.926 ± 0.03 turns (the six CS modules validate the method, reproducing their undisputed 554 to +0.03) — favouring the design's 248.64 and ruling out the gap-implied 249.36: the gap is not a turn count. The wound-filament ladder then attributes it: against the converged continuum's +4.14e-03, the measured turn count is worth +1.62e-03, the as-built outline and turn positions +2.71e-03, and the true 3-D path −9.25e-04, leaving +7.30e-04 — 82 % of the field coil's gap is as-built geometry, with the remainder at the granularity scale the finite-section stage tests. The PF1–CS2U mutual agrees to 1.5e-06. The solenoid modules invert the story: the machine table's CS values match the SHIPPED outline revision, while the 111004/1 centreline packs sit 202 mm higher / 19 mm inside (worth −2.14e-02, forty times the winding effect) on the older itergeom revision — a geometry-revision conflict to reconcile upstream before any solenoid winding effect is readable. Nothing was tuned toward the table. Full record: wound filament landed (develop merge b92d2c88).

The finite section ruled out ✓ landed 2026-07-29: sweeping the true 35 mm cable space along the true path (PolyBow on the 893 arcs, PolyBeam on the 189 lines, corner-count convergence quadratic and carry-validated to nine decimals) moves PF1 by +2.0e-06 H solid and −4.2e-05 H with the cooling channel — the as-built conductor opens the residual to +7.670e-04, so the granularity-scale remainder is not the section (closing it would need a 19.4 mm cable space against the real 35). The channel superposition (coupled ±J pair) validates against a cell-by-cell annulus partition to 1e-16. The feeder run — 36 m of conductor no rung ever carried — is worth +3.335e-04 H, 46 % of the residual, conditional on whether the machine table is terminal-to-terminal or pack-only. Decisions locked on this evidence: conductor-model → uniform + correction, reference-status → cross-check. Also found: the frame's poly column is a swept footprint, not a cross-section — a winding pack cannot yet reach the framed elements with its true section (recorded for the element plans). Full record: finite section landed (develop merge 682a26ef).

The honest framing survives measurement: the two coils' discrepancies have different causes — the field coil's is largely its as-built geometry plus the circuit boundary, the solenoid's is which outline revision the reference was computed on. Closing either by tuning a conductor parameter would be reproducing the model we removed.

The device wins everywhere and the margin grows with the tile — 41× the eight-process host pool over the whole build at a 256-section tile. The crossover for this problem is below the smallest tile measured: there is no tile width at which the host is competitive. A single tile beats the whole build at the same width (1.02 against 1.46 at 256) because a build pays per-tile dispatch and a zarr write on top of the kernel, and that gap closes as the tile grows and the tile count falls.

Host and device agree. Over every pair of the complete operator, all three components, the worst absolute deviation is 5.638e-17 — relative 1.81e-12. The traced kernel and the numpy kernel are the same arithmetic in float64. Per tile the worst deviation runs 1.6e-18 at eight sections to 1.3e-17 at 256, growing with the tile only because a bigger tile holds more pairs to be worst over. No disagreement to report.

§ Decisions

What conductor model should the shipped inductance operator carry: uniform current over the gross section (today, with the assumption now stated), uniform current plus an explicit documented granularity correction, or a discrete-turn source built from the as-built centrelines?

Granularity is measured and robust: concentration contributes +6.72e-04 to +6.81e-04 H on a solenoid module across every spare-site placement policy, a 1.3 % spread. But it explains the solenoid and not the field coil, so a correction fitted to close both would be reproducing the geometric-mean-distance model that was just removed.

Measured on the full PF1 ladder (develop 682a26ef): the step that matters is discrete turns AT THE AS-BUILT POSITION, +4.33e-03 (0.61%) from the smeared continuum to the ring stack; the helical path is worth -9.25e-04 (-0.13%); the true swept section is worth +2.0e-06 solid to -4.2e-05 with the channel, three to four orders below the placement term. So the shipped operator stays uniform over the gross section with the assumption stated, and a per-coil correction for turn count, pack position and path is derived ONCE from the wound ladder and carried on the smeared coil. A per-turn near-diagonal finite-section treatment at runtime (20-57 min CPU per coil per section variant, measured) buys a term smaller than the residual that remains, and a correction fitted to close both coils would reproduce the removed geometric-mean-distance model — the correction is derived from as-built geometry, never fitted to the table.

What standing does the machine-description inductance table have — oracle, cross-check, or a value with its own stated model? It reproduces the solenoid once granularity is modelled, and disagrees with the field coil by four times what granularity can buy.

The table cannot arbitrate its own construction. It is above the uniform-current continuum on all three self terms, which is the direction granularity predicts, and the solenoid gap is closed by a cable space near 30 mm. The field coil's is not closed by any plausible conductor.

The table cannot arbitrate as-built fidelity. (a) PF1's self sits 79% of the way from the wound path back to the coaxial ring stack and within -1.95e-04 of the ring stack — 21% of what the helix is worth — so it behaves like a discrete-turn AXISYMMETRIC computation that does not know the winding is a helix. (b) The PF1-CS2U mutual lands +1.5e-06 of the table at the wound-path rung, confirming path and turn count. (c) The solenoid selfs match the SHIPPED outline revision while the as-built centreline packs sit on the older one (worth -2.14e-02, forty times the winding effect) — an upstream geometry-revision conflict the table cannot see. (d) The circuit boundary (feeders in or out, worth +3.335e-04 on PF1) is undetermined. Such a reference flags gross errors; it is not an oracle for either coil family.
