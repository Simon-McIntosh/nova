Wave 6 verdict and the new arrangement fault

The inherited ~1050-line quadrature replacement of the rectangle kernel was evaluated and rejected on measurement: route gap 8.7e-13…3.5e-12 (indistinguishable from the closed form's own distance to extended precision), cost 81×/125× in interior/near regimes (per-pair Python dispatch replacing one vectorised elliptic pass), a traced route that compared the kernel against itself, and a pinned NaN-Jacobian contract. The salvage: Cylinder now routes through the canonical kernel (duplicate four-corner rule deleted; on-axis limit gained; μ₀ coherence — a +1.3203e-10 pure-permeability shift, reported; axis nan → finite). New fault, measured and mechanism-attributed: the four-corner combination amplifies the widest corner term by 9.4e2/1.5e4/3.4e4 (compact/small/slender), so achievable precision is eps×ratio; the per-term rearrangement was measured and ruled out; the working fix (closed-form removal of the third-kind pole moments' divergent part against cphi) is scoped with the mpmath fan as its gate. This amplification is a conditioning argument for the polygon route over the corner rule on slender sections.

The code already ships the position: the product default is the exact closed-form polygon source; banded/standoff/filament/quadrature-ring are immutable PolySectionPolicy identities in the cache key (pinned by tests). The decision as posed is a ratification. Cost per pair (hexagon): boundary quadrature 858–869 µs, closed form 162.9 µs (5.2× cheaper than quadrature), three-band 31.5 µs, filament 3.7 µs; whole 2339-cell grid: 274.5 s quadrature / 54.5 s closed form / 19.2–36.4 s banded / 1.2 s filament. GPU: on one H200 the closed form costs what the quadrature costs for one-to-two orders more accuracy; no eight-device figure exists (the projection was withdrawn), so a banded-after-gpu choice has no number to condition on.

The force-target quadrature study

Commits a3beda4e/7e2bd619 (merged 2026-08-11). Verdict: the coil force computation CAN move onto the section quadrature, by a wider margin than linked flux gained — and the premises around the locked hybrid decision were corrected by measurement. First, the knob: force resolution is set by nforce through a separate PolyTarget tiling, not by dcoil — totals are bit-identical across dcoil −2/−5/−20 because the exact source route makes a tiling sum to the section identically (§4's dcoil sentence is corrected accordingly). Second, the consumer census: every Force reader in nova/, apps/ and tests/ consumes per-conductor totals; reduce=False (the in-coil map) has no caller — subdivision's real surviving role is the plasma force path, whose per-cell turn densities one fan cannot represent (the new rule refuses it rather than averaging).

Measured against a lattice-refined clipped-fan arbiter validated three independent ways (self-consistency ≤7e-10; Newton's third law to every digit; the constant-current energy gradient agreeing to 7.3e-07 with fourth-order stencil convergence): on a solenoid module's self-dominated radial force, 18 fan nodes match 4096 tiled cells (227×); action-reaction balance reaches at 256 fan nodes what 8192 tiled cells reach (32×); through the operator on eleven ITER-scale conductors, fan p=8 costs 2.8 s where the accuracy-equivalent tiling costs 371 s (~130×). The self term is where the fan wins most — the integrand is analytic inside the section and the midpoint rule's constant is largest exactly there. One measured inversion: on a component that is a near-total self-field cancellation (vertical force on a voided section) the tiling is 5× better at equal nodes. The shipped default stays subdivision; the fan is an opt-in ForceTargetPolicy frozen into the cache identity, refusals over silent approximation, 17 pinning tests.

A divergent shipped value was found and fixed on the default path: Fc, the crushing first moment, normalised its arm by the tile-cell height instead of the conductor height and grew without bound under refinement (−861 → −16,927 MN across nforce 4→1024; plotted by nova/imas/matrix.py, asserted nowhere, which is how it survived). Fixed by binding each target row's arm to its parent conductor — Fr/Fz unchanged, convergence now pinned. The moment-arm code path has exactly one importer and one consumer, so the fix is contained.
