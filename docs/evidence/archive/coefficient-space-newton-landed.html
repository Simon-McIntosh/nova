The shipped coupling is already second order. The repair gains 0.00957 in band order — inside its own uncertainty — and reduces no error. So the monopole-plus-dipole clipped representation does not account for the banked 0.97, and that hypothesis is refuted rather than qualified.

The figure being explained was the artefact. Tracing its provenance, 0.966596902394278 is one of six per-reference values computed as log(coarse residual / fine residual) / log(coarse spacing / fine spacing) — a two-point estimator, once per reference. Its siblings: 21983 → 2.7019, 21985 → 3.3074, 21989 → 3.3870, 22086 → 1.6372, and 21978 → 0.9666. Orders above three are not physical for a second-order method; they are what a two-point estimator on noisy residuals produces. And the receipt that carried 0.97 into this campaign selected reference 21978 precisely because it was weakest_refinement_response_of_six. The campaign was therefore explaining the minimum of a noise-dominated scatter running to 3.39, quoted as though it were the method's order, while the controlled ladder measures 1.99–2.01 at ±0.015.

The order deficit is retired as a finding. Two attributions were raised and both refuted — a Δ* stencil that is not in the residual path, and a clipped-source coupling that is already second order. The correct disposition is not a third attribution but the withdrawal of the premise. The node's own follow-on proposed isolating the remaining responsible term; that hunt is declined on this evidence, and the reason is recorded here so it is not re-opened by someone reading only the follow-on.

A zero edge current is sufficient for the band never to bind — four of four. A large edge current is necessary but not sufficient: it binds on both coarse oracle carriers and on neither fine one. So the curvature jump is real where the profiles do not taper, and whether it becomes the binding regional term depends on resolution — refining the carrier moves the band error below the closed-flux and scrape-off terms without removing the jump that causes it.

What that means for the plan, stated as scope rather than as a win. The experimental and stored-reference lane — which is what the DIII-D and MAST work reads — carries no curvature discontinuity, so a globally smooth coefficient basis is sufficient there on this evidence. The analytic oracle lane does carry one, at up to the full peak of the operator, and it is the accuracy oracle. So curvature-jump support is required for the case set as a whole while not being required for the lane the campaign's open questions live on. Any decision on boundary support should be taken against that split rather than against the aggregate verdict.

Two limits on this reading. Both receipts are solve-free fits and sizings, so neither establishes anything about solver behaviour in a coefficient basis — that is §5, and it has not run. And the band is a fixed ψN window of [0.95, 1.05] rather than a fixed physical width, so its cell content varies between coarse and fine carriers; the resolution dependence reported above is consistent with that and is not independent of it.
