Two elliptic derivative repairs fell out of the work: the third kind's forward mode no longer returns NaN on the unreflected side, and the Carlson degenerate form's slope takes an even series in v² below ratio 0.1, moving slope error from 3×10−9 to 3×10−15 near small poles at unchanged values and +5.4% cost on the arc path alone. §2 — What the array can and cannot resolve

Merge b4521803. The identifiability spectrum at the real MAST sensor set, taken before any solve, is what bounds every claim below it. The resolvable set is rigid+affine: rigid conditioning is O(1), affine O(10³), and per-vertex freedoms are hopeless — free corners 10⁸, free turns 10¹³ — so they are frozen. Leading modes run from 54 µm (P5 lower) to 2.2 mm (P4 upper) marginalised; tilt and dilation are unresolvable at 15–180 mm.

Two findings here are load-bearing for anything downstream. Cutting the near-field probes costs ×3.2–17.3 in best-mode resolution, so the geometry information lives in the near probes — a solve keeps them and drives the 23-cell lattice rather than excluding them for the hull-smear trap, and a spectrum quoted without that cut is not evidence. And the 18 admitted flux loops carry 20–290× less geometry signal per channel than a probe, because flux is a smoother functional of pack shape than field: they buy two extra affine modes inside 10 mm and lift the degenerate directions up to 40× by breaking the geometry/current degeneracy, but they change no verdict. The geometry solve is probe-carried — the opposite of the harmonic fit, which the loops transform.

A standing kernel constraint for any solve: a zero-weight flat edge carries no derivative, so tilt and shear Jacobians are taken at mirrored small tilts and averaged (FLAT_EDGE_TILT, regression-pinned). Bypassing it silently corrupts those gradients by 5–26×.

§3 — The solver is calibrated before it is believed

Commit 81ef6739, merge e941cfb8. Metric-aware damped Gauss–Newton / Levenberg–Marquardt on exact-Jacobian callbacks, with per-channel floor whitening, a 13-column current-nuisance projection, and a 10 mm one-noise mode cutoff. The optimiser choice is the locked decision: after freezing per-vertex the parameter count is small and conditioning benign, the exact Jacobian is already in hand, and the normal-equation curvature is where the confidence bounds come from.

The recovery ladder is what licenses the verdict. Across 4,096 recoveries at the measured noise floors: maximum synthetic mean bias 0.27 σ, empirical-to-predicted mode resolution 0.946–1.099, and nominal 95% coverage realised at 93.3–95.5%. The solver recovers what it is given, and its error bars mean what they say.
