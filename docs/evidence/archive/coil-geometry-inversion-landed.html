§2 — What the array can and cannot resolve

Merge b4521803. The identifiability spectrum at the real MAST sensor set, taken before any solve, is what bounds every claim below it. The resolvable set is rigid+affine: rigid conditioning is O(1), affine O(10³), and per-vertex freedoms are hopeless — free corners 10⁸, free turns 10¹³ — so they are frozen. Leading modes run from 54 µm (P5 lower) to 2.2 mm (P4 upper) marginalised; tilt and dilation are unresolvable at 15–180 mm.

Two findings here are load-bearing for anything downstream. Cutting the near-field probes costs ×3.2–17.3 in best-mode resolution, so the geometry information lives in the near probes — a solve keeps them and drives the 23-cell lattice rather than excluding them for the hull-smear trap, and a spectrum quoted without that cut is not evidence. And the 18 admitted flux loops carry 20–290× less geometry signal per channel than a probe, because flux is a smoother functional of pack shape than field: they buy two extra affine modes inside 10 mm and lift the degenerate directions up to 40× by breaking the geometry/current degeneracy, but they change no verdict. The geometry solve is probe-carried — the opposite of the harmonic fit, which the loops transform.

A standing kernel constraint for any solve: a zero-weight flat edge carries no derivative, so tilt and shear Jacobians are taken at mirrored small tilts and averaged (FLAT_EDGE_TILT, regression-pinned). Bypassing it silently corrupts those gradients by 5–26×.

§6 — What the section representation is worth, measured

Prototype commit a7eedaf7 with banked artifacts under ~/.cache/nova-mast/rectangle-sections/, and the read-only winding-geometry investigation delivered 2026-08-16. The chamfered P4/P5 pack resolves into five series-weighted rectangles carrying 6+6+6+4+1 of the 23 drive turns — the chamfer uniquely identifies the vacant 24th cell, so the decomposition needs no fitting. Per drive ampere at the real array, the three representations are indistinguishable: lattice-vs-hull at most 5.1×10−7 T/A (1.3×10−3 of the 387 µT floor), rows-vs-lattice at most 3.7×10−8 T/A. At operating current the ranking changes: the prior symmetry study measured the lattice-vs-hull difference at 565–571 µT at the nearest probes — 1.46–1.48× the floor — while rows-vs-lattice stays below it. The hull is therefore the one representation that is measurably wrong near the pack at operating scale, and rectangle rows recover the lattice's prediction at a fifth of its element count.

The winding investigation generalises this machine-wide with citations: every active family's store record is already finite rectangles (CS 656 half-weight cells; P2 12+8 per side; P3 8; P4/P5 23 as 18+5; P6 4), the packaged registry's convex hulls overstate section area by 6.3–26.0% per family, and three electrical facts must not be inferred from geometry — the CS 304/328/344.657 turn-count conflict under its two-parallel-feed model, the unresolved P2 inner/outer interconnection, and the archive counterexamples against the published P3 series pairing. Consequence for ownership: the rectangle decomposition is description content and lands in the machine map, not in this plan's solver, which consumes whatever sections the description declares.
