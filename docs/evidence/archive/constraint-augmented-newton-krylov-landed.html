The compensating circuit is read off the constraint-response matrix (a6f7bc89, merged at e247cff6)

Commits: d41f771b (response matrix, selection rules, derived pairs), d608eaf9 (selection restricted to circuits a caller can drive), a6f7bc89 (two-row receipt docs/figures/constraint-augmented-newton-krylov/compensator-selection/two-rows.json and figure). H200 job 1262840, exit 0. Gate: 25 tests on CPU, seven of them the new response-matrix contracts on a four-node, four-circuit fixture whose only vertical authority is one antisymmetric pair.

The response matrix is one reverse-mode pass per residual row contracted with the operator's prescribed-current response carrier, exact because the circuits enter the flux linearly, so its cost is the rows and not the 101 circuits. Dividing each row by its declared scale gives an authority matrix whose entries are that row's own scales moved per ampere. A row's steepest direction is its own authority row, normalised so the largest participating circuit carries unity; rows whose steepest directions are nearly parallel (normalised Gram above 0.5) are competing and take the pseudo-inverse columns instead, which move one row and leave the others still. The rule is chosen from that test or named outright, and the singular values of the drivable block ride on the record.

No circuit dominates (the top six sit within a factor of two), so the derived direction is an antisymmetric combination spread over the solenoid and the P3, P6 and P4 pairs, and the compensating current cannot be the pair's -4103 or +3022 A while the direction is derived. Two caveats travel with the improvement: the matrix is read at the seed flux, and authority per ampere says nothing about collateral change, so a rule that maximises it will use a circuit whose effect on the centroid is mediated by reshaping the plasma as readily as one that displaces it. Whether the compensating current should keep its reading as the current the machine would have driven for vertical control is the lead's question (q-cank-compensator-reading).

The wall-point boundary flux tangent is exact where a derivative exists; the 51 percent was a kink (84b23ee6, merged at be67af33)

Node: cank-wall-null-flux-tangent, Opus, run r-20260905T172708183399 against base 194c79fc, 29 minutes; commit adds tests/test_wall_null_flux_tangent.py (20 CPU contracts, 8 s, no solve) and changes nothing under nova/biot. Gate: 71 passed over the null, admission, import and tangent files on CPU.

The reported figures reproduce exactly and are not a gradient defect. On the bootstrapped Solov'ev free-boundary machine, differentiating the wall read along conductor 0's flux image at the analytic seed gives autodiff 2.314332e-06 against a central difference of 1.534682e-06 Wb/A (error 5.08e-01) while the axis-flux tangent agrees to 6.9e-08. The seed puts the material boundary on a seed flux surface, so the sampled wall flux is bit-exactly constant (max minus min 0.0 Wb), every node is a maximiser and the extremum is a kink: the forward quotient promotes the node where the image is largest (2.314612e-06), the backward quotient the node where it is smallest (7.547510e-07), and the central difference is exactly their mean, which no tangent can equal. The autodiff value is the forward slope to 1.2e-04 relative, the derivative that exists on that side.

At a strict extremum the tangent is correct. On the converged limited free-boundary equilibrium (newton_krylov, tolerance 1e-8, wall flux spread 0.0955 Wb, promoted node 59) autodiff 2.27875170e-06 against central difference 2.27875161e-06, 3.99e-08 relative at a step of 1e-4 of the span and 1.32e-05 at 1e-6, the latter being the quotient's own cancellation noise. Probing sixteen conductor images: conductor 0 agrees to 3.3e-12 at every step from 1e-2 to 1e-6 of the span; conductors 2 and 6 sit at step-independent 1.58e-05 and 4.77e-05 because the promoted cluster index flips (31 to 30) between the backward and forward states, so the central difference averages two different three-node fits; against the side that keeps the base cluster the tangent agrees to 3.2e-07. Ruled out with measurements: the least-squares JVP (1e-13 against a dense solve, condition 3654), the vertex-length gradient path (bit-identical under an envelope form), and the generic non-degenerate case (an independent analytic probe agreed to 5.5e-10).

What is real, and outside the node's fence. The wall polish selects its three-node bracket by a discrete argmax, so the boundary flux is piecewise-smooth in the flux state with a slope jump wherever the promoted wall node changes, and a Newton step, a constraint row or an elimination on a limited machine can cross that jump. A selection-continuous polish (blended adjacent brackets, or a fixed window on a continuous arclength estimate) in nova/geometry/select.py (traced_wall_flux, traced_wall_index) would remove it; recorded as the next followup on the plan. Two fence clauses were unreachable by construction and the contract states the reachable figures: 1e-4 relative at the fine step (cancellation noise) and no 1e-8 axis agreement by central difference at double precision (floor near 5e-07).

A selection-continuous wall polish: no slope jump when the promoted node changes (0081c3e9, merged at 3fbd24a7)

Node: cank-selection-continuous-wall-polish, local DeepSeek lane, run r-20260905T205338987536 against base a4451bec, about 3.3 hours; nova/geometry/select.py (+164/-?), tests/test_wall_polish_continuity.py (308 lines, six contracts) and tests/test_wall_null_flux_tangent.py (flip cases against a central difference, one-sided checks upgraded to a second-order quotient). Gate 62 passed on CPU over the continuity, tangent and null files; one failure in the adjacent tests/test_forward_operator_tangent.py reproduces identically at the base revision and is pre-existing.

Mechanism. The wall read names the argmax three-node bracket and its strongest adjacent neighbour and blends the two fitted stationary points by a C2 smoothstep weight in the sampled-flux margin between them, with a window of 2 percent of the wall-flux span (chosen by sweeping 0.5 to 10 percent: agreement with a central difference sits near 7e-10 across the range) and a 1e-30 floor below which a bit-flat wall keeps the discrete bracket, since a kink has no selection to smooth. Host and traced routes share the logic; closed-ring duplicate points are excluded from the competitor margin.

The compensating currents carry the commanded sign on both arms, and that is all the manifest's phrase "steers in the commanded direction" can mean: the boundary itself moves a fifth of the command as a rigid shift in the first arm and not at all along the commanded axis in the second. Both solves ended on the Newton-Krylov route's active-set termination after two and four trips with the rows three decades above tolerance, and tens of amperes on circuits that need hundreds to move a MAST boundary by centimetres. Two mechanisms are on the table and the receipt does not separate them: the worker's, that the four-and-a-half-decade compensator spectrum sets a closure floor near 1e-3 of row scale, so the singular-distribution direction cannot close ten competing rows; and the coordinator's, that the active-set stop cut the solve short before the currents grew, since a cycle detected at trip two is a termination and not a convergence. The steering-effectiveness measurement is the next node on the plan; until it lands the bounding-box rows are a vocabulary the playable solve can command but not yet a control that reaches its target.

Reading. Neither hypothesis of the followup survives in the form it was posed, and the numbers settle which was closer. Achieved motion does not scale with the command: 3.3, 4.1 and 3.9 mm for 5, 10 and 20 mm, with a compensating current that is not even monotonic. Lifting the stops changed nothing: the 20 mm arm is bit-identical to the original receipt because the cycle detector, the one stop not exposed as a toggle, fires at the same state, and the elongation arm given sixteen extra trips ends at the residual and current it had at trip four. The reduced route shows the other face of the same floor: it closes the rows to machine precision by spending megaamperes down the 7.9e-11 direction and destroys the equilibrium doing it. One compensating direction per row over a spectrum spanning thirty thousand cannot both close ten competing rows and keep a plasma. That is the record behind the lead's decision of the same morning to steer shape through the pulse-design inverse step instead (decision shape-control-formulation on playable-forward-solve); the constraint rows remain the tool for diagnostic placement, where one row has one physical actuator. The reduced route's willingness to accept a megaampere compensator is a defect in its own right and is recorded as the next followup.
