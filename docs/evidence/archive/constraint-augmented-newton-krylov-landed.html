The compensating circuit is read off the constraint-response matrix (a6f7bc89, merged at e247cff6)

Commits: d41f771b (response matrix, selection rules, derived pairs), d608eaf9 (selection restricted to circuits a caller can drive), a6f7bc89 (two-row receipt docs/figures/constraint-augmented-newton-krylov/compensator-selection/two-rows.json and figure). H200 job 1262840, exit 0. Gate: 25 tests on CPU, seven of them the new response-matrix contracts on a four-node, four-circuit fixture whose only vertical authority is one antisymmetric pair.

The response matrix is one reverse-mode pass per residual row contracted with the operator's prescribed-current response carrier, exact because the circuits enter the flux linearly, so its cost is the rows and not the 101 circuits. Dividing each row by its declared scale gives an authority matrix whose entries are that row's own scales moved per ampere. A row's steepest direction is its own authority row, normalised so the largest participating circuit carries unity; rows whose steepest directions are nearly parallel (normalised Gram above 0.5) are competing and take the pseudo-inverse columns instead, which move one row and leave the others still. The rule is chosen from that test or named outright, and the singular values of the drivable block ride on the record.

The plan's gate asked the centroid row to reproduce the P6-pair result where P6 is the dominant singular direction, and that condition is false on the bank: at the seed on 21986/46 the vertical-centroid authority in micrometres per ampere is solenoid 0.914, P3 upper 0.732, P6 upper 0.586, P4 upper 0.515, P6 lower -0.428, and on 21989/55 P3 lower -0.572, P3 upper 0.554, P6 lower -0.514. No circuit dominates (the top six sit within a factor of two), so the derived direction is an antisymmetric combination spread over the solenoid and the P3, P6 and P4 pairs, and the compensating current cannot be the pair's -4103 or +3022 A while the direction is derived. The pair reproduction is carried by the CPU contract instead, where the derived direction equals the named pair to 1e-12 component by component. The first receipt derived over every column of the response carrier and let passive structure carry the current (circuit 15 at 0.733 micrometres per ampere, second in authority); the committed receipt restricts the selection to the thirteen-entry active mapping. Two caveats travel with the improvement: the matrix is read at the seed flux, and authority per ampere says nothing about collateral change, so a rule that maximises it will use a circuit whose effect on the centroid is mediated by reshaping the plasma as readily as one that displaces it. Whether the compensating current should keep its reading as the current the machine would have driven for vertical control is the lead's question (q-cank-compensator-reading).
