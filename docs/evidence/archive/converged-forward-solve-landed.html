The measurement that changed the design. The proposed field representation, the six-coefficient own-node quadratic, reconstructs the flux at third order on both analytic states, but the support it induces is case-dependent. On the limited (weak) boundary the per-cell membership error is second order: 6.5e-4 of the cell at 132 cells, 3.2e-5 at 2616. On the diverted boundary it is half order: 3.3e-2 at 132 cells, 9.4e-3 at 2616, and the two X-point samples set the whole budget while the next-worst smooth cell sits at 3.8e-3 falling to 1.2e-4 with order 2.3. The vanishing gradient at the saddle turns a third-order flux error into a half-order position error. Consequence: the saddle cell needs its own representation or its own budget, and sub-cell membership can never be held to a resolution-independent cap.

Follow-ons carried into the implementation gates (from the second claude-opus review): budget the saddle-neighbourhood cells separately from smooth-boundary cells and assert the measured order is at least p; declare each constraint row's scale so the 1e-12 row clause cannot be met by choosing the scale; land versioned acceptance-binding and budget-coefficient tests with the gates; bind the document check logs into the suite fields.
