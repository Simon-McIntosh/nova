§1 — the design: four pure functions and the inventory they replace

Three runs on 2026-10-04, all merged. The design document is docs/research/converged-forward-solve-design.html.

  • r-20261004T123242201996-cfs-design (codex-astra), merged at a9fb45dcc, review 86. Wrote the document: four signatures with invariants, 17 null-finding and 23 region-finding source bindings each with a destination or a deletion, the three defect mechanisms against their evidence, and the acceptance as 10 clause tests and 26 row tests.
  • r-20261004T155210829845-cfs-design-readback-repairs (codex), merged at 3326f6712, review 77 with durability exempted as a prose document. Resolved the five two-reading sentences the local read-back found.
  • r-20261005T163635854835-cfs-design-saddle-rule (codex), commit 6ea685c39 merged on 2026-10-05, review 96 with durability exempted as a prose document. Brought the document in line with the two decisions the lead locked on 2026-10-05: the saddle cell's support comes from the null's normal form, never from level-set roots, and the null position, gradient and Hessian come from the Biot kernels' analytic derivatives, never from differentiating the mesh flux. The half-order level-set probe is kept as the before arm; the saddle-neighbourhood acceptance is second order with its own coefficient; test_saddle_support_order[analytic-row] joins the clause table; receipts above a few MB live on GPFS with a path pointer.
  • r-20261004T161650844174-cfs-design-opus-repairs (codex-astra), merged at 0d70adb6c, review 88. Resolved the two blocking findings of the first claude-opus re-review (score 70): the DIII-D clause set restated from the plan with an EFIT-null test; and the resolution-independent membership and moment caps replaced by budgets C·(h/R₀)ᵖ measured from 490 local fits of the proposed quadratic. Second claude-opus re-review: 88, no blocking finding.

The measurement that changed the design. The proposed field representation, the six-coefficient own-node quadratic, reconstructs the flux at third order on both analytic states, but the support it induces is case-dependent. On the limited (weak) boundary the per-cell membership error is second order: 6.5e-4 of the cell at 132 cells, 3.2e-5 at 2616. On the diverted boundary it is half order: 3.3e-2 at 132 cells, 9.4e-3 at 2616, and the two X-point samples set the whole budget while the next-worst smooth cell sits at 3.8e-3 falling to 1.2e-4 with order 2.3. The vanishing gradient at the saddle turns a third-order flux error into a half-order position error. Consequence: the saddle cell needs its own representation or its own budget, and sub-cell membership can never be held to a resolution-independent cap.

Follow-ons carried into the implementation gates (from the second claude-opus review): budget the saddle-neighbourhood cells separately from smooth-boundary cells and assert the measured order is at least p; declare each constraint row's scale so the 1e-12 row clause cannot be met by choosing the scale; land versioned acceptance-binding and budget-coefficient tests with the gates; bind the document check logs into the suite fields.

Read-backs: the plan went through six local read-backs (11, 4, 3, 2, 0, 0 two-reading sentences), the design document through one (5, then 0 after repair). The design read-back loop is recorded under ~/.config/reckon/crew/reports/nova/s21-nova/.
