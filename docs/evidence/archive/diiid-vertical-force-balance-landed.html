The grid axis under both flux polarities

The defect, reproduced before the repair. The restored helper normalised the flux by the boundary-minus-axis span and then took nanargmin or nanargmax of the normalised array according to the sign of that span. The map psi -> (psi - axis)/(boundary - axis) is affine with slope 1/(boundary - axis), so it preserves the ordering of psi when the span is positive and reverses it when the span is negative. Under the falling span nanargmax of the normalised array is therefore argmin of the flux — the outermost grid corner — while the axis under that sign is the flux maximum.
