What survives is neither branch the section posed

The map admits the solution and the inputs match it, yet the solve does not arrive. The terminal attempts are retained and explicitly excluded as unqualified: requested 300 achieved 1.2549724178436488 and requested 500 achieved 1.2998050585857959 against a 1e−10 criterion. Where the solve does converge, the banked roots sit 19–38 % from the closed form under a common gauge. So the residue is in which fixed point the solver reaches.

That is a materially better position than either branch of §2, and it should be said plainly: a map that admits the right answer with a solver that misses it is a bounded, local problem, whereas an inconsistent operator would have invalidated every forward result in this repository.

It also retro-explains two results on the plateau lane that were otherwise loose. That plan's four documented input substitutions converged on nothing — no wall, wiring, profile or discretisation candidate accounts for the residual — which is what no-wrong-input-plus-wrong-root predicts. And its §6 showed the same solver reaching 2.409e−16 the moment the class refusal is relaxed, on 0 of 10 diverted terminals: it finds roots easily and finds the wrong class. Both plans now point at the same question, from opposite directions.

Retraction 1 — the pure arm contracts, and the measurement is clean. All six ratios are below one with r² ≥ 0.99 on five, so slow geometric contraction is resolved rather than inferred from noise. The earlier claim that the map "does not contract" about the diverted root was wrong; against imas-efit's 0.33 the stall is a rate problem. Quote the range as 0.9613–0.9835: the lower endpoint 0.9503 is 21978/35's, whose pure fit is the single weak one at r² = 0.548, and taking a range endpoint from an unresolved fit is the same over-read withdrawn from the mixed column below. That reference resists a single geometric ratio in both arms — weak pure fit, period-2 mixed — which is a property of the neighbourhood rather than a fitting nuisance.

Retraction 2 — a diverted root has a machine-precision demonstration. §2 above recorded 2.409e−16 on 0 of 10 diverted terminals, and this sprint recorded that no diverted root had been demonstrated anywhere. 22086/43 reached 3.584e−16, achieved_class = diverted, topology_consistent = true, pdiverted = 1.0, converged = true against a 1e−8 criterion, along 1.243e−3 → 1.357e−4 → 1.730e−7 → 7.078e−14. The asymmetry between branches is quantitative, not existential.

The converging arm was unpenalised, which is not what its label implies. 22086/43's margin operand is absent, so max(−margin, 0) was identically zero at all twelve promotions — an unsteered residual-ranked Newton. This strengthens existence, since nothing pushed the solve toward the diverted class, and says nothing about whether margin grading helps. Zero penalty is not sufficient either: 21978/35 ran the same way and settled into a period-2 cycle alternating 1.076e−2 / 1.050e−2.

21989/55 is characterised. It carries the only negative class margin (−0.00340) and the only unsaturated class observable (pdiverted = 0.41 against 1.0 elsewhere), so it is not a diverted configuration at its own label; its defection in both arms is the comparator reporting a boundary case. Its mixed residual falls to 1.727e−3 then rises monotonically to 2.823e−3 as the active penalty fights it.

Measurement-quality caveat: the mixed column does not support a rate claim. Mixed fits carry r² of 0.21, 0.00, 0.48, 0.08, 0.06, 0.72 — three unresolved, and 0.9997 at r² = 0.00 is a null fit rather than a near-unity ratio. "Expands on three of six" should not be quoted as a contraction measurement. All cross-arm terminal comparisons additionally cross two different iterations, per the artifact's own qualification.

Positive result. The terminal projected condition of the Newton residual Jacobian I − J separates the pair in the intuitive direction: the reference that fails to converge is the more ill-conditioned, at both seed and terminal.

The load-bearing negative result. The fixed-point map's spectral radius exceeds one at both references and is larger at the converged one at terminal, so the map is locally expanding in both neighbourhoods. The fitted ρ of 0.9613–0.9835 recorded above is therefore a property of the Newton promotion policy's residual sequence, not of the flux map's local spectrum — the promotion policy contracts slowly while the map beneath it expands, and the comparison against imas-efit's 0.33 compares promotion policies.

The alternation is not an orbit of the map. Alternating states repeat by parity to 6.214e−10 and differ across parity by 9.983e−3, yet replaying the fixed-point map on each misses its partner by 7.711e−3 and 7.327e−3 against a 5e−12 identity tolerance. Nor is it step control: both final promotions accepted a complete undamped Newton action at factor 1.0, effective fraction 1.0, topology penalty 0.0. The alternation belongs to the promotion policy.

Stated limit. A 1.748× conditioning separation is modest against the gap it is asked to explain, and both references are well conditioned in absolute terms at roughly 21 and 37. Conditioning is established as the discriminating direction, not as a sufficient cause.
