Its class-margin operand is absent, so the merit function's max(−margin, 0) term was identically zero at all twelve promotions: the run was an unsteered residual-ranked Newton, which strengthens the existence claim (nothing pushed it toward the diverted class) while saying nothing about whether margin grading helps. Two references, identical inactive penalty, quadratic convergence against a limit cycle. The exact-margin operand is absent (+∞) on both of those references in both arms: the connectivity-localisation defect measured live on the production cohort. 21989/55 is characterised rather than anomalous — it holds the only negative class margin and the only unsaturated p_diverted, so it is not a diverted configuration at its own label and defecting to limited in both arms is the comparator reporting a boundary case. A weak contraction is evidence a diverted fixed point exists; the why of ρ ≈ 0.95–0.98 remains owned by discrete-operator-analytic-error §4's perturbation ladder, deliberately not duplicated here.
