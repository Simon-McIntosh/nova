Whether that makes the operator pair defective or the bound mis-specified is the open question this measurement hands upward; it is not resolved here, and the bound is not moved in the meantime.

What this means for the 1e-6 identity, stated as arithmetic rather than as a recommendation. Extrapolating the control point at the measured order, reaching 1e-6 of span requires the characteristic cell size to shrink by a factor of about 40, to h ≈ 0.0016 m — roughly 1600× the cells. The bound is therefore satisfiable only in the discretisation limit, not at any working resolution. No bound was registered, moved or applied by this node, and the plan's 1e-6 identity stands exactly as written pending an owner decision.
