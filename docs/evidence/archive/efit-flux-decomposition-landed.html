1. There is no floor. The composition converges monotonically at observed order 2.197033799, standard error 0.100329202, R² 0.995846601, and is still improving at the finest grid. The 1.176524998e-3 point is not a limit. Nothing in the operator pair is broken.

Because it is flat rather than growing, the two terms converge at similar order and the coupling simply carries a ~30× larger constant. A near-constant excess is the signature of a dropped constant term rather than a lower-order scheme — which is what makes the far-field treatment in hybrid_greens the first place to look.

With flux and current exact and no stored map anywhere, the failure to reach 1e-6 belongs to nova's operator pair and cannot be charged to the stored reference's own truncation. The 5.152548851e-3 measured on EFIT data is therefore not explained by the input field's quality.

If one is going to pay four-to-five evaluations, the exact kernel is the better buy; the moment filament's advantage would only appear where the near-field fraction is high.

Confirming a docstring claim: rectangular cells have zero third moments, so the order-2 and order-3 moment matrices are bit-identical at every grid and both cost five far-field ring evaluations. The skew term is free and buys nothing for a rectangle.
