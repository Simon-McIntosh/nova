§4 — current density and flux functions on the LCFS interior

Commit 69c7f92a; gate passed. Artifact benchmarks/efit_affine_extraction.py.

Multiplying the Grad–Shafranov current-density expression by R makes it affine in R², so a straight per-surface regression returns both flux functions: R j_φ = R² p′(ψ) + FF′(ψ)/μ₀ — slope p′, intercept FF′/μ₀. The operator is delta_star in nova/equilibrium/conservation.py with the convention documented in place, μ₀ j_φ = −Δ*Φ / (2π R).

The mask's effect is measured, not argued. The same prototype without it admitted 1421 cells and closed the plasma current at −42.88%; masking to the stored boundary admits 938 and closes at −0.0275% — a factor of about 1560. Conductor current living outside the plasma domain was being read as though it were a flux-function profile, which is why the stored-LCFS interior is a locked decision rather than implementation taste.

Two terms are not enough in about half the bins. Refitting each bin with a third R⁴ column resolved that coefficient above its own standard error in 11 of 24 bins, reducing scatter by a median 1.171796e-02. Whether that term is physical (an R-dependent pressure, i.e. toroidal rotation), numerical (thin bins over a short R² lever) or geometric (mask-clipped near-boundary surfaces) is not settled by this measurement. The scatter distribution is consistent with the numerical reading — maximum two-term scatter fraction 0.571 against a median of 0.014, so a small number of bins dominate.

§5 — the third term is numerical, and the sign test is what says so

Commit d5c3d14c; gate passed. Artifact benchmarks/efit_third_term_separation.py (280 lines). Worker time 301 s. Shot 21978 slice 46, all 938 LCFS-interior cells assigned exactly once under both partitions.

The convenient evidence did not appear, and the verdict still went numerical on a stronger test. Equalising cell population was expected to reduce the count of bins resolving an R⁴ coefficient above its own standard error. It did not: the count went 11/24 → 12/24. Had the separation rested on that count alone it would have had to report the term as physical.

The physical criterion fails decisively instead: a single rotation profile cannot alternate the coefficient's sign. Under equal population the coefficients span −28,130.836 to +212,634.549 with 9 positive and 15 negative overall, and the significant subset splits exactly 6 positive and 6 negative. An R-dependent pressure from toroidal rotation would carry a consistent sign across surfaces. So the two-term model stands and no candidate rotation parametrisation is promoted as a third forward input.

Equal-population binning is a stable sort of the identical interior cells by normalised flux followed by an even split into the same 24 bins, so the intervention changes only the partition. Every bin reports its flux range, population, coefficient, standard error, coefficient-to-error ratio, sign, significance, two-term scatter, R² and three-term scatter reduction under both partitions.

Interpretability floor: 39 cells — the minimum population the intervention produced. Below it a third coefficient is not interpreted; at 39–40 cells formal significance is still insufficient without cross-bin sign consistency. The node was explicit that this is a conservative floor and not a claim that 39 cells guarantees physical resolution.

A superseded sibling node, recorded so the gap is not read as lost work

A node dispatched as efit-content-census asked the same question as the forward-input census above and delivered nothing: it was routed at a read-only sandbox tier, which made both its fenced benchmark and its mandatory manifest impossible to write. It reported that correctly as a decision brief rather than failing silently. Its goal is fully covered by 213458a2, so it was not redispatched.

The transferable finding is a routing one: a node whose delivery contract requires a manifest on disk cannot be run at a read-only tier, whatever its investigative character.
