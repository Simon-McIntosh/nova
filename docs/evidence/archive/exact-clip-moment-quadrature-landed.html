Decision brief

Blocked pending lead choice. Lock one boundary representation—chord, chord plus sagitta, or the sampled arc with per-edge Gauss—and pair it with explicit allowable relative error for each of current, radial moment, vertical moment, and frozen-image sup/span. Chord plus sagitta has the measured 66-evaluation bound but would require a budget that admits at least this weak-row result before the full matrix can test it. The sampled arc preserves the fan region and achieved zero discriminator delta with pointwise density, but the measured live work is 8,192–8,448 evaluations per cut cell at the fan's rule, so it cannot satisfy the current under-200 cost gate without a separately justified lower-order edge rule. No density-order repair or full matrix was run.

Receipts: density-region-discriminator.json and discriminator-rerun.log. The first instrument run, job 1271950, correctly refused its own assumption that weak 110 carried the nominal 196,480-point shape; job 1271951 retained that discrepancy and completed.
