Sampled-arc boundary route

The lead locked the arc representation to spline-gauss on 2026-09-16 and the route is implemented at commit 1ad4bea9. Integrating by Green's theorem, _sampled_arc_polynomial_moments takes the clip's own sampled arc as the region — every one of its fixed 128 segments a straight edge carrying a per-edge Gauss rule with the nodes on the edge — so the region integrated is the region the clip books. The chord-plus-sagitta machinery is deleted and survives only as the benchmark's reference arm.

The density is a pointwise sample of the profile at 25 Chebyshev tensor points, contracted through a fixed pseudo-inverse onto a degree-≤4 local monomial basis. Because that model's radial antiderivative is degree five in the edge parameter, 3-point Gauss is exact on it and is the lowest order that is: a closed-form order study reports the relative line-integral defect falling from 1.0 at order one and 0.444 at order two to 3.9e-15 at order three, with orders one and two left in as a live negative control. The shape is fixed — arc count, edge capacity and node count are compile-time constants, padding is exact-zero, and no branch depends on data — and the declared live bound is 25 + 149 × 3 = 472 evaluations per cut cell (25 density samples, 149 reserved edges, order 3), under the expected 500 and down from the fan's 245,632 at the weak support's 3,840-vertex capacity.
