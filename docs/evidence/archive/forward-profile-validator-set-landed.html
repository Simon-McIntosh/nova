§5 rung 1 — the analytic vacuum map, to 9.4e-10 of its peak

nova/imas/validator_case.py opens a fetched validator case through imas-python at the per-dataset DD version from tests/data-manifest.json, converting NetCDF to a per-case HDF5 directory with master.h5, never with h5py. The analytic reference layer (analytic_baseline_circle.nc) is now pinned in the manifest.

The analytic case is a vacuum test, a single filamentary coil at 1 A with no plasma, so it carries no plasma current, axis or boundary. Its rung gates on the 2-D flux map. Nova's coil Green function reproduces the reference 65×65 map to a maximum absolute residual of 1.04e-12 Wb, 9.4e-10 of the 1.106e-3 Wb peak, over 4,224 of 4,225 nodes; the excluded node is the coil's own singular position. No fitted scale is applied, and the COCOS transform is the identity: total poloidal flux in COCOS 17, with no stray 2π or sign. Scaling the coil current by 1.5 raises the residual to 0.44 of peak and fails the 1e-3 bound. Gate: tests/test_forward_profile_analytic_smoke.py, 5 passed. Re-review 117/120, after a first review caught a caption that overstated the residual about 900-fold and a panel that broke the plotting rules; both were corrected before landing.
