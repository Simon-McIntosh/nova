§3 — validator layers fetched by digest

fetch_layer in nova/database/layer_fetch.py pulls one layer of ghcr.io/iterorganization/efitpp-test-data by its sha256 digest with oras blob fetch, never by tag, verifies it, and publishes it into the content store. tests/data-manifest.json gains efitpp_validator_layers: the analytic pair and the CORSICA 130506, DINA 105027 and ASTRA 131046 input and reference layers, each with its full digest, size and per-dataset DD version (4.1.0 throughout). The digests were re-derived from the registry manifest sha256:aac593a7…, not transcribed. The independent review confirmed all eight digests and sizes against the live v0.0.14 manifest. The v0.0.14 manifest digest quoted in §1 (7d96e70a…) resolves nowhere and is superseded by this record.
