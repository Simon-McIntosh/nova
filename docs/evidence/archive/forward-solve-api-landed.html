The repair replaces the fixed subdivision with root counting from the cubic the spline restricts to an edge, which has at most two stationary points in the edge parameter and therefore at most three monotone pieces.

Whose cubic it is. Every cell edge of the structured lattice is axis-aligned, so the restriction of the global tensor spline to an edge is exactly a cubic in the edge parameter. The edge derivative is the chain rule of the spline's own partials, delta'(t) = dr/dt · dp/dr + dz/dt · dp/dz, quadratic in t; three derivative evaluations fix its coefficients, its roots are the interior split points, and one root is bracketed on each monotone piece whose ends straddle the level. Three pieces per edge is the fixed shape the traced program keeps: a negative discriminant is clamped rather than masked, which leaves a spurious split point on a monotone edge, and spurious splits cost one evaluation and cannot hide a crossing because splitting a monotone interval anywhere keeps every piece monotone.
