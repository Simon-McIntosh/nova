Cache-only topology replay

PASS — 0 of 42 private-mask cells differ. The replay consumed only the regenerated schema-4 topology operands, including the X-candidate selection step, and invoked the production edge-admissibility, saddle-aware connected-component, and private-flux-mask authorities. All 42 candidate selections match and no replayed domain label differs. The receipt records source arrays of 1,089 cell fluxes, 5×1,089 candidate-domain labels, 1,089 four-vertex cell polygons, and 1,156 atomic-node signed fluxes. The zero is therefore the flood-on-cells answer, independent of the subsequent focused-suite regression.

The cache extension and its receipt remain in the isolated hdg-cache-replay worktree rather than in the integrated bank. This is deliberate: the unchanged focused suite reports 6 failed and 5 passed, including partition counts 250/38 versus 277/20 and 244/36 versus 244/33 plus a wall-notch mismatch. Those failures do not rewrite the zero-difference replay; they block its promotion.

Field accuracy: the fired gate and its replacement

The original test is not retroactively called a pass. It fired at 0.626 on MAST and 0.612 on DIII-D. Lead review found that both fitted EFIT maps were globally smooth, so the test could not exercise the claimed interface mechanism and was re-adjudicated as confounded. The jump-bearing forward solve answers the intended question and passes, but by only 1.15%; this is evidence for admissibility, not a claim of a large accuracy reserve.

Solver settlement and qualified suffixes

Settlement is common enough to support the suffix analysis, but it is not universal. In particular, the MAST rows do not acquire a usable strict qualified suffix merely because their masks stop moving; topology semantics and solver convergence remain separate requirements.

The suffix census is the bridge to the backend margin. Width-1,024 replication is therefore not representative of every production arm: the performance gate passes while the pure MAST arm still lacks a converged GPU receipt.
