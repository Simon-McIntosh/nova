§2 — the plasma prescribed from DINA landed

nova/imas/profiles.py is extended and nova/imas/prescribed_plasma.py added (merged at c39d9aba9 from fa74d76). Each DINA equilibrium slice's plasma current is prescribed inside a closed boundary: the separatrix cut at its X-point, or DINA's own closed boundary outline, with no ψ-contour fallback needed. The current comes from the 1-D flux functions and the 2-D ψ map, scaled to DINA's plasma current. The flux it links at any conductor geometry is computed with nova's own kernels.

On 105014/1 (2 MA) all 867 active slices are prescribed and on 105052/1 (15 MA) all 2,036; the centroids follow DINA's within 4.3 mm and 27.8 mm, under one grid cell. The reference is nova's reconstruction of DINA's plasma flux: DINA's ψ map less the coil and passive contributions nova computes from DINA's currents. The prescribed plasma's linked flux matches it to at most 1.08 Wb and 2.52 Wb within the case bounds the record states. The 105014/1 magnetics loops agree to at most 0.87 Wb. DINA's coupling matrices are not used. A 1 cm vertical shift changes the linked flux by the predicted ∂ψ/∂Z, and a tightened gate fails as it should.

DINA's ψ map covers only 2 of the 14 coil sections, so the other 12 are checked against DINA's current density through nova's kernels, which is a weaker reference. The independent geometric VS3 comparison moves to §3, which has §1's sections. The review scored 18 to 20 after three rounds. Record: iter-prescribed-plasma.

§1 — the ITER conductor set, sectioned and valid to 5 kHz

Run 204's fourteen coils become 295 series winding sections, every source turn total kept, and the 458 passive inputs (450 shaped walls) a surface-graded mesh of 1,312 circuits with a 12 mm outer layer or 2,163 with 6 mm (merged at c20fda519 from 973f030b1). Every coupling is computed by nova; no DINA matrix is used, even as a check. Each sectioned coil's self inductance is within 0.46 % of an independent calculation from the unsectioned IDS elements (PF2 worst, against a 0.5 % limit). The thin-ring and filled-rectangle analytic checks are within 0.45 % and 0.03 %. The two meshes' terminal impedances agree within 0.30 % over 101 points from 0.1 Hz to 5 kHz (VSU worst, at 300 Hz); a tightened 0.1 % criterion is refused, and removing the passive structure returns the bare coil impedance within 1e-16.

Against power_convertor's earlier whole-coil export the raw self terms differ by +0.22 to +0.48 % on CS and PF, −1.10 % on VSU and −0.01 % on VSL, with the largest mutual at 0.09 %. The VSU difference is already present with the unsplit geometry, so it comes from the two whole-coil calculation routes rather than from sectioning. It is reported, not reconciled. Linkage builds took 268 s and 815 s on twelve fleet workers; cached 101-point sweeps take 0.9 s and 3.3 s. The review scored 15 to 19 with no added failures. Both follow-ons were declined. Comparison with the power_convertor export is reported by decision. The second geometry adapter is the independent check. Record: iter-sectioned-conductor-set.

§3 — the vessel currents, evolved through a scenario

The sectioned passive set from §1 is driven by DINA's coil currents and the prescribed plasma from §2, and evolved with nova's exact zero-order-hold eigenmode propagation, on 105014/1 (12 mm and 6 mm) and 105052/1 (12 mm). Merged at 8e3676bcb. The 6 mm interval reproduces the 12 mm result, so the passive current is resolved at 12 mm. Grouping uses geometric containment through CrossSection: 41 % of the sectioned circuits, carrying 58 % of the current, fall inside a DINA loop; the rest lie in structures DINA's pf_passive does not model. 91 of 103 DINA loops agree within max(10 kA, 25 % of the loop's peak) on 105014/1, but only 33 of 103 on 105052/1, worst on the triangular support.

For the coil voltages, R I + dΨ/dt with zero winding resistance: on PF2–PF5 the coupled flux agrees with DINA's coil voltages within 93–235 V rms against peaks of about 3.2 kV, and the passive coupling removes about 40 % of the bare-coil residual. That is consistency between two codes, not a validation of the hardware, because a shared geometry or passive-structure assumption would pass both. The measured basis for the coupling uncertainty is JT-60SA's 5–8 % of M/L. On the CS modules, PF1 and PF6 a 1–2.4 kV rms residual remains. There DINA records the converter-side voltage, pinned at its limit, while the coils discharge through their switching-network resistors, which sit apart from the converters (lead, 15 October 2026). The plasma term is a few volts, because the plasma ramps over tens of seconds. Adding the evolved passive flux does not close §2's VS3 ψ-map residual; it grows. A single passive ring recovers the analytic L/R decay to 1.4e-13, and a 2 % interval mutation in the integrator breaks it. The review scored 15–18, with reuse at 5: a point-flux helper duplicates sensor_grid_couplings, and the driver is untested. Both are folded into §4. Record: iter-vessel-evolution.

§5 — a plasma source that takes reconstructions (first check)

A generic plasma source in nova/imas/plasma_source.py prescribes the plasma current from any IMAS equilibrium and its coil-current series through §2's path. It states each reconstruction's conventions (ψ normalisation, a last closed flux surface rather than a separatrix, a different coil-current cadence) through nova's COCOS handling. Merged at 6c0f4db5d.

  • MAST: a MAST reconstruction prescribed through it matches its own ψ at 71 coils and 46 flux loops below 8 mWb.
  • DINA: 105014/1 run through the generic source reproduces §2's prescribed cell currents identically.
  • Negative control: a reconstruction with its ψ sign flipped is refused.

The review scored 13–19. Three follow-ons are carried to the section's remaining checks: the slice extraction belongs in the library with the script calling it, one assertion compares a constant with itself, and the MAST tests skip silently when the data mirror is absent. The two plant checks wait for §4's call. No JT-60SA data is held in nova; the JT-60SA application lives in power_convertor. Record: reconstructed-plasma-source.

§4 — the coupled plant through one narrow imported call

nova.circuit.coupled_plant.coupled_plant(machine, source, …) returns a CoupledPlant carrying the section-level coil inductance and resistance, the reduced passive modes with their decay times and couplings to every section, the plasma flux drive at every section and mode against time, provenance, and the reduction error against frequency. Merged at c4c7abd78; power_convertor imports it at Python 3.14. A cold build and a cached load return the same plant, and perturbing one cached array fails that comparison. On the real ITER sectioned set the reduced plant's terminal impedance matches the full model's within 1 % for all 14 coils from 0.1 Hz to 5 kHz, on both meshes. §3's point-flux helper now goes through sensor_grid_couplings, and its driver has a test. The review scored 13–19, with reuse at 4: the cache uses a private serialiser pair, contrary to the record's claim that it extends passive.py's, and the screened terminal impedance and mode selection duplicate owners in passive.py. These are internal and leave the public surface unchanged; they are being repaired in a follow-on node. Record: iter-coupled-plant-call.
