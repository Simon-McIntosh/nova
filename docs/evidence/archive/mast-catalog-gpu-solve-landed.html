Entry-gate state — the ladder stays shut

No throughput rung has been dispatched, and deliberately so. The plan is held at its entry gate under an owner directive that forward-solve stability and the multi-basin question be settled first, on the reasoning that a fast catalog of incorrect equilibria has no value. Recorded here so the gate's state is legible beside the census rather than only inside the plan's comment history.

Discharged: the multi-basin question. Closed by dual-basin-solve through attribution rather than by lifting the stalls. Branch selection is closed by construction — amplitude elimination leaves one admissible plasma branch at max closure error 2.495e-16 across all eleven frozen references. A DIII-D pinning control reached 1.59e-09 diverted in four accepted iterations where its paired unpinned control ended at 0.514 limited after twenty-four, from the same seed and the same 24-conductor input. Basin reachability was then attributed rather than solved: 5/5 stall floors scale with mesh (ratios 0.096–0.512, observed orders 0.97–3.39), so the stalls are discretisation-limited and no basin-entry driver can close them.

Not discharged: three live forward-solve defects. None is a basin problem and none is resolution alone.

  • The exact autodiff residual-Jacobian action was non-finite on the first Arnoldi vector, returning a finite zero step. FIXED and root-caused (commit 06b09f5b): division by a zero singular-value gap in the JAX SVD JVP, reached through jnp.linalg.lstsq in the Null2D quadratic null fit, because symmetric fixed-grid stencils carry repeated singular values. Also screened (6143221d) so a future latent non-differentiable branch surfaces as a named refusal rather than a plausible answer.
  • Topology-preserving trial admission was absent. LANDED (257af9dc): on the banked case all 89 promoted iterations hold diverted with a finite terminal X point, where the unqualified route crossed topology at 31, 50 and 88 and ended limited. But on the five score-blind frames it converts a confidently wrong answer into a stalled correct one rather than into convergence: admission is 4 to 9 of 89 trials because topology caps the step near one thirty-second of the Newton step, so an 89-iteration budget traverses only about 2.8 Newton-step-equivalents. Extending the factor ladder to reach that step was measured and is NULL. The frontier needs a predictor-corrector advancing along the topology-preserving manifold, not finer backtracking into it.
  • The mesh-independent closure gain (0.6098 / 0.6392 / 0.6378 points across realised 566 / 1587 / 2214 cells against a 0.15 ceiling) is identified as a PHYSICAL near-marginal mode: the vertical free-boundary axis-displacement channel at eigenvalue 0.987429, Ritz residual 6.4e-16, 85 percent passive-drive overlap with axis motion, with constraint, normalisation and mesh-artifact classes measured out. Not a defect. The elongated case carries a distinct radial-dominant channel at 1.3372, so each configuration owns its own near-marginal mode and no scalar screen or ceiling can serve both.

Two consequences already recorded against the plan's own architecture. The batched Picard loop §2 specifies is not available: the map is non-contractive with spectral radius 1.1456 after current pinning and 1.2577 without, so the loop is Newton-only and the iteration distribution the first rung profiles is a Newton-step distribution. And the default residual action was re-ruled twice: a finite-difference action first, then amended to the repaired exact autodiff tangent once the repair landed and the differenced arm proved blocked by lax.custom_linear_solve, which demands an exactly traced transposable map. The per-slice budget is therefore read on the exact arm, where one linearisation is reused across all Krylov vectors, and the differenced arm stands deferred unbuilt as robustness insurance not taken.

  • Not a semantic difference. Every case's one-map separation is 2.220446049250313e-16 absolute, 1.8900776655575514e-16 relative — pure float64 rounding, no operation exceeding that band before iteration.

Independent corroboration from the cluster structure: of the 38 passing leaves, 35 are exact categorical, policy and structural invariants and are bit-identical; all 31 failures are continuous iterated quantities (14 state/domain/topology/solver-trace, 9 integrated-moment/current-ledger, 8 conservation/source-normalisation). Nothing categorical moves. Iteration is the only difference between the machine-precision seed and the diverging solve.

One measurement kept because it breaks the tidy story. This is not geometric growth at the spectral radius: ρ = 1.1456 over twelve updates predicts ~5.1×, the measurement is 5.4e9 — nine orders larger. Per-update ratios are irregular (≈2.8, 87, 7.2, 6.2, 2.5, 5.4, 41, 0.81, 2.9, 2.6, 144) with three bursts and one contracting step. No single dominant eigenvalue produces that; the map is strongly non-normal and its transient growth dominates its asymptote, so a terminal eigenpair is necessary but insufficient to explain the trajectory that reached it.

What this retires, and what it does not. §1's claim that the jitted stack pins batched==per-slice at 1e-10 is struck for the solve half: on a non-contractive map that is unmeetable by any implementation, not merely unmet. The batched==per-slice requirement stands unchanged — a 1,341,435-slice catalog must not disagree with a per-slice one on any physical observable. Retired is the belief that agreement on the raw converged state at a fixed absolute depth expresses it. Successors: a one-map bound (machine precision demonstrably attainable) plus separately registered physically meaningful terminal-observable bounds, consuming the criterion derivation in flight on forward-operator-refinement rather than authoring a fourth untraced number.
