Reading

  • A single real member solves in seconds, not milliseconds. The recorded solve time is the whole trip loop: per-trip host reconciliation, any retrace below the top-level compiled boundary, and the final device synchronisation. The banked 0.784 ms per member and 1.163 ms per slice are batched-throughput figures over a replicated operator and are not comparable to this width-1 latency.
  • The strict settled exit is a modest saving. Between 1.4 % and 20.4 % per MAST member, 2.1 % and 10.7 % on the two DIII-D frames; the members that converge quickly (21985/51, 22086/43) gain the most, the stagnating members the least.
  • Heterogeneous frames recompile. Each DIII-D frame compiled for about 1470 to 1510 s with resident memory reaching 67 to 68 GiB; MAST pure arms compiled for 645 to 681 s against 133 to 144 s for the mixed arms of the same members. This is the member-data-as- trace-constant cost the batched-operator-boundary plan exists to remove.
  • Not persisted. Executed and no-op trip counts, terminal residuals, state hashes, bit-identity where the exit fired, and the per-trip quantum at HEAD are recorded as null: the driver's atomic receipt write came after the scheduler timeout and the flushed stage log did not carry them.
