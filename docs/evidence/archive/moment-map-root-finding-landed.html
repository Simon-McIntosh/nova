§2 — the fine census and the two-element ablation: a path-consistency defect

Two measurement windows on the fine fixture (1069 plasma cells, 330 wall targets), all states banked. The fine census cleared every discrete class: along 20 densely sampled segments the smoothed cycle crosses only smoothing-band surfaces — zero atomic-edge flips, zero clip hand-offs, zero ring changes, zero topology switches, and limited↔diverted excluded (count 0) on both arms. The unsmoothed arm meets its dense 118-event clip/ring population only at fraction 0.6. Tangent amplification is excluded too: the smoothed tangent is 0.939× the unsmoothed at matched states. Two elements carried the crossings: node-band element 1642 and full-cell hand-off cell 933.

The ablation convicts one of them, and the clamp proves causality. Node 1642 (R=6.583, Z=−2.490 m) contributes 1.79e-6 Wb transiently, zero at the terminal pair; clamping it slightly worsens the residual — exonerated. Cell 933 (R=8.037, Z=0.567 m) sits at inside fraction 0.99972 with occupancy 1 and hand-off weight 0.989 — so band placement, width units and weight normalisation are all correct. The defect is path consistency: at 0.028% missing area the clipped path differs from the full path by 23.19% in cell current (−16174.0 A full vs −3751.5 A difference), coupling to 9.29e-4 Wb — 28.8% of the total map displacement. Clamping cell 933 to its unsmoothed path improves the fine 0.45 residual 20.24× to 4.228e-7 — below the unchanged 1e-6 gate — with diverted topology preserved. A methods correction rides with it: the census's "displacement" figures were differences of separate sup norms; the true map-difference norms are 3.224e-3/3.340e-3 Wb (4.7–6.0× larger), and the ablation supersedes them.

So the smoothing is not intrinsically costly at these widths — the two evaluation paths simply do not agree in the limit where they must. The repair is to make them identical at the near-full hand-off (or reformulate the blend about their exact common limit), pin that equality, and rerun.
