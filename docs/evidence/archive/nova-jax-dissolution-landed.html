Accuracy and policy findings

  • Global fp64 was a diagnostic workaround, not the final select policy: direct fp32 imports misclassified 12 of 18 ITER-scale analytic surfaces, while exact index-native normalized fp32 produced zero wrong classes and at most 0.01143-cell error in the clean resolved matrix.
  • The select behaviour change landed before arithmetic collapse: four-value wall results, all-NaN at zero polarity, NaN plane classification, a sign-preserving nonzero determinant sentinel, and the three-array subnull signature.
  • Stencil relocation must reject flat plateaux, preserve unresolved weak evidence, deduplicate same-root cells, and surface exact count/overflow metadata rather than carry the measured defects into the domain package.
  • JAX-free eager boundaries remain required while namespaces consolidate.
