Mechanisms traced

  • Limiter capture. The class is read by the connectivity classifier, whose wall half admits any wall node whose nearest cell on a 96 by 18 raster lies in the axis-connected pre-saddle flood, with no line-of-sight test; the shadow flag it computes is discarded. All three rows bind governed-wall node 10 at (0.5649, 1.7281) m, the back wall of a re-entrant notch EFIT's LCFS never touches. The rule to apply is reachability plus line of sight through the flooded region.
  • Axis admission. When no O candidate qualifies, o_point_index returns index zero and the second admission pass seeds material at that arbitrary cell; the candidate table keeps five extrema and truncates silently; nothing penalises a qualified extremum far from the plasma. The exact side of the certificate runs the same reader and falls back to closed-form values under a different status when it fails, so its status alternates with the hex layout of each resolution.
  • Disjoint axis. O-point selection is a pure flux argmax over candidates whose qualification is self-referential: each candidate's own cell is forced into the material mask it is tested against and the flood is seeded there, so any extremum inside the vessel qualifies; the second admission pass seeds at the first pass's winner and confirms it. On 21985/51 a coil-adjacent well an order of magnitude deeper than the boundary flux wins on both arms, and the Newton residual converges around it because nothing in the residual or the class margin validates the axis. Rule: connectivity to the plasma's confined component tested against the original mask with a size floor, ranking only among connected candidates, and a second pass that can rescue but never change the winner.
  • Boundary flux chain. Twenty hops from candidates to the residual mask; the 38 to 29 and 31 to 29 trip-1 mask moves at 260a8b44 are the wall-height shadow's X-candidate source switching from the raster read to the census read.
