A gauge-carrying flux sign is not a plasma-current polarity (2026-09-07 07:30Z, coordinator review)

The sign-aware axis read for the reversed-current block was reviewed before merge, after its first two relabel jobs exited zero with zero converged and zero qualified slices on all three shots. The change has two halves that contradict each other. In nova/equilibrium/stencil_nulls.py the axis selection is correct: it widens the candidate slots from one to eight and picks the extremum whose ntype matches the polarity, positive current selecting a maximum and negative a minimum. That is the right instrument, because ntype comes from the sign of the Hessian trace at the stationary point and so does not move with the flux gauge.

In nova/equilibrium/topology.py the same change infers a polarity from the sign of the flux value at the largest-magnitude O-point and then substitutes that inferred value for the caller's polarity through the whole read. The equilibrium guidance states that the Biot-Savart composition sets nova's poloidal-flux gauge and that raw flux amplitudes may be compared only within a declared gauge, so the axis flux carries the external-coil contribution and its sign is not a function of the plasma-current direction. The inference therefore inverts the read on any shot whose gauge places the axis flux on the other side of zero, positive-current shots included, which is both the observed all-zero result and a latent regression on the shots that work today. The correction is to drop the inference and pass the caller's polarity through, using ntype wherever a gauge-free orientation is needed, exactly as the same commit already does one module away.

Two smaller things travel with it. The inline reconstruction of the census rows from candidate_census drops the extremum-type filter that read_census applies; the filter is inert in production because its polarity defaults to unset, but a test still pins it, so the contract is split between two implementations. And the inferred polarity was threaded into every selection call while the component flood kept the original, so the flood direction and the candidate selection could disagree; both resolve to the caller's polarity once the inference goes. The relabel job was cancelled rather than left to measure the defective path, and the node continues.

The reading worth carrying past this node: when a physical sign is needed, take it from a curvature or an index, never from an amplitude that carries a gauge. The failure signature to recognise is a selection that returns nothing at all rather than something wrong, on every input, while each line of the change reads plausibly on its own.
