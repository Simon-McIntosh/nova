Measures. A synthetic three-frame session round-trips bitwise on every variable (NaN-equal on floats); every field of two or more dimensions carries time last; frames reconstruct from the store equal to the originals; masked components stay NaN and False; the policy digest is deterministic and 64 hex characters.
