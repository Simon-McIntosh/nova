Candidate mechanisms for the run-to-run movement, after the map
MechanismStanding after the map
Atomics-based scatter or segment-sum reduction whose order depends on scheduling Unfit on the observation seam. StencilMesh._scatter places one derivative per unique centre with .at[centre].set, construction rejects repeated centre indices, and a test guards that property. No segment sum or scatter-add exists on the equilibrium path. This does not exclude a nondeterministic library kernel elsewhere.
Reduction association inside the canary Uncontrolled rather than mis-controlled. conservation.divergence_b derives the poloidal field and its divergence and then applies a masked jnp.max; it does not use layout_invariant_sum at all. That is why repairing the moment reductions made moments.volume and moments.major_radius bitwise equal without touching the movement.
Reduction-order freedom XLA may resolve differently between compilations Strongest surviving candidate, and it is about executable identity rather than arithmetic. No acceptance driver calls the persistent compilation cache, so the two banked repetitions may have run separately compiled executables inside one allocation. Nothing on the acceptance route pins deterministic GPU kernels, cuBLAS workspace determinism, an algorithm id or an autotune level, and configure_dtypes holds float64 policy without pinning association.
Whole-route reduction fixing Not claimable. Only the selected moment reductions have an explicit association; the fixed-point ladder and flux operator still carry backend reductions, dense products, Gram solves and norms. The complete route must not be described as reduction-fixed.

The recommended measurement shape was adopted rather than reinvented: extend the existing acceptance driver rather than author a second acceptance route — one frozen profile and seed per case, the solve callable created once per case and width, three invocations in one process on byte-identical copied inputs, every result blocked and every raw flux retained losslessly with repetition and case axes, then bitwise unequal-element count with maximum absolute and relative difference against repetition 1 for both the state and all 69 leaves, naming the first differing leaf. Cache-off and fixed-cache are named control arms, never evidence of a deterministic production route on their own.
