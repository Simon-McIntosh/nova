The gap is narrow and decisive. ForwardEquilibrium.flux publishes the complete terminal flux array on every solve; _case_measurement in benchmarks/observable_batch_acceptance.py already holds both routes' flux transiently at exactly the right case boundary; _named_tree and _leaves already name every one of the 69 registered leaves including the canary. But raw terminal arrays retained across repetitions is zero — a scan of the acceptance, discriminator, conditioned-convergence and parity receipts found no scalar path holding a flux value and no JSON carrying both a repetition collection and a flux array, because _repetition_snapshot deliberately discards terminal flux and raw observable values. The state verdict therefore cannot be recovered from banked evidence; it has to be run.

Candidates narrowed before §3 opened

Three of the mechanisms §3 lists as testable are now differently placed, and one is stronger than the section assumed.

StencilMesh._scatter places one derivative per unique centre with .at[centre].set, construction rejects repeated centre indices, and a test guards that property. Whole-route reduction fixing Not claimable. Only the selected moment reductions have an explicit association; the fixed-point ladder and flux operator still carry backend reductions, dense products, Gram solves and norms. The complete route must not be described as reduction-fixed.

The recommended measurement shape was adopted rather than reinvented: extend the existing acceptance driver rather than author a second acceptance route — one frozen profile and seed per case, the solve callable created once per case and width, three invocations in one process on byte-identical copied inputs, every result blocked and every raw flux retained losslessly with repetition and case axes, then bitwise unequal-element count with maximum absolute and relative difference against repetition 1 for both the state and all 69 leaves, naming the first differing leaf. Cache-off and fixed-cache are named control arms, never evidence of a deterministic production route on their own.

Both anticipated outcomes are eliminated. The section was written to distinguish a varying state from a repeating state with moving labels. Neither happened: nothing moves at all. Each case/width callable was created once, compiled on first invocation, and repetitions 2 and 3 reused that executable on fresh host copies whose shape, dtype and byte digest matched repetition 1, every result blocked before retention. So the banked movement, measured across separate driver invocations, belongs to a process or executable boundary — independently the candidate the reuse map ranked strongest, on the ground that no acceptance driver calls the persistent compilation cache.

One corroborating detail: these counts — 411 and 412 case evaluations at 67 bounds — sit between the two banked repetitions (410/412 at 67 and 68 bounds, 408/410 at 67 and 68) rather than matching either. A third independent compilation looks like that.
