Actionable conclusion

The DIII-D convergence gate does not pass. The MAST geometry gates, H200 throughput ladder, and strong visual containment criterion are positive results, but none changes the five-frame convergence verdict. The evidence does not establish a residual floor: it establishes only that the tested bounded candidate families halt above tolerance for reasons other than a local minimum, gradient collapse, or step-length floor.

The single named remaining direction is an own-mask-aware adaptive descent model that can search beyond the bounded Newton and Levenberg candidate families. It is high cost because each candidate must be promoted, have its mask rebuilt, and be rescored on that own mask throughout a long DIII-D chain. No tolerance relaxation is supported by this record.
