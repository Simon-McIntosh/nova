The contour quadrature was the first-order stage, and the radial moment is still flat (2026-09-19)

Node sos-grid-imaged-biot-field-converges-to-the-volume-definition; the grid-route order question of shafranov-observed-side §2. Commits 3818bd05 (the quadrature) and f633a00a (the test and the receipt).

Result — the first-order stage. Of the three candidate stages, the contour quadrature in nova/equilibrium/diagnostics.py shafranov_contour_integrals was first order. It evaluated the integrand at each chord's leading vertex while weighting it by that chord's length and outward normal. The length and direction belong to the segment; the leading vertex sits half a chord from the midpoint they describe, so the rule was first order in the contour sampling however fine the contour became. _contour_metric now returns the segment midpoint and the integrals average each field to it through the new _segment_mean, which is the trapezoidal rule on a closed polyline and second order. The mechanism is stated in both docstrings.

The reported order 1.10 was an instrument artifact. The test's ladder advanced the grid and the contour sampling in lockstep (21/501, 31/1001, 41/2001), so the order fitted against "grid resolution" was the contour quadrature's order wearing the grid's name, and the 0.5 floor could not separate a first-order route from a second-order one.

Measure — after the fix. The test now holds the grid at 161 and refines only the contour sampling against the 8001 rung: fitted order 2.064, departures 9.4388×10-4, 2.2850×10-4, 5.4124×10-5. A second loop holds the sampling at 2001 and refines the grid 21/41/81 against the analytic-field route at the same sampling, so the grid's own contribution is isolated from the volume reference: 2.870×10-6, 2.015×10-6, 6.890×10-7. The assertion moved from 0.5 to 1.8, which a first-order rule cannot reach. The order, its ladder and its mechanism are printed and persisted to docs/figures/constraint-augmented-newton-krylov/shafranov-integral/receipt.json.

The named defect is radial_moment, and the two ladders localise it to the contour side. Each term's residual is flat in exactly one ladder and responds to the other, which is what names the side without assuming it. The radial residual is flat in volume resolution and falls only when the contour sampling is refined — so the error is on the contour side of the radial identity, and moving the sample to the segment midpoint did not remove it; a first-order term survives in that surface integral. The vertical and toroidal residuals behave oppositely: flat in sampling, first order in resolution (0.824 and 0.925), the signature of the volume side's cell-centre plasma mask, which carries no boundary correction.

Lesson. The three-term residual vector was being read as one number. Fractionated per term and per ladder, the flat coordinate stops being a mystery and becomes a location: a residual that does not move with the refinement you applied is on the other side. The failing bound was hiding a real defect, not a tolerance to relax — the bound is left untouched and the three rungs of test_shafranov_contour_integrals_equal_their_volume_side stand red until the radial surface term is repaired.
