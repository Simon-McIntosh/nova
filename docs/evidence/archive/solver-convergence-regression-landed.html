Root cause — a deliberate acceptance policy, commit a2e65fda

Receipt: root-cause.json, root-cause.png, head-mechanism.json; commit 1926a170.

The candidate window is the ten nova/equilibrium commits between d47f7cd1 and a4bec44f. Per-trip trajectory capture with each commit's behaviour re-enacted locates the first divergence at trip 2 of 22086/43 pure: the own-induced-mask acceptance (_backtracked_promotion, sufficient &= ~own_mask_acceptance | (residuals < incumbent_residual)) refuses the Newton promotion the banked code accepted, because candidate and incumbent are each scored on the mask they induce and the candidate's residual (3.13e-3) does not strictly beat the incumbent's (3.49e-3) under that scoring. The banked path then re-opened the mask at trips 4 and 5 ([1, 22] cells), rose from 2.23e-3 to 2.83e-3, and converged to 6e-12 in twelve trips; the HEAD path keeps the mask settled from trip 3 and stagnates honestly at 4.80e-4 after seven trips. Classification in the receipt: deliberate policy consequence, not a solver defect. The later commits in the window (model-trust selection, continuation across settled masks, best-state retention, the stagnation stop, telemetry) are each read and excluded as the cause.

Mechanism

With the partition frozen the solve should contract quadratically. It does not because the iteration-local projected Krylov condition discriminator in nova/equilibrium/fixed_point.py judges the linear model of these physical branches ill-conditioned (ratio limit e) and scales the proposed step to between 0.05 and 3 percent of the raw Newton direction before the nonlinear merit ladder sees it; the line search then accepts the crippled step at factor 1.0 on all eight promotions, so each update looks accepted while the residual barely moves. The Jacobian is right, the linear solve is exact, and the direction is never rejected by the merit test. The alternatives were each excluded by a direct measurement: a missing derivative through the topology read (finite difference agrees with the JVP to 1e-9 to 5e-7), a non-smooth residual from a local-support read (the candidate's unified support leaves the fractions and ratios unchanged), an inexact-Newton cap (linear residual 1e-12 to 1e-15 with the proposed steps far inside their bounds), and saddle mis-selection (the frozen mask reselects nothing).

Confidence

High that this is the mechanism of the plateau: it is measured directly (the shrink fraction is read from the solver's own receipt), replicated on four rows and two code revisions, and the competing explanations were tested rather than argued. The frozen-mask regime matches production, where the bank telemetry shows the mask settling after one to four trips while the residual plateaus. Two things are not yet shown and the repair node measures them: that admitting the raw direction undamped restores superlinear contraction on these branches (the discriminator may be firing on a genuinely stiff linear model whose full step the line search must then cut), and how many of the twelve bank rows converge once it does. The old converged results on 21985/51 were roots of a false topology (anchor above), so no earlier bank state is a control for this.

Open record

The regression is located to one commit and classified as a policy consequence; five remedies that leave the policy in place are negative; the bank is honestly re-baselined. The corrected Solov'ev certificate (gs-absolute-accuracy-gates) shows the same settled-above-tolerance signature on fourteen of sixteen exact-solution rows, so the remaining remedy has an exact test bed. The one untested remedy is the policy itself: score pre-settlement candidates on the incumbent partition (a scoped re-opening of a2e65fda), which the lead locked on 2026-09-03 (decision presettlement-acceptance-scoring, prototype in flight); see the handoff at convergence-stall-handoff.
