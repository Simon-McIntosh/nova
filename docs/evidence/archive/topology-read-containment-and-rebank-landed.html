Change. Each structured pass now evaluates containment from the state that pass holds. The pass screens the strongest fitted contact reached by the axis-enclosing component at that contact's own level against the pass' X-point level, and requires containment only when that contact outranks the saddle and would therefore bind the boundary — which is exactly what that pass' own boundary selection publishes. The traced program stays fixed-shape: the screen enters as a jnp.where operand and the X-point flux is an optional argument, so the contact-only caller is unchanged.

The negative control shows what the guard costs and why the rank comparison is the criterion. Requiring containment whenever the reachable set is non-empty (jnp.any(eligible)) engages the screen on diverted reads too, because low-level contours of any plasma reach the wall; that form failed two tests in the gate — test_wall_refinement_preserves_spline_authored_contact[diverted-single-null] (contact moved 0.024815630484964643 m against a 0.02473095024234173 m median panel) and the diverted position contract (0.1594393405540552 m). The rank comparison keeps a diverted read on its raw wall extremum and is what the four-file gate passed with.
