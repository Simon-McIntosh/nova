DIII-D EFIT match

The production current-constrained Nova forward solve is compared frame by frame with the EFIT label on five DIII-D efit_psirz frames. The two error sources are kept in separate columns and read independently: a gauge-aligned solve-label RMS over the labelled core, and the banked EFIT label's own Grad-Shafranov inconsistency floor. All five frames fail — none converges, and four of the five solve-label RMS fractions sit far above the 0.083 floor. The whole-plasma vertical displacement visible in the figures is the dominant signature.

The two error sources read independently

The two columns are independent measurements with different norms and units, and the DIII-D solve-vs-label column is not on the same scale as the MAST document's column: it is a fraction of the centred EFIT label RMS over the labelled LCFS interior, where the MAST column is a fraction of the EFIT label peak-to-peak span over the whole registered grid. The two must never be read on one scale or averaged.

  • solve against label — gauge-aligned solve-label RMS over label-core variation, the fractional RMS on the score-blind labelled LCFS interior (the registered stride-two solve grid) after additive gauge alignment, relative to the centred EFIT label RMS;
  • label GS inconsistency — EFIT label under unchanged Nova interior operator, the relative interior Grad-Shafranov sup of the EFIT label at stride one, expressed as a fraction of the interior drive sup. This is the independent banked floor, 0.0832365867.

The per-frame pass rule is solver converged and solve_against_label.value <= label_gs_inconsistency.value. A frame fails if either arm fails.

Frame-by-frame

Verdict

0 of 5 DIII-D frames pass. None converges, four of five sit above the label floor, and frame 44 ends non-finite. The figures show a whole-plasma vertical displacement at roughly 0.3 of span; the large additive gauge values and the reported RMS fractions exceeding the figures' plotted range mark the magnitude normalisation of this column as an open reconciliation item rather than certified accuracy. The receipt's aggregate verdict stands: FAIL_ONE_OR_MORE_FRAMES_ABOVE_LABEL_GS_FLOOR_OR_UNCONVERGED.
