How to read every panel

Blue contours are the analytic Solovev field and ochre contours are the terminal forward field, drawn at the same absolute flux levels. The red loop is the analytic separatrix and the dark vessel outline is the typed wall geometry. A solid triangle marks the magnetic axis and a filled cross an admitted saddle; containment is passed to the null painter, so a finite point outside the vessel is not allowed to impersonate an in-vessel X-point. The marker vocabulary matches the poloidal convergence atlas: solid triangle = magnetic axis; filled cross = admitted saddle; hollow circle = another qualified X-point inside the wall; hollow square = qualified X-point outside the wall.

A wrong answer is visible without a residual calculation: a red boundary that does not follow the terminal separatrix, a marker displaced from the corresponding reference critical region, a finite X marker against or beyond the vessel, or contour families with kinks, extra islands, or an implausible compression are each a topology or field-shape failure. A small scalar residual alone cannot exclude any of them.
