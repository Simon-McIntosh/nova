The four support_read contradictions, resolved

The line map reports read 0/96, read_batch 0/208, read_and_book 0/27 and bank_program 0/11 body lines on both routes, while the device trace attributes kernels to each. The resolution is in the call sites, not in the trace: the routes read topology through Topology.read_qualification, the flux-map null census, and reach support_read only on the bank-solve arm, whose slice-profile bank fixture is absent from shared storage at this revision. The device trace therefore measures a compiled program whose Python body the routes never enter, which is exactly the shape the line map reports. The two implementations compute the same quantity and agree to the tolerances above, so the contradiction is not a correctness defect but a duplicate that only one arm currently exercises.

How further candidates were found

The scan reuses scripts/iron_operator_bound/reuse_check.py's definitions() and callers() over the 210 modules that executed at least one line on either route; no third scanner was authored. It reports 314 function names defined in more than one route module, mostly dunders, overrides and per-geometry kernels that are genuinely distinct per shape. Grouping by an AST digest of the body (name, decorators and docstring stripped) finds one exact match across modules, the choose reducer already covered by the extreme-selection entry, and two exact matches under the ten-line threshold: choose again and update_columns in the two plotting indexers, which is the plot-columns entry. A near-identity pass at 0.85 on the shared names finds only those two pairs.

Two further candidates are registered but not measured, and neither is a route-level duplicate. nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py defines against_arctan twice, a local closure in terms and again in flux_and_moment_terms, with an identical body; it is one module's internal repetition and cannot be reached as two route implementations. The remaining shared names are Biot-Savart kernels that differ per geometry (arc, bow, polybow, beam, line), which is the correct shape for a per-shape kernel rather than a duplicate.

Negative controls

Three entries rerun with a perturbed input report a disagreement beyond their stated tolerance, which is the check that the comparison can fail: the topology read under a uniform exterior gradient of 0.1x the nodal derivative (per-cell membership fraction 0.878 against 0.05); the extreme selection with one element raised 7.0 past the maximum (index gap 1 against 0); and the route check with the forward-route expectation applied to the module that reads supplied points (passes: false, as it must). The storage, level-polish and plot-columns entries are exact-equality comparisons whose disagreement is a difference in the returned strings, points or call counts.
