The four support_read contradictions, resolved

The line map reports read 0/96, read_batch 0/208, read_and_book 0/27 and bank_program 0/11 body lines on both routes, while the device trace attributes kernels to each. The resolution is in the call sites, not in the trace: the routes read topology through Topology.read_qualification, the flux-map null census, and reach support_read only on the bank-solve arm, whose slice-profile bank fixture is absent from shared storage at this revision. The device trace therefore measures a compiled program whose Python body the routes never enter, which is exactly the shape the line map reports. The two implementations compute the same quantity and agree to the tolerances above, so the contradiction is not a correctness defect but a duplicate that only one arm currently exercises.

How further candidates were found

The scan reads the 210 route modules the line map records as executing at least one line, using scripts/iron_operator_bound/reuse_check.py's definitions() and callers(); no third scanner was authored. It reports 314 function names defined in more than one route module, mostly dunders, overrides and per-geometry kernels that are genuinely distinct per shape. Grouping by an AST digest of the body (docstring stripped) finds 45 exact-body groups spanning two or more modules. Restricting to one name defined in two modules with at least two statements leaves seven: subgroup, choose and update_columns, which are the storage-group-mode, extreme-selection and plot-columns entries, and tree_unflatten, _tensor_bernstein, ids and canonical_bytes, which are registered here and not measured. tree_unflatten, ids and canonical_bytes are two-line protocol adapters over a registry key; _tensor_bernstein (three lines) is a tensor-product Bernstein basis repeated between nova/equilibrium/flux_surface_extraction.py and nova/linalg/tensor_spline.py, a shared-kernel candidate for a later node rather than a route duplicate measured here. A near-identity pass at 0.85 over the shared names finds 79 pairs, 42 of them exact; besides the seven above it recovers get_mode and the two _argm*_exact wrappers of the existing entries, and flags Aphi (cylinder against polysection), _first_residual (polygonanalytic against polygonarc) and for_member (ensemble against window_batch) as unmeasured near-duplicates.

Two further candidates are registered but not measured, and neither is a route-level duplicate. nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py defines against_arctan twice, a local closure in terms and again in flux_and_moment_terms, with an identical body; it is one module's internal repetition and cannot be reached as two route implementations. The remaining shared names are Biot-Savart kernels that differ per geometry (arc, bow, polybow, beam, line), which is the correct shape for a per-shape kernel rather than a duplicate.
