End-to-end brute-force control for the contour tree

Measure. An independent positive control for nova.equilibrium.contour_tree.build_contour_tree on stored fixtures: for each fixture the superlevel components of σψ on the clipped hex-centre triangulation are counted by a plain host graph search (breadth-first over the carrier's own vertices and edges) and compared with the component count the computed tree implies at each sampled level. The brute-force side shares no union-find, no merge sweep and no tree arc with the receipt; it shares only the mesh and the field values. The comparator lives in benchmarks/contour_tree_brute_force.py and its gate is tests/test_contour_tree_brute_force.py.

Fixtures

  • Solov’ev certificate rows. Every compact persisted certificate terminal field under docs/figures/gs-absolute-accuracy/solovev/production-route-parts/ (*production-route-reduced.json), read through certificate_fixtures(). These are forward states.
  • MAST 27079 row 16. The limited shot named in the plan’s primary-selection section. The carrier is the hex plasma mesh built from the MAST machine description (nova.media.sources.plasma_mesh.hex_mesh over the stored limiter polyline, cells=400, 464 delivered hexagons) and clipped through nova.equilibrium.contour_tree_mesh.build_contour_mesh. The field is the stored EFIT reconstruction (efm/psirz, converted to total webers at the read by nova.media.sources.mast_efit.read_frame) sampled at the hex-cell centres with a bicubic spline (scipy.RectBivariateSpline, kx=ky=3). It is a reconstruction sampled onto the carrier, not a forward state. σ = +1 because the reconstructed axis flux (+3.694e-1 Wb) exceeds the boundary flux.
  • MAST 22475 row 50. A reversed-current row from the 22475–22626 block. Row 50 is the mid-pulse slice carrying the largest reversed current in that store (Ip = -7.342e5 A), so the plasma is well formed rather than a startup or runaway tail; its reconstruction is diverted with two X-points. σ = -1 because the axis flux is negative, so the axis is again a maximum of σσψ. Same carrier and spline route as 27079 row 16.

Both MAST carriers carry 644 valid vertices (464 hex centres plus 182 wall vertices) and 1737 edges; neither overflows the mesh capacities.

Result

The independent superlevel component count is well formed on both MAST carriers, and the figure below plots it against the signed flux level. The count is 1 at the highest sampled level (the single global maximum) and 1 at the deepest (the whole connected chamber), rising to a maximum of 6 components on 27079 row 16 and 9 on 22475 row 50 — the intermediate critical events of each equilibrium. Every count is a positive integer and never exceeds the number of live vertices at its level.

What is blocked, and why

The tree-implied counts do not agree with the brute force on any fixture yet, and that is not this node’s defect. The tree receipt refuses every carrier at its declared fixed capacity, and even a synthetic 9-vertex wall-bearing carrier returns a malformed receipt (9 nodes and 17 edges, so the node count minus the edge count is −8, not 1). The end-to-end comparison is therefore only half landed: the independent brute-force side is complete and committed, the tree side waits on ctta-contour-tree-edge-invariant-repair, which root-causes and repairs the receipt on a base carrying this node’s commit. This node does not edit nova/equilibrium/contour_tree.py.
