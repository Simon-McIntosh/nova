Explanatory figure regenerated from the computed tree

benchmarks/contour_tree_explanatory_figure.py renders the plan’s explanatory figure from the tree nova.equilibrium.contour_tree.build_contour_tree actually computes for the persisted diverted single-null Solov’ev certificate state at its 340-cell rung, rather than from a hand-placed tree. Every node and edge is the receipt’s own; the figure cannot drift from it, because a guard rebuilds the tree independently and asserts the drawn set matches in count and carrier vertices (tests/test_contour_tree_explanatory_figure.py). Outputs land under docs/figures/contour-tree-topology-authority/explanatory/: the two-panel image (poloidal panel and the tree as a signed-flux graph), its SVG, a JSON receipt carrying the caption and every node row, and the render log.

The measure. The certificate is fixture diverted-single-null-340-cells from certificate_rung_fixtures(), with σ = +1 (the raw axis flux exceeds the boundary flux). The computed tree carries 13 nodes and 12 edges, so the node count exceeds the edge count by one. Two of the nodes are the analytic nulls — the magnetic-axis maximum and the admitted X-point saddle (the saddle the tree attaches directly to the axis). The other 11 nodes lie beyond them: 6 piecewise-linear extrema (1 maximum and 5 minima) and 5 further saddles. One of the minima is the positionless virtual outside slot the padded carrier emits; the private-region wall maximum is the tree’s own node at the highest-flux wall vertex. These are low-persistence piecewise-linear extrema of the discretised field, drawn rather than hidden, and the critical-point geometry and selection sections (§3 and §4) must absorb them by persistence and primary selection.

Gate. tests/test_contour_tree_explanatory_figure.py rebuilds the tree independently and asserts the drawn node set equals the receipt’s valid nodes in count and carrier vertices, and that the node-minus-edge invariant holds: 2 passed, EXIT=0. Negative control. Dropping one valid node from the drawn set (through CONTOUR_TREE_FIGURE_DROP_NODE) makes the guard fail — 1 failed, 1 passed, EXIT=1, the failure test_figure_draws_exactly_the_receipts_valid_nodes reading assert 12 == 13 — log at docs/figures/contour-tree-topology-authority/ctta-explanatory-figure-from-computed-tree/negative-control.log.

Scope note. This node measures only its own change: the figure and the guard that pins it to the receipt. The authority’s tree builder, the field representation, and the persistence and primary selection that reduce the 11 extra nodes to the analytic axis and X-point are the subjects of sibling sections and are not built or verified here.
