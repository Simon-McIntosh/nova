Explanatory figure regenerated from the computed tree

benchmarks/contour_tree_explanatory_figure.py renders the plan’s explanatory figure from the tree nova.equilibrium.contour_tree.build_contour_tree actually computes for the persisted diverted single-null Solov’ev certificate state at its 340-cell rung, rather than from a hand-placed tree. Every node and edge is the receipt’s own; the figure cannot drift from it, because a guard rebuilds the tree independently and asserts the drawn set matches in count and carrier vertices, and a second guard compares the committed image to the receipt’s own SVG digest (tests/test_contour_tree_explanatory_figure.py). Outputs land under docs/figures/contour-tree-topology-authority/explanatory/: the two-panel image (poloidal panel and the tree as a signed-flux graph), its SVG, a JSON receipt carrying the caption and every node row, and the render log.

The measure. The certificate is fixture diverted-single-null-340-cells from certificate_rung_fixtures(), with σ = +1 (the raw axis flux exceeds the boundary flux). The computed tree carries 13 nodes and 12 edges, so the node count exceeds the edge count by one. Two of the nodes are the analytic nulls — the magnetic-axis maximum and the admitted X-point saddle (the saddle the tree attaches directly to the axis). The other 11 nodes lie beyond them: 5 piecewise-linear extrema (1 maximum and 4 minima) and 5 further saddles, plus the positionless virtual outside slot the padded carrier emits, typed outside the DD critical-point vocabulary; the private-region wall maximum is the tree’s own node at the highest-flux wall vertex. These are low-persistence piecewise-linear extrema of the discretised field, drawn rather than hidden, and the critical-point geometry and selection sections (§3 and §4) must absorb them by persistence and primary selection.

Gate. tests/test_contour_tree_explanatory_figure.py rebuilds the tree independently and asserts the drawn node set equals the receipt’s valid nodes in count and carrier vertices, keeps the node-minus-edge invariant, requires the committed SVG to match the receipt’s own digest, and requires every direct graph label to clear every other label and every node marker in display coordinates: 6 passed, EXIT=0. The graph labels are placed by the first candidate offset that collides with no placed label box and no node marker, so the private-region wall maximum and the outside node — drawn at the same flux level — cannot stack their labels. Negative control. Restoring the single fixed label offset (CONTOUR_TREE_FIGURE_FIXED_LABELS=1) makes the collision guard fail — 1 failed, EXIT=1, the failure test_graph_labels_do_not_collide reading graph labels ‘wall maximum’ and ‘outside (wall contact)’ overlap — log at docs/figures/contour-tree-topology-authority/ctta-explanatory-figure-polish/negative-control-fixed-labels.log. A figure left from another revision is caught by the SVG-digest guard: a scratch copy with one altered coordinate fails test_committed_figure_files_exist with the committed SVG does not match the receipt that describes it — log at docs/figures/contour-tree-topology-authority/ctta-explanatory-figure-polish/stale-figure-mutation.log.
