Contour-tree explanatory figure

benchmarks/contour_tree_explanatory_figure.py renders the explanatory figure for the topology authority from the diverted single-null Solov’ev reference (CerfonFreidbergSingleNull) and reuses the media painters: line contours on stated raw-flux levels in webers masked to the vessel interior, the wall as its own unit, both null kinds through draw_nulls, and the contour tree overlaid. Outputs (PNG, SVG, JSON receipt, run log) land under docs/figures/contour-tree-topology-authority/explanatory/.

The tree. Three nodes and two edges, so the node count minus the edge count is one. Sigma is +1: the axis is the in-vessel maximum of sigma·psi. Nodes: maximum at the polished magnetic axis (R=1.78706 m, Z=+0.04677 m, psi=+5.7096e-3 Wb); saddle at the polished X-point (R=1.50253 m, Z=-1.01728 m, psi=−4.0e-17 Wb, i.e. the separatrix level); outside (virtual) at the first wall contact (R=1.50258 m, Z=−1.20768 m, no flux of its own). The outside node is valued below every interior level; the level at which it joins the tree is recorded on its edge, not on the node. That join level is the highest wall vertex of the region that first meets the wall — the first contact descending from the axis, here +9.9548e-4 Wb at the divertor wall below the X-point. Because a probe level between the X-point and that contact still carries two components (core and outside separate), the contact was reached through the X-point, so it lies in a private region and can never be a limiter: the class reads diverted. The tree's descending order is axis, then saddle, then the outside join.

Shape check (independent of how the nodes were located). A brute-force connected-component census of the superlevel sets of sigma·psi, with every wall-touching component glued to the single outside node, sampled at eight levels bracketing every node value. Positive control: a level 1.5 and 2.5 flux-spans / scale above the X-point carries zero components, so an empty count there is a real absence. Between the wall contact and the axis: one interior component touching no wall. Between the X-point and the wall contact: two components, one on the wall (the core and the outside are separate). Below the X-point: one wall-touching component (core and outside merged). No level disagrees with the three-node path.

Negative control. Dropping one edge from the tree and re-running makes the node-minus-edge guard refuse with a non-zero exit (3 nodes, 1 edges); log at negative-control.log in the run directory.
