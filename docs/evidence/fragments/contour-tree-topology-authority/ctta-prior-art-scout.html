Prior-art reuse map for §2 to §4

The reuse map is docs/evidence/contour-tree-topology-authority/reuse-map.md. Read-only survey of nova (b2cc96f4), imas-efit (a1028cda), imas-ambix (2cede036) and imas-codex (ddec2385), by bounded greps over the named subtrees recorded in the map's own header; no source was edited, no GPU and no SLURM job was used.

Measure. Nine capability rows, one per capability the plan's §2 to §4 need, each with candidate implementations carrying file:line, what it does, jit traceability and a verdict. Verdict tally: 5 reuse, 3 adapt, 1 absent-and-build capability classes across the rows (rows carry mixed verdicts; the row-level summary table is the authority).

What the map found that changes the dispatch

  • §4 already exists as working prior art. imas-efit/src/EFIT/contour_tree.f90 is a 4131-line contour-tree module: Kruskal union-find over the critical-point graph (build_contour_tree, line 130), a wall-attached variant (build_contour_tree_with_wall, line 1132), and the production limited-vs-diverted merge-tree decision merge_tree_decide (line 3389) whose own header states §4's first-event rule: "the axis component's FIRST event (interior saddle-merge ⇒ DIVERTED, direct wall touch ⇒ LIMITED) is the boundary". mark_private_subtrees (line 1673) is the polarity-aware private-flux classifier. A readable Python oracle (output/m2-build/mergetree_oracle/merge_tree.py) reimplements the same five steps and agrees with truth to ≈5e-6 Wb on TCV and HL-3. Nothing of this lives in nova.
  • §2's carriers are already jit-shaped. The triangulation + ring packing is PlasmaGrid.tessellate (nova/nova/biot/plasmagrid.py:382), the true triangulation-to- polygon clip is _covered_triangles (nova/nova/biot/sectionaverage.py:120), multi-unit containment is already jnp (connectivity_boundary._points_inside_wall_units:233, connectivity_boundary._points_inside_polygon:169), and the fixed-shape labelling and link-admissibility kernels are parallel_components.label_parallel_graph_components_with_steps:77 and flux_surface_connectivity.hex_edge_admissibility:329.
  • Two §2 capabilities are genuinely missing. The containing-triangle flux lookup for a wall vertex (row 3) and simulation-of-simplicity tie breaking have no implementation in any searched tree; the nearest tie handling is ad hoc (union by rank with a born-earlier root in the imas-efit oracle, first-index _arg_extreme in nova). The imas-efit battery's case A4 is a ready-made degenerate fixture (A4sym twin identical-ψ saddles, A4pert proving the break follows genuine outward ψ).
  • §3's open decision has measured candidates in-repo. stencil_nulls._refine_selected_vertices:1803 (Hessian determinant sign → min/max/saddle; the 9/9-saddle, 15/15-axis own-node quadratic) and flux_surface_connectivity._polish_stationary_points_in_bounds:703 (fixed-slot Newton on ∂ψ=0 with in-vessel bounds) are both jax; the accuracy reference is Fortran null_detection.f90 (8-neighbour cyclic sign-change census, quadratic patch, DGELS, then Newton on ∇ψ=0 against a bicubic spline with a Hessian re-test).
