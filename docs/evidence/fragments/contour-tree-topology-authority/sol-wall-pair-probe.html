The scrape-off-layer wall pair is a carrier artefact

The recorded defect still reproduces on the diverted-single-null 340-cell certificate: interior vertex 10 has carrier ψ = −4.009710 mWb, below every linked vertex (31, 32, 447 and 448). Vertex 447 is the adjacent wall contact at −3.609201 mWb. The analytic Solov'ev flux uses σ = +1 here. Its gradient at vertex 10 is 31.252 mWb/m, and the minimum gradient over in-vessel grid samples within two cell pitches is 12.497 mWb/m, versus a 14.672 mWb/m median over the certificate's SOL cell centres. The detector finds the known analytic magnetic axis at |∇ψ| = 3.29 × 10−17 Wb/m; it finds no stationary point within two pitches of vertex 10.

The smooth field decreases by 554.019 µWb from 10 to 447, with directional derivative between −13.360 and −13.195 mWb/m all along that segment. The carrier instead rises by 400.509 µWb. The term that reverses the ordering is the wall interpolation error: ψcarrier − ψanalytic is +954.529 µWb at 447 and +770.071 µWb at the other linked wall vertex, 448; it is below 5 × 10−11 µWb at the three cell samples 10, 31 and 32. Vertices 447 and 448 are barycentric interpolations of valid centre cells 81, 10 and 3 (carrier ψ values −5.645122, −4.009710 and −1.485026 mWb). Vertex 447 uses weights 0.420944, 0.147744 and 0.431312; the weighted flux reproduces its carrier value to floating-point rounding. The clipped carrier triangles incident to 447 are 338, 507 and 638.

Across the twelve exposed certificate rungs, eight interior piecewise-linear extrema (four minima and four maxima) have no analytic stationary point within two pitches. Replacing only their wall-neighbour carrier values with analytic values destroys three of those extrema: minimum 10 on the diverted 340-cell rung and minima 71 and 72 on the diverted 550-cell rung. The diverted 1074-cell rung retains one nonstationary minimum under that substitution; the five remaining nonstationary extrema have other local carrier causes. The full per-rung vertex list and the five-vertex flux audit are in the probe log.

Proposed repair: evaluate the physical smooth field at wall vertices instead of assigning their flux by linear interpolation from centre samples, then recheck the carrier topology and its convergence with pitch.
