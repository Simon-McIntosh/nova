Axis-facing X-point owner

At 2,098 cells one booked owner cell carries 59 % of the diverted map's flux error, and its area fraction matches the analytic one to 1.5e-6 while its booked current is off by 3.3e-3 relative. Nine owner representations spanning the natural design space are measured on all three carriers and none passes all three guards, so the axis-facing owner remains the continuous bottleneck. The candidate the plan names — taking the owner conic from the saddle the read already locates — passes 2,098 at 9.41e-5 and fails 282 and 550 by 9.3x and 9.2x. The diverted cold solve converges to 1e-12 at 282 and 550 under the delivered owner; at 2,098 the map qualification gate refuses the run before its first correction.

The verdict is reported unwidened.

The declared negative control does not reproduce

The control declared for this node reads: restoring the fitted Bell stationary point as the owner conic anchor brings the 2,098 map error back to 2.49e-4. It was run at all three sizes and it does not. The fitted stationary-point anchor gives 1.34080430954e-2 at 282, 3.58585602656e-2 at 550 and 1.79194122881e-2 at 2,098 — 72 times the value the control names.

The reason is that 2.49e-4 is not a pre-repair value at all: it is what the saddle-anchored half-plane owner produces, and the base revision already anchored there. The base integrate_cell sets origin = saddle for its half-plane branch and origin = centre + pitch * stationary_local only for its Bell branch, and the owner's default component was already the half-plane. So the defect the node inherited came from the saddle anchor, and the anchor is not what the repair changes. One caveat is carried with the number rather than hidden: the fitted stationary-point anchor moves the branch restriction as well as the origin (the restriction follows the sign of the critical level at the stationary point rather than being unconditional), so the measurement is of the Bell rule as the code implements it, not of a pure origin swap. A pure swap — the fitted field, the fitted stationary origin, the unconditional half-plane restriction — is named as a follow-on rather than built here.

Verdict

Clause 1 is answered: cell 47 at 2,098 carries 59 % of the error over the floor, in the cell current, with its area right to 1.5e-6. Clause 2 is not met and the evidence says why it is structural: the delivered owner sits 2.316e-4 over the floor at 2,098 against 4.61e-4 at 282 and 2.275e-4 at 550, a nearly resolution-independent excess that a better conic does not remove. Re-measuring the booking the owner replaces settles which way the defect runs: that booking passes 550 and 2,098 (4.87161211918e-4 and 9.47260774384e-5) and fails 282 by 1.07e-10, and the 282 guard 1.0866e-3 is its own error to eight figures, so the owner's replacement is a net gain at two sizes and a 2.63x regression at the third. What the numbers point at is the region rather than the field — the reference booking builds exact normal-form fragment rings from the saddle's own cubic and the edge intervals the read has already computed, while the owner approximates that region with a conic, and a conic cannot represent a cubic separatrix. Clause 3 passes at 282 and 550 and is refused at 2,098 by clause 2's gate. Clause 4 holds by construction and its recorded brackets classify as crossings. The declared negative control does not reproduce: the mutation it names gives 1.79194122881e-2 at 2,098, not 2.49e-4, because the base already anchored the owner at the located saddle and 2.49e-4 is the delivered representation's own value.
