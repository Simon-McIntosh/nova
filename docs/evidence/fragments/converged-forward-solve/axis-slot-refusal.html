Comparison adapter refuses an invalid-axis read

The field-swap adapter read_state published a limited or diverted label from a read that licensed no axis, so a NaN axis still produced a plasma decision. It now refuses whenever the read is invalid or its axis is not finite, returning the label refused invalid axis with a NaN axis and a NaN boundary, and naming the refusal in its note. A synthetic field whose single interior extremum is a minimum under the declared positive-polarity convention (a certified loop, winding +1, index sum +1) is the exercising case: the present label path returns limited from it and fails the new test, the fixed path returns the refusal and passes.

The near-fold persistence gate in the fixed-shape read had no suite coverage. The added test takes the double-well field under a large constant background, which leaves the stationary roots and their signed persistence unchanged while the fitted coefficients, and so the binary64 enclosure of the fitted level, grow with the background. At that background the persistence lies at the rounding scale, the root-saddle pair is unresolved, and the read publishes no axis; the same field with no background is accepted. Deleting the persistent comparison makes the refusal disappear and the test fails.

Both arms ran the same two test files with the repository-root conftest in fresh processes. The baseline arm (base crosscheck source, head test files) reached its summary with one failure - the new invalid-axis test - and 12 passes; the after arm reached its summary with 13 passes and zero added failures. The adapter change is measured on the synthetic invalid-axis field.
