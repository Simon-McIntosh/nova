Current booking cost on fine grids

The inherited vmapped booking keeps every cell in the largest fragment-ring shape. Each full or empty cell therefore reserves the clipped-ring representation. The committed implementation books full cells on their original small polygons, packs cut cells in batches of 32, and integrates only live rings within each cut cell. It preserves the signed ring order and the existing eight-by-eight Duffy quadrature and 512 chord segments.

The inherited bank scales with cell count times the maximum fragment count per cell times sampled ring width, which grows with chord segments. Each ring also generates 64 Duffy samples per triangle. The new whole-cell path uses original cell vertices; the cut path allocates at most 32 cells' sampled rings at a time and loops over each cell's live rings. Its cut integration scales with the actual cut-cell and live-fragment counts instead of every cell multiplied by the worst fragment count.
