Clause 2 — the wall contact's effective level, with the location it already had. Base already gives the wall candidate the more scrape-off-side of its tangency level sigma * wall_flux and its _wall_path_ceiling bottleneck, at arm/kernel_base.py:287: wall_scores = jnp.minimum(sigma * wall_flux, ceiling[wall_cell]). The rule is now the owned function _wall_effective_level(tangency, bottleneck) = jnp.minimum(tangency, bottleneck), called from that same site, so the behaviour is unchanged and the rule has a name, a docstring and a test. The synthetic carrier (five hand-built quad cells, CPU only) is decisive: a private-flux contact at level −0.05, reachable from the axis only through a saddle gate at −0.5, takes −0.5 — the saddle's level — removing the 0.45 advantage its own tangency would have given it; a directly reachable control at −0.3 keeps −0.3. A second test sweeps the tangency through the bottleneck crossing at 0.5 ± 10−6 and asserts the level moves with it, so there is no step. Both tests pass.
