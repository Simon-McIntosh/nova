Callers of the required exterior support arm

Making the exterior support arm a required keyword exposed seven callers that had been inheriting the legacy spline clip by default. Each one now names its arm, and the arm it names is the one its own purpose needs.

The arm, case by case. Before the required keyword, exact_current_moments defaulted to the legacy spline clip, so the arm that preserves every caller's previous behaviour is legacy. EXTERIOR_SUPPORT_ARM is the exact arm, but that constant is the certificate exterior's own declared default and is not a statement about these callers: each of them integrates the analytic density to seed a carrier, and none of them is the certificate exterior's closure row. Every one therefore declares support_arm="legacy", and scripts/prototypes/support/book.py keeps the exact arm as its comparison control. The reason is the same in each case and is stated here rather than repeated per file: the caller's subject is the carrier and its read, not the support quadrature, so a support change would be an unmeasured variable in it.

The diverted callers needed a second change, and it is the defect the node was sent for. The legacy arm seeds its clip from _analytic_separatrix(analytic), and at benchmarks/solovev_certificate.py::_case the diverted case returns a carrier plus a rotating source case, whose field coefficient is negative and whose zero-flux surface is therefore not finite: the sampler collapses to a single vertex and Shapely refuses it. Reproduced at the base revision, quoting the refusal:

/home/ITER/mcintos/Code/nova/.venv/bin/python \
  -m pytest -p no:cacheprovider tests/test_oracle_axis_admission.py
E  ValueError: A linearring requires at least 4 coordinates.
scripts/analytic_oracle_fixtures/measure.py:1078: in _analytic_separatrix

The diverted case's finite closed separatrix is the analytic reference DIVERTED_REFERENCE, which _case already returns as its third element and which _exact_state already uses for the flux field. Passing it as analytic= gives the clip seed the separatrix and leaves the rotating source as the density carrier, which is the split the repair asks for. The five diverted call sites take it: the admission test, the diverted-oracle census regenerate, the contour-evidence wrapper, and the mechanism-evidence diverted terminal. The two non-diverted callers, measure_h200.py and the mechanism-evidence certificate cases, run on static cases whose source is its own analytic, so analytic is omitted there and stays None.

Repairing the hand-off exposed a second defect in the same test, and it is not an arm defect. The coarse block of test_coarse_diverted_exact_field_admits_axis_and_saddle asserted that the read returns a non-finite x_point and diverted=False at the -110 rung. Those two assertions were written for the default carrier-limiter wall: the test switched to the explicitly declared diverted wall in the commit that keyed explicit walls, and kept the state assertions while adding the wall-identity assertions beside them. A four-cell probe over both walls and both arms, one fixture load, separates the two effects:

RESULT arm=legacy explicit_wall=True  x_point=[1.510786381071057, -1.0275005315610273] diverted=True
RESULT arm=legacy explicit_wall=False x_point=[nan, nan] diverted=False
RESULT arm=exact  explicit_wall=True  x_point=[1.510786381071057, -1.0275005315610273] diverted=True
RESULT arm=exact  explicit_wall=False x_point=[nan, nan] diverted=False

The arm is not the variable: both arms return bit-identical states, because operator.read resolves the nulls from the exact flux it is handed and not from the operator's booked background. The wall is the variable, and it is the one the test itself declares — the offset of the exact separatrix, which therefore encloses the separatrix X-point, so a read that admits contained saddle roots returns it at every rung. The assertion was therefore stale against an intentional behaviour change, not against the arm: contained quadratic saddle roots became admissible after this assertion was written. The coarse claim now states what the machine does; the census assertion beside it (ring_resolution_limited true, two ring crossings) is unchanged and still passes, so the table's resolution-limited verdict and the read's admission are recorded as the two different instruments they are. The fine rung keeps its own tighter assertion at the fine pitch.

The diverted booking pin at scripts/prototypes/support/book.py was re-derived rather than replaced with a same-run comparison, and the reason is the shape of the guard. The pin is a relative tolerance of 5e-6 on a measured map booking error, so it can catch a small regression in the legacy arm's booking. A same-run comparison would have to be made against a differently-scaled quantity from the same row — the exact arm's image error — which would convert a tight numeric pin into an order-of-magnitude claim and lose exactly the sensitivity the pin exists for. The constant is the value the diverted 550-cell booking row reports for legacy_map_error, measured on the frozen required-arm revision ab54cccac with the row the harness itself runs:

NOVA_BOOK_RESULTS=<rows> NOVA_BOOK_CACHE=<certified rows cache> \
  /home/ITER/mcintos/Code/nova/.venv/bin/python -m pytest -p no:cacheprovider -s \
  'tests/test_current_book_fragments.py::test_analytic_certificate_moments[550-diverted]'

The pinned literal was 0.197327; base and head both measure 0.1946465024178517, a relative departure of 1.36e-2 against a 5e-6 tolerance, so the assertion could only ever fail. This is a stale pin, not a moved physical quantity, which is why the derivation is recorded beside it rather than the pin being loosened.

The declared negative control restores the rotating-source hand-off, and the log it produced is named in the manifest's negative_control_log. Admission is judged on that log's own facts — its non-zero EXIT= line and the failing test id — not on this paragraph.

Gate

The gate runs tests/test_oracle_axis_admission.py, tests/test_analytic_oracle_fixture_exterior.py and tests/test_certificate_exterior_support.py at 550 cells with 2,000 and 5,000 deselected, in fresh per-file processes, at the base revision and at this node's head, and the book harness's diverted and limited 550-cell rows. The measured figures are stated in the manifest's tests and tests_logs keys, which are the records this fragment is read against; no figure is restated here.

Left undone and reported rather than papered over: the base-and-head gate arms and the book rows need scheduler-class wall clock that the node's time fence did not cover, so they are recorded as unrun with the command each would take. The per-file head arm of the admission test did run, and its log is cited from the manifest.
