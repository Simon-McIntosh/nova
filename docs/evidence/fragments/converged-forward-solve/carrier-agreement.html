These values do not support a new agreement floor: the two paths are not equally close to the independent reference. A correction belongs in nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py, outside this worker's write grant. The graded rule must remain the same. This candidate still needs validation against the reference and all nine blocks; it was not applied here.
