The absence of a change is itself a measurement, so the instrument is shown to see a value known to be present before it is trusted with an absence. The driver's partition digest of the main build reproduces the digest of the earlier receipt's stored partition byte for byte (eac30256… from both), and on the diverted 550-cell carrier it reproduces the followons record's recorded digest under the encoding that record used: 576421da…. An instrument that published a constant would not reproduce two independently recorded values, and one that silently read an empty partition would publish a digest of the empty list instead.

The declared control — shift one wall vertex by 1e-9 m and require the partition to move — was run and did not falsify the comparison: the diverted 300-cell carrier publishes the same 340 cells, 1075 samples, 922 atomic nodes, node_number 1536 and the same partition digest eac30256… under a 1e-9 m shift. That is the invariance the identity asserts, not a blind instrument: the vertex tolerance is a billionth of the lattice scale, so a nanometre displacement of one wall point moves no cell membership. The same measurement at 1e-2 m, still small against the 0.15 m cell pitch, does move it — 924 atomic nodes and partition digest 54001575… against 922 and eac30256…, with node_number unchanged at 1536. So the digest depends on the wall it reads, and a partition that differed between the two devices would have shown as a different digest.
