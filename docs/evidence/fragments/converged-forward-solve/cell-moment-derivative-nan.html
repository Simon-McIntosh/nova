Cell 103 moment derivative: the non-finite term and the per-cell qualification

The CPU diverted 300-cell certificate row still refuses its first Krylov action, and this record locates the term that turns the derivative non-finite: the level-set arc root's square root is taken at a discriminant clamped to zero, so its tangent is non-finite exactly across the band of samples where the discriminant changes sign. That expression is in nova/equilibrium/separatrix_clip.py, outside this node's write grant. The second defect, one cell's non-finite moment scaling every cell through the common amplitude, is repaired and tested.

Reproduction at the base revision

At base 2967db463, on CPU in place on the fleet allocation, scripts/certificate_300_carrier/trace_normalisation.py at the production diverted single-null seed (target current 37174.65342635735 A) reports the raw cell-moment derivative non-finite in 1 of 340 entries, index 103, with every value finite (largest value 313.543 A). The total-current derivative is non-finite, and the normalised moments are non-finite in 340 of 340. The reproduction log is repro-normalisation.log under the run directory.

The expression

Staged differentiation at the same seed narrows it. Every upstream stage is finite at cell 103: the support vertices' tangent is identically zero, the flux-polynomial coefficient tangent is finite ([-0.1981, -0.6826, -0.3137, 0.2767, -0.1531, 0.0298] against the value [1.0007, 0.01215, 0.005441, 0.006906, -0.007116, -0.005519]), and the density tangent at the cell's fixed quadrature points is finite. The first non-finite tangent is the moving level-set clip vertices of SeparatrixClip: 46 of 366520 entries, all in cell 103.

Those entries are arc slots 483 to 505. Recomputing _traced_quadratic_arc's own discriminant per sample for that cell gives its smallest magnitudes at samples 481 to 507, where it changes sign: sample 482 +4.876e-08, sample 483 -2.254e-07, sample 505 +3.261e-07, sample 506 +2.970e-08. The non-finite band is exactly that sign-change band. The term is

discriminant = jnp.maximum(linear * linear - 4.0 * quadratic * zero_value, 0.0)
root_scale = jnp.sqrt(discriminant)

in _traced_quadratic_arc (nova/equilibrium/separatrix_clip.py, the root_scale line). The clamp makes the value finite on the negative side, where its tangent is zero, and leaves the positive side with a tangent of dD / (2 sqrt(D)); across the seam the tangent is undefined, so the composed tangent is non-finite while every value stays finite. This is the tangency signature the brief named, confirmed here at a sign-changing discriminant rather than an exactly-zero one.

Why it is not repaired here

The site is nova/equilibrium/separatrix_clip.py. This node's exclusive write grant covers nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py and nova/equilibrium/source.py only, and the clip geometry files belong to the cut-cell node. The repair belongs there, as a guarded-argument sqrt: take the square root of a discriminant held at a positive floor (sqrt(max(D, floor)) with floor of order 64 eps × the local scale), and carry the linear branch (-zero_value / linear) across the whole seam band rather than below a per-scalar floor. The cut-cell node's edge-discriminant repair is the same signature in a different bookkeeping.

Per-cell qualification (repaired)

Commit e10705e61e6562c59326f28b8b70cd3173efaa5a adds qualify_cell_current_moments to nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py. It zeroes every moment component of a non-finite cell and returns the offending indices; it is applied before each sum and before the normalisation amplitude, in _internal_on_partition, normalised_current_moments and normalised_current_moments_and_observation. ForwardFluxOperator.cell_moment_receipt returns those indices for a concrete state. A non-finite total reaches the existing CurrentNormalisationError, which names the normalisation instead of returning an amplitude that scales every cell.

The expression was never guarded in this node's tree, so the unmutated head is the control arm: diag-nan.log records the full staged derivation at head and reports stage4 non-finite tangent vertices in 1 cells: [103], and repro-normalisation.log reports the raw moment derivative non-finite at index 103.
