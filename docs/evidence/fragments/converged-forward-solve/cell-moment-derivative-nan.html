Cell 103 moment derivative: the non-finite term and the arc-root seam repair

The CPU diverted 300-cell certificate row now has a finite first Krylov action and converges in two trips at residual 5.63e-15. The non-finite derivative was the sampled level-set arc taking the square root of a discriminant clamped at zero: its tangent is undefined across the band where the discriminant changes sign, which is the arrangement cell 103 held at arc slots 483 to 505. The square root is now taken off that seam with the double-where pattern. That repair is what this node lands. Two later attempts to refuse a non-finite cell by index are recorded and reverted below, because neither could fire or be carried out of a traced step; the base normalisation behaviour, where a non-finite amplitude poisons the residual and the solver refuses it, is kept.

The expression

Staged differentiation at the same seed narrows it. Every upstream stage is finite at cell 103: the support vertices' tangent is identically zero, the flux-polynomial coefficient tangent is finite ([-0.1981, -0.6826, -0.3137, 0.2767, -0.1531, 0.0298] against the value [1.0007, 0.01215, 0.005441, 0.006906, -0.007116, -0.005519]), and the density tangent at the cell's fixed quadrature points is finite. The first non-finite tangent is the moving level-set clip vertices of SeparatrixClip: 46 of 366520 entries, all in cell 103.

Those entries are arc slots 483 to 505. Recomputing _traced_quadratic_arc's own discriminant per sample for that cell gives its smallest magnitudes at samples 481 to 507, where it changes sign: sample 482 +4.876e-08, sample 483 -2.254e-07, sample 505 +3.261e-07, sample 506 +2.970e-08. The non-finite band is exactly that sign-change band. The term is

discriminant = jnp.maximum(linear * linear - 4.0 * quadratic * zero_value, 0.0)
root_scale = jnp.sqrt(discriminant)

in _traced_quadratic_arc (nova/equilibrium/separatrix_clip.py, the root_scale line). The clamp makes the value finite on the negative side, where its tangent is zero, and leaves the positive side with a tangent of dD / (2 sqrt(D)); across the seam the tangent is undefined, so the composed tangent is non-finite while every value stays finite. This is the tangency signature the brief named, confirmed here at a sign-changing discriminant rather than an exactly-zero one.

The repair (commit 4688864ee)

Under the lead's 2026-10-24 ruling that concurrent edits to one file from separate worktrees are reconciled at integration, the expression is repaired at its site in nova/equilibrium/separatrix_clip.py with the double-where pattern: the discriminant keeps its sign, a safe value of 1.0 replaces it where it is not resolvable, and the square root is selected by that same mask.

discriminant = linear * linear - 4.0 * quadratic * zero_value
resolvable = discriminant > 0.0
safe_discriminant = jnp.where(resolvable, discriminant, 1.0)
root_scale = jnp.where(resolvable, jnp.sqrt(safe_discriminant), 0.0)

The value is unchanged — sqrt(D) where D > 0 and 0 elsewhere, which is what the clamp produced — while neither the value nor the tangent touches sqrt at zero. At the production seed, cell 103's moment tangent is 2824.328634996701 against a central difference of 2824.179876803612, relative difference 5.267e-05 on a step of 1.0e-6; no cell's moment or tangent is non-finite and the median relative difference over the 340 cells is zero. Reverted to the base behaviour

Two later attempts at reporting a non-finite cell by index were made on top of the arc-root repair and both are removed by commit e39ffd109, because neither closed the gap it was aimed at. The first refused a non-finite cell from a concrete evaluation, and no production route is concrete: gs.apply, the jitted reduced map and the certificate jvp all pass tracers, so the refusal could not fire. The second carried a per-cell mask out of the step and replaced the amplitude's non-finite signal with an admissibility flag; on the traced route an inadmissible total current then returned a zero amplitude, which scales every cell to zero and lets the solve proceed on a zero source instead of poisoning the residual, and the mask itself was discarded at all three solve paths because the driver seam returns a bare jax.Array. The base behaviour is the safe one: a non-finite amplitude poisons the residual and the solver refuses it as a non-finite linear action.

Commit e39ffd109 restores nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py and nova/equilibrium/source.py byte for byte to 2967db463. git diff 2967db463 -- nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py nova/equilibrium/source.py prints nothing, so the node's surviving code change is the arc-root repair in separatrix_clip.py alone. The traced normalisation amplitude returns jnp.nan where the base did, current_normalisation_admissible, the mask helpers and their plumbing at the three solve paths are gone, and the unit file now carries only the arc-root seam test (gate-unit-final.log, 1 passed, EXIT=0).

The report-by-index is moved to a successor with an exact specification rather than attempted again here: the jitted step must return the non-finite flag and the offending cell indices as outputs, and the host loop in each solve driver — the certificate solver's linear_step loop and the reduced Newton driver — must refuse by name with CurrentNormalisationError when the flag is set. That requires editing the drivers, which are outside this node's write scope.
