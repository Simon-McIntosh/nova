The repair and the counts it restores

A seed is now admitted when its cross product is negative only beyond the rounding the value carries: the determinant's forward error, 4·eps·(|edge|1)·(|relative|1), plus the seed's solve-propagated uncertainty eps·|pitch|·||H⁻¹||1·(||H||1·|offset|1+|gradient|1) scaled by the edge length. The second term dominates at the saddle because it is unbounded in the conditioning of the 2x2 Hessian. Both terms are computed from the operands at the state being classified, so the decision is continuous in the field and no switch was added. Measured after the repair, at the fixed prior: offset 0.17323108126295622 reads 3 roots / 2 saddles, and offset 0.17323108126295625 reads 3 roots / 2 saddles.

The determinant term alone was measured first and is insufficient: at 2.86e-18 it does not cover the 5.77e-17 departure, and the pair still read 3 -> 2. It is retained as one addend rather than replaced, because it is a real part of the error and dropping it would make the sum a fit rather than a bound.

Carrying the prior from 0.16 to 0.18 in 41 states, the census reads 2 roots / 1 saddle at 0.16, 3 / 2 from 0.161 through 0.173, and 1 / 1 from 0.1735 onward. The 3 -> 1 step near 0.1735 is pre-existing, not introduced here: the predecessor recorded 3 at 0.17 and 1 at 0.18 on the same field. It remains uncharacterised between a genuine fold and a second admission defect, and is reported open.

The unused psi_rounding_enclosure in fast_map_production/kernel.py bounds flux along the map, not the gradient or the edge cross product this admission test evaluates, so it does not fit. It was left in place rather than deleted; it is unused by this path either way.
