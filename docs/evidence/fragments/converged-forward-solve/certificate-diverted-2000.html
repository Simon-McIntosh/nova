Certified diverted 2,000-cell connectivity membership made continuous

The surviving stall of the diverted 2,000-cell carrier is the connectivity gate's saddle-cell membership. _continuous_path_weight in scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production/kernel.py closed its near-saddle weighting with jnp.maximum(near_clip, topology._inside_cells(geometry, form.position)). topology._inside_cells (nova/equilibrium/topology.py:2572, comparison at line 2582, cross >= -1e-12 * pitch**2) is a hard point-in-cell test: it returns one for exactly the cell that contains the saddle and zero elsewhere. The saddle position is a quantity of the map, so as it crosses a cell edge the forced cell flips and the membership steps. Because that indicator is max-ed over the smooth distance clip, the geometric width length_width cannot soften it — this is the decision the 2026-10-19 column probe localized (job 1290519): at cell 47 block 0 the path_weight forward-mode derivative is 4.379 against a central difference of 5615.57 at h = 1e-5 and 3.446 against 56135.5 at h = 1e-6, so the central difference grows tenfold as the step shrinks tenfold — a value jump of about 0.056, not a slope — while geometric_membership stays smooth.

The fix replaces the indicator with _cell_containment_weight, a continuous membership: the saddle point's signed distance to each cell edge, positive inside, reduced by that cell's own shortest edge length. It is one deep inside a cell, zero half an edge length beyond an edge, and the linear share between, so the ramp is as wide as the cell's own resolution of the position — a quantity of the map, never a tuned constant — and no step survives the point crossing an edge. The two edge cells are still reduced by the minimum of their shares, both boundary candidates are still computed from the same weight, and the value is not branched on topology.
