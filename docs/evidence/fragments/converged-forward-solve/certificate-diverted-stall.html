Diverted carrier map derivative localization

Two H200 stage probes at the stalled diverted carriers, each comparing every read stage's forward-mode derivative against a directional central difference and then comparing the map's own Jacobian columns against a basis central difference, put the disagreement at the read's connectivity weight rather than at the boundary level. At the stalled diverted 2,000-cell iterate (scaled residual 0.003627426010221647, 2,000 cells) cell 40 block 0 reports path_weight base 1.0 with a forward-mode derivative of 0.0 against a central difference of 50000.0 at h = 1e-5 and 52001.4 at h = 1e-6 — a step of 1.0 in the value across a step of 1e-5, with the derivative reported as exactly zero. The membership it scales reports 0.538 against 42113.5 at h = 1e-5. At the two residual-dominating cells 40 and 50, path_weight and membership are the only stages whose disagreement exceeds 1e-4 relative, and every other stage — bell_area, bell_moments, boundary_flux, cut_moments, four_crossings, geometric_membership, root_position, split_area — agrees to roundoff at both step sizes there. That scoping is the whole of the claim: the receipt also records smaller disagreements above 1e-4 at the three further probed cells 1644, 1748 and 1750 — root_position at all three, worst 1.079e-3 at cell 1748, and at cell 1644 also bell_area, bell_moments, cut_moments, geometric_membership and membership, worst 5.31e-4 — while those three cells' full-map columns agree to about 1e-5, so their stage-level disagreement does not reach the map.

The mechanism is in _continuous_path_weight in scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production/kernel.py. It builds flux_width = jnp.asarray(1e-8) and then flux_weight = jnp.clip(0.5 + (passage - level) / flux_width, 0, 1). A ramp 1e-8 wide around level is five orders below any step a central difference or a Newton correction can take, and it is below the working scale of the carrier itself, so at the scale the solve operates on flux_weight is a step: 0 below level, 1 above. The clip then saturates on both sides, and the value downstream is carried by a maximum-propagated connectivity, so the forward-mode derivative is exactly zero and the value jumps by one. A map stage that is a step function reports a derivative that cannot match its values in either direction: autodiff sees the locally flat branch, the finite difference sees the jump, and neither is a Newton derivative. That is the signature the brief describes — a rejected correction after a converged GMRES solve — and it is why nine shrinking corrections can be accepted while the tenth is thrown away.

The full-map Jacobian columns confirm it reaches the map. At the stalled diverted 2,000-cell iterate, cell 50's three blocks report a forward-mode column of −1.348941e+00, 6.182090e-01 and −2.199384e+00 against central differences of −6.540807e+04 and −6.433632e+05 (block 0, h = 1e-5 and 1e-6), 3.383283e+03 and 3.382554e+04 (block 1), and 3.142720e+03 and 3.143260e+04 (block 2) — the solver's own Jacobian is wrong by four to five orders of magnitude in the columns that dominate the rejected correction. Where the connectivity jump cancels against the smooth part of geometric_membership the column happens to agree (cell 40 block 0: 1.0 against 0.99999999999998), which is why a directional probe along the residual alone did not expose this and the basis-direction column probe did.

The connectivity width comes from the cell's own flux excursion

The fix replaces the constant ramp. _flux_past_level_weight(peak, trough, level) returns the share of a cell's own flux excursion that lies past level: one where the whole excursion is past the level (must-connect), zero where none of it is (must-not), with the linear share in between, and a degenerate span falls back to the sign of the excursion so the map stays finite. Its ramp width is peak - trough, the difference the read's own edge quadratic spans inside one cell, so the width is a property of the map rather than a tuned constant: it narrows as the mesh refines, and at any iterate the derivative it reports is on the scale of the values it differentiates. _continuous_path_weight now takes peak and trough from that same quadratic — the endpoints and the turning value — and weights an edge by the minimum of its two cells' shares, so an edge connects only where its colder end has passed the level. Both boundary candidates are computed from the same weight; nothing branches the value on topology, and the limited-to-diverted transition stays continuous because at a cell whose excursion straddles the level the weight moves smoothly through the interval. The change is committed at d03faa230.
