Run conditions

The H200 node carried REBOOT_REQUESTED, so all three rows ran in one labelled titan job (--partition=titan --gres=gpu:1, job 1302899, node 98dci4-gpu-0002, Tesla P100-PCIE-16GB): the two certificate rows on the CPU platform and the private moment solve on the card. Nothing ran on the login node. The two certificate rows ran at revision e1ffd3a69 and the private step at 224880f50, which adds only a figure to the tree, so the three rows measure the same code. Every process was launched with TMPDIR=/tmp exported in the submit environment and again in the payload, called nova.jax.config.configure_dtypes() before building any array, printed nova.__file__, the imported module and the resolved working directory, and ended its log with a bare EXIT= line.

The driver's stale-part refusal is what makes these readings measurements rather than served receipts: the earlier readings of 0.0749 and 1.2465e−3 came from part receipts the driver now refuses on a declared-current, carrier-digest or realised-cell mismatch. No part existed in any private root when these rows started, so each one entered the solver, and the recorded wall is the solve's own. The receipt column above is the driver's own marker rather than an assumption: each certificate row was refused unless its part carried figure.render_source = fresh_production_solve and no banked receipt block, and both rows report exactly that.

The two certificate panels are drawn by the certificate driver's own _plot, reusing the committed painter rather than a second composition; the private row's panel is the route renderer's. _plot places two labels without measuring them: a long subtitle centred on the narrow flux axis, which ran off the left canvas edge, and a level readout at the bottom-left of every error axis, which lay across the wall and the contours. The panel writer therefore re-places both before saving — the subtitle is wrapped onto two lines and the level arrays move out of the axes into a caption band beneath the panels, prefixed with the quantity each belongs to — and then measures every text artist, refusing to write the panel if any leaves the canvas, overlaps another label, or sits inside an axis. Both delivered certificate panels pass that check, and the same check applied to the driver's unaltered composition reports the clipped subtitle and the three level readouts over the data.
