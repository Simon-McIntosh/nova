Certified carriers exported into the boundary trace format

nova's own certified analytic carriers were written the other way round from the field swap: into imas-efit's BOUNDARY_LEVEL_TRACE 2 layout, so imas-efit's reader and escape-level rule can be run on fields nova generated. One limited and one diverted carrier, each loaded from the content-addressed certificate fixture store at 550 cells, were written as version-2 records carrying the analytic flux on a 65×65 grid over the wall bounding box, the read axis, the record polarity and the per-loop wall. Both round-trip through the codec and through nova's own topology read, and a negated flux grid is refused on each record's declared COCOS-17 polarity.

The analytic cases declare their boundary at zero flux. The limited contact sits 2.2×10−14 Wb inside that bound and the diverted saddle 1.4×10−9 Wb inside it; the read axis reproduces the analytic axis to 1.6×10−7 m and 1.1×10−7 m respectively. The analytic X-point is (1.50253, −1.01728) m, so the read saddle agrees with the certificate to 4.4×10−4 m.

Negative control

Negating the exported flux grid must make the round-trip test refuse the record on its declared COCOS and polarity rather than silently reproduce the decision. Both records are refused: the re-read is either invalid or returns a boundary class different from the one the export declared. A green round trip on the unmodified record therefore shows the polarity guard fires, not merely that some path succeeded.
