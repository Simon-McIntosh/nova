Certified carriers exported into the boundary trace format

nova's own certified analytic carriers were written the other way round from the field swap: into imas-efit's BOUNDARY_LEVEL_TRACE 2 layout, so imas-efit's reader and escape-level rule can be run on fields nova generated. One limited and one diverted carrier, each loaded from the content-addressed certificate fixture store at 550 cells, were written as version-2 records carrying the analytic flux on a 65×65 grid over the wall bounding box, the read axis, the record polarity and the per-loop wall. Both round-trip through the codec and through nova's own topology read, and a negated flux grid is refused on each record's declared COCOS-17 polarity.

Negative control

A record whose flux sign disagrees with the polarity it declares is refused with the refusal's own type, NO_QUALIFIED_AXIS and an unqualified axis, rather than by a disjunction over validity and class. Declaring the opposite polarity on the same negated grid restores the read to the same boundary class with an equal and opposite level, so the guard is the declared COCOS-17 orientation rather than a broken record. The gate drives that state through the producer (NOVA_CERTIFIED_EXPORT_MUTATION=negate-psi), so the round-trip test discovers the corruption and both tolerances are tightened to a stated ~60× margin over the measured agreement: axis 1×10−5 m against a measured 1.6×10−7 m, boundary 1×10−7 Wb against a measured 1.4×10−9 Wb.
