Elliptic and harmonic-moment tangent recurrences

The point jet's jaxpr reaches 15 named helpers in the two elliptic modules: _descent, _accumulate, _finite_part, complete_kind, _complete_kind, complete_pole, harmonic_moments, _harmonic_rising_step, _harmonic_ratio_step, harmonic_pole_moments, _pole_forward_step, _pole_backward_step, harmonic_root_moments, cn_pole_moment and sn_pole_moment. It reaches no paired form. Each has a private tangent written as its own recurrence. None calls jax.jvp, jax.grad or jax.jacfwd, and each returns the structure jax.jvp returns. Every tangent compiles within three times its primal. The primal functions are unchanged and bit-identical to the base revision.

The lead ruled on 2026-10-06 to accept scan-form tangents, with the tangent rows held to about 1e-10 relative against jax.jvp of the base and the primal kept bit-identical. The reasons given: the primal quantities (flux, field, booking, residual, null positions) are unchanged, and the Hessian only steers Newton and the implicit tangent, so 1e-10 sits far below every solve tolerance. The exact hand-written tangent of harmonic_moments compiled as slowly as autodiff, 54.1 s against a 1.30 s primal. So complete_kind, harmonic_moments and harmonic_pole_moments now carry their tangents as lax.scan steps over per-order coefficient arrays, with static trip and order counts, and return the primal from the primal function. The helpers that already met the bound keep the unrolled tangent, which uses the arrangement jax.jvp uses for each elementary rule and agrees with it exactly.

The second kind's tangent needs one qualification. At a small complement it is a cancellation of terms of order 1/k'^2, and there jax.jvp of the base returns round-off rather than a derivative. Against the classical dE/dk'^2 = (K - E)/(2 k^2), the base jvp is wrong by 1.4e-10 at complements of 1e-7 and above, and by 4.8e-6 at 1e-12 and above. The scanned tangent is wrong by the same amounts, 1.2e-10 and 4.7e-6. So the difference between the two is the base's own error. The complete_kind row therefore holds the scanned tangent to 1e-9 against jax.jvp for complements of 1e-7 and above, which is 4,096 of the 10,000 samples, where it reads 1.09e-10. Below that, it requires the scan to be no less accurate than the base jvp against the classical derivative. The first kind's tangent agrees to 1.7e-15 on the whole domain.

Every tangent's primal half equals its primal function exactly: 0 for all nine rows, and the separate base-revision bit-identity row passes. When the same primal is compiled inside the jax.jvp program instead, its round-off differs, by 4.4e-8 for harmonic_moments. That comes from XLA's CPU arithmetic depending on the shape of the surrounding program, amplified by the upward recursion near a parameter of 0.99. No primal arithmetic was changed.

The negative control sets NOVA_TANGENT_TRUNCATION=1, which drops one term from each tangent recurrence inside the identity rows. All nine identity rows then fail, against bounds of 1e-9 or exact, with maxima from 3.3e-5 to inf. The truncation rows of the head arm assert the same failure against 1e-9.
