Certificate exterior on the exact analytic support

The prescribed exterior is the analytic state minus the internal flux image of the analytic-density plasma current. When that image is integrated on the production spline clip, the exterior and the map's own booking disagree on the same support, and the disagreement appears as an exterior closure term that can exceed the booking error the map-fidelity gate exists to measure. The fixture now integrates that density on the independently computed analytic-separatrix polygon support instead, so the booked image cancels the analytic-density image the certificate books on the same support and the closure term falls to roundoff.

Two pieces implement this. analytic_support_polygons reuses scripts/prototypes/support/geometry.py:analytic_polygons, the certificate's own exact-support definition, applied to the cell polygons the moment geometry carries; _polygon_current_moments integrates the analytic density over those clipped polygons with the fixture's order-15 fan rule, sign-correcting each ring so holes subtract. exact_current_moments selects that path through support_arm, and the exterior cache identity now carries both the support arm and the content digest of nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py, so replacing the support or the contraction module cannot silently reuse an exterior built on the old clip.

At 550 cells the exact-support exterior closes at 7.06e-15, four orders below the 2.386e-02 booking term, and the map relative sup equals the booking term to every printed digit: the residual the map-fidelity gate now measures is the map's own booking error rather than the exterior mismatch. The same identity holds by construction at every rung, because the fixture's exact-support moment integral and the certificate's analytic_moments are the same order-15 fan rule evaluated on the same analytic_polygons(exact, cells) polygon set; that is an argument, not a measurement, and the 2000 and 5000 rows above remain unmeasured.

The decomposition residual printed for the 550 row is the value obtained before the map's residual-shadow cells are zeroed. The map leaves those cells unevaluated, so both booked terms are masked there before the decomposition is required to reconstruct the map, which is how the prototype forms the same identity.
