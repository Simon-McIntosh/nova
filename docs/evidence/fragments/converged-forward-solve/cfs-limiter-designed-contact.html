One owner for the analytic limiter's designed contact

scripts/analytic_oracle_fixtures/measure.py now exposes designed_contact(wall), the single owner of the limiter's authored contact. It returns the midpoint of the wall edge that crosses the midplane at the largest major radius -- the short vertical edge limiter_contour pins on the outboard supporting line -- as an (R, Z) pair in metres, so the reader depends on the polygon geometry and never on a vertex index. On the weak-rotation carrier the contact reads R = 7.80139579 m, equal to boundary_midplane_radii()[1], with the height at flux rounding; the old (n-1)//2 lookup on the same repaired wall reads the inboard vertex at 3.738 m, 4.063 m away. tests/test_topology_limiter_contact.py's helper now delegates to the fixture, and the two benchmarks that still read the tangency as the (n-1)//2 vertex -- benchmarks/limiter_read_resolution_audit.py's _limiter_node_construction (an explicit-index read) and benchmarks/wall_at_cell_pitch.py's _wall_metrics (the outboard panel) -- now locate the contact through the fixture and pick their node or panel by nearest position. A repeat grep of tests/, benchmarks/ and scripts/ for any further index-based contact reader finds none.

The former 241-to-481 convergence test is replaced by test_weak_contact_position_is_exact_at_both_wall_resolutions, which asserts the 241-node and 481-node contact errors both sit at or below a stated roundoff bound (CONTACT_ROUNDOFF_BOUND_M = 1e-9). The contact lies on a vertical edge, so its radial error is exact by construction and its height error is flux rounding, 2.6e-15 m at 241 nodes and 6.2e-13 m at 481, independent of the panel size; a ratio between the two rungs would compare rounding residuals, not a convergence order the operating point cannot exercise. Measured in per-module fresh processes on the fleet node (job 1277272): the three gate modules test_topology_limiter_contact, test_analytic_oracle_limiter_wall and test_first_moment_centroid_row give 9 / 10 / 3 passed at the head and 9 / 10 / 3 passed at the base revision dcfbaed2a, so the change adds no failure.
