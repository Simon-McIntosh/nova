Map fidelity at the certificate rungs and the map derivative

The map is gs.apply: the booked plasma current imaged through the booked coupling blocks and added to the exterior flux. Evaluated once at the analytic state with no solve, it must reproduce that state within 1e-2 relative sup at the analytic-certificate rungs, in exact mode, on both analytic cases. The exterior is posed on the exact analytic separatrix support so the exterior's own closure residual is at roundoff and the residual the bound measures is the map's booking term rather than a fixture mismatch.

The relative sup is the largest absolute difference over the solve grid, divided by the flux span between the analytic axis and the analytic zero level. The solve grid is the mesh nodes; wall and sample coordinates are carried in the same state vector but are not part of the image comparison. Every row below also carries the booking term, the exterior closure, and the decomposition residual booking + closure - map, which must be zero to roundoff for the decomposition to mean anything.

All six rows are under the 1e-2 bound, and the exterior closure is at roundoff (1e-14) in every one, so each row's residual is its booking term. The bound holds with margin at every rung of both cases: the largest reading is the diverted case's 9.850e-04 at 10,229 cells, a tenth of the bound, and the limited case never leaves 7e-05.

The rung table is a bound check and not a convergence sequence. The diverted case is not monotone in the rung — 5.780e-03, 2.801e-04, 9.850e-04 — because the booking term measures how the mesh's cell polygons close on the analytic separatrix support, which moves with the rung rather than shrinking under it. Every reading stays well inside the bound, and the exterior closure stays at roundoff, so no reading is a fixture artefact.

Booking terms against the earlier fixture

The two diverted rows are directly comparable with the rows recorded before the fixture's exact-support arm and the moment-vectorisation work landed: at 2,098 cells the booking term was 0.005778474116893 and at 5,158 cells 0.000279365986148. Restated against the current fixture they are 0.005780384296774049 and 0.0002801417654969288, relative moves of 3.31e-4 and 2.78e-3. Both moves are far inside the 1e-2 bound, and neither crosses it: the measurement is a restatement of the same quantity, not a new regime.

The map's derivative

The derivative arms compare the map's own tangent, taken with jax.linearize on the map callable, with a central difference of the map at 1e-6 of the flux span. The comparison direction is the map's residual mapped - psi, normalised, which is the direction the map moves the state along towards the fixed point. The boundary-flux arm is the component a padded dummy saddle once doubled: the read maximised the wall level against a zero-flux dummy saddle, so autodiff returned one branch's slope while a central difference returned the average of both.

The gate readings are MAP_JVP_RELATIVE_SUP=2.8861443945634642e-08 and BOUNDARY_JVP=0.97246858630808686 against CENTRAL=0.97246859433637478, a relative difference of 8.2555755220287577e-09.

The negative control restores the clamp in the boundary-flux read, which resolves the wall-level selection at the tie with jnp.where(value >= 0, value, 0). On the state posed exactly at the tie the same measurement reads BOUNDARY_JVP=0 against CENTRAL=0.48623429315398581, a relative difference of 1.0: the select keeps only the wall branch's slope, so the whole boundary-flux response is annihilated instead of averaged. The historical symptom of the same select on the padded dummy saddle was the factor-of-two doubling (−6.91055e-3 AD against −3.45528e-3 central); here the base sits on the kink itself and the loss is total. Either way the run fails by test id test_map_boundary_flux_jvp_matches_central_difference, which is what the control asserts.

How the rows and the derivative are reproduced

The rows cost a carrier build each, so the driver at the foot of tests/test_gs_map_fidelity.py takes one case at one rung in a single case allocation and prints its row; the tests themselves read the row block above and never rebuild a carrier. The derivative arms are ordinary collected tests and carriers a 550-cell build each.

python tests/test_gs_map_fidelity.py --case diverted-single-null --cells 5000
python -m pytest tests/test_gs_map_fidelity.py -k jvp -vv -s
