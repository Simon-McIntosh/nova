The sample contains 131 pairs selected from the largest absolute differences across the nine grid, wall and sample blocks, plus 120 random pairs (seed 9173) spread across those target families and source cells. Random pairs stay at least three source diameters from the expansion point so the fixed Gauss rule does not cross the filament singularity. This is an off-singularity accuracy measurement, not self-cell qualification.

The reference directly integrates the circular-filament elliptic formula on a triangle fan over each authored polygon, using 48×48 and 96×96 Gauss rules.
