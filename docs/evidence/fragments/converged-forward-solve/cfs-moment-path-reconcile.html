Conditioned source-area accumulation

Product commit b54a1af9b131d58a5ecf10771cc2ce508a3be3db replaces subtraction of corner antiderivatives for the three flux rows with signed triangle integrals over the authored polygon. The integrand carries the source offset from the requested moment centre before multiplication and accumulation. Nearby targets are the fan origin; fourth-power Duffy coordinates grade the logarithmic endpoint, while the filament modulus complement is formed directly from local displacements. Distant targets use the area centroid as fan origin. Both paths integrate the same material, with no rectangular or filament replacement.

The graded tensor rule uses 64 radial nodes and two 64-node edge panels; the smooth rule uses 12×12 nodes.
