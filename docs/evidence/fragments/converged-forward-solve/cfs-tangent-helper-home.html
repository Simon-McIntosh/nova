One home for the point jet's tangent helpers

Every shared tangent helper the Biot point jet's jaxpr reaches now has one definition in nova/biot/rangefunction.py, and completeelliptic, elliptic, gradedresidual and momentchannel import them rather than carrying local copies. A tangent is then written in one place and read in one place, and the modules cannot drift apart. The arithmetic order each helper forms is unchanged, so no tangent value changes: every identity row agrees with jax.jvp of the base revision, every truncation row still fails against it, every primal half is bit-identical, the cold-compile ratio holds under 3.0 on the median of seven fresh processes in two separate runs, and tests/biot adds no failing test against the base revision.

The helpers homed in rangefunction.py are _tangent_sum, _tangent_difference, _product_tangent, _quotient_tangent, _scale_tangent, _deflate_step_tangent, _deflate_tangent, _contract_tangent, _harmonic_multiply_tangent and _across_the_range_tangent, each defined exactly once there. The four importing modules were checked for a duplicate definition and carry none.

One helper is a genuinely different computation and was not collapsed into the other. The momentchannel _across_the_range_tangent took only the input tangents (d_bulk, d_near, d_far) and returned the output tangents alone, because the map is linear; the rangefunction namesake takes the primal term beside the tangent and returns both. Rather than fold a tangent-only map into a primal-and-tangent one, the momentchannel helper was renamed _across_the_range_differential, by what it computes, and its two call sites follow. The momentchannel _deflate_tangent, _contract_tangent and _harmonic_multiply_tangent are the same computation as their rangefunction namesakes and differ only in whether the primal returns beside the tangent; those three were collapsed into one definition each, and the call sites now take the pair.
