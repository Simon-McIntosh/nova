Moment-channel and graded-residual tangents

This record covers seven functions. In nova.biot.momentchannel: factorise, _pole_contraction at both ends of the range, and Channel.across. In nova.biot.gradedresidual: graded_residual, _regularised and _model_integral. Each now has a private tangent that returns the structure jax.jvp returns, with respect to all of its floating inputs. None of them calls jax.jvp, jax.grad or jax.jacfwd. Each is a closed form or an unrolled recurrence: the Clenshaw deflation inside the pole contraction carries its tangent as its own downward recurrence. Every elementary rule is applied in the order jax.jvp applies it, so terms whose two halves cancel round identically. No primal line changed: the two modules gain 0 deleted and 429 added lines.

The domains span what the jet reaches. Factorisations take x y coefficients from 1e-12 to 1e6, with 10 percent exactly zero for a vertical edge. Their end values run over the same decades, and 5 percent of them are equal, the level-target case. A negative coefficient is excluded because it gives negative shifts, which the jet never forms; there the base primal itself loses every digit. Pole shifts run from 1e-9 to 1e12, including zero, the exact switch value and samples within 1e-3 of it, and the ceiling. Graded panels cover a target on a corner, level with a corner, or on its radius, plus finite arcs. Where the base jax.jvp is not finite (0.42 percent of factorise samples, at a zero end value, and 1.00 percent of graded-residual samples, at a 1e-300 radius), the tangent is non-finite in the same way and the row counts that as agreement. Those samples were not checked against a higher-precision reference.

For the negative control, NOVA_TANGENT_TRUNCATION=1 drops one term from each tangent inside the identity rows. All seven identity rows then fail the 1e-13 bound, with maxima from 82 to infinity.

Not done in this record: the polygonanalytic vertex and edge terms (_Vertex.arsinh_terms, _Vertex.flux_moment_residuals, _first_residual, _Edge.terms and _second_residual), Channel.split with its seeds and families, and the enumeration of the packed_analytic_moments jaxpr. The graded-residual tangent takes a pieces_tangent callable, so the vertex and edge residuals reuse it once their integrands' tangents are written.
