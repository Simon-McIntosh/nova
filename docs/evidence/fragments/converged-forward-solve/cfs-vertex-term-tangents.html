Tangents of the polygon-analytic corner terms

Private tangents now exist for every corner quantity the point jet differentiates in nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py: _vertex_tangent (the _Vertex construction: geometry, modulus and complement, harmonic and root moment stacks, the ring denominator's split with both pole seeds and families), _first_residual_tangent, _arsinh_terms_tangent, _against_first_arsinh_tangent, _flux_moment_residuals_tangent and _horizontal_flux_line_moments_tangent. Each is composed from the landed elliptic, moment-channel, graded-residual and range-function tangents with no jax.jvp, jax.grad or jax.jacfwd of the base, and returns the primal method's own result beside a tangent of the same structure. The corner_part closures are host-only NumPy paths of polygon_analytic_greens, polygon_analytic_flux_moments and _central_field_moments_direct; the packed jet builds its _Vertex inline, so _vertex_tangent is the tangent of that construction and no closure-specific tangent was written.

Identity, control and compile

On 10 000 corner geometries and targets (corners and targets a centimetre to twenty metres in radius over forty metres of height; 4 000 targets beside a corner down to a picometre, 600 level with it, 600 on its radius, 1 500 beside an edge leaving it; no sample masked; the base jax.jvp finite on every sample), each tangent's primal half is bit-identical to the base revision's primal compiled in the same program. Each series (a moment stack, a pole family, a harmonic bulk) is measured normwise against its own largest order, and the root moments against the moment tangents they are formed from, because the base revision's own moment stack differs by 2.9e-8 between two compiled programs at its top order and a root-moment tangent cancels from 1e8 to 1e-8 beside a corner. The construction tangent agrees to 1.86e-11 with 100 percent covered at the scan bound 1e-9. arsinh_terms does not yet meet the measure: 1.87e-6 at worst, 98.50 percent covered, every one of the 149 failing samples in the radial component for a target within about 1e-9 m of the corner; which instrument is off there is unresolved, and the next step is an mpmath reference on those samples. The rows for against_first_arsinh, flux_moment_residuals and horizontal_flux_line_moments were still running when the time budget ran out (head-identity.log in the run directory).

Negative control: with the right factor's term dropped from the corner product rule (NOVA_TANGENT_TRUNCATION=1), the construction fails at 2.82e12 and arsinh_terms at 1.63e6, with 0 percent covered (negative-control.log).

Cold compile, fastest of three fresh cache-disabled processes, tangent over primal: construction 2.31 (13 864 against 4 840 expanded equations), arsinh_terms 1.74, against_first_arsinh 2.21, flux_moment_residuals 2.37, horizontal_flux_line_moments 1.88, all at or below three (compile-arsinh.log, compile-all.log).
