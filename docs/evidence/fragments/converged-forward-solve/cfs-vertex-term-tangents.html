Tangents of the polygon-analytic corner terms

Private tangents now exist for every corner quantity the point jet differentiates in nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py: _vertex_tangent (the _Vertex construction: geometry, modulus and complement, harmonic and root moment stacks, the ring denominator's split with both pole seeds and families), _first_residual_tangent, _arsinh_terms_tangent, _against_first_arsinh_tangent, _flux_moment_residuals_tangent and _horizontal_flux_line_moments_tangent. Each is composed from the landed elliptic, moment-channel, graded-residual and range-function tangents with no jax.jvp, jax.grad or jax.jacfwd of the base, and returns the primal method's own result beside a tangent of the same structure. The corner_part closures are host-only NumPy paths of polygon_analytic_greens, polygon_analytic_flux_moments and _central_field_moments_direct; the packed jet builds its _Vertex inline, so _vertex_tangent is the tangent of that construction and no closure-specific tangent was written.

Staged calls in the jaxpr of packed_analytic_moments for the two source kinds the jet takes, a section with no horizontal edge and one whose horizontal edges are dropped (log: jaxpr-census.log in the run directory). _Vertex methods are not staged and inline into these calls. Identity, control and compile

Domain: 10 000 corner geometries and targets, with corners and targets a centimetre to twenty metres in radius over forty metres of height. It includes 4 000 targets beside a corner down to a picometre, 600 level with it, 600 on its radius and 1 500 beside an edge leaving it. No sample is masked, and the base jax.jvp is finite on every sample. On the H200 (job 1281231, h200-final.log in the run directory; the three rows that did not finish on CPU first ran there as job 1281224, h200-rows.log), every tangent agrees with jax.jvp of the base element for element. The maximum relative difference is 0.000e+00 for the construction, arsinh_terms, against_first_arsinh, flux_moment_residuals and horizontal_flux_line_moments, so the elementwise and normwise maxima coincide, no element fails, and coverage is 100 percent at the scan bound 1e-9. That zero is not an empty instrument: the median magnitude of the base tangent on those rows is 1.10e-3, 3.68, 2.36, 13.4 and 9.37, and the truncated rows below differ by up to 3.8e10. Every primal half is bit-identical to the base primal compiled in the same program.

On CPU the two programs fuse differently, and the per-series normwise metric is provisional, because the locked tangent-identity-tolerance decision is stated per value. The base-to-base floor is measured between two compiled programs of the base itself: the base primal jitted alone, and the primal half of the jitted jax.jvp of the base. They differ by up to 2.21e-8 relative, in moments[42] (917 elements), moments[41] (1.48e-8) and root_moments[41] (1.25e-8). Every element where the normwise metric passes and the elementwise one fails is in the construction row: 31 299 elements in total. Of those, 161 lie in the eight highest orders of moments (37 samples, at most 1.94e-8, inside the base-to-base floor). The other 31 138 lie in root_moments across 42 orders and 1 729 samples, all beside a corner, where the root-moment tangent cancels from about 1e8 down to about 1e-8. The full per-order list with sample indices is metric-audit.json in the run directory. On CPU the construction meets 1.86e-11 normwise, while arsinh_terms reached 1.87e-6 on 149 near-corner samples (head-identity.log). The H200 rows above show both are compilation effects: there the same comparison is exact.

Near a corner, jax.jvp of the base is not ground truth. The radial arsinh_terms component is 4 r times the integral over a quarter turn of cos^2(2a) arsinh(N/W). A 50-digit mpmath reference of that integral, differentiated analytically under the integral sign in a process that never imports jax, reproduces the base primal to 1.19e-11 on the 149 CPU-failing samples. A 50-digit central difference confirms its derivative, for example 13.6086557744502 on sample 2869 (mpmath-difference-check.log). Against it, the hand tangent and the base jax.jvp are both off by up to 9.91e-2 there, while they agree with each other to 1.87e-6 on CPU (arsinh-reference.json). Neither of the two branches the ruling anticipated holds: the hand tangent reproduces the base program's own derivative, and that derivative leaves the exact integral's because the 128-node graded quadrature clips its layer width at its floor when the target is within about 1e-6 of the corner radius. The arsinh_terms identity row therefore judges the 8 811 samples farther than 2e-6 of the corner radius against jax.jvp of the base. The 1 189 nearer samples are judged against the reference, less those exactly level with the corner or on its radius, where the integral has a |u| or |offset| kink and no two-sided derivative. Over all 1 189 near-corner samples (none is exactly level with its corner or on its radius) the reference reproduces the base primal to 1.19e-11. The hand tangent is within 1e-9 of it on 688 samples and at worst 1.07 relative from it; the base jax.jvp is at worst 1.07 from it as well; hand and base agree to 1.87e-6 on CPU and exactly on the H200 (near-corner-reference.json, 146 s on 12 CPU processes). The row asserting the hand tangent within 1e-9 of the reference is therefore a strict expected failure: it records a defect of the base program's derivative near a corner, which a tangent that reproduces the base program cannot meet. The near-corner rows did not finish on the H200 (job 1281231 ended in TIMEOUT after the five identity rows had passed), because the probe's process pool, under the forkserver start method Python 3.14 uses by default, could not resolve a function defined in a -c main; an explicit fork context fixes it, and those rows ran on CPU in place (near-corner-rows.log).

Negative control: the right factor's term is dropped from the corner product rule. Under NOVA_TANGENT_TRUNCATION=1 the identity fails for the construction at 2.82e12 and for arsinh_terms at 1.63e6 with 0 percent covered (negative-control.log, CPU). On the H200 the truncated rows fail at 3.23e4 for against_first_arsinh, 1.23e3 for flux_moment_residuals and 3.77e10 for horizontal_flux_line_moments.

Cold compile on CPU, fastest of three fresh cache-disabled processes, tangent over primal: construction 2.31 (13 864 against 4 840 expanded equations), arsinh_terms 1.74, against_first_arsinh 2.21, flux_moment_residuals 2.37 and horizontal_flux_line_moments 1.88, all at or below three (compile-arsinh.log).
