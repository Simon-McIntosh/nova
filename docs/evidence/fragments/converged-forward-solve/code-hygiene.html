Rulings on the two reported clauses

  • separatrix_clip.padded_polynomial_current_moments stays. The clause asked that it be deleted as a duplicate reduction and its consumers rerouted to the owner. Reading the body falsifies the premise: the function localises to centroid and scale, calls the owner, and contracts a complete polynomial basis of degree two through nine against the returned table. It is the localise-and-contract layer over the owner, not a second reduction, and it is the scripts-facing API for complete bases above degree two — the degree at which the closed-form route the package already carries stops covering the basis. Deleting it would either remove that route (used at degree nine by density_moment_projection and density_forcing_ladder) or inline the same body into eight call sites. The coordinator accepted the finding and the function is unchanged.
  • polygon_density_moments and its module stay. The duplicate name is gone; the contraction the module exposes is what benchmarks/exact_clip_closed_form_floor.py measures against the fan reference and the locked per-row budget. That benchmark is a live measurement of the surviving owner route, and its consumer outside this node's fence (docs/figures/exact-clip-moment-quadrature/) imports the benchmark module, so deleting the contraction would delete a measurement rather than a duplicate.
  • The certificate-row reconciliation moves out of this node. It is a two-stage pipeline: scripts/prototypes/support/measure.py writes the {kind}-{cells}-fixture.json identity files and scripts/prototypes/support/book.py books against them, with a GPU lane required and a second checkout at the merge under test for the reference rows. None of the fixture files exist in the tree, and the adjacent read gate was the subject of a dedicated node. The coordinator moved it, together with the exterior fixture's move to the support owner, to a node of its own; it is not attempted here.
