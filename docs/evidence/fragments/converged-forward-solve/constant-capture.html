Operands are program arguments, not captured constants

The Newton factor programs were compiled from jax.linearize over concrete operands. A linearization taken over concrete values embeds every operand array as a literal in the jaxpr it returns, and the solve then compiles one factor program per correction with jax.jit(factor). Each compiled program therefore carried its own copy of the window rows and the interior jets, so the copies multiplied with every step: the cut-cell split node measured 84.36GB total of captured constants when the limited 550 cold solve lowered, with float64[10,16,1177600] (1.51 GB) repeating about twenty-five times. The report's frames name the wall-events step at topology.py:2847, reached through program.py:46 (FusedField.evaluate) and fixed_points.py:294 (CachedField.evaluate) — the path along which the closed-operand window rows are read.

The tangent map built by operand_tangent in scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production/solver.py now takes the operands as arguments, so the lowering reads them as program parameters and one compiled program serves every correction. The same treatment covers the one-off read and booking programs, which had closed over the field and the reading.

tests/test_solve_operand_capture.py pins the distinction by measuring the constant bytes of a lowered tangent program. Its positive control linearizes over a concrete operand and requires the instrument to see at least half that operand's bytes as constants, so a green result cannot come from an instrument that never reports a capture.

Measure

Measured through the certificate's own production entry, python -m scripts.prototypes.fast_map_production.solver --kind limited --cells 550 --method gmres --max-steps 1 --line-search --diagnose-step --no-panel, with JAX_CAPTURED_CONSTANTS_REPORT_FRAMES=32, in one H200 job (1295107) that ran git archives of both revisions on the same device. At the base 48d7adda6 the lowering reports 4.20GB total: float64[121,13,9,3,553,3,6] at 3.38 GB, float64[2135,553,3,6] at 170.01 MB, float64[9954,553,3] at 132.11 MB, float64[121,2,3,553,3,6] at 57.81 MB and float64[553,7,553,3] at 51.38 MB. The 3.38 GB capture is created at topology.py:2849 in _wall_events.one.step, reached through program.py:42 (FusedField.evaluate) and fixed_points.py:294 (CachedField.evaluate) — exactly the chain the defect named. At the tip 1f2cfd143 the same row reports no captured constants at all: its lowering produces no large-constants warning, which fires only above JAX_CAPTURED_CONSTANTS_WARN_BYTES (2,000,000,000 by default), so the tip's per-lowering capture is under 2 GB against the base's 4.20 GB and the 3.38 GB window-rows constant is gone.

The same two runs carry the cost. Both reach the first Newton correction and then refuse the solve on its residual, as they must at --max-steps 1, and the two residuals agree to round-off: 0.009258578737131882 at the base and 0.009258578737133318 at the tip, a relative difference of 1.6e-13 on a value thirteen orders above the 1e-12 limit. Peak host RSS falls from 28.35 GiB to 5.40 GiB (5.25×) and the wall from 333 s to 216 s. Device memory is not reported here: the job ran with XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_PREALLOCATE=false, so the device holds only what the run demands, and the brief's device figures belong to the certificate rows rather than to this lowering measurement.

The 84 GB does not reproduce at main, and its sites are not main's. The defect's 84.36GB total was measured on the cut-cell node's branch over a full cold 550 solve. Through main's production entry at one correction the total is 4.20 GB, so the 84 GB is the sum over a cold solve's many per-correction factor compiles rather than one lowering: the per-correction repetition is the mechanism, and it is the one the tangent change removes. What a converged solve reaches further along is carried by the cut-cell node's branch; no booking geometry or quadrature was touched here.

The row the certificate's candidate.py entry runs was measured too, and it is a different driver: the limited 550 row gives 4.68GB total at the base (48d7adda6) and at the tip (0d1dbf435) on that entry, with float64[121,13,9,3,610,3,6] at 3.73 GB, float64[2385,610,3,6] at 209.50 MB, float64[10980,610,3] at 160.75 MB, float64[121,2,3,610,3,6] at 63.77 MB and float64[610,7,610,3] at 62.51 MB; the diverted row gives 4.67GB at the base. Its frame report names the closed-over linearization as scripts/prototypes/diverted_stall/measure.py:542-543 (run.linear_step), reached through :232 (run.equation) and :209 (run.mapped): candidate.py rebinds measure.map_moments, so the cold solve runs there, not in fast_map_production/solver.py.
