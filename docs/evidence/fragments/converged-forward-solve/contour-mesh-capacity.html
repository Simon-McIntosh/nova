Size the topology contour mesh from its geometry

Defect (reproduced at the base revision). TopologyGeometry.from_cells sized the contour mesh as vertex_capacity = len(tree_centres) + len(points) + 1, and build_contour_mesh blanked every index when that was exceeded, returning a mesh with zero valid vertices. The read then failed at nova/equilibrium/topology.py:2483 with IndexError: index -1 is out of bounds for axis 0 with size 0. Measured on the imas-efit boundary traces at the coarsest lattice: required 247 vertices against a declared capacity of 231, on all eight records, so all eight rows were unread.

Fix

The carrier is now sized from what it must hold. build_contour_mesh leaves a capacity unset to size it from the carrier the geometry actually produces — the tree centres plus every wall vertex the clipped triangulation generates — so a wide wall cannot overflow a formula. A capacity that is declared is a ceiling: a larger carrier raises ContourMeshOverflow naming both the required and the declared count, rather than blanking every index. from_cells no longer passes the fixed formula and refuses a carrier that admits no valid vertex.

Measure. Every record builds a mesh whose valid vertex count is below its capacity, at every lattice resolution in use. The refusal is exercised by forcing a capacity below the geometry: contour mesh vertex capacity 4 is below the 106 the geometry holds.

Gate

The focused pair — tests/equilibrium/test_topology_contour_mesh_capacity.py and tests/test_contour_tree_mesh.py — passes at head: 17 passed in 12.21 s. The new wide-wall test does not pass at the base revision, where the geometry required 247 vertices against a declared capacity of 231 and the read raised IndexError.

The three-arm regression gate over those two files and tests/equilibrium/test_topology_read.py ran as one job — base archive, head worktree, and a control tree with the fixed capacity formula restored — each arm a fresh process writing its own log with an EXIT= line. Base: 5 failed, 34 passed, 4 skipped, 9 errors. Head: 5 failed, 43 passed, 4 skipped, 7 errors. Control: 13 failed, 35 passed, 4 skipped, 7 errors.

The five failures and seven errors in test_topology_read.py are the same twelve ids in base and head, so this change adds none of them — several are the compute node's missing CUDA and four rows skip for want of a certified machine cache, including test_census_keeps_the_wall_contact_saddle_across_the_adjacent_pair, which is unexercised here rather than passing or failing. The head arm's nine extra passes are exactly the new file's eight wide-wall records and its overflow test; at base that file cannot import, since ContourMeshOverflow does not exist there. Restoring the fixed formula turns all eight wide-wall records red and leaves every other row unchanged.

The field-swap prototype's capacity refit is retired

With the read sizing its own carrier, the field-swap prototype's refit is dead code. _fitted_capacity, fitted_geometry and CAPACITY_GROWTH are deleted from scripts/prototypes/efit_field_swap/crosscheck.py and from its other importer scripts/prototypes/efit_maximin_certificate/adapter_probe.py; both now build the geometry through TopologyGeometry.from_cells directly. The refitted-capacity column is removed from the cross-check row, the adapter probe output and the TCV read row rather than left hard-coded, and test_a_wide_wall_record_gets_a_fitted_mesh is replaced by test_a_wide_wall_record_meshes_through_the_plain_read, which meshes the same wide-wall record through the plain read.

No reference to the deleted names remains in scripts/ or tests/. The named test files ran as two arms in fresh processes. Base (74c613d71): 2 failed, 58 passed. Head (124ad219f): 1 failed, 59 passed. The head's only failure, the TCV limited-slice test, is also the base's — a ReferenceSignError from the orientation guard, not this change — so no failure is added. The negative control restores the fixed capacity formula in a scratch copy of topology.py; the new test then fails with ContourMeshOverflow: contour mesh vertex capacity 231 is below the 247 the geometry holds. scripts/prototypes/tcv_liuqe_known_answer/measure.py drops its write-only fitted column, beyond the brief's two named files, so no caller of the removed field is left broken.
