The composite cut-cell support is built and measured, and it over-books

Each cut cell now books one binary support split at the effective-disk boundary about the selected saddle: the normal-form lobe inside the disk and the ordinary quadratic cut outside it, both integrated on the shared clip-quadrature nodes with one density contract, so neither a scalar blend nor a whole-cell replacement chosen by centre distance, crossing count or ordinary validity remains in the booking path. The read carries the disk position and radius, the signed-flux convention, and a named refusal (none, owner-not-contained, saddle-capacity-overflow) folded into valid, so a read refuses rather than switching support or truncating cells. Both booking consumers, ordinary_cut.bell_moments and the prototype _saddle_moments, share the one owner in nova/equilibrium/current.py.

The new focused test tests/equilibrium/test_cut_cell_split.py sweeps a disk across a square support and requires the booked moments to stay continuous to 1e-12 as the disk enters and leaves, having first proved by intersection measure that the disk really did enter and leave. Under the declared mutation --force-conic-right the same assertion fails on element 0 of the moment triple, [8.24845539, 1.28775801, 1.17756890e-04] against the reference [8.0, 1.33333333, 9.99200722e-16]. The green arm passes the same assertion. The mutation is applied literally and both logs are kept.

The first gate submission found that the head tip it archived could not run its own consumer path at all: composite_cut_moments still carried the pre-split seven-argument signature while both consumers call it with sixteen positional arguments, so selected received the profile and _normal_rings raised TypeError: 'RotatingDomainProfile' object is not subscriptable. The corrected eight-parameter owner was committed and both gates resubmitted at the fixed tip. The focused test had stayed green across the broken tip because it drives _disk_split_moments directly and never enters that signature, and no test calls the owner through a consumer's argument list.

At the fixed tip the paired suites report two added failures, and one is a real regression. test_census_keeps_the_wall_contact_saddle_across_the_adjacent_pair passes in the base arm (33 passed, 1446.90 s) and fails in the head arm (1 failed, 32 passed, 1739.11 s) with booked current 23.126692873310773 disagrees with quadrature 12.432355242424107, against a two percent tolerance, after the independent flood, the independent stationary-point polish and the first-separator checks have each already passed. The booking therefore claims current the field does not carry, and the failure is isolated to how much current the booking claims rather than to the topology the read recovered.

Where the over-claim comes from was measured directly, by re-running the booking on the tie pair with the disk radius used as a toggle: an effectively infinite radius leaves only the normal-form piece and zero leaves only the ordinary-cut piece. At offset 0.1732 the independent quadrature is 12.4324 against a composite of 23.1267, a normal-form-only 24.4154 and an ordinary-only 13.4881; at offset 0.17325 the same three read 17.7620, 18.7603 and 11.5323 against a quadrature of 8.7939. The composite reproduces the sampler's own booked current to 3e-13, so the reproduction is faithful before it is toggled. The over-claim is therefore the normal-form piece, at about twice the admitted current, and not the ordinary piece, which over-books by roughly eight and thirty-one percent on its own.

The mechanism is a builder swap rather than an arithmetical error. The base consumer chose between current._closed_normal_rings, the closed normal lobe the read admits, and current._normal_rings; this composite uses _normal_rings alone for its inside piece, so open rings are integrated whole over a region the closed lobe would have bounded. The measurement above is what separates that reading from the obvious one: the ordinary cut outside the disk, which a first look blames, is the closer of the two pieces to the admitted current.

The node ends blocked. The change is committed and its focused test is green with a valid negative control, but it is not landed: the inside piece must be built from the closed normal lobe the read admits, the question of whether the outside piece must additionally be intersected with the confined component is open, and the composite must be traced once from the booked cells only so it can be lowered. The disk boundary is resolved by quadrature-node density masking rather than by explicit arc edges, so the ruling's clause requiring circle arcs closing mismatched cuts to be explicit support edges is not implemented.
