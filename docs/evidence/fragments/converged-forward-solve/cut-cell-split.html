The composite cut-cell support books at base accuracy, at a fraction of the memory, and the 550 solve still does not converge

Each cut cell now books one binary support split at the effective-disk boundary about the selected saddle: the normal-form lobe inside the disk and the ordinary quadratic cut outside it, both integrated on the shared clip-quadrature nodes with one density contract, so neither a scalar blend nor a whole-cell replacement chosen by centre distance, crossing count or ordinary validity remains in the booking path. Each conic fragment is booked compositely: one ring per fragment, split into sub-intervals that each end on an interior strip break of that fragment, so a fragment crossing several breaks is integrated over its own pieces instead of across a corner. The read carries the disk position and radius, the signed-flux convention, and a named refusal (none, owner-not-contained, saddle-capacity-overflow) folded into valid, so a read refuses rather than switching support or truncating cells. Both booking consumers, ordinary_cut.bell_moments and the prototype _saddle_moments, share the one owner in nova/equilibrium/current.py.

The composite booking reproduces the base booking's accuracy rather than trading it. At the 553 realised cells of the limited 550 certificate row the clip current error is 2.3926218373262417e-11 against the base's accepted 2.3927324327703263e-11, and the clip first-moment error is 7.441770346967406e-12 against 7.441934348012688e-12: unchanged to four figures (2.393e-11 and 7.442e-12), both very slightly smaller, which is what re-integrating the widest fragments on their own breaks should do. The net current relative error is exactly 0.0 and the private current is zero. The clip clamp stayed at 8.0e-06 on cell 300, the cell the earlier degraded booking moved, and cell 300's own cell moment now agrees with a 2048-segment reference to 6.3e-06 relative at the booked 512 segments and 4.9e-07 at 2048, against 2.4e-02 when the base booked the same cell on 8 segments (gates/clip-d13-b-frag-new.log, gates/book-d15/limited-550.json).

The cold residual is unchanged at the same state and is reproducible rather than backend-dependent. Independent runs of the certificate's own limited 550 row report 0.028751188658923062 to 0.028751188658923094, a spread of about 4e-15 relative, and the composite tip's value 0.028751188658923076 sits inside it against the base's 0.028751188658923073. This matters because the span-only booking it superseded moved 15 per cent between backends, which is why that earlier attempt was rejected and this one accepted: the per-strip booking is backend-portable.

The number of static subdivision slots is measured, not guessed, and the measurement inverted the premise it was given. At the certificate state the 492 live conic fragments span: 4 fragments cross 2 strips, 415 cross 3, 35 cross 4, 8 cross 5, 22 cross 6, 6 cross 7, and 2 cross 9 (fragment 115 is the widest); 488 need more than 2, 73 more than 3, 38 more than 4, maximum 9. At the old static count of 8 those two widest fragments had their remainder folded silently into the last interval and integrated across an interior break, and no slot anywhere carried zero width, so a smaller count would have cost accuracy rather than memory. Quadrature.subdivisions is therefore 9, from that maximum, and a fragment that crosses more breaks than the count now refuses to book by name instead of absorbing its remainder into one interval (gates/probe-d15-span.log).

The booking change also removed the constant capture that had been killing every solve at this size. Lowering the same program cold at both arms, with a 1 MB reporting threshold, the base arm captures 84.38 GB at its second lowering, its largest single constant float64[121,13,9,3,553,3,6] at 3.38 GB, and dies of host memory inside the 120 GB limit (SLURM OUT_OF_MEMORY, exit 137, no traceback of its own). The composite tip captures 3.10 MB over the whole run, one constant float64[553,2,12,13,2] at 2.76 MB (gates/capture-d14-f-tip.log, gates/capture-d14-d-tip.log).

With the capture gone the remaining blocker was the step, not the booking: storing a jax.linearize of this program costs 90.95 GiB of device residency before any step executes, against a 99.99 GiB peak, and three memory fractions (0.75, 0.98, 1.0) were all exhausted by it. The step now builds each matrix-vector product from a forward-mode push at the current point, jax.jvp(equation_at, (argument,), (vector,))[1], whose compiled operator is 1,071,436,139 bytes (1.07 GB) and whose probe peaked at 2.27 GiB. GMRES then converged the linear solve in 13 iterations at the first correction and 15 at the second.

Both corrections at that step are accepted, with residual 0.028751188658923241 → 0.016403546971928286 → 0.006054264986508195, and the Taylor slope read from history[i].step_diagnosis.rows[-2:] at a scale ratio of four is 1.992 at correction 1 (7.684848715367218e-07 → 4.854158204287357e-08) and 1.984 at correction 2 (4.4835377039051166e-10 → 2.8659047668907512e-11). Both are second order, which is the order the composite booking was built to deliver.

The certificate's own command does not converge at this tip. Run at its test value of twelve corrections on the limited 550 row (--line-search --diagnose-step), the solve takes six corrections and then stalls: residuals 0.0164035, 0.00605426, 0.00586498, 0.00568162, 0.00568127, 0.00568110, with accepted line-search scales 0.5, 0.5, 0.03125, 0.03125, none, none. It stops at 5.68e-3, five orders above the certificate's 1e-12, and the qualification gate refuses it on that residual. The descent is real but it flattens: the last two corrections move the residual by 3e-7 and 2e-7, which is a stalled iteration rather than a slow one (gates/converge-d16-tip.json).

The stall is caused by a non-finite trial state, and the site is now named. The full-step merit is NaN at line-search scale 1.0 in every correction, and the set of NaN scales grows through the run (1.0; then 1.0 and 0.0625; then 0.25; then 0.5; then 0.125), while the other scales stay finite. Evaluated under JAX's nan check at the trial point, the first non-finite primitive is nova/equilibrium/topology.py:2024, in _normal_form_cell_fragments, the line that writes the fragment area as jnp.where(valid, jnp.maximum(area, 0.0), jnp.nan): when the normal form is not valid the fragment area is written as NaN rather than as an inadmissible value, and it propagates through kernel.read and the moment map into the step. The route is solver.linear_step → equation → mapped → program.map_moments → kernel.read → vmap(topology._normal_form_cell_fragments) (gates/nan-d16-tip.log). This base predates the repair that returns an admissibility flag in place of the NaN; that repair belongs to a separate node and is not made here.

How the booking reached this point is worth keeping, because the first composite attempt over-booked and the measurement that found it still reads clearly. At the tie pair the independent quadrature is 12.4324 against a composite of 23.1267, a normal-form-only 24.4154 and an ordinary-only 13.4881, and at the adjacent offset 8.7939 against 17.7620, 18.7603 and 11.5323: the composite reproduced the sampler's own booked current to 3e-13 before it was toggled, and the over-claim was the open normal-form piece at about twice the admitted current, not the ordinary cut outside the disk, which was the closer of the two. The base consumer used the closed normal lobe the read admits, which books 12.463979 and 8.822742 on those offsets, 0.25 and 0.33 percent from quadrature. The per-fragment span booking measured that over-claim out: it is what restored the per-strip accuracy, and it is the reason the clip errors above sit at the base's values rather than at twice them.

An earlier gate submission also caught a signature fault that the focused test could not see: composite_cut_moments still carried the pre-split seven-argument signature while both consumers call it with sixteen positional arguments, so selected received the profile and _normal_rings raised TypeError: 'RotatingDomainProfile' object is not subscriptable. The focused test had stayed green across the broken tip because it drives _disk_split_moments directly and never enters that signature, and no test calls the owner through a consumer's argument list.

The node lands what it has and the solve's stall is handed on. Landed: the composite per-fragment booking with its measured nine slots and named over-span refusal, the removal of the 84 GB constant capture, and the forward-mode step that makes this state fit on one card at 1.07 GB. Not met: the certificate's 550 solve does not converge at this tip, stalling at 5.68e-3, and the cause is the normal-form fragment area written as jnp.nan when the form is not valid at topology.py:2024. That repair is a separate node's, from main, and is recorded here as a follow-on rather than fixed on this branch. The disk boundary is still resolved by quadrature-node density masking rather than by explicit arc edges, so the clause requiring circle arcs closing mismatched cuts to be explicit support edges also remains unimplemented.
