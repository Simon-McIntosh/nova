Escape-level maximin certificates

Three same-axis rows of the escape-level comparison are settled by certificates on each record's own scipy spline. The maximin of polarity·ψ from the axis to a target is the descending union-find merge level; an attaining path is a breadth-first path inside that superlevel set. The maximin reproduces imas-efit's escape level to 6 significant figures on all three rows; imas-efit is right on all three.

  • mastu-fullPulse-s1-4-5-B: maximin axis→nova contact (0.33280, 1.30309) is 0.0196524, at the X-point (0.57376, 1.12305), 8.8e-4 below nova's level 0.020531. A blocking cut exists at nova's level, so nova's contact is unreachable there.
  • synthetic_crossing_a: maximin axis→(0.85, 0.2) is 0.0142147, attaining imas-efit's level; no cut blocks it and nova's 0.011338 is an underestimate.
  • synthetic_crossing_b: maximin axis→(0.85, 0.2) is 0.0125771, attaining imas-efit's level; nova's 0.011404 is an underestimate.
