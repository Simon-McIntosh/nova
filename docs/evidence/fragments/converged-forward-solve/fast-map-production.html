Fast fixed-shape map evidence

Measured research record

The corrected read (branchless wall candidate) and single-owner booking are implemented under scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production and measured on the H200. On the diverted carrier the cold solve is run at two ladder rungs and converges at neither: at 250 requested cells (282 hex nodes) it descends from a scaled residual of 1.798960437380231 to 1.0208587667876892 over ten accepted dense corrections, and at 550 requested cells (606 hex nodes) from 1.8974674535249814 to 1.230818e+00 over twelve; the stalled iterate at 282 nodes is not stationary (gradient ratio 0.407) and a finite Newton step raises the residual to 0.278865e+02. Both stalls stay in boundary class 0 with no booked saddle cell. The same pair converges the limited carrier at both 289 nodes (three corrections to 3.996e-15) and 610 nodes (two corrections to 4.104338e-13), and a step-scale scan from the converged 289-node state stays within 9.0e-16 of roundoff along the Newton direction. The unfixed-pair control stalls after ten corrections at 2.684581554642e-03 with boundary class 1 and one booked saddle cell, against 1.0208587667876892 with class 0 and no saddle cell for the corrected pair: restoring the uncorrected read and booking lowers the residual 380-fold, so on this carrier the corrected mechanisms are a regression. Isolating the cause, restoring the wall candidate's boolean eligibility alone recovers the control's reference residual (4.696461790766224e-03 against 4.696461790759203e-03) and its stall (2.6898417865827873e-03 against 2.684581554642e-03) in the control's boundary class, while restoring the normal-form set's diverted condition alone moves the stall only to 9.350137856046135e-01 and never leaves class 0. The branchless wall candidate is therefore the whole regression on this carrier and the normal-form condition a second-order term. The diverted carrier does not reach 1e-12 at any measured size, so the node's convergence claim is not met; the 2098-cell rung and the spec's random-direction scans, warm neighbours, chord batch and state sweep remain unmeasured.
