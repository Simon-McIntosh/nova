Limited 550-cell carrier geometry acceptance

The reported refusal of the limited 550-cell certificate carrier at AtomicCellMesh.from_cells does not reproduce at base 6ccf43de9a42707444627079ac5da06b097d9c74. The carrier at the coarse certificate rung is the requested -500 hex generator on the weak-rotation (limited) case, realising 553 cells. Four separate fresh CPU processes, each with x64 enabled by configure_dtypes() before any array was built, drove the carrier through every geometry owner on the path. All four completed, so no predicate was found that refuses the carrier and no product repair was warranted; the missing regression test that pins this acceptance was added instead.

The predicates that AtomicCellMesh.from_cells can raise are _normalise_cells's three shape/finiteness/area checks, the zero-length-edge check, the fewer-than-three-vertices check after atomisation, and the centroid shape check. None fired: the refusal is not reproducible from any of them here. Because no predicate names any cell, the recorded refusal is not a correct refusal of a malformed carrier observed at this base; it is either a stale observation from a different revision or a defect that needs its own reproduction before a repair can be aimed.

The certificate row itself reaches the solver. Re-rendering the stored row for weak-rotation-reactor-static at requested_cells=-500 reports qualification=unqualified and terminal_residual=0.0012464509730622084, tabled here and not gated, per the brief.

The added regression, tests/test_limited_550_carrier.py, asserts the realised cell count, that the atomiser returns one finite centroid per carrier cell with at least three vertices each. It also asserts that the topology geometry builds one centre, one vertex-count row and one positive area per cell, and that the forward operator carries one moment cell per carrier cell. A future change that starts refusing this carrier fails by the owner's name rather than as an obscure downstream error. This is a guard added where the behaviour is already correct, not a repair.

Both suite arms ran the same four files in fresh processes, one per file, each with its own basetemp, on the same fleet node and CPU lane. Base 6ccf43de9: 4, 29, 19 and 8 passed, EXIT=0. Head ea95f45c7 (the test commit): 4, 29, 19 and 8 passed, EXIT=0. Added failures: 0, added ids: none. The carrier file was then marked slow with a 900 s per-test bound, and that arm was re-run over the same file on both sides: base 6ccf43de9 and head 348f545f6 each report 4 passed, EXIT=0, so marking the module cost no coverage. The negative control is the declared mutation applied to the committed head: the atomiser is made to raise on a 553-cell carrier, and the new test then reports four not-passing ids (test_carrier_realises_the_coarse_rung, test_atomiser_accepts_the_carrier, test_topology_geometry_accepts_the_carrier, test_forward_operator_accepts_the_carrier) with EXIT=1, so the guard does fire on a refusal of this carrier.
