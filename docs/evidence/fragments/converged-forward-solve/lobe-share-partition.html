Lobe share and cell partition

An owner cell is booked once from a single ring fan: the cell polygon enters positive beside the lobe entered negative, both built by current._normal_rings and reduced by the clip-quadrature owner's own imported rule. The vertex-list reconstruction and the local signed-fan reduction are deleted, and the truncated degree-five rule that set the booked lobe's floor went with them. The partition agrees with dense references to at most 7.10542735760 × 10−13 for the lobe and 1.13686837722 × 10−13 for the whole cell; the lobe partition passes all three map guards; and the 282 cold solve converges to 3.0 × 10−14. The 550 cold solve stalls on a collapsing line search at 1.5 × 10−3, reported below and left as the next item.

The fan the remainder takes

The remainder of an owner cell — the cell with the lobe removed — is the cell polygon's star fan entered positive beside the lobe's fan entered negative, reduced in one call, so the read's membership and the booked current are one integral over one set of points. Both fans come from current._normal_rings, the builder that already produces the lobe; the earlier reconstruction walked the polygon's vertex list instead, and it is deleted.

One call cannot carry both selections. The builder takes a single selected vector and a single interval array and answers them with two families of rows: straight rows carrying the polygon's edges, and separatrix-arc rows carrying the boundary the normal form supplies, emitted after the straight rows for the sectors the selection makes live and only where the sector's sign is nonzero. The lobe's selection is the read's own — two live sectors of four, with the read's edge intervals. The cell polygon's is the identity over every edge with a degenerate interval, live in a sector the read has marked dead. Asking one call for both would need the builder to hold a second selection and interval array and to tag which row belongs to which request, which is a change to the accessor rather than a use of it. A second invocation is the smallest construction that keeps one builder: exactly one fragment slot is selected — straight rows are emitted per slot, so two live slots carry the polygon twice — one identity interval is placed on the first strip of every edge, and the separatrix-arc rows that follow are dropped by the static straight-row count.

The slot count was the one defect found here. Selecting every fragment slot emitted the straight rows once per slot, so the cell fan carried the polygon twice and the booked remainder was the polygon rather than the polygon less the lobe. It is fixed, and the analytic check pins the single-slot contract.

The reduction is the owner's

The signed-fan reduction is clip_quadrature._quadrature_from_arrays, imported and called with signed=True. A row of three live vertices contributes exactly one triangle and a row of six contributes four, which is the effective triangle set the lobe reference integrates, so the rule that books an owner cell here is the rule that books one in the clip-quadrature owner. The wrapper that remains applies the row sign and the booked density and nothing else. The deleted degree-five Dunavant rule was carried to ten digits, and that truncation, not the fan, was the floor under the booked lobe; no ten-digit constant remains on this path.

The analytic check

The check builds the cell fan through current._normal_rings itself, driven with a form whose separatrix rays never reach the cell so only the straight rows are live, and compares the booked reduction against the closed form over the polygon's triangles. Five tests pass: one live slot carries the polygon once; its reduction matches the closed form on a quadratic density; a negative slot subtracts; the remainder uses the counted polygon's closing edge even when the padded vertex differs; and the independent lobe reference runs while the booking ring builder is forbidden. Restoring the vertex-list remainder fails the counted-polygon test, and restoring the builder-based lobe reference fails the forbidden-builder test. In fresh processes on the same test file, the base reaches a summary with two collection errors because it lacks _ring_moments; the head passes all five.

Verdict

Clauses 1 to 5 hold. The remainder takes its fan from the same builder that builds the lobe, the vertex-list reconstruction and the local signed-fan reduction are deleted, the reduction is the imported clip-quadrature rule, and the truncated rule that set the booked lobe's floor is gone with it. Clause 4 holds at 282, 550 and 2,098: the independent lobe reference agrees to at most 7.10542735760 × 10−13, and the whole-cell partition to at most 1.13686837722 × 10−13. The 550 carrier books cell 8, so the lobe and remainder are both exercised. Clause 5 holds at all three sizes: the lobe partition passes the guards 1.0866 × 10−3, 5 × 10−4 and 1 × 10−4, and at 2,098 it passes where the half-plane cut fails. Clause 6 holds at 282 and is reported, not met, at 550: the solve stalls on a collapsing line search at a fixed point near 1.55 × 10−3, which is the item clause 6 named in advance and is left as the next item. Clause 7 is this fragment. No number above is an estimate.
