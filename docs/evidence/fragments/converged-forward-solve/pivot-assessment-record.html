Resolution-pivot assessment: resolution is not the binder, support is

The lead asked whether the certificate map's fidelity failure is a resolution failure or a support failure, and whether refinement alone can be trusted to converge it. This node answers that question from the eight resolution-pivot reports and their row receipts, and renders the answer as three figures whose every plotted number is read from a persisted receipt.

Verdict: the accuracy failure came from the support, not the resolution; the saddle normal form is needed at any resolution; compile cost is flat in cell count and comes from the kernel point jet; and refinement converges once the support is right. Decision: refine-with-right-support — carry accuracy on cell count from about 2,000 cells upward, keep the topology read with its saddle normal form, and stop the kernel-tangent and custom-rule route.
