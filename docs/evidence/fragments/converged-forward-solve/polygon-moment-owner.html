Consolidated polygon-moment reduction

Each candidate reduction was measured against the exact rational signed-fan monomial integral of the same doubled coordinates (fractions.Fraction), cross-checked against mpmath at 50 digits (agreement 0.0). The corpus is 600 polygons: 120 convex, 120 concave, 80 sliver triangles below 1e-8 m², 80 near-collinear quads, 80 hex cells and 60 hole-fragment rings. Errors are reported per candidate and per monomial degree. max_rel is over the whole corpus, max_rel_trim drops the single worst cell of each degree so one pathological sliver cannot decide the ranking, and max_abs is the true error magnitude.

Every surviving reduction routes through the owner

The names above survive because their callers live outside this node's write fence, but the reductions they named do not. In commit 32d463cf7 polygon_moments.polygon_monomial_moments returns the owner table directly and its Green-theorem edge-weight machinery (_edge_weights, _residual_exponents, the binomial and exponent tables, 129 source lines) is deleted; polygon_density_moments contracts the density against that same table. padded_polynomial_current_moments reads the owner table at the degree its complete basis requires. Commit 90bb6f21e holds the claim: each surviving reduction on the traced path is required to reach the owner in its own source, and the numpy area view is required to reproduce the exact rational area, first and second moments.

Two reductions are deliberately kept, each for a measured reason. stencil_mesh._polygon_monomial_integral is the independent signed-fan oracle that scripts/clip_independent_representation/score_candidates.py compares the production reductions against, so making it call the owner would make that comparison the owner agreeing with itself. separatrix_clip._area_moments was delegated and then reverted in 4523dc2f3: AtomicCellMesh.clip calls it once per mesh cell inside a Python loop, and an eager traced call per cell turned that loop into a compile-and-dispatch per cell, taking test_moving_separatrix_padded_contraction_traces_once past the 300 s pytest timeout where the numpy closed form finishes in 6.25 s. Its absolute error, 6.661e-16, is the smallest of any candidate, and it returns the same total-degree-two block of the same monomial integrals.

The reason it cannot move it is a property of the path, and the control that shows it: topology.read books each cell's area from the atomised mesh's own shoelace sum (nova/equilibrium/topology.py:2414), not from the polygon-moment reductions, so a deliberate 1e-9 relative perturbation of the owner leaves all certificate rows green. The check does bite when the booked quantity itself moves: perturbing limited-550-cpu.npz's membership by 1e-9 turns test_closed_form_read_is_accurate_and_compatible[550-limited] red at the 1e-13 assertion.
