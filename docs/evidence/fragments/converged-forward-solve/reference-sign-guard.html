Refuse a reference whose stored flux sign contradicts its declaration

The orientation helper built a read convention from a trace record's polarity, or from an IDS equilibrium's written DD COCOS and plasma-current sign, but nothing checked that the flux the reference stores agrees with that declaration. orientation.py gains a guard that samples the stored psi grid at the magnetic axis and over the stored boundary and compares their difference - a quantity an additive gauge constant cannot change - against the axis type the declared convention fixes: with a maximum required, the grid axis minus grid boundary must be positive, and with a minimum required it must be negative. A grid that disagrees is refused with ReferenceSignError naming the slice, the declaration (COCOS and Ip, or polarity) and the measured signs. The guard never infers a compensating sign flip.

The TCV 77549 LIUQE store is the measured case. Twelve slices are read through the guard by the new driver scripts/prototypes/tcv_liuqe_known_answer/reference_sign.py. Every slice declares DD 3.41.0 (COCOS 11) with negative Ip, which fixes the axis as a qualified psi maximum; the stored scalars agree (axis minus boundary positive, 0.015 to 0.195 Wb/rad), but the stored grid reads the axis as a minimum on all twelve (grid span -0.014 to -0.207). All twelve are refused, LIUQE_ROWS 12 REFUSED 12. The same guard applied to all twenty-eight field-swap trace records refuses none, FIELD_SWAP_ROWS 28 REFUSED 0, so every field-swap row is unchanged.

A synthetic IDS-shaped field whose grid is the negation of its scalars - the axis a minimum where the declaration requires a maximum - is refused by the guard, and the refusal survives an additive constant of any size. Under the declared negative control (the guard entry points replaced with no-ops) the negated-grid test fails with EXIT=1; the unguarded log records the failing id test_negated_grid_against_its_scalars_is_refused.

Both suite arms ran the same five test files (the efit_field_swap and tcv_liuqe_known_answer test modules) with the repository-root conftest from fresh per-process invocations. The baseline arm at a5ebd580f reached its summary with one failure (test_limited_slice_is_read_only_when_coverage_is_certified, a pre-existing slow real-store case) and 32 passes. The after arm did not complete: it reached thirty-one tests and was killed at the 480 s bound before its summary, exit status 124, so the suite delta is not measured here. The node's own behaviour is evidenced instead by the nine passing unit tests, the twelve-of-twelve refusals and zero-of-twenty-eight field-swap refusals, and the red negative control; completing the delta is a follow-on.
