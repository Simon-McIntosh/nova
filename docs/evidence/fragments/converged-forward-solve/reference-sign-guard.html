Refuse a reference whose stored flux sign contradicts its declaration

The orientation helper built a read convention from a trace record's polarity, or from an IDS equilibrium's written DD COCOS and plasma-current sign, but nothing checked that the flux the reference stores agrees with that declaration. orientation.py gains a guard that samples the same spline field the comparison reads at the stored axis and boundary. Their difference and the grid-range-scaled tolerance cannot be changed by an additive gauge constant. A trace without an in-grid wall instead checks the spline Hessian at its stored axis. A contradictory grid is refused with ReferenceSignError naming the slice, declaration (COCOS and Ip, or polarity) and measured signs. An IDS slice without a boundary is refused with a named reason. The guard never infers a compensating sign flip.

The TCV 77549 LIUQE store is the measured case. Twelve slices are checked by scripts/prototypes/tcv_liuqe_known_answer/reference_sign.py, and the comparison's measure_slice now checks the same declaration before its topology read. Every slice declares DD 3.41.0 (COCOS 11) with negative Ip, which fixes the axis as a qualified psi maximum; the stored scalars agree (axis minus boundary positive, 0.015 to 0.195 Wb/rad), but the stored grid reads the axis as a minimum on all twelve (grid span -0.014 to -0.207). All twelve diagnostic and all twelve actual comparison reads are refused, LIUQE_ROWS 12 REFUSED 12 and TCV_READ_ROWS 12 REFUSED 12. The same guard applied to all twenty-eight field-swap trace records refuses none, FIELD_SWAP_ROWS 28 REFUSED 0; four crossing records are checked by axis curvature. A post-wiring receipt is identical to the preceding twenty-eight rows as parsed JSON.
