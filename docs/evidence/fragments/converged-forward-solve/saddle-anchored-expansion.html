The expanded record, with the cost decomposition and the fitting change, is the saddle-anchored expansion research note.

Speed gate

Rebuild: runtime arguments and the measured decomposition

The rows above are the first build, which lowered each arm through a closure over the carrier, the operands and the batched state so XLA hoisted them into the compiled program. The rebuild gives every one of those arrays to the lowered function as a runtime argument. Generated code falls from 152913270 to 1684704 bytes at width 128 on the same carrier, a factor of 90.7, and the largest dense HLO constant in the lowered module is one element at widths 1, 16 and 128 and at 282, 550 and 2,098 cells alike, so no constant scales with the carrier or the state. The size condition now passes with the ceiling at 50 MB; the compile condition passes at 1.46 s.

The ratio does not move: 30.3036 against the first build's 29.9319. The constants were never the cost. The rebuild's ratio decomposes into two measured factors read off its own two width-128 rows. The axis-facing arm provisions a fixed 16 members and spends 0.223044 ms, 13.9 µs per member; the saddle arm provisions 194 of the carrier's 282 cells and spends 6.759026 ms, 34.8 µs per member. The member factor is 12.1× and the per-member factor is 2.5×, and 12.1 × 2.5 = 30.3 is the measured ratio.

The rebuild carries its own correctness guard, and it fails. The gate configuration is required to reproduce the first build's integration to 1e-12; the measured maximum absolute difference in moment_residual over four states is 0.6593670573 against a tolerance of 1e-12, and the runner exits nonzero on it. The two configurations differ only in outer degree (8 against 128) and in the candidate list, and the candidate list cannot explain the gap: it holds every cell within 1.40 m of the saddle while the disk radius is 0.40 m, so the live set is identical in both and the difference is the outer rule alone. The disk cap contributes a half-width sqrt(radius² − u²) with unbounded slope at the cap, so the integrand is only Hölder-continuous at that endpoint and an 8-node rule converges to it slowly. The closed-form slice-discriminant branch the brief requires is not implemented; only the low-order branch was built.

Two rungs have no ratio. At 550 cells the saddle arm's width-128 lowering is refused for 81.95 GiB, and at 2,098 cells the axis-facing control arm's width-128 lowering is refused for 263.80 GiB, so the gate's own 2,098-cell condition is unmeasured and the obstruction is in the control, not the expansion.

The candidate list was grown by a literal one metre of margin while the radius is given in pitches; on this carrier the pitch is 0.1005 m, so the 0.40 m radius was smaller than the margin and 194 of 282 cells were admitted, 332 of 550, and 1062 of 2,098. The margin is now one median pitch (commit e3353b26c), which is resolution-independent. The rows above were measured before that correction, so the 12.1× member factor is an upper bound and the corrected measurement is outstanding.

What the rows do establish

Every state in every row returns a finite moment residual: 1/1, 16/16 and 128/128 across both arms. The map builds, the batch vectorises, the owned disk cut returns a fraction and a booked moment for each state, and nothing in the representation is degenerate at 282 cells. The failure is a cost failure and not a numerical one, which is what makes the cost decomposition in the research note the whole of the result: the representation is not implicated, the integration rule that consumes it is.

Carrier cache

The read-only cache named in the node brief, r-20261009T181553786165-proto-support/ordered/rows/cache, holds one 5,000-cell machine for the moderate-rotation and weak-rotation analytic cases and no diverted carrier; a request against it raised FileNotFoundError in _machine during input loading and produced no numerical evidence. The diverted carrier is cached under r-20261014T174345284407-lean-map/cache, the cache the accepted fast-map run uses, which is the one this gate reads. Its realised cell count runs ahead of the requested count: 250→282, 500→550, 2000→2098.

Declared negative control

Not run. The control is that replacing the Newton-located saddle with the fitted Bell stationary point reproduces the connected-lobe map error. It belongs to the map-error clause, which compares against the analytic flux sample; with the speed gate failed first the map was never exercised against the analytic flux, so the control has no host measurement. It is recorded as owed, not as passed.

The absolute bar: missed at every realised size

The 1.25× ratio above was retired for an absolute bar: at each of the realised 282, 550 and 2,098 cells, the saddle map's per-state wall at most 0.90 ms at the largest width up to 128 that fits on one H200, with compile still under 60 s and the executable under 50 MB. Job 1287882 measured all three rungs as rows in one allocation, one width per process, and completed 2026-10-17T07:29:56Z per sacct. The bar is missed at all three.

The fitting width falls as the carrier refines because the device binds first. At 550 cells the saddle arm's width-128 lowering is refused for 81.93 GiB while the control at that width succeeds; at 2,098 cells the control itself is refused at width 128 (263.80 GiB) and at 64 (131.90 GiB). Compile is 0.86–1.30 s and the executable 1.68–7.72 MB at every rung, so the size and compile conditions pass and the failure is wall alone. Under the lead's ruling the line stops and the axis-facing half-plane is adopted.

The disk was narrowed to one median pitch, provisioning 19 members where the rows above provisioned 194. The 282-cell wall moved from 6.759026 to 6.681955 ms: ninety per cent of the members removed, 1.1% of the wall. The member factor was therefore not the cost, and the cost is carrier-wide — the wall rises 6.68 → 9.28 → 25.01 ms with the cell count while the member count stays near twenty.

The 0.659 agreement failure is located, not inferred. A degree ladder against the 128-node reference over the same states gives 0.2110304721853531 at degree 8, 0.04833218463634381 at 16, 0.004051944956813713 at 32, 0.006582352677924064 at 64 and 0 at 128, that rung being the reference. candidate_set_effect is 0.0, so the candidate list contributes nothing and the disagreement is the outer rule alone. The rule does not converge: the error rises from degree 32 to 64 and plateaus near 1e-3, four orders above the guard, which is the disk cap's Hölder endpoint and not a tolerance that a larger degree would buy past. Clause 2 is still not implemented, and with the member factor shown not to be the cost, replacing the outer rule attacks the 351.68 µs per member and not the bulk of the gap.
