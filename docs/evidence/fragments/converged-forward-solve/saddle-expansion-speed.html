The disk shrink did not buy the wall, and that is the finding

The disk was narrowed from 4 median pitches to 1 median pitch, so it now owns the containing cell and its neighbours rather than most of the plasma. Read from the program, the provisioned member count falls from 194 of the carrier's 282 cells to 19, and to 22 of 2,098. At 282 cells the wall moves from 6.759026 ms to 6.681955 ms: removing 90% of the members changed the cost by 1.1%. The cost therefore is not the disk; it is carried by the cell count of the whole carrier, and the disk merely rides on it.

The per-member figures follow from each arm's own row and its own member count rather than from a shared denominator: at 282 cells the axis arm spends 13.99 µs per member over its fixed 16 and the saddle arm 351.68 µs over its 19, at 550 cells 26.07 and 488.25, and at 2,098 cells 169.24 and 1136.75. Both figures rise with the cell count because both arms carry carrier-wide work; the saddle arm's per-member cost is 8 to 25 times the control's at every rung.

What the 0.659 disagreement is

The brief required the cause of the agreement failure to be found before anything was tuned, and the ladder in this job locates it exactly. The gate configuration is compared against a 128-node reference over the same states and the same candidate list, varying only the outer degree: 0.2110304721853531 at degree 8, 0.04833218463634381 at 16, 0.004051944956813713 at 32 and 0.006582352677924064 at 64. candidate_set_effect is 0.0: the 19-candidate disk reproduces the 282-candidate result exactly on nodes where every one of the 282 is live, so the candidate list contributes nothing to the disagreement and this is settled rather than inferred.

The error is therefore the outer rule alone, and it does not converge. It falls by 4.4× and 11.9× and then rises from degree 32 to 64, plateauing near 1e-3 — four orders of magnitude above the 1e-12 guard. That is the signature of the disk cap, whose half-width sqrt(radius²−u²) has unbounded slope at the endpoint, so the integrand is only Hölder-continuous there and no moderate-order Gauss–Legendre rule reaches the reference. It is a correctness failure of the rule and not a tolerance question, which is the brief's own reading of it. The agreement check fails, on every degree the ladder measures.

The receipt's own passed field first read true, and that was a defect in the guard rather than a result. Its ladder carried a fifth row at degree 128, which is the reference degree: that row compares the reference with itself and reads exactly zero, so a verdict reduced over the ladder was satisfied by a tautology whatever the cheaper degrees did. The reference degree is excluded from the ladder, the verdict is taken from the degrees that measure something, and a CPU check pins both halves — that the ladder excludes the reference and that the decision returns a failure on the four recorded disagreements.
