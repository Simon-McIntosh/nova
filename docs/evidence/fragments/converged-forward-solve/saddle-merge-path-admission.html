Merge-path saddle admission

The admitted saddle is now the bounding saddle of the axis merge path, not the highest-level root. The read at scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production/kernel.py selected saddle_index = 1 + jnp.argmax(saddle_score_rows), ranking every valid saddle by its level. A second root at the wall contact ties the boundary level by construction — the wall candidate is the more scrape-off-side of the contact's tangency and the axis-path bottleneck, and the bottleneck is that saddle's own level — so it won the argmax over the whole tie island and then left the census between adjacent doubles. One owned function, _merge_path_saddle, replaces the argmax: it ranks each live saddle by the maximin axis-path passage to the saddle's own cell minus the saddle's own level, decided before the wall-versus-saddle contest and fixed-shape and traced.

282-A is the tie island's mechanism and it is a fold. A saddle root enters the census at offset 0.16095036467954557 with its level bit-identical to the wall level (wall_candidate == saddle_candidate == boundary_flux == 0.007967410111760847), so the persistence of the pair is exactly zero and crosses the enclosure of 9.05 × 10−18. Its det H is −1.6194 × 10−4, thirteen orders of magnitude above the enclosure — so the fold is the ruling's, not the determinant's: the pair has no persistence to speak of, and the root arrives already hyperbolic. Base admits it and switches class 0 → 1, stepping the booked current 2215.3501 → 2206.1106.

282-B is a census departure, not a fold. The same wall-contact root leaves the census between 0.17323108126295567 and …570 while still hyperbolic (det H = −2.7385 × 10−5) at a persistence of 6.05 × 10−7, eleven orders above the enclosure of 9.32 × 10−18. Base's argmax then falls back to the primary X-point, switching class 1 → 0 and stepping the booked current 14.4806 → 129.2342 — the +114.75 step this node exists to remove. Head holds class 0 and the primary X-point's identity on both sides of both ties: at 282 the head arm carries no change over the 61-state window, at 550 none over its own island window, so the 115-to-125 step is present at base and gone at head.

At 550 the same two mechanisms appear six times, and none is a fold. Base carries six changes in the 0.10–0.14 island window, each bisected to adjacent binary64 in 45 or 46 steps. The three entries (550-1, -3, -5) are crossings: the admitted saddle's level passes the wall candidate's level with a resolvable difference of 2.81 × 10−10 to 3.93 × 10−7, eight to ten orders above the enclosure. The three exits (550-2, -4, -6) are census departures: the root leaves while still hyperbolic, det H between −4.45 × 10−5 and −1.44 × 10−4, at a persistence of 3.72 × 10−7 to 5.46 × 10−7. Head carries zero changes over the same window and each of the six brackets reports class 0 on both sides. Booked-current steps at the exits reach 112.20 → 184.74 and 15.91 → 114.07 — the 115-to-125 class of step, gone at head.
