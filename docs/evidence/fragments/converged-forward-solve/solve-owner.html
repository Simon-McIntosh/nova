Production chord-Newton fixed-point solve owner

The module and its interface

nova/equilibrium/solve.py carries the settled method out of the prototypes. It exposes one frozen result type and two entry points, and nothing else public:

  • Factor — the dense LU factorisation of the map's Jacobian at a state;
  • SolveResult — state, residual (the scaled infinity norm), converged, corrections and factor;
  • factor(residual_fn, state, operands) — builds the dense Jacobian by a vmapped jax.jvp over identity directions and LU-factors it;
  • fixed_point(residual_fn, state, operands, *, factor=None, tolerance=1e-12, max_corrections=…) — cold from a fresh factor per correction with a damped backtracking line search, or a chord reusing a supplied factor.

The map is pure and takes (state, operands); the state is flat. There are no policy objects, receipts or host loops: the correction loop and the line search are lax.while_loop constructs and the chord corrections unroll at trace time, so both paths trace under jax.jit and jax.vmap.

Negative control — a perturbed factor must fail the chord clause

Declared mutation: a factor perturbed by 2 percent fails the two-correction chord convergence test. Applied by wrapping solve.fixed_point so a supplied chord factor is scaled by 1.02, leaving cold solves (no factor) untouched. The head arm passes the chord test; under the mutation exactly that test fails and the other five pass (EXIT=1), so the chord clause is a real measurement rather than an identity that cannot fail. Log: negative-control.log.
