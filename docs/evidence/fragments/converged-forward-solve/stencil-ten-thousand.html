The normalized quadratic design has singular values 3.07893, 2.10317, 1.24004, 0.203490, 0.0367522 and 0.00175102; the extreme condition is therefore a shape defect, not a major-radius scaling effect. Six of the seven cells, including the centre, have less than a full hexagon's area; the smallest neighbour carries 1.1383%. Only three neighbour polygons intersect the sampled wall line, so the existing boundary-cell test does not exclude this clipped centre. The centroids lie in a narrow diagonal strip beside the wall. The refused ring is the only ring above condition 20 in the eight-case census, so the observed failure is a wall-clip ring-selection defect rather than a general fine-resolution conditioning floor.
