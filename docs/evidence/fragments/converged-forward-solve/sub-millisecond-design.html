Forward-solve latency design evidence

No measured Nova implementation produces a converged section-6-accurate warm slice below 1 ms. The research document inventories the implementations, reports the candidate controls and gives a conditional 0.90 ms target budget whose stage ceilings remain unachieved.

Connected support

H200 job 1285727 measured exact fragment label propagation at 0.506/0.456 ms and host-compacted dense reachability at 0.309/0.325 ms for limited/diverted 289/282-cell carriers. Both agreed with every exact fragment; the diverted control had six excluded private fragments, and selecting them all made six errors. Job 1285734 built full-capacity adjacency on device and matched the same exact component at 578/564 slots in 0.547/0.341 ms, with a two-member identical-input vmap. A 32×32 doubling raster agreed on only 90.66%/89.01% of cell selections, and a local level rule on 93.77%/88.65%. A literal sorted device union sweep took 1.070/1.907 s and did not reproduce the diverted boundary decision. The existing Nova fixed-capacity join/split contour tree took 457.2/406.1 ms on the same carriers in H200 job 1285731 with nonempty critical-node receipts. The full lean-map connected read previously took 6.120 ms diverted; it supplied roots and skipped tree construction, so fragment propagation is only one part of that cost.

H200 job 1285746 timed a fresh single-pass dual-candidate cell join sweep at 37.09/47.92 ms limited/diverted. A four-node control observed both wall and saddle events and delayed wall contact when only a foreign basin touched it. The diverted saddle level was 0.005697 versus a 0.005710 reference, while the limited candidate was 0.331 versus 49.941; the 41-state diverted sweep jumped by 1.119 reference spans. Its identical-input width-two vmap passed, but latency, parity and continuity did not.

Cut booking

Jobs 1285729 and 1285730 agreed exactly on analytic map error after replacing only cut-cell moments: limited 4×4 and 8×8 fixed subcells gave 0.003013 and 0.000534; diverted gave 0.014758 and 0.014147, both failing the 10−2 ceiling. Exact controls were 0.000143 and 0.000619. Inverting the cut orientation raised error to 0.214–0.215 limited and 0.735–0.739 diverted, proving the error check can reject a wrong rule. Constant-field fan weights reproduced full-cell area within 4.1 × 10−15 m² limited and 2.4 × 10−16 m² diverted, so the diverted plateau is not an area-weight omission. Submillisecond dispatch-and-wait times varied between repeat jobs and do not establish a precise cut speedup. The accuracy plot shows the diverted plateau.

H200 job 1285745 used analytic edge roots and four conic arc points with closed-form quadratic-density moments. It reached 0.000180 limited and 0.000904 diverted analytic map error, with candidate walls 0.305 and 0.284 ms respectively. The straight chord gave 0.003199 and 0.018360, so boundary curvature is decisive on the diverted carrier. The candidate replaced 80/80 limited cuts and 54/57 diverted cuts; the three diverted saddle-normal cuts used exact moments computed before timing. Exact support and raw-current normalisation were also supplied. The measured wall is an ordinary-cut lower bound, not complete booking.

H200 job 1285747 applied a binary 0 ≤ ψN ≤ 1 cell-centre mask inside the bounding box of the exact selected polygons. It chose 150 of the diverted carrier's 282 cells against 179 selected by connected support, mismatched 29, and produced 0.314436 analytic map error against the 0.000619 exact control. Its isolated whole-cell booking wall was 0.089 ms. The positive exact-control and nonzero disagreement establish that the hard-mask row is measuring a real support loss.

Cold map-entry refusals

H200 job 1285726 used the same nonzero current total for uniform and centroid-circle moment seeds. On the limited carrier, zero moments returned root status 1 and booking status 2; uniform returned 3 and 2; the circle qualified its root and booking but a newly cut cell returned band status 1. On diverted, zero returned root statuses 2 and 0, uniform 2 and 3, and the circle 2 and 0; all three distributed or zero arms booked invalidly. The analytic-moment positive control had zero statuses and finite output on both carriers. Job 1285732 freshly reproduced one valid read among 41 diverted states in each of its vertical and wall-local perturbation sweeps. The wall-local class switch moved boundary flux by 7.34 × 10−10 of the span but one side refused; a separately valid interior-band crossing had a split-route current jump 7.59 × 10−12 versus 8.33 × 10−17 for exact booking while the fixed lookup was incomplete. Distributed current alone does not repair Nova's production read; mobile Bell rows and a robust root census or continuation are required.

Design consequence

Retain exact connected Bell support as the accuracy authority and advance the four-arc Bell cut to a complete three-moment booking benchmark with saddle-normal sectors included. The measured cell join sweep cannot replace the read. Keep both limited and diverted wall/saddle candidates in one wall-aware decision, and use a mobile row window and device moment-space Newton before claiming a warm 1 ms solve. The published 282/289-cell lean prototype takes about 10 ms per live map and its strict limited cached-factor warm slice takes 291.7 ms; the proposed 0.90 ms budget remains a falsifiable target.
