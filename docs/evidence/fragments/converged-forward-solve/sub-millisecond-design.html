Forward-solve latency design evidence

No measured Nova implementation produces a converged section-6-accurate warm slice below 1 ms. The research document inventories the implementations, reports the candidate controls and gives a conditional 0.90 ms target budget whose stage ceilings remain unachieved.

Connected support

H200 job 1285727 measured exact fragment label propagation at 0.506/0.456 ms and host-compacted dense reachability at 0.309/0.325 ms for limited/diverted 289/282-cell carriers. Both agreed with every exact fragment; the diverted control had six excluded private fragments, and selecting them all made six errors. Job 1285734 built full-capacity adjacency on device and matched the same exact component at 578/564 slots in 0.547/0.341 ms, with a two-member identical-input vmap. A 32×32 doubling raster agreed on only 90.66%/89.01% of cell selections, and a local level rule on 93.77%/88.65%. A literal sorted device union sweep took 1.070/1.907 s and did not reproduce the diverted boundary decision. The existing Nova fixed-capacity join/split contour tree took 457.2/406.1 ms on the same carriers in H200 job 1285731 with nonempty critical-node receipts. The full lean-map connected read previously took 6.120 ms diverted; it supplied roots and skipped tree construction, so fragment propagation is only one part of that cost.

H200 job 1285746 timed a fresh single-pass dual-candidate cell join sweep at 37.09/47.92 ms limited/diverted. A four-node control observed both wall and saddle events and delayed wall contact when only a foreign basin touched it. The diverted saddle level was 0.005697 versus a 0.005710 reference, while the limited candidate was 0.331 versus 49.941; the 41-state diverted sweep jumped by 1.119 reference spans. Its identical-input width-two vmap passed, but latency, parity and continuity did not.

H200 job 1285751 used the wall-clipped cell polygons for Bell-level edge crossings and a fixed-shape axis flood. Limited agreement was 254/254 cells at 0.407 ms, with 0.000181 map error and ordinary fractional-area error at most 0.00203. Diverted selected 184 versus 179 exact cells at 0.570 ms; five extra private cells raised map error to 0.158374 despite ordinary area error at most 0.00166. Zero generated polygons escaped their wall cells. An injected crossing 135 mm beyond a wall edge was rejected on both carriers; this is a synthetic analogue, not a replay of EFIT's ITER t = 400/580 waveform.

H200 job 1285761 gated wall-domain edges near the saddle by the fixed-point frame sector containing the axis. This removed exactly the five private diverted cells admitted without the gate: 179/179 diverted and 254/254 limited cell agreement, 0.000900/0.000181 map error, and 0.361/0.527 ms graph walls. The gate's nonzero effect is observed on the diverted control. The frame and boundary level came from the exact read before timing, and saddle cuts still reused exact moments; this is a stage candidate rather than an independent full support read.

H200 job 1285764 traced the diverted Bell-level outline from the X-point cell in one fixed-length device scan. It closed through 55 points in 8.224 ms, never hit the wall or repeated a cell, had zero crossings outside wall-clipped cells, and wound 2π around the axis. The inside test therefore accepted the axis. It is an outline check on one analytic state and does not by itself choose fractional current support or replay the actual ITER wall-tangent slices.

Cut booking

Jobs 1285729 and 1285730 agreed exactly on analytic map error after replacing only cut-cell moments: limited 4×4 and 8×8 fixed subcells gave 0.003013 and 0.000534; diverted gave 0.014758 and 0.014147, both failing the 10−2 ceiling. Exact controls were 0.000143 and 0.000619. Inverting the cut orientation raised error to 0.214–0.215 limited and 0.735–0.739 diverted, proving the error check can reject a wrong rule. Constant-field fan weights reproduced full-cell area within 4.1 × 10−15 m² limited and 2.4 × 10−16 m² diverted, so the diverted plateau is not an area-weight omission. Submillisecond dispatch-and-wait times varied between repeat jobs and do not establish a precise cut speedup. The accuracy plot shows the diverted plateau.

H200 job 1285745 used analytic edge roots and four conic arc points with closed-form quadratic-density moments. It reached 0.000180 limited and 0.000904 diverted analytic map error, with candidate walls 0.305 and 0.284 ms respectively. The straight chord gave 0.003199 and 0.018360, so boundary curvature is decisive on the diverted carrier. The candidate replaced 80/80 limited cuts and 54/57 diverted cuts; the three diverted saddle-normal cuts used exact moments computed before timing. Exact support and raw-current normalisation were also supplied. The measured wall is an ordinary-cut lower bound, not complete booking.

H200 job 1285747 applied a binary 0 ≤ ψN ≤ 1 cell-centre mask inside the bounding box of the exact selected polygons. It chose 150 of the diverted carrier's 282 cells against 179 selected by connected support, mismatched 29, and produced 0.314436 analytic map error against the 0.000619 exact control. Its isolated whole-cell booking wall was 0.089 ms. The positive exact-control and nonzero disagreement establish that the hard-mask row is measuring a real support loss.

Point-booking discrimination

H200 job 1285765 replayed the 2,098-cell diverted point booking. The unbounded and 0 ≤ ψN ≤ 1 windowed masks both selected 1,214 cells and gave 0.418807 map error; both axis-connected variants selected 1,193 and gave 0.087848. The exact booked control was 0.00001766. All 21 disconnected centres were inside the normalized window, ψN 0.00421–0.16719, density 19,750–23,610 A/m² and 1.2164% of raw point current; their positions are in point-window-diverted.json. The window changes no cell or error. Removing disconnected support changes error by 0.33096, so connectivity sets this carrier's 0.41 plateau, not profile extrapolation.

A same-reference CPU replay of the independent imas-efit single-null node-grid study reproduced its known axis-connected rows exactly at 65², 129² and 257² nodes: 0.017226, 0.021435 and 0.020634. Adding the profile window again changed no node and no error. Its unrestricted errors were 0.012798, 0.005825 and 0.002114. The reference is a clipped 385²-node booking, whereas Nova's comparison uses analytic exterior subtraction on a hex carrier; the matched replay lands near the independent 0.02 rather than Nova's 0.088. The reference and source placement both differ, so this does not isolate either one alone.

Batched device stages and contour reference

H200 job 1285778 measured synchronized jax.vmap widths 1/16/128 on the 282-cell diverted carrier. Whole-batch walls in milliseconds were: exact fragment labels 0.540/0.453/1.003; dense reach 0.416/1.412/5.932; saddle-frame graph 0.415/0.577/1.450; four-arc Bell polygon 0.347/0.879/1.752; exact cut booking 4.526/22.034/151.812. At width 128 the respective per-state walls were 0.00784/0.04635/0.01133/0.01369/1.18603 ms. Every output was nonempty. Inputs were small numerical perturbations on one valid fixed topology; these are throughput rows, not moving-component or converged-batch tests. The run's fifteen rows are in vmap-stages.json, reconstructed from its complete logged result after the first writer saved only the final stage.

H200 job 1285781 masked the wall inside a 193² grid and called Nova's existing contourpy wrapper as the host accuracy reference. It returned one 1,541-point contour; the device Bell outline held 55 points. Device-to-host nearest distances were 0.0111 pitch median and 0.0366 maximum; host-to-device maximum was 2.25 pitches, so the device route omits host branches. Both had zero outside-wall points. The host outline took 1.354 ms but cannot vmap; the device outline took 10.228 ms on its first post-compile call and remains too slow for the target.

Cold map-entry refusals

H200 job 1285726 used the same nonzero current total for uniform and centroid-circle moment seeds. On the limited carrier, zero moments returned root status 1 and booking status 2; uniform returned 3 and 2; the circle qualified its root and booking but a newly cut cell returned band status 1. On diverted, zero returned root statuses 2 and 0, uniform 2 and 3, and the circle 2 and 0; all three distributed or zero arms booked invalidly. The analytic-moment positive control had zero statuses and finite output on both carriers. Job 1285732 freshly reproduced one valid read among 41 diverted states in each of its vertical and wall-local perturbation sweeps. The wall-local class switch moved boundary flux by 7.34 × 10−10 of the span but one side refused; a separately valid interior-band crossing had a split-route current jump 7.59 × 10−12 versus 8.33 × 10−17 for exact booking while the fixed lookup was incomplete. Distributed current alone does not repair Nova's production read; mobile Bell rows and a robust root census or continuation are required.

A production limited cold-portfolio probe captured 161 nonzero uniform-disc current cells summing to 1,627,957.013 A at the requested positive current, and zero cells at zero requested current. Constructing the zero-current cold portfolio left an explicit warm flux seed bitwise unchanged. The builder's zero arrays are the radial and vertical first moments, while its positive zeroth-current array feeds the cold state; this route does not erase a shared warm current.

Design consequence

Retain exact connected Bell support as the accuracy authority and advance the four-arc Bell cut to a complete three-moment booking benchmark with saddle-normal sectors included. The saddle-frame-gated cell graph passes these analytic cell sets but inherits its frame and level from the exact read; the cell join sweep fails boundary parity, and the ordered X-point outline costs 8.224 ms. Keep both boundary candidates in one wall-aware decision and use a mobile row window and device moment-space Newton before claiming a warm 1 ms solve. The four-arc full prototype takes 4.895/8.269 ms per live map and its matched-terminal warm corrections take 314.2/405.2 ms; the proposed 0.90 ms budget remains a falsifiable target.

Its saved exact-booking predecessors rechecked at 0.002315/0.002451 Bell-rule residual; limited warm correction passed at 260.6 ms and 4.37 × 10−14, while diverted stopped at 789.6 ms and 5.54 × 10−12. A matched Bell-rule cold solve in job 1285752 converged in three accepted steps on both carriers: cold residual 4.77 × 10−15 limited and 2.94 × 10−13 diverted, terminal map errors 0.000452/0.001040, and farthest null shifts 0.000535/0.000323 pitches. From those matched terminals, warm corrections passed at 314.2 ms and 4.64 × 10−15 limited, and 405.2 ms and 8.36 × 10−13 diverted. Cold Newton compilation took 88.780/86.918 s, over the 60 s ceiling.

A positive-controlled JAX profiler parser counted 1,474 GPU kernel events and 134 host CUDA-graph launches for one limited four-arc live map, and 2,675 kernels and 275 graph launches for one diverted map. It required a named GPU process, the compiled jit_mapped module and real kernel details before accepting either trace. A profiler-wrapped warm correction failed with CUDA_ERROR_LAUNCH_FAILED in job 1285753 after the limited map trace; the same warm program passed untraced in jobs 1285748 and 1285752. The retry job 1285758 confined profiling to the diverted map. Thus the counts are per map and cannot be promoted to a full warm-correction launch count.
