Every other slice in the store is a byte-copy of its block's grid and is not an independent answer. The window imas-efit checked (0.7930 to 0.8390 s) lies wholly inside the block starting at 796 and ending at 1256, which is why that window is byte-frozen: the freeze is local to one block, not a property of the pulse. The wall store is unchanged.
