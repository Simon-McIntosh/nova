TCV 77549 LIUQE known-answer: nova's boundary read scored against LIUQE

nova's read was scored against LIUQE on twelve TCV 77549 slices spanning the limited and diverted phases. It agrees with LIUQE's limited-or-diverted label on 5 of 12 slices. The disagreement is not in the boundary flux level, which tracks LIUQE closely, but in the axis nova selects on diverted fields: it lands in a vessel corner with a flux of the opposite sign, and the label follows from that axis.

The diverted slices it reads as limited are those whose census admits no saddle, and an empty saddle set is not on its own evidence of a limited plasma while the census proposal-coverage defect is open. Such a slice is read as limited only when the degree of ∇ψ on the domain boundary equals the index sum of the admitted critical points inside it — outer loop winding less each obstacle loop's — and no loop's minimum |∇ψ| falls below a rounding-referenced floor; otherwise the read is refused as coverage uncertified. On the two such slices measured so far (t = 0.014 and 0.027 s) the winding is +1 against an admitted index sum of 0, so they are uncertified coverage misses rather than real disagreements.
