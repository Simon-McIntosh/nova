The exact-tie boundary-class switch

Measured research record

The switch is a label, and the number that moves is not its. The comparison at scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production/kernel.py:302, diverted = saddle_live & (saddle_score >= wall_score), is consumed at three places and none of them changes a number at the tie. The booked current does step, by up to 125, but it steps because the saddle selection at kernel.py:239, saddle_index = 1 + jnp.argmax(saddle_score_rows), changes which root is chosen — the same root that creates the tie. The kernel is therefore left unmodified, and the defect is recorded against the selection.

Classification

An implementation switch at kernel.py:302, not a crossing and not a fold.

  • On the two rising edges (282 at 0.16–0.17, 550 at 0.11–0.12) the second saddle’s level arrives bit-identical to the wall candidate, 0.007967410111760857 at 282 and 0.007973164696008964 at 550, so the non-strict >= takes the diverted branch on an exact tie. The tie is not a coincidence: the wall candidate is min(tangency, bottleneck) and the bottleneck is the saddle level, so the two operands meet by construction at the wall contact the census has just returned.
  • On the two falling edges the second root leaves the census entirely (two saddles → one, root count three → two) between adjacent doubles, and the class falls with it. This is not a fold: the departing root’s |det H| is 2.74 × 10−5 at 282 and 2.22 × 10−5 at 550 on the last state that carries it, orders of magnitude off roundoff, so the root does not degenerate to zero curvature — the root finder stops returning it.
  • The saddle position at the tie, (1.4317, 1.1054) at 282 and (1.4321, 1.1042) at 550, sits on the wall, and its level equals the wall candidate exactly. Across the 49-state fine scan at 282 cells, all 25 rows carrying two live saddles show that second saddle tied to the wall candidate, so the island is systematic rather than a single unlucky sample.

The step that does move a number is the saddle selection. At 282, the 0.17–0.18 bracket steps the booked current by +114.75 while the boundary flux moves by 6 × 10−7; the label is class 1 on the left and class 0 on the right, but so is saddle_index, which moves off the wall-contact root and onto the primary X-point. That change re-derives form and with it normal, booking, the booked-cell set and the path weight, all of which the booked current is built from. The sweep’s booked current does not read diverted at all. Since the comparison’s own consumers are label-only, the kernel is left as it stands and the number-changing defect is recorded as a follow-on against kernel.py:239.

The wall level is now pinned at the read, and the control is not its own input

The bottleneck-level test previously asserted the wall level only through its helper, which a swap of the read’s composition could not have failed. It now composes the wall candidate per contact the way the read does — effective level, then the most axis-ward contact wins — and asserts both that the gated contact takes the bottleneck and that the directly reachable control keeps its tangency. Both forbidden compositions move the selected candidate, so the assertion rejects each: raw tangency hands the wall candidate to the gated cell, and a maximum hands it to the control’s bottleneck. The control’s expectation is a declared module constant rather than the value handed in, and it is asserted to differ from the bottleneck it was given.
