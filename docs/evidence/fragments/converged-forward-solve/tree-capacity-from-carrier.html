Contour-tree capacities derived from the carrier

The receipt's node and edge capacities are now the carrier's own vertex and edge slot counts, and the neighbour table of a caller that supplies no measured degree takes the safe edge-count bound. At the base revision 7430c618f657a0a169c9fa04135654514e2298c6 the 10,000-cell limited read refused with valid=False and the receipt's overflow true; at head the same read is valid=True, reason=0 and qualified=True. The seven receipt fields stay byte-for-byte what the build's own arithmetic produces; the fixture comparison that pins them is red at both revisions for an unrelated carrier-size refusal in its data, recorded below.

Rule, in nova/equilibrium/contour_tree.py. carrier_node_capacity(declared, vertex_slots) returns the carrier's vertex slot count when nothing is declared and the declaration otherwise, and carrier_edge_capacity(declared, edge_slots) does the same over the carrier's edge slots. Every critical point the build can emit is one carrier vertex, and the merged join and split arcs are collected over the carrier's edge slots, so those slot counts are the smallest capacities that cannot refuse a legitimate receipt. build_contour_tree takes both as static parameters, always sizes its sweeps by the carrier, and reports a declaration below what the carrier produces through overflow rather than truncating the tree. _carrier_adjacency takes edge_count as the neighbour default, replacing the fixed-width fallback: no vertex can hold more neighbours than the graph has edges, so the bound never refuses a graph whose degree the graph itself permits. The production read is unaffected in kind, because topology.read already supplies the carrier's measured degree as static mesh metadata.

The carrier at 10,000 cells. From one fresh CPU process with x64 enabled: the limited carrier holds 10,400 live vertices in 10,537 slots and 31,225 live edge slots, with a measured maximum degree of 10. The tree it produces holds 193 nodes and 192 edges, so the receipt is emitted at 10,537 node rows and 31,225 edge rows and its overflow is false. A split-sweep candidate count of 193 cannot fit a fixed 256-and-255 pair only if the pair is smaller than the count, which it is not: the base refusal came from the capacity pair the build carried, and the derived pair removes it.

The overflow probe asserts the refusal against the carrier. scripts/prototypes/contour_tree_overflow/test_overflow.py still reads its carrier through probe.mastu_inputs. At each of the three probe resolutions it asserts the measured degree the mesh reports, that a declared width one below that degree refuses, and that the measured-degree table fits. The refusal record measured degrees 19, 22 and 18 on the pre-repair carrier, all above its then fixed width of 16; the wall-coverage repair re-cut this carrier and the probe now reports 9, 11 and 12, so the fixed width of 16 no longer sits below the degree and the reproduction is stated against the carrier's own degree instead. That discrepancy is a deviation from the brief, which named the historical degrees, and is carried in the manifest's evidence_inputs.
