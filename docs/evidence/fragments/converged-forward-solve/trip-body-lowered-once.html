Newton-Krylov trip body lowered once

Four guards in tests/test_solve_trips_are_a_scan.py reported that the lowered solve contains three copies of its trip body. It contains one. The count is a count of stablehlo.optimization_barrier occurrences, and the solve path carried two barriers of its own: a fence around the stream operator application, added after the guards, that wraps the requested vector and the returned action so the operator's arithmetic stays out of its consumers' fusions. The third barrier is the marker the guard itself installs. Measured on the trips=4 guard case, the lowered solve goes from two barriers at base to none at head, and the four guards pass. The fence is removed from the product; every solver output compared across the two trees is bit-identical.

The count, decomposed

At orientation_base_sha the guarded solve lowers with exactly two barriers and one _newton_krylov_inner trip body; with the guard's marker installed it lowers with three barriers. Both product barriers came from one helper, _fenced, applied at the two sites that hand the requested vector to the operator: the single-member stream at _serve_krylov_stream.serve and the batched stream at _serve_krylov_batch.serve. Instrumenting _qualified_krylov_step with a stack-recording wrapper shows it traced exactly once, from _newton_krylov_inner.attempt_step, so no call site duplicated it. The instrument and the product shared one primitive, which is what made the count read as three bodies.

Read from history without a bisect. The guards landed 2026-09-20 (d8bbc0491); the fence landed 2026-09-23 (e84cbcaee, fence the stream operator application from its consumers), and the helper took its current name at 4e4f2d1ab on 2026-09-24. The guards have therefore been red since the fence, and the fence's commit says nothing about them: the barriers were added for numerical fidelity, and the instrument that counts barriers broke as a side effect.

The fence bought less than its cost. Its own landed evidence records that it restores no bit identity on the real operator: a diverted certificate rung differs by 3.07e-12 under the fenced stream. What it did establish was a like-for-like fusion boundary for the toy comparison that verified the stream rewrite against the per-site body, where the fenced stream matched on eight of eight dense cases against a base body fenced the same way. That comparison is an evidence script, not a gate, and it can fence its own reference arm.

What changed

One product file. _fenced is deleted and its two call sites apply the operator directly, so the operator application is one shared lowered body with no barrier of its own. No test assertion is changed and no other module is touched.

Lowered program for the trips=4 guard case (_active_solve), unoptimized stablehlo and then the optimized text: base 378,380 characters and 4,196 lines with two barriers, head 378,238 characters and 4,194 lines with none; optimized 1,679,851 against 1,679,255. The program is smaller by 142 characters of stablehlo and 596 of optimized text. The compile walls taken beside them, 2.496 s at base against 4.895 s at head, are not a controlled comparison: one sample each, on a shared allocation that was running a peer gate file at the time, so no compile-cost claim is made from them. The executable-size reading is deterministic and is the one to use.

Verification

The four guards pass, in one fresh JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu process: tests/test_solve_trips_are_a_scan.py reports 16 passed in 35.72 s, exit 0. At base the same file fails the four guards, each with assert 3 == 1.

Numerics are unchanged, compared bit for bit across two trees rather than two branches of one process. The base arm ran against a shadow package holding the base revision of fixed_point.py; the head arm ran against the worktree. Both arms froze four fixed small problems from the existing tests — the active-set solve at two and four trips on zeros(1), and the smooth solve at three and five steps on zeros(1) and on linspace(0.1, 0.9, 17) — as the hexadecimal bytes of seven result fields each: state, residual, trace, the before-residuals, the projected condition, the active-set residuals and the active-set mask differences. Twenty-eight of twenty-eight fields are bit-identical, and both arms report fixed_point resolving to their own file. The comparison is in numerics_base.json and numerics_head.json in the run directory.

Negative control, declared rather than run: reintroducing a second direct call of the qualified Krylov step outside the shared body brings the guard count back above one. The instrument is sensitive to the thing it names; what it could not separate was a duplicated body from a product barrier, and that is the reading this node corrects.

Stated limits. The real-operator reading on the diverted certificate rung could not be taken here: that test exceeds its 300 s pytest-timeout on a CPU allocation, both before and after the change, so the end-to-end bitwise reading is on the four fixed small problems only. The remaining gate modules ran in one sequential fresh-process run, one file per process. tests/test_fixed_point.py reports 45 passed in 440.48 s and tests/test_fixed_points.py 15 passed in 43.74 s. The two reduced Newton modules did not pass on this allocation and the reason is a clock, not an assertion. tests/test_reduced_newton_constraint_bounds.py gives 1 failed, 1 passed in 561.07 s, the failure being test_compensator_ceiling_refuses_without_changing_an_in_bound_receipt stopped on the 300 s pytest-timeout; tests/test_reduced_newton_termination.py aborts with INTERNALERROR … Timeout (>300.0s) and runs no test at all. Both sit in the same class as tests/test_reduced_newton.py, whose diverted-rung test times out identically on CPU at both the current head and a revision without this change, and both ran while this node's own program-size and numerics arms shared the allocation. No assertion in either module is reported as failing, so the node makes no claim either way about them: a delta on those two modules needs a clean allocation with the suite run alone, and that is recorded as a follow-on rather than inferred here.
