The cold fill spends its work on flux pairs

The diverted 282-cell carrier's published (5076, 282, 3) values entry took 12,452 s to build and 6.4 s to reload. On the same fleet CPU allocation, the pre-repair builder took 13.751, 28.918 and 96.965 s at 1, 4 and 16 points. The 4-to-16 slope was 5.670 s per point, reproducing the recorded 6.7 s scale.

The packed moment tile computes nine rows although the values entry consumes only the three flux rows. Its flux quadrature takes 64 × 128 nodes for a near source and 12 × 12 for a distant one; the previous batch applied the near rule to all pairs in a mixed 128-pair chunk. On this carrier only 16.9 of 282 sources per target are near on average. A flux-only probe cut the 16-point wall from 96.965 to 32.177 s; grouping near and distant pairs cut it to 4.247 s on the same allocation. The two costs are thus the unused field rows and the mixed-batch near rule, rather than recompilation per tile or reflection setup. For the complete entry, four concurrent batches preserve bitwise results on a 128-point check and shorten its wall from 20.733 to 12.603 s; one batch is warmed before the pool starts.

Values and complete entry

The repaired source builds only flux rows, using the same conditioned flux integral. It classifies pairs with that integral's distance rule, then batches near and distant pairs separately. Against the published entry at digest sha256:d1e1e0926e8d6d4e25f502a6da1d318ef4b75aab7100b225a99c1a94b6e7870f, 64 fixed points spanning the 5,076-point carrier have worst row-relative differences of 3.56e-15, 9.93e-15 and 9.14e-15; each is below 1e-12.

The complete (5076, 282, 3) entry built cold in an isolated scratch store in 755.543 s (build_seconds 755.072 s; 34,354,368 matrix bytes), with cache_hit=False and EXIT=0. Its digest is sha256:84998fb8546f1b3857d80072439d2482b05a4b57284d69d577cb25c916534980; the published entry remained at the original digest. Reading the saved scratch matrix back gives the same three 64-point row-relative differences as above. The complete wall is 16.48 times faster than the recorded 12,452 s, or 0.149 s averaged over all 5,076 points. That average is above 0.12 s; the specified 4-to-16-point marginal is 0.036 s. The full carrier has 16.9 near sources per point on average, while the first sixteen points have ten, so the small-slice marginal does not predict the full-grid average.
