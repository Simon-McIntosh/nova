Support read route: identity and step cost

The locked read now exists as one production step: the batched fixed-point solve, Bell-only membership read at those points, and current.book, in nova/equilibrium/support_read.py, with gs.apply taking the booked moments through an operand. It reproduces the recorded booking rows on the H200. The legacy support partition is still the production map's reader: no production caller has the state moments the locked read is parametrised by, so deleting it is blocked on one ruling (below).

Booked-moment error is the largest absolute difference between booked and exact-support moments over all cells and the three moment channels, divided by the largest exact moment, the measure the diverted 2,000 and 5,000-cell reference rows were recorded in (book-ten-thousand). Map flux error is the largest flux at any carrier node of the image of (booked minus exact) moments through the Biot kernels, divided by the axis-to-boundary flux span; exterior plus the exact-moment image is the closed form by construction, so it is the difference of the map output from the closed form. The closed-form state supplies the nodal jets directly, so these rows measure the read and the booking, not a plasma-coupling build. The recorded 1.075e-6 is the 5,000-cell diverted map flux error quoted in the membership ruling; no other map flux reference was recorded.

Each part is its own compiled program called with ready operands; the whole step is the one program support_read.read_and_book. Operand values are placeholders of the production shapes (one source per cell, a dense array over every carrier node, a 512-node row window), because the contraction and the solve are data independent in cost; the identity rows above use the closed-form jets. Booking is a sequential scan over cells in current.book and dominates the step; that is the cost the next measurement should attack, not the fixed points or the membership.

An X-point-only miss would otherwise read as a valid limited state: test_a_row_miss_at_the_x_point_alone_does_not_read_as_limited.
Deleted codeNone. Nothing was deleted because the legacy partition, the spline-clip support and the fixed-design read are still the only support reader a flux-only caller has (below). Removing them now would remove the forward solve.
BlockerThe locked read takes the state's booked moments (nodal jets are the exterior jets plus dense kernel rows contracted with those moments); ForwardFluxOperator._support_partition(psi) and every solver caller carry only the flux vector, and the moments are not recoverable from an arbitrary flux. A Picard iterate is the exterior plus the image of the previous booking, so its moments are the previous booking; a Newton trial flux has none. The route needs a ruling on how the solve carries the state moments (a moment-space state, or the previous booking carried beside the flux through the trip), and one on where the residual shadow comes from once the legacy read is gone. Exterior jets from the conductor kernels are also not yet an operator operand.
