One clause of it is not green and is measured rather than explained: psi_boundary does not match its raw counterpart in magnitude while psi_axis matches it exactly. The reader's own gate is green by delta on all nine covered files, and the node is recorded as partial.

Why the inverse negation goes rather than stays

The value was coupled, not internal: tests/test_pulsedesign.py::test_ids_file_cache shows psi_boundary tracking an input IDS field, so it arrives in the source read's convention. Under DD3 that is COCOS 11, whose psi is the negative of COCOS 17's, so -psi_11 was exactly the internal COCOS 17 flux and the negation was an implicit 11 → 17 conversion. The reader now performs that conversion once, so the negation would apply it twice. For a DD3 source the value handed on is unchanged (the factor is -1); for a DD4 source the sign is corrected, which is the defect the line carried.

The reader renormalises to psi_norm, and a psi sign reversal leaves (psi - psi_axis) / (psi_boundary - psi_axis) unchanged, so no normalised quantity moves and only a signed one can show the translation at all.

The open clause, and the wall that was never in the way

The measure's signed arm reaches its assertions. One field does not match, and it is the only clause of done-when (1) left red:

  1. psi_axis matches the raw global_quantities.psi_axis exactly under the 11 → 17 factor, on all three conforming pulses. The translation and its sign are therefore right, and the digit check reads the same observables the conversion uses.
  2. psi_boundary matches in sign and not in magnitude: reader -100.637 against raw 102.454 at slice 0 of CORSICA 130506/6, 1.8 percent on all three conforming pulses.
  3. Only one source is involved. nova/imas/equilibrium.py:510 feeds the dataset's psi_boundary from equilibrium/global_quantities/psi_boundary, the same path the raw read and the digit check use, so a second source is not the explanation. The candidates, none of them measured yet, are the reader's time index for itime=0 against raw time_slice[0], a renormalisation between the index read and the dataset, and a difference between the reader's walk and the test's lazy=False read.

A wall failure recorded earlier at this node is withdrawn, and the correction matters more than the failure did. The reader was reported as dying in nova/frame/ on the machine-description read. It does not: constructed as EquilibriumData(pulse, run, dd_version=...), it builds a DD3 pulse end to end. The KeyError was reached only by the earlier fixture, which was built around an already-opened IDS and so carried no URI and no Data Dictionary major of its own. The pre-existing nova/frame defect is real when the machine is read that way, but it was never this node's to clear, and the Wall(...dd_version=...) change landed against it stands on its own as a correctness fix rather than as the repair of a blocker.

Two defects on the way were this node's to find and are fixed, each with the line named. And one instruction in the brief was wrong, which cost most of a node's time:

  • nova/imas/scenario.py:89 stored ids_properties.homogeneous_time as the IMAS node itself, and the zarr group refuses to serialise an IDSInt0D, so a first read of an uncached pulse could not store at all. Now int(...).
  • nova/imas/equilibrium.py's wall property read the machine at the latest published major rather than the pulse's. It now carries ids_dd_version.
  • The brief's version source is wrong. The written DD versions this node was told to take from tests/data-manifest.json are not there. They are in tests/test_dd_translator.py, in DD3_PULSES and NON_CONFORMING: CORSICA 130506/6 at 3.41.0, DINA 105052/1 at 3.38.1, DINA 105069/3 at 3.39.0, and DINA 105014/1 and 105013/1, both at 3.38.1.
  • The fixture's construction was the reason the machine looked like it defaulted to DD4. EquilibriumData(ids=..., filename=...) leaves the reader's own URI and major unset, and the machine read then falls back to the latest published major. The tree's own idiom, used by tests/test_database.py, is EquilibriumData(pulse, run, dd_version=...), which carries both. The earlier reading of this node's failure as a machine-major defect came from the fixture, not the reader.

Negative control

Declared: bypass the translation in EquilibriumData in a scratch copy, and a signed assertion fails on its own assertion. Still not admissible, but no longer for the earlier reason. The reader now builds a live pulse, so a mutation can reach its assertion; the log on disk at logs/negative-control-bypassed-translation.log is the pre-fix attempt and is not the control. It stays inadmissible until the psi_boundary clause above is green, because while the unmutated head fails that same id, a bypass and the head fail the same test and the outcome cannot distinguish them. The recipe is recorded in the run manifest: mutate build_convention to skip the per-field translated() call, and require the run to fail on its own psi_axis assertion, which is the id already matching under the unmutated head.
