DD equilibrium translator

landing: one entry point, nova.io.sourcemap.translate_equilibrium, now translates a Data Dictionary equilibrium read into COCOS 17 or refuses it by name. It declares each equilibrium quantity as a source-map row whose convention_factor comes from nova_cocos.convention_transform between the convention the read's DD version carries and COCOS 17, and it refuses through the same map's blocked path when the digits measured from the data name a convention other than the one its DD version carries. determine_digits moved out of nova/scripts/identify_source_cocos.py into nova/io/sourcemap.py, cocos_from_digits and the shim nova/io/cocos.py are deleted, and the four benchmarks read nova_cocos directly. The sourcemap can carry an equilibrium IDS read, so no new module was added. One done-when clause is not met as the brief states it and needs a decision: on a full-pulse scan DINA 105013/1 measures sigma_rho_theta_phi = −1 and is refused, and the census read that digit as underivable from its sampled slices.

The sourcemap carries the read; the entry point is one function, not a module. The brief's fallback — record in this fragment why the sourcemap cannot carry the read, then add only the entry point — was not needed. SourceSignal already holds source_convention, target_convention, transformation and a derived convention_factor, and SourceSignalMap.blocked is already the refusal channel, so an equilibrium read is one row per quantity and one BlockedSignal on failure, with no new type. What the map did need was the two facts a DD read has that a MAST archive read does not: the convention its DD version carries, and the digits measured from its own observables. Both are added there. translate_equilibrium's docstring states the two conventions — DD3 data is COCOS 11, DD4 data is COCOS 17, nova works in COCOS 17 — and that e_Bp = 1 and sigma_RphiZ = +1 are fixed by the DD version while sigma_Bp and sigma_rho_theta_phi come from ip, psi_axis, psi_boundary, q and b0. Twelve quantities are declared: profiles_2d/psi, profiles_1d/psi, the two radial-boundary and interior flux nodes, global_quantities/ip, q_axis, q_95, psi_axis and psi_boundary, boundary_separatrix/psi, vacuum_toroidal_field/b0 and the two flux derivatives.

(1) The DD3 read translates by the analytic COCOS 11 to 17 mapping, and the DD4 fixture passes through untouched. CORSICA 130506/6 is written at DD 3.41.0 and carries 55 slices above 1 MA; every one is accepted, and on every one every declared signal carries source_convention = 11 and target_convention = 17 with a convention_factor equal to the factor the two conventions' digits compose to. That factor is −1 on psi, psi_axis, psi_boundary, boundary_separatrix/psi and the two flux derivatives, and +1 on ip, b0, q, q_axis and q_95: the flux and its radial derivative change sign, the current, the toroidal field and the safety factor do not. The factors asserted in tests/test_dd_translator.py are transcribed from the two conventions' digits inside that file (COCOS_11 = (+1,1,+1,+1), COCOS_17 = (-1,1,+1,+1)) rather than imported from the algebra, so a change to either side of the map has to disagree with the arithmetic written in the test instead of with itself, and each row is checked twice — once on its declared factor and once by applying it to a sample array. The repository's DD 4.1.0 conversion of the same case (iter_corsica_130506 in tests/data-manifest.json, cached at ~/.cache/imas-efit-publish-169/ where its sha256 matches the manifest digest) is opened at DD 4.1.0 and every quantity's factor is exactly 1.0 with the converted samples equal to the raw samples bit for bit, NaNs included — a DD4 read is neither rescaled, re-signed nor re-ordered on the way in. One digit is deliberately left unchecked there: the DD4 reference layer carries no psi_axis, so sigma_Bp is undetermined on it, and an undetermined digit is left unchecked rather than guessed, which is what lets a DD4 read whose flux node is unpopulated still pass through. The DD3 read of the same case, 130506/403, is then compared against the DD4 fixture at their shared times: the translated boundary flux flips sign to match the fixture's, and q agrees to within 2 % — the mapping itself leaves q at a factor of exactly 1.0, and the residual is the difference between two independent reconstructions of the case rather than a convention effect.

The refusal is the map's own blocked path, and it states the DD version, the expected COCOS and the measured digits, by name. DINA 105014/1 reads: “DINA 105014/1 is written at DD 3.38.1, which nova reads as COCOS 11, but its measured digits are sigma_Bp=-1, e_Bp=1, sigma_RphiZ=+1, sigma_rho_theta_phi=+1, which name COCOS 17 — the data does not conform to its own Data Dictionary version”, with the unmet condition beside it (“a conforming DD 3.38.1 equilibrium carries COCOS 11: sigma_Bp=+1 and sigma_rho_theta_phi=+1”). The two 5.3 T pulses accept 2,077 slices with zero refusals, so the guard is discriminating rather than refusing everything it is handed.

The departure from the brief's clause (2), stated as a measurement rather than smoothed over. The brief lists DINA 105013/1 among the pulses accepted. It is refused. The census that produced that list read the pulse as “σBp+1 (σρθφ not derivable: q or B0 unpopulated) … consistent with 11; the full convention is not determinable from the sampled slices”, from 20 sampled slices above 1 MA. Two facts decide it against that reading. First, the full scan above 1 MA returns 766 slices, not 20, and q_95 is populated on every one of the pulse's 2,352 slices, so the digit the census could not derive is derivable. Second, and load-bearing, the reason it was read as underivable: the Data Dictionary declares equilibrium/vacuum_toroidal_field/b0 a b0_like COCOS-dependent field (queried through the DD service, DD 3), so its sign carries convention information and is not a magnitude. With its sign used, 105013/1 measures (sigma_Bp=+1, sigma_rho_theta_phi=−1) on 766 of 766 slices and does not name COCOS 11. The two pulses are a mirrored pair and the mirroring is coherent: they are the only two 2.65 T pulses in the set, 105014/1 stores B0 negative and reads exactly COCOS 17's digits, 105013/1 stores it positive and reads COCOS 17's q-mirror, and the two 5.3 T pulses store B0 negative with q>0 and name COCOS 11 on every slice. Leaning: keep the guard strict and treat 105013/1 as genuinely non-conforming — a check that accepts a measured sigma_rho_theta_phi contradiction is not a conformance check, and a guard that passes its own test by not applying is worse than no guard. The brief's clause (2) as written is the deliverable that is wrong, not the translation. If the coordinator rules instead that the pulse must be accepted, the decision needed is which digit the DD3 path should not read — that is a change to the physics of the check, not to its implementation, and the acceptance list and this table then both change with it.

The literal negative control. Negating psi_axis and psi_boundary on a conforming DD3 slice of CORSICA 130506/6 — a slice the map accepts unmodified — flips sigma_Bp to −1, which is COCOS 17's digit, and the read is then refused by name: no signals, one blocked row, whose reason contains the source name, the DD version 3.41.0, sigma_Bp=-1 and COCOS 17. The mutated slice is byte-for-byte the same data as the accepted one apart from that sign flip, so the verdict follows the flux and not the pulse name. Log: logs/negative_control.log.
