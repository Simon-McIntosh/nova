The iter-passive-plasma-circuit section 6 reference: revised

The reference choice is revised, not confirmed, and the threshold it is judged against is the stated 1.0e-3. Two facts carry the decision.

First, the repository’s flux-function sense is still the one nova/equilibrium/convention.py books at this base, and the stored profiles read anti-parallel to the map under it: the profile current integrates to a median of −1.13, −1.09 and −1.10 times each slice’s own Ip while the map current integrates to 0.97–1.00 of it. So the reported inconsistency is dominated by a known sign defect in the owner rather than by the sources. That bounds what the numbers can say about the sources: a pure sign disagreement with equal magnitudes puts the relative residual near 2, not above it.

Second, that bound separates the sources anyway. The two DINA pulses sit at 1.73 and 1.71, the sign floor, and this measure does not distinguish them (1.2 %). CORSICA 130506/6 sits at 7.93, four and a half times the floor, which a sign flip cannot produce: its flux functions disagree with its own map in magnitude and shape as well. Its relative form is in any case unusable on the slices whose local profile current crosses zero, where it reaches 9.79e15.

The decision this node records: CORSICA 130506/6 is not confirmed as the section 6 reference and should not be chosen on this measure; between the two DINA pulses edge consistency does not discriminate, so no source is confirmed on it and the choice must rest on grounds this measure does not cover.
