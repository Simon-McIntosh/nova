The edge-consistency measure on the conforming ITER sources

The three forms of nova/imas/edge_consistency.py are reported for DINA 105052/1, DINA 105069/3 and CORSICA 130506/6, each read through PlasmaSource.from_imas — the route whose every convention-bearing quantity passes through nova.io.sourcemap.translate_equilibrium, so a DD3 read leaves the reader in nova’s internal COCOS 17. The two refused pulses, DINA 105014/1 and 105013/1, are read through the same route and appear as refused rows with the digit their data contradicts.

The route is proved before the data are read

The analytic lower-single-null state is the instrument that tells a route defect from a data defect, because all three of its answers are known exactly. Written into an equilibrium IDS in a scratch database at DD 3 in the COCOS 11 sense a DD3 write carries and read back through the same route, the state returns the grid current integrating to +0.99491 of its own Ip, the profile current integrating to +0.99830, and a band residual of 5.8001e−4, below EDGE_BAND_RELATIVE_THRESHOLD = 1.0e−3. That is the analytic floor the measure’s own record reports, so the route hands the measure the operands it needs.

The two operand scalings follow from COCOS 11 and COCOS 17 sharing e_Bp = 1: the translator’s factor is a sign flip on every psi-like and derivative-like row and no 2π enters. A first run that applied 2π to the map and 1/2π to the derivatives is what the round trip refused. Nothing else in the route was adjusted, and no sign was flipped to make a ratio come out positive.

The stored profiles_2d grid is not uniformly spaced while the structured-grid stencil is, so the map is resampled onto a uniform grid carrying the stored point counts over the stored extents — and it must be resampled with a cubic spline. A piecewise-linear interpolant has a discontinuous second derivative at every stored node, which is exactly what the stencil differentiates, and the round trip’s band residual grows under refinement with it (1.7163e−1 at 65 points, 8.4353e−1 at 129, 1.5573e0 at 257, 2.3824e0 at 513). With the spline it falls and plateaus at the state’s own floor (5.8001e−4, 1.7582e−4, 1.6165e−4, 1.8364e−4).

Recommendation for the iter-passive-plasma-circuit §6 reference

This measure does not support keeping CORSICA 130506/6 as the §6 reference. Its band-relative median is 7.93, the largest of the three, its maximum is 9.79e15 on one slice, and it clears the threshold on 24 of 24 sampled slices; DINA 105052/1 has the smallest median on all three forms (1.73 band relative, 0.767 whole slice) and the most stable integral current check. Given that every source clears the threshold on every slice, the recommendation is to not settle §6 on the strength of edge consistency alone; if the section needs the measure to choose, DINA 105052/1 is the better candidate and CORSICA 130506/6 is the worst of the three.

What the recommendation rests on: the operand scaling, which the analytic IDS round trip settles at three exact answers rather than by assertion; the grid current, which integrates to the slice’s own Ip within 0.3 per cent on all 72 sampled slices and so shows the map side of the comparison to be sound; and the threshold, fixed on the analytic state before any IDS row was read.

Which sign the operator assumes, stated rather than implied: nova.equilibrium.convention pins jφ = −2π(R p′ + FF′/(μ0R)) with Δ☆Φ = −2πμ0R jφ and the total flux Φ = 2πψ, so p′ and FF′ mean derivatives against the negated flux (p′ = −dp/dΦ), not against ψ. That is the convention the map side satisfies here, and the check that says so is its own integral: the grid current integrates to +1.0 times the slice’s stored Ip, and a toroidal current density shares its sign with the current it integrates to, so the map side is consistent with the operator and with the pulse. The translation does not match it on the flux functions, which is the −1 above. No sign was flipped anywhere to make a ratio come out positive.

What would overturn it, stated plainly: the profile current integrates to about −1.1 times Ip on all three sources while the grid current integrates to +1.0 — the two sides of the residual are of equal magnitude and opposite sign, which is the signature of a sign convention rather than of a physical mismatch, and it is what drives a band residual of order one. The analytic round trip cannot separate the two, because the state is written with the same sign on its flux map and on its flux functions: a route that negates both, or neither, returns the same round trip, and only a real DD3 file can adjudicate. If that inversion is a read-convention defect — the sign the translator takes on profiles_1d/dpressure_dpsi and profiles_1d/f_df_dpsi, or the assumption that a DD3 file’s flux map and its derivative rows flip together — then every band residual above measures that sign, all three sources must be re-measured, and the ranking between them could invert. If it is real, then all three sources’ stored flux functions stand anti-parallel to their flux maps, which is a finding about the data and not a choice of reference. The bounded check that separates them is named under the gate below and was not reached in this node’s budget.
