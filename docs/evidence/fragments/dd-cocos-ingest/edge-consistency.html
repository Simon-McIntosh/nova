A measured edge consistency between flux functions and flux map

The measure exists as nova/imas/edge_consistency.py and is proved on the analytic lower-single-null state. Its band residual falls at a fitted order of 1.97 against the central-difference stencil’s theoretical 2.00; a one per cent perturbation of FF′ over the whole plasma is detected above the fixed threshold; and the literal negative control — FF′ perturbed only in the edge band, with the flux map left byte-identical — is reported inconsistent. The IDS table and the reference recommendation are the section’s other two done-when items and are carried by a separate node on top of this one.

The grid-operator read is lifted

The inline structured-grid Grad–Shafranov read inside mast_parity_chain._profile_reproduction_residuals is now the single function edge_consistency.structured_grid_current_density. It assembles the central-difference operator and returns −Δ*Φ / (TOTAL_FLUX_FACTOR · μ0 R) in A/m2, arithmetic for arithmetic. The parity chain calls it and changed in no other way, so its per-slice residuals are value-identical to the base: test_profile_reproduction_is_current_density_rms_against_grid_read pins the lifted arithmetic to a closed form at 10−12 relative and passes unchanged at the head.

The operator takes the total poloidal flux Φ = 2πψ in Wb on a uniform (Z, R) grid, the quantity nova’s internal COCOS 17 chain carries. The profile side is formed by nova.equilibrium.convention.toroidal_current_density, the single statement of that chain, so the two currents are commensurate by construction rather than by a re-derived textbook formula.

Three forms, and only the band forms judge the edge

The measure returns an EdgeConsistency record of three numbers, each over its own cell set.

  • whole_slice_relative = RMS(jφ,profile − jφ,grid) / max|jφ,grid| over the scored cells of the whole slice: the parity chain’s own figure, judged against the bound the chain already registers for that field.
  • band_absolute = the same RMS residual in A/m2 over the edge band, the cells with 1 − EDGE_BAND_WIDTH ≤ ψN ≤ 1.
  • band_relative = the RMS of the residual relative to the local profile current over those same band cells.

The band forms never borrow the slice-wide denominator, so a slice whose total current is large cannot flatter its own edge. EDGE_BAND_WIDTH is a named constant (0.10 in normalised flux, which is 307 cells of the 65 by 65 grid on the analytic state) and band_width is an argument.

The edge threshold is fixed from the analytic state, before any IDS row

The edge criterion is judged on band_relative against EDGE_BAND_RELATIVE_THRESHOLD = 1.0e-3, a named constant in edge_consistency.py. It is not a ScorecardField, and nova/imas/parity_tolerances.py is unchanged.

The basis is measured, not chosen. On the 65 by 65 grid the IDS profile reads carry — the grid nova/imas/diiid_description.py asserts — the analytic state’s own second-order discretisation error puts the floor at 5.96e−4, while a one per cent FF′ perturbation over the whole plasma moves the same figure to 2.32e−3. The bound sits 1.7 times above the floor and 2.3 times below the perturbation, and it was fixed before any IDS row was read.

The residual falls at the stencil’s second order

On CerfonFreidbergSingleNull, whose grad_shafranov_source gives the exact strong-form source, the measure is evaluated on four uniform grids over R ∈ [1.08, 2.30] and Z ∈ [−1.10, 1.00]. That state’s flux functions are the two Solov’ev constants, read off the exact source by matching the constant and R2 terms of Δ*Φ = 4π2(μ0R2p′ + FF′); they reproduce the analytic jφ to machine precision, which is what makes the only remaining error the stencil’s own.

A least-squares fit of log band_relative against log ΔR over the four resolution runs gives a slope of 1.97; the same fit on whole_slice_relative gives 1.97. Both are the central-difference stencil’s theoretical second order. Each refinement by two halves the residual, the factor 22 of a second-order scheme.

The perturbation is detected, and the band-only control is refused

Scaling FF′ by 1.01 over the whole plasma, with the flux map left exact, moves band_relative from 5.9634e−4 to 2.3200e−3 — 3.9 times the consistent value and 2.3 times the threshold. The perturbation is detected.

The literal negative control is the band-only case: FF′ is scaled by 1.01 for ψN ≥ 0.90 and left exact everywhere else, while the flux map is the same array in both runs — the test asserts array_equal on the two flux maps and the same band cell count — so every cell of the difference comes from the profile current the perturbed flux function drives. The measure reports band_relative = 2.3200e−3, above EDGE_BAND_RELATIVE_THRESHOLD. A measure that passed that state could not confirm a reference.
