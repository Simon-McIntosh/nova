The source switch is distinct from the reported active set

Four masks play different roles: axis-connected plasma cells, profile participation, residual-shadow nodes frozen for a Newton solve, and the pointwise ψN cutoff inside the source evaluator. The last can switch while the other three do not. On 137D, the final direction crosses that cutoff at (R, Z) = (1.211875 m, 0.800000 m), on the upper inboard shoulder rather than near the lower X-point. The sample changes from ψN = 1.000000000802 at step fraction 10−7 to 0.999999994079 at 10−6. Connectivity, participation, residual shadow and diverted class are unchanged.

nova/equilibrium/source.py:_FluxSelectedProfile.current_density selects zero current above ψN = 1 when no common-SOL profile is supplied. The DIII-D builder supplies value-only coupling blocks to ForwardProfile.from_lattice, selecting nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py:_point_current_moments. Its cell-average stencil has weights 5/6 at the centre and 1/24 at each axial neighbour (nova/equilibrium/forward.py:_cubic_cell_average_stencil). Applying this smooth-density quadrature across a hard pointwise cutoff gives a finite jump when one sample crosses the boundary.
