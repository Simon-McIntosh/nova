The contract: the booked integral is continuous where the point route steps

An EFIT profile carries a finite toroidal current density on the last closed flux surface and zero beyond it. The booked route integrates that density over the polygon a cell's support cuts out of each sampled cell, so the integral follows the flux level continuously as the level sweeps across a cell. The value-only route applies the five-node cubic cell-average rule to densities taken at cell centres: a cell whose centre crosses the level drops a weighted contribution from the finite edge value to zero at once, and the integral takes a step.

The contract lives in tests/equilibrium/test_finite_edge_booking.py on an analytic field, and it is stated as an analytic comparison at an ordinary cut cell and on an X-point sector. Each positive-control step is small against the scale of the quantity being integrated, and the negative control is the present point-current route, which must reproduce the jump.

The tangent matches a central difference away from the crossing

book_tangent is checked against the analytic derivative of the same integral at each bracketing step, and agrees to eleven significant figures. The exact crossing level is an entry event for the normal-form fan: the value is continuous there while the derivative steps, so a central difference taken across the crossing measures the event rather than the derivative, and the brackets sit clear of it.

What the rows establish, and what they do not

The booked route removes the finite-edge step the diagnosis identified, on the cohort's own rows as well as on the analytic field.
