DIII-D forward convergence: what stops each gate frame

All five DIII-D gate frames fail the registered 1e-5 fixed-point criterion, at relative residuals from 0.0273 to 4.28. The banked receipts localise the failure to one mechanism on four frames and to a refusal upstream of the fixed point on the fifth. It is not an alternating-mask switch: the active set has stopped changing (mask difference zero) at the terminal promotion of every branch, and the relative residual is bit-identical across the last two promotions. The inner Newton–Krylov solve accepts no further promotion at that frozen mask, and the outer active-set loop then terminates on mask stability rather than on the residual criterion.

Is the stall the mechanism, or the consequence of a plasma that left the vessel?

The frozen-mask terminal is established for four frames. Whether it is the cause of the non-convergence or the symptom of a plasma that has lost its position or its current is a separate question, and the banked record answers it only partly:

  • Frame 146's kept terminal places its magnetic axis at Z = +1.56 m, above a vessel whose top is near +0.6 m, and its solved saddle outside the wall as well: the terminal state is not a confined plasma, and the residual it stalls at (5.50e-2) is the mismatch of a plasma that has left the machine rather than of a converged one.
  • Frame 44's kept terminal achieves the limited topology at residual 4.28 — the largest of the cohort by two orders of magnitude — while its seed requested diverted. A terminal that changes topology and misses its current by that much is a lost plasma, not a slow contraction.
  • Frames 179 and 137 terminate with the topology their seed asked for and residuals of 4.18e-1 and 6.24e-2; 179's solved axis sits inside the wall but low and inboard of the label's, which is the displacement the vertical-force-balance plan already documents.

Pending measurement. The both-branch probe records, per promotion and per branch, the axis position and its wall containment, the area of the plasma support, and the toroidal current the profile carries inside its confined core against the frame's target. It also runs a continued arm with the mask-stability exits disabled, so a loop that would otherwise declare settlement has to spend its whole trip budget at the frozen mask. Those are the numbers that separate a stall which is the mechanism from a stall that is what is left after the plasma leaves. The probe is running as one job (sched/1307367, H200) over all five frames; its receipt is convergence-receipt.json's measured_branches block, and until it lands the mechanism below is stated as the terminal symptom, not as an established cause.

The branch decision on frames 179 and 44

Both frames run two seeds from the bank's own cold portfolio and record both terminal residuals. The lower-residual terminal is discarded, and the rule that discards it is in the benchmark itself, not in a policy document:

  • benchmarks/diiid_forward_gs_match.py:2381 binds diverted = receipts[TopologyClass.DIVERTED] — the limited receipt is built and then never returned.
  • benchmarks/diiid_forward_gs_match.py:2406 accepts a branch only when branches["diverted"]["converged"] and branches["diverted"]["topology_consistent"].
  • benchmarks/diiid_forward_gs_match.py:2437 returns diverted.equilibrium unconditionally, so the limited seed's equilibrium cannot reach the gate however low its residual.

Verdict. The limited state is not a distinct limited root the bank should have preferred: on both frames the limited seed's own terminal topology is diverted. What the bank discards is a lower-residual diverted terminal reached from the limited seed, at 15.3× and 21.7× below the terminal it keeps. It is a lower stall of the same topology, not a root of the other branch, and it is itself above the 1e-5 criterion on both frames. Preferring it would improve the residual without converging anything; the mechanism above is what has to be fixed.

Recommended fix

Module: nova/equilibrium/fixed_point.py, the active-set loop in _active_set_newton_krylov and its reconcile reduction.

Change: make the outer loop's terminal condition fail closed on the residual. Today settled and stagnated (the two conditions that stop the loop at a stable mask) are reachable while selected_residual > convergence_tolerance, because both require only selected_difference == 0 and zero accepted promotions. Two edits, in this order:

  1. Carry the inner step-acceptance receipt. Publish attempted_newton_promotions and accepted_newton_promotions per trip on FixedPointResult and through ForwardSolveReceipt. The banked receipts do not record them, so the reason the frozen-mask step is refused is currently invisible; this node could not measure it.
  2. Distinguish a refused step from a converged mask. When a trip ends with mask difference == 0, zero accepted promotions and a residual above tolerance, return a distinct termination reason (ACTIVE_SET_STEP_REFUSED) and keep the loop alive to its trip budget rather than declaring settlement. A branch that exhausts the budget at a frozen mask must report the residual it stalled at, which is what the gate already reads.

Frames it should converge: 137 and 179 first — they are already diverted-consistent and the smallest residuals of the cohort (6.24e-2 and — on its limited seed — 2.73e-2) — then 44 and 146. Frame 144 is a separate defect upstream of the fixed point: its axis identification returns no qualified candidate, and no change to the active-set loop can help it.
