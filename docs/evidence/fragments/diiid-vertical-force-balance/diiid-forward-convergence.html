DIII-D forward convergence: what stops each gate frame

All five DIII-D gate frames fail the registered 1e-5 fixed-point criterion, at relative residuals from 0.0273 to 4.28. The banked receipts localise the failure to one mechanism on four frames and to a refusal upstream of the fixed point on the fifth. It is not an alternating-mask switch: the active set has stopped changing (mask difference zero) at the terminal promotion of every branch, and the relative residual is bit-identical across the last two promotions. The inner Newton–Krylov solve does keep accepting its steps — 24 of 24 attempted on the measured frame — and the promoted state reproduces the same residual to the bit, so the outer loop either stops on mask stability or spends its whole trip budget without moving. On the bank that is the outer active-set loop terminating on mask stability rather than on the residual criterion.

Is the stall the mechanism, or the consequence of a plasma that left the vessel?

The frozen-mask terminal is established for four frames. Whether it is the cause of the non-convergence or the symptom of a plasma that has lost its position or its current is a separate question, and the banked record answers it only partly:

  • Frame 146's kept terminal places its magnetic axis at Z = +1.56 m, above a vessel whose top is near +0.6 m, and its solved saddle outside the wall as well: the terminal state is not a confined plasma, and the residual it stalls at (5.50e-2) is the mismatch of a plasma that has left the machine rather than of a converged one.
  • Frame 44's kept terminal achieves the limited topology at residual 4.28 — the largest of the cohort by two orders of magnitude — while its seed requested diverted. A terminal that changes topology and misses its current by that much is a lost plasma, not a slow contraction.
  • Frames 179 and 137 terminate with the topology their seed asked for and residuals of 4.18e-1 and 6.24e-2; 179's solved axis sits inside the wall but low and inboard of the label's, which is the displacement the vertical-force-balance plan already documents.

Frame 44's diverted seed carries 1.097× the target current with its axis inside the wall, and it still terminates at 4.28 — the largest residual of the cohort — while achieving the limited topology. Its limited seed starts with a mask difference of 0: the partition never changes from the first trip, so there is nothing for a promotion to act on. So on this frame too the plasma is present and the miss is not plasma loss; what differs between the two seeds is the state they enter, not a path the loop fails to travel.

Two facts follow, and both sharpen the verdict rather than merely confirming it. First, the stall is not a slow contraction that the settled exit cuts short: with that exit removed the loop spends all 16 trips and the residual is bit-identical on every one of them, so continuing buys nothing. Second, it is not a plasma that has left the vessel on this frame: the axis sits inside the wall on both seeds, and the confined core carries 1.057× the target current on the limited seed and 0.763× on the diverted one. What stalls is the outer promotion itself — every attempted inner step is accepted (24 of 24) and the promotion still returns the same residual, so the fixed-point map is idempotent at that state.

The area, corrected. The probe first reported a closed plasma area of 0.0 m² on every arm beside a confined current of about 1 MA. That was a defect in the measurement, not in the plasma: the area was counted from a mask attribute the domain partition does not carry, and the handler that exists so one bad read cannot kill a frame turned the error into an all-false mask. The area is now the partition's own CORE label — the cells the field closes around the axis and connects to — and it is re-derived from the saved grids without re-solving. Re-derived: frame 179 reads 1.233 m² diverted and 1.833 m² limited against a label field of 1.679 m²; frame 44 reads 1.870 m² diverted and 1.126 m² limited against 1.248 m². The measure carries its controls in presence_control.py: the frame's own EFIT label field, a known closed plasma, must read a positive area, and the counting arithmetic must reproduce a disc of known area to within twenty percent. Both hold. The area now agrees with the current read, and both say the plasma is confined at every one of these terminals.

Verdict. The limited state is not a distinct limited root the bank should have preferred: on both frames the limited seed's own terminal topology is diverted. What the bank discards is a lower-residual diverted terminal reached from the limited seed, at 15.3× and 21.7× below the terminal it keeps. It is a lower stall of the same topology, not a root of the other branch, and it is itself above the 1e-5 criterion on both frames. Preferring it would improve the residual without converging anything; the mechanism above is what has to be fixed.

Recommended fix

Module: nova/equilibrium/fixed_point.py, the active-set loop in _active_set_newton_krylov and its reconcile reduction.

Change: make the outer loop's terminal condition fail closed on the residual. Today settled and stagnated (the two conditions that stop the loop at a stable mask) are reachable while selected_residual > convergence_tolerance, because both require only selected_difference == 0. The measured arms put that in the sharpest form: on frame 179 ACTIVE_SET_SETTLED fires at a residual 2.7e-2 with 24 of 24 inner steps accepted, and with the exit removed the loop spends its full 16 trips at the same residual. A stable mask is therefore not evidence of a converged fixed point — it is evidence that the outer map stopped changing the state. Two edits, in this order:

  1. Carry the inner step-acceptance receipt. Publish attempted_newton_promotions and accepted_newton_promotions per trip on FixedPointResult and through ForwardSolveReceipt. The banked receipts do not record them; the probe reconstructs the counts from globalisation_decisions for one frame and cannot do so for the rest. A frozen mask with every step accepted is a different defect from a frozen mask with the step refused, and the receipt cannot currently tell them apart.
  2. Distinguish a refused step from a converged mask. When a trip ends with mask difference == 0, zero accepted promotions and a residual above tolerance, return a distinct termination reason (ACTIVE_SET_STEP_REFUSED) and keep the loop alive to its trip budget rather than declaring settlement. A branch that exhausts the budget at a frozen mask must report the residual it stalled at, which is what the gate already reads.

Frames it should converge: 137 and 179 first — they are already diverted-consistent and the smallest residuals of the cohort (6.24e-2 and — on its limited seed — 2.73e-2) — then 44 and 146. Frame 144 is a separate defect upstream of the fixed point: its axis identification returns no qualified candidate, and no change to the active-set loop can help it.
