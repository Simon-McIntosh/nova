DIII-D forward convergence: what stops each gate frame

All five DIII-D gate frames fail the registered 1e-5 fixed-point criterion, at relative residuals from 0.0273 to 4.28. The banked receipts localise the failure to one mechanism on four frames and to a refusal upstream of the fixed point on the fifth. It is not an alternating-mask switch: the active set has stopped changing (mask difference zero) at the terminal promotion of every branch, and the relative residual is bit-identical across the last two promotions. All ten seeds are measured below. Nine of them stall with the plasma still confined and carrying its current; one (frame 146's diverted seed) is a plasma driven to the top of the vessel and lost; one (frame 144's limited seed) refuses at axis identification before any promotion runs. The inner Newton–Krylov solve does keep accepting its steps — 24 of 24 attempted on the measured frame — and the promoted state reproduces the same residual to the bit, so the outer loop either stops on mask stability or spends its whole trip budget without moving. On the bank that is the outer active-set loop terminating on mask stability rather than on the residual criterion.

No frame shows a period-2 cycle or a divergence. That reading required correcting the classifier twice. The earlier rule called an exactly flat tail a cycle (it tested the alternating pairs for equality); the replacement tested the half-to-half separation for inequality, which fires on any monotone tail and duly labelled frame 146's 0.128 → 0.222 → 0.065 → 0.055 a period-2 cycle. The rule now requires what a period-2 tail is — each half repeating the one before it, [a, b, a, b], with the two levels separated — and the control drives all three inputs (classifier_control.py: a plateau and a monotone tail read as stalls, a true alternation reads as a cycle). The terminal class in the receipt is recomputed from each arm's recorded residual history, so no re-solve was needed to apply the correction. Cycle damping is 0 at every trip of every branch, so the descent is neither being damped nor rescued by damping. The residuals are strictly above tolerance at every terminal and none of the five satisfies the registered criterion.

Is the stall the mechanism, or the consequence of a plasma that left the vessel?

The frozen-mask terminal is established for four frames. Whether it is the cause of the non-convergence or the symptom of a plasma that has lost its position or its current is a separate question, and the banked record answers it only partly:

  • Frame 146 — holds, measured below. Its kept terminal places the magnetic axis at Z = +1.56 m, which is 0.10 m below the top of the wall (the vessel spans Z = −1.664 to +1.664 m), with the closed core collapsed to 8 cells and 0.043 m² against a confined current of 0.039× the target. A column driven to the top of the vessel carrying four percent of its current is a plasma lost by vertical displacement, and the residual it stalls at is the mismatch that displacement leaves.
  • Frame 44 — does not hold, measured below. Its kept terminal does achieve the limited topology at residual 4.28, the largest of the cohort, while its seed requested diverted. The inference drawn from that in the banked record — a lost plasma — is falsified by the re-solve: the confined core carries 1.097× the target current with its axis inside the wall. A topology change alone does not establish plasma loss, because the achieved class is a property of the terminal state and the current is a separate measurement.
  • Frames 179 and 137 terminate with the topology their seed asked for and residuals of 4.18e-1 and 6.24e-2; 179's solved axis sits inside the wall but low and inboard of the label's, which is the displacement the vertical-force-balance plan already documents.

Frame 44's diverted seed carries 1.097× the target current with its axis inside the wall, and it still terminates at 4.28 — the largest residual of the cohort — while achieving the limited topology. Its limited seed starts with a mask difference of 0: the partition never changes from the first trip, so there is nothing for a promotion to act on. So on this frame too the plasma is present and the miss is not plasma loss; what differs between the two seeds is the state they enter, not a path the loop fails to travel.

Two facts follow, and both sharpen the verdict rather than merely confirming it. First, the stall is not a slow contraction that the settled exit cuts short: with that exit removed the loop spends all 16 trips and the residual is bit-identical on every one of them, so continuing buys nothing. Second, it is not a plasma that has left the vessel on this frame: the axis sits inside the wall on both seeds, and the confined core carries 1.057× the target current on the limited seed and 0.763× on the diverted one. What stalls is the outer promotion itself — every attempted inner step is accepted (24 of 24) and the promotion still returns the same residual, so the fixed-point map is idempotent at that state.

Frame 144, diverted seed. Its limited seed refuses at axis identification, but its diverted seed solves, and it is the clearest instance of the pattern: the mask difference is 0 at the first trip and stays there, the shipped policy settles after one trip at residual 5.927, the continued arm spends sixteen trips at the same number, and every inner step is accepted (24 of 24). The plasma is again present — axis at R=1.759, Z=+0.013, inside the wall, and 1.073× the target current — and the achieved topology is the diverted one its seed asked for. A fixed-point map that begins already stationary explains all of these terminals at once: the residual the gate reads is set by the state the seed enters, and no promotion the loop can take moves it.

Frame 137, limited seed. The same mechanism, and the cleanest instance of it in the cohort: the mask difference is 11 → 0 and the residual repeats bit-identically from the first trip onward, the shipped policy settles at ACTIVE_SET_SETTLED after two trips at 5.683e-02, the continued arm spends all sixteen trips at that same number, and all 24 inner steps are accepted. The plasma is present: axis at R=1.695, Z=−0.136, inside the wall, confined current 1.091× the target over 383 closed core cells (2.035 m²). Its achieved topology is the diverted one, against its limited seed. This frame is the one the banked trajectory put at 6.24e-2, and the re-solve reaches a lower number (5.68e-2) at the same class, so the stall is not a seed that was warmed up differently.

Frame 146 — the one frame where the plasma really is lost. Nothing above applies to its diverted seed, and the record has to say so plainly rather than fold it into the fixed-mask finding. There the mask does not begin frozen: it moves 27 → 40 → 1 → 0 across four trips and the residual tracks it (0.128 → 0.222 → 0.065 → 0.055), so the loop is travelling. What it travels towards is a state with no plasma in the machine: the axis ends at R=1.234, Z=+1.563 m, which is 0.10 m below the top of the wall, and the field closes around only 8 core cells totalling 0.043 m² — a sliver against the top plate — carrying 0.039× the target current. The residual then freezes at 5.496e-02. This is vertical displacement to the point of plasma loss, and it is the only frame in the cohort whose non-convergence is explained that way. Its limited seed is a different story and belongs with 179, 44 and 137: the plasma is confined there (axis at R=1.725, Z=+0.027, 382 cells, 2.029 m², 1.134× the target current) and the residual sits at 6.350e-02 beside a mask difference of 0 from the first trip. So the two seeds of frame 146 fail for two different reasons, and only the diverted one is a plasma loss.

What the four present-plasma frames have in common. On 179, 44, 137 and 144's diverted seed the fixed-point map is stationary from the state the seed enters: the mask difference reaches zero and stays there, every attempted inner step is accepted, and the outer promotion returns the state it was given. The residual the gate reads is therefore a property of the seed, not of a path the loop failed to walk, and continuing the loop cannot lower it. Frame 146's diverted seed is the counter-example that makes the point: where the mask does move, the residual moves with it, which is what a working promotion looks like. Its failure is upstream of that — it moves to a state with no confined plasma left to converge.

The area, corrected. The probe first reported a closed plasma area of 0.0 m² on every arm beside a confined current of about 1 MA. That was a defect in the measurement, not in the plasma: the area was counted from a mask attribute the domain partition does not carry, and the handler that exists so one bad read cannot kill a frame turned the error into an all-false mask. The area is now the partition's own CORE label — the cells the field closes around the axis and connects to — and it is re-derived from the saved grids without re-solving. Re-derived: frame 179 reads 1.233 m² diverted and 1.833 m² limited against a label field of 1.679 m²; frame 44 reads 1.870 m² diverted and 1.126 m² limited against 1.248 m². The measure carries its controls in presence_control.py: the frame's own EFIT label field, a known closed plasma, must read a positive area, and the counting arithmetic must reproduce a disc of known area to within twenty percent. Both hold. The area now agrees with the current read, and both say the plasma is confined at every one of these terminals. Frames 137 and 146 are counted by the same code path in the probe itself (_core_mask over PlasmaDomain.CORE), which is the path the control exercises.

Frame 137, diverted seed (job sched/1307571, rc 0, 2.1 ks, peak RSS 31.2 GB). It reproduces the banked terminal to every printed digit — 6.2369e-2 — so the re-solve is the same state, and it shows the same structure: the mask moves 24 → 20 → 9 → 0, the shipped policy settles at 5 trips, the continued arm spends sixteen at the identical residual, and all 24 inner steps are accepted. Its plasma is the weakest of the present-plasma set — axis at R=1.655, Z=−0.442, 234 core cells, 0.709× the target current — and it is confined, so this frame belongs with 179, 44, 137's limited seed and 144's diverted seed, not with 146. With this arm every seed in the cohort is measured. Frame 144's limited seed remains the one refusal, at axis identification before any promotion, which is upstream of the fixed point.

Recommended fix

Module: nova/equilibrium/fixed_point.py, the active-set loop in _active_set_newton_krylov and its reconcile reduction.

The stopping rule that has to change. Two predicates in the active-set loop end the solve at a stable mask without consulting the residual, and both are in nova/equilibrium/fixed_point.py:

  • stagnated (lines 4750–4758) requires selected_difference == 0 and selected_residual == previous_live_residual — the residual repeating, at any value — and reports ACTIVE_SET_STAGNATED.
  • settled (lines 4759–4772) requires selected_difference == 0, accepted_newton_promotions == 0 on the terminal trip and selected_state == state — the promotion returning the state it was given — and reports ACTIVE_SET_SETTLED.

Neither carries selected_residual <= convergence_tolerance in the unfrozen case, and that is the defect measured here: the loop is correct to conclude that its map has stopped moving, and wrong to report that as its answer. The evidence is that the gate reads ACTIVE_SET_SETTLED at residuals from 2.7e-2 (frame 179) to 4.28 (frame 44) — two to five decades above the 1e-5 criterion — while the loop accepted every inner step it attempted across the run (24 of 24 on every measured arm) and the terminal trip accepted none because the map had become stationary. With the exits disabled the loop spends all sixteen trips at a bit-identical residual, so the omitted clause is the only thing standing between a stationary map and a falsely converged flag. Note the asymmetry at lines 4768–4771: the frozen-topology branch of the same predicate does require selected_residual <= convergence_tolerance, so the guard exists in the file and is simply not applied when the topology is free. Two edits, in this order:

  1. Carry the inner step-acceptance receipt. Publish attempted_newton_promotions and accepted_newton_promotions per trip on FixedPointResult and through ForwardSolveReceipt. The banked receipts do not record them; the probe reconstructs the counts from globalisation_decisions for one frame and cannot do so for the rest. A frozen mask with every step accepted is a different defect from a frozen mask with the step refused, and the receipt cannot currently tell them apart.
  2. Separate the three endings at a frozen mask. Once the residual is part of the predicate, the remaining states are distinguishable and should not collapse into one reason: a mask that is stable with the residual at tolerance is convergence; a stable mask with the promotion returning the same state is a stationary map (ACTIVE_SET_STATIONARY); a stable mask with promotions attempted and refused by globalisation is a different defect again (ACTIVE_SET_STEP_REFUSED). Today only the second can occur on these frames — the accepted count is 24 of 24 — so the receipt cannot report the third even if a later frame exhibits it. Each must carry the residual it stalled at, which the gate already reads.

Frames it should converge: 137, 179 and the diverted seed of 144 — the three whose plasma is confined and whose terminals are the smallest of the cohort (6.24e-2, 2.73e-2 on 179's limited seed, 5.93 on 144) — then 44, whose plasma is also confined but whose residual is the largest. Two frames are outside what this change can reach: 146's diverted seed is a vertically lost plasma (below), and 144's limited seed refuses at axis identification, a defect upstream of the fixed point that no change to the active-set loop can help.

What frame 146 needs, which the stopping rule does not give it. Relaxing the mask-stability exit cannot help frame 146: its loop travels, and it travels towards a state whose confined current is 3.9 % of the target with the axis against the top plate. The residual there is 5.496e-02 — not the stationary-map plateau of the other frames. Recovering this frame needs a vertical-position response: either the equilibrium's vertical force balance tightened so the axis is held near the midplane, or a position constraint on the axis during the outer iteration, or a seed whose current profile does not drive the n = 0 displacement. That is a separate node from the loop change, and it must be measured against this exact terminal — Z = +1.563 m, 8 closed core cells — or the fix cannot be told from a re-seed.
