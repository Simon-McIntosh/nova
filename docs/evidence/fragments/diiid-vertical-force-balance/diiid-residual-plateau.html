The MAST builder uses the fitted-circuit, passive-inclusive reference case and starts from its EFIT state. DIII-D uses the cold limited/diverted branch seeds. This comparison separates the observed numerical paths; it is not a controlled claim that machine identity alone causes the difference.

The corrective product change, named but not written: globalise the Picard warmup, retain a finite trajectory and its matching shadow/partition, and stop or damp a warmup proposal whose resulting map is nonfinite. Begin Newton from the retained finite best state when warmup fails, instead of passing the NaN trajectory into the already-correct finite-residual guard.
