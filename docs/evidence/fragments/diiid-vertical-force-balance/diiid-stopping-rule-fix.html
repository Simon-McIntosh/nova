What ends the DIII-D forward solve, and what converges

Nothing converges. Across sixteen measured solver runs on five DIII-D gate frames and both topology branches, not one arm reaches the solve tolerance of 1e-5. Terminal relative residuals run from 0.027 to 5.93. The stopping rule no longer misreports that as convergence: every arm ends with converged false and a termination reason that says what actually stopped it.

Why the solve stops without stepping

On seven of the eight branch measurements the plasma-support mask falls to zero cells on the first active-set trip and never changes again. The residual is then flat to every printed digit for the rest of the trip budget, including in the arm that disables the mask-stability exit and spends all sixteen trips at the frozen mask. A reduce solve at an empty mask has no free degrees of freedom: the direction it forms is zero, every backtracking candidate scores the same merit, and the admission predicate (reduced_newton.py:1653-1671, found = jnp.any(valid) with valid = isfinite(merit) & (merit < current)) admits nothing. No Newton promotion is proposed because none can be, and the frozen-mask continuation added in this work therefore has nothing to run.

The mask history is what makes this concrete. On every branch except 146 diverted the support empties on the first trip: 179 limited 25 → 0, 179 diverted 13 → 0, 44 diverted 75 → 0, 146 limited 9 → 0, 137 limited 11 → 0, and 44 diverted and 44 limited start at zero. The two arms that do run Newton — 137 diverted (mask 24, 20, 9, 9, 0) and 146 diverted (27, 40, 1, 0) — both freeze at exactly the trip that takes the mask to zero, having descended steadily until then.

A re-run of 179 limited and 44 limited with the direction and score kernels wrapped in a debug print produced no output at all, so no candidate was formed in those solves. That wrap did not reach the executed path, so it is an instrument defect and is not quoted as evidence about the solver; the mask history above is the evidence, and the two are consistent.

The gate is not suppressing a step that exists. What the fix node must add is an answer to the mask collapse itself: either the mask read is wrong for these seeds, or the reduced route needs a linearisation that is meaningful at an empty support. Until one of those is resolved, no stopping rule and no continuation can move these frames.

137 diverted: Newton runs and still stalls

137 diverted is the one arm where the solve performs real Newton work — 72 attempted and 62 accepted promotions across five trips — and it still ends at 0.0624. The residual descends 0.1299 → 0.1114 → 0.0670 → 0.0624 and then repeats the last value exactly for every remaining trip while the mask empties 24 → 20 → 9 → 9 → 0. Accepted steps that leave the residual unchanged, with the mask collapsing underneath them, mean the accepted direction is being applied to a problem whose active set is being removed as fast as the step is taken.

Terminal states

Each panel draws the terminal flux on seven levels, the governed DIII-D limiter ring read from the machine description (84 points, R 1.017–2.351 m, Z −1.359 to +1.363 m), the EFIT boundary of the frame dashed, and the nulls the arm recorded; levels of the opposite sign are dashed. The flux grid extends beyond the wall, so the outermost contours leave the vessel outline. The limiter is the scale: the panels carry no axes, so the vessel outline is what maps a position on the page to a place in the machine, and the magnetic axis is labelled in metres beside its marker, placed by the renderer in the panel cell carrying the fewest contour vertices (zero on all four) and drawn over an opaque white patch with a leader line to the axis.

The measured wall relation is what the panels are checked against, and it contradicts any reading of these states as wall-bounded. Where the framework traces a terminal boundary at all — 144 diverted (665 nodes) and 137 diverted (585 nodes) — its closest approach to the wall is 0.162 m and 0.329 m, with zero nodes within 1 cm of the wall. On 179 limited and 44 limited the read returns no boundary nodes at all. The magnetic axis sits 0.56–0.59 m inside the limiter on all four — 0.589 m (179 limited), 0.577 m (44 limited), 0.591 m (144 diverted) and 0.559 m (137 diverted) — at Z between −0.44 m (137 diverted) and +0.04 m (179 limited), while the limiter's upper shelf is at Z near +1.36 m. No terminal in this table touches the upper limiter shelf, and nothing in the figures shows contact.

A bounding surface traced from each terminal flux by host_boundary_read is not recorded on these panels, and every panel says so: its legend carries the entry bounding surface: not recorded beside the curves that are drawn. Run against the artifact lattice alone, the read resolves only 8 of its ray radii and reports a class margin of -inf, so the curve it returns is a fragment rather than a surface and drawing it would misstate the state. The numeric wall relation above carries that evidence instead.

Both null sets are drawn. The solved arm contributes its axis (filled triangle) and its admitted saddle (filled cross) in the ink colour; the reference contributes the EFIT X-point, the lowest point of the frame's EFIT contour, as a hollow cross in grey. In the two diverted panels the two saddles are what the comparison is for. On 137 diverted the solved X-point sits at R 1.265 m, Z −1.336 m while the EFIT contour's own low point is at R 1.290 m, Z −1.188 m, a 0.15 m vertical separation the panel shows as two distinct markers. On 144 diverted the two coincide to the millimetre — R 1.382 m, Z −1.061 m for both — so the panel draws them on top of each other, which is itself the reading: the solved saddle reproduces the reference X-point there.

What the stopping-rule change does and does not do

The rule now requires a Newton promotion to have been attempted and refused at the frozen mask before a settlement may end the solve, and it reports that ending as a stationary map with converged false, never as convergence. On 137 diverted it publishes the real promotion history (72 attempted, 62 accepted) that the earlier receipt bridge discarded. It does not converge anything, and it cannot: on seven of eight branches there is no step to take.
