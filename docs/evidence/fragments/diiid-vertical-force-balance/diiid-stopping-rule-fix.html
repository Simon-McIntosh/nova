What ends the DIII-D forward solve, and what converges

Nothing converges. Across sixteen measured solver runs on five DIII-D gate frames and both topology branches, not one arm reaches the solve tolerance of 1e-5. Terminal relative residuals run from 0.027 to 5.93. The stopping rule no longer misreports that as convergence: every arm ends with converged false and a termination reason that says what actually stopped it.

Why the solve stops without stepping

On seven of the eight branch measurements the plasma-support mask falls to zero cells on the first active-set trip and never changes again. The residual is then flat to every printed digit for the rest of the trip budget, including in the arm that disables the mask-stability exit and spends all sixteen trips at the frozen mask. A reduce solve at an empty mask has no free degrees of freedom: the direction it forms is zero, every backtracking candidate scores the same merit, and the admission predicate (reduced_newton.py:1653-1671, found = jnp.any(valid) with valid = isfinite(merit) & (merit < current)) admits nothing. No Newton promotion is proposed because none can be, and the frozen-mask continuation added in this work therefore has nothing to run.

The mask history is what makes this concrete. On every branch except 146 diverted the support empties on the first trip: 179 limited 25 → 0, 179 diverted 13 → 0, 44 diverted 75 → 0, 146 limited 9 → 0, 137 limited 11 → 0, and 44 diverted and 44 limited start at zero. The two arms that do run Newton — 137 diverted (mask 24, 20, 9, 9, 0) and 146 diverted (27, 40, 1, 0) — both freeze at exactly the trip that takes the mask to zero, having descended steadily until then.

A re-run of 179 limited and 44 limited with the direction and score kernels wrapped in a debug print produced no output at all, so no candidate was formed in those solves. That wrap did not reach the executed path, so it is an instrument defect and is not quoted as evidence about the solver; the mask history above is the evidence, and the two are consistent.

The gate is not suppressing a step that exists. What the fix node must add is an answer to the mask collapse itself: either the mask read is wrong for these seeds, or the reduced route needs a linearisation that is meaningful at an empty support. Until one of those is resolved, no stopping rule and no continuation can move these frames.

137 diverted: Newton runs and still stalls

137 diverted is the one arm where the solve performs real Newton work — 72 attempted and 62 accepted promotions across five trips — and it still ends at 0.0624. The residual descends 0.1299 → 0.1114 → 0.0670 → 0.0624 and then repeats the last value exactly for every remaining trip while the mask empties 24 → 20 → 9 → 9 → 0. Accepted steps that leave the residual unchanged, with the mask collapsing underneath them, mean the accepted direction is being applied to a problem whose active set is being removed as fast as the step is taken.

Terminal states

Each panel draws the terminal flux on seven solid levels, the governed DIII-D limiter ring read from the machine description (84 points, R 1.017–2.351 m, Z −1.359 to +1.363 m), the EFIT boundary of the frame dashed, the bounding surface the host boundary read traces, and both null sets. The flux grid extends beyond the wall, so the outermost contours leave the vessel outline. The limiter is the scale: the panels carry no axes, so the vessel outline is what maps a position on the page to a place in the machine. The axis coordinates are written in the margin below the panel, joined to the marker by a leader line, so no label sits on the contours; the renderer refuses a figure whose notes enter the poloidal field or overlap one another.

The nulls are drawn in four distinct marks, each named in the legend. The solved arm contributes its magnetic axis (filled red triangle) and the X-point it recorded (hollow red cross). The reference contributes the axis of the frame's own EFIT label field (hollow grey circle) and the low point of its EFIT contour (hollow grey cross). The solved X-point is recorded but not admitted: qualified is false on all four arms, so the mark is hollow and its legend entry says so. On frame 44 that candidate lies above the limiter's upper shelf, outside the drawn field, so the panel carries neither the mark nor a legend entry for it.

The measured wall relation is what the panels are checked against, and it contradicts any reading of these states as wall-bounded. Where the framework traces a terminal boundary at all — 144 diverted (665 nodes) and 137 diverted (585 nodes) — its closest approach to the wall is 0.162 m and 0.329 m, with zero nodes within 1 cm of the wall. On 179 limited and 44 limited the read returns no boundary nodes at all. The magnetic axis sits 0.56–0.59 m inside the limiter on all four — 0.589 m (179 limited), 0.577 m (44 limited), 0.591 m (144 diverted) and 0.559 m (137 diverted) — at Z of +0.04 m (179 limited), −0.11 m (44 limited), +0.01 m (144 diverted) and −0.44 m (137 diverted), while the limiter's upper shelf is at Z near +1.36 m. No terminal in this table touches the upper limiter shelf, and the panels show no contact.

The bounding surface traced from each terminal flux by host_boundary_read is recorded with the geometry every panel reads, and is drawn as a solid grey ring on all four. It is an eight-vertex polygon: that is what the read returns on this lattice, and the panel says so in its margin note — 8 vertices, class margin −0.021 on 137 diverted, and class margin undefined on the other three, where the read reports -inf. Its closest approach to the governed limiter is 0.043 m (179 limited), 0.061 m (44 limited), 0.048 m (144 diverted) and 0.006 m (137 diverted), so on 137 diverted the traced surface comes within 6 mm of the wall while the axis stays 0.56 m inside it. A read that returns nothing writes no bounding surface with its reason into the same margin note; none of these four takes that branch.

The panels are reproduced rather than transcribed. Every field a panel draws is written by scripts/prototypes/diiid_stopping_rule/record_machine_geometry.py from the frame's own artifact and receipt, and run_geometry_dump.py rewrites those files on every run, so a panel cannot be drawn from geometry older than the recorder. Two contracts behind the panels are pinned by scripts/prototypes/diiid_stopping_rule/test_terminal_panel_geometry.py: that the recorder writes every field a panel reads, and that the renderer refuses a note placed inside the poloidal field or two notes that overlap. Neither contract is visible in a finished PNG, which is why each is tested rather than checked by eye. The panel constructor reuses the probe's own text-artist check, its null-handle vocabulary and its wall-distance routine, so a panel here and a panel elsewhere in the probe reject a bad label and measure a gap the same way.

The two null sets are what the diverted panels exist to compare. On 137 diverted the solved X-point sits at R 1.265 m, Z −1.336 m while the EFIT contour's own low point is at R 1.290 m, Z −1.188 m, a 0.15 m vertical separation the panel shows as two distinct markers. On 144 diverted the two coincide to the millimetre — R 1.382 m, Z −1.061 m for both — so the panel draws them on top of each other, which is itself the reading. The axes disagree more than the saddles: the reference axis is at R 1.743 m, Z 0.000 m on 144 diverted and 137 diverted, 1.743 m, −0.100 m on 44 limited and 1.796 m, 0.000 m on 179 limited, against solved axes of 1.759 m, 0.013 m; 1.655 m, −0.442 m; 1.773 m, −0.106 m and 1.760 m, 0.043 m respectively.

What the stopping-rule change does and does not do

The rule now requires a Newton promotion to have been attempted and refused at the frozen mask before a settlement may end the solve, and it reports that ending as a stationary map with converged false, never as convergence. On 137 diverted it publishes the real promotion history (72 attempted, 62 accepted) that the earlier receipt bridge discarded. It does not converge anything, and it cannot: on seven of eight branches there is no step to take.

Correction, 2026-11-08: the mask does not empty

Withdrawn: the conclusion above that the plasma-support mask "falls to zero cells on the first active-set trip", and every count quoted from it (25 → 0, 13 → 0, 0, 0, 9 → 0, 11 → 0, 24, 20, 9, 9, 0, 27, 40, 1, 0). The numbers are real; the reading of them was wrong. ForwardSolveReceipt.mask_history was assigned from history.active_set_mask_differences, which counts the cells whose label or support membership changed between trips. A settled mask changes nothing, so a settled trip reports zero whatever its size. The column never measured a population, and a zero in it says the mask stopped moving, not that it stopped holding cells.

The support is populated. The support-mask trace (diiid-support-mask-collapse) read the axis-connected flood at the same production seam and kept 345, 298, 10 and 8 connected cells on the 179 limited, 137 diverted, 146 diverted (trip 3) and 146 diverted (history slot 3) arms respectively, against change counts that had settled at zero. The archived default receipt carries mask_history=[25, 0] beside closed_core_cells=345 — both correct, one measuring movement and the other measuring population, and the report above conflated them.

What the receipt publishes now. The change history is changed_mask_entries, carrying exactly the values this record quoted, and the population is a separate support_cell_count holding the per-trip axis-connected support size. Read together, a changed_mask_entries that reaches zero beside a positive support_cell_count is a settled mask over a populated support. The mechanism this record identified — a settled mask above tolerance ending the solve — stands; only the empty-support cause is withdrawn, and the nonconverged residual plateau remains unexplained here.
