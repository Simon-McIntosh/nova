What differs on 146, and what would fix the report

146 does lose its physical plasma location. Its reported axis at R = 1.2303 m, Z = 1.5595 m polishes with zero displacement, but lies above the physical limiter. All ten connected cell centres are outside that limiter. This is spatial displacement of a nonempty support. It is distinct from an empty flood.
