Own-field forces and boundary gaps

The Lorentz read is (Fᵣ,Fᶻ) = ∫2πR jφ(Bᶻ,−Bᵣ)dA, with Bᵣ = −Φᶻ/(2πR) and Bᶻ = Φᵣ/(2πR), from each map's own public central derivatives. It excludes poloidal-current stress and pressure gradients. Fᵣ is the toroidally integrated radial scalar or hoop loading; the Cartesian radial vector cancels by symmetry. The analytic uniform-current case independently pins the force sign and magnitude.
