Re-judging the five fallback rows against the fan's refinement floor

Node ecq-fence-in-moment-l2, plan nova:exact-clip-moment-quadrature §3, base a42dc665c. Discharges followup f-ecq-fence-in-moment-l2 and applies the locked decision fallback-fence-norm to the five fallback rows. No product source and no test is touched.

What this replaces. The five fallback rows — the rows with no independent coupling error term — were previously read against two fences, neither of which is the retained fan's refinement floor. Both are retired here:

  • one tenth of the fan refinement floor (fan-floor-tenth, current 2.924e-17, radial 5.109e-16, vertical 5.064e-16) — a budget, not the floor, and it re-imports the tenth the decision removed;
  • the closed-form round-off envelope (closed-form-envelope, current 2.038e-16, radial 2.060e-15, vertical 4.789e-16) — a property of the closed-form route's own accumulated round-off, so comparing the route against it measures the route against itself rather than against the fan's refinement.

What replaces them. Decision fallback-fence-norm is locked to moment-relative-l2: the fence is the retained fan's own refinement floor, (4/3)·||fan@order16 − fan@order8||₂ / ||fan@order8||₂ on the three moment integrals, measured on the weak 110 row and shared by every fallback row — the same instrument that supplies each row's reference. At base a42dc665c that is current 2.26749e-16, radial 4.44149e-15, vertical 5.06496e-15. A row's error is the closed-form route's moment relative L2 against the retained fan at the clip's native arc resolution of 128 segments.

That margin is one part in seventy at the round-off scale; the ordering is not physically meaningful on its own and this row should be read as marginal rather than settled.

The fence's own value moved with the clip's arc sampling. The fan-floor study recorded current 2.92403e-16 at revision d9b594b2; measured here at a42dc665c it is 2.26749e-16. The floor is a property of the fan arm on the geometry the rows are built on, and that geometry changed between the two revisions, so the fence moved with it. The verdicts above use the fence measured in the same process as the rows — the only pairing in which a row error and its fence describe the same instrument. Read against the recorded floor instead, moderate-rotation-conventional-static @ 1000 would sit just under it and pass, and the strong-110 failure is unchanged.
