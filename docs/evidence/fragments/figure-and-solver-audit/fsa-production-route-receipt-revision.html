Production-route receipts now stamp their source revision

2026-10-02 worker landing. Every benchmark that writes a production-route receipt now records a source_revision field — the 40-hex git rev-parse HEAD of the tree that produced it — in the shape used by benchmarks/centroid_constrained_fixture_receipt.py:_revision. Each writer exposes a cheap, testable stamp_source_revision(payload) seam (the pattern of benchmarks/sol_ledger_current_census.py:stamp_revision) and its payload builder routes through it. This answers the triage sweep that found four drivers mentioning production-route receipts but carrying no revision at all.

Per driver. benchmarks/solovev_certificate.py already stamped its certificate writers but not the _production_solver_receipt payload builder; it now does. benchmarks/fieldnull_production_route.py (schema nova.fieldnull-production-route) stamps both its measure() and assemble() receipts. benchmarks/plasma_cell_production_ladder.py stamps its assembled production-route-ladder receipt. benchmarks/exact_placement_discriminator.py stamps each arm summary and the final discriminator-receipt.json, and benchmarks/carrier_step_attribution.py (receipt carries verdict.production_route) stamps its receipt alongside the existing source_commit.
