A source scanner for wall-unmasked scattered-field renders

A field sampled at scattered nodes and interpolated onto a raster is finite across the whole convex hull of those nodes, so a contour drawn from it crosses a concave first wall unless the raster or the node triangulation is first masked to the wall units. Two painter routes enforce the mask: nova.media.poloidal.draw_scattered_contours requires a wall and masks the triangles that leave the vessel, and draw_flux_contours accepts an optional wall and blanks every grid point outside it. This node lands a reusable scanner that reports every call reaching a contouring routine on scattered data while passing neither.

What was built

  • tests/renderer_wall_scan.py — an importable module exposing scan_paths(paths) (one UnmaskedCall(path, line, kind) per unguarded call) and a main(argv) CLI that prints those records and exits nonzero when any exist. It is a read-only AST scan: per-scope name binding, alias resolution, receipt-dict key provenance, and loop-tuple provenance, so a raster read from a built record or iterated out of a tuple of solved fields is resolved to its producer.
  • tests/test_renderer_wall_scan.py — pins the rule on named controls and on the base count.

Rule (as implemented)

  • A draw_scattered_contours call is never reported: its wall is required.
  • A draw_flux_contours call is reported when it passes no wall= and an argument traces to a scattered producer (_raster_field, _state_raster, _raster, _structured_flux, LinearNDInterpolator, griddata) and the raster is not already blanked to the wall (_masked_field, _blanked_field, or a raster formed through inside_wall_units).
  • An Axes.tricontour/tricontourf call is reported wherever it draws a figure, because triangulation of scattered nodes is scattered by construction; a fixed set of non-renderer probe calls is exempt.
  • An Axes.contour/contourf call is reported only when its value argument traces to a scattered producer, because a regular-grid field needs no wall mask.

Excluded by an explicit allowlist

Two calls are exempt and named in the module: the post-filtered probe at plasma_cell_trip_panels.py:406, which extracts polylines only to reject every curve with a vertex outside the wall and closes the figure without drawing it; and the analytic regular-grid panel at render_mechanism_evidence.py:510, whose value is the exact analytic flux on a regular mesh (its sibling at 511 draws the interpolated solved field and is reported).
