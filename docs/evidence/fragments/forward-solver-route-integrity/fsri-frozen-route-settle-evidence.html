Frozen-Newton settle repair: residual, terminal state and route cost

The settle repair freezes the partition within each Newton pass, re-reads it live between passes, terminates on the live residual only, and falls back to separately compiled live-read Newton steps. Its evidence rests on one after receipt and the log trail of the arms that led to it. This node adds the residual-against-step figure, a poloidal flux panel of the terminal state, and the route-cost accounting; it changes no solver source.

Before. The pre-repair frozen route recorded a live residual of 2.372871479533e-06 and terminated ACTIVE_SET_SETTLED with the mask unchanged: the loop ended on the retained state without consulting the live residual, so the frozen partition settled a fixed point that had not converged. The Newton-Krylov live residual is flat across its two active-set steps at that value, above the registered 1e-6 tolerance (figure, left-of-right panel, solid).

After. The repaired route terminates CONVERGED with a live residual of 1.282728063088e-11: two frozen passes, three live partition reads, and one live-read Newton step. The live-read step crosses the tolerance on its own step (figure, dashed). The Picard reference route, unaffected by the repair, drives the shared map from 1.2948 to 2.1257e-08 over its 480 map evaluations (figure, left panel).
