Arm (b) against arm (c). Arm (b) recompiles with the per-fusion autotune cache kept, 40.0 s of cold compile wall; arm (b) is the arm that runs the row again after the compile entries are removed, so its autotuner reads its cached emitter choices. Arm (c), with the autotune cache disabled, recompiles the same 259 programs in 45.0 s. The 5.0 s difference, spread across the cold pass's 259 compile misses, is about 19 ms of compile wall per miss — at the guard's one-second resolution this is one second on each of the largest programs, so the defensible statement is that turning the cache off costs no more than about 5 s on a 259-program cold compile and nothing visible in the steady state, where all twelve repeats across the four arms sit between 77.1 and 86.7 s.
