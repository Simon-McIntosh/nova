The sensor-noise spectrum: where imas-efit's reconstruction fails on the held ITER state

The noise size, the spectral index and the corner frequency of the coloured-noise model behind plan section 7 were swept on the held 500-cell ITER state, and every point was reconstructed by imas-efit: twelve arms of 100 slices each, one IMAS pulse per arm, written by the drifting-sensor study's own pulse writer. At the reference spectrum the axis error first exceeds 0.05 m at a noise size of 0.05 of each row group's own RMS, between 0.02 (0.0243 m) and 0.05 (0.0850 m), and the q95 error first exceeds 0.2 at 0.10 (0.2193), between 0.05 (0.1345) and 0.10. The generator's negative control holds: spectral index 0 reproduces the white draw's lag-one correlation exactly, 0.00150 against 0.00150 averaged over 72 sensors, with the largest per-sensor deviation 0.0487.

Every receipt cited below is under /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261119T065419793680-drift-spectrum-sweep/study/. The twelve arm files were measured at revision d537b5032 and are reused unchanged by every later run of the gate, so a sweep log at a later revision records them as reused rather than remeasuring them; each gate log's own header names the revision that produced it, and the reduction and the controls were re-run at the head this record is committed with, with the payload reproducing byte for byte.

The sweep and its reduction

One generating state is held fixed: axis (6.288633, 0.498205) m, q95 2.955747, boundary flux 2.811282 Wb over a span of 76.703791 Wb, plasma current -13.705 MA, and the fixture's own row sigmas 83.969 Wb on the 24 flux loops and 0.6731 T on the 48 poloidal probes. Each arm is 100 slices one second apart; noise is drawn per sensor from one white draw of the study's seed, so a size arm is that draw scaled and a spectrum arm is the same draw shaped — the points are nested realisations, not independent ones. The reference spectrum is spectral index 1 with a corner frequency of 0.02 Hz (two corner cycles across the 100 slices), which is the corner the upstream 200-slice reference sequence carried (four cycles across 200 slices). The size sweep holds the reference spectrum and varies the factor over 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.1, 0.2 and 0.4; the index sweep holds the reference size and corner and varies the index over 0, 0.5, 1 and 2; the corner sweep holds index 1 and varies the corner over 0.002, 0.02 and 0.2 Hz, a span of two decades. A white arm at the reference size is the control: a hundred slices of independent Gaussian noise through the same pulse and the same reader.

Reported quantities are the root-mean-square error against the generating state over every slice; the same error taken on the mean of each 50-slice block; their ratio, which is 1/sqrt(50) = 0.141 when the slices are independent and 1.0 when they carry one common offset; and the lag-one correlation of the reconstructed series. The imas-efit reader returns no per-slice covariance matrix, so the ratio here is the measured one — the error of the 50-slice mean divided by the single-slice error — and not a spread predicted from a stated covariance.

The one clause the drift sequence does not satisfy is on the axis: the plan's control asks every reported quantity to exceed 0.5 on the 1/f sequence, and the axis correlation is 0.432 at the reference spectrum and reaches 0.610 only at index 2. The boundary flux and q95 pass at the reference, and the shortfall is the same one the upstream 200-slice reference sequence measured (0.462), so it is a statement about which quantity EFIT++'s fixed point damps hardest rather than about this sequence length.

The negative control: index 0 reproduces the white draw

Two arms test the generator and the pipeline separately. On the generator alone, over 2000 slices of the same 72-sensor set, the mean per-sensor lag-one correlation is 0.00150 for the white draw, 0.00150 for the index-0 coloured draw and 0.6076 for the index-1 draw; the largest per-sensor index-0 correlation is 0.0487, so every sensor is within the 0.15 the clause allows, and the marginal standard deviations agree to four significant figures (0.484971 against 0.484914, a relative difference of 1.2e-4, which is only the per-sensor normalisation).

Through the pipeline, the index-0 arm gives lag-one correlations of -0.014 on the axis, 0.125 on the boundary flux and 0.100 on q95, against the white arm's -0.003, 0.114 and 0.113 on the same 100 slices: the two agree within 0.013 and every value is within 0.15 of zero. The check could have failed — a shaping error that left even a fraction of the DC power in the index-0 draw would raise these correlations above the 0.0487 the generator shows — and it did not.

Where imas-efit stops returning a state, and how that was found

At factors 0.2 and 0.4 imas-efit stores an equilibrium for every requested slice but fills no global quantities on most of them. The first reduction read those slices as values and printed an axis error of 1.8e40 m, which is the DD 4.1 sentinel for an unwritten float, not a measurement; the reducer now holds any value beyond 1e30 in magnitude as absent, exactly as a missing boundary outline is held. The counts are 77 of 100 slices without a usable state at 0.2 and 59 of 100 at 0.4, and the frequency of failure is not monotone in the noise size. The two arms are therefore reported on the slices that carry a state, and their counts sit in the table above so the censoring is visible rather than folded into an average.

The reduction, the wall and the gate

The reduction has one owner. probe_rows/analyse.py carries the series extraction, the NaN-aware root-mean-square, block-mean and lag-one measures, the crossing search and the per-arm reduction, and the sweep driver calls them rather than holding copies; the sweep's table is unchanged by the move, its payload reproducing the earlier numbers to every digit. A quantity a slice did not carry, whether a missing boundary outline or an unwritten global read back beyond 1e30, is held absent in the one place both the sweep and the upstream analysis read it.

The limiter wall is written beside its final name and renamed into place instead of being unlinked and rewritten. Five concurrent writers and one reader over three seconds, exercising the two write patterns directly: the unlink-then-write pattern handed the reader 9573 truncated or missing arrays, and the rename pattern handed it none. Every arm of this sweep shares one study directory, so that window was live.

The analyse step is now a gate rather than a printer. It reduces, prints the payload, and then tests six things before writing spectrum-summary.json: that every declared arm is present, that every arm carries its full slice count, that arms at or below 0.10 carry a boundary on every slice while no arm loses more than four fifths of its states, that the generator's index-0 draw reproduces white within 0.01 and every sensor within 0.15 while its index-1 draw stays above 0.5, that the white arm's three streams stay within 0.15 of zero, that both crossings exist above their limits, and that both nova receipt files reduce. A failure prints CHECK-FAILED with the reason, exits nonzero through gate.sh, and leaves the payload unwritten: with one arm's jsonl withheld the run reports CHECK-FAILED arms: missing size-0.0500 and writes no summary, and a control value forced to 0.40 reports the generator check by name.

The second figure plan section 7 asks for is an overlaid two-code contour panel at the slice of largest disagreement. It is not drawn here: paint.py draws that panel from a payload carrying both codes' flux maps on one slice, and this node measured nova only through the upstream receipts, which record axis, q95, flux and wall time rather than a flux map. Limits

Every wall and every number here is a CPU-cluster measurement of imas-efit on four cores; nothing in this record is an H200 number. The arms run at 100 slices, not the plan's 200, so the lag-one estimates carry the sampling spread of 100 pairs rather than 200 — the white arm's three streams land within 0.114 of zero, which is that spread, and the control clause is read against 0.15 rather than against zero. The arms are nested draws rather than independent ones, so a difference between two adjacent sizes is a difference in one draw's scale, not two draws' average. The nova column has two converged slices per sequence and cannot support a sequence statistic at all.

Two gaps are against the plan's §7 done-when rather than against this record: that node asks for at least 200 slices per arm where these arms carry 100, and for every 1/f lag-one correlation to exceed 0.5 where the axis lands at 0.432. Both are stated above and neither is hidden by an average. Closing them is a plan decision — a 200-slice re-run of all twelve arms, and whether EFIT++'s axis damping is a defect of this instrument or a property to report — so this record leaves the clauses unmet and names them.
