Fit inside the equilibrium iteration

The reconstruction holds the flux map within a pass: one weighted least-squares solve for the profile coefficients and the fitted conductor currents at that map, then one Grad-Shafranov read-write cycle at the coefficients and currents the solve just chose. No forward solve is converged for a proposal the data did not choose; a pass costs one flux cycle, and the passes repeat until the map stops changing.

Every row comes from one batch job on 98dci4-gpu-0002 (titan, one P100, two cores, 24 GB) at revision 77283793bc, which runs the cases in sequence inside its allocation and writes each row as it lands. flux cycles counts the Grad-Shafranov read-write cycles the fit applied; nested is the forward-solve count the known-answer recovery record measured for the same state, which is the cost the held-map pass replaces. flux error is the largest flux error against the generating map over the span and residual the largest whitened sensor residual; the known-answer bands are 1e-6 and 1e-8.

Relaxation arms of the Solov'ev known state

The held-map update is a fixed-point step, and the undamped arm runs it at the full step. These arms repeat the same fit with the step damped, so the recorded failure or convergence can be laid to the step rather than to the fit, with the same rows, basis and budget per pass. Each arm is its own batch job on the same node, launched after the six-case job above.
