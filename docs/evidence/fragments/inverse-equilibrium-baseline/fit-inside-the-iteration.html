Fit inside the equilibrium iteration

The reconstruction solves the least-squares fit on every pass of the equilibrium iteration instead of once at a held map. Each pass forms one response column per profile coefficient by stepping that coefficient at the held map, adds the conductor columns the case carries, solves the whitened system, adds the correction to the coefficients, and lays the updated coefficients down as the map the profile's own forward solve reaches for them. The pass ends when the map moves less than the tolerance of the axis-to-boundary span, and the iteration stops when a solve moves the map by more than one span, which is a solve that has left the branch the rows were measured on.

Why the stored rows lose the axis

The stored branch passes the fitted conductor currents to the fit's columns and to its coefficients but not to the map. _stored_case reads getattr(data.get('problem'), 'prescribed_current', None), and the §2 study sets data['request'], whose current is the measured conductor vector, so the fit's map call receives no prescribed vector and the operator keeps its stored external_current. The flux_solver(source, flux) seam carries no conductor argument either, so a fitted coil current cannot reach the solve. The least squares then absorbs the coil-field rows into the profile basis at the opposite sign, the map that basis asks for has no qualified axis, and the row is refused. The measured current is 760952 A where the fit's coefficients give 761996 A on the first affected case, and the same reversal appears on every stored case that reached a fit. The seam is closed at 1a52c9fd5 — the solver now takes the pass's fitted conductor vector onto the request's current= channel, and a new test asserts it — and the three stored cases are being re-measured at that revision; these rows predate it.
