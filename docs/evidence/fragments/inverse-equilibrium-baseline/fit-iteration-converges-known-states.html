The held-map pass that converges the Solov'ev known state

The predecessor record closed with the band unmet: the pass laid down a fraction of one Grad-Shafranov read-write cycle chosen by a halving ladder, and from 1.76 spans out on the Solov'ev known state it reached 0.9164 of the span at the third pass and then stalled. The mechanism it named is the one this record acts on. The ladder descended the cycle's own residual |F(ψ) - ψ| at the fitted coefficients, and that quantity is not monotone along the step when the fixed point of the cycle lies three spans away, so the criterion the pass followed pointed away from the answer.

The pass now descends the map the rows and the flux equation agree on. Every pass fits the coefficients the held map's rows name (exact weighted least squares, because the rows are linear in the coefficients at a fixed map), then lays one whole cycle of those coefficients down and Anderson-mixes that walk over the steps it has already taken; the mixture solves the least-squares problem over the history of (state, change) pairs, with a ridge proportional to the Gram trace and a fallback to the damped step when the mixture is singular or non-finite. The base step is half a cycle rather than a whole one, because the mixture extrapolates past the step it is given and is capped at twice that step, so a whole-cycle base lets a pass travel two spans — a branch change the one-span rejection fence must refuse. The pass budget default is raised from sixty-four to ninety-six: the mixed walk contracts by about three quarters a pass on average on the state that starts farthest from its answer (the applied update falls on 72 of its 79 passes and rises locally on the other seven, by at most a factor of 1.29), and the decades between that start's residual and the 1e-10 tolerance need the passes.

Both choices were measured rather than reasoned. On the Solov'ev known state, the same coupled walk at a whole-cycle base is rejected at its eighth pass — the applied updates swing 1.5569, 0.2465, 0.1352, 0.4298, 0.7614, 0.9007, 0.7333, 0.6941 of the span while the rows go on disagreeing with the map, and the coefficients walk to [4.501, -6.814] against the generating [1, 1], leaving a flux error of 0.8627 and a whitened residual of 0.2191 (job 1334124, titan). At half a cycle the same start keeps contracting over the whole run — the applied update falls on 72 of its 79 passes, and the seven local rises are each under a factor of 1.29 and never carry it back above 0.032 of the span — and reaches the true amplitudes; the receipt below is that walk carried to its tolerance.

Passes and their applied updates

Each pass reports the largest change of the step it laid down divided by the axis-to-boundary span, and the whitened row residual at the coefficients that step holds. Sampled every fifth pass, then at the last three, every sampled interval of the Solov'ev walk is smaller than the one before. Pass by pass it is not monotone: the applied update falls on 72 of the 79 passes and rises on seven (passes 8, 10, 12, 31, 33, 34 and 36), each rise by at most a factor of 1.29 and never carrying it back above 0.032 of the span, while the residual falls on 73 of the 79.
