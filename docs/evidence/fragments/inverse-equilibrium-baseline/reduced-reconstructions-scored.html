Scoring each reduced reconstruction as its own column

The harmonic, moment and profile reconstructions stand beside the section 3 columns on the same row-exchange case, scored by the same comparator (nova.imas.mast_efit_referee.compare_states) against the same bands, read through IMAS from each column's own equilibrium at data dictionary 4.1.0 and identified by the digest it carries. The reference is the section 3 reference: axis (6.28987, 0.49809) m, span 76.3657 Wb, its q95 reading 2.9832 from its own flux map.

Each reconstruction owns its conversion, placed beside the reconstruction rather than in the benchmark: nova.equilibrium.moment.write_moment_equilibrium, nova.equilibrium.harmonic.write_harmonic_equilibrium and nova.equilibrium.profile.write_profile_equilibrium. Each writes the same field set — boundary outline, global quantities, the flux profile on the reconstruction's own grid and the vacuum toroidal field at radius 6.2 m with b0 5.3 T — so the comparator reads them as it reads any state. No reconstruction supplies a toroidal-field function, so every column and the reference carry F dF/dψ = 0, the vacuum closure, which keeps the safety factor a comparison of flux maps on one surface definition rather than of four stored conventions.

What the reuse repair changed

The three conversions no longer assemble an IDS each. Every column writes through one shared function, nova.equilibrium.reconstruction_state.equilibrium_from_state, which owns the boundary ring, the global quantities, the stored map convention (the IDS psi array is indexed (radial, vertical), so the internal (vertical, radial) map is transposed once on write) and the stored level convention. Each conversion passes only its reconstruction's own arguments: the moment conversion its ring, map and levels, the harmonic conversion its ring, centroid axis and the levels its boundary read found, and the profile conversion its iteration's levels, its own boundary level and the map value at the axis node it reports. The private assembly copies that moment.py, harmonic.py and profile.py each carried are deleted.

The two pre-existing IDS writers were read before deciding where a shared writer belongs. benchmarks/estimator_scoreboard.py assembles a declared manifest entry from a record rather than a reconstruction result, and nova.imas.pulsedesign writes a pulsedesign equilibrium rather than a reconstruction, so neither is a reconstruction writer and neither is reused here.

Three further defects in the same finding are repaired with it. The profile conversion now writes result.boundary_flux, the level its own iteration produced, as psi_boundary, and the map value at its reported axis as psi_axis. Before the repair it re-read a boundary through a second traced read and discarded ProfileResult.boundary_flux; the negative control below shows that reverted tree writing −40 Wb where the iteration measured −7.25 Wb. profile.__all__ now lists profile_equilibrium and write_profile_equilibrium. The driver that wrote these states is committed beside the figure, so the table is regenerable from the repository rather than from a session's scratch directory: case.py builds the row-exchange case and emit_states.py writes all six states.

The controls are likewise reconstructed rather than copied. Each column's control is the section 3 perturbation applied to the reconstruction — plasma current scaled by 1.06, the state shifted 0.03 m radially, the flux span grown 2 % at each end and the map scaled by 1.04 about its axis node — and the perturbed reconstruction then goes through that column's own conversion. The moment and profile conversions take the perturbed map, ring, axis and levels as arguments; the harmonic control perturbs the pole and the coefficients, and its conversion re-derives ring, levels and map from the perturbed inversion, which is the path the conversion takes for any inversion. So each control exercises the conversion rather than a copy of an already-written equilibrium.

Reading the margins. The moment column localises its axis to 0.041 m, twice its 0.02 m band, while its axis flux sits 0.8515 as far from the reference's as a full reference span and its boundary flux 0.8406, so the reconstruction's gauge and its boundary level both miss badly even though its current matches by construction (4.1e-16 of the reference). The profile column is the closest: axis 0.2725 m, axis flux 0.05181 of a span, boundary flux 0.08272 and a q95 difference of 0.5022, from 2.4811 on its own map against 2.9832 on the reference map. Its boundary level is now the iteration's own value rather than a second read, which is what the repair changed. The harmonic column does not close a physical interior at all: its total flux diverges toward its own focal ring, so its axis flux sits 3487 reference spans from the reference's, and the field has no elliptic stationary point. That is why its conversion withholds the 2-D map and its q95 reads not supplied rather than borrowing the reference's, why the comparator records the absent measure as a failed verdict, and why its axis is the traced ring's centroid: the only interior position the read supplies, with no magnetic extremum to report. The reference-free checks hold for all three columns; the harmonic control is the harmonic column's own property rather than a pass.
