The harmonic, moment and profile reconstructions now stand beside the section 3 columns on the same row-exchange case, scored by the same comparator (nova.imas.mast_efit_referee.compare_states) against the same bands, read through IMAS from each column's own equilibrium at data dictionary 4.1.0 and identified by the digest it carries. The reference is the section 3 reference: axis (6.28987, 0.49809) m, span 76.3657 Wb.

Each reconstruction owns its conversion, placed beside the reconstruction rather than in the benchmark: nova.equilibrium.moment.write_moment_equilibrium, nova.equilibrium.harmonic.write_harmonic_equilibrium and nova.equilibrium.profile.write_profile_equilibrium. All three write the same field set — boundary outline, global quantities, the flux profile on the reconstruction's own grid and the vacuum toroidal field at radius 6.2 m with b0 5.3 T — so the comparator reads them as it reads any state. Each conversion states one closure in its docstring: no reconstruction supplies a toroidal-field function, so every column and the reference carry F dF/dψ = 0, the vacuum closure, which keeps the safety factor as a comparison of flux maps on one surface definition rather than of four stored conventions.

Reading the margins. The moment column localises the axis to 0.041 m, twice its 0.02 m band, while its interior flux sits 0.85 of a span from the reference's, so the reconstruction's gauge and its boundary level both miss badly even though its current is exact by construction. The profile column is the closest: axis 0.2725 m, axis flux 0.0507 of a span, boundary flux 0.0816 and q95 difference 0.502, from 2.481 on its own map against 2.983 on the reference map. The harmonic column does not close a physical interior at all: its total flux diverges toward its own focal ring, so the read's interior extreme sits 176.6 spans from the reference, the traced ring covers an area fraction of 0.285 of the bounding box (its reference-free FAIL) and the field has no elliptic stationary point. That is why its conversion withholds the 2-D map and its q95 reads not supplied rather than borrowing the reference's. The registered rule is that a metric absent from the state fails, so that band is recorded as a failure too; what is absent is the measure, not the verdict. Its axis is the traced ring's centroid, the only interior position the read supplies, because no magnetic extremum exists to report.
