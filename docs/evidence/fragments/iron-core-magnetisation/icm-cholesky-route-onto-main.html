Route equivalence on the persisted uniform 80 mm WEST operator

One process loads the persisted uniform-0.08-operator.npy interaction (geometry digest 9d219482…, 10 170 unknowns) and solves the same state by the existing combined LU route and by the in-place Cholesky route. Both converge to the round-off floor in six iterations, so the comparison is of two converged fixed points and not of one unfinished solve.

The probe fields, which are the physical output, agree three decades under the 10−12 bar. The coefficient vector agrees to 8.5×10−13 in the relative Frobenius norm, inside the bar. Its worst single entry sits at 1.67×10−12, a factor 1.7 over: the mutual difference is the round-off floor of two different dense eliminations (LU on the full differential Jacobian, Cholesky on the metric-symmetric Galerkin matrix), and it does not shrink with a tighter convergence tolerance. Tightening both routes to a 10−14 stop moved the Frobenius figure only to 1.01×10−12 and the worst entry to 2.37×10−12, so the floor is arithmetic, not a stopping rule. The route changes no value: no interaction entry, quadrature order or tolerance was altered.

The same comparison on the CPU lane measured 1.79×10−12 Frobenius and probes inside 10−12; the P100 lane, the device the uniform 0.08 mm operator was measured on, carries the tighter x64 reductions reported above.
