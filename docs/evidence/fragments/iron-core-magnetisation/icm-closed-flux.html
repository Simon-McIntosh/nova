What this node established

Node icm-closed-flux transcribed eqs 13a, 18a and 22a from the 300 dpi page renders and derived, from the same integrand the landed semi-analytic route integrates, the exact closed form of its D part. That part is settled. The arsinh part of eq 22a — the delta_{j phi} b_1^2 r / a_1^3 g term together with the third-kind sum — was not reduced to a form that reproduces the verified quadrature, so the closed-form flux is not delivered and the node is blocked rather than landed.

The printed block, as read from the renders

From eq (13a), with c_1^2 = r_1^2 + b_1^2 r^2 and d_1 = b_1^2 r − r_1:

n_p^2   = 2 b_1^2 r / (d_1 -/+ a_1 c_1)          (upper p = 1, lower p = 2)
S(k)    = b_1^2 a [1 + k^2 (gamma + b_1 b)/(4 b_1 r)]
T(k)    = b_1^2 a k^2 / 2
P_j(n_p) = (c_1 +/- a_1 r') { 2 r_1^2 + r^2 (1 + a_1^2),
                               (r + r_1)^2 n_p^2 - 2 (r^2 + r_1^2) }

The symbol b in S(k) is not defined on the page we read and is the first ambiguity. From eq (18a):

L(k) = 2 b_1 r' [gamma + b_1 (r' + r)] / (a k^2)
M(k) = S(k) - a (d_1 + a_1^2 r')/(2 r) - r' (r^2 + r_1^2)/(a r)
N(k) = 2 r' (a_1^2 r' - r_1) / (a k^2)

and eq (22a), the complete-limit vector potential of a conic cylinder:

A_phi(pi/2) = b_1^2 r/a_1^3 g(pi/2)
              - (1/(a_1^2 b_1)) [M(k) K(k) + N(k) E(k)]
              - (1/(4 a_1^3 b_1 a r)) sum_p (-1)^p P_phi(n_p) Pi(n_p^2, k)

with g(pi/2) = int_0^{pi/2} d alpha sinh^{-1} beta_1(alpha) (eq 12b), which the paper states has no analytical treatment and which the plan accordingly assigns to the Part V module's fixed-node rule.

The D part, derived and checked

The landed route integrates over half the turn, R = int_0^pi cos(phi) D(phi) d phi, with D^2 = (r - r')^2 + gamma^2 + 4 r r' sin^2(phi/2). Translating to the Jacobi variable u (d phi = 2 dn u du, D = a dn u, cos phi = 2 sn^2 u - 1) and using the complete integrals int_0^K dn^2 u du = E and int_0^K sn^2 u dn^2 u du = [(1-k^2)K + (2k^2-1)E]/(3k^2) gives

R = 2a [2(1 - k^2) K + (k^2 - 2) E] / (3 k^2),   k^2 = 4 r r'/a^2

Checked at (r, z) = (0.5, 0), segment end (r', z') = (1.5, 0.6), b_1 = 0.636364: the closed form gives -0.71669812; a 4,000,001-point trapezoid of the same integrand gives -0.71669812, a relative agreement of 3.1e-16. The other end, (r', z') = (0.8, -0.5), gives -0.62232451 both ways, relative 5.4e-16. The D part of the closed form is therefore settled to machine precision.

The discrepancy that stops the node

The printed M(k) K + N(k) E does not reduce to that expression. At the same end, with S read at b = r + r', the printed coefficients are M = -3.3651, N = +2.0656 and M K + N E = -4.3465, whereas the derived R times the module's prefactor a_1 b_1 is -0.5406. The two disagree in sign and by a factor near nine, which is the same disagreement the previous node measured and reported. Reading S at b = r' or b = c_1 moves M by only a few per cent and cannot close it, so the misprint is not in that one symbol.

What this node did not resolve is whether the printed M, N belong to the same quantity as the module's R. Eq (13) is written after an integration by parts and its {2 sn u cn u, cn^2 u - sn^2 u} bracket may carry a factor and a boundary term that the module's R does not. Settling that, and reducing the arsinh/third-kind block to a form that the verified flux agrees with at 1e-10, is the remaining work. No quadrature was substituted for a closed form and no coefficient was fitted.
