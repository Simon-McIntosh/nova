imas-efit closed-shell control from an IMAS pulse: S is reproduced to 1.1 % and 0.8 % once the iron outline is refined

Verdict. The section 10 control now runs end to end on a pulse with no XML machine file, and it reproduces the exact closed-shell shielding factor at both radius ratios: S = 60.42 at a/b = 0.9 (exact 61.101838, −1.12 %) and S = 193.49 at a/b = 0.5 (exact 195.055750, −0.81 %), both inside the 2 % gate. The equilibrium slices report CONVERGENCE_SUMMARY: status= 2 iterations= 1 cerror= 0.000E+000 and the iron relaxation reaches relErr = 1.1E-06; the flux scale is fully linear (IRON_BMOD Bmax = 3.0897E-07 T, four orders below any saturation). The number is only reproduced at a refined outline: the twenty-vertex half-arc a naive case produces over-shields by 20 %, and the reason is a hard 500-knot ceiling in the dependency.

The case, as IDSs

A spherical shell (inner radius a, outer b = 1 m, μr = 1000) with a one-turn coil of radius 0.03 m at 104 A inside it, and no plasma: the pulse constrains magnetics/ip to 0, so the reconstruction is the vacuum poloidal flux of the coil in the iron's presence — exactly the quantity S is defined for. make_pulse.py writes five IDSs at DD 4.1.0: pf_active (one rectangular coil, one supply, one circuit whose connections make the coil its own single-coil circuit), iron_core (the shell's poloidal half-annulus as one closed polygon plus a flat B–H table, b_field = [0, 10³], permeability_relative = [1000, 1000]), wall, magnetics (four probes, σ = 10 T) and tf. imas-efit logs Iron source: IMAS iron_core IDS (preferred) and builds the boundary itself; the solver XML carries only solver settings and the data-selection attributes.

S is ψ(with iron)/ψ(without iron) at three exterior probes; for a dipole source inside a spherical shell every exterior point must give the same S, so their spread is the internal check.

The 20- and 40-vertex rows each appear twice in the full table, once at relativeError 1e-2 (e2, fine) and once at 1e-4 (e4, v40): the pairs differ by 0.16 % and 0.20 % at a/b = 0.9 and by 0.50 % and 0.52 % at a/b = 0.5, so the solver tolerance is not the lever and the outline resolution is. The last step moves the answer by 0.32 % (a/b = 0.9) and 1.50 % (a/b = 0.5), and the probe spread at the finest outline is 0.40 % and 0.84 %. The series is not monotone: at a fixed knot count the uniform knot grid aliases against the polygon's corners, so S reads 55.15 at 80 vertices and 60.01 at 160 on the same 500 knots.

The interior probes separate the two effects. Their flux ratio is the shielded interior field, and it is flat across the whole outline series — 0.9219 to 0.9221 at a/b = 0.9 and 0.6732 to 0.6750 at a/b = 0.5, a range of 0.03 % and 0.27 % — while S ranges over 55.15 to 73.52 and 180.18 to 196.42 on the same arms. The reconstruction's interior solution is therefore stable and the exterior variance is the polygon's flux at the probe positions (the knot aliasing above), not a global field error that a larger solve would fix.

Finding for the owners: the iron outline's resolution is unreachable from a pulse

IronCoreBuilder derives nBP = max(nVertices·10, 50) and caps it at maxBreakpoints = 500 (src/ironCoreBuilder.h:20). The only lever a pulse has is the number of vertices, which changes the answer — by 20 % at a/b = 0.9 — and no pulse can ask for more than 500 knots or for a corner-aware knot distribution. The ask is a pulse-level breakpoint count (or an exposed cap), so resolution is a stated input rather than an accident of the outline's vertex count.

Findings for the owners: the options must declare the probe selection, and a plasma breaks the fit

In unified IMAS mode the probes come from magnetics, but their data selection must still exist in efitOptions: without <magneticProbes interpolationMethod=…/> (and the same for <efitCircuits> and <bVacRadiusProduct>) every slice dies at DataSelection::getValue: invalid interpolationMethod=0. Probes at weight 0 leave the fit rank-deficient and cerror never leaves its plateau; weight 1 with σ = 10 T fixes that without pulling the field. With ip = 100 A the same case never converges: chi2 = 2.03 × 10−20 yet cerror plateaus at 7.079 × 10−3, 70 times the 1.0e-4 tolerance, and the slice ends status= -1 iterations= 80 with PLATEAU_REJECTED_BY_CEILING on every late iteration. Scaling every current by 104 and constraining the probes both left that plateau at the same value; pinning Ip to zero is what converged, and it is also the correct setting for a shielding measurement.

Verdict, one-directionally

This is positive evidence and only positive: an independent code, reading the same geometry as a polygonal iron_core and solving for a linear μr = 1000 shell, agrees with the exact exterior shielding factor to 1.1 % at a/b = 0.9 and 0.8 % at a/b = 0.5, and thus corroborates the closed-shell representation and nova's own prototype reading (60.4 against 61.1). It does not test §9's WEST pulse-57410 probe comparison, which stays open: no WEST data was read here, and the case has no plasma. It also does not clear imas-efit's saturation path — the B–H table carries no curvature, so nothing about μr(B) was exercised.
