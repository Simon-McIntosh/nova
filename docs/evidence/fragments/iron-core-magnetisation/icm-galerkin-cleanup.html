§4 — Galerkin interaction cleanup

Node icm-galerkin-cleanup closes the review findings on the volume-tested iron interaction. Two duplicated capabilities now call their owners: the filament loop flux of every distinct quadrature pair comes from nova.biot.greens.traced_filament_greens, and the averaged external field is integrated by nova.biot.sectionaverage.section_nodes with the axisymmetric 2*pi*r volume weight. The volume-tested route is a named _galerkin_interaction, so _interaction evaluates point targets only and the measurement caches key on what they are handed. The reciprocity test is no longer vacuous: it measures the defect of the raw contraction before the two triangle halves are averaged, and a scratch mutant that mirrors one off-diagonal edge-pair quadrature fails it.

One pair type cannot delegate, and the reason is numerical rather than architectural. The owner derives its squared separation from the target and source coordinates, (ar-r)**2 + dz**2, which cancels to exactly zero at the graded corner nodes of a coincident pair once the parameter square is folded about its diagonal at quadrature order 16 and above; the flux then returns a non-finite value and the energy guard refuses the assembly. A coincident pair therefore keeps the exact squared separation of the folded square, |b-a|^2 (x^3 y^3)^2, which is derived from the parameter nodes instead. Every other pair bit-matches the owner's arithmetic path.
