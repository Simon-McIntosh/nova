The persisted WEST operators, the demagnetisation bound, and the direction that refuses

The persisted WEST operators were built on the H200 (1335154) and have now been measured against the demagnetisation energy bound 0 ≤ xTQGx ≤ mu0 xTQx. All three exceed it. The excess the uniform pair carries is a mesh artefact that shrinks under refinement, and the graded operator carries about 1 700 times the finest uniform operator's excess at a comparable cell count: grading is what sets the size of the violation, by two and a half orders of magnitude.

The two uniform operators bound how much of the violation is discretisation: halving the cell from 80 mm to 40 mm cuts the excess from 1.91é−4 to 1.51é−5, a factor 12.7, so the uniform family's violation is a small mesh artefact that shrinks on refinement. The graded operator carries 1 700 times the 40 mm excess at a comparable cell count (5 864 against 6 632), so the graded operator's violation is not a refinement of the uniform one: at the same cell count, grading makes the excess three orders of magnitude larger.

On the graded operator the route's seed Newton matrix is indefinite, with minimum −0.02463311084853905 (residual 1.7é−9) and a skew part at 3.0é−15, so the object the route factors is its symmetric part. That negativity is the bound violation transmitted by the material: the seed matrix's smallest element is 1 − (mu0 chi) q with chi = dM/dB, and the measured per-cell susceptibility band gives mu0 chi ∈ [0.995366, 0.999197], hence a predicted minimum in [−0.024683, −0.020754]. The measured −0.024633 sits in that band, 0.2 % above its lower end.

An earlier revision of this instrument evaluated the same closed form against the fitted law's origin reluctivity dH/dB instead of the susceptibility, reporting a seed limit of 215.11 where the material sets 1.00080. That is recorded below: it is why the first reading showed a negative Newton minimum the seed model predicted to be +0.995, and it is a defect of the instrument, not of the operator.

What the instrument reads

Each operator is a Galerkin interaction G with a per-cell metric Q from the linear geometry's gram blocks. The quotient is the largest eigenvalue of (sym(QG), Q), reduced to S = Q−1/2 sym(QG) Q−1/2 because QG is unsymmetric; the witness is w = Q−1/2x, normalised so wTQw = 1 (the receipts carry metric_quadratic = 1.00000000000000). The solve is a Lanczos iteration to a stated tolerance with the residual beside every reading, so a reading can be compared against its own convergence rather than against a convention.

The operators are read from the persisted files, through load_magnetisation_interaction, which refuses an operator whose declared geometry digest does not match the mesh rebuilt here. Which receipts record that digest varies, and it is worth stating exactly. The witness receipts carry it (witness-graded-16-80.json reads 13fcda09…4bb124910); the bound and control receipts were written before the loader reported the sidecar's digest and read the placeholder "synthetic" instead. The digests are therefore recorded once for all three operators in operator-digests.json, read from the *-operator.json sidecar each persisting job wrote beside its *.npy: graded-16-80 13fcda09e91b7a1e07ed334b2bdb3bc0443ad2b0ca522e9ccb6bf4a4bb124910, uniform-0.04 d16678432b45074ee19df1dc9d3793c650a1040742183da256adcdefe7864b25 and uniform-0.08 9d2194829695172cb57fe0ac61fefb8cf27e9c3196f9d0cf503b3aedf8c34562. That receipt also tabulates what every receipt on disk reads in its geometry_digest field, so the placeholder cannot be mistaken for a digest.

The gate-shell row is the one that matters for the headline: the instrument reproduces the operator owner's own dense answer on the shell that owner solves densely, to machine precision. The closed-form row is new and exists because the seed relation is what attributes the Newton witness.

Each identity carries a stated tolerance, and --mode check exits nonzero naming the identity that misses it rather than printing a fresh reading, so a regression fails a gate instead of being read as a measurement. The guard is exercised rather than asserted: with one identity displaced by a thousand tolerances the mode exits nonzero naming that identity, and the log sits beside the clean run in the receipts.

The negative control

A rank-one term G' = G + mu0 PLANT p (Qp)T adds PLANT (xTQp)2/(xTQx) to every quotient, so on the operator's own top direction it lifts the reading by exactly PLANT and on a random direction by PLANT/n. The control plants along the measured top direction, so it tests the instrument's dynamic range rather than a lucky overlap.

A first attempt planted along a random direction and produced a lift of 3.1é−6 — a correct instrument reading an unmeasurable overlap, which would have failed the arm; the plant now takes the measured direction, which is what makes the lift law exact.

The refusal witness

The route assembles B = I − R G W with D = Q A, R = sqrt(sym D) (negative eigenvalues clamped) and W = Q−1R, and factors sym(B) because it reads one triangle. On the graded operator at the seed state sym(B)'s smallest element is −0.02463311084853905 at residual 1.7é−9, and the skew part's spectral norm is 3.0é−15: the factored object is the symmetric part, so the refusal is not a one-triangle artefact. The per-cell differential is positive definite (minimum 3.23) and the material block asymmetry is 1.1é−4, so the negativity is in the seed matrix, not in a material block the route should have refused.

Two things follow, and they are different facts.

The magnitude is the operator's. The seed matrix goes indefinite exactly when (mu0 chi) q exceeds one. The graded operator's largest quotient 1.0255 is above every value of the measured band's limit, and the closed form lands within 0.2 % of the measured minimum. So the route's refusal is the demagnetisation bound violation, not a separate material defect, and the distance from the bound to the refusal is only the vacuum-response factor 1 − mu0·reluctivity of the law.

The operator's own direction is already indefinite. Read at the operator's bound-breaking direction rather than the Newton minimiser, the same matrix gives −0.021339473744693815: the negativity is there with no reference to the witness the eigen-solve chose. The two readings differ by 0.0033, which is the cell weighting moving between the two functionals, not a change of sign or of cause.

The direction is not the operator's top eigenvector. The Newton minimum is attained on a direction whose ordinary metric-weighted quotient is 1.0009205090868052 and whose Q-cosine with the top direction is 0.04829664604562009, fields witness_operator_quotient and alignment_q of witness-graded-16-80.json. That is not a contradiction: sym(B) is I − alpha Q1/2 S Q−1/2, a similarity rather than a congruence, so its eigenvectors are Q-images of S's and the two functionals weight the cells differently. The cells that carry the witness are two small lobes on the inner and outer shells (cells 2412, 2411, 2410, 2413 at (0.707, −2.74) m and cells 5343, 5344, 5342, 5345 at (4.916, 2.07–2.26) m). Their per-cell shares of the witness numerator, in that order, are 0.213218, 0.166659, 0.025319, 0.051148 and 0.201499, 0.181692, 0.050656, 0.022525, as localisation.cells in witness-graded-16-80.json records them. The lobes are near-equal partners — the inner lobe carries 0.4563 of the numerator and the outer lobe 0.4564 — so no single lobe or cell is the defect.

What kind of region carries it. region-graded-16-80.json reads each carrying cell's size, the sizes of its neighbours and the distance to the nearest cell it does not touch, from the same mesh the operator was built on. The inner lobe's cells are quadrilaterals 18.3 mm across the wall by 60.0 mm along it (4 vertices, area 1.098×10−3 m2); the outer lobe's are 9.375 mm by 76.2 mm (area 7.139×10−4 m2), so their aspect ratios are 3.3 and 8.1. Against the three candidate causes:

  • Not a grading transition. Every carrying cell has eight neighbours whose equivalent size is identical to its own, giving a neighbour-size ratio of exactly 1.000. The cells sit at 33.1 mm (inner) and 26.7 mm (outer) in a field carrying 302 distinct sizes between 9.2 and 73.8 mm, whose median is 37.3 mm: the middle of a smooth gradation, with no jump at the cells that carry the witness.
  • Not a near-singular cell pair. The closest cell a carrying cell does not touch is 18.3 mm away in the inner lobe (0.55 of its own equivalent size) and 9.375 mm in the outer (0.35), so no two cells come to a degenerate separation. The four cells within each lobe touch one another (separation 0), which is what makes their shares add: contiguous cells, not a close pair.
  • Distorted cells. Their compactness 4 pi A / P2 is 0.563 (inner) and 0.307 (outer) against a mesh median of 0.630, so the outer lobe's cells in particular are slivers stretched along the wall.

The witness therefore sits on elongated cells in the thin limbs where the shell narrows at the limb/yoke junctions, with the two lobes near-equal in the energy they carry.

The form discriminates. The uniform 80 mm operator's quotient 1.000191 lies below the corrected seed limit 1.000804, so the form predicts its seed matrix to be definite, and it is: the measured minimum is +0.0008142576389899497 against a predicted band [0.000612, 0.004436], and the same matrix read at that operator's TOP direction gives +0.061765. A form that merely tracked the sign of the bound excess would have called this operator indefinite too; it does not.

What the measurement leaves to be decided

The operator breaks the demagnetisation bound and the seed Newton matrix's indefiniteness is that excess transmitted by the material: the closed form places the sign and the size of the minimum within 0.2 % of the measurement, and no material block is at fault. Under the lead's ruling, changing the linear solver — LU or GMRES — is therefore not admitted as a remedy, because the factorisation is not what makes the matrix indefinite, and the volume rule needs repair at the two lobes the witness sits on. Locating them is where this node stops; the repair itself is not made here.

A defect the measurement found in its own instrument

The first witness receipt read newton_minimum = −0.02463 beside predicted_newton_minimum = +0.99523 and seed_limit_quotient = 215.11. The algebra behind those fields is right and the constant was wrong. The law is written M = (1/mu0 − H/B) B, whose tangent, the susceptibility dM/dB, is 1/mu0 − H/B; the fitted origin value is the reluctivity H/B at zero field. The closed form was evaluated against the reluctivity, which differs from the susceptibility by 1/mu0 (a factor 215.1 here), so it predicted a matrix that is definite by a wide margin while the measured one is indefinite by a narrow one. Three checks fix it: the measured per-cell generalised spectrum of the differential is the susceptibility band and is now the quantity the form reads; a dense synthetic identity reproduces the closed form to 1.1é−14; and the measured minimum now sits inside the predicted band. The witness receipt predates one further change: it also carries a newton_skew_norm_residual field that divides the skew norm by an eigenvalue at round-off, which reads as a large ratio of a quantity that is numerical noise on both sides. That field is no longer written and is not read anywhere here; the skew norm itself, 3.0é−15, is what the argument uses. The gate-shell calibration and both operators' bound readings are unaffected, and the uniform 80 mm operator's quotient 1.00019 is below the corrected limit 1.00080, so its seed matrix is predicted definite — a discrimination the corrected form makes and a second witness arm records.

Why the two large operators needed a different path

Each operator action was first written as a jax.jit closure over the interaction. Lowering then reported 9.91 GB of constants captured for the graded operator and 12.67 GB for the uniform 40 mm one, and the compiled executables could not be loaded beside the matrices: the graded operator died with CUDA_ERROR_OUT_OF_MEMORY on the H200 and the 40 mm step was OOM-killed on the host. A single matrix-vector product gains nothing from a compilation, so the actions are plain BLAS products and cellwise is numpy; the receipts record backend = numpy-blas. The BLAS path was validated against a dense generalised eigenproblem on a synthetic operator (quotient agrees to 2.2é−16) and against the GPU-path readings on uniform-0.08: 1.000191462860167 against 1.000191462860172, and control lift 0.049999999999996714 against 0.04999999999999116.
