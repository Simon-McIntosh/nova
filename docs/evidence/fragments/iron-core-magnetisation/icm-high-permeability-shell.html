High-permeability shell: amplified discretisation error, with continued convergence

Verdict. The dominant mechanism is high-permeability amplification of the piecewise-constant, centroid-collocated magnetisation error. The recorded non-monotone refinement reproduces at 40 → 20 mm, but every probe improves at 20 → 10 mm: at μr = 1000 the errors become 1.10182%, 1.29591% and 0.90253%, with orders 1.520, 1.327 and 1.395. There is no measured fixed error floor on these meshes. At μ = 1000 the geometric-volume prediction is thousands of times smaller with the outer radius held nominal, and vanishes to roundoff when both effective radii are used. Radial layers alone do not control the error.

The defect is in the represented field, not the Newton stopping criterion

For the ideal dipole, sample the analytic shell magnetisation at the same cell centroids. In the iron, HR = 3DRZ/ρ⁵ and HZ = −C + D(2Z²−R²)/ρ⁵, with q = 0.05²/(4S), C = 2(μ−1)q/(3μ), D = (μ+2)q/(3μ), and M = χH. Inserting those samples into the discrete material equation gives a nonzero truncation defect despite the converged algebraic solve:

These are Euclidean relative norms at μ = 1000. Writing Ah = I − χGh/(μ₀(1+χ)) and τ = AhM* − χBsource/(μ₀(1+χ)), the exact algebraic identity is Mh − M* = −Ah−1τ. The last column measures the gain on this defect vector, not a condition number or an identified eigenmode. The finite loop and ideal dipole differ by at most 0.04736 percentage points in shielding error anywhere in the sweep. Source multipoles therefore do not account for the percent-scale gap or its non-monotone coarse refinement.

Repair implied, owner and limits

Owner: nova/biot/magnetisation.py. The measured formulation continues converging, so these data do not prove that it must be replaced to obtain an eventual limit. They also do not provide a certified global error bound or qualify a WEST mesh. Even 10 mm leaves 1.296% error at μ = 1000 and 3.100% at μ = 3000, above the 0.5% high-permeability target.

Recommended repair route: compare a reduced scalar-potential material formulation, H = Hsource − ∇φ, against these same controls. It represents the curl-free material field directly rather than relying on cancellation of fields from constant-M cells. Retain the qualified sheet operand (see the sheet-pair reference); the change belongs to the material representation and solve. This is a recommendation for the next measured comparison, not a claim that a potential implementation has already met the target. A higher-order M basis remains a legitimate comparison candidate. No few-line production repair is indicated, and no production source or test was changed here.

A refinement-only policy would need a bound that includes permeability, tangential resolution and cell shape. Three Cartesian sizes and one fixed-μ directional sweep cannot establish that bound. Extrapolating the observed orders is an estimate, not an accuracy certificate; adding radial layers or tightening the Newton tolerance does not supply the missing guarantee.
