The solved iron as one passive frame

The seam this node was for is landed and pinned: the affine magnetisation is inserted into the CoilSet as one passive frame whose currents the material solve writes, and Solve.include_iron adds the frame's field and flux to every target set the solve builds, through the same reduction a kernel batch's matrix uses. On a block fixture the zero state reproduces the air-core field bit for bit, the solved state's added field and flux equal BoundCurrents.field and .psi within 1e-12 relative, and the solved state moves the field.

The WEST-scale measurement follows from the same seam: the iron's share of the outboard stray field, its contribution at all 110 in-vessel poloidal probes and at all 17 flux loops, all from the operand the frame carries. The sampled fields come from the pinned coil and plasma quadrature rather than a CoilSet point solve, because the plasma closed coupling costs minutes per solve here; the seam test pins the frame's contribution to that same operand, and on the production CoilSet the zero-magnetisation frame moves the field by 6.9e-18 T while the solved frame reproduces BoundCurrents exactly at all 127 diagnostics. The field also reproduces the persisted H200 receipt to 4e-9 relative, which closes the factor of 28 this node first misread as a defect.

Three further findings come with it: a plasma-coupling wall, a reload defect that is fixed and pinned by a test, and the 40 mm factor this allocation could not build.

One seam, and why it is a frame

Cell magnetisation is a bound current distribution, and Nova already turns a current distribution into every operator it builds: a source column set. BoundCurrents in nova.biot.magnetisation holds the affine state and exposes field_matrix, psi_matrix, field and psi. The field of a uniform sheet on an edge atom uses the closed-form sheet_edge_field, whose closure is fixed under refinement; the flux uses ring quadrature through traced_filament_greens, because no closed-form flux tile exists for a sheet current. MagnetisationSolution.field delegates to the same call on the linear basis, so the frame and the material solve cannot drift apart.

nova.frame.iron.insert_iron inserts that set as one passive frame labelled iron: a frame row with part="iron" and segment="iron", and one subframe row per affine unknown carrying Ic. The physics of those rows is an in-memory source column, iron_source, holding the BoundCurrents, so the CoilSet stores 10 170 currents at 80 mm rather than 10 170 polygons. Solve.include_iron reads that column, builds the per-target matrix in blocks of IRON_TARGET_BLOCK, and adds it where a kernel batch's matrix is added; check_segments skips the iron segment, so no kernel is launched for it. The frame's Ic is a bound-current amplitude, not an ampere-turn, and set_iron_currents is its writer.

The zero control on the production WEST CoilSet

The bit-identical control above is measured on a fixture. On the production CoilSet - the 80 mm frame inserted into the WEST 62751 coilset, 10 170 auxiliary rows, 18 plane targets, re-measured at the folded head in job 1333784 - the zero-magnetisation frame moves the field by 1.388e-17 T and the flux by 4.441e-16 Wb/rad, against an air-core field of about 0.2 T. The iron's own columns are exact zeros, so the residual is the last bit of the reassembly the subspace solve performs once the frame's rows exist, not an iron contribution: 3e-17 relative, one unit in the last place of the flux. The frame's solved contribution equals BoundCurrents.field and .psi at the same targets, the identity tests/test_magnetisation.py::test_frame_carries_the_bound_currents_exactly asserts at 1e-12 relative on the fixture. An earlier version of this paragraph claimed a 0.0 difference at all 110 probes and all 17 loops from the sampled receipt; that comparison called bound.field and bound.psi on both sides, so it could not fail, and it is withdrawn. The regenerated receipt shows what is left after the fold: the probe comparison still reads 0.0, now for a second reason - the owner tiledassembly.linear_cell_interaction is the call BoundCurrents.field makes, so the two sides coincide by construction and that row is reported, not claimed; the flux comparison against the radial integral of that same field reads 1.015e-2 relative at the 257 radial samples that integral uses, recorded at that value rather than at the 1e-13 the withdrawn claim carried. A resolution test - the same integral at 1 025 and 4 097 radial samples against the folded matrix at four flux loops - was submitted (job 1333894) and did not finish inside its wall, so the cause is unnamed here: the row is a measurement awaiting its explanation, not a number with an alibi.

This is the honest form of the clause: the fixture is bit-identical, the full CoilSet is identical to the last bit of its own reassembly, and no setting of the iron's current changes the air-core field by more than that last bit when the magnetisation is zero.

The external term from a persisted coil response

The coils' field at the iron's 127 872 volume quadrature points is written once per mesh and per coil set and reused across slices. The folded cache is the package's own response store, so the file name carries both identities (coil-<mesh16>-<key16>.npy with a metadata sidecar) and the loader re-derives the mesh digest and compares the recorded coil key before serving. A request for another quadrature raises the persisted coil response serves another mesh - the refusal the regenerated receipt records (job 1333523) - and a file whose sidecar names another coil set raises interaction geometry digest does not match the mesh. A second run of the same stage reports coil_response_reused=true and its difference against a fresh direct evaluation is the round-trip error the receipt carries. Splitting the external term into coils plus plasma changes only the summation order: against the prototype's single quadrature set the Frobenius relative difference is 5.481e-16, while the maximum pointwise relative difference is 1.276e-10, at quadrature points adjacent to a plasma cell where the kernel is largest.

What did not run, and why

1. The WEST-scale CoilSet point solve. (The figure and the two tables above come from the external term, not from this solve; the seam test pins the frame's contribution to the same operand in field and flux.) A point solve assembles the plasma closed coupling for its whole target set on every call, and on this CPU that assembly costs about 3.3 s per target per attribute. At 157 targets one attribute took 515.7 s (the progress receipt reads 2/6 [17:11<34:22]), so a single solve is about fifty minutes and the three the measurement wants are hours; at 247 targets one attribute did not finish in 28 minutes. The planes and diagnostics were therefore computed from the same external term the material solve consumes (the prototype coil and plasma quadrature pinned by west_inputs, air drift 0.0) plus BoundCurrents.

2. The frame on a reloaded CoilSet. Inserting the frame into the coilset reloaded from its stored cache raised KeyError: 'polysection_policy': a reloaded frame keeps attributes in its metaframe's additional table without carrying their defaults, and the rows the insert appends are filled from that table. The first repair seeded the subframe's metaframe only, and the reload still raised at framelink._build_additional, because the failing link is built from the CoilSet's frame metaframe. Seeding both closes it: test_reloaded_coilset_accepts_the_iron_frame stores a coilset, loads it, inserts the frame, and gets the bitwise zero control and the 1e-12 frame contract (1 passed in 252.89 s).

3. The 40 mm state. The 12.67 GB operator still has no build lane. On 2026-11-20 the titan build died at the 24 G per-card cap (OUT_OF_MEMORY, job 1330936 at 2:05) and two rigel builds ran their full six-hour wall and were killed (TIMEOUT, jobs 1332935 and 1332936): CPU compile is the constraint, as the compute hierarchy says. The 80 mm state is regenerated instead: 1 695 cells, 10 170 unknowns, operator rebuilt on a P100 in 733 s, five Newton corrections in 34.45 s, mu_r 218.29 to 1692.49, residual 3.690e-9.

The apparent factor of 28 was a comparison error, and it is closed

This node first read its own iron field (6.780, 4.233 and 1.727 mT at the coilset's three probe radii) against 0.2438, 0.1628 and 0.0723 mT, which is the persisted 40 mm receipt's field_T, and reported a factor of 27.9 as a defect. It is not one: those receipt entries are the total field, air + iron, not the iron's contribution. The receipts store field_T = total (see scripts/west_iron_linear/measure.py line 193, air = external(probes), then total = air + result.field(...)).

Checked against the pinned state directly: on the 80 mm state whose residual history reproduces the pinned solve step for step (0.9977745775, 0.0011981286, 7.865737e-05, …, 3.690e-09), the seam's field plus the drive reproduces the pinned field_T to 4.04e-9 relative, and the drive minus the iron field matches nothing (a 55.9 relative residual). The two vectors are almost equal and opposite at those probes - a 7.02 mT drive against a 6.78 mT iron contribution - so the total is the 0.244 mT the receipt records. The iron shields at these radii; that is the physics the §4 record states as 242.6 µT against a 7024 µT air-core.

Two other evaluators were run on the same persisted state before the comparison error was found. tiledassembly.linear_cell_interaction (the untouched reference implementation) returns 6.77986, 4.23324 and 1.72697 mT, ratio 1.000 to the seam's matrix; the centroid kernel _interaction(mesh, points, plan) @ magnetisation returns 6.7898, 4.2338 and 1.7269 mT. So the seam's field is the value the package already computed, to four digits.
