Paired affine assembly and the completed fine shell

Paired batching preserves the delivered operator and reduces 40 mm P100 assembly from 901.041 s to 403.361 s. The 10 mm shell assembly completes in 4107.743 s, above the 900 s target; this is recorded as a measured outcome under the coordinator's ruling. Its persisted interaction solves on rigel CPU at 19008 unknowns, with 0.0495673 % worst-probe dipole error and 0.000436058 % finite-loop error. No formulation decision or WEST run is made here.

The delivered assembly bottleneck

The 40 mm shell has 228 cells, 1368 unknowns, 2635 atomic edges, 2576 source triangles and 92736 volume points. Its 901.041073 s wall splits into 7.346338 s compilation, 544.571334 s synchronised evaluator execution and 349.123402 s host remainder. Compilation is only 0.8153 percent. Six evaluator programs launch 693544 polygon calls: 370225 near calls with 64 padded pairs and 323319 far calls with 1024 padded pairs. Padded evaluations per cell pair are 6934.984303.

At 20 mm the delivered assembly takes 1780.271290 s: zero new evaluator compile, 1041.915724 s execute and 738.355566 s host remainder, with 1224093 polygon calls. The six fixed shapes compiled at 40 mm serve both meshes. The receipts count assembly evaluator programs, excluding unrelated JAX support operations.

One owner and fixed paired tiles

Boundary quadrature is batched across atomic edges and volume quadrature across cells. Edge-current pairs are batched in tiles and reciprocal entries written in vectorised blocks. The triangle incidence, angular rules and near/far criteria remain the delivered ones. No parallel assembly module is introduced.

The duplicate polygon trace is folded into that existing owner behind its quadrature/closed kernel flag. The affine public entry points are included in tiledassembly.__all__. The constant route's operator and defaults are unchanged. Product source was restored to the qualified paired route in commit 9603eaf7b79cd9b493dd16ab5c156378caa057b0.
