Discrete energy and body boundaries

For each shared cell face inside one authored iron body, w = shared face length / centroid distance. The energy is (lambda/2) sum w |M_i - M_j|², whose gradient is a graph Laplacian applied independently to the radial and vertical components. This two-point face approximation integrates the squared poloidal gradient: the face measure times the distance represents the dual area and the difference divided by that distance represents its normal gradient.

Outer faces, point contacts and vacuum gaps contribute nothing. Authored bodies carry separate constant null modes even when they touch. Callers pass the distinct outlines through smoothness_bodies when the meshed region is their union; a union cannot retain a touching segment boundary. A cell spanning an authored boundary is excluded rather than assigned one body's unresolved trace.
