Material solve blocked on axis-touching sheet edges

The production material-solve scaffold calls the existing sheet-tile interaction owner. It has a merged-cell mesh, a monotone cubic H(B) interpolant with positive differential permeability, and dense differentiated Newton updates with runtime state, operands, LU factors and backtracking scales. These are partial work: the analytic control has not passed and this record claims no delivered solve.

The 44-cell sphere at 0.1 m cell size reaches the production tile, which produces 528 nonfinite target–edge pairs out of 26,972 pairs (44 targets, 613 edges). Twelve edges lie on or end at R = 0. For example, the target (0.04913072, −0.44836919) m against the edge (0, −0.5) to (0, −0.4) m returns B_R = NaN and B_Z = −0.0. The neighbouring sloped edge (0, −0.5) to (0.003067942, −0.499990588) m returns B_R = 1.2510684759427282e−11 T per A/m and B_Z = NaN. The solve explicitly refuses the resulting nonfinite matrix before Newton.

The defect is in the existing sheet owner, outside this node's write grant. Replacing NaNs with zero would erase the finite field of a sheet that ends on the axis, so the solver does not mask them. No kernel repair or alternative interaction derivation is included. The axis-source and axis-target limits must be supplied by the sheet owner before the sphere and closed-shell acceptance measure and its differential-versus-secant negative control can be completed.
