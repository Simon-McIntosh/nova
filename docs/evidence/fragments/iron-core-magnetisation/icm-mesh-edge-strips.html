Clearance bound and exact unions

A rectangular strip of width w has centroid clearance w/2 regardless of length. A clearance target h/4 therefore excludes strips narrower than h/2 even if their area exceeds the sliver floor. The companion target is h²/2, the area of a h by h/2 rectangle. The shared-endpoint grid is retained; no authored iron boundary moves. Cells failing either target are agglomerated through exact polygon unions. A component-aligned-grid probe still leaves 44 cells at 80 mm and 74 at 40 mm below h/4, so alignment alone does not control the clipped pieces.

The committed repair chooses the smallest adjacent union already meeting both targets, falling back to the longest shared edge when further agglomeration is needed. Irreducible thin material may miss the targets but must admit an interior point at 0.01 h. A maximum-inscribed-circle point supplies the fallback when the area centroid is inadequate, with search tolerance 1e-5 h. The focused controls preserve genuinely thin iron and holes, and refuse the long 1 mm plate and the existing isolated 1 mm square at h = 100 mm.

Blocked: a resolution-preserving mesh design is needed

Clauses concerning WEST clearance and the area-only negative control are met. The analytical preservation clause is not met. Both measured agglomeration choices reduce shell counts from 228/828 to 200/742 and change the discretisation enough to exceed the stated preservation measure. The worker stops after the second failed accuracy comparison, as its contract requires. No third repair or acceptance-threshold change is made.

The next concrete design choice is to repartition paired cells after their exact union, retaining boundary-normal resolution while enforcing clearance and area, or to use a boundary-fitted grid. Repartitioning is the smaller extension of the existing machinery and is the preferred next investigation. A wrong choice costs another mesh implementation and roughly 53–58 minutes for a paired CPU suite, based on these completed runs. The source, tests, figure, failed accuracy comparisons and all commits are preserved for that decision.
