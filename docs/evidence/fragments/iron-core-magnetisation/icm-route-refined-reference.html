WEST uniform 40 mm: the 1.2e-12 route gap is rounding at a Newton-matrix condition number near 1e8

The in-place Cholesky Newton route and the existing LU route differ on the persisted uniform 40 mm WEST operator (6 632 cells, 39 792 affine unknowns) by frobenius_relative 1.1953e-12 and maximum_entry_relative 2.2044e-12 over the converged coefficient vector, both routes at tolerance 1e-12 in one process. This reproduces the H200 measurement 1335154 (1.2125e-12 / 2.2887e-12) to within the revision change. Both routes were then solved against an iteratively refined reference and both lie within 1.1e-12 of it, so the gap is double-precision rounding at this condition number, not a defect in either route.

What was compared, and what was not

The reference is the solution of the first Newton linear system J(0) d = -F(0) on the persisted operator, refined by iterative refinement whose float64 residuals are accumulated in longdouble until the correction stalls. It is a reference for the first Newton direction, not for the converged state: the first step carries the state to leading order, so its distance predicts the converged coefficient difference only to that order. The converged coefficient gap between the two routes is measured directly and reported above; each route's converged coefficient distance from a reference converged in extended precision is not measured (see the limits below). Probe fields from the two converged solutions are compared in the same run.

Neither route was compared entry by entry against the reference in the converged state, and no interaction value, solve or bar was changed by this measurement.

Measurement

The Newton matrix condition number is a Hager one-norm estimate at the same step (||J||_1 = 11.58, ||J^-1||_1 = 8.80e6). The resolvability check tests the nearest route distance, which is the smaller over routes of each route's larger of its two measures: 3.2889e-13 (the Cholesky route's maximum-entry distance; the LU route's maximum-entry distance is 1.0863e-12). The reference's own forward error, 2.3889e-16, sits about 3.1 decades below that tested figure, so the comparison resolves the gap.

Control: a real route defect is refused

The comparison was tested against a genuine route fault rather than a scaled copy of the reference. The Newton matrix is multiplied by 1 + 1e-10 before its own LU factorisation and solve, and that perturbed route is fed to the same distance comparison and the same verdict classifier. It reads Frobenius 9.9998e-10 / maximum-entry 9.9990e-10 from the refined reference, about 3 000 times (3.5 decades) the nearest unperturbed route distance, and the classifier returns defect rather than rounding. The detector therefore fires on a route fault the instrument can see, and the rounding verdict on the two real routes is not a blind spot of the comparison.

Verdict

Rounding. Both route distances are more than four decades below kappa*eps = 2.26e-8, the worst-case bound that rounding at condition number 1.02e8 allows. The LU route is the farther of the two, by 3.3× in Frobenius, consistent with the Cholesky route carrying two built-in refinement passes in the original differential system while the LU route does not; neither route is systematically wrong.

Bar proposal

The 1e-12 route-comparison bar is below the rounding band of this operator: the routes themselves differ by 2.2e-12 at their largest entry at tolerance 1e-12 while each lies within 1.09e-12 of a reference that is three decades more accurate. The bar is proposed reset to 1e-11 for the route-to-route coefficient comparison, or stated as ten times kappa*eps (2.26e-7) at the measured condition number, which admits this rounding-level gap while still refusing the planted 1e-10 route defect. No code, test or interaction value was changed.

Limits and follow-up

The extended-precision reference covers the first Newton linear system only. A nonlinear reference converged in extended precision would require the material residual and the 39 792-unknown Jacobian to be formed in extended precision on every Newton step, which the current route cannot do within this measurement; each route's converged coefficient distance from such a reference is therefore not reported. The converged coefficient gap between the routes, and their probe-field gap, are reported directly.

Follow-up work: the measurement rebuilds the affine Newton operands in scripts/west_iron_linear/measure.py, duplicating the assembly that nova/biot/magnetisation.py performs inside the solve.
