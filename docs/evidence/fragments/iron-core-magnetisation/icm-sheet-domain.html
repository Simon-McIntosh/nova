Closed form beyond a sloped edge's ends

nova/biot/sheet.py returned NaN for any target beyond a sloped segment or on the far side of its supporting line. The pole-reduced antiderivative's phi = 0 panel took 2 sqrt(r (r_1 + r b_1^2)), whose argument is negative once r_1 < -r b_1^2: on a 30 × 61 box at slope pi/8, 120 of 1830 targets returned NaN in both the flux and the field, and the Phi3 sheet-tile prototype measured 47488 of 162816 interaction entries (29 %) NaN on the 1696-cell WEST mesh. The phi = pi panel's offset r_1 + r was passed unsigned too, so the logarithmic model there was built from a negative offset.

Both quantities enter the log model as magnitudes — the model is the square root of a squared term — so they are now taken unsigned, the same form the phi = pi curvature already used.

A second defect surfaced in the field while the new box grid was checked. The arsinh gradient in _arsinh_gradient graded its azimuthal rule at phi = 0 only, which resolves the logarithmic layer where r_1 = r but not where r_1 = -r, a confluence a far-side target crosses. There the analytic field disagreed with its own flux's derivative by 16 % (target r = 3 m, z = -1.875 m, slope pi/8) and with the ring reference by the same amount. The rule is now placed at whichever end is nearer the confluence, which brings the box grid to 8.7e-12 worst relative error.

Measurement

tests/test_edge_sheet.py adds beyond-the-end and far-side targets at the section's four standoffs for slopes pi/8, pi/4 and 3 pi/8 in both segment orientations, and a box grid over r = 0.05 to 3 m and z = −1 to 1 m (7 × 9 targets per slope), each compared with scripts/edge_sheet/reference.py. Gate: 654 passed. Restoring the unguarded square root fails two of them by NaN (EXIT=1, gate-negative-control.log). The field is held to 1e-10 of the reference's own vector scale, since a component that is a cancellation residue (the radial one where it is a thousandth of the vertical) cannot carry a per-component relative floor.

Recorded limitation

At the half-cell standoff (0.01 m) on the far side of the line, the pole-reduced flux meets only 1.1e-10 to 2.2e-10 against the reference while the graded cross-check meets 1e-14: the two ends' antiderivatives cancel five digits there. That is the reduction's own conditioning, unchanged by this node, and the near-field test asserts 1e-9 with the reason stated.
