The antiderivative, and what it replaces

Part II's final expressions are antiderivatives in the source coordinate, not definite values: the finite segment enters as the difference between the two ends. This was not assumed. In the straight-cylinder limit the eq (24) expression evaluated at one end alone misses the reference by 75 per cent, and the difference of the two ends reproduces it to 3e-16.

For a sloped segment, parameterised r' = b_1 z' + c_1, the azimuthal integral's antiderivative in z' is, with gamma = z' - z, r_1 = c_1 + b_1 z and a_1^2 = 1 + b_1^2,

a_1^3 f(phi) = a_1 b_1 D + (r_1 + b_1^2 r cos phi) arsinh(beta_1)

with D = sqrt(gamma^2 + r^2 + r'^2 - 2 r r' cos phi), beta_1 = Gamma_1 / B_1, Gamma_1 = a_1^2 gamma + b_1 (r_1 - r cos phi) and the exact squared denominator B_1^2 = r_1^2 - 2 r r_1 cos phi + r^2 (1 + b_1^2 sin^2 phi). It reproduces the paper's eq (8) and reduces to eq (24) at b_1 = 0. The flux is psi = mu0 a_1 r [f(end_2) - f(end_1)] with the azimuthal integral taken on a fixed panelised Gauss-Legendre rule — the same class of rule the paper's eq (12b) leaves its residual integral to.

The closed D part is off by 7.5 and 7.4 units on those two ends while the quadrature's own value stays near -0.6; no reading of the (2r) factor or of the P_j denominator tried closes that gap. The same applies to the flat-segment coefficients of eq (26): the flat case is therefore evaluated as the exact surface-current integral of the ring flux (nova.biot.greens.greens_psi) on a fixed panelised rule, which is what the reference integrates, and the sloped case uses the antiderivative above. Shipping a formula whose error is unknown is worse than shipping one whose error is measured.

Negative control

Flipping the sign of the closed form's sheet current fails 52 of the 58 tests (EXIT=1): all 25 reference comparisons, the Snow cylinder form, the pancake form and all five flipped-current controls. The six that still pass are the linearity and segment-order identities, which a global sign flip leaves true. The head arm passes all 58.
