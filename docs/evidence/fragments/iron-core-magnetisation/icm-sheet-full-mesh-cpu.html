The all-target WEST sheet-tile agreement on CPU

The device build and the host route were compared entry by entry over all 1696 targets of the prototype WEST mesh and all 1696 cells, on one 36-core node, inside the test's own 3600 s bound. The comparison finished in 747 s and printed its receipt. That receipt fails the section's 1e−13 bound: 3077 of 11,505,664 entries are above it, and the worst entry is 2.282e−12, 22.8 times the bound. Every entry is finite on both sides. The measurement is reproducible and worker-count sufficient; the bound is not met.

The bound is the sum of the absolute values of the per-end antiderivative terms that make up each entry, as the section defines it, not the net entry. The receipt therefore measures what the section says it measures.

The same measurement at the same source revision printed a byte-identical receipt under job 1308770 earlier in the same node's work (2.282e−12 at (1692, 2116), 3077 of 11,505,664), so the result is deterministic across runs and not a transient of one allocation.

Where the failing entries are

The fixture holds its error matrix in memory and prints only the aggregate, so no per-entry array was persisted and the 3077 entries cannot be classified by target distance, edge slope or sheet route from this run's artifacts. The printed coordinate is the only per-entry fact retained: (1692, 2116) is, in the fixture's own layout, the BR row of physical target 1692 against the MZ column of cell 420 (column 2116 is in the MZ block that begins at column 1696). That is the worst entry only; it says nothing about the distribution of the other 3076.

Two facts about the distribution are nevertheless recorded. The failure is concentrated far out in the tail: p99.9 is 2.205e−14, so the bulk of the matrix agrees far below the bound and the 3077 entries sit beyond the 0.1 % percentile. And the count is not a property of the eight targets the earlier preflight measured: on those eight, no entry exceeds 1e−13 at all.

The subtractive-bracket control

The declared control restores the subtractive flat-sheet radial bracket, in which the endpoint radial kernel is the difference of nearly equal complete integral terms rather than a source-multipole series. It was applied to a complete scratch extraction of the committed tree — not to the tracked source — and the step refused to run unless the imported module was that extraction's copy. The log's header records module: /tmp/mcintos-1308778-VjJS/nova/biot/sheet.py, and the mutant blob 3ac10917783725f2a6f9c4ceb03de36879f2adeb differs from the head blob 1533f1c3edaffde3df5fffcb0618a715cb6fffee.

Two witnesses were required to fail. One did and one did not.

So the control is only half a counterexample on this host: the mutation restores a batch-dependent radial kernel, which the batch witness catches, but it does not move the eight-target agreement at all on 98dci4-clu-5008. The shape of that non-result matters for reading the failure above. The same mutation run as a pre-flight on the fleet allocation (98dci4-clu-2068, eight targets, one worker) failed both witnesses, in 65 s. A mutation that moves the eight-target agreement on one CPU and leaves it untouched on another does not change the physics and is not a change of formulation: it changes which arithmetic the compiler emits. That is the same mechanism as the all-target failure, whose residual is likewise a difference between two evaluations of one closed form at different array shapes.

The tracked source was never edited: the worktree blob and the HEAD blob at the end of the job are both 1533f1c3edaffde3df5fffcb0618a715cb6fffee.

Verdict against the section's clause (1)

Clause (1) requires the device build and the host route to agree over the 1696-cell prototype mesh, entry by entry, to within 1e−13 relative to the sum of the absolute per-end antiderivative terms, with every entry finite. Three of those four conditions hold: the comparison now runs over every target and every cell, it finishes well inside the test's bound, and every entry is finite. The fourth does not: 3077 entries are above the bound and the worst is 2.282e−12.

The measurement is therefore complete and its answer is negative. Nothing in this node's work changed product code, so the disagreement stands at the merged head and is not a regression introduced here. It is the risk the previous node named when it reported itself blocked on the full numerical receipt rather than on a measured disagreement: that receipt now exists, and it is a disagreement. What remains is a decision this node cannot take — whether the four edge-sheet routes (pancake, sloped, cylinder height, flat) each need their residual pinned against a per-entry reference, or whether the bound's own definition is what the tail is measuring. The per-entry arrays needed to answer that were not persisted and would have to be produced by a further measurement.
