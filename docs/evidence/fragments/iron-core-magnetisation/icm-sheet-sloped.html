§2 — The sloped sheet, held to the floor at the near standoffs

Node icm-sheet-sloped takes the sloped conic sheet of nova/biot/sheet.py to the section's 1e-10 relative floor at every one of the fixed target set's near standoffs. The previous precedent node measured the sloped form at 9.2e-05 at half a cell width and declared twenty-one targets xfail(strict=True); those marks are gone and every sloped target now asserts the floor outright.

Two integrands needed the remedy, not one. The arsinh term is log-singular where the denominator vanishes — a target level with a segment end, which is exactly the “start” and “end” stations — and the D term carries a square-root layer of width D_0 = hypot(gamma, r - r_p) at the same azimuth. The arsinh term now runs on the graded two-panel rule that nova.biot.polygonanalytic already owns: each half of the turn is stretched by a sinh map from its own end, a model of the logarithm is subtracted in closed form, and the bounded remainder is integrated, with the sheet's own polynomial weight carried through _weighted_residual. That helper is private to the Part V module and is imported rather than copied, so one implementation of the machinery serves both. The D term gets the same map anchored at phi = 0, which is exact for sqrt(D_0^2 + 4 r r_p sin^2(phi/2)). Both squares are formed from positive terms rather than by subtracting nearly equal radii; that change alone is worth two further digits at the start and end stations, where the difference of two antiderivatives is the whole answer.

The far standoffs move from about 1e-15 to 3e-13: three orders of margin to the floor are given up there in exchange for six orders at the near standoffs, which is the trade the section's production range asks for.
