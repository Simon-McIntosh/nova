Source-radius expansion of the exterior cylinder height endpoint

Write g=z′−z, target radius r, source radius s, d=√(r²+g²), t=g/d and v=s/d. The dimensionless height endpoint carried before μ₀/(2π) is

H_Z = g ∫₀^π s(s−r cos φ) / (L² D) dφ,
L² = r²+s²−2rs cos φ,  D² = g²+L².

Its complete-elliptic identity is HZ=(g/a)[K(m)+(s−r)/(s+r)Π(n|m)], with a²=g²+(r+s)², m=4rs/a² and n=4rs/(r+s)². For an exterior target the two terms cancel as s/r tends to zero. The angular projection of the flux primitive carries the same small remainder.

Introduce the disk potential V=∫disk(s)1/D dA. The planar divergence of g(ρ−r)/(L²D) is −g/D³ plus its 2π sign(g) point contribution at ρ=r. The divergence theorem therefore gives 2HZ=∂gV+2π sign(g)1r<s. For r>s the point contribution vanishes. The angular addition theorem and the analytic source-radius integration give

V = 2π Σₖ≥₀ P₂ₖ(0) P₂ₖ(t) s²ᵏ⁺² / [(2k+2)d²ᵏ⁺¹],
∂g[d⁻ⁿ⁻¹ Pₙ(t)] = −(n+1)d⁻ⁿ⁻² Pₙ₊₁(t),
H_Z = −π t v² Σₖ≥₀ [(2k+1)/(2k+2)] P₂ₖ(0)
                         [P₂ₖ₊₁(t)/t] v²ᵏ.

The factor tv² is formed before summation. Carrying the odd Legendre polynomial divided by t keeps g=0 regular and avoids losing relative accuracy near the edge's plane. With E=P₂ₖ(t), O=P₂ₖ₊₁(t)/t and p=P₂ₖ(0), the recurrence is

E_next = [(4k+3)t² O − (2k+1)E]/(2k+2),
O_next = [(4k+5)E_next − (2k+2)O]/(2k+3),
p_next = −p(2k+1)/(2k+2),   E₀=O₀=p₀=1.

The qualified region is r>s and v≤4/5. Ninety-six terms leave a geometric tail below double-precision rounding. Elsewhere the complete-elliptic identity has no large opposing terms: r≤s gives the same sign, while the remaining exterior region bounds (r−s)/(r+s). No source or angular quadrature is added.
