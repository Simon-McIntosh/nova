One owner for the edge-sheet closed form

What changed

The sheet tile no longer carries its own transcription of the Part II closed form. nova/biot/tiledassembly.py held a second copy of the sloped antiderivative, the graded arsinh rule, the flat pancake route and the azimuthal rule — the nine _sheet_* helpers plus packed_sheet_field and packed_sheet_end_scale — alongside the scalar owner in nova/biot/sheet.py. The batched, padded entry point the tile needs now lives in sheet.py, beside SheetFlux, sheet_flux and sheet_field, and tiledassembly imports it. The tile and the host route are one implementation, so a repair applied to one is applied to both.

_sheet_graded_rule, the quadrature-only helper the tile carried and no route called, is deleted with the rest of the copy.

The per-end scale now comes from the terms it scales

The measure node's blocking finding reproduces: packed_sheet_end_scale built the BR scale from the derivative of the antiderivative in radius, while packed_sheet_field builds BR from its derivative in height. The gate therefore divided the BR half by the wrong term sum.

The repair is structural rather than a second transcription of the branch structure: one helper, _sheet_end_terms, returns the two per-end field terms of each component for both the sloped and the flat route, already in field units. packed_sheet_field is the signed sum of those terms; packed_sheet_end_scale is their absolute sum. Neither can restate the branch structure independently, so a component's scale cannot be built from another component's terms again.

The independent ring reference floor is a formulation gap, not a precision defect

The measure node recorded a 3.0518e-8 disagreement between the tile and the prototype's ring-quadrature reference, flat across orders 32, 64 and 128, and read it as a single-precision path in the reference loader. Measurement rejects that reading.

  • The prototype (scripts/prototypes/iron_magnetisation/magnetisation.py) is pure numpy float64: np.polynomial.legendre.leggauss nodes, nova.biot.greens.greens_bz_br cast to float64 on entry, no jax, no float32 constant anywhere in edge_rings, ring_field or interaction.
  • Single-cell check at order 32/128/512/2048 against sheet_field: the four edges of cell 0 agree to 2.3e-15, 3.0e-15, 2.3e-15 and 4.6e-14 (edge 1) and 3.1e-15, 4.2e-15, 3.6e-15, 4.3e-14 (edge 3) — the closed form and the ring integral agree to double-precision rounding, so no single-precision array is in the path.
  • The flat worst entry is cell 432, a horizontal edge from (0.05, 3.5224) to (0.13, 3.5224), seen by a target at (0.09, -0.834). The closed form returns B_R = -1.622552985639e-13, the ring quadrature -1.622552963224e-13 at order 32 and identically at order 512; the difference is 2.24e-21, and B_Z agrees to every printed digit. It is flat in order because the quadrature has converged: what the entry measures is the flat route's own accuracy where the two components cancel, not a refinement of either.

The per-end terms of that pair are 1.82e-13 for BR against a net -1.62e-13, so the gap is 1.2e-8 of the scale the gate divides by. With the corrected scale the worst over eight targets is 3.7868e-8, unchanged from the wrong scale to three digits — the reference floor is therefore independent of the BR scale defect. REFERENCE_GATE is set to 1e-7, just above the measured floor and tighter than the 1e-6 it replaces.

The tile gate still fails on CPU — the dedup is landed, the gate is not met on that backend

With the tile and the host route now the same function, the gate that compares them measures the tile's batching and padding alone, and on CPU it does not hold. Eight targets, two workers, NOVA_SHEET_TILE_TARGETS=8 NOVA_SHEET_TILE_WORKERS=2: worst entry 6.258e-9 at (8, 425), 2051 of 54272 entries above 1e-13; percentiles p50=0.000e+00 p90=1.886e-16 p99=0.610e-12 p99.9=1.023e-09 p100=6.258e-09. Most entries are bit-*identical* between the tiled (vmap over pair blocks) and unbatched (flat chunk) launches, which rules out a systematic formulation difference and points at a subset of pairs.

Chasing it to the component: on the same pairs, the BZ rows agree bit for bit and only the BR rows differ, by e.g. 5.85e-21 on a value of -5.465e-12 whose per-end terms are of order 1e-13 — about 6e-8 of the scale, far above double-precision rounding of those terms. The packed function's own gradient (_sheet_closed_gradient, all three returned components) is bit-identical between the flat and blocked launches in isolation, so the difference is introduced by the surrounding program rather than by the closed form's arithmetic.

This node does not resolve it. It is reported blocked on clause (2) rather than loosened: the gate stays at 1e-13 and the tile's agreement with the closed form on CPU is the open item, alongside the H200 run that clause (2) requires and which this node did not reach.
