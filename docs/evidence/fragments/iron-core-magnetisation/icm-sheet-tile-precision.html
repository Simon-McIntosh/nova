Why the batched BR differs on XLA CPU

Outcome

The disagreement is not in the sheet's formulation and not in the tile. It is a rounding difference that XLA's CPU backend introduces between two program shapes: the same pair's value changes with how many pairs share the program. No product change is landed; the gate stays at 1e-13, no test is marked xfail and no tolerance is moved. This node reports blocked on clause (2) with the reduced reproducer below.

The reproduction, and the reduction that rules out the tile

Two targets of the 1696-cell WEST prototype mesh, all 3744 edges, on XLA CPU: the blocked launch (jax.vmap over pair blocks) and the flat launch (one jitted call over a chunk) disagree on the BR rows and agree bit for bit on most of them. 

B_R vmap-vs-flat: identical 5421/7488 bitwise; max |diff| 5.854e-21 at pair (0,1129)
                  tile=-5.465098408938728e-12 ref=-5.4650984030847755e-12
B_R scan-vs-flat: identical 5421/7488 bitwise; max |diff| 5.854e-21 at pair (0,1129)
B_R vmap-vs-scan: identical 7488/7488 bitwise; max |diff| 0.000e+00

vmap and lax.scan are bit-identical to each other, so the tile's blocking, padding, pair ordering and gather are not involved: the difference is between a program carrying many pairs and one carrying fewer, and the tile happens to be the many-pair side.

The operation: the value of one pair depends on its batch

Pair (0, 1129) is a horizontal edge, so it takes the flat (pancake) route of _sheet_pancake_terms and never enters _sheet_closed_gradient. Comparing the three returned terms of that one helper, flat launch against a vmap launch of the same pairs: 

n=1:  potential identical | radial identical | height identical
n=2:  potential differs 1.2e-13 | radial 1.3e-13 | height 4.2e-15
n=8:  potential differs 1.5e-13 | radial 4.3e-13 | height 3.0e-14
n=512:potential differs 2.4e-13 | radial 2.7e-11 | height 2.2e-12

A single pair agrees exactly; from two pairs upward it does not. The decisive measurement is that the flat program's value for the fixed pair moves with the batch it is placed in, while the value itself is deterministic: 

pair (0,1129), flat launch of packed_sheet_field:
  n=1  B_R = -5.4650984030847755e-12
  n=2  B_R = -5.465098408938728e-12
  n=8  B_R = -5.4650984030847755e-12

So this is not a formulation difference between two routes and not a defect in any one expression: the same arithmetic on the same inputs rounds differently depending on the program shape it is compiled into.

Every ingredient is bit-identical; only the composition moves

Each block the pancake route is built from was compared between the two launch shapes over the same 3744 edges and found identical in every entry, for two targets: complete_kind (first and second kind), traced_zeta at pi/2, and both complete_pole terms. On the sloped route the same holds for _sheet_root_gradient and _sheet_arsinh_gradient (all three returned components, 3360/3360 identical), while _sheet_closed_gradient's composed dS/dgamma differs in 283 of 3360 entries by up to 1.8e-11. The only step that differs is therefore composition — the multiply-adds that combine blocks, not the blocks.

The flags, and what identifies contraction

  • XLA_FLAGS=--xla_cpu_enable_fast_math=false: no change, the same five failing bits in the same pairs.
  • XLA_FLAGS=--xla_cpu_use_thunk_runtime=false: no change (the flag is deprecated and ignored on this build).
  • XLA_FLAGS=--xla_cpu_max_isa=SSE4_2, which removes the fused multiply-add from the instruction set: the flat program's value for the fixed pair becomes batch-independent (identical at n=1, 2 and 8), which is the signature the default build lacks. Contraction is therefore the mechanism of the lane dependence; a residual flat-versus-blocked difference remains, so contraction is one of the shape-dependent roundings rather than the only one.
  • jax.lax.optimization_barrier around each composed addend, to pin the composition to separately rounded adds: the results were bit-identical to the unbarriered run, so the barrier is elided by the CPU backend and cannot be used to pin rounding from nova.

Why it is not fixable in the expressions as they stand

The amplified quantity is a bracket that cancels: the pancake route's 2 source K - Σ(pole) and the sloped route's a_1 b_1 dR/dgamma + dA/dgamma each combine blocks of like magnitude into a result many orders smaller, so a last-bit difference in a block arrives in the answer multiplied by the ratio of the two scales — that is how 5.854e-21 of absolute difference becomes 6e-8 of the gate's per-end scale. Neither block is wrong and neither expression can be re-ordered from Python: the contraction is applied by the backend, per SIMD lane, and is not observable or pinnable at the JAX level.

The identified next step is a cancellation-free reformulation of the pancake route's radial block (and the sloped gradient's composition, which the same measurement shows differs by the same mechanism), so that the answer is a sum of terms of its own size rather than the small remainder of two large ones. That makes the gate hold whatever rounding the backend chooses, which is what the plan's clause (1) asks for; it is a formulation change in a part of the closed form this node did not reach inside its budget.
