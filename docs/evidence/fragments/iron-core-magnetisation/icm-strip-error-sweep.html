§2 — Where the thin Part V strip's error turns over

Node icm-strip-error-sweep measures the only quantity the sheet-route decision rests on: the relative error of a Urankar Part V polygon strip against an exact line integral of nova's ring Green's functions, as the strip width eps runs from 1e-2 to 1e-8 of the segment length. The answer is that the strip does not reach 1e-10 anywhere in that range: its best attainable worst-case error over the fixed target set is 6.7e-08, at eps/L = 1e-4, and no single edge-length fraction meets 1e-10 at every slope. Every one of the 60 target references converged, so the failure is the strip's and not the instrument's.

The reference, and the control that found a defect in it

The reference is a Gauss-Legendre integral of greens_psi and greens_bz_br along the segment (scripts/edge_sheet/reference.py), with the initial panels graded geometrically toward the target's foot and every panel bisected each round, stopping when two successive rounds agree to 1e-13 relative. A component whose value is an exact symmetry zero (the radial field on the segment's mirror plane, whose integrand is odd about the foot) is held out of the convergence test, because its relative change is a roundoff residue rather than a signal. That exclusion was found the hard way: the first full pass flagged the four broadside targets at slope π/2 as non-converged, at 1e-4 to 1e-3 relative change on a component whose value is exactly zero, and holding those components out of the test cleared all four. The slope was then re-run with the corrected test and spliced into the receipt; the numbers below are the spliced run.

The control is an independent 80-node Gauss-Legendre quadrature of the same segment. On its first run the reference disagreed with it in the radial field by a factor of two in sign and in the vertical field by 1.4e-4 relative at eps = 1e-2: the ring Green's functions take the target first and the source loop last, and the reference had them swapped. The flux component hid the fault, because the flux of two coaxial loops is reciprocal in them. With the arguments in their documented order the reference agrees with the control to every printed digit (psi 7.891555298525e-07, bz 3.941125183429e-08, br -5.491738057798e-07 at the half-cell-width target of the zero-slope segment). A second defect — an unbounded bisection that drove one unconverging target to 16 GB and the allocator's kill — is closed by a panel budget of 200000, which reports non-convergence instead of exhausting memory.

Geometry and target set

One segment of length L = 0.10 m — the long edge of a thin cell — centred at (R, Z) = (1.5, 0.0) m, observed at the section's fixed target set: 0.5 cell width (0.01 m), 1 cell width (0.02 m), one segment length (0.10 m) and ten segment lengths (1.00 m), each off the segment's middle and off each of its two ends, for a cell width of 0.02 m. Four distances and three stations give twelve targets per slope and sixty in total, at slopes 0, π/8, π/4, 3π/8 and π/2. The strip is the rectangle of width eps on the segment carrying sheet density K/eps through polygon_analytic_greens, so both evaluations carry the same total current K·L.

The model branch is 1.8e-4 to 2.1e-4 at eps/L = 1e-2 and falls as eps², as the plan predicts; the rounding branch rises as the strip's two long edges cancel over a separation that shrinks with eps. The crossing sits near eps/L ≈ 3e-5 to 1e-4.

One production width for every slope

A production strip takes one fixed fraction of each edge's length, so the strip route survives only if some fraction meets 1e-10 at every slope. No fraction does. The combined curve — the maximum over every slope and every target at each width — bottoms at 6.748e-08 at eps/L = 1e-4, seven hundred times above the 1e-10 the strip route requires, and the best width of any single slope is 1.328e-08. The list of fractions meeting 1e-10 at every slope is empty, so no single edge-length fraction qualifies.

What this settles for the sheet route

The Part V strip is a 1e-8-to-1e-7 representation of a toroidal sheet at its best width, and no width does better, so the sheet does not ship as a Part V polygon strip. The limiting mechanism is the cancellation between the strip's two long edges, whose separation relative to the O(1 m) major radius falls to 1e-7 at eps = 1e-8·L while the kernel's own arithmetic is exact to double-double; the fitted cancellation factor in the table above is the measured multiple of machine epsilon that the subtraction costs. The next node (`icm-edge-sheet-route`) therefore implements Urankar Part II in nova/biot/sheet.py, and can reuse this module's sheet_reference unchanged as its 1e-13 reference.
