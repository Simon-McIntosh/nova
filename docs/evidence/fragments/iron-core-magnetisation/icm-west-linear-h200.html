WEST affine iron on the H200: three meshes converge; strict replay bytes differ

All three required meshes converge from M = 0 at full drive on an NVIDIA H200 NVL. The linear basis reduces the uniform-pair movement from 13.1% to 0.7575% at +2 m and from 17.2% to 1.1735% at +4 m.
