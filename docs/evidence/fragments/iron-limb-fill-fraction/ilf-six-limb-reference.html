The saved prototype states reproduce column fluxes of 12.7222264 Wb at 0.133333 m and 13.4730559 Wb at 0.1 m for relative permeability 200: a 5.5728% change relative to the finer value. Their represented column areas are 1.280 and 1.320 m², against the circle's 1.385116 m².

A fixed-boundary triangular surface-charge solver and a measurement driver were added. Exact triangular fields match the independent brick kernel inside and outside a cuboid. The circuit's oriented surface integral preserves its volume. These checks do not certify convergence.

The initial centroid formulation with a diagonal conservation correction is rejected: between 0.18 and 0.13 m its flux changes by 16.91% and 21.36% at relative permeabilities 50 and 200, and its in-vessel vector-field changes are 62.77% and 68.51%. Small linear residuals do not make these values a usable reference.

The subsequent area-weighted adjoint formulation preserves the solid-angle identity without a diagonal correction. Its 1,280-panel sphere check at relative permeability 50 gives interior H/H₀ = 0.06146019, against 3/52 = 0.05769231, outside the test's 0.003 absolute tolerance. The repeated-failure rule stopped further numerical work. The final code remains experimental; no accepted six-limb or annulus result is claimed.
