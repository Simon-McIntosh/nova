WEST iron on its true flat outline

The flat outline, the toroidal fill fraction and the cross-field factor are read once from the machine description. StaticMachineDescription.read_west_iron_outline opens the iron_core at its manifest DD pin through the existing read_iron_core, classifies the six stored segments by their own geometry, and returns an IronCoreOutline carrying six regions on the true outline, a fill profile per arm and limb, and the stored and filled section per radian of every region.

Regions and section accounting

A region's fill profile is read from the stored outline's own thickness, not invented: an arm's knots sit at every stored vertex radius with fill = thickness / limb_depth, so the linear interpolant reproduces the stored piecewise-linear taper exactly; the return limb carries a constant fill times radius sampled on seventeen nodes. The limb and arm outlines use the limb-geometry decision of iron-core-magnetisation: 0.800 m wide by 1.200 m deep. The limb keeps its stored inner face at R = 4.7965 m and extends outward to 5.9965 m; the arms keep their stored radial extent and become 1.200 m thick panels from the stored flat face.

What the scoped readers do

No script under this node's write paths constructs a flat outline: every WEST iron reader there reads through StaticMachineDescription, asserted by a test over the twelve scoped scripts. Two files outside this node's write paths still hold a second construction of the same outline — scripts/prototypes/iron_limb_fill/axisymmetric.py (west_regions, flat_fill, limb_demagnetising, WEST_JOIN) and its consumer scripts/iron_limb_production/measure.py. Removing them belongs to the production path, not to a write this node may make; the node manifest records it under outside_scope. The prototype's flat arm reaches R = 6.0 and its limb is 1.2035 m deep, so its section over the arm differs from the stored one by about 2 m² per radian; the regions returned here are the section-preserving choice.
