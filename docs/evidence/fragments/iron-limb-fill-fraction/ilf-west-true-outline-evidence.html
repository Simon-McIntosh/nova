§4 — WEST 62751 on the stored and the true outline

The production measurement builds both geometry models from the machine description. StaticMachineDescription.read_west_iron_outline supplies the true flat arm and limb regions with their per-region fill profile, the section-derived cross-field factor 0.4 and the stored 2.10 mm limb-to-arm gap; the stored model is the union of the stored tapered segments at unit fill with no cross-field factor. scripts/iron_limb_production/measure.py::measure_west now delegates to scripts/iron_limb_production/outline.py, so no production solve consumes west_regions, flat_fill or WEST_JOIN.

Two assertions were added to validate_control_receipt. The receipt's revision must be the one the gate is told to expect and the refusal names both; the solid-outline control, read from the receipt's own rows or from the source receipt it names and hashes, must stay above 100 % of the in-vessel reference. Both are covered by fast tests in tests/test_magnetisation.py; the gate selection reads 5 passed at the head, and removing the solid test's assertion leaves 1 failed, 4 passed.

The true-outline arm solved at 0.08 m on one H200 card (job 1318852): 3480 cells, converged in 8 corrections to a terminal residual of 4.5e-11 in 143 s. Its recorded material is the read's: the per-cell fill fraction spans 0.128 to 1.000 and the cross-field factor reaches 0.400, so the solve is the flat-region model and not a solid stand-in.

Not measured: the stored-versus-true field at the in-vessel ring, behind the limbs and above the arms. The stored arm at 0.08 m and both arms at 0.04 m did not reach a row in the node's compute window, so no mesh pair exists for either model and no model difference is reported.
