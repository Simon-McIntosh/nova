§4 — WEST 62751 on the stored and the true outline

The production measurement builds both geometry models from the machine description. StaticMachineDescription.read_west_iron_outline supplies the true flat arm and limb regions with their per-region fill profile, the section-derived cross-field factor 0.4 and the stored 2.10 mm limb-to-arm gap; the stored model is the union of the stored tapered segments at unit fill with no cross-field factor. scripts/iron_limb_production/measure.py::measure_west delegates to scripts/iron_limb_production/outline.py, so no production solve consumes west_regions, flat_fill or WEST_JOIN.

Two assertions were added to validate_control_receipt. The receipt's revision must be the one the gate is told to expect and the refusal names both; the solid-outline control, read from the receipt's own rows or from the source receipt it names and hashes, must stay above 100 % of the in-vessel reference. Both are covered by fast tests in tests/test_magnetisation.py: the gate selection reads 5 passed at the head, and removing the solid test's assertion leaves 1 failed beside 4 passed at the same selection.

The four solves ran in one H200 allocation (job 1318852). True 0.08 m: 3480 cells, 8 corrections, terminal residual 4.5e-11, 143 s. True 0.04 m: 13732 cells, 12 corrections, 4.0e-11, 713 s. Stored 0.08 m: 1695 cells, 4 corrections, 5.8e-14, 68 s. Stored 0.04 m: 6632 cells, 4 corrections, 6.6e-10, 252 s. The true arm's recorded material is the read's: the per-cell fill fraction spans 0.128 to 1.000 and the cross-field factor reaches 0.400, so it is the flat-region model and not a solid stand-in.

The model difference is the relative norm of the true-minus-stored probe field at 0.04 m; the mesh-pair difference is the same norm between the 0.04 m and 0.08 m fields of each model, largest of the two. A model difference no larger than its mesh pair is reported as not resolved.

The declared negative control ran in one H200 allocation (job 1319012, exit 0). The true outline at fill fraction 1 solved at 0.08 m in 4 corrections to a residual of 2.8e-13 in 151 s, and at 0.04 m in 4 corrections to 1.9e-10 in 658 s. Its in-vessel displacement at 0.04 m is 0.0834 against the true model's own in-vessel mesh-pair difference of 0.0081, so the control passes: unit fill is refused as a stand-in for the read fill profile, and the receipt records both numbers under the mutation key.
