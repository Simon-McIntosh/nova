The coupled plant on the circuit tier's owners

landing: the coupled plant caches, screens and reduces through the capabilities nova.circuit.passive already owns, with its public surface unchanged — coupled_plant(machine, source, *, n_modes, frequencies, error_tolerance, cache_dir, provenance, refresh) and CoupledPlant keep every field and terminal_impedance(frequency_hz) — and the plant built through the refactored path is bit-identical to the pre-refactor plant on every array field, on the reduced fixture and on the real 12 mm ITER sectioned set.

Equality, measured rather than argued. The pre-refactor revision and the working tree each build the plant through coupled_plant and dump nine array fields per machine; the comparison reports identical=True and max_relative=0.000e+00 on all eighteen fields, worst 0.000e+00 against a tolerance of 1e-12 (equivalence/compare.log, arms in equivalence/base.npz.log and equivalence/head.npz.log).

Serialisation moved to the owner. save_coupled_plant and load_coupled_plant now live in nova.circuit.passive, beside save_circuit_system and save_eigenbasis, and are re-exported by nova.circuit.coupled_plant so the call reaches them without naming the tier. A test asserts the call's serialisers are the module's, so a private pair cannot reappear.

Screening and reduction moved to the owner. terminal_impedance_spectrum gained an already-reduced mode form (mode_coupling with mode_rate), which the call uses and which reproduces the dense eigenbasis solve it performs without them; select_modes_to_tolerance ranks through the tier's mode_relevance and doubles the retained prefix until the caller's error criterion is met, which is the search the call previously carried. The dead _relative_error is removed.

The negative control. A scratch copy of the module scales the stored mode_tau by 1.5 as the plant is written. The cold-against-cached comparison then fails on that field: the mutant arm ends EXIT=1 with test_cold_and_cached_plants_are_identical and test_the_cache_is_written_by_the_circuit_tier_serialisers failing, while the head arm over the same neutral directory passes 4 with EXIT=0. Both arms print the module file they imported, so the arm is known to have loaded the tree it names.
