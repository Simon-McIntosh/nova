The section level, with the terminals as its series reduction

landing: CoupledPlant carries the winding at section level beside its terminal fields. section_names names the 295 winding sections, section_coil gives the coil each section belongs to in its series order, section_inductance is the sections×sections linkage, section_resistance the section resistances, and mode_section_coupling the retained modes' flux per ampere of each section. Every existing field is unchanged; the terminal fields are the series reduction of the section fields over section_coil.

Source. The section fields are taken from section 1's computed linkage (nova/circuit/conductor.py, linkage.py), not recomputed: section_inductance is the coil block matrix[:n,:n] of the cached sectioned set and section_passive_mutual its passive–section block matrix[n:,:n]; mode_section_coupling = (VT · section_passive_mutual)[keep] for the retained eigenvectors V of the passive system.

The terminal fields are the 1e-10 series reduction, all 14 coils, on the real 12 mm set. With S the wiring matrix built from the plant's own section_coil, the call checks coil_inductance == ST · section_inductance · S, coil_resistance == ST · section_resistance and mode_coil_coupling == mode_section_coupling · S to rtol=1e-10. On the cached sectioned set the maximum relative error is 0.000e+00 (exact, since the terminals are formed by that same reduction), logged at gate-logs/coupled-plant-sections/real-set.log.

The negative control. Perturbing one section's self inductance must break the terminal series-reduction check. Scaling section_inductance[0,0] by 1.5 leaves coil_inductance[0,0] at 3.438e-05 while the perturbed reduction gives 3.952e-05 — relative difference 0.13, far outside 1e-10. The control ends EXIT=1 with an AssertionError at gate-logs/coupled-plant-sections/negative-control.log, and the same invariant passes at EXIT=0 without the perturbation.

The section self terms hold section 1's 0.5% unsectioned check. Section 1 certified that the series sum of a coil's sections and their mutuals differs from nova's independently integrated unsplit-element boxes by at most 0.46218%. Reproduced at the quadrature section 1 used — the coil sections integrated at section_n_max=24, matching the unsectioned side — the maximum per-coil self fraction is 0.004621797, matching section 1's figure. The cached linkage carries its section block at a coarser quadrature (section_n_max=6) chosen for the coupling solve; its reduction differs from the unsectioned boxes by 0.84%, so the certification compares like quadrature with like.
