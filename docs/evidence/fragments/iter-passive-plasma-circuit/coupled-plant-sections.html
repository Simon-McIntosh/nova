The section level, with the terminals as its series reduction

landing: CoupledPlant carries the winding at section level beside its terminal fields. section_names names the 295 winding sections, section_coil gives the coil each section belongs to in its series order, section_inductance is the sections×sections linkage, section_resistance the section resistances, and mode_section_coupling the retained modes' flux per ampere of each section. Every existing field is unchanged; the terminal fields are the series reduction of the section fields over section_coil.

Source. The section fields are taken from section 1's computed linkage (nova/circuit/conductor.py, linkage.py), not recomputed: section_inductance is the coil block matrix[:n,:n] of the cached sectioned set and section_passive_mutual its passive–section block matrix[n:,:n]; mode_section_coupling = (VT · section_passive_mutual)[keep] for the retained eigenvectors V of the passive system.

The call performs the reduction; the test rebuilds the wiring itself. A machine that supplies only the section level gets its terminal fields from the call: coupled_plant forms coil_inductance = ST · section_inductance · S and coil_resistance = ST · section_resistance when the machine carries no terminal of its own. The tests no longer build both sides from one fixture matrix: they pass a section-only machine through the call and rebuild the expectation from the plant's own section_coil, so a wiring defect in the call fails the test instead of cancelling out. A supplied terminal is kept as given.

The terminal fields are the 1e-10 series reduction, all 14 coils, on the real 12 mm set. With S the wiring matrix built from the plant's own section_coil, the call checks coil_inductance == ST · section_inductance · S, coil_resistance == ST · section_resistance and mode_coil_coupling == mode_section_coupling · S to rtol=1e-10. The real-set arm builds its machine with no terminal fields, so the reduction under test is the call's own; the maximum relative error is 0.000e+00, logged at gate-logs/coupled-plant-sections/real-set.log.

The negative control calls coupled_plant with the perturbed section block. A machine is built whose section self term section_inductance[0,0] is scaled by 1.5 while its supplied terminal linkage comes from the unperturbed matrix, and the perturbed machine goes through the call. The reduction of the stored section block then no longer reproduces the stored terminal, so ST · section_inductance · S == coil_inductance fails by a relative 0.13, far outside 1e-10. In the suite the control is wrapped in pytest.raises(AssertionError) so the gate stays green; the negative-control log runs the same test with that wrapper stripped, so the assertion escapes and the log carries the failing test id FAILED test_negative_control.py::test_series_reduction_check_fails_on_a_perturbed_section_self and EXIT=1 at gate-logs/coupled-plant-sections/negative-control.log. The same assertion passes at EXIT=0 on the unperturbed machine.

The section self terms hold section 1's 0.5% unsectioned check. Section 1 certified that the series sum of a coil's sections and their mutuals differs from nova's independently integrated unsplit-element boxes by at most 0.46218%. Reproduced at the quadrature section 1 used — the coil sections integrated at section_n_max=24, matching the unsectioned side — the maximum per-coil self fraction is 0.004621797, matching section 1's figure. The cached linkage carries its section block at a coarser quadrature (section_n_max=6) chosen for the coupling solve; its reduction differs from the unsectioned boxes by 0.84%, so the certification compares like quadrature with like.

The cache key folds every input that feeds a stored output. The plant digest covers the coil and section wiring and linkage, section_names, section_passive_mutual (which forms mode_section_coupling), the passive system, the source's time base, currents and prescribed distributions, the reduction settings and the provenance. Two machines differing only in their section labels, and two differing only in their section–passive mutual, land in three distinct cache entries; the test asserts three plant.npz paths and that the mutual-differing pair's mode_section_coupling differs.
