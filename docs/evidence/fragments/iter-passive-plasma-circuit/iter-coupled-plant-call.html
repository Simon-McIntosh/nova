Returning the coupled ITER plant through one call

landing: the sectioned coils, the reduced passive modes and the plasma flux drive are returned through one public call, nova.circuit.coupled_plant.coupled_plant(machine, source), and one result type, CoupledPlant, cached through nova's serialisers with the location resolved through FilePath; the call returns the same plant cold and from its cache, and the reduced plant's terminal impedance matches the full model's to under 1% for every coil from 0.1 Hz to 5 kHz.

The result carries the section-level coil inductance matrix and resistances, the retained passive modes as decay times and coupling to every coil, the plasma flux drive at every coil section and passive mode against time, the reduction error against frequency, and provenance naming the machine label, the plasma source and the nova revision. The plasma source is accepted through a minimal interface — a prescribed-current list plus the coil-current series — so no concrete source type is named.

The negative control perturbs one cached array (mode_tau scaled by 1.5 where the plant is written) in a scratch copy outside the repository; the cold-and-cached comparison then fails on that field and the log ends EXIT=1, while the head arm on the same tree passes with EXIT=0.
