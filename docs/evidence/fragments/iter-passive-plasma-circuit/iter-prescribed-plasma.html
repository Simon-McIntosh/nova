DINA current prescription and nova plasma linkage

On native-DD ITER 105014/1 and 105052/1, 867/867 and 2,036/2,036 slices above 0.1 MA were prescribed. The maximum current-centre coordinate errors are 4.303 and 27.817 mm, each below the 93.75 mm grid cell. Their median current scales are 1.0426 and −0.99979. Neither case needed a reconstructed ψ contour: 105014/1 used 532 closed separatrices and 335 stored boundary outlines; 105052/1 used 42 closed separatrices, 924 X-point cuts and 1,070 stored outlines.

Nova computes the coil and passive flux from geometry and DINA currents interpolated on each IDS time base. DINA's signed ψ map, corrected for the axis gauge, gives the plasma reference at the two map-covered VS3 sections; the other 12 coil sections use DINA's independent j_tor through nova's Green kernel. On 105014/1, 24 magnetics flux loops also provide recorded total-flux references. At 24 times per case, the maximum geometric residual is 1.081 Wb at 105014/1 VS3 and 2.523 Wb at 105052/1 VS3; the loop maximum is 0.872 Wb. They pass the stated absolute-or-relative bounds of 1 Wb or 10% (105014/1) and 3 Wb or 5% (105052/1). The direct j_tor maxima are 0.041 and 0.090 Wb against 0.1 Wb or 1% bounds. The absolute floors prevent the small low-current plasma flux from inflating a percentage.

One-centimetre vertical source shifts changed flux by −0.0183351 Wb and −0.0379121 Wb against gradient predictions of −0.0183045 Wb and −0.0378468 Wb. A retained mutation that ignored the shift failed its focused test. The research record carries the reference construction, case-specific figures, full metrics and the measurement receipts.
