Evolving the ITER vessel currents through the DINA scenarios

landing: the sectioned passive set is driven by DINA's coil currents and the prescribed plasma, integrated with the exact zero-order-hold eigenmode propagation of nova.circuit.propagate, and measured against DINA's own pf_passive currents, the whole coupled-flux coil voltages and the VS3 psi-map residual section 2 left open.

The ITER windings are superconducting, so their winding resistance is zero and each coil voltage is dPsi/dt alone; DINA's stored zero coil resistance is the physical value, not a gap in the data. The passive flux at VS3 is compared against exactly the source term the psi-map check subtracted, restricted to that kernel's passive columns, so a flux-model difference cannot hide inside the coil contribution.

The comparison therefore covers the DINA-represented subset, and the 42% of current in structures DINA does not model is unmatched by construction.

On 105014/1 the passive coupling removes 64 V of the 857 V rms the bare coils miss, and 72-77 V of it lands on PF2-PF5 (PF2 168 → 93 V, PF5 242 → 178 V). The plasma term adds back 10 V of rms: driven by the evolved passive currents the plasma contributes its own dPsi/dt. Dropping the plasma drive entirely (the negative control) leaves 780 V, so it is the passive coupling, not the plasma term, that carries the reduction. On 105052/1 the same arms read 783, 593 and 591 V, so the PF2-PF5 miss there is roughly twice as large and the coupling removes roughly half of it (PF2 388 → 177 V, PF4 780 → 301 V).

The VS3 residual does not close

Section 2 left a psi-map residual at the two VS3 sections of 0.88 and 1.08 Wb on 105014/1, and up to 2.52 Wb on 105052/1. Substituting the evolved passive flux for DINA's stored passive flux there does not close it; it grows. On 105014/1 the evolved passive flux peaks at 8.40 and 10.08 Wb against DINA's 7.37 and 8.46 Wb, a difference of 2.0 and 3.5 Wb. Those differences are larger than the residual itself, so the residual is not a passive-current modelling gap: DINA's own stored passive currents already reproduce the psi-map at VS3 to about 1 Wb. On 105052/1 the same substitution moves the 2.52 Wb residual to 16.3 Wb. Removing the plasma drive leaves the substitution essentially unchanged (6.19 Wb on 105014/1), because the passive flux at VS3 is coil-driven.

Controls

The positive control is a single passive ring under a coil-current ramp, integrated with the same zero-order-hold scheme and compared with the analytic L/R solution; it recovers the analytic peak to a relative error of 1.4e-13, so the propagation is exact for a linear ramp and the ring's decay time is set by its resistance, not its inductance. The negative control mutates the ring resistance handling in a scratch copy, where the analytic decay test fails while it passes on the head arm.
