DINA current-profile convention and internal inductance

Native-DD 3.38.1 data select the current prescription already in nova. Both derivatives carry the same ψ convention and total-Wb normalisation. On 105014/1 all 867 active slices have negative Ip, Bφ, ψaxis−ψboundary, p′ and FF′, giving a median current scale +1.0426. On 105052/1 all 2,036 active slices have negative Ip and Bφ but positive ψ span, p′ and FF′; its median −0.99979 scale reverses both derivative terms together. Under the IDS coordinate orientation these are COCOS 17-like and 11-like flux/current senses. The 105014/1 q array is nonfinite in every active slice, so its poloidal-angle digit remains indistinguishable between COCOS 17 and 13; this digit does not affect the current prescription.

The present prescription's median full-grid RMS error against DINA's independent profiles_2d.j_tor is 3.73% on 105014/1 and 0.295% on 105052/1. p′ inversion raises those to 9.13% and 5.02%; p′ alone per radian gives 5.76% and 2.10%; FF′ alone per radian gives 9.41% and 10.85%. Both terms per radian reproduce the Ip-scaled shape but require median scales +6.551 and −6.282, exposing the incorrect 2π magnitude. Maximum Euclidean centroid offsets are 4.30 and 28.14 mm, below one 93.75 mm cell.
