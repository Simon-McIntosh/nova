Reconstructed plasma into the coupled plant

coupled_plant now calls a generic plasma source wherever it called a DINA case, so a reconstruction or a simulation reaches the plant through the owner interface rather than a second path, and it refuses a machine whose supplied terminal inductance or resistance disagrees with the series reduction of its own section block. On DINA 105014/1 the generic source returns today's plant bit-for-bit (0.000000000 Wb maximum flux difference); the same slice presented as a reconstruction — EFIT-style p′ and FF′ on its own ψ normalisation, the last closed flux surface as boundary, coil currents resampled to every fourth row of their clock — meets a predeclared relative tolerance of 1e-3, measuring 1.163e-05 on the section flux and 1.163e-04 on the modal projection, with provenance naming the reconstruction-style source. The tolerance is bounded by the boundary polygon the two routes resolve, not by the coil cadence, which never enters the prescription. Both negative controls fire: a flipped-sign reconstruction and the real ITER machine carrying a terminal inductance moved one percent off its section reduction are each refused by the plant call. The research record gives the method, the tolerance reasoning and the scope.
