An unseen plasma source reaches the sectioned-set cache, and the plant build stops one layer past it

landing: the sectioned-set cache was exercised with sources the plant cache has never seen. Both real entries were copied to a scratch cache root and the build wrote geometry.npz there at their unchanged keys, with linkage.npy and system.npz byte-identical before and after; load_sectioned_machine then assembles the full 1,607-filament ITER machine from that copy, and an entry with the geometry removed is refused by name. The plant build itself, which is where these sources were to be driven through coupled_plant, stops on a defect one layer past the loader, in a module outside this node's write fence. The two DINA arms and the reconstruction-tolerance arm are therefore not met and are reported as not met.

The cache root is written, not rebuilt. The copy of each real entry (68df15665ab2ea45f427 at 12 mm and 06ff06d3eb7a4448f0fe at 6 mm) was handed to scripts/iter_sectioned_conductor_set/build.py, which wrote geometry.npz into the existing entry directory at the key that already covers every geometry input. Measured in scratch/part1_report.json: for both entries the sha256 of linkage.npy and of system.npz are identical before and after the run, and each entry gains exactly one file, the geometry, whose sha256 is recorded there. The plant cache under coupled-plant/ is listed with sha256 and mtime, the pinned entry 28148563521d92a2198b/plant.npz included; neither DINA case nor the reconstructed pulse has an entry, which is what makes them unseen.

A new source cannot drive an entry whose geometry is gone. The literal negative control copies the real 12 mm entry, unlinks geometry.npz, and drives a DINA 105069/3 source at it: load_sectioned_machine raises SectionedGeometryMissing matching geometry.npz rather than rebuilding the sectioned set or serving a reduced machine. Passed at head in 97.25 s; log at scratch/negative_control.log.

The plant build stops in nova/imas/prescribed_plasma.py. Driving DINA 105069/3 through coupled_plant on the cached machine reaches plasma_linked_flux_history (nova/circuit/coupled_plant.py:304) and then plasma_linked_flux (nova/imas/prescribed_plasma.py:98), which raises ValueError: polygon circuit 828 has no quadrature points. The function grids a polygon's bounding box at section_spacing=0.05 capped at section_n_max=8 and refuses when no grid point lands strictly inside; a subdivided passive filament is thinner than that spacing, so its box admits no interior point and the refusal is correct by the function's own rule. This is a property of the sectioned machine the cache supplies — every sectioned passive circuit is now a polygon of that kind — so it is the sectioned-set path that exposes it. Full traceback with the serving nova.__file__: scratch/probe_time.log. nova/imas/prescribed_plasma.py is not in this node's write fence, so the repair is handed on rather than made here.

Three review follow-ons are repaired. The plant's dimensions are counted against the fixture's own inputs (SMALL_COIL_NAMES, SMALL_SECTION_COIL) rather than against the machine the loader returns, since comparing a plant with the machine it was built from cannot show that either carries the right number of channels. The zero-rebuild spy walks sys.modules and replaces every attribute that is subdivide_passive_thickness or circuit_linkage_matrix, asserting the bound set is non-empty, so the count no longer reads a false zero while a live binding survives in another module. And conductor_geometry in scripts/iter_vessel_evolution/evolve.py is renamed sectioned_conductor_geometry with its single caller updated: its first return element was the unsectioned source winding when the function rebuilt the geometry, and is now the refined sectioned set the loader exposes as subdivision, so the old name promised the caller something the return no longer held. The sibling fragment for the cache node names the older spelling.

The reconstruction arm's gap is one boundary polygon, not the coil cadence, and it lands on small cancelling modes

landing: the arm that misses its predeclared tolerance was localised by measurement rather than argued. Four presentations of one 105014/1 slice were driven through the full sectioned machine in one job, each differing from the generic presentation in one thing, and the difference belongs to a single input: whether the case's stored separatrix outline is offered to the boundary resolver at all. The coil-clock cadence the reconstruction-style presentation also applies moves nothing — its prescription is byte-identical to the generic one. And the 13 array elements that cross the predeclared relative bound are not conductors next to the plasma: they are small-amplitude passive modes of the plant's projection, 1.1 m to 13.4 m outside the boundary, whose relative sensitivity is a cancellation effect rather than proximity.

A correction to the earlier reading of the failing arm. The gap is in mode_plasma_flux, the plasma flux projected onto the plant's passive modes, not in section_plasma_flux. scratch/gate.head_unseen:34 shows the failing assertion is the second of the two, and the first passed: section_plasma_flux agrees to 3.11e-05 relative with zero violations of the predeclared 1e-3, thirty times inside the bound. The tolerance is met on the winding sections and missed on the projection; the earlier report of "13 of 1312 sections" was a misreading of the mode count (the full machine carries 295 winding sections and 1312 passive modes).

What the two changes do, measured. The generic slice offers DINA's stored separatrix outline, which closes (endpoint gap 0.0 m) and is therefore returned first: 163 points enclosing 11.013883 m2. The reconstruction-style slice offers no separatrix at all — its outline is empty and its x_points are empty, as a reconstruction arrives, and this case stores no separatrix x-point — so the resolver falls through to the stored last closed flux surface: 89 points enclosing 10.931300 m2. The two polygons differ by 0.75 % in area, mask 15 grid cells differently, and redistribute 393.692 A of the 1.967469×106 A plasma current, a relative 2.0e-04. Every other slice field is shared: psi, ff_prime and boundary_outline are bit-identical, and p_prime is read from the same IDS array. The coil clock enters none of this: 507 coarse rows against 2027 full rows give an identical prescription digest, an identical plasma-flux digest, and coil currents interpolated onto the slice time (5.9275978 s, inside the coarse span) that agree to 0.0 A bit for bit. There is no interpolation defect to repair and no snapping to a sample.

No violating element sits near the plasma: the closest is 1.1318 m out, the median 7.36 m, and the machine's nearest passive point at all is 0.5329 m from the separatrix, 59 of 1312 within a metre. Nor is it a large-amplitude effect: the two modes with the largest linked flux (−2.19 Wb and −1.82 Wb) cross the bound only marginally, at 1.03e-03 and 1.26e-03, while the largest relative differences, up to 2.92e-02, sit on modes whose linked flux is 3e-04 Wb and below, where an absolute difference of 9e-06 Wb reads large against a near-cancelling sum. The largest absolute difference anywhere in the mode array is 8.20e-02 Wb, on a dominant mode that stays inside its own relative bound. The gate's reported extremes are each one of these rows: its maximum absolute violation, 0.00228984 Wb, is mode 74, and its maximum relative violation, 2.921503e-02, is mode 401.

Why the reduced machine passed at 1e-3. The reduced machine's nearest channel sits 1.3398 m from the separatrix and 1.3456 m from the last closed surface, and it carries four sections and six large passive rings: max relative difference 1.163e-05 (scratch/localise_reduced_machine.json). Its projection has no small near-cancelling modes to amplify the 2e-04 current redistribution, which is the structural difference between the two machines rather than a defect in either.

No repair is available inside this node's fence, and the decision is the lead's. Nothing measured here is broken: the boundary resolver prefers a stored separatrix when one is offered and the reconstruction presentation offers none, which is what makes it a reconstruction; the coil clock does not enter the prescription at all; and the plant is a faithful function of its inputs, its section array agreeing to 0.0 Wb with a computation that shares no cache. Two readings need a ruling. If the precondition is that the two presentations must agree on mode_plasma_flux to a relative 1e-3, then either the reconstruction presentation should offer the stored separatrix outline this case in fact carries — which removes the difference at its source and leaves the arm testing less — or the comparison needs an amplitude-aware criterion, because a relative test on a near-cancelling projection measures the cancellation rather than the physics. If instead the winding sections are the physical measure, the arm already passes at 3.11e-05. This node reports status: blocked with the measurement rather than widening the bound or marking the check xfail, because both options change that arm's precondition and neither is the agent's call.
