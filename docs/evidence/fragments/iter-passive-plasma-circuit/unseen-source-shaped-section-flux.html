A shaped section is sampled in its own interior, and the unseen sources now reach the full plant

landing: plasma_linked_flux averaged the plasma flux over a polygon circuit by laying a midpoint grid on the section's bounding box and keeping the points that fell inside. A section whose box is far larger than the shape it holds can contain none of them, and the function then refused a perfectly good circuit: on the full ITER sectioned machine, 42 of its 1,304 shaped circuits had no interior grid point and the plant build stopped on the first of them at nova/imas/prescribed_plasma.py:98, ValueError: polygon circuit 828 has no quadrature points. The section is now fanned into triangles from its first corner, each triangle split four ways, so every quadrature point is a sub-triangle centroid and lies inside the section by construction whatever its shape, thickness or orientation. Weights are sub-triangle area fractions and sum to one; each circuit's turn share is folded into them, every shaped circuit's points are gathered before the flux is evaluated so the kernel is built once for all of them, and a section enclosing no area is refused by name.

The rule was chosen by measurement. Reciprocity through nova.biot.polygon.polygon_greens — the flux a shaped section links equals the plasma cell currents weighted by the flux each cell links from unit current spread over that same section, the kernel the circuit linkage already uses for shaped source sections — was implemented first and then timed, not assumed. On one 105014/1 slice against the full machine, one section through that kernel costs 2.1071 s, while the bounding-box rule evaluates every one of the machine's 1,304 shaped sections together in 4.63 s. Reciprocity would put a plant build at tens of minutes, so the triangle quadrature was taken instead, and the reason is recorded in the function's own docstring rather than only here.

The repair moves the pinned plant by about one percent on the circuits it rescues. One 105014/1 slice against the full sectioned machine: 1,304 shaped circuits, 1,262 of which the bounding-box rule could sample and 42 of which it could not sample at all. The largest relative change in linked flux is 1.014535e-02 on circuit 1371 (new −2.032482410e-03 Wb against old −2.053313998e-03 Wb), and the twelve largest all fall between 8.6e-03 and 1.0e-02 in adjacent pairs, which is the pairing of the two faces of one wall section. The pinned power_convertor plant is not rebuilt, so this is how far a new cold build moves from it. The rectangular circuits are bit-identical at the same time, asserted with np.testing.assert_array_equal against the rectangular branch recomputed inside the same check, because the repair touches only the shaped-section branch.
