That is the measured representation defect.

Quadrature is not the cause. Integrating the coil self-block at the same hybrid rule as the coil-to-jacket mutuals (removing the separate finite-source rule) moves the smallest eigenvalue by 1.5×10−13 H on the coil self terms and the largest generalised eigenvalue by 2×10−9, against the measured 2.2×10−5 excess. The separate finite-source rule contributes essentially nothing to the over-screening.

The geometry defect is present but its repair is not yet validated. Inflating the jacket square to its outer diameter makes S more negative (−1.94×10−7 against −1.51×10−8), so "a bigger square outside" is not the fix. The remaining candidate is the IDS-faithful annulus itself. The obvious realisation — one circuit holding an outer disc at +1 and a core disc at −1, both at the section centre — is invalid here and was rejected: with a net-zero circuit current the observer weighting cancels and the jacket block computes as exactly zero (passive eig [0,0,0,0,0,0,0,0] on the 8-jacket block), which would remove the negatives by removing the jacket's coupling rather than by relocating it. Splitting the pair into two circuits makes the 16×16 jacket block singular (smallest eigenvalue −1.38×10−8) because the two discs are nearly coincident. A validated corrected-geometry arm is therefore still owed, and no geometry repair was committed.
