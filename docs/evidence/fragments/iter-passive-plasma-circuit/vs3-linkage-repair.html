The build now gives each annulus sector its own circuit — a section-shape override is indexed by circuit, so sectors sharing an id leave all but one invisible to the linkage kernel — which moves both keys. Two things follow, and the second is why this ends blocked rather than fixed. Sub-cell count is inert: 24 and 48 sub-cells give the same eigenvalue to every printed digit, because the observer rule caps the subdivision at the section scale, so refinement in that knob buys nothing. And the trend runs the wrong way: as the remaining knob is loosened, this neighbourhood's smallest eigenvalue falls monotonically, 1.841×10−5 → 1.646×10−5 H, so the negatives are not quadrature error converging to zero as the quadrature is refined. No floor was applied, and no tolerance was widened.

VSU's and VSL's bare inductance against their own four-turn references

A vertical coil is four turns in series at the four turn annuli pf_active gives, not one ring. The reference is therefore built per coil: each turn's self term from the repository's own closed form at the annulus's geometric-mean wire radius, plus twice each of the six distinct mutual pairs between the turns, the mutual being the coaxial ring form through the elliptic integrals. The reduced series map is compared with it directly.

The stored values stand 0.25 and 0.28 per cent above their references for the two bare inductances, and the mutual agrees to 0.0006 per cent. The declared tolerance is 1 per cent, about four times the largest measured disagreement; the residual sits in the self terms, which is where the sector discretisation lives — the mutual is an off-diagonal pair and reproduces to six parts in 107.

A single ring at R = 6 m, 4.3701378×10−5 H, is one turn at a radius no VS3 turn sits at, and neither coil is that quantity: the coil reference is 12.9 times it. Measuring the coils against it is what produced the earlier 12.88× and 17.30× ratios, and it is why that constant no longer appears as a reference for either coil.

Negative control

The loader half fires: a pre-repair joint linkage copied into a scratch entry is refused by name, measured at 45 negative eigenvalues and a minimum of −2.527958×10−7 H before the refusal. The square-arm half did not reproduce the declared count: with the area-equivalent square representation restored, the coil Schur complement came out indefinite with 56 negatives over 128 coil arms and a minimum of −5.706064×10−7 H, against the two the declaration names, because that arm's Schur complement is taken over the 128 coil arms rather than reduced to the fourteen coil channels first. The qualitative control holds; the declared count was not reproduced and is reported rather than adjusted.

Receipt: logs/negative-control.log.

What remains open

Done-when (1) does not hold for the 6 mm entry, so the run is blocked on it and no entry is offered for installation as a finished pair. The next step the measurement points to is the passive block's own linkage at the 6 mm layer's sub-floor rows, not a finer observer grid: a floor cannot be the remedy because applying one is what the clause forbids, and the trend above shows the neighbourhood is not converging to positivity.
