VSU's and VSL's bare inductance against their own four-turn references

A vertical coil is four turns in series at the four turn annuli pf_active gives, not one ring. The reference is therefore built per coil: each turn's self term from the repository's own closed form at the annulus's geometric-mean wire radius, plus twice each of the six distinct mutual pairs between the turns, the mutual being the coaxial ring form through the elliptic integrals. The reduced series map is compared with it directly.

The declared tolerance is 1 per cent, about four times the largest measured disagreement; the residual sits in the self terms, which is where the sector discretisation lives — the mutual is an off-diagonal pair and reproduces to six parts in 107.

A single ring at R = 6 m, 4.3701378×10−5 H, is one turn at a radius no VS3 turn sits at, and neither coil is that quantity: the coil reference is 12.9 times it. Measuring the coils against it is what produced the earlier 12.88× and 17.30× ratios, and it is why that constant no longer appears as a reference for either coil.

The overlap hypothesis, measured

A section thinner than SECTION_FLOOR is represented at the floor's size rather than its own, so its footprint can overlap its neighbours' and a pair's mutual can exceed the geometric mean of the two selves. That is the mechanism proposed for the 6 mm negatives. Two measurements test it, and the second is the one that decides.

No ratio reaches one: no pair in any of the six most negative modes carries a mutual larger than the geometric mean of its two selves, which is the signature the hypothesis predicts and the measurement does not find.

The footprints do show the floor at work. 372 of the 6 mm entry's 2,698 rows carry |dz| below SECTION_FLOOR = 10−2 m (minimum 5.017192×10−3 m, median 1.238420×10−1 m); the sub-floor pairs are represented at exactly 10−2 m, which raises a neighbour pair's overlap from 5.377×10−3 to 7.075×10−3 m and takes one pair whose true gap is zero from 7.340×10−3 to 10−2 m. The same rows at the same |dz| are in the 12 mm entry — 192 of its 1,847 rows, the same 5.017192×10−3 m minimum, and the sub-floor pairs' counterparts found at distance 0.0 m — and that entry has no negative eigenvalue at all. Separately, the two most negative modes sit on sections 0.24 m thick, where the represented extent equals the true one and the floor does nothing.

No two rows coincide within a millimetre in either entry, so the one-conductor-many-circuits reading is out as well. What the measurement leaves is a near-singular collective mode rather than an over-coupled pair: the 6 mm passive block's smallest eigenvalue is −9.192799×10−4 of its mean self term (4.361469×10−5 H) against +5.692051×10−5 for the 12 mm block (4.414977×10−5 H), so the finer set carries a collective mode both sets hold near zero and tips it through.

Figure provenance

The cross-section figure above is generated by docs/figures/iter-passive-plasma-circuit/build_vs3_jacket_positive_linkage.py, which the tree did not carry: the committed SVG had no generator, so any change to it was a hand edit. The generator saves through a check that refuses to write a figure whose text is clipped by the canvas, overlaps another text artist, or sits on a rendered patch — the last measured by re-rendering with every text artist hidden and asking whether a non-background pixel falls inside that text's box. It refused twice before it passed, both times on the conductor caption: on its first placement the caption's own box reached into the blue band, and an annotation's bounding box spans its leader as well as its text.

Both leaders now touch only the band they name. The conductor caption sits in the annuli's inner hole and both of its leader's endpoints lie within radius 20.5 mm, and a segment between two points inside a disc stays inside it, so no part of that leader can reach the jacket ring at 26.5 mm — which is the property the earlier figure lacked, its leader having left the caption outside both rings and crossed the red band on the way in. A caption outside radius 26.5 mm cannot have that property at all, because the two rings are concentric and a straight segment from outside the outer ring to the inner one must cross the outer one; that is why the conductor caption is inside the hole.

What remains open

Done-when (1) does not hold for the 6 mm entry, so the run is blocked on it and no entry is offered for installation as a finished pair. Three explanations are now excluded by measurement rather than argument: a finer observer grid (the negatives do not move with sub-cell count), a section floor (applying one is forbidden, and loosening it moves the neighbourhood's smallest eigenvalue down), and the overlap this round tested (no pair is over-coupled, the floor's footprint inflation is shared with the positive-definite 12 mm entry, and the two most negative modes sit well clear of the floor). What the measurement leaves is the question the next attempt should take up: what in the finer mesh drops a collective mode that both entries hold near zero through zero — the 12 mm block's smallest eigenvalue is +5.692051×10−5 of its mean self term and the 6 mm block's is −9.192799×10−4.
