Where the point jet's cold compile spends its time

One H200 job (1296870, node 98dci4-gpu-0003) compiled the point jet at revision b506f8075 in a fresh process with the compilation cache disabled, dumping the module after every HLO pass. The wall splits into trace 3.27 s, StableHLO lowering 0.45 s and XLA compile 96.12 s. The pass-level chain covers 92.10 s of that compile (95.8%), leaving 3.45 s before the first dump and 3.69 s after the last. The compile ran with per-pass dumping enabled, so its wall is longer than the 82.43 s measured without dumping; the split, not the total, is what this instrument reports.

The HLO instruction count grows and then collapses across the fusion pipeline: 5,813 instructions at the entry to optimization, a peak of 100,579 inside it (548 MB of HLO text), 23,807 on leaving multi_output_fusion, and 10,714 after the dead-code pass that follows. The expanded-equation count is a different number and does not follow this one.

Positive control, same job and same instrument: a chained elementwise program of 1,024 steps compiles in 7.72 s and the profile attributes 95.2% of that wall, with the same leading pass (variadic-op-splitter, 3.55 s). A 64-step chain of the same shape compiles in 0.74 s and the instrument attributes only 11% of it, which is the failure mode the control is there to expose: below about a second the dump chain does not bracket the work and a profile that reports nothing would go unnoticed.

Recommendation. The lever is HLO size at the entry to the fusion pipeline, not an individual arithmetic term. The graph that reaches fusion is not the traced one: variadic-op-splitter takes the module from 146 to 100,579 instructions in a single pass, and the two passes that then walk that expanded graph — variadic-op-splitter at 34.67 s and multi_output_fusion at 17.55 s — account for 52.22 s of the 92.10 s attributed, which is 56.7% of the profile and about 47 s of the 82.43 s cold compile measured without dumping. Codegen stages (autotuner 7.62 s plus 7.58 s of LLVM IR stages) add roughly 15 s, and nothing else exceeds 2 s. So §2 and §3 should bound what the program hands to the variadic splitter — the number of operands carried together through the point jet's vmap and the tuple structure of the source-jets carrier — because that is what turns a small module into 100,579 instructions before fusion runs. Removing census-ranked arithmetic terms will not reach it, which is what the earlier 4.04% equation reduction and 0.07 s compile change already showed.
