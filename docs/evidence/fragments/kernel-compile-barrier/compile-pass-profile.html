Where the point jet's cold compile spends its time

One H200 job (1296972, node 98dci4-gpu-0003) compiled the point jet at revision 56097c60b in a fresh process with the compilation cache disabled, dumping the module after every HLO pass. The wall splits into trace 3.25 s, StableHLO lowering 0.43 s and XLA compile 95.12 s. The pass-level chain covers 91.19 s of that compile (95.9%), leaving 0.85 s before the first dump and 3.07 s after the last, and those three parts sum to the compile wall to the millisecond. The compile ran with per-pass dumping enabled, so its wall is longer than the 82.43 s measured without dumping; the split, not the total, is what this instrument reports.

The multi-second control is the one that qualifies: a program whose compile lasts several seconds is attributed at 95.1%, so the instrument works on a program it was not tuned to. The two sub-two-second controls are the failure mode it discloses rather than hides: below a couple of seconds the dump chain does not bracket the work and the instrument reports only a few per cent, exactly the state in which a profile that attributed nothing would also look like it had run.

Recommendation. The lever is HLO size at the entry to the fusion pipeline, not an individual arithmetic term. The graph that reaches fusion is not the traced one: variadic-op-splitter takes the module from 146 to 100,579 instructions in a single pass, and the two passes that then walk that expanded graph — variadic-op-splitter at 34.23 s and multi_output_fusion at 17.08 s — account for 51.31 s of the 91.19 s attributed, which is 56.3% of the profile and about 42 s of the 82.43 s cold compile measured without dumping. Codegen stages (autotuner 8.37 s plus 7.54 s of LLVM IR stages) add roughly 16 s, and nothing else exceeds 2 s. So §2 and §3 should bound what the program hands to the variadic splitter — the number of operands carried together through the point jet's vmap and the tuple structure of the source-jets carrier — because that is what turns a small module into 100,579 instructions before fusion runs. Removing census-ranked arithmetic terms will not reach it, which is what the earlier 4.04% equation reduction and 0.07 s compile change already showed.
