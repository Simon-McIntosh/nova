Attribution supported by these rows

The height rule at nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py:3486–:3591 contains two stable tangent directions in scan(unroll=2), an unchanged value expression, and six stable-pole height samples in one mapped body. The shifts are ±1, ±0.5 and ±0.25 mm; Richardson coefficients 64, −20 and 1 are divided by 45. The current rule computes samples for all entries before jnp.where selects field channels at index 3 and above when the nearest corner height is within 0.1 µm.

The pole-product rule at nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py:1107–:1123 computes jax.jvp of the joint expression, then uses jnp.where to choose the finite slope at zero root. The stable tangent expression reaches it through _regular_pole_contraction at :1161. Ordinary expressions and samples are therefore exposed to forward-mode nesting before their selected output is assembled.

Graph attribution: ordinary-primal autodiff removes 68,487 instructions, 56.2% of the current Hessian's 121,842. Omitting the height-sampling addition removes 21,782, 17.9%. The remaining contrast between stable ordinary tangents and plain autodiff is 46,705 instructions, 38.3% of the current module. Those are ablation deltas after lowering and dead-code elimination, not additive measurements of disjoint blocks. In particular, the remaining contrast changes the stable-pole expression and custom differentiation route together; it does not isolate _pole_product_tangent alone. Conditional sampling adds 6,931 instructions, 5.7%, while its own attributed packed-op count is almost unchanged (62,927 versus 62,935). Conditional execution alone does not erase the sample branch from the compiled module.

All contrasts hold target batch size at one and source size at 24 cells. Derivative order and rule form therefore enlarge this point program independently of the wall-event batch. Higher-order raw-coupling growth is counterfactual for the direct TotalField primal read: its curvature path uses nine Hessians, while the concrete nested-path callers in the inventory differentiate analytic or cached polynomial fields. Outer differentiation of a stationary solve can still differentiate its Hessian through the implicit JVP.

Memory attribution remains bounded: plain reaches approximately 111 GiB and without-samples approximately 149 GiB at their 30-minute stops; current and masked reach approximately 200 GiB in about nine minutes. Unequal stop times and absent executables prevent a completed peak-memory ratio or a device-temp comparison. The observations establish large host compile demand even at one target, and a strong raw-graph dependence on the stable tangent route. They do not assign a percentage of the full read's 1.07 TiB device request to derivative rules versus wall batching.

Smallest measured candidate, named and not made in product: substitute ordinary autodiff of the packed primal for the height-stabilized custom route and unwrap the pole-product rule, exactly the plain probe arm. That is the largest measured raw-graph reduction among these variants: 56.2% at order 2 and 60.7% at order 4. The small accuracy cohort does not qualify a product replacement or validate singular-corner behavior, and its native compile still fails to finish within 30 minutes. A product-safe change should target the stable tangent route first, retaining an independently qualified corner limit; simply guarding the existing Richardson samples has no measured compile-graph benefit. No product or tolerance change is made here.
