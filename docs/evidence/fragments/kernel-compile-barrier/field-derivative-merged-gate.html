The delivered change: two primal field derivatives from the same contour reduction

The packed polygon moment kernel takes field_derivatives=True and appends d_r Br at rows 9:12 and d_z Br at rows 12:15, in the same uniform, radial and vertical source-moment order as the nine field rows. They are ordinary boundary-reduction values, so nothing differentiates the kernel's custom derivative rule to obtain them. The two remaining field-gradient components are reconstructed by the Maxwell relations: d_r Bz = d_z Br - mu0 J_phi and d_z Bz = -Br/r - d_r Br, with J_phi the section's own current density inside a source and zero outside it.

The tensor also stays exact at a target level with a corner: d_r Br and d_z Br are finite everywhere except at a section vertex, where the field gradient of a uniformly filled polygon diverges logarithmically.
