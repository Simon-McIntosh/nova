The instrument, checked against itself. The published point-jet profile recorded 95.12 s of compile with the splitter at 34.23 s over 100,579 post-splitter instructions, and this job's point jet records 327.22 s with the splitter at 128.22 s over 319,849. That is not instrument drift: the program's expanded graph has grown by a factor of 3.2 since that revision, and the splitter's cost has grown with it, at 40.1 s per 100,000 post-splitter instructions here against 34.0 s in the published run — an eighteen per cent difference in per-instruction cost, of the size expected where two measurements two weeks apart run on a shared node. The production program sits at 39.9 s per 100,000, so all three agree to within that margin. The absolute seconds in this job are scaled by the graph, not by the host, and the shares are what the two programs are compared on.
