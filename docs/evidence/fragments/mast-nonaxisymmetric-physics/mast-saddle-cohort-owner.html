Saddle reach benchmark — admission owned by the cohort module

Verdict: single-source admission. benchmarks/mast_saddle_voltage_reach.py no longer declares the vacuum-cohort thresholds or restates its predicate. The light admission moved into nova/imas/mast_vacuum_cohort.py as light_census() returning a LightCensus whose admitted() method applies the module's own PLASMA_FREE_CURRENT, EXCITATION_CURRENT and a single MINIMUM_PROBES = 40 constant that select_vacuum_cohort() now defaults to. The benchmark imports those constants for its receipt rather than re-stating them, and read the module's census for each candidate shot. Previously the benchmark carried its own PLASMA_FREE_CURRENT, EXCITATION_CURRENT, MINIMUM_PROBES, a private probe regex, a private drive-channel tuple and an admits() predicate: five places the cohort module could have drifted from without either noticing.

What the light census reads. Only the current-group peaks (plasma-freeness, deliberate excitation through the module's COIL_DRIVES) and the field-group channel names (the over-determining probe count, through the module's own _CHANNEL_PATTERN). It does not read field waveforms, which is the whole cost of the full survey_shot() census. Its one defensible divergence from the full survey is recorded on the dataclass: a probe whose sample shape does not join the field clock is dropped by the full survey and counted by name here, so the two counts can differ by such a channel.

Raw-key resolution. The benchmark's per-loop raw key is resolved from the store's own key set, never assumed at one width. A loop's candidates are offered at two digits and at three, and the store's key set decides: this is what makes saddle_m_10 resolve to sad_out_m010 where it would otherwise be reported absent.
