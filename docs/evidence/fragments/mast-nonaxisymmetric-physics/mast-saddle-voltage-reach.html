Uncorrected MAST saddle-loop voltage — reachability

Verdict: reachable. An uncorrected saddle-loop voltage — the raw XMB export with its axisymmetric poloidal-field pickup retained is reachable for 36 of the 36 saddle loops over the vacuum-cohort frame, through the level-1 XMB group at xmb/sad_out_<family><index>. Each loop's raw key was resolved from the store's own key set, because the m family beyond loop 9 is spelled with a three-digit index (sad_out_m010, sad_out_m011, sad_out_m012) while every other loop is two-digit (sad_out_l12): assuming one width reports saddle_m_10 to saddle_m_12 absent when the store carries them, which is the defect this pass corrects. No fit was run and no sign was promoted.

Coverage caveat. The admitted cohort is 86 shots, but only 26 of them carry an xmb group at all; 60 carry none, so over this frame an absent loop is partly a statement about acquisition era rather than about that loop. Within the 26 xmb-bearing shots every one of the 36 loops is present, at either 3 or 26 of those shots (per-loop counts in the table below). Whether a sign node can proceed rests on those 26 shots and not on the full 86.

Frame. The vacuum cohort is the no-plasma, coil-driven, over-determined set of nova.imas.mast_vacuum_cohort (plasma-free below 5e3 A, a coil driven above 1e3 A, at least 40 field probes). Its own census reads every field waveform of the store; this benchmark draws an even-strided 4000-shot sample across the whole range (shot range 13725–28575) and applies the criteria in a light census reading only current-group peaks and field-group channel names. The verdict is a statement about that frame.

Receipt. benchmarks/mast_saddle_voltage_reach.py wrote docs/figures/mast-saddle-loops/reach.json. The pass ran on all_debug (job 1282006, log saddle-reach-all_debug.log).

Raw minus published. On shot 14811 the raw sad_out_m01 record and the published XMB_SAD/OUT/M01 product were interpolated onto the published clock and differenced over 26190 overlapping samples: raw mean 0.272696 V, published mean 0.164532 V, difference mean 0.108164 V, difference RMS 0.108175 V. The difference is a standing offset, not a scaled copy of the coil pulse: the published product has had the axisymmetric pickup removed and the raw record has not, and the comparison is reported as that measured difference rather than as a fitted scale.

The loop's poloidal centre was read from the magnetics IDS arrays of the level-2 store at r=1.9989999532699585 m, z=0.014285714498588007 m over 28 vertices, the source the plan names in place of a catalog machine description. The coil-to-loop kernel the vacuum response composes needs the coil cross-sections, which the magnetics IDS does not carry, so the predicted-pickup comparison is recorded unavailable with that reason (coil cross-sections for the response kernel are not present in the magnetics IDS; the catalog machine description that carries them is unavailable to this benchmark) rather than sourced from the retired registry.

Negative control. The deliberately misspelled channel sad_out_zz99 was probed on every cohort shot and recorded unreachable on all 86 (shots_where_present: 0), so a name that resolves to no loop is reported unreachable rather than graded.
