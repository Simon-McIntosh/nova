Uncorrected MAST saddle-loop voltage — reachability

Verdict: reachable, partially. An uncorrected saddle-loop voltage — the raw XMB export with its axisymmetric poloidal-field pickup retained — is reachable for 33 of the 36 saddle loops over the vacuum cohort, through the level-1 XMB group at xmb/sad_out_<family><nn>. It is not reachable for saddle_m_10, saddle_m_11 and saddle_m_12, whose channels are absent from the store at every cohort shot. No fit was run and no sign was promoted.

Receipt. benchmarks/mast_saddle_voltage_reach.py wrote docs/figures/mast-saddle-loops/reach.json, a per-loop record of every access path tried with its signal name, source and reachability per shot range. The pass ran on all_debug (job 1281983, log saddle-reach-all_debug.log); a first identical pass (job 1281981) produced the same cohort and the same 33/36 counts.

Frame. The vacuum cohort is the no-plasma, coil-driven, over-determined set of nova.imas.mast_vacuum_cohort (plasma-free below 5e3 A, a coil driven above 1e3 A, at least 40 field probes). Its own census reads every field waveform of the 17 111-shot store; this benchmark draws an even-strided 4000-shot sample across the whole range and applies the criteria in a light census reading only current-group peaks and field-group channel names. 86 shots were admitted, spanning 13725–28575. The verdict is a statement about that frame.

What each loop reached. Every l and u loop and m_1–m_9 are present at the same 26 cohort shots (15051–25836); l_3/l_4 and their u counterparts appear at only 3 of those shots (25818–25836), so a loop's raw channel is not co-extensive across the cohort. The three m loops with no channel at all are the exception the receipt names explicitly.

Published path and the pickup comparison. The published, pickup-removed product lives in the level-2 store-like store at magnetics under b_field_tor_probe_saddle_voltage; over this cohort the level-2 store is sparse, and no cohort shot resolved the published channel under the key probed, so the raw-minus-published difference and its comparison with the predicted poloidal-field pickup (nova.imas.mast_vacuum_response) is not reported here rather than reported as a negative. Establishing it is a follow-on.

Negative control. The deliberately misspelled channel sad_out_zz99 was probed on every cohort shot and recorded unreachable on all 86 (shots_where_present: 0), so a name that resolves to no loop is reported unreachable rather than being graded — the receipt's own reachability test discriminates.

Why this matters for §5. The traversal sign a coil pulse fixes is measured from the pickup the published product removes; this node's job was only to establish whether that pickup is recoverable at all. It is, for 33 loops, through a path that keeps it. The admission, traversal-sign and n=1–6 nodes can proceed against the level-1 XMB channels, with the three m loops excluded and the level-2 published path treated as unavailable on this cohort.
