Horizontal mechanism. In the small-corner regime the root and arsinh antiderivatives cancel to produce a cubic radial primitive. Their separately differentiated contractions retain large contributions while the observable can be about 1e-10. On sample 8067 the 50-digit base tangent is 2.5396347707542727e-10; the baseline hand and JVP give 2.5396833290905474e-10 and 2.5396775221037116e-10. Both are inaccurate. This is an ill-conditioned formula selected outside its stable domain, not evidence for relaxing the test.

The repair pairs opposite angles before integrating the filament kernel: c(1/Dminus - 1/Dplus) = 4 r s c² / (Dminus Dplus (Dminus + Dplus)). Integrating this positive kernel from source radius zero to the corner evaluates the small primitive directly. The radial moment retains its exact axis constant 2 pi r |u|, including the symmetric zero derivative at u=0. The explicit scalar product, quotient and square-root rules carry geometry and expansion-centre motion. Both the primal branch and its hand tangent use this well-conditioned formula; no automatic differentiation is used in product code.

Arsinh mechanism. Opposite-angle arsinh terms cancel when the source corner is small. Their even combination is evaluated as 2 atanh(2 corner_radius / (Dminus + Dplus)) before applying the even angular weight. Its explicit quotient and atanh derivatives carry the same small quantity. This avoids recovering the small flux derivative from the pole contraction.
