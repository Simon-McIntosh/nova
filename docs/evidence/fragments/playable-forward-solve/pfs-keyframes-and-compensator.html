f-fsaudit-repair-axis-configuration-poloidal-panel

Defect reproduced before repair. At the base revision the left panel of axis-configuration.png carried full spines, ticks and gridlines and drew no first wall, against the repository rule that a poloidal panel carries no axes and shows the vessel; its arm labels were addressed at their own coordinates and collided with the markers. The right panel's P and W bars read 0.02 cm against L/S/R at -4.11 cm and were unlabelled, so a zero read as a missing series.

Repair. The arm scatter is now a poloidal panel drawn through nova.media.ink.poloidal_axes and nova.media.poloidal.draw_wall, with the EFIT axis marked through draw_nulls and every arm labelled directly; the bars carry a printed value with the sub-resolution arms (P, W = +0.02 cm) labelled in their own tone. The machine wall was not in the receipt, so it is now carried in axis-configuration-receipt.json as machine_wall_rz_m with machine_wall_source naming its committed origin (the MAST machine-level outline in the playable early-frame-placement receipt; the wall is shot-independent). The receipt values are unchanged: arms L/S/R at -4.11 cm, P/W at +0.02 cm, EFIT axis at (0.90593, 0.00048) m.

f-fsaudit-repair-empty-and-invalid-data-panels (remainder: compensator)

Defect reproduced before repair. compensator-authority.png drew a single straight segment from row 17 to row 95, bridging seventy-seven rows that were never measured, and plotted the selected and best drivable series coincident on every row while the landed record claimed the solenoid is selected on nine of ten.

Repair. The line is broken between the two measured groups, which the receipt names as measured_early_rows = [15, 16, 17] and flat_top_rows = [95, 96, 97]; no line crosses rows 18 to 94. Because the selection rule chooses the strongest drivable circuit on every measured row, selection_matches_best_drivable is true and selected_circuit, best_drivable_circuit and best_all_circuit are identical on all ten receipt rows, so the figure draws one series rather than two coincident ones; the row-96 point is annotated with its p6_upper selection. Across the ten measured rows the selected circuit is the solenoid on nine, p6_upper on row 96, which reconciles the landed record's nine-of-ten claim with the receipt; both the record's lead sentence and its figure caption now state that denominator explicitly. No circuit reaches the 1e-5 m/A target.
