Reversed-current-guard diagnosis panels carry a receipt and name the state they show

The three panels under docs/figures/playable-forward-solve/reversed-current-guard/ are regenerated by benchmarks/reversed_current_guard_diagnosis.py from one committed receipt, diagnosis.json, written beside them. Every number a panel title states re-derives from the receipt's own JSON: the converged flag, the terminal residual, the termination reason and the requested-class admission verdict. Before this the directory held three PNGs and zero JSON, so no number in any of them could be checked. The census panels also plotted the zero-filled census padding as thirty O-point candidates at R = 0.000 m, with no admission verdict and no first wall; the census read now masks by the census's own retained-valid flag, and the panel draws the first wall and the admitted candidates through the committed nova.media painters (poloidal.draw_wall, poloidal.draw_nulls). The empty achieved vs EFIT target z middle panel is gone.

What each panel shows

Row 30 converges to a zero relative residual and the census panel is titled O-point census on the converged (terminal residual 0) — class DIVERTED refused (NoQualifiedAxisError). Rows 50 and 55 never reach the 1e-8 criterion, and their panels say so: row 50 reads O-point census on the unconverged (terminal residual unreported, ACTIVE_SET_SETTLED, 11 trips) and row 55 reads O-point census on the unconverged (terminal residual 1.380e-01, ACTIVE_SET_ITERATION_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED, 16 trips). All three refuse the requested class at admission, and none of them reads converged unless it is.

Measure

Head: tests/test_reversed_current_guard_panels.py passed 5 of 5 in a fresh process on one compute node, JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu. The test re-derives each panel title from the receipt and asserts no census candidate sits at the machine origin. Negative control: run against the committed pre-repair panels with their receipt absent, 5 of 5 fail (exit 1); run against the committed pre-repair panels beside a receipt built from the pre-repair payloads, the census-padding assertion and the title re-derivation both fail (2 failed, 3 passed, exit 1). The driver reuses the shared compilation cache and writes each row's receipt as it lands, so a fence loss keeps the rows already solved.
