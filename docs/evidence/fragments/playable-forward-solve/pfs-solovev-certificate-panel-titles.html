Three Solovev certificate chord panels title their axis-admission read, not an accuracy claim

Repairs f-fsaudit-repair-solovev-certificate-qualified-title. Each of the three 1000-cell chord panels was titled bare {case} · cells-1000 · qualified: the word was the axis-admission verdict (banked_read.read_status = qualified_axis), while the receipt's own analytic_flux_regions.all_carrier_cells gives a relative RMS flux error near 0.12. The panels were re-rendered from their persisted part receipts through the committed benchmarks.solovev_certificate._plot painter — no forward operator is built and no solve is entered — with a two-line title that names the read status, prints the relative flux error, and carries the terminal residual and converged flag, every number read from solve-parts/chord/<case>-production-route-cells-1000.json. The title is recorded back into that receipt as figure.panel_title.

Panels

Accuracy, not admission

The qualified_axis verdict admits the axis; it says nothing about flux accuracy. The relative RMS on these three arms is 0.1176, 0.1155 and 0.1178, roughly two orders of magnitude above the accuracy of the diverted control row in the same directory (diverted-single-null-production-route-cells-500, relative_rms=4.60015e-15), which is why the title now states both facts side by side. The diverted row was not re-rendered: its title was never a false accuracy claim.

Check and negative control

tests/test_solovev_certificate_panel_titles.py re-derives each title from the part receipt keys and asserts the receipt's recorded title matches, that the title is not the bare qualification, that it names the read status and the relative error, and that the receipt digest matches the panel on disk. Against the committed pre-repair panels the same test fails on the bare qualified title (9 failed, exit 1).
