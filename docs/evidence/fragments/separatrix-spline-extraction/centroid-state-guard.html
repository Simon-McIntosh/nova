Committed MAST centroid panel states are renderable

The repository test opens all three committed panel-fields.npz states and redraws each panel pair. Its negative control used the CPU state before regeneration: the test failed on the missing coil_outlines and five branch_* keys (one failing test, exit 1). The regenerated CPU, full-axis and titan states passed all four checks at the landed renderer revision.

The CPU receipt again states why the original lane ran on CPU: reservation gpu_0003_grpA admitted no job while its node had idle cores, memory and cards. The panel schema regeneration stayed on CPU for continuity with that lane.

A not-a-knot bicubic fit of each committed vacuum field was checked at the grid marker and polished by Newton using analytic first and second derivatives. Stationarity requires the polished gradient to be at most 10^-6 of the median in-wall grid gradient, a negative Hessian determinant, and an in-wall root. A manufactured quadratic saddle gave gradient below 3e-17 Wb/m and determinant -4 Wb²/m⁴, confirming that the probe detects a known saddle.
