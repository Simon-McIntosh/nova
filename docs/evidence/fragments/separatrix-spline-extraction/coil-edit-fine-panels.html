Coil-edit panels drawn from a fine display map

The coil-edit panel drew its contours from the lattice raster alone, so a 20 mm lattice showed as a 20 mm staircase and no reading finer than the solve grid was available. The panel now draws the flux a stored state's own currents produce, evaluated at 10 mm. The coupling is built in the benchmark from the machine the state was solved on: the external term is the stored circuit fields contracted with the panel's prescribed currents, and the plasma term is the uniform, radial and vertical moment triple over the operator's own cells, taken from the operator's normalised-current route at the solve's target current. That route is asked for its moment triple directly, because the builder's own normalised branch traces a clipped support that an operator carrying point moments and no moment geometry cannot provide; the render receipts name the observation entry point of the same route. nova/equilibrium/display_flux.py is unchanged.

The map is pinned against the operator that produced the state. A re-solved state's panel map is compared with operator.raster_image at the lattice nodes and must reproduce it to 1e-10 relative, and it asserts the drawn map's own reproduction_relative_error rather than a value it recomputed. On the real MAST states the two lanes record 6.78e-12 and 7.20e-12, so the coupling frames agree with the operator's to eleven digits rather than merely passing the floor.

Each of the four figure sets was re-solved on the lane its receipt names, twenty 2% warm edits from a cold solve, one job per set. Every job reached edit 20 with the compilation cache warm after the first edit, and every job's terminal marker is EXIT_STATUS=2, which is the benchmark's own non-converged verdict rather than a crash. The terminal pair for each set is edit 12 (fraction +0.06, residual 5.73e-09, converged) and edit 9 (fraction 0.00, residual 6.61e-03, active set settled).

The converged set moved between the revision the sweep was first run at and the one the panels are drawn at. Twenty 2% edits from a cold solve converged on edits {0, 2, 13, 17} at 15b3aa2f8 and on {0, 1, 11, 12, 13} at 6fef9cc33, so the panels draw edit 12 and edit 9 where the earlier record drew edit 2 and edit 7. Three of them change the convergence decision in reduced_newton.py: a359f8fed serves the reduced residual and tangent requests from one owner, and 2acda367b and 624f8727d refuse a non-finite cell moment at the solve host loops and at the last trip. None of the three can raise a converged count, since two of them only add refusals and the third is a refactor, so the shift is in the iterates the residual is measured on rather than in the criterion. Naming the single commit that carried an edit across the tolerance would need the sweep re-run at intermediate revisions; this node did not run it, and the record says so rather than attributing the shift to whichever subject line reads closest.

The panel draws the operator's own normalised current moments of the stored state at the solve's target current, so it images the route the fixed point is terminated on rather than the unnormalised moments the archive carries. Measured against the state it draws, that route is further away, not closer, while the map still reproduces the operator raster to 6.77e-12 (converged) and 7.18e-12 (non-converged): the largest node mismatch rose from 2.405e-03 to 4.585e-03 Wb on the converged panel and from 3.438e-02 to 5.577e-02 Wb on the non-converged one. The term that separates them is the normalisation factor applied in the opposite direction. The operator derives an amplitude of 0.99841 (target / sum(cell_current)) and scales the moment triple by it; the stored state instead stands above the unnormalised image by the reciprocal, 1/0.99841 = 1.00159, which is the 1.587e-03 fitted plasma scale of the 1.039 Wb plasma peak. Multiplying the moments by 0.99841 therefore moves the drawn field away from the state by exactly that amount, which is why the converged panel's mismatch does not fall to the solver residual but grows: the stored state is not a fixed point of the normalisation this route applies, and no value of that amplitude closes it. The other candidates were ruled out at the lattice nodes: the operator's thirteen-conductor external current is all zero, so an omitted conductor contributes 0.0 Wb; and the difference is not a gauge constant, since removing its mean (4.54e-04 Wb of a 2.41e-03 Wb maximum) or a fitted plane leaves 1.95e-03 Wb. Redrawing stopped here: the panels are drawn on the normalised route as directed, the before and after mismatch are both recorded, and the state's own relation to the normalisation is the term a further redraw cannot settle.

Each figure carries a panel-receipt.json beside it. Every field in it is read from an artefact on disk: the solve's job id, partition, source revision and exact command from the lane log; the render's job, partition, device, host and command from the render receipt; the map spacing and both reproduction errors; and the digest of the state archive the render read. The two titan solves were cancelled at their time limit after writing their states and before drawing their panels, which the receipt records rather than leaving a missing exit status to be read as a clean one; the panels were then drawn from those states by the render-only job.

Each regenerated panel-states.npz carries the drawn states' conductor currents, cell currents and both linear moment columns, so a panel can be redrawn from the archive alone. The three figures fed by the H200 archive are byte-identical, which is expected: they are the same sweep read by three documents.
