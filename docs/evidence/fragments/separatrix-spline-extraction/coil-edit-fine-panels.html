Coil-edit panels drawn from a fine display map

The coil-edit panel drew its contours from the lattice raster alone, so a 20 mm lattice showed as a 20 mm staircase and no reading finer than the solve grid was available. The panel now draws the flux a stored state's own currents produce, evaluated at 10 mm. The coupling is built in the benchmark from the machine the state was solved on: the external term is the stored circuit fields contracted with the panel's prescribed currents, and the plasma term is the uniform, radial and vertical moment triple over the operator's own cells. nova/equilibrium/display_flux.py is unchanged.

The map is pinned against the operator that produced the state. A re-solved state's panel map is compared with operator.raster_image at the lattice nodes and must reproduce it to 1e-10 relative, and it asserts the drawn map's own reproduction_relative_error rather than a value it recomputed. On the real MAST states the two lanes record 6.78e-12 and 7.20e-12, so the coupling frames agree with the operator's to eleven digits rather than merely passing the floor.

Each of the four figure sets was re-solved on the lane its receipt names, twenty 2% warm edits from a cold solve, one job per set. Every job reached edit 20 with the compilation cache warm after the first edit, and every job's terminal marker is EXIT_STATUS=2, which is the benchmark's own non-converged verdict rather than a crash. The terminal pair for each set is edit 12 (fraction +0.06, residual 5.73e-09, converged) and edit 9 (fraction 0.00, residual 6.61e-03, active set settled).

Each figure carries a panel-receipt.json beside it. Every field in it is read from an artefact on disk: the solve's job id, partition, source revision and exact command from the lane log; the render's job, partition, device, host and command from the render receipt; the map spacing and both reproduction errors; and the digest of the state archive the render read. The two titan solves were cancelled at their time limit after writing their states and before drawing their panels, which the receipt records rather than leaving a missing exit status to be read as a clean one; the panels were then drawn from those states by the render-only job.

Each regenerated panel-states.npz carries the drawn states' conductor currents, cell currents and both linear moment columns, so a panel can be redrawn from the archive alone. The three figures fed by the H200 archive are byte-identical, which is expected: they are the same sweep read by three documents. The two titan figures share a second archive whose states differ from the H200 set in the last bits only.

The persisted-state terms are re-measured against the regenerated archives. Fifteen of the twenty states now close a separatrix where eleven did: the four that changed close with two open branches each, state 19 among them, and the four that remain open trace a cycle admitting no closed candidate while state 1 admits no cycle at all. The connectivity test passes 42 of 42 at the regenerated archive, and with the panel render test beside it the pair passes 43 of 43 at the landed revision, in one CPU process on the fleet allocation.

A one-percent rise of one stored conductor current moves the drawn map by 2.90e-2 Wb, 0.79% of its 3.67 Wb peak, so the contours follow the state rather than a cached current vector. The first form of that control read exactly zero, and it was the control that was wrong: it perturbed the panel's current but reused a coupling that had already contracted the unperturbed one. The coupling is now built per current, as the machine path builds it.

The cutover figure is titan-converged-vs-nonconverged, the name its landing record and the archive citing it use; the render wrote its own file name, so the pair was moved onto that name and the stray one dropped rather than left as a second figure. The caption is wrapped to the canvas. A first render set it as one line, which ran past both edges of every figure it was drawn on; the caption is now filled to 150 characters and every redrawn figure is rendered to PNG and inspected for clipped and overlapping text before it is committed.
