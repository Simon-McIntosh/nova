The display map is judged on the nodes the solve claims

A reduced solve's closing trip keeps the trial state on the nodes it promotes instead of the map's image (mapped = jnp.where(promoted, state, image)), so those nodes carry a piece of the iterate that no field of currents reproduces. Judging a display map against the terminal state over the whole lattice therefore charges the display for a gap no display can close: on the converged coil-edit state the promoted paste is the largest part of the measured 2.4 mWb gap, and no field of currents continues it. build_display_flux_map now takes the solve's own promoted mask and reports the node mismatch over the nodes the solve does not promote, beside the unmasked figure it has always reported. The mask is read from ForwardEquilibrium.promoted_mask; nothing recomputes it.

The field the builder evaluates is the solve's own: its normalised moment triple through ForwardFluxOperator.normalised_current_moments_and_observation, its conductor currents, and the compensating conductor current a constrained route folds into its prescribed vector before imaging. That compensation enters through compensation as a conductor-current flux and is added to the same sum the external conductor flux is carried in, so DisplayCoupling stays the pair it was — no third callable, no second coupling.
