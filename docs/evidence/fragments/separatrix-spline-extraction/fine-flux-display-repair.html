Fine-grid display flux map — repair

The display flux map now reads its saddles and its separatrix off its own field with the same machinery the solve uses, and evaluates its plasma coupling through the operator's own FluxTarget. The finite-difference saddle search is gone: both the fine map's saddle and the lattice spline's saddle are polished with polish_stationary_points on a tensor spline fit of the field, and the map's separatrix branches are assembled with assemble_separatrix_branches at the level boundary_flux_at_admitted_saddle reads from the map's own saddle cell, so a panel draws the separatrix and the contours from one field.

Negative control

Swapping the radial and vertical moment columns in the display build's FluxTarget makes the reproduction test fail: the map then misses raster_image by a relative 2.53e-2 against a 1e-10 gate. The log is negative-control.log in the run directory; its last line is EXIT=1.

The moment columns are exercised

The fixture carries non-zero radial and vertical moment columns that differ from each other, and a test asserts that zeroing them moves the flux by more than 1e-2 relative, so a dropped or swapped column cannot pass unnoticed.

What was not produced

The convergence arm is not met as stated. Solving the manufactured operator to convergence needs a free-boundary fixed-point solve, and the manufactured machine's iteration leaves its axis unqualified: profile.solve raises NoQualifiedAxisError on the first iterate after the seed, for both the picard and anderson routes. The delivered test measures the same reporting contract without a solve, by declaring the increment that separates the state from the operator's image and asserting the reported mismatch equals it. A solved-state version belongs on a real machine, which is the panel sections' producer path.
