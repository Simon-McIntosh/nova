§8 — Boundary separation at the display-map head

The five-frame DIII-D forward bank was run to completion at committed head 0205c75abae050d29681fdd69808045fa20d0e05 on one H200 card, as SLURM job 1339248 submitted by the coordinator outside the worker fence. It published forward_gs_receipt.json, frame_flux_comparison.png, cohort_match_summary.png and displacement_decomposition.png into docs/figures/diiid-forward-onboarding/forward-gs-2026-11.

The cohort does not pass. One of the five frames met the registered convergence criterion and four did not, so the gate verdict is FAIL (result.passed: false, converged_frames: 1). The score bar itself is met — the registered median interior r² bar is 0.90155 and the cohort's median is 0.99844 — but convergence is a separate conjunct of the same criterion and four frames failed, so no frame is reported as a pass. Every frame nevertheless produced a finite, diverted field except frame 144, which refused before any geometry, and four of the five assembled a separatrix and measured its distance to the labelled EFIT boundary.

Verdict

Of five declared frames at this head, one converged and four did not; the gate is FAIL and every frame's verdict is stated as FAIL. Four frames nonetheless carry a non-null fine-map boundary separation, three of them within 9 mm rms of the labelled boundary, and the assembler refused no frame for want of branch capacity. The remaining obstacle to a passing cohort is convergence, not the display-map boundary measurement, and frame 137's flat residual history is where a solver-side follow-up should look first.
