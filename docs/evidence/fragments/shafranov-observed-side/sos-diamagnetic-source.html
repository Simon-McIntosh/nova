The magnetics reader publishes a verified sign and provenance; the MAST store carries no calibrated diamagnetic flux

landing: a diamagnetic loop can now be read and stored with its source, weber units, COCOS convention and calibration on the row, and a row whose excursion does not follow the toroidal field is refused. The measurement the toroidal volume term needs on MAST is not in that store, so no MAST row is written and no solved-state value is substituted.

Reader. nova/imas/io_magnetics.py reads magnetics.diamagnetic_flux(:).data/.time at whichever dictionary the entry was written in, and falls back to the flux_* loop types labelled diamagnetic_internal, diamagnetic_external, diamagnetic_compensation and diamagnetic_differential when an entry publishes the loop only through flux_loop. Each row carries its archive path, its units, the COCOS convention of the entry it was read at, and the calibration the reader applied, and diamagnetic_flux_dataset writes those to the store row. Every stored channel is checked against the toroidal field first: a b0_like quantity reverses with that field, so its largest excursion must carry the field's own sense.

With the guard switched off the same test fails, and the red log is what this node's negative control records; with it in place the test passes. A row with no excursion (every sample zero), no field, a field that changes sign, or no finite sample states no b0_like sense at all and is refused too.

The census reads the store at /work/projects/imas_gpu/mast/level1/shots read-only and records group names, array names, sample counts, finite counts and extents. Its first pass asked only whether an array name contained diamag, found the empty EFIT channel, and would have reported that the store carries no diamagnetic diagnostic at all. The group-level scan that followed found the live xma channels: an absence claim needs a scan whose positive control can see the thing being searched for, and the same pass must count the flux loops. The channel exists and varies; what it does not carry is a calibration to weber, and the archive's own equilibrium never adopted it.

Why the term is still not sourced on MAST. The row this node exists to write must state its units, its convention and its calibration, and then reproduce the archived constraint within its stated error. The store publishes no calibration for the raw loop signal, and the archived constraint value is zero wherever it is present, so there is nothing to reproduce and no honest factor to apply. Reading the raw signal and calling it weber would be a silent substitution of exactly the kind the decision forbids, and integrating an uncalibrated derivative behind a ±10 rail would invent the very quantity at issue.
