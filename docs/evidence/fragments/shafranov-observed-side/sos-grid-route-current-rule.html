Why the order is two, and why it plateaus

The contour quadrature is the periodic trapezoidal rule over the vertices with a fourth-order tangent. The separatrix sampling places a vertex exactly on the X-point corner, where the central tangent stencil is off by order one, so the vertical moment and the toroidal stress decay at second order in the contour sampling. The shared-node stencil is third order, and its gap to the analytic route (grid ladder above) is at most 2.84e-06 against a plateau of 2.230e-01, six orders below it, so the fitted order is the quadrature's.

The route settles onto a plateau with a 22.3 percent offset against the exact 1.23255 instead of converging to it. The cause is the combination's own cancellation: twice the vertical moment is 5.88 against a toroidal volume of 5.87, so the volume side's 2e-4 identity residual in the vertical moment is amplified by 2μ0|Sv|/|W| ≈ 7000 and holds βp + li/2 at 0.223. Refining the contour sampling converges the contour side to the plateau rather than to exact, which is why the fitted order is the order of departure from the plateau.
