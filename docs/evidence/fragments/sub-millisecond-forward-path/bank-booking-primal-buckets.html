Primal booking in separate geometry populations

The requested structure preserves precision, but does not make each booking cheaper. At one slice the paired fresh processes take 96.369–96.669 ms at base and 100.004–100.341 ms at head, with 4 / 4 reads. At sixteen slices, base takes 603.760–604.689 ms with 7 reads and 6 batch updates; head takes 461.597–462.133 ms with 6 reads and 4 updates. Wall per read falls from 86.251–86.384 to 76.933–77.022 ms. Residual device work per call rises from 2.865–2.866 to 3.934–3.941 ms at one slice and from 9.124–9.140 to 9.807–9.817 ms at sixteen. Launches per call also rise. The lower sixteen-slice total includes a shorter trajectory and must not be presented as a booking speedup.

Primal and tangent booking share one cell-bucket loop and one live-ring loop. Conic and normal-form cells enter separate populations, and live rings integrate in groups of four. The sequential primal reference stays in the tests.
