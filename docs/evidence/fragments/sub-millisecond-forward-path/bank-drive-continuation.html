## What changed `nova/equilibrium/drive_continuation.py` advances a drive change in adaptive warm-started sub-steps above `support_read.solve_batch`. `solve_batch` runs a 550-cell diverted slice warm-started from its neighbour in two updates and three reads at a 1e-5 relative profile-amplitude step, and in four updates and six reads at 1e-3. At 1e-2 it stalls at the sixty-update ceiling, warm or cold. The stall is not a warm-start defect and not a wrong step direction: the assembled Jacobian agrees with central differences of the fresh residual to about 1e-8 at the stalled state. The linear model is simply invalid that far from the seed — the unit trial's predicted residual is 1.5e-13 against a measured 20.40, or 0.94 of its starting value. The remedy is drive continuation. A drive change is split into sub-steps, each a warm-started `solve_batch` from the previous sub-step's converged state. Nothing inside the solve changes: the Newton step, the fresh-residual line search and the terminal certification are the product's, called with the same arguments; only the drive each call is asked for is smaller. ### The sub-step rule - **Initial size: 1e-3**: the largest relative step the warm-start record shows every lane converging, at three to six updates against a sixty-update ceiling. - **A sub-solve inside a six-update budget doubles the sub-step** and the drive advances. - **A sub-solve that leaves a lane unconverged, or the batch above that budget, is rejected**: the drive does not advance, the sub-step halves, and the same drive is retried. - **A floor of 1e-9 relative** ends the attempt: the driver reports the drive it reached and why, rather than halving forever. - **Intermediate sub-solves stop at 1e-6.** That only has to leave the next sub-step a usable seed, which the record shows a residual two orders above the certification budget already is. - **The sub-step that reaches the target is solved at 1e-10**, and the state is certified by that solve's own terminal fresh read against the locked residual clause. A certificate is reported only when every lane's fresh residual is at or under 1e-10 at the target drive. ## CPU evidence `tests/test_bank_drive_continuation.py` drives the seam with a scalar residual whose Newton basin is limited — `r(x, d) = t + C t³`, `t = x − d`, with an exact step direction and the product's own `_damped_scale` line search — so the fixture has the property the H200 record shows without any code deciding when a solve should fail.
