Factor carry is opt-in after a measured negative result

The secant driver carries the last accepted Newton factor only when the caller explicitly supplies solve_options={"reuse_factor": True}. The default uses fresh Newton factors. The first carried correction is provisional: its fresh residual must contract by a finite ratio at most the contraction limit, otherwise the factor is rebuilt at the incumbent. Only the terminal fresh read at residual at most 1e-10 certifies a slice. Rejected continuation attempts do not replace the accepted factor.

Both arms use the first eight saved lanes, drive steps 1e-5 and 1e-3 and seven slices at each step, with one warmup and one measured process per arm. The bank and seed hashes match across arms. Main uses secant seeds with fresh factors; head enables guarded factor reuse within solves and carries factors across slices. Assemblies fall by 86.2%, accompanied by more fresh reads and accepted updates. All 224 lane/slice results certify, at maximum residual 8.024e-11.

Factor carry is a measured negative result for the total work. Using the H200 kernel costs as an estimate, main costs 43 × 35.7 + 29 × 44.9 = 2837.2 ms (about 2.84 s) of batch time. The carried arm costs 107 × 35.7 + 4 × 25.5 + 93 × 19.4 = 5726.1 ms (about 5.73 s), where 19.4 = 44.9 − 25.5 ms is the update cost without assembly. Estimated H200 batch time therefore rises by a factor of 2.02, roughly doubling despite the assembly reduction. These are estimates from counts and measured kernel costs, not H200 timing measurements.

A carried factor can retain linear convergence at the allowed 0.5 contraction while fresh Newton steps converge quadratically. The extra reads and corrections outweigh the skipped assemblies in this measurement, with reads dominating the estimated carried cost. Carry remains available as an explicit opt-in; it is disabled by default. The terminal fresh read and the precision budgets are unchanged.
