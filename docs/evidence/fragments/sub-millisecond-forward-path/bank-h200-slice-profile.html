Ranked changes and a conditional budget to reach 1 ms

Width 64 needs a further 4.782 ms/slice reduction. The following are measurement-led engineering targets, not observed speedups. Their optimistic expected-saving budget totals 4.79 ms/slice, leaving about 0.99 ms/slice; achieving that requires the large reductions stated below, and none is established by this profile alone. No saving is credited by removing a qualification check or freezing continuous geometry.

  1. Share and fuse primal/tangent quadrature for booking and assembly. current.book, book_tangent_channels and step_jacobian together cost 181.186 ms/batch, or 2.831 ms/slice at width 64. Reuse booked primal quantities at the unchanged iterate, retain the runtime bank operands, and fuse the populated ring/strip reductions and tangent channels. Expected saving target: 2.40 ms/slice (about 85%). Verify with the same fresh-process width-64 profile, an exclusive booking/assembly budget at most 0.431 ms/slice, unchanged fresh residuals, and tangent agreement against the existing fresh-read derivative checks.
  2. Reuse current-state geometry between trial testing and the full certificate, and compact local fragment work. Full plus carried geometry costs 124.917 ms/batch, or 1.952 ms/slice. Share coefficients, curvature, fragment areas and edge intervals already evaluated at the exact same trial state, while still running full connected qualification; batch only the live strip/fragment work. Expected saving target: 1.70 ms/slice (about 87%). Verify full-versus-carried moved-state equality and rebuild refusals, unchanged terminal certificates, executed strip-kernel counts and an exclusive geometry budget at most 0.252 ms/slice.
  3. Reduce Krylov reductions and avoid dense matrix traffic. Block GMRES, balancing and update scaling cost 31.243 ms/batch, or 0.488 ms/slice. Apply the analytic Jacobian in a compact batched form with a carried preconditioner only where measured contraction certifies it; do not assume that factor carry wins when it adds reads. Expected saving target: 0.35 ms/slice. Verify a budget at most 0.138 ms/slice, condition-estimate × Krylov-residual at most 1e-8, terminal fresh residual at most 1e-10, and no extra nonlinear reads that erase the saving.
  4. Fuse stationary tracking and keep the stream’s small control state on device. Tracking costs 17.084 ms/batch (0.267 ms/slice), other/fused kernels 6.655 ms (0.104 ms/slice), and measured copies/fills 1.357 ms (0.021 ms/slice). The native wall exceeds total traced device activity by only about 0.12 ms/slice; the 0.383 ms/slice profiled gap is inflated by profiling and cannot all be credited as a native saving. Expected saving target: 0.34 ms/slice across this combined surface, using fixed-shape scalar operands, fused stationary iterations and one device-resident stream graph. Verify with unprofiled end-to-end walls, transfer-byte counts, stationary-point precision and complete kernel accounting. Finally remeasure all four changes together; summed isolated targets do not prove the combined 1 ms gate.
