Sharing the cut-cell conic ring geometry across booking channels is neutral, and was reverted

This is a negative result. The dense assembly's dominant stage is the local booking sensitivities, and inside it the cut-cell conic tangent rebuilt its ring geometry once per direction inside the channel vmap — fragment breaks, positive vertical intervals, the vertical polygon interval and the edge table, none of which depends on the direction. Assembling that geometry once per cell instead is algebraically exact, and it was measured on the H200 at three widths. It buys no measurable time: base and head agree within 0.3% of wall per slice at every width. The product change and the tests added for it have been reverted; what this record keeps is the measurement and the instrument defect that stopped the stage split.

Which stage was targeted, and what it scales with

The existing split of the production Jacobian (bank-step-jacobian-split) puts _local_booking_sensitivities at 13.343 ms of an 18.617 ms whole call at one slice and 215.842 ms of 291.947 ms at sixteen, about 74% and the worst scaling at 16.18×. Its cost is set by cut cells: it is per-cell ring integration with the geometry held once and every channel contracted through one einsum, so it grows with the cell population and not with the channel count. That record is why the conic tangent was the place to try.

What was tried, and that it is no longer in the tree

The change split current._conic_ring_tangents into _conic_ring_geometry, which assembled the direction-independent ring geometry once, and _conic_ring_velocity, which evaluated the implicit-root velocity for one direction from it, with the original function kept as their composition. A keyword-only shared_ring_geometry selected between the shared form and the per-direction rebuild, threaded through _local_booking_sensitivities and step_jacobian. Because the timing below says it buys nothing, the product files and the added tests were returned to their pre-change state in one revert commit: git diff <base> HEAD -- nova tests is empty. The prototype runners and this record stay, so a next attempt starts from the measurement rather than from the idea.

Read this as a null result, and read it carefully. The reduction removes recomputed geometry from one stage of the assembly, and the total wall does not respond at any of the three widths: base and head agree to within 0.3%, which is inside the process-to-process spread of a single arm. Two explanations are consistent with the numbers, and the stage split that would separate them did not run. Either the stage's share of the solve is smaller at these widths than the committed 16-slice record suggested, or the geometry it recomputes is cheap beside the ring integration that dominates the same stage — in which case hoisting it is algebraically free but not where the time goes. What the table does establish is that the change cost nothing while it was in the tree: no width regressed, peak memory is identical, the read and update counts are unchanged, and the precision lanes are at least as good in the head arm.

The 256-slice width was not measured; at 128 slices peak memory reads 86.5 GiB of the card's 141.

Why the stage split was not produced, and the anchor that must be fixed

The single H200 allocation (betelgeuse, reservation gpu_0003_grpA, one card, four cores, 96 GB) started within a minute of submission. Its first stage was the fresh split of one batched step_jacobian at 16 and 128 slices with the bank_solve_trace_four_read instrument, and it exited on AssertionError at bank_step_jacobian_split/measure.py:34 before timing anything.

The cause is an instrument defect, and it is exact. parts() extracts contiguous regions of step_jacobian by locating their source text, and two of its regions end at the literal " matrix = matrix - response" — the matrix region and the closing region both use it. That line no longer exists: the production code now reads

        matrix = jnp.where(
            count <= _FRESH_GEOMETRY_NODE_CAPACITY, matrix - response, jnp.nan
        )

so region()'s single-occurrence assertion fails and the split measure aborts. The fix belongs in the extractor: anchor that region on the matrix = jnp.where( line and treat the response subtraction as its interior, or terminate the region on the dedented return matrix that follows the block. Until then the stage ranking in this record is the committed split's, not a fresh measurement.

The 256-slice width was cut from that allocation and is not reported here. The four 550-cell parity cases were not cut: they ran in a separate allocation, job 1307768, and their outcome is in the parity table above.
