Cut-cell conic ring geometry is shared across every booking channel

The dense assembly's dominant stage is the local booking sensitivities. Inside it, the cut-cell conic tangent path rebuilt its ring geometry once per channel direction inside the channel vmap: fragment breaks, positive vertical intervals, the vertical polygon interval and the edge table were recomputed for each of the eleven booking channels, although none of them depends on the direction. The geometry is now assembled once per cut cell and the direction-dependent implicit-root velocity is evaluated from it. The change is algebraically identical; the assembled matrix matches the per-direction rebuild at the peak-scaled tolerance below. On the paired H200 timings it is a null result: the hoist costs nothing at 16, 64 or 128 slices and does not measurably reduce the total solve either, so the stage it targets is not where the wall clock is at those widths.

Which stage dominates, and what it scales with

The existing split of the production Jacobian (bank-step-jacobian-split) puts _local_booking_sensitivities at 13.343 ms of an 18.617 ms whole call at one slice and 215.842 ms of 291.947 ms at sixteen, about 74% and the worst scaling at 16.18×. Its cost is set by cut cells: it is per-cell ring integration with the geometry held once and every channel contracted through one einsum, so it grows with the cell population and not with the channel count. The bank-reused-scale-krylov record settles why the dense assembly stays: one matrix-free product costs three times one assembly.

The fresh stage split was not produced, and the reason is a stale source anchor rather than the allocation. The single H200 allocation (betelgeuse, reservation gpu_0003_grpA, one card, four cores, 96 GB) started within a minute of submission and ran the paired timings below. Its first stage, the split, exited on AssertionError at bank_step_jacobian_split/measure.py:34: parts() extracts contiguous regions of step_jacobian by anchoring on their exact source text, and its matrix region ends at " matrix = matrix - response", which no longer exists — the production line is now the jnp.where over the fresh-geometry node capacity. That prototype directory is outside this node's write grant, so the anchors were not repaired here and the split at 16 and 128 slices with bank_solve_trace_four_read was not re-measured. The stage ranking above is therefore the committed record's.

The change

Two functions replace the body of current._conic_ring_tangents: _conic_ring_geometry assembles the direction-independent ring geometry, and _conic_ring_velocity evaluates the velocity for one direction from it. _conic_ring_tangents remains as the composition of the two, so the existing callers and the scalar-reference test are unchanged. book_tangent_channels takes a keyword-only shared_ring_geometry; the default shares the geometry, and passing it false restores the per-direction rebuild as the reference. The keyword is threaded through _local_booking_sensitivities and step_jacobian.

Paired timing, base against head

The pairing follows the paired-timing rule: each arm has its own compilation cache, each cache is warmed once, and three fresh processes per arm and width then measure from it with XLA_FLAGS unset. Both arms load the same common seed arrays, built once at the base revision and copied into the head arm's directory. Every process ran the same 4 bank reads and 3 Newton steps, so the arms are compared at equal reads per slice; the box below gives the median of the three processes.

Read this as a null result, and read it carefully. The reduction removes recomputed geometry from one stage of the assembly, and the total wall does not respond at any of the three widths: base and head agree to within 0.3%, which is inside the process-to-process spread of a single arm. Two explanations are consistent with the numbers and the split that would separate them did not run. Either the stage's share of the solve is smaller at these widths than the committed 16-slice record suggested, or the geometry it recomputes is cheap beside the ring integration that dominates the same stage — in which case hoisting it is algebraically free but not where the time goes. What the table does establish is that the change costs nothing: no width regresses, peak memory is identical to the resolution shown, the read and update counts are unchanged, and the residual and precision lanes are at least as good in the head arm.

Every lane sits inside the locked precision budget at every width and in every process: fresh residual at most 8.97e-12 against a 1e-10 bound, and the three geometry ratios at most 2.59e-5 against 1e-8 for flux and 5e-4 for the axis and the saddle. The 256-slice width was not measured — the allocation carried three widths and 256 was not among them, so whether it now fits is unanswered here; at 128 slices peak memory reads 86.5 GiB of the card's 141.

Paired suites

Five test files ran base against head on sirius, one fresh process per file with its own basetemp. The base arm fails tests/test_bank_step_booking.py::test_book_primal_keeps_exact_diverted_moments at the base revision, and the head arm fails exactly that one and no other: test_bank_step_booking.py is 1 failed 4 passed at both arms, while test_bank_step_booking_batch.py, test_book_primal_buckets.py, test_bank_newton_step.py and test_bank_solve_driver.py exit 0 at both arms. No failure is added.

The first head arm exposed one added failure: a test double whose signature predated the new keyword. The keyword is now supplied only on the per-direction reference path, so a caller that does not ask for it sees the established signature, and the re-run of all five head files adds none.
