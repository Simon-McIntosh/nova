The step Jacobian is right at the stalled drive

The engaged forward step solves a linear model it assembles itself, I - d(booked)/du. Compared with central differences of the full fresh-read residual, that matrix is correct at the state where the 1e-2 drive stalls: the relative mismatch is between 6 × 10−8 and 6 × 10−7 at the two larger steps in every direction class, and it moves with the step the way roundoff does rather than the way a missing term would. What stalls the solve is not the matrix. A unit Newton step, which the linear model predicts will annihilate the residual, leaves 93.9 % of it standing: the fresh residual is strongly nonlinear over the step the step takes.

What is compared

support_read.step_jacobian assembles the matrix from the bank channels, the closed-form cell-local booking sensitivities and, with fresh_geometry=True, the moving saddle and edge-interval term. The residual the loop evaluates is step_residual(operands, moments, moments, support, profile, quadrature), whose blocks are moments - booked[:3] (nova/equilibrium/support_read.py:547); the solve uses it at support_read.py:1417 as right = -residual against the same matrix, so the matrix must equal d(residual)/du.

The comparison central-differences the fresh-read residual, re-running support_read.read at every perturbed state so the reading, ownership and clip set move with it, along 33 unit moment directions: five in each of the cut, normal-form, whole and stationary-point cells plus eight random, at relative steps 1e-5, 1e-6 and 1e-7. The displaced state moves by eps · scale · e, where scale is the block's largest moment magnitude, and the quotient is divided by that same magnitude, so both sides of the comparison act on the same vector.

The control that has to pass first

Before any state is reported the driver differentiates a linear map with a known matrix — twelve directions over three blocks — through the same displacement, step magnitude and comparison code. It returned a relative mismatch of 2.7 × 10−10, roundoff. The run aborts if that figure exceeds 1e-8, so a comparison that is wrong in sign or scale cannot reach the states below.

Three readings fix the interpretation. The mismatch falls at the large step — 3.6 × 10−8 at 1e-5 against 5.4 × 10−7 at 1e-6 — which is the signature of a fixed absolute error in the residual divided by a shrinking step, not of a term the matrix is missing: a missing term would grow with the step, not shrink. The normal-form and saddle cells come in one to two decades lower than the rest, so the moving saddle term is the best-behaved part of the assembly, not the matrix's worst. Every class is below 1e-6 at 1e-5 and at 1e-6, which is where the plateau sits. At 1e-7 the quotient's own roundoff takes over and the mismatch rises to between 2.4 × 10−6 and 4.8 × 10−6; that is a floor on the step size one can difference at, not a property of the matrix, and it is why the acceptance reading is taken at 1e-6.

Splitting the mismatch by term

Only one split moves the number, and it moves it in the safe direction. Replacing the moving-geometry contribution with its own central difference takes the median from 5.6 × 10−7 to 8.3 × 10−8 (cut 5.4e-7 to 8.3e-8, whole 4.5e-7 to 8.2e-8, axis 5.2e-7 to 6.6e-8, random 5.6e-7 to 9.4e-8). The cell-local booking sensitivity, by contrast, is already exact at this level: replacing it with its own difference changes nothing (5.4e-7 stays 5.4e-7). So the analytic booking tangent is not the source of the residue, and the fresh-geometry term carries a relative discrepancy of a few parts in 107 — three decades below the acceptance floor and not a defect this measurement can pursue.

The read refresh is the one large number in the receipt, and it is not a mismatch: at the normal-form and saddle cells the fresh and frozen reads differ by 73 % and 100 % of the derivative, so the read moving inside the stencil is most of the signal there. The matrix reproduces the resulting derivative anyway, to 3e-9 and 1e-9 respectively.

The discrete decision inside the stencil

Across all 33 directions and all three steps, no cut set, normal-form set, fragment selection or ownership changes between the perturbed read and the unperturbed one, and the admitted saddle moves linearly in the step (7.96e-9, 7.96e-10, 7.96e-11 at the cut cell; no jump). The stencil does not straddle a discrete boundary, so the central difference is differentiating a smooth map.

Why the solve stalls

The matrix being right does not make the step succeed. Taking the full Newton correction δ from the assembled matrix and the fresh residual and evaluating the fresh residual at u + δ:

The linear solve is exact to machine precision — the model predicts it will finish the job — and the true residual keeps 93.9 % of its magnitude. The step therefore takes a Newton direction that is correct at the point it is evaluated at and wrong over its own length: the residual's curvature over one unit step is far larger than its gradient. The damped search then contracts the step until the contraction test is met, which is exactly the behaviour that burns sixty updates without converging. The stall is the nonlinearity of the fresh residual over a unit step, not a defect in the Jacobian.

The supplied gradient

The fixture supplies a zero density gradient at both states (supplied_gradient_max = 0.0, shape (9,)), and zero is the true derivative here: the booked profile's p_prime and ff_prime both have zero flux derivative at nine probe points, so both profiles are constant in flux. The amplitude scaling is consistent too, 2787.512 × 1.01 = 2815.387. A control gradient of 1e-3 changes the booking column by the same 1e-9 as the analytic one, so nothing in this measurement turns on the gradient.

Retraction of the first reading

An earlier attempt at this node reported a mismatch of 1.70 in every direction class, and read it as the booking tangent disagreeing with the residual everywhere and independent of the step. That conclusion is retracted. The number was a defect in the comparing driver: it negated a difference quotient of moments - booked, whose derivative is the very matrix it was comparing against, and it displaced the state by eps · scale while applying the matrix to e, which predicts a relative mismatch of 1 + 1/scale — exactly the three block constants (1.0056, 1.7009, 1.9939) it measured. The tell was that the figure was identical at a stalled state with residual 1.79 and at a certificate state with residual 1.5 × 10−12; no property of the matrix is invariant across twelve decades of residual. The positive control above exists so that this failure cannot repeat: it fails loudly, before any state is reported, if the comparison is wrong in sign or in scale.

What remains

The certificate state did not reach its own summary line before the allocation's wall — the run completed the stalled state, which is the decisive half, and was cut short on the second. Re-running it is the same command with a longer wall; nothing in the driver changes. The stall itself now points away from the Jacobian: the next question is what makes the residual this nonlinear over a unit step, and whether the damped search's contraction test should prefer a smaller trial step when the linear model predicts a residual three decades smaller than the one observed.
