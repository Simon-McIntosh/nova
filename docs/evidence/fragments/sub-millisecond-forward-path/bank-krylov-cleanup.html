Unwritten Arnoldi columns kept out of the bank least-squares

A restarted cycle that ends before the restart width leaves the tail of the Hessenberg matrix unwritten. Seeding those columns with an identity and then solving the small least-squares by QR put a spurious component into the Newton update: a QR back-substitution couples every trailing column into the leading coefficients, so the corruption survived even when the trailing basis vectors were zero. The cycle now zeroes the unwritten columns before the QR and sets their zero diagonal to one so the triangular solve stays finite; the basis columns they would scale are already zero, so only columns an Arnoldi step actually wrote enter the solve.

Two tests pin the behaviour against independent float64 references at 1e-10 relative. An early breakdown is a guard rather than the defect: the basis vector a breakdown produces is zeroed, so an identity seed cannot reach the update there, and the test stays green with the seed restored. The mid-cycle cap is where the defect bites. Restoring the identity seed on a cycle capped at five columns of a width-20 restart reddens that test at 5.6e-3, against 4.7e-16 with the fix; the deciding log is logs/negative-control-eye-breakdown.log.

The cycle driver's toy factor now derives its block inverse and gather indices from the same block preconditioner the step uses, so a three-component double carries the fields' actual meaning rather than a shape-correct placeholder. Nothing the cycle test asserts changed.
