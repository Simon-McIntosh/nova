Block GMRES cuts the batch linear solve to 6.44–10.50 ms; 1 ms remains unmet

Use equilibrated dense GMRES with a per-cell 3 × 3 block inverse as the next linear route for newton_step. On sixteen diverted 550-cell systems it takes 6.439 ms at the trial state and 10.495 ms at the converged state, versus 105.735 and 105.945 ms for double LU: 16.4 and 10.1 times faster. Every lane meets the 1e-8 physical update-error limit, and every equilibrated condition-estimate × relative-residual product is at most 1e-8. No measured arm reaches about 1 ms for the sixteen-lane update. Even the best route remains 6.4–10.5 times above that target. These are linear solves only; they exclude the read, residual booking and Jacobian assembly. No product source or tests change in this measurement.

Routes and stopping rules

  • Double LU: the present vmapped lu_factor plus lu_solve on B.
  • Single LU + refinement: factor B in float32, solve in float32, then apply the named number of corrections using the same factor. Each correction forms its residual against the original float64 B and accumulates the update in float64. Zero corrections is retained as the precision control. One correction meets the update bound, but at sixteen lanes the installed float32 factor route costs about 399 ms including that correction, much slower than double LU. Two or three corrections improve accuracy without curing the timing.
  • Block GMRES: left preconditioning with the inverse of each cell's 3 × 3 diagonal block, restart width 20 and at most 20 cycles. Arnoldi and least squares reuse the installed JAX primitives; the instrument retains the executed inner-step count that the public GMRES result omits. It checks the true unpreconditioned residual of B after every restart, not the preconditioned residual. The tolerance is 1e-8 / κ̂(B), with κ̂ supplied by support_read._krylov_condition_estimate. The estimator and block-inverse setup are included in the timed call. Trial lanes take 20 Arnoldi steps; converged lanes take 40. These are restart-quantized counts, not Newton iterations.
  • GMRES, both coordinate bounds: a deliberately stricter diagnostic also requires κ̂(A) ‖A x − b‖₂ / ‖b‖₂ ≤ 1e-8. It retains both estimates in the timed call. This arm reaches its 400-step cap and misses that condition despite accurate updates. It is kept in both tables and marked as a miss.
  • Stacked double LU: direct jax.lax.linalg.lu on the stacked batch followed by the batched triangular solve. It is about 106 ms at sixteen lanes, indistinguishable in practical benefit from the present route; spelling the factor as a stacked operation does not expose a faster route in this installed JAX.

κ̂(B) is 38.928–39.200 at the trial states and 38.480 at convergence, giving relative-residual tolerances about 2.55–2.60e-10. κ̂(A) is 1.737–1.779e8, whose corresponding tolerance is about 5.6–5.8e-17, below float64 epsilon. The equilibrated bound concerns y, so the independent physical x update check is also required. The main GMRES arm's largest physical error is 3.700225e-9; its largest balanced estimated bound is 8.435076e-9. The condition estimates are eight-direction Arnoldi estimates, not certified full condition numbers. The strict diagnostic shows why the coordinate system must be named when stating a residual tolerance; it is not silently substituted for the requested equilibrated solve.

Adoption and reuse rule

Adopt the measured equilibrated block-GMRES route for the next implementation comparison, keeping double LU as a fallback when the condition-set residual bound is not reached. Require the same physical update validation during that integration. This measurement establishes the linear route's accuracy at the two fixture states; it does not establish nonlinear convergence or the final whole-solve latency with that route substituted.

There is no dense LU factor to carry on the GMRES path. Rebuild the small block inverses and condition estimate whenever the Newton matrix is rebuilt, with one set per lane. The measured policy rebuilds all three every update. Retain that policy initially; this record does not justify reusing a stale matrix or preconditioner. If optional chord reuse is subsequently enabled, the existing finite fresh-residual contraction ratio ≤ 0.5 remains the eligibility rule, with a fresh-read nonlinear convergence check. It cannot be inferred from a small linear residual of the carried matrix. Present solve_batch defaults factor reuse off, even though newton_step supports that optional rule.

The remaining limit is visible in the traces: useful GMRES still launches 724 kernels at sixteen trial lanes and 1134 at convergence. The measured speedup therefore supplies a better route to implement, not a sub-millisecond result.
