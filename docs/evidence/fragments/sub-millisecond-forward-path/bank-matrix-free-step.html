Bank Newton step applied matrix-free inside GMRES

This section records an attempt to take the bank Newton step matrix-free: to hand the Krylov solve a Jacobian-vector product instead of an assembled matrix. The product is support_read.step_jacobian_vector: the identity minus the bank, the booking tangent and, on the fresh-geometry path, the moving saddle normal form. The block preconditioner for that attempt is each cell's own 3×3 diagonal block, obtained without an assembly: a cell's moments enter only its own booking sensitivities and its own bank row, so the block is its local sensitivities contracted with its own unit-direction channel vector. A target-current quadrature sums across cells and stays on the assembled path.

The attempt failed and was withdrawn. The product path does not converge at the bank's real width, so it was removed from support_read and its test file rather than left behind a default-off flag, and the assembled path is what the code does. The negative result, the measurement behind it and the diagnosis that explains it all stand, and they are what this section records.

Both operators ran through one Arnoldi routine, so the restarted least-squares cycle, the true-residual restart decision and the condition-estimate times relative-residual 1e-8 stop were the same code on both paths. The assembled path keeps its row and column equilibration; the product path scaled by the physical moment scales alone, because row and column norms need the matrix entries the product exists to avoid. That difference is the whole of the defect, as the diagnostic below measures.

The stall is the equilibration, not the product

The assembled matrix is used only as a source of preconditioning: its row and column scales and its cell-block inverses are applied around step_jacobian_vector, and the same GMRES runs over the same product. Nothing else changes. If the product then converges, the product is sound and the missing equilibrating scales are what stalled it.

The product converges in exactly the assembled path's forty iterations once it is given those scales, at 1e-14 agreement with the assembled update and a condition estimate near two thousand instead of the 1e6 that stalled it. The stall is the equilibration and nothing else: the product, the cell-block preconditioner and the stopping rule are all sound, and the only thing the product path lacked was the row and column balancing it could not compute without the matrix. The second arm matters as much as the first, because a solve cannot assemble the matrix it is trying to avoid: scales and blocks carried over from a state one Newton update behind give the same forty iterations and the same 1e-14 agreement, so a reused factorisation does not have to be current to be good. What this does not show is how a product path would obtain those scales without assembling, which is the work a working matrix-free step would still owe.

The same failure at sixteen slices, and its cost

The width is not what breaks it. On the same reserved H200, at sixteen lanes, the assembled update converges on every lane in 40 iterations and 49.6 ms, while the product update spends the 400-iteration allowance on every lane and takes 44.4 s — 895 times the assembled wall for an answer that is not a solution.

Verdict: the matrix-free Newton step does not work at the bank's width and has been withdrawn, so the assembled path is what the code does again. The product path exhausted a 400-iteration allowance on every lane at a 1e-2 relative residual at both 550-cell states and at sixteen lanes, against 40 iterations assembled, and took 895 times the assembled wall at sixteen slices to return a non-solution. The negative control failed as declared and the paired suites showed no added failure, so the measurement is sound rather than a broken harness. The diagnosis explains the stall exactly: given the assembled path's row and column scales and cell-block inverses, the same product converges in forty iterations at 1e-14, including when those scales come from the state one Newton update behind. What remains unmet is the step itself, and what it still owes is a way to obtain those scales without assembling the matrix the product exists to avoid. No product rule is proposed here; the assembled path is unchanged and correct, and the next attempt starts from this diagnosis rather than from the premise that the product alone would be fast enough.
