Bank Newton step applied matrix-free inside GMRES

The Newton step can hand its Krylov solve a Jacobian-vector product instead of an assembled matrix. The product is support_read.step_jacobian_vector: the identity minus the bank, the booking tangent and, on the fresh-geometry path, the moving saddle normal form. The block preconditioner for that path is each cell's own 3×3 diagonal block, obtained without an assembly: a cell's moments enter only its own booking sensitivities and its own bank row, so the block is its local sensitivities contracted with its own unit-direction channel vector. A target-current quadrature sums across cells and stays on the assembled path.

Both operators run through one Arnoldi routine (_block_gmres_operator), so the restarted least-squares cycle, the true-residual restart decision and the condition-estimate times relative-residual 1e-8 stop are the same code on both paths. The dense path keeps its row and column equilibration; the product path scales by the physical moment scales alone, because row and column norms need the matrix entries the product path exists to avoid.

Run against the unmutated product on a reserved H200, the test fails at both states. The same failure at sixteen slices, and its cost

The width is not what breaks it. On the same reserved H200, at sixteen lanes, the assembled update converges on every lane in 40 iterations and 49.6 ms, while the product update spends the 400-iteration allowance on every lane and takes 44.4 s — 895 times the assembled wall for an answer that is not a solution.

Verdict: the product path is implemented and committed, the negative control fails as declared, and the paired suites show no added failure. The path is not usable at any real width: on a reserved H200 its Krylov solve exhausts a 400-iteration allowance on every lane, at a 1e-2 relative residual, so it returns no solution and costs 895 times the assembled wall at sixteen slices to do it. The done-when asks for GMRES within 60 iterations; the measurement is 400 without convergence, at sixteen lanes and at the 550-cell states alike. The section's measure is unmet at this record. The candidate cause — a preconditioned operator conditioned at 1e6 because the product path carries only the physical moment scales, where the assembled path balances by row and column norms — is stated as a hypothesis this record does not test.
