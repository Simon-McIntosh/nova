Bank Newton step applied matrix-free inside GMRES

The Newton step can hand its Krylov solve a Jacobian-vector product instead of an assembled matrix. The product is support_read.step_jacobian_vector: the identity minus the bank, the booking tangent and, on the fresh-geometry path, the moving saddle normal form. The block preconditioner for that path is each cell's own 3×3 diagonal block, obtained without an assembly: a cell's moments enter only its own booking sensitivities and its own bank row, so the block is its local sensitivities contracted with its own unit-direction channel vector. A target-current quadrature sums across cells and stays on the assembled path.

Both operators run through one Arnoldi routine (_block_gmres_operator), so the restarted least-squares cycle, the true-residual restart decision and the condition-estimate times relative-residual 1e-8 stop are the same code on both paths. The dense path keeps its row and column equilibration; the product path scales by the physical moment scales alone, because row and column norms need the matrix entries the product path exists to avoid.

The product path reproduces the assembled update

tests/test_bank_newton_step.py::test_matrix_free_update_matches_dense_update solves the same linear system twice at the diverted 550-cell trial and converged certificate states, once through the assembled matrix and once through the product, and compares the whole physical update vector.

The declared negative control: the booking-tangent term dropped from the product in a scratch copy of the head tree, so the product describes the bank alone. The same test then fails on its own agreement assertion.

Device timing: not measured

Two H200 allocations were taken for the base-against-head timing and neither produced a table. The first ran with a prototype loader that sliced the saved lane states on the wrong axis, so every mode raised before timing; it was cancelled by id once the traceback was read. The second was launched with the loader corrected and had reached only the shared seed build when the node's budget expired. The driver that would produce the table is scripts/prototypes/bank_matrix_free_step/device.sh: it builds the seed arrays once at the base revision, loads them in every arm, gives each revision its own compilation cache, and times only repetitions after a warm call. Its numbers, and the wall-per-slice figure against width, are outstanding work for this section.

Verdict: the product path is implemented, committed and pinned by a negative control that fails as declared. The done-when's device table, its iteration-count evidence for the chosen preconditioner and the paired base-against-head suites are not measured, so the section's measure is unmet at this record.
