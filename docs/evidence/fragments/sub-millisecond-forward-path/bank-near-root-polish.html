Negative result: fixed polishing stabilizes reads at higher latency

The fixed polish is rejected as a product change. The experimental schedule used 20 reads per lane on every one of the six seed sets, against 7–15 at base. Whole-solve latency was 1684.921–1688.424 ms for the experiment versus 525.585–983.463 ms at base in the two fresh measured processes. Wall per read was 84.246–84.421 ms versus 65.473–75.106 ms, including Newton work. Stable counts therefore came with a substantial latency regression.

The four product and test files were restored byte for byte to base 73864287c5db07bac083e61dca1f160859452351 in commit 511d789cbfbe4e95e11a9da96b0dd06879a3d280. The prototype, figure and receipts remain as negative evidence. In all tables below, head names experimental revision 42039a936366f4b71a04c3573c46d90213fb5ec6, whose product change was withdrawn; the suite and negative-control results belong to that experiment.

The experiment entered the near-root band when every active lane had a valid, finite residual at most 1e-8. It took P=16 full Newton updates without backtracking, kept each lane's lowest-residual valid state, and certified those states with a separate fresh read at the unchanged 1e-10 limit. Failed certification resumed the adaptive search, within the original update cap.

The 1e-8 band was above the measured 1.6e-9 to 2.9e-9 residuals at update 2 and below the preceding 3.7e-4 to 4.4e-4 residuals. P=8 failed on seed 5, lane 10: its best fresh residual was 1.3227463568910025e-10, above the certificate limit. That seed needed adaptive fallback and 16 reads, while the other five took 12. Doubling the polish to P=16 produced 20 reads on every seed set, at the latency reported above; every lane still passed the unchanged fresh certificate and the paired precision budgets.

Near the root, full Newton steps did not reliably reduce the fresh residual. Even eight further full steps could leave a lane above 1e-10, while tiny changes in initial moments changed which evaluations crossed that threshold. These results identify the residual's own noise floor near 1e-10 as the constraint. The longer polish bought more chances to retain a certifiable state, at substantial cost; this experiment does not establish a product rule.
