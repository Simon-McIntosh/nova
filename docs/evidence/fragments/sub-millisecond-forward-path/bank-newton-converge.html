Bank Newton convergence audit

The bank's own equation is the selected target. Its baseline step solves a frozen read and stalls under a fresh read. An added saddle-form and edge-interval tangent agrees with small-step central differences, but the dense Newton matrix does not compose to that tangent at the diverted seed. The fresh solve, oracle comparison and converged contour remain open. Production-route parity is no longer a gate because the routes discretise differently.

Same-iterate systems

The published diverted 550-cell bank was read from /work/projects/imas_gpu/sophelio/nova-assembled-step-bank. The limited bank was built on the H200. At each physical moment iterate the diagnostic applied the same prescribed total-current normalization to both maps. The first disagreement is in the reconstructed flux field: the bank contracts nodal jet rows and the production map fits its separate Gaussian-point values. The axis and boundary levels agree at the sampled states, but the cell coefficients do not. The production read then selects one different cell at the diverted trial state. The two booking implementations also differ: current.book integrates its clipped support, while the production map combines interior triangles, Bell polygons and saddle sectors with path weights. Consequently a factorization of the bank residual cannot establish production-route parity.

Fresh geometry tangent and dense update

The bank now differentiates the admitted saddle's stationary location, Hessian, third and fourth derivatives, cubic normal form, and selected edge intervals. Moving-polygon moments carry these ring velocities into the booking tangent. At the diverted 550-cell seed, a central difference of the full fresh-read residual saw a relative tangent gap of 0.0300271 at fraction 1e-5, 0.000239573 at 1e-6 and 2.44017e-6 at 1e-7. The original frozen tangent stayed near 0.80. This shrinking error supports the added directional derivative.
