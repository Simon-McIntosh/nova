Bank Newton convergence audit

The bank's own equation is the selected target. Its baseline frozen-read step stalls, but the repaired saddle geometry Jacobian and dense assembly now converge the diverted 550-cell seed with a fresh read at every step: three accepted steps reach a residual of 1.00e-11. The axis, diverted X-point and boundary flux meet the analytic-oracle precision budget. The warm step remains 366 ms, above the section's 0.5 ms performance bound, and launch count is unmeasured. Production-route parity was dropped because the routes discretise differently.

Same-iterate systems

The published diverted 550-cell bank was read from /work/projects/imas_gpu/sophelio/nova-assembled-step-bank. The limited bank was built on the H200. At each physical moment iterate the diagnostic applied the same prescribed total-current normalization to both maps. The first disagreement is in the reconstructed flux field: the bank contracts nodal jet rows and the production map fits its separate Gaussian-point values. The axis and boundary levels agree at the sampled states, but the cell coefficients do not. The production read then selects one different cell at the diverted trial state. The two booking implementations also differ: current.book integrates its clipped support, while the production map combines interior triangles, Bell polygons and saddle sectors with path weights. Consequently a factorization of the bank residual cannot establish production-route parity.

Dense assembly repair

Column comparison localized the first error to the saddle geometry response. The frozen flux, booking and level columns matched the matvec within 1e-14, while fresh column 183 differed by 0.916 relative. The normal-form curvature stencil samples nine nearby positions, including adjacent Bell triangles, but the dense assembly had used only the central triangle's three nodes. Its geometry-row flattening also ordered cell before moment although the Newton vector orders moment before cell. The repair includes every distinct stencil node and transposes the bank rows to moment-major input columns. The normal form is the first affected term; its edge intervals and booked moments inherit that incorrect column.

The repaired 550-cell check gives a 9.14e-12 relative difference for column 183, a 1.45e-11 difference for column 184, and 4.02e-13 for a random vector. A six-column small-case test checks every basis vector and a random vector against step_jacobian_vector; its head arm passed. The pre-repair source arm and a scratch mutation that collapses the stencil to the central triangle each fail the same test. The mutation log prints the imported scratch module path and ends EXIT=1.

At the diverted seed the repaired dense update has ‖R + JΔ‖/‖R‖ = 2.56e-11 by the directional tangent. Central differences of a fresh read at a 1e-4 fraction give 1.29e-5, of the perturbation's order. Both sides of that difference were valid.
