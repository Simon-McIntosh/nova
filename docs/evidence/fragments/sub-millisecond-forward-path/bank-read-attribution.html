Ranked read-cost changes

Ranked by the conservative estimated CPU saving on this fixture. The following savings are proposals inferred from measured stage costs and source execution shape, not implemented speedups. They do not establish a per-slice H200 wall.

  1. Inspect only live saddle candidates. Target topology._read_at_nulls.compact_candidates, _fragments_at_level and _support_at_level. This single-saddle fixture packs three slots: the axis, the saddle and a padded axis duplicate. Keep the axis normal form for the limited fallback, but avoid the axis and padding's unnecessary support inspections. The full read makes three candidate support evaluations plus initial and terminal support evaluations; removing two of these five gives a first CPU estimate of 2070.4 ms per full read (a discounted 35% budget of its joint field/support stage). Vectorization and fusion make this a scaling estimate, not a timed saving. Verify admitted saddles, boundary ownership, membership and qualification against the full owner, including zero, one and multiple saddles and the overflow fallback; then measure the candidate dimension and device time on the H200.
  2. Widen scalar booking's conic tile. Target current.book and current._SCALAR_CUT_BATCH_WIDTH: try 128 instead of 32. The 113 conic cut cells then need one bucket instead of four; the four normal-form cut cells keep their independent bucket. Expected CPU saving: 653.2–1306.4 ms per width-sixteen read, a 25–50% reduction of the measured 2612.8 ms booking stage. This range is an engineering estimate informed by the analogous tangent-booking tile result, not a scalar-booking ablation. Verify both process pairs, all booked moments and normalization against the unchanged scalar owner, trial and converged residuals at the existing tolerance, and dynamic conic-bucket counts on the H200. Include fewer than 32 and more than 128 cut cells so the population bound is tested.
  3. Move diverted wall skipping outside the lane map. Target topology.refresh_at_stationary_points.wall_contact and its batched caller.
