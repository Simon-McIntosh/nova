The shared precision comparison reproduces both dependent reports

scripts/prototypes/bank_solve_linesearch/report.py and scripts/prototypes/bank_step_booking_batch/report.py import compare from scripts/prototypes/bank_solve_cycle/report.py. Both were replayed on scratch copies of their existing receipts after the comparison's clean-up. The linesearch SUMMARY line is 953 bytes and the booking PRECISION line is 304 bytes; each is byte-identical to the corresponding line in the recorded report.log and report-corrected.log, with no field changed.

tests/test_bank_report_precision.py pins the comparison on constructed two-lane receipts. One pair sits inside every limit and reports zero gaps. Four further pairs each exceed exactly one bound — flux over span 2e-8 against 1e-8, axis over minor radius 6e-4 and X-point over minor radius 6e-4 against 5e-4, and boundary flux over span 2e-4 against 1e-4 — and each raises the owner's own assertion. The pairs also assert each gap against its reported limit and denominator, so redefining a limit or a normalising unit fails the test rather than the report. The receipt's span and minor radius are distinct (0.7 and 2.3) for exactly that reason. The five tests pass in 4.96 s on the login node.

Two scratch copies of the owner show the test fails against both kinds of change. Moving the flux limit from 1e-8 to 1e-7 fails exactly one test, the flux pair, at 2e-8 reported against the widened 1e-7 bound; the other four pass. Exchanging the span and minor-radius denominators in pair_gaps fails all four limit pairs and leaves only the inside-limit pair passing. Both negative-control logs end EXIT=1.
