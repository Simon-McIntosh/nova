Fresh-residual floor: rounding amplified into the saddle geometry

The fresh residual is at its numerical floor near the 1e-10 certificate. Across 64 perturbations of each converged H200 lane, the median residual ranges from 3.2244e-11 to 1.5436e-10. All sixteen lanes cross the certificate at least once; four have medians above it. The largest residual is 2.9128e-10. CPU repeats of lanes 0 and 10 have medians 5.3227e-11 and 7.9666e-11 and also cross the certificate. This is not an H200-only effect.

The dominant path is the freshly reconstructed saddle geometry. Holding the saddle normal form and its edge intervals together at the unperturbed values reduces the maximum residual change from 2.05873e-10 to 1.53477e-12 (134-fold), and the maximum componentwise spread from 2.46899e-10 to 1.79057e-12 (138-fold). This is an attribution experiment, not a fresh-read certificate or a solver change. Product source, tests and the acceptance threshold are unchanged.

Measurement and controls

The numerical source is 6adab8c7a4799d094522e5f87a8b560c9fbe2bd2. The probe imports bank_solve_profile.fixture.build(16), the existing mask/signature observers and fingerprint helper. Its 550-cell diverted batch converged in 12 batch reads, with all sixteen terminal residuals at or below 1e-10. Those moments are saved once and reused by every later experiment; the CPU repeat loads precisely lanes 0 and 10 from that file. No paired latency comparison is made.

Double precision is configured before array construction and asserted in each process. A deterministic sign pattern moves each nonzero moment entry by one, two, three or four representable steps, cycling the magnitude over 64 patterns. Structural zeros are retained; subnormal perturbations of zero are not part of this population. Each evaluation calls read_batch afresh without tracking, then step_residual(m, m, fresh_read). The norm is exactly the solver's maximum absolute concatenated moment residual. Table 1's spread is the maximum norm minus the minimum norm over the 64 evaluations.

Attribution uses one signed ulp per nonzero output entry, with 64 patterns at each boundary. Wrappers and live-local trace hooks instrument the existing arithmetic, and the hook-presence assertion must see every requested boundary. The main and saddle-geometry probes reproduce their ordinary zero-injection residual vectors bit for bit. The deeper field probe unwraps the existing stationary JIT boundary so its cached-jet hook actually traces; its zero-injection vector differs from the ordinary evaluator by at most 7.9581e-13. Its injection changes are measured against its own zero-injection result. That scheduling effect is retained as a limit on interpreting its smallest rows.

All moment perturbations retain the same cut, normal-cell and selected-fragment masks. The known-present whole-cell, conic-cell and normalization perturbations produce nonzero responses. Uniform moment-output rows reflect their binary spacing, not an empty observation. The fresh state's difference passed to _directional_field is exactly zero; both its jets and sampled coefficients are checked to be zero. The field-coefficient row perturbs the refreshed coefficients after that update. Flux-normalized density coefficients and the raw-current sum produce zero response in this fixture: its source is independent of normalized flux and its quadrature has no prescribed total-current normalization. The normalization-factor injection itself is detected.

Where the bits are lost

The worst moment perturbation is pattern 14 in zero-based lane 12, at current component/cell 184. That cell belongs to the eight-cell saddle normal-form population. Across the population, saddle position changes by at most 2.8866e-15 m (13 ulps), direction by 4.8406e-14 (436 ulps), curvature by 3.5269e-12 (161,704 ulps), and the cubic ray coefficient by 1.1447e-10 (32,993,940 ulps). Thus the one-ulp normal-form output rows do not represent the much larger errors arriving at those outputs.

Bank contraction and exterior cancellation. support_read.nodal_jets and the stationary cache contract the moment rows and add the exterior. An 80-digit sum of the same 1,650 products and exterior, using the saved moments and cached bank, shows an absolute jet error up to 1.6135e-16. At flux node 681, a sum of absolute terms 0.112928 produces 3.38838e-5: cancellation ratio 3,332.79, and the recorded flux differs from the extended-precision sum by 2.8791e-18, or 424.88 output ulps. Across the selected jets the change in rounding error reaches 7.5888e-17. Consequently an injection of only one ulp at the final nodal value understates the rounding already present in that value. This reference evaluates the stored binary64 operands, not an exact analytic Biot kernel.

Bell reconstruction and fourth derivatives. fixed_points.field_from_jets contracts the signed Taylor remainder with mesh.transforms. In the inspected saddle triangle, high-order coefficient sums have cancellation ratios up to 62,709.52 (about 15.9 bits), and an 80-digit sum of the captured operands differs from a recorded coefficient by up to 5.0819e-18. The triangle scale is 0.0714198 m; taking fourth derivatives introduces h^-4 = 38,434.87 m^-4, before polynomial factors. The captured pair changes a coefficient by 3.4185e-17 and a fourth derivative by 8.3480e-12 while selecting the same cell and triangle and the same stationary point. The normal-form construction then carries the higher-derivative noise into its cubic rays and the edge intervals. These snapshots are an independent compiled inspection of the worst input pair; the exact floor attribution comes from the frozen-output contrasts, not from treating the snapshot as the original executable's bitwise trace.

Freezing only the cubic ray coefficient leaves a maximum change of 1.42094e-10; freezing only the edge intervals leaves 6.15330e-11. Freezing both the entire saddle form and the intervals leaves 1.53477e-12. By contrast, freezing field coefficients or axis/boundary fluxes leaves 2.05873e-10. The two continuous geometry outputs therefore have to be considered together.

The downstream booking reductions and final subtraction do not set this observed floor. One-ulp changes of whole, conic and normal-cell moments change the residual by at most 2.8422e-14, 5.6843e-14 and 1.4211e-14. Normalization-factor changes reach 8.5265e-14. A one-ulp change at the final residual is at most 1.2925e-26. The near-root subtraction exposes upstream error; subtraction of the close, same-sign binary64 operands is exact. The conic and normal vertex controls reach 3.7375e-12 and 9.3792e-13. The two used cubic intersection roots at the worst cell agree with 80-digit Newton refinement within 8.2529e-16, and cubic-intermediate injections stay below 8.6687e-13. The root formula is not the dominant source in these measurements.

Estimate for a product experiment

If the bank contraction and the cancellation-prone reconstruction of the saddle's higher derivatives were effectively exact, a residual-variation floor near 2e-12 is a reasonable hypothesis: roughly two orders below the observed 2e-10 variation and fifty times below the certificate. The 1.79057e-12 spread with both geometry outputs fixed supplies the scale for that estimate. It does not prove that a fully fresh solve will reach it. Making only the final subtraction exact offers no corresponding gain, and an exact Bell coefficient sum alone leaves rounding in its input jets.

A product experiment should evaluate accurate third/fourth derivative bank rows or a compensated field reconstruction, preserving the same discretized equation and fresh-read certificate. It must repeat the saved-state perturbation population and demonstrate convergence and read counts without freezing any geometry. No such numerical change is made here.
