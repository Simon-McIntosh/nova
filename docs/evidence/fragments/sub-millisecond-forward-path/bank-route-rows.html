Fast-route convergence and oracle precision beyond the diverted seed

The fast route converges on both 550-cell certificate rows and, on the diverted row, stays inside the locked precision budget with its X-point 6.80e-6 m from the analytic one. The limited row converges at 9.64e-11 of the 1e-10 residual limit — under four percent in hand — and its oracle ratios are not measured here, so that half of the row is a shortfall rather than a pass.

Each row runs the fast route from the certificate seed — the analytic read, booked — to a fresh-read residual, then runs the default present route on the same certificate row. Geometry is judged against the analytic oracle under the locked precision budget: magnetic axis and X-point within 5e-4 of the minor radius, boundary flux within 1e-4 of the flux span, and a fresh-read solved residual of at most 1e-10. The present route is judged only on its own convergence status and terminal residual, since §4 holds it to no regression rather than to convergence.

Every X-point gap between the routes is stated with the representation it was read on. On the solve lattice a gap above a few micrometres is real; on a display map the gap is reported beside that map's own interpolation offset.

The diverted row converges in three updates and its stationary points land 6.80e-6 m from the analytic axis and 6.80e-6 m from the analytic X-point on a 0.7343 m minor radius, with the boundary flux 9.29e-7 Wb inside a 0.03587 Wb span. The axis and X-point are about fifty times inside their budget and the boundary flux is 3.9 times inside its own — the row's closest approach, and the clause that would give first on a coarser bank. The limited row needs seven updates and terminates at 9.64e-11, which is inside the 1e-10 limit by four percent — a pass this measurement would not repeat confidently under a different compile of the same program, and the row is named here as marginal for that reason.

The limited row's oracle ratios are missing for a reason that belongs to the harness rather than to the route. Its first archive read a second stationary row a limited case does not have, so the process raised at the oracle after the seven updates and before it archived the state that the ratios and the panel are drawn from; that defect is repaired in row_oracle, which judges a limited row against its limiter contact. Re-running the repaired row needs the limited operator bank rebuilt in the new process: 328.8 s for 1743 nodes on a contended H200, and 436 s for its first batched launch on the P100 fallback, where the remaining 1739 nodes would have taken hours. The row is therefore tabled with its convergence measured and its precision unmeasured, and it is the one piece of §4 clause 2 this node leaves open.

The present route does not converge on either row. Its own status is cited from the committed certificate receipts at the base revision (docs/figures/cut-cell-current-attribution/gate-c-resolve/parts/), because the present arm could not be re-run in this allocation: the H200 jobs stayed PENDING on a resource reason past ten minutes and were cancelled, and the titan fallback asked for 11.92 GiB of one P100 and failed with CUDA_ERROR_OUT_OF_MEMORY on a card that shares its memory with the node's other jobs. The receipts are the same benchmark, the same cases and the same sizes: the diverted -500 row realises 550 cells and ends unconverged at 0.1938 Wb after 109.8 s, and the limited -500 row realises 555 and ends unconverged at 0.08986 Wb after 144.1 s. Against a stalled present state there is no converged X-point to compare on the lattice — its own topology read admits no X-point on the diverted row, reporting class limited — so the fast-against-present X-point gap and the asymptote angle difference are recorded as not measurable rather than as zero.

The flux gap over unpromoted nodes is not measurable on these rows for a second reason: the two routes carry different node sets, and nearest-neighbour matching of the 1741 fast lattice nodes against the present receipt's 2226 coordinates finds no pair closer than 4.8e-5 m and a median nearest distance of 6.8 mm, so any difference read across them would be an interpolation artefact. Reading it needs the present route's own promoted mask and an interpolation onto one node set, which is the measurement the promoted-node record already carries for its own case rather than one this node can assemble from a stalled state.

Why the 2000-cell and 5000-cell rows were not run. Only the diverted 550-cell bank is stored; every other row builds its own operator matrix through build_jet_matrix. That build took 328.8 s for 1743 lattice nodes and 550 cells on an H200 carrying five other jobs, and its first batched launch alone reported 294 s while compiling. A 2000-cell hex realisation carries about 6300 nodes, so its matrix is nodes × cells × 3 × 6 × 8 = 1.8 GB and its build is roughly three and a half times the 550-cell wall, before the present route's own machine build and solve; two 550-cell rows, two 2000-cell rows and their present arms do not fit one allocation, and the two jobs that did share the reservation ran concurrently until the certificate benchmark's analytic-flux process pool broke under the contention. At 5000 cells the matrix alone is 7.2 GB and the build is nine times the measured 550-cell wall. Both rows are reported as unrun rather than dropped, and the 550-cell pair is the part of §4 clause 2 this node answers.
