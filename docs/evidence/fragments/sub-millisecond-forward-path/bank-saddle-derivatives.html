Why the contraction is included

At the original base, the saved lane-12 perturbation moved the fourth derivative by 2.19977e-10. An 80-digit polynomial from already-rounded nodal jets still moved by 2.24605e-10; carrying 80 digits from the bank contraction onward reduced that movement to 9.90973e-13.
