Bank solve read

The staged bank solve cannot take the tracked read between Newton steps. A carried decision supplies the discrete cells exactly, but its continuous geometry belongs to the previous state, so the step solves a lagged equation. Measured on one H200 from the diverted 550-cell seed, the carried variant converged in 51 steps and 3.31 s at one slice against the base arm's 3 steps and 0.31 s, and at sixteen slices it left nine lanes uncertified with four at the step cap and their fresh residual stuck at 1.6e-7. The landed change is therefore the removal of the redundant acceptance conjunct only; the per-slice wall is unchanged because the read and step paths are unchanged.

Why the carried read cannot feed the step

rebuild_needed compares the tracked cells and the saddle's admitted ownership, so a carry is exact in the discrete decision it supplies. The continuous fields the Newton step books with — the clipped cells' edge intervals, the boundary flux and the fractional membership — are the previous state's, because read_batch's carry branch returns tracking.reading whole. A step taken from that reading is a Newton step on the equation of the previous iterate.

Three variants were measured and each fails differently. Carrying on every iterate that stayed inside the freeze threshold diverged outright: sixty steps, no freeze, a non-finite iterative residual, no certification. Rebuilding only for the acceptance read drove the batch to stall at 1.6e-7 — the level the carry began at — while the single-slice arm still converged in twelve steps. Rebuilding on the iterate after each carry recovered certification but at 51 steps at one slice and 7 of 16 lanes at sixteen slices. This is the same failure the section already records for a frozen-read Jacobian: the tracked residual falls while the fresh residual does not.

The read's tracked branch remains sound for the steady batch it was built for, where nothing moves between reads. It is not admissible as a Newton iterate's read, and the fresh acceptance read the plan requires cannot substitute for one.

Acceptance conjunct removed

The acceptance test carried & ~changed beside & ~drift. A lane whose fresh decision differs from the locked set has lock false at that iterate, so drift is false there and the two terms are not equivalent: the removed conjunct did suppress acceptance on the iterate the lock was released at, where the step had already been taken with the fresh decisions. Removing it changes no step count on the diverted seed (3 steps, one freeze, residual 5.71e-12) and leaves the fragment-switch case at two steps and one unfreeze. The driver file's five fast tests pass at the base revision and at the head.
