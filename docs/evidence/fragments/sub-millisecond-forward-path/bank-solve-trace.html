Source bindings, limits and reproducibility

provenance.py decodes the compiler's serialized source stacks using the HLO protobuf schema and OpMetadata schema. Kernel events are joined to hlo_op where available. CUDA graphs often expose only their command-buffer name; those events use kernel symbols and the enclosing host HLO loop's source metadata. Reused compiled scalar kernels are charged to that invocation's loop, rather than the first function that emitted the shared symbol. Each ledger row preserves the resolution method, candidate instructions, enclosing HLO operators and source binding.

  • Census: support_read.stationary_stencil → fixed_points.solve. Continuous geometry: nodal_jets, field_from_jets, sampled coefficients and the terminal _support_at_level.
  • Inside topology.read_at_stationary_points: curvature is _curvature_derivatives; normal form/set is saddle_normal_form plus the returned normal-form cell set; wall events are _wall_events; candidates are _read_at_nulls.inspect and its candidate support, excluding separately identified curvature, normal form and connectivity; fragment selection is _connected_fragment_labels and core-wall selection; cut-cell membership is the initial support evaluation. Qualification retains eigensystem/SVD and validity checks.
  • Step groups use step_residual, step_jacobian and its booking/normal-geometry helpers, _equilibrate with lu_factor, and lu_solve with update scaling. _damped_scale's row is exclusive trial selection; trial reads and residuals remain in their function groups.
  • solve_batch (other) includes magnitude reductions and graph layout/copy/GEMM work for which the retained source context ends at the solve loop. It is an inventoried cost, not a fitted remainder. Unbound compiler/library and shared-source rows retain the attribution limit. Fused kernels are indivisible here; source ownership does not imply a measurement of every constituent arithmetic operation.
