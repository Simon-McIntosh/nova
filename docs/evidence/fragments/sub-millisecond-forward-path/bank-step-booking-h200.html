Booking tangent arithmetic on the H200

The warm local booking tangent costs 27.85 ms in 518 launches on the reserved H200 and 580.32 ms in 602 launches on titan. On the diverted 550-cell bank, 99 of the 550 cells are cut and 95 of those carry a moving boundary edge, so the batch evaluates the moving-edge integral for 113 cells where 95 is the live population. Two arithmetic cuts were implemented and measured. Contracting the channel-independent edge kernel once per ring cost 40.76 ms on the H200 (46% longer) and 592.86 ms on titan. Adding separate moving-edge and normal-form cut buckets recovered part of the H200 loss to 31.87 ms and cut titan to 508.26 ms (12.4% shorter). Neither change was kept: the device this section exists to speed up is the H200, and both forms regress it. Both forms still converged to a fresh residual of 2.99e-11 with oracle ratios of 1.048e-5, 9.25e-6 and 2.59e-5.

Correctness of the attempted change

The changed formulation was run through the fresh-read solve on the reserve card. It converged in eight refactorized steps to a fresh residual of 2.99e-11, and its oracle ratios are axis 1.048e-5 against 5e-4, saddle 9.25e-6 against 5e-4 and boundary flux 2.59e-5 against 1e-4. So the replacement is numerically correct and the rejection is purely about wall time. The station's own focused booking suite passes on CPU against the changed source, three tests in 51.87 s.
