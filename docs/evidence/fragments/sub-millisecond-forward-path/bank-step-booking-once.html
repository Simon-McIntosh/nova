Where the second copy comes from

The local booking tangent compiles three copies of one cell interior. Each interior contributes exactly one reduce and one concatenate to the optimised HLO, counted by labelling each Python call site with an XLA frontend attribute and reading the tags back off the compiled program: interior:whole for the full cells, interior:conic for the conic cut-cell branch and interior:normal for the normal-form cut-cell branch. The third copy is the one the trace could not name. jax.lax.cond(read.normal_form_cells[index], normal, conic, None) is evaluated inside jax.vmap(cut_cell), so its predicate is a per-cell array and the batching rule rewrites the choice as a select: both branches are computed for every cut cell and one is discarded. On the diverted 550-cell bank only four of the 99 cut cells take the normal-form branch, so the interior that the select discards is almost entirely wasted work.

The removal and what it cost

The cut-cell branches differ only in which ring set they integrate, so the ring sets are selected before the interior is evaluated: both are padded to a common ring count and ring width, chosen with one jnp.where on the branch flag, and the interior is called once. The compiled copies fall from three to two, and the retained two are genuinely distinct work: one integrates the full cells from their own polygons, the other the cut cells from the selected rings. The remaining moving-boundary term stays unconditional, as it already was under the select lowering. On the sirius CPU the batched tangent is byte-identical to the base source (sha256 of the value buffer equal, maximum absolute difference 0.0), so the change costs nothing numerically.

It costs wall. Padding the normal-form rings from their own 66-wide axis to the conic 130 and selecting between two 346-ring sets before the quadrature turns the discarded copy into a strided read of the same size as the copy it replaced, and the H200 pays for the selection rather than saving the discarded interior. The tangent therefore regressed by 9.83 ms and the step by 10.92 ms, which is the wrong direction on the device this section exists to speed up, and the source change was reverted rather than landed.

The duplicate is real and it is the per-cell branch inside book_tangent_channels, not a caller repeating the tangent: the residual books through current.book and the Jacobian reuses that booking, and the tangent is traced once per step. Removing the copy by selecting rings costs more than the copy, because the discarded branch is cheaper than the selection that replaces it. The next cut is to stop the branch being per-cell at all — a uniform branch over the cut batch, taken only when some cell in the batch is on the normal form — which keeps the base program's shapes and removes the discarded interior without padding either ring set.
