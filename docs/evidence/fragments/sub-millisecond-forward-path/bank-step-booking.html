Bank step booking and factor reuse

The existing bank Jacobian already forms its booking block from one batched 15-channel sensitivity call inside one compiled program; a new assembly test pins that call and a scratch serial-loop mutation makes it fail. This node adds a carried balanced LU factor, reused while the observed fresh-residual contraction ratio is at most 0.5. On titan P100, a reused warm step falls from about 1,476 to 323 ms with launches falling from 8,381 to 1,816. Two complete carried-factor solves took 10 and 13 steps, so total step wall ranged from 9,037 to 14,573 ms against 11,832 ms for the first fresh-factor solve. All terminal states meet the 1e-10 fresh-read residual and analytic-oracle budgets. The local booking tangent itself remains 1,077 ms on P100; this is a measured partial result, not a claim that the H200 step target is met.

Guards and limits

The base speed file passes three tests; at the head the speed file passes three and the booking file passes three, each file in a fresh pytest process. The scratch mutation replaces the single sensitivity call in Jacobian assembly with 15 host calls. The assembly test fails on that mutant (15 calls versus one, EXIT=1) and passes at the head. A first P100 carried solve stopped at its 12-step measurement limit with residual 1.525e-10; extending only that bound reached 2.235e-11 at step 13. The H200 request stayed pending from 11:55:48 to 12:06:38 Europe/Paris (10 min 50 s) and was cancelled by job ID, as recorded by h200-admission.log. Thus neither the H200 0.5 ms nor 100-launch target is judged here. The subsequent attempt was granted nova/equilibrium/current.py; its parity failure and kernel attribution are recorded below. The last committed product still uses the measured 1,077 ms tangent.
