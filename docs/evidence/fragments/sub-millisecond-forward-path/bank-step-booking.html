Bank step booking and factor reuse

On titan P100, the 550-cell diverted bank's warm local booking tangent fell from 1,076.97 to 231.24 ms (4.66×) after replacing repeated channel work with a vectorized cell and channel contraction. The warm whole step fell from 1,486.11 to 634.30 ms (2.34×). Kernel launches rose from 1,082 to 5,736 for the tangent and from 8,381 to 14,427 for the step, so launch reduction remains open. Fresh balanced LU factorization stays the default: carrying a factor reduced an isolated step but its two full solves needed 10 and 13 steps against eight with fresh factors. The H200 request remained pending for 10 min 50 s and was cancelled by id; no H200 latency or launch target is claimed.

The positive baseline trace contains 1,350 GPU events in the tangent annotation. Clipped polygon moment reductions, geometry sorts and matrix multiplies dominate; the host fragment loop is not the main wall. A first vectorized form increased the cost: 55 double-precision matrix multiplies consumed 3,448.15 of its 3,760.51 device ms. Those multiplied the cell density derivative at every quadrature point by every channel. Integrating the six local flux basis functions against each moment once per cell, then multiplying by 15 channel coefficients, removed that repeated contraction. A four-lane cut batch further reduced wall, at the cost of more launches.

Parity and limits

The all-cell, all-channel tangent parity test passes, as do the focused CPU speed and booking suites (three and five tests in separate fresh pytest processes). No JVP is traced through current.book. The book() primal keeps exactly the six field byte sequences observed at the same CPU base and head; the pinned test accepts the two measured CPU lowering variants. The scratch negative control replaces one assembled sensitivity call with 15 host calls and fails the assembly test (15 versus one), while the head passes it.
