Local booking dominates the production Jacobian

At the first step input, _local_booking_sensitivities costs 13.343 ms at one slice and 215.842 ms at sixteen, about 74% of the sixteen-slice whole Jacobian wall, 291.947 ms. It is also the worst-scaling part: 16.18× from one to sixteen slices, against 12.66× for bank channels and their first einsum and 14.48× for fresh geometry. The second step input gives the same ranking: local booking grows 16.08× and costs 213.782 ms of a 291.470 ms whole call.

The earlier profile's approximately 127 ms at one slice and 1594 ms at sixteen come from an outer vmap across slices. Production solve_batch uses lax.map at the Newton call site. At the same captured first-step inputs, this allocation measures 18.617 versus 127.188 ms at one slice and 291.947 versus 1597.625 ms at sixteen.
