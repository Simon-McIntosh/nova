The deciding same-allocation H200 comparison gives a warm 550-cell Newton step of 73.11 ms and 15,310 launches at the archived base, versus 43.11 ms and 2,804 launches after geometry and booking batching: 41% less wall and 82% fewer launches. book_tangent_channels compacts cut-cell indices on device and integrates the 99 live cut cells in fixed 64-cell batches in one static device scan. The whole Newton step is one JIT call that contains booking, geometry tangent, Jacobian assembly, equilibration, and LU without a host materialization inside it; that compiled program still emits thousands of kernels. Read precedes the step, and the outer solver synchronizes afterward to assess the fresh residual and line-search acceptance. The all-cell batch attempted a 63.88 GiB allocation on the 16 GiB P100; the bounded batch avoids it. On P100, widths 4, 8, 16, 32, and 64 show a wall-versus-launch tradeoff, with the retained width 64 giving the lowest measured launches.
