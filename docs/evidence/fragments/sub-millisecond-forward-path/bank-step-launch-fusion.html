Bank step launch reduction

On titan P100, vectorizing the moving geometry channels reduced the warm 550-cell bank Newton step from 14,427 to 9,701 kernel launches and from 635.24 to 607.25 ms wall. The full Jacobian fell from 10,341 to 6,075 launches and from 305.08 to 275.98 ms. The booking tangent still takes 5,736 launches and about 231.5 ms. The head reached a fresh-read residual of 2.417e-11 in five Newton steps against ten at the archived base, with all three analytic-oracle ratios within their locked budgets. These are P100 results; no H200 timing or 100-launch verdict is claimed.

The profiler was warmed before tracing and saw GPU events in every row. Device time sums GPU events inside each annotation; wall is a separate median, so overlapping device activity can make device time larger than wall. The stage profiler deliberately calls block_until_ready between independently timed stages. The production newton_step wraps booking, Jacobian, equilibration and LU in one JIT call with no host materialization inside it. A fresh read precedes that call, and the outer driver synchronizes to evaluate fresh residuals and line-search acceptance afterward. Dynamic cut-cell and geometry loops inside JIT still generate many GPU launches.
