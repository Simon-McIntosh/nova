Bank Newton step launch reduction

On titan P100, the warm 550-cell bank Newton step moved from 635.24 ms and 14,427 kernel launches at the base, through 607.25 ms and 9,701 launches after geometry batching, to 957.50 ms and 3,029 launches after booking batching. The final change cuts launches by 79% against base but raises P100 wall by 51%. It does not meet the sub-millisecond or 100-launch target; no H200 measurement was possible while that node was unavailable.

The stage programs were compiled and warmed before tracing; every row produced device events. Device time sums events inside a profiler annotation, while wall time is a separate warm median. Read runs before the Newton step. The outer solver synchronizes after that read to inspect a fresh residual and after the step for line-search acceptance; the compiled newton_step keeps booking, Jacobian assembly, equilibration and LU on device without a host materialization inside the step.

The moving-geometry channels were vectorized at the middle checkpoint. The final book_tangent_channels compacts cut-cell indices on device and integrates them in fixed 64-cell batches inside one static device scan. The P100 case has 99 live cut cells among 550 cells, so two of nine batch positions do work. The tangent is one compiled program but emits 602 kernels; the whole compiled step emits 3,029. The initial all-cell vectorization tried to allocate 63.88 GiB on the 16 GiB P100 and failed. A width sweep showed the P100 tradeoff: width 4 used 233.63 ms and 7,777 tangent launches, width 8 used 403.25 ms and 4,249, width 16 used 509.60 ms and 2,397, width 32 used 571.37 ms and 1,388, and the retained width 64 used 580.29 ms and 602. All sweep tangents matched the width-4 result within 8.1e-19 relative norm.
