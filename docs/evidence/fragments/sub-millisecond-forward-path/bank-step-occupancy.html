Warm Newton step spends most of its wall on the H200

The unsynchronised, one-slice, 550-cell Newton call takes 43.736 ms at the archived base and 29.185 ms with bucketed cut cells, measured sequentially in one reserved H200 allocation. The device trace contains 40.021 and 25.308 ms of activity. Relative to the normal warm wall, those are 91.5% and 86.7% occupied: device work dominates both arms. Both converged with a fresh read, at residuals 2.553e-11 and 3.254e-11, below 1e-10.

The normal-call occupancy estimate exceeds 0.8 in both arms, so the present bottleneck is device work; the bucketed change reduces that work by 14.713 ms and normal wall by 14.552 ms. Profiler overhead is material, especially at the head: it adds 6.161 ms over the normal median and lowers the directly paired trace fraction to 0.716, so that fraction alone would overstate normal host cost. The three largest base kernels are two input_reduce_fusion_9 instances at 3.870 and 3.869 ms, then loop_concatenate_fusion at 2.809 ms. At the head they are two input_reduce_fusion_9 instances at 2.277 and 2.273 ms, then a cuBLAS FP64 GEMM at 1.265 ms (full kernel name in the trace receipt). The 74 predicate/value copies and the largest host command-buffer gaps remain useful follow-up targets once device arithmetic falls further; no Python loop or extra jit boundary is inside this measured step.
