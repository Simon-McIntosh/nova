Same-state measurement

At every accepted iterate, the head supplies x and its fresh-factor Newton direction d. For both revisions, a fresh support_read.read evaluates the residual at x plus and minus h times d. Each h is chosen so that the displacement norm is the table's fraction of the state norm. The full column compares the assembled fresh-geometry Jacobian action with that central difference. Held-read booking compares the assembled Jacobian without moving geometry against a held-read central difference. The read geometry increment subtracts the held response from the fresh response on both sides. Bank flux compares the linear bank rows against a central difference of sampled nodal flux; its fixed exterior field is excluded from the action. Each error is the norm of the action minus its difference divided by the norm of that difference. Every plus and minus read in all 120 rows was valid. The base code was imported from an archive of revision 731c57cae, as the base receipt's module path confirms; both arms used the head trajectory on the same P100.
