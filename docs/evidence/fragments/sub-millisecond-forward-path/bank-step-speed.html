The published diverted 550-cell bank reproduced the warm-step defect at 368.64 ms on the H200. The booking tangent alone takes 120.47 ms; the full Jacobian takes 143.03 ms. Exact condition diagnostics account for much of the 213 ms between the separately timed residual, Jacobian, equilibration, LU and the whole step. A fast option now omits those diagnostics without changing the linear update in the small-matrix control. The algebraic geometry derivative is still under numerical review, so no speed or convergence claim is made for that path.
