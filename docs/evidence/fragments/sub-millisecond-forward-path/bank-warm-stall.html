bank-warm-stall — why a 1e-2 warm-started slice stalls

Measures the first coarse-step slice of the warm stream: the 550-cell diverted certificate at amplitude 1.01, sixteen lanes, one H200. Each lane's every update is observed — the fresh residual, every dyadic trial's scale and residual, the discrete decision (cut cells, normal-form cells, selected fragments, admitted saddles, limited or diverted class) at the seed and at each trial, the block-GMRES condition estimate and iteration count, and whether the factor was rebuilt — and a cold solve of the same drive is run beside it through the same instrument.

Measured on base revision 5f34f03c4a850904ec0432db511adf5ba3ac0133 of nova/equilibrium/support_read.py, in H200 job 1307804 on node 98dci4-gpu-0003 (partition betelgeuse, reservation gpu_0003_grpA), which ran the warm and cold arms of the 1.01 slice; the amplitude-1.0 reference arm is job 1307805 on the same node.

The finding is that the stall is not warm-start specific. At the 1e-2 drive a cold solve of the same slice behaves the same way through the window where either arm makes progress: the two seeds take the same discrete decision and their residuals agree to within 2.4% for the first ten updates. From update 11 the two paths separate — the cold arm sits 83% above the warm arm at update 11 and up to 321% above it at update 19 — and neither arm converges in sixty updates: 0 of 16 warm lanes, 1 of 16 cold. What the warm start does not buy is a better seed: at this drive the residual at the seed is set by the drive change, not by where the seed came from, and both arms then stall short of the tolerance.

How the loop is observed

The staged driver returns only per-lane counts, so three module-level names are wrapped in the positions the driver calls them from: _damped_scale reports every trial state (its per-lane scale is recovered from the trial's displacement against the correction) and the scale the search retained; newton_step reports the factor, the block-GMRES condition estimate, its iteration count and its linear residual; read_batch reports each fresh read's discrete decision. jax.lax.while_loop is wrapped to delimit updates. Because the loop body is traced once, every callback in it carries the same trace-time identity, so update identity comes from execution order — which the driver fixes: the linear solve, then per trial the read that scored it and the trial's own scale and residual, then the retained scale, then the carried update.

The reduced record these two arms produce — every update's incumbent, retained scale, retained residual, trials and discrete decision, for both arms — is committed beside the instrument at scripts/prototypes/bank_warm_stall/results/bank-warm-stall.json; the full event logs, which carry the per-lane masks, stay in the run directory.

Both arms load the same saved slice-0 arrays; the warm arm seeds from the converged slice 0, the cold arm from the fixture's sixteen perturbed seeds. Both solve the same profile, amplitude 1.01, to the same 1e-10 tolerance with the same 60-update ceiling, reuse_factor=False.

Two things in that table are the whole finding. The unit-scale Newton trial raises the fresh residual at every one of the eleven updates tabulated, by between 1.30× and 25.94× — 6.92× at update 1, 25.94× at update 11 — and it never lowers it in any of the lane's sixty warm updates (minimum ratio 1.17); the correction it predicts is therefore larger than the true map supports, by an order of magnitude in most updates. The search then has to spend 2–8 reads halving it, and the accepted scale falls from 1/4 to 1/128 as the state approaches the plateau. And the linear solve itself is exact: its linear residual is at or under 4e-16 with 40 block-GMRES iterations, a condition estimate between 1.8e2 and 2.0e3, and a factor rebuilt from scratch every update. The model is solved precisely; the fresh read says the model is wrong.

Warm against cold on the same slice

The cold arm starts from the fixture's own sixteen perturbed seeds at the same amplitude and runs through the same instrument. It does not converge in three updates at this drive, and it follows the same path through the first ten updates, where the two arms agree to within 2.4%; past that the cold path leaves the warm one and stays above it. A third arm — the same cold seed solved at the slice-1 drive of 1.0 rather than 1.01 — is the reference that shows what the same code does when it works: a seed residual of 0.112, the unit step accepted on every update at 4e-4 then zero, and all sixteen lanes converged in three updates.

The two seeds are not merely similar in residual: they take the same discrete decision — the same cut-cell count, the same eight normal-form cells, the same selected-fragment count — and their first update chooses the same scale and lands within 0.2%. The axis positions stay within 4.2e-5 of each other over the first ten updates, and within 7.9e-4 over all sixty. Through that window the residual at this drive is a property of the drive, not of the seed. A 1% profile-amplitude change displaces the seed from the new root by 1.8, whether the seed is last slice's converged state or a cold perturbed one, and both paths then stall short of the tolerance: from update 11 they diverge, the cold arm reaching 2.273 at update 19 against the warm arm's 1.529 maximum at update 45, and neither converges.

The mechanism

The stall is not a discrete decision flip and it is not a stale factor. The admitted saddle, the diverted class and the eight normal-form cells are unchanged at the seed and at every accepted state, and the cut-cell count moves within a band of 115–120 of 550 cells without ever settling anywhere that would explain a residual jump of 26×; the factor is rebuilt every update and the linear solve is converged to 4e-16. What changes between a trial at scale 1 and the incumbent is the accuracy of the linear model: the fresh residual at the predicted state is between 1.3 and 26 times the incumbent over the tabulated updates — the correction points the right way but carries a magnitude the true map does not support — so the search accepts a quarter or less of it and every update contracts the residual by about 10%, one linear step rather than one Newton step.

The first update at which the warm path stops making progress is update 11: the accepted residual rises from 0.4773 to 0.4813, because the search runs with keep_incumbent=False and retains the smallest-residual valid trial even when it is worse than the incumbent. From there the warm lane climbs to 1.529 at update 45 and ends at 1.271, and the cold lane climbs further, peaking at 2.273 at update 19 and ending at 1.350; both exhaust the sixty-update ceiling.

The quantity that differs from a cold solve at a converging drive is not a decision but the size of the seed displacement. At amplitude 1.0 the seed's residual is 0.112 and the unit Newton step is accepted on every update, contracting to 4e-4 and then to zero; at 1.01 the same seed is displaced to 1.8, and at that distance the step's linearisation over-predicts the correction by an order of magnitude. Section §3 names exactly this failure mode for the fast route — a Jacobian that omits the derivatives of the continuous geometry a fresh read moves (the saddle normal form and the clipped cells' edge intervals) converges a frozen-read equation while the fresh-read residual stalls. The present measurement locates the same signature in the bank step, and shows it is the seed's distance from the root, not the seed's provenance, that selects whether the omission bites.

What would let the warm path converge (a hypothesis for a later product node)

Give the step the sensitivity it is missing at the trial, not only at the incumbent: carry the derivative of the fresh read's continuous geometry — the saddle normal form and the clipped cells' edge intervals — into the Jacobian, which is §3's own requirement for the fast route, and evaluate the linearisation at the midpoint of the intended step rather than at the incumbent. A Jacobian that predicts the trial residual to first order would let scale 1 be accepted from the first update, and one Newton step would then take 1.8 to the tolerance instead of to 1.35. Second, gate the keep_incumbent=False retention so that a trial worse than the incumbent can be retained only when every trial is worse by less than a stated margin, so the sequence cannot accept a rise as update 11 does.
