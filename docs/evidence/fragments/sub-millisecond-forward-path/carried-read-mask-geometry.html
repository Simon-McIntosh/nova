A carried cell is reused only on the geometry its decision was read on

The carried-read reuse mask certified a cell from its signed coefficients alone. A cell whose carrier changed while the same coefficients were retained was reused with its stale fragments, so the reuse decision named a polynomial and never named the polygon it was evaluated on. The topology read now records the carrier it was built from (cell_vertices, cell_vertex_count, cell_centre, cell_pitch), and _fragment_reuse_mask adds _same_carrier_geometry to its conjunction: a cell is reusable only while its live vertices, vertex count, centre and pitch all match that record, whatever its coefficients say.
