The rebuilt ITER bank carries the repaired polygon derivatives

The bank is rebuilt on the repaired kernel and re-certified against the independent source-gradient Richardson reference. At the 64 retained interior nodes of states 220, 333 and 336 the rebuilt plasma-row contraction agrees with that reference inside every component budget, while the previously published bank is wrong in exactly the two derivatives the repair touched: its ψRZ relative error is 0.643, 0.709 and 0.679 at the three states and its ψZZ 0.239, 0.292 and 0.230. The value rows and the coil-field rows are unchanged inside 1e-10; the first-derivative rows are not: among entries of magnitude above 1e-3 they depart by 7.204e-8 at the worst and 4.17e-12 at the median, and 48 of them depart by more than 1e-7 in absolute terms, the largest 1.887e-6 on a row of magnitude 48.10. The added plasma-row Hessian case passes at head with errors 1.8217e-10 and 5.8486e-11 against a 1e-8 budget and fails on the base kernel at 8.4037e-3.

One script certifies both the row builder and the published store

scripts/prototypes/iter_bank/recertify.py (commit 60af91b43d3d00aea7962b2490a5983f79d09a94) reads the retained reference geometry, samples the plasma filaments at the retained points through the production row builder, contracts those rows against the retained moments, and compares the four jet components with the independent source-gradient reference. Two arms are reported separately: ROWS_MODE, where the script evaluates the row builder itself, and BANK_MODE, where it reads the published bank npz and contracts the stored rows. When both a bank and a previous bank are given it also reports UNCHANGED, the relative departure of the value, first-derivative and coil rows from the previously published bank.

The row builder path and the stored bank are independent instruments: the stored rows are what the ITER operator bank actually serves, and the builder path proves the script's own contraction. Three defects were repaired while bringing the script up, all in the script and none in product source: the content-store manifest is absent beside a retained bank copy, so the bank is read from its npz (commit 5edcb1df8a62e63609c092044bd4972ecd911a10); the comparison reduces over the retained nodes, so each state yields one relative error rather than one per node (commit 0a73119c5aa485f3f05e4292bd2b71dbf3c3d2ed); and the longdouble reference is cast to double before the receipt is written (commit 366155652077a1cf979a0b2d8fa2c124c8e7d3d1).

It reports three different numbers, and only the middle one is the 7.20e-8 that the table quotes. The unfiltered per-entry maximum relative departure is 7.49e7, at cell 527, node 9, moment 1, dpsi/dZ: the entry's magnitude there is 9.44e-24 and its absolute departure 7.07e-16, so that number is the ratio of two rounding residues, and 1,612 of the 5,932,656 entries have exactly zero magnitude. The receipt's own first_derivative_rows maximum, 5.548857e-1, is a node-aggregated norm over the 64 retained nodes and is dominated by the same near-zero denominators; over all 1,772 active nodes the aggregated norm reads 0.1726. Restricted to entries whose magnitude exceeds 1e-3 — the 603 entries where the ratio means something — the maximum is 7.204e-8, the median 4.17e-12, and 119 entries exceed 1e-10 (53 dpsi/dR and 66 dpsi/dZ, the counts this fragment quoted before the script existed). Job 1325117 left EXIT=2 because it was run while the script still carried the 1e-7 absolute stop line as its default, and 48 entries cross it; the ruling of 2026-11-18 withdraws that line, so the boundary is now opt-in and the script run with no boundary exits 0 over the same statistics (job 1327698, logs/derive-rows-v2.log).

Those 603 filtered entries are not all near-zero: 48 of them depart by more than 1e-7 in absolute terms, and the largest absolute departure on a large-magnitude row is 1.887e-6, on a dpsi/dR entry of magnitude -48.10 at cell 7, node 101, moment 1. Its relative departure, 3.923273659969061e-08, is smaller than the 7.204e-8 maximum, so the largest rows move by a smaller fraction than the small entries do; but in absolute size a value of 48.1 moves by 1.9e-6, which is the figure the lead's materiality condition names. Both archives store rows as float64 of shape (2048, 558, 3, 6) over 1,772 active nodes, so the 5,932,656 entries are 1,772 nodes by 558 cells by 3 moments by 2 derivative components.

The 1,612 exactly-zero entries are not a rounding artefact of the comparison and they are not cell corners: they are far-field quadrupole terms that the previous kernel cancels to zero. derive_rows.py --zero-entries lists every one of them with its value in each bank, and evaluates the same derivative directly from two kernels — this tree's at 681a6ecd2 (nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py, byte-identical at the repair head) and the git base at 02997905193b, extracted into the job's /tmp and run in its own subprocess. Job 1328165 on one P100 of titan (two earlier CPU attempts died in the derivative program's compile, 1327927 at 64G and 1328015 at 96G with MaxRSS 100.5 GB) reports 1,612 entries over 1,023 distinct nodes and 337 cells, receipt receipts/zero-entries.json, log logs/zero-entries6.log. All 1,612 are dpsi/dZ: the previous bank stores exactly 0.0 there, and so does the base kernel at 1,577 of them, the other 35 coming out at most 3.079120140989685e-16 absolute — zero to machine precision. The repaired kernel returns values up to 8.457795039488852e-06 (the same zero_magnitude_max_abs_delta the earlier receipt named), and this tree's kernel evaluated at those points agrees with the rebuilt bank to 2.5667713804607487e-15 absolute, inside the 1e-10 budget by five orders of magnitude.

Geometry says why they were zeros. The distance from each node to the boundary of the cell it is evaluated against is at least 0.149, median 2.676, maximum 7.156, and the nearest-vertex distance is identical at every entry, so no entry sits on an edge or a corner; 1,607 of the 1,612 nodes are evaluated against a cell that is not their own. The largest new value, 8.457795039488852e-06, is at node 1321 (which owns cell 434) against cell 3, moment 1, 6.141 away. These are the long-range pieces of the polygonal integral that the base kernel summed to exactly zero and the repaired kernel does not. The old zeros therefore come from the base kernel's own arithmetic, not from masking in the bank's build; a first run of the diagnostic that did not slice the scan to the 1,772 active nodes reported 925,660 "zeros" from the padded rows, which is why the mode now slices to the active count.

The first-derivative rows do not reproduce the previous bank inside 1e-10: 53 of the 306 ψR entries and 66 of the 297 ψZ entries whose magnitude exceeds 1e-3 depart further, with a worst case of 7.204033878373014e-08 at cell 502, node 1538, moment 2, where the dpsi/dR entry has magnitude 4.148e-2 and absolute departure 2.989e-9. This is reported as measured rather than absorbed. Neither bank is wrong against the only independent instrument available here: both contract to the reference inside 1.3e-12 at the retained nodes, because the departing cells carry no booked moment. The rebuilt bank is thus certified where it is tested and differs from its predecessor where nothing measures. The two builder arms that would have separated the store from the kernel at two retained nodes, 1324664 and 1324665, were both killed by the CPU allocation at 96 GB (EXIT=137) during the row builder's compile, so the departure is measured but not attributed between the device and the kernel. The row builder is the same expression at both revisions and the only independent instrument available at these cells is the previous bank itself, so the departure is recorded as an open question rather than explained.

On every entry of magnitude above 1e-3 the rebuilt rows depart from the current bank by at most 7.204e-8, with a median of 4.17e-12; in absolute terms the largest departure is 1.887e-6, on dpsi/dR entries of magnitude around 48.
