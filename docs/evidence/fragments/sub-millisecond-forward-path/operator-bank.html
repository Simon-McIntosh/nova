The support operators as one bank per shape bucket

support_read.SupportOperands was extended, not replaced. It carries a static bucket, pads rows, exterior and nodes up to that bucket with zeros, keeps the machine's own count in a traced active_nodes field, and applies the bank through bank_program, a minimal traced matvec. The bucket enters forward._request_compilation_cache_key through ForwardProfile.support_bank, so it selects a program inside the existing compilation cache rather than a second one. The bank is published and reloaded through the existing content store (write_bank / read_bank). At the diverted single-null certificate with 550 realised cells (1,741 nodes, bucket 2048) on one H200 the bank builds in 94.5–96.1 s over three runs, reloads in 0.114 s, reproduces the analytic jet at its kernel-backed moments to 2.9×10-13 relative, costs zero further compilations for a second geometry in the same bucket and one for a geometry in the next bucket, and occupies 162.3 MB of operators, leaving 100.7 GiB free for a batch of 16 slices.

Build and reload

The done-when's 1 s bound is the load, not the build: the bank builds in 94.5–96.1 s and the published bank reloads in 0.114 s, so the reload is the measured quantity and it sits an order of magnitude inside the bound.

The bank was built by scripts/prototypes/operator_bank/measure.py on one reserved H200, from the same certificate route scripts/prototypes/read_derivatives/measure.py uses: tests.equilibrium.test_topology_read._analytic_inputs("diverted") for the analytic state, _realised_hex_geometry(wall, 550) for the mesh, benchmarks.solovev_certificate._case("diverted-single-null") for the source, and build_jet_matrix for the dense rows.

The bank contracts the kernel-backed booked moments of the certificate and is compared with the analytic jet at every node, entry by entry. The worst entry is the mixed second derivative at 2.9×10-13 relative, four orders inside the 1e-10 clause.

Compilations per bucket

The program is applied three times on one device: the bank itself, a second geometry of the same bucket (perturbed operator rows, same shape), and a geometry of the next bucket (4096). Compilations are read from the jit cache size, which is the instrument that observes a reuse; the persistent cache file count rose with the different bucket too.

The negative control captures one operator as a closure constant. In the same H200 job it compiles once more on a same-bucket geometry change (closure_same_bucket_new_compiles: 1) where the traced bank compiles nothing (traced_same_bucket_new_compiles: 0). The same mutation carried into a scratch copy of tests/test_operator_bank_compile.py makes both reuse tests fail (assert 2 == 1, assert 3 == 2) with EXIT=1.

The bucket reaches that cache through one declared place: ForwardProfile.support_bank carries the machine's bank and _request_compilation_cache_key reads its static bucket. A profile with no bank keys None; two banks padding to different buckets key differently; two geometries in one bucket key identically, which is why the second geometry costs no compilation. tests/test_operator_bank_compile.py::test_the_compile_key_carries_the_bank_bucket pins the seam, and scripts/prototypes/operator_bank/run_compile_key_control.sh runs that test against the pre-repair key, where it fails on assert None == 64 with EXIT=1 (logs/negative_control_compile_key.log).
