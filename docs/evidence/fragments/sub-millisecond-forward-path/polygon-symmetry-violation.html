The moment channel's two row families are not a derivative pair

Every violation lives in the moment-1 column, and inside that column the booked Z gradient is not the Z derivative of the booked flux. The uniform column is right, and an independent route proves it: at the 43 tested entries the packed flux row agrees with polygon_analytic_greens to 7.293e-13 absolute and the packed field row to 7.214e-14. Inside that same program, the moment-1 column's flux row is flat in height while its Z gradient reaches 6.06230122870705e-6, and on the up-down plane a mirror-symmetric cell forces that gradient to vanish. Five kernel variants — the corner stencil, both corner bands, and the stable primal — leave the group-A values unchanged or change them without removing them, so no kernel branch is producing these values: the defect is the relation between two row families the booking treats as a pair.

The smallest change, and what it costs

No kernel change removes these values, and the arms above are the reason: the four corner/band/stencil branches are excluded by construction, and the one flag that reaches the value row changes the violation without removing it. The change belongs where the two row families are formed. The shipped route this file's own docstring points at already shows the construction: polygon_greens forms the field by dividing a flux gradient by 2πr, so its field row and its flux row are one object by construction — which is why the uniform column agrees across the two routes in every digit that matters here.

The smallest supported change is therefore to book the moment-1 and moment-2 Z-gradient rows from the Z derivative of the matching flux row, replacing the independently built field rows for those two moment rows with the same construction the uniform row already uses. Group A then vanishes by parity at the plane, group B's booked value becomes its own central difference by construction, and the uniform column is unchanged.

Cost is bounded by the mechanism: this replaces a field-row construction with a derivative of a row already present, so it adds no program to the compiled set. Estimate: instruction count within a few percent of head, cold compile wall unchanged within noise, to be measured if the change is implemented. The measured anchors are the production row's 1.565208× instructions and 1.871670× cold compile against main, both inside the unchanged 2.3 limits of polygon-stable-evaluator.html.

Two limits are stated plainly. This is not a kernel-branch repair: the report locates the defect in the relation between two row families and recommends the construction that makes it hold. Second, the group-B disagreement means the current moment channel's field rows are wrong off the plane as well, so a change that only zeroes the plane would hide the violation rather than fix it; that is why the recommendation is the derivative construction and not a plane mask.

Repairs to the record's own instruments

Five defects in these prototypes were repaired, each visible in the provenance above. The flux identity's loop broke on the first record whose arm was not psi, and the plan emits a direct record first, so the value this record quoted was the loop's initial zero rather than a measurement: it now skips with continue, returns the maximum with a count of the ladder records it examined, and raises rather than returning when that count is short of the plan or zero.
