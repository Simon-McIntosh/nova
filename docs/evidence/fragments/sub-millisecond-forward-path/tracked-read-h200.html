tracked-read-h200 — one cell rule, the H200 wall and the trial-state error

The cell selection has one owner

support_read.stationary_cells re-implemented the nearest-generator cell selection that fixed_points._CachedField._location already owned, so the cell a tracked read recorded and the cell the cell-local Bell fit evaluated in were two rules that could drift apart. The rule now lives once, in fixed_points.nearest_cell, vectorised over any leading position dimensions: a single point returns one index and a batch of points positions returns points indices. _location, BellField.value and the tracked read all call it, and stationary_cells is deleted. tests/test_tracked_read.py passes 4 of 4 over the change.

Which stage carries the trial-state error

Every reference is a direct Newton solve on the analytic flux of the certificate state. A second solve runs on the field the read differentiates — the Bell interpolant over the nodal jets it contracts — so the distance from the read to that field is the solve's own error and the distance from that field to the analytic extremum is the derivative source's.

The shared store publishes the diverted certificate's bank only (receipt.json reads "kind": "diverted"), so binding the limited case to it fed that case another state's field and the read reported invalid with a boundary-flux error of 259 in span units. A case the store does not hold is built from its own rows; the numbers here are each certificate measured on the field it books.

The stage is fixed by the iteration count. Both points terminate after two Newton iterations with status=0 on a position tolerance of 1e-10 of the pitch, so the read's position is the stationary point of its derivative source to far better than the budgets above; the distance the table reports is that source's distance from the analytic flux, not a solve that stopped short. Widening the iteration budget cannot move it, and the seed cannot either — a converged Newton solve has forgotten its seed. What carries these numbers is the column of booked moments at the trial state, the sampled-field booking the fast route consumes, together with the Bell interpolant that turns nodal jets into a field. Both are upstream of the read.

At the converged state the same instrument lands at 3.88e-6 m on the axis and 5.28e-6 m on the X-point with the boundary flux inside 1.5e-5 of the span, so the read reproduces the analytic state it is handed. The trial state is the one that misses, by 1.3 and 22 times the budgets, and no change inside the read reaches it: the repair belongs to the trial booking.

The gate runs as tests/test_tracked_read_accuracy.py over the four states and reports 3 failed, 5 passed: the limited trial axis at 4.12e-3 m against 2.00e-3, the diverted trial X-point at 8.82e-4 against 6.81e-4, and the diverted trial boundary flux at 2.21e-3 of the span against 1e-4. Both converged states pass every clause. That is the recorded defect reproduced on the H200, and it is left failing rather than relaxed: the budgets are the section's, and a test that stopped asserting them would report a read that meets them.

The warm wall on the H200

One job on 98dci4-gpu-0003, an NVIDIA H200 NVL, at the 550-cell diverted state. The bank is loaded through support_read.read_bank from /work/projects/imas_gpu/sophelio/nova-assembled-step-bank (digest sha256:6ebe02e0…, 1,741 nodes, 162.2 MB), so nothing is re-derived from source jets. Every row is the minimum of five warm repeats of one compiled step:

The steady read is 1.225 ms, six times the section's 0.2 ms bound. The launches table says where they go: the stationary solve is 55 launches and 0.634 ms, and the remaining 750 launches carry the other 0.591 ms, over 21,000 of the 24,178 HLO instructions. The remainder is not the fit — a steady step pays no whole-mesh fit — and the stages it contains are read from the code rather than timed separately: the whole-mesh containment scan topology._inside_cells evaluated at each tracked point, which tests every cell's edges for each point, the Hessian eigenvalues that classify it, and the tracking predicate. Even a perfect fusion of all of it leaves the solve's own 0.634 ms, more than three times the bound, so the bound is not reachable by fusing the read: the warm-started solve has to get cheaper, and that is §3's subject. The stage split is left as measured for the solve and structural for the remainder, which is stated rather than presented as a measurement it is not.

The rebuild row is reported as measured but the instrument is suspect: 35.65 ms against 1.649 ms reported for the same call on a P100 is twenty-two times slower on the faster device, where the steady row is only 1.25 times faster. A number that moves the wrong way by an order of magnitude is a claim about the instrument before it is a claim about the code, so this row is not used to price a rebuild step.
