tracked-read — a read that tracks its stationary points

support_read.read now returns each tracked point's hex cell, the limited/diverted ownership and a rebuild counter, and rebuilds the connected-domain decision only when one of the first two changes. The two whole-mesh costs §2 names are paid by a rebuild step alone: the own-node fit over every node (fixed_points.sampled_coefficient, called from topology.read_at_stationary_points) and the whole-mesh Bell rebuild (fixed_points.field_from_jets). A steady-state step reuses the previous TopologyRead.

The refinement itself is unchanged and already cell-local: the warm-started stationary-point Newton in fixed_points.solve differentiates fixed_points._CachedField, which contracts only the selected triangle's rows and builds the Bell coefficients for that one triangle. That is the cell-local fit §2 asks for, read from the coupling operand; no jets are averaged across cells. The lead's reuse map names stencil_nulls._quadratic_fit_matrices for this fit and the implementation reuses the existing one instead — recorded under outside_scope in the manifest for the coordinator to rule on.

The gate

tests/test_tracked_read.py (4 passed, 112.57 s at head):

  • the first read rebuilds once and the steady state rebuilds nothing, carrying the same cells and passing the validity check;
  • a field change that leaves the X-point in its cell (a uniform linear term moving it ≲ 0.1 pitch) reports rebuilt=False and leaves the rebuild counter unchanged;
  • a field change that moves the X-point out of its cell (cell 15 → 23, both stationary points converged, read valid) reports rebuilt=True and increments the counter by exactly one;
  • both stationary points land on the analytic reference within the locked precision budget (5e-4 of the minor radius).

Negative control

Averaging the cell-local fit across each cell's nodes in a scratch copy puts the axis 0.3049 m from the analytic extremum against a 7.49e-4 m budget. The accuracy test fails there (EXIT=1) and passes at head, so the gate discriminates: it is the cell-local fit, not the averaging, that carries the accuracy.
