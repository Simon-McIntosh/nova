Evidence · terminal equilibrium geometry

MAST bank terminal-flux overlays

All twelve regenerated bank rows are retained in a four-by-three comparison and as individual figures. Every panel uses one physical contour-level array for Nova and EFIT, includes the Nova binding flux as a heavy solid contour when the terminal map exists, and shows the EFIT LCFS separately as a heavy dashed curve. EFIT flux contours were read and drawn for 12/12 panels; Nova terminal contours were available for 11/12.

Authority and reading contract

The row identities, terminal residuals, termination reasons, active-set trip counts, class verdicts, LCFS distances, geometry markers and Nova flux maps come from the secured regeneration outputs current-bank.json and current-operands.npz under ~/.config/reckon/crew/reports/nova/bank-regeneration-raw-20260902/. Their SHA-256 identities are 8e3ab989b884aba9e5b2a679a2e1fa7f5f604824cb5880327ae1e92c58bf8165 and 42adaac7151f0c16eb48c6220e97ee7e2e6c20a9f1f7b4599726bbb148903d90, respectively.

The EFIT maps are fresh read-only loads of efm/psirz, gridr and gridz from the same MAST shot catalogue used by the bank producer: /work/projects/imas_gpu/mast/level1/shots/<shot>.zarr. The catalogue map is already in Wb/rad. Nova carries total poloidal flux in Wb, so the renderer divides Nova's map and binding value by exactly 2π. It applies the EFIT map's 1st-to-99th-percentile level array, augmented with the Nova binding value, verbatim to both maps. There is no per-map rescaling, normalization or additive gauge alignment.

EFIT is the independent magnetics-fitted referee used by this bank, not physical ground truth. A close overlay demonstrates agreement with that reconstruction; it does not prove uniqueness or experimental truth. The heavy EFIT LCFS comes from the retained referee geometry rather than being re-derived from the raster. The heavy Nova curve is the recorded terminal binding-flux level.

What the overlays show

The converged mixed controls at 21978/35 and 22086/43 preserve the EFIT diverted class and show closely interleaved solid and dashed core contours. Their available LCFS RMS distances are 33.7 mm and 29.2 mm. The 21985/51 mixed arm also reaches machine-level residual, but its assembled closed boundary is unavailable, so the contour agreement may be inspected while an LCFS distance must not be claimed.

The two explicitly settled rows, 21985/51 pure and 22086/43 pure, retain coherent terminal flux fields rather than numerical debris: their Nova and EFIT contours remain comparable across the vessel, but the bank cannot assemble a valid closed Nova boundary for a scalar LCFS score. This is the spatial face of the stagnation result—the terminal map exists and has recognizable nested structure, while the accepted state does not deliver a convergence-qualified boundary.

The strongest geometry failures are not the lowest residual plateaus. The 21986/46 pure and both 21989/55 arms terminate on the iteration budget in the wrong limited class; their available LCFS RMS separations span 182–236 mm and the heavy Nova binding contour visibly departs from the EFIT LCFS. Conversely, 21978/35 pure cycles rather than converges but retains the diverted class and the smallest available diagnostic RMS distance, 19.9 mm. Thus terminal convergence, class correctness and EFIT shape distance are distinct evidence axes; none can stand in for the other.

Reproduction

From the Nova repository root, run:

UV_PROJECT_ENVIRONMENT=~/Code/nova/.venv PYTHONPATH="$PWD" \
  uv run --no-sync python benchmarks/bank_efit_overlay.py \
  ~/.config/reckon/crew/reports/nova/bank-regeneration-raw-20260902

The script validates the twelve-row JSON/NPZ identity alignment, requires one common governed wall across populated rows, reads each distinct EFIT shot/slice once, emits the four-by-three grid and twelve row PNGs, and prints explicit EFIT/Nova contour coverage counts. It performs no equilibrium solve and writes only the figure directory.
