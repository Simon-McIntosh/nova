Evidence · Nova forward Grad-Shafranov versus EFIT on MAST

MAST EFIT match

The production current-constrained Nova forward solve is compared frame by frame with the EFIT label on six MAST efm/psirz frames. The two error sources are kept in separate columns and read independently: a gauge-aligned solve-label RMS fraction of the label span, and the banked EFIT label's own Grad-Shafranov inconsistency floor. Every solve-label fraction sits below the 0.0116 floor, yet only one of the six frames converges, so the match does not pass as accuracy evidence until the solver stalls are closed.

The two error sources read independently

The two columns are not one measurement and must not be compared on one scale with the DIII-D column of the sibling document. Each is a different quantity:

  • solve against label — gauge-aligned solve-label RMS fraction of label span, the RMS of the gauge-aligned difference between the production solve and the EFIT label over the complete registered MAST solve grid after additive gauge alignment, expressed as a fraction of the EFIT label peak-to-peak span;
  • label GS inconsistency — EFIT label under unchanged Nova interior operator, the relative interior Grad-Shafranov sup of the EFIT label at stride one, expressed as a fraction of the interior drive sup. This is the independent banked floor; the label itself is not an exact Grad-Shafranov solution, so a solve can sit below this floor and still not be a certified match.

The per-frame pass rule is solver converged and solve_against_label.value <= label_gs_inconsistency.value. A frame fails if either arm fails; the two arms are deliberately not averaged.

Frame-by-frame

Verdict

1 of 6 MAST frames passes; 5 of 6 fail on convergence while sitting below the label floor. The accuracy half is encouraging — every solve-label RMS fraction except 21983/35 is between 0.0034 and 0.0072 against a 0.0116 floor — but it is not claimable as certified accuracy while the solves do not converge. The per-frame difference field is the same up-down antisymmetric vertical mode the solver-convergence-regression forensics identified, at about 1e-2 of span. The receipt's aggregate verdict stands: FAIL_ONE_OR_MORE_FRAMES_ABOVE_LABEL_GS_FLOOR_OR_UNCONVERGED.
