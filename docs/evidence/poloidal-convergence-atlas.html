Poloidal convergence atlas

Every committed frame of the MAST twelve-row bank and the five-frame DIII-D demonstration route, plus the 27079 limited-class collapse frames and one reversed-current-block slice, as one poloidal scene: full-map flux contours, the governed wall, the magnetic axis, every qualified X-point with the out-of-vessel members distinguished rather than dropped, the chosen boundary beside the EFIT reference surface, and the shadowed wall segments drawn as shadow. Low and high plasma current, converged and non-converged, closed and missing boundaries, and the lower-centre-column anchor are all in the grid rather than curated away.

Grid and coverage

17 panels, two machines, two current levels, one grid. The corrected bank reads every committed row as diverted (each row agrees with EFIT on class), so the class axis carries the honest corrected reading; the four rows whose read returned no closed boundary are marked unclassified rather than hidden. MAST low current is 0.80 MA (low) (21978, 21983) and high current 0.93 MA (high) (21985, 21986, 21989, 22086); DIII-D low current is 1.02 to 1.28 MA and high 1.6 to 1.63 MA. One frame converged to machine precision (22086/43 mixed, residual 1.74e-14); 22086/43 pure did not (residual 4.79e-4, active set settled), and the rest are the honest non-converged states the sprint chased.

Showcased failures rather than a curated set. The non-converging frame (21986/46 pure, residual 5.9e-3), the no-closed-boundary frame (DIII-D 00000c4a7b:179, four such frames in the route), the centre-column-anchor frame (21983/35, wall anchor at R 0.28, Z -1.64), and the converged healthy reference (22086/43 mixed) are tagged in their captions. A frame from the reversed-current block, where every slice fails axis admission for campaign-sign reasons, is not in the committed poloidal corpus: no reversed-current flux raster exists in this tree, so it is recorded as a follow-on rather than fabricated.

DIII-D block — five-frame demonstration route

The DIII-D demo read returned no convergence-qualified diverted root on all five frames, and the committed operands persist nulls and geometry but not the flux field. The poloidal contours on these panels are therefore the EFIT reference psirz raster read from the source parquet (65 by 65), with Nova's admitted axis, X-points, wall point and boundary overlaid: the comparison the atlas exists to expose.

JT-60SA block — a five-unit wall drawn as five structures

JT-60SA's machine description carries five wall units: one closed vessel outline (89 vertices) and four open strike-point first-wall structures (6, 6, 10 and 8 vertices), read losslessly as a typed unit collection. The landed unit-aware painters draw each unit as its own polyline — the closed vessel solid, every open unit dashed, with no joining edge invented between units — so this panel is the visual proof that a multi-unit wall renders as five structures rather than collapsing into a single polygon.

Data, method and deviations

Panels are rendered from the committed operands of docs/figures/topology-visual-corroboration (MAST mast-topology-operands.npz at base revision e6a83802; DIII-D diiid-topology-operands.npz) plus the DIII-D source parquet's EFIT psirz raster, by benchmarks/poloidal_convergence_atlas.py. The render ran on the debug partition with a per-job TMPDIR; measured 0.593 s per frame mean and 8.34 s cold-start max over 17 panels. Machine-readable receipt: docs/figures/null-identification-authority/convergence-atlas/convergence-atlas.json. Renderer revision 52ca3c4e5a.

Two deviations from the painter set are deliberate and stated. First, nova.media.poloidal.draw_nulls is not used for the X-points because its containment filter drops out-of-vessel candidates, and showing that population is the atlas's purpose; the dedicated null painter here keeps every qualified candidate and draws the wall exterior with a distinct marker and a per-panel tally. Second, the production shadow rule is proposed & private_wall, and the committed store persists neither the per-wall-node private flux nor a private-flux raster on the MAST rows, so the dark shadow is drawn only where the committed domain labels carry private-flux cells and the broad light band is labelled as the saddle-height footprint. The qualified X set is recovered from the raw 30-slot candidate table by the visual proxy of lying within the operator's X-qualification radius of the admitted or EFIT saddle, because the committed store persists only the raw table and the single admitted selection.

Supplement — the limited-class collapse and the reversed-current slice

The banked atlas rows all read diverted after the connectivity correction; the limited-class collapse is a different population. These frames predate the committed bank operands, so no raster existed for them: the rows of the 27079 limited-anchor session (rows 11 to 17) and one slice of the reversed-current block were solved for this document on the debug partition with at most four cores and a per-job TMPDIR, their flux rasters persisted beside the panels, and drawn in the same atlas grammar: contours, wall, magnetic axis, admitted X and wall points, the stored selected boundary, and the two candidate anchors the limited-anchor census tracks — node 10 (R 0.5649, Z 1.7281, the upper re-entrant notch) and node 25 (R 0.5649, Z -1.7281), drawn as blue diamonds per docs/figures/playable-forward-solve/limited-anchor/limited-anchor-candidates.csv.

Rows 15, 16 and 17 are the collapse frames (geometry mask closed, boundary shrunk to a fraction of the vessel); rows 11 to 14 are the unconverged controls the census keeps. One reversed-current slice (shot 22475) is drawn as it terminates — axis admission fails for the campaign-sign reason.

Measured 0.063 s per frame render (max 0.09 s) over 8 supplement panels; the solve wall is dominated by the one-off reduced-Newton compile.

Coverage limits and follow-ons

The banked atlas was extended by the eight-panel supplement: the 27079 limited-class collapse frames and one reversed-current-block slice are now drawn in the same grammar (see the supplement section above), with their flux rasters persisted beside the panels. What remains outside the committed corpus is the pre-correction limited reads of 21986/46 and 21989/55 as flux maps (they survive only as classifications), and a converged positive-termination frame of the reversed-current block — the block terminates in the all-NaN axis-admission failure the supplement shows by design. Both would need a targeted solve that persists the raster; they are left as follow-ons rather than fabricated.
