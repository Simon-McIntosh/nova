The requested “hex cells with clipping” contained two premises that the production geometry corrects. On these tensor axes, the six shared-edge support-line intersections from _raster_hex_partition_geometry collapse pairwise to four unique vertices, so each physical Voronoi cell is a rectangle. The hexagonal structure belongs to the six-neighbour half-offset adjacency graph, not to an area tessellation; every panel therefore overlays the wall-admissible production shared-edge segments that make that connectivity visible. Hulling those segment endpoints would invent six-sided areas that overlap neighbours by 49.093 mm radially and 23.810 mm vertically, so no hexagon-shaped fill is drawn.

The governed material decision is reproduced by the production _points_inside_polygon symbol at cell centres and checked against the cached labels. The solver performs no partial-cell clipping: it admits or excludes each whole rectangle according to its centre. The wall is therefore drawn over full rectangles, including excluded-material cells that visibly straddle it; no polygon-wall intersections are invented. The centre/label wall check is exact for 14 of 16 populated rows. 2 retained-failure rows carry a mismatch, named per row in the figure ledger: MAST 21985/51 pure, MAST 21985/51 mixed.

The coloured raster is a connectivity partition, not a catalogue of current-cell types. Purple is the exact PRIVATE_FLUX component: closed-flux raster nodes disconnected from the primary O-point by the X-point flood-fill cut. Pale blue is the axis-connected component, grey-green is the common-SOL component, and pale grey is excluded material. The solve consumes this partition as a private mask and as the selector for continuation domains. It still resolves current by evaluating the pressure and field-function gradients at each cell's normalised flux; outside the closed boundary, OPEN_DOMAINS and gradient_tail supply the declared continuations of p-prime and FF-prime. It never consumes a per-cell “type”. The selected wall marker is Nova's governed closest plasma-wall candidate; it is shown even when the topology class is diverted and it does not bind the LCFS.

The flux raster and plasma-current mesh are different grids. The panels can reproduce the former exactly from the committed operands. Nova's production current mesh is a separate, half-offset tiling of genuine hexagonal cells constructed by PolyGrid; its material polygons are atomised by AtomicCellMesh.from_cells, and AtomicCellMesh.traced_clip traces the separatrix through those polygons from signed flux at the shared atomic nodes. The committed topology caches contain neither the production current_cell_polygons nor atomic_node_signed_flux. A truthful traced-clip layer therefore cannot be reconstructed here: selecting an unstored tiling resolution or deriving node signs from the plotted boundary would manufacture geometry or flux. Those two fields must be serialized during the next bank regeneration before the current-mesh layer is published.

Deferred primary-selection variants

Primary-versus-alternate domain variants require cache fields that are not present: per_cell_flux_values and per_candidate_domain_labels. Adding them is a cache-schema migration followed by a full seventeen-row bank regeneration, measured at roughly one day of lane time, so it is deferred to the next scheduled bank regeneration rather than triggering a solve campaign here. The existing synthetic-geometry sensitivity measurements remain available at docs/figures/wall-height-shadow-safety/metrics.json.

Deferred plasma-current mesh layer

The genuine current mesh cannot be substituted by the flux raster. Publication requires the exact production current_cell_polygons and the atomic_node_signed_flux consumed by AtomicCellMesh.traced_clip. Neither is present in these committed operand caches. Bank both fields alongside the topology operands during the next scheduled extraction regeneration; then the traced core/common support polygons can be rendered without a solve or a geometric inference.

Reproduction

Run UV_PROJECT_ENVIRONMENT=~/Code/nova/.venv PYTHONPATH="$PWD" uv run --no-sync python docs/figures/topology-visual-corroboration/generate_topology_visuals.py. The committed generation.log records the successful run and ends with EXIT_MARKER=0.
