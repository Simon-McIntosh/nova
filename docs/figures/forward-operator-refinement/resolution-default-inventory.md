The inventory boundary is every production or reference-lane default that controls (a) plasma/source discretisation, (b) the structured flux lattice or rectangular boundary used by the forward solve, or (c) a Biot target cloud consumed by that solve. Algorithmic iteration counts, contour *level* counts, profile-coordinate samples, plotting-only grids, and explicit one-off test arguments are not spatial-resolution defaults and are excluded.

## Self-consistency verdict

The inventory contains **28 rows: 27 numeric/`None` defaults and one explicit no-default lattice owner**. Five sites require direct alignment before the reference lane can be called self-consistent: `Frame.dplasma`, `Frame.dshell`, `SUITE_CELLS`, `REGISTERED_GRID_STRIDE`, and the 33-per-side `pseudo_wall` default once the lattice returns to native resolution. The remaining non-pass rows are either duplicate authorities to consolidate or geometry-dependent settings that need their own convergence evidence; they are not silently promoted to passes.

The coherent forward-reference bundle is therefore:

- one canonical plasma request, `dplasma=-2100`, demonstrated as 2,210 realised cells;
- one native stored-map solve lattice, stride 1 over 65×65 axes (4,225 nodes);
- a matching 65-samples-per-side pseudo-wall (260 boundary nodes);
- a levelset target of 4,225 when contour extraction is judged on that stored map, while generic Biot owners remain fail-closed at `None`;
- component meshes (`dcoil`, `dshell`) expressed as per-object physical spacings or separately converged counts, never copied from the global plasma count.

This bundle makes the area-grid densities comparable without pretending that a boundary sample, a coil filament, a passive-loop element, and a plasma cell are the same unit. Its dominant cost is the 33×33 → 65×65 solve-lattice change: 3.88× target rows and about 15.1× for a dense lattice-to-lattice block. The wall change is only 1.97× in its two wall-target blocks; generic optional Biot targets stay at zero cost until a consumer explicitly enables them.
