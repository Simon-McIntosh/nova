Anchor treatment bisect on MAST 21978/35

The banked pair on 21978/35 pure is the load-bearing row of the drift campaign: the bank producer revision fae50f15 converges to 3.857344474796854e-16 while the current tree returns 2.4506488565449e-03 under active_set_cycle_detected, a ratio of 6.35e12. The paired probe located the first differing stage at the operand partition_structure. This bisect asks the next question in that chain: is the traced-anchor hook — the arrangement under which the three declared anchors enter the operator partition as traced member data rather than as host constants — the carrier of that cycle?

Verdict: no. The traced-anchor hook is exonerated on the drifting arm. On 21978/35 pure the two anchor treatments terminate at the same residual 2.4506488565449e-03 under the same termination reason active_set_cycle_detected, while the treatment demonstrably moved the operand partition: the hook answers with the three declared anchors in one arm and with none in the other, the partition loses three leaves (76 to 73), the partition structure and value digests both move, and the cached geometry identity changes. A hook that changes the partition on four independent instruments and leaves the terminal residual bit-identical is not the mechanism. The drift therefore lies downstream of the anchor store, among the solve-path commits below.

The two treatments differ observably and the terminal state does not

One H200 allocation, one identity, four arms: each treatment is solved under the pure policy overrides and then under the mixed class. The traced arm is the tree as it ships, with _dynamic_extra_names() answering the three anchor names. The constant arm overrides that hook to return the empty tuple from the driver for the duration of the operand construction and the solve, so the three anchors join geometry["specialisation"] as host constants exactly as they did when they did not exist as hook-named extras. Nothing under nova/ changes in either arm.

The flag constant_matches_bank_producer_stage=false holds on both classes and is the honest limit of the treatment: the constant arm is still a pytree with 73 leaves, so it equalises the anchors' placement, never the producer's operand object, whose partition_values is empty.

What moved inside the cycle even though the terminal state did not

The trip trace digests differ between the treatments on the pure class (ce027543956b6dc6 traced against ee9401feed45ec0c constant) and so do the terminal flux map (77482bce5b1e8ff6 against 933fb3009abaf6d1), the axis (2d7be2d85adc22d4 against da515b61c154c1b9) and the selected saddle (09fc087a8535c2e5 against 70a29d2af29b36ed), while the residual is identical. That combination is a statement about the instrument as much as about the solve: a cycled fixed point's residual magnitude is set by the cycle it entered, not by the round-off path that carried it there, so an identical residual does not certify an identical state. What it does certify is that the hook does not decide which cycle is entered, which is the question asked. Four digests are shared across the two arms and are the invariant spine of the comparison: class_margin 9e09211058c77fcd, radius 39edd644e2a70398, height 3a66374813f2f90c, wall a8294a08dee65e76 and the profile-support digest 5683f88ca97c076e, which the arm also checks under its own profile_support_invariant flag.

What remains: the difference this bisect did not remove

The producer revision fae50f15 (fix(banked-reads): catch malformed reference points with the tuple except form, 2026-09-08) does not contain the hook's name at all: its nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py is 1805 lines and holds zero occurrences of dynamic_extra against the current tree, where the hook is the mechanism this bisect switches. The arrangement was introduced afterwards by 879a33ee feat(equilibrium): separate operator geometry from member data. So on this identity the traced-anchor arrangement did not exist at the producer revision, the constant arm is a faithful rendering of that condition for the three anchors, and the pure arm's cycle survives the change — which is the exoneration. Between the producer revision and this tree, 175 commits touch nova/equilibrium/. The anchor names cannot be the carrier, and the standing candidate is what the operand partition contains: the producer's partition_values is empty — no numeric leaves at all — against 76 leaves now. The families that add leaves to that partition are the traced profile-coefficient work (a4de1caf, 77b149b4, a8443a12, and the merge 45048423 that carries the flux-function coefficients as traced program arguments) and the traced-polygon-capacity refactor (f7fefbd7 with its merge be29cb0e). This bisect narrows the drift to downstream of the anchor store; it does not name the commit, and the record does not pretend it does.
