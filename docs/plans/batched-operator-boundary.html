Batched operator boundary

Two S19 measurements failed for one reason. The strict-exit incidence measurement on the real bank mixes could not be compiled: with twelve heterogeneous MAST members in one width-12 program the member operators are closed into the trace as constants, and XLA compile memory passed 100 GB and then 267 GB while solve-time memory stayed under 4 GB (jobs 1261068 and 1261110); the first attempt to pass operators as traced arguments then traced shared host geometry and failed at RectilinearMesh.radial_step on a traced scalar. The EFIT reproduction gate paid a full compile on all eleven frames for the same reason: each frame's data is baked into its trace, so the persistent compilation cache can never hit. Every batched throughput number Nova has banked (0.784 ms per member, the 25.0 ms trip quantum) was possible only because those batches replicated one operator. This plan gives the request contract from forward-solve-api the boundary the operator needs so heterogeneous batches become ordinary.

§1 — The boundary

Classify every array the forward operator holds into host geometry (lattice coordinates, connectivity axes, wall nodes, cell polygons, hex stencil, material mask, mesh steps: NumPy, identical across the members of a batch, never traced) and member data (coupling blocks, responses, profile tables, boundary and axis flux anchors, target current, seed state: stacked along a leading member axis and passed as traced arguments), and make the operator a pytree whose geometry leaves are static and whose member leaves are dynamic, with the host-side reads (_structured_grid_axes, RectilinearMesh.radial_step, the mesh-step scalars) taking geometry only. An assertion at batch construction proves geometry identity across members and falls back to sequential per-member solves sharing one compiled program where it does not hold (the DIII-D frames may differ). Done-when: a unit test constructs a width-12 heterogeneous MAST batch and shows the jitted solve traces with no member-specific constant in the HLO, and the per-member results are bit-identical to the width-1 solves.

Landing a possibly stale ground truth in order to tidy a worktree is precisely what that adjudication followup forbids, and it would be unfalsifiable afterwards. So the correct state is the one that now exists: the work is preserved in its worktree, recorded as unmerged, and folded into the adjudication decision rather than pre-empting it.
