§1 — Why this is a new plan and not another section

The predecessor plan asked two questions about how to step, delivered both, and got negatives on both. It is the negatives that make this a new subject rather than one more section: the two mechanisms that were supposed to close the frontier have been built, measured against same-tree controls, and refuted, so the remaining question is no longer about the step.

What was refuted, quantitatively. Extending the backtracking ladder to the probed admissible fraction moved residuals by +4.5%, −1.4%, −7.5%, +0.14% and +2.8% — noise. A topology-preserving predictor-corrector advancing along the state-space arclength did lengthen the step, to a mean of 1.00 to 1.76 Newton-step lengths against 0.36 to 0.81 for backtracking, and traversed farther, 16.82 step-equivalents against 12.38 — and terminal residual got worse on four of five frames while admissions fell from 22 to 13 of 445. A preregistered seed-alignment observable, carried unchanged from five frames to eight, reached Spearman 0.524 at p = 0.183 against cumulative growth and 0.143 at p = 0.736 against terminal residual, at achieved power 0.34 and 0.05.

And a correction that reframes everything. The frontier was stated as a step-length cap of one thirty-second. The paired control showed that figure belongs to the refusal iterations: among admitted trials the largest factor is 1.0 on all five frames. Admitted steps were always full Newton steps. So neither shortening nor lengthening the step was ever going to be the lever, and the measurements agree.

The observation this plan is built on. While enlarging the alignment corpus, frame 22086/43 solved to a terminal residual of 2.911868346631881e-16 with cumulative growth 3.0. The solver is not incapable of converging on this map. Five score-blind DIII-D frames instead sit at 0.087 to 0.155 with topology held, and the unqualified route that abandons the requested class reaches 0.026 to 0.085. That ordering is the whole question.

Four measured populations on one axis. One frame reaches machine precision. The derived criterion sits an order below both plateaus. Abandoning the requested topology class buys roughly a factor three in residual and returns the wrong branch. The question this plan answers is whether the thick band on the right is a solver failure or the constrained problem's own minimum.

§2 — Does a diverted-class root exist below the plateau?

Every mechanism tried so far has assumed a root exists below the plateau and the solver cannot reach it. That assumption has never been tested, and it is cheap to test relative to another solver mechanism. If the constrained minimum for these frames simply is near 0.1, then no stepping strategy will move it and the whole line of work should stop — which is a far more valuable answer than another refuted mechanism.

The discriminating evidence does not require a new solver. Approach the question from the answer side rather than the iteration side: construct or exhibit a state that is simultaneously in the requested diverted class and at a residual materially below the plateau, or show that no such state exists in a bounded neighbourhood. Candidate routes that reuse landed machinery include driving the fixed-boundary construction at the requested class and measuring its free-boundary residual, and evaluating the residual over a sampled neighbourhood of the terminal state restricted to states the existing predicate admits.

Done when each of the five score-blind frames carries one of two verdicts with its evidence: ROOT_EXISTS_BELOW_PLATEAU, quoting the exhibited admissible state's residual against that frame's plateau value from docs/figures/topology-preserving-continuation/conditioning-repair.json; or PLATEAU_IS_THE_CONSTRAINED_MINIMUM, quoting the sampled neighbourhood, its extent, and the lowest admissible residual found in it. A verdict of the second kind on all five frames closes the convergence line honestly and releases the consumers to be told so.

§3 — What separates a frame that converges from one that plateaus?

One frame reached 2.9e-16 while five plateau near 0.1, and nothing yet explains the split. The candidates are all measurable without a new solver: the requested topology class itself (the converged frame's class against the five diverted ones), the conductor and current authority available per frame, the profile shape, the distance from the seed to the admissible set, and the conditioning severity already banked per frame.

This section is deliberately second. If §2 returns PLATEAU_IS_THE_CONSTRAINED_MINIMUM then the split is explained by the constraint rather than by the solver, and this section reduces to confirming that the converged frame's constraint is different in the way §2 predicts. If §2 returns ROOT_EXISTS_BELOW_PLATEAU then this section is the search for what the reachable frames have that the others do not.

Done when the frames that converge and the frames that plateau are separated by at least one measured property with its effect size, or the receipt states which candidate properties were tested and found not to separate them, naming the number of frames each test had. A null result here must carry its sample count, because the predecessor plan's alignment negative was dominated by power at n = 8 and that lesson transfers directly.

§4 — Method requirements carried forward, unchanged

These four bound every comparative node here. They are inherited from measured failures on the predecessor lane, not invented, and each one was violated at least once before it became a rule.

Name the production diff and decline attribution when more than one mechanism sits in it. The predecessor's own control declined historical attribution because seven production paths differed between the banked and current trees. Tree-pinning records what was measured; it does not license a causal claim.

Never derive an acceptance criterion from an achieved value of the quantity it scores, and a criterion must be passable by something. A bound nothing can clear is diagnostic about the bound.

Report an admission or intervention count only beside achieved traversal. This one is new and it was learned expensively: a conditioning rule was measured admitting 445 of 445 trials — an apparently perfect record — while collapsing traversal to 1.15 step-equivalents, because it shrank the step before the predicate saw it. A count without a traversal beside it reads success into damping.

Preregister an observable before scoring it, and do not tune it afterwards. When a ranking comes back underpowered, enlarge the sample rather than proposing a second candidate against the same points, and state the achieved power either way.

§ Decisions

Which route exhibits or excludes a lower-residual admissible state — driving the landed fixed-boundary construction at the requested class and measuring its free-boundary residual, or sampling the residual over a bounded neighbourhood of the terminal state restricted to predicate-admissible states? The two differ in what a null result means: the first can only exhibit, never exclude, while the second excludes only within its sampled extent and must state that extent.

§ Followups

Start at §2 and resist building another solver mechanism

THE ONE THING THAT WOULD WASTE THIS PLAN is treating it as the next solver mechanism. Three have now been measured on this lane - a finer backtracking ladder, iteration-local step conditioning, and a topology-preserving predictor-corrector along state-space arclength - and all three either moved residual by noise or made it worse. Two of them were built well and refuted cleanly, which is the evidence that the missing thing is not another stepping rule. §2 asks a question no mechanism answers: does a state exist that is simultaneously in the requested class and materially below the plateau? Answer that before writing any solver code.

READ THESE RECEIPTS FIRST, because between them they contain every number this plan starts from. docs/figures/topology-preserving-continuation/conditioning-repair.json holds the current per-frame plateau - residuals 0.086 to 0.153 with topology held, 47 admitted advances of 445, 18.93 traversal equivalents - and is the comparator §2 must quote against. docs/figures/topology-preserving-continuation/manifold-advance.json holds the refuted predictor-corrector arm and, importantly, the fact that admitted steps run at factor one. docs/figures/topology-preserving-continuation/seed-alignment-power.json holds the converged frame 22086/43 at 2.911868346631881e-16 that motivates §3, and the power figures that show why a five-frame or eight-frame null is not an answer.

THE OPEN DECISION IS ABOUT WHAT A NULL RESULT WOULD MEAN, which is why it should be settled before dispatch rather than after. A fixed-boundary exhibit can only ever produce an existence proof; it cannot exclude. Neighbourhood sampling can exclude, but only inside a stated extent, and a plan that samples without declaring its extent has not excluded anything. If both arms run, the exhibit arm goes first because a single positive result makes the sampling arm unnecessary.

CONSUMERS, AND WHAT THEY MAY AND MAY NOT BE TOLD. mast-catalog-gpu-solve and diiid-forward-onboarding are both held on convergence, and the predecessor plan did NOT close that hold - it closed its own two hypotheses as honest negatives. Do not report the hold released until §2 either exhibits a lower-residual admissible state or establishes that the plateau is the constrained minimum. The second outcome releases them too, by telling them the target they were waiting for does not exist as specified.
