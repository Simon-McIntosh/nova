§1 — The fixture holds no position, so the iteration must

The Solovev certificate fixture poses its exterior as the analytic total flux minus the plasma's own clipped image, whatever field makes the analytic solution exact. That exterior has no coils and no decay index. The positional-stiffness measurement (cut-cell-current-attribution, merged 6958819b, H200 job 1271814) showed that every rigid displacement of the analytic state from 2 to 60 mm grows over four production trips, outboard and vertically, on weak 110, weak 300 and moderate 300: the oracle is a repelling fixed point of the trip iteration. The consequence, measured by the fixed-point discriminator (b56931bd), is that the production map has two converged fixed points with the same target current and the same exterior: the oracle-near state that an analytic start keeps to 1.1e-4 of span, and a state displaced by 36 mm leaning on a different part of the wall, with the axis flux six to seven percent low and the boundary four percent of span inside, which the production seed selects on five of six exact-clip rows. Every displaced Solovev terminal state of the sprint, whole-cell and exact-clip alike, is this mechanism.

The lead's ruling of 2026-09-16 is to constrain the position rather than re-pose the fixture or rely on the seed: pin the plasma current centroid to the analytic centroid with a Newton-Krylov constraint row, so the iteration cannot fall into the displaced basin, and the certificate converges to the equilibrium the oracle describes. The constraint machinery exists: CurrentCentroidConstraint and the imposed constraint interface in nova/equilibrium/constraint.py, the reduced-route constraint pairs in nova/equilibrium/reduced_newton.py, and the bounded compensating unknown owned by constraint-augmented-newton-krylov.

§2 — The compensating unknown for a coil-less fixture

A constraint row needs a compensating unknown. On a machine the centroid row drives a coil circuit; the fixture has none. The natural unknown is a rigid exterior field increment, a uniform vertical field for the radial centroid and a uniform radial field for the vertical centroid, each with a bounded amplitude, added to the fixture exterior and eliminated by the row. At the true equilibrium the analytic exterior already balances the plasma, so the converged compensating amplitude measures the force imbalance of the discretisation and must tend to zero with cell count: the row is both the restraint and a new absolute-accuracy diagnostic. Done-when: the fixture exposes the two field increments as a compensating unknown implementing the existing protocol, the centroid row is imposed on the four analytic rows through the same interface as the machine rows with no fixture-specific branch in the solver, and a positive control shows the row drives a deliberately displaced seed back to the analytic centroid.
