§1 — The unknown is far larger than the solution it represents

The forward solve iterates a flat one-dimensional flux vector carrying one unknown per grid node, and the fixed-point residual is measured on that vector. But the thing being solved for is an elliptic PDE solution: smooth in value and in gradient, analytic wherever the source is analytic. A representation with one degree of freedom per cell is not describing a ten-thousand-dimensional object — it is describing a smooth surface with ten thousand numbers because that is the discretisation it inherited, not because the solution needs them.

Carry the coefficients as the state instead. Let the unknown be the coefficient set of a tensor-product spline on a knot lattice coarser than the cell lattice. Evaluate that spline, and its derivatives, at the existing cell points; form the source there at full cell resolution exactly as now; project the resulting map back onto the coefficient set. The fixed point becomes a condition on coefficients. The spline order and knot count become the effective solution degree, and the plasma cell resolution and the current moments are untouched — fine quadrature over a coarse unknown, which is the standard and correct separation.

Why this is worth doing now rather than as a tidying exercise. Every mechanism this campaign has built and refuted was fighting a consequence of the large unknown. The projected Krylov condition reaches 4087 on the production route. Cumulative non-normal amplification reached 5.379792627e9 before conditioning, and the conditioning built to tame it was itself a two-order regression until repaired. A 24-vector Krylov subspace is a vanishing fraction of a ten-thousand-dimensional space, which is why the projected operator is ill-conditioned and why step damping was ever needed.

At order one hundred unknowns the entire apparatus becomes unnecessary. A 12×12 cubic knot lattice is 144 coefficients. At that size the Jacobian can be formed explicitly — 144 forward-mode tangents, a 144×144 dense matrix — and solved directly. No Arnoldi, no GMRES, no projected condition number, no step conditioning, no non-normal amplification to damp, and a Newton step that is exact to machine precision rather than to a Krylov tolerance. That is not an optimisation of the current route; it retires several of its hardest components.

§2 — The machinery mostly exists, and one thing about it must change

nova/linalg/tensor_spline.py already provides a traced tensor-product cubic spline with not-a-knot end conditions as a Pytree. Its TensorSplineEvaluation returns value, radial and vertical first derivatives, both second derivatives and the mixed derivative — which is exactly the set Δ*ψ requires, since Δ*ψ = ∂²ψ/∂R² − (1/R)·∂ψ/∂R + ∂²ψ/∂Z². The fit is two batched tridiagonal solves and the per-cell Bernstein blocks are a linear function of the sampled values, so the whole construction is already differentiable and already traced. The Bernstein basis is also the right conditioning choice: this repository has banked the lesson that a monomial contraction loses four decades by degree six, while a well-conditioned basis does not.

What must change is the parameterisation. The existing class is parameterised by the sampled grid values, one Bernstein block per cell, so its degree-of-freedom count is still the cell count. The compression this plan proposes requires the coefficients on a coarse knot lattice to be the primary object, with evaluation and differentiation at the fine cell points. Whether that is a re-parameterisation of the existing class or a sibling construction sharing its Bernstein evaluation is a design question the reuse map answers, not one to guess.

Traced-JAX and GPU-batch compatibility is favourable, and for a structural reason. The map from coefficients to cell-point values and derivatives is a fixed linear operator — a tensor product of two one-dimensional matrices, applied by einsum. Fixed shapes, no data-dependent control flow, no gather that depends on the iterate. It is a better vmap target than the present state, not a worse one: the basis matrices are shared across frames and only the coefficient block varies, so batching a catalog rung means stacking a 144-wide axis rather than a ten-thousand-wide one. Memory per batched frame falls with it, which bears directly on the 13.6× memory cost measured for cell refinement.

§3 — The separatrix kink is the real risk, and it must be measured by region

This is the one place the smoothness argument is not free, and it deserves stating precisely rather than discovering late. Δ*ψ = −μ₀·R·Jφ, and the toroidal current density jumps to zero across the plasma boundary. So ψ is continuous with continuous gradient there, but its second derivatives are discontinuous: the solution is C¹ and not C² across the separatrix. A globally smooth spline cannot represent a curvature jump without error, and that error concentrates exactly where it hurts most — at the boundary the topology predicate reads, which already refuses roughly 85 of every 89 Newton updates.

This is also why a single global error norm would be the wrong measurement. A spline can fit a flux map to six digits in the core while carrying its entire error in a thin band at the separatrix, and a sup or L2 norm over the whole domain can report either as the headline depending on which region dominates. Every fit result in this plan is therefore reported split by region: inside the closed-flux surface, within a stated band of the separatrix, and in the scrape-off layer.

Three mitigations are available if the boundary error is material, and they are cheap to distinguish: place a knot line on or near the separatrix so the representation has a break where the physics does; use repeated knots to reduce continuity across that line deliberately; or accept a measured boundary error and state it as a floor.

Resolved 2026-08-24, and there is no order deficit to explain. Two hypotheses were raised here and both are now refuted by measurement, which turns out to be because the figure they were explaining does not mean what it was taken to mean.

First, the deficit was attributed to a second-order Δ* stencil losing order at cut cells. Withdrawn: there is no Δ* stencil in the residual path. ForwardFluxOperator maps a trial flux to cell currents and back through precomputed coupling operators; the stencils in that module are a quadratic least-squares null fit and a cell-average stencil.

Second, it was attributed to the clipped-source coupling being first order, since a separatrix-cut cell couples as a monopole plus a dipole at its parent centroid rather than as the exact clipped polygon. Refuted by direct controlled measurement over six spacings from 0.24 m to 0.04 m, both arms sharing one clip, current and target set: the shipped clipped-moment coupling is already second order in the separatrix band at 1.98555 ± 0.01519, the exact-clipped-polygon repair gains 0.00957 — inside the uncertainty — and does not reduce the finest band error; closed-flux order is 2.00722 ± 0.01497 and 2.00131 ± 0.01398.

The figure itself is the artefact. The 0.97 is one of six per-reference values computed as log(coarse residual / fine residual) / log(coarse spacing / fine spacing) — a two-point estimator applied once per reference. Its siblings are 1.637, 2.702, 3.307 and 3.387. Orders above three are not physical for a second-order method; they are what a two-point estimator on noisy residuals produces. And the receipt that carried 0.97 into this campaign selected that reference precisely because it was weakest_refinement_response_of_six. So the campaign has been explaining the minimum of a noise-dominated scatter running to 3.39, quoted as though it were the method's order. The controlled ladder measures 1.99 to 2.01 with an uncertainty of ±0.015. The order deficit is retired as a finding, and no further node should hunt its cause.

§6 — Method requirements carried forward

Report a fit or residual norm only with its regional split. This lane has already lost time to a sup norm that discarded every trace of where the residual lived; a global fit error would repeat the mistake in a new place.

Name the production diff between compared trees and decline attribution when more than one candidate mechanism sits in it.

Never derive an acceptance criterion from an achieved value of the quantity it scores, and a criterion must be passable by something.

Keep the conditioning claim and the convergence claim separate. A better-conditioned Jacobian is a real result on its own. Reaching a lower terminal residual is a different result. A receipt that reports one as though it established the other fails review.

§7 — A support that carries a curvature jump, and the quantity that decides which

First, the jump has a known size, and it may be zero. The curvature discontinuity in ψ across the boundary is not free-floating: Δ*ψ jumps by exactly −μ₀·R·Jφ(ψb⁻), the edge current density. If p′ and FF′ taper to zero at the boundary there is no curvature jump and a globally smooth basis is entirely adequate. So the first measurement is not a basis study at all: evaluate the edge current density these cases actually carry. That sizes the problem before any support is chosen, and it is nearly free because the profiles are banked.

Second, and this is the strongest option: do not represent the jump — generate it. Nova is a Green's-function code. If the unknown is the plasma current rather than the flux, then ψ = ψcoils + G·Jφ, and the Green operator maps a jump in Jφ to a curvature jump in ψ exactly, because it is a smoothing operator of order two. The discontinuity is produced by construction and never approximated. Two further consequences follow: Δ* is never discretised, so the observed convergence order of 0.97 — a property of the stencil — cannot arise at all; and the fixed operator is a single dense contraction, which is the ideal vmap target.

And the hard geometric part of that route is already built. nova/equilibrium/separatrix_clip.py carries atomic shared-edge crossings with canonical exact-zero corners, conserving patch area to 2.2e-16–4.4e-16 m² on every grid; its clipped zeroth and first moments about fixed centroids move continuously at first order under boundary motion, ε-doubling ratio 1.99998, where a centroid rule jumps by a factor of a million; and its padded jit contraction traces once over a moving separatrix with x64 enforced at the entry point. The moving-support problem is therefore solved already — on the current side, not the flux side. That is a strong argument for putting the unknown where the machinery is.

§9 — The carrier need not represent the jump

The iterated object and the answer are different objects, and only the answer has a kink. Writing the map as ψ = ψext + G·s, the converged spline carries s, and the flux is what G returns. The jump lives in the source support, which the separatrix clip already carries exactly; G converts a jumping source into a kinked flux by construction. So nothing smooth is ever asked to represent a discontinuity: the smooth basis carries a smooth factor, the clip carries the support, and the kink appears only in the output. The C² objection in §3 applies to a basis that is the answer, not to a carrier.

And the topology read takes the exact map, not the carrier — which removes the consistency worry rather than managing it. An earlier version of this section warned that a fixed point of the smooth-carrier map need not be a fixed point of the exact-flux map, because the carrier and the output could disagree about where ψb lies. That worry was self-inflicted: there is no reason to read topology from the carrier. ψ = ψext + G·s is one dense contraction against a precomputed operator, so the exact flux is cheap to evaluate at every cell point on every iterate. Every physics read — axis, X point, ψb, the separatrix, the domain labels, the profile arguments — takes the exact Green output. The carrier is never read for physics; it is only ever fed to G. With that rule the two objects cannot disagree, because only one of them is ever consulted.

What the carrier is then for, and what floors it. Its job is to compress the iterate, and the loop closes by projecting the exact output back onto the coefficient space — a fixed least-squares operator, one more matmul. The residual worth reporting is the one on the exact field, since that is the physics; the projection then sets a floor on what any coefficient iteration can reach, and §4 has already measured that floor. Sizing follows directly: 36 coefficients put the closed-flux representation error just below the current production residual, while reaching the 1e-3 range wants the 20-knot rung at 400 coefficients — still a 2.5-fold compression on 1,069 cells, and a 400×400 dense Jacobian is 160,000 entries, which is nothing. The carrier also compresses the smoother of the two available objects: G gains two orders, so the flux is smoother than the source that produces it, and §4 measured the flux.

§10 — Order raising on every cell, not refinement on some

Buildable, verified, and priced. The three second-order weights do not close symbolically — no exact quadratic weighted-edge endpoint primitive was obtained for RR, RZ or ZZ — so each routes through a fixed tensor-Gauss triangle quadrature, stated as an implementation result rather than a proof that no reduction closes. §11 — Two independent levers, one solution matrix

They are separate ideas and were entangled in the first two drafts of this plan. Carrying the iterate in a coefficient basis and raising the order of the in-cell current support are orthogonal changes: either can be made without the other, they act on different objects, and they could plausibly help, do nothing, or interfere. Assessing them together as a single proposal would make any result uninterpretable. So they are assessed separately and in combination, as a two-by-two factorial.

The exact-ψ route is retained, not replaced. Arm A must remain a first-class production path after this work, and the carrier must be an explicitly selected option at the call site rather than a global switch or a wrapper — the repository's standing preference, and the only arrangement in which a factorial stays runnable afterwards. An implementation that makes the spline carrier the only route fails this section regardless of how it measures, because it destroys the baseline the comparison depends on.

Nothing is accepted on plausibility. Each of arms B, C and D is scored against arm A on three axes, and an arm that does not improve on all the axes it claims must say so:

  • Convergence — terminal residual on the exact field, admitted-advance count, achieved Newton-step-equivalents, terminal topology class and X-point finiteness, on the same frames and the same source revision.
  • Speed — wall clock per frame and per batched frame, peak device memory, Jacobian formation and solve cost, and the per-iterate cost separated from any one-time build so that a build is never reported as throughput.
  • Accuracy — error against the banked converged roots, split by region as every measurement on this plan is, plus the projection floor for the carrier arms since a coefficient iterate cannot beat its own representation error.

Prerequisite, and it is a hard one. Arms B and D require the second-order weighted kernels to exist. Until §10 reports whether the weighted edge-antiderivative family admits exact second-order members, only arms A and C are buildable, and the matrix cannot be completed. This is why the prototype waits on that result rather than starting alongside it.

Done when all four arms are measured on a common frame set at one source revision, with the three axes reported per arm against arm A, the interaction stated explicitly — whether B and C combine additively in D, sub-additively, or interfere — and the receipt naming which arms improve on which axes and which do not. An arm that improves accuracy while costing speed, or convergence while costing accuracy, is a legitimate outcome and must be reported as a trade rather than summarised as a win. A matrix in which no arm beats A on any axis closes this plan honestly, and arm A remaining the production path is then the result.

§ Decisions

Is the coarse-knot coefficient state a re-parameterisation of the existing TensorBSpline, or a sibling construction that reuses its Bernstein evaluation while owning its own knot lattice? The existing class is parameterised by sampled cell values with one block per cell, so it cannot express a coarse-knot state as it stands. A re-parameterisation keeps one authority for spline evaluation; a sibling risks two. Decide from what the reuse map finds rather than in advance.

Reuse map verdict 2026-08-24: re-parameterise the existing evaluator authority rather than fork it, since fit_tensor_spline already permits a coarse nodal-value state and TensorBSpline.evaluate already returns the full second-derivative set. Whichever is chosen, the exact-ψ route stays a first-class path and the carrier is selected explicitly at the call site — see §11.

If the separatrix band carries the binding fit error, which support carries the curvature jump: a level-set enrichment of the form max(ψ − ψb, 0)² that follows the actual curve, repeated knots giving the right continuity class along axis-aligned lines only, an accepted and stated error floor, or moving the unknown to the current side so the Green operator generates the jump exactly? A knot line that follows the moving boundary makes the basis depend on the iterate and threatens the fixed-shape traced contract the whole approach depends on. Answer only after the regional split in section 4 and the edge-current sizing in section 7 are both measured.

§ Followups

Measure the degree before building anything, and split every error by region

SECTION 4 BEFORE SECTION 5, and the ordering carries the whole risk of the plan. The central claim is that the effective solution degree is low. That is answerable with no solve, on banked maps, by fitting spline lattices of increasing knot count and reporting the error. If a modest lattice reproduces a banked converged root well below the residual the production solve is fighting, the prototype is justified; if the error stalls near the plateau, the compression is not available at this order and the honest outcome is to say so and stop. Building the solver first would spend the expensive part of the plan on an unmeasured premise.

THE MACHINERY IS MOSTLY THERE AND ONE THING IS NOT. nova/linalg/tensor_spline.py is already a traced Pytree tensor-product cubic spline with not-a-knot ends, and its TensorSplineEvaluation already returns both second derivatives and the mixed term, which is exactly what Δ* needs. What it does not do is carry a coarse-knot coefficient set as its primary object: it is parameterised by sampled cell values with one Bernstein block per cell, so its degree-of-freedom count is still the cell count. That gap is the plan, and the open coefficient-parameterisation decision is about how to close it without creating a second authority for spline evaluation.

EVERY ERROR IS REPORTED BY REGION, never as a single global norm. A spline can fit a flux map to six digits in the core while carrying all its error in a thin band at the separatrix, and that band is exactly where the topology predicate reads. The physical reason is not subtle: the toroidal current density jumps to zero across the plasma boundary, so ψ is C¹ but not C² there, and a globally smooth basis cannot represent a curvature jump without error. Closed-flux region, separatrix band, scrape-off layer — three numbers, every time.

THE PAYOFF TO MEASURE IS CONDITIONING, and it is separate from convergence. At order one hundred unknowns the Jacobian can be formed explicitly and solved directly, which removes Arnoldi, GMRES, the projected condition number, step conditioning and non-normal amplification from the path altogether. Quote the dense condition number against the banked projected Krylov condition of 4087.40844825275 on the same frame. Then quote the terminal residual separately. A receipt that lets a conditioning win read as a convergence win fails review.

WATCH THE FIXED-SHAPE CONTRACT if the boundary treatment tempts you toward a knot line that follows the moving separatrix. A basis that depends on the iterate threatens exactly the traced, batchable property that makes this approach attractive for the catalog. That tension is the second open decision and it should be answered from the section 4 measurement, not in advance.
