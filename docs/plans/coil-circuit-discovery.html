§1 — The premise, and why it is practical now

A forward solve cannot find the right plasma if it is given the wrong vacuum field. Five DIII-D ohmic conductors — ECOILB, E567UP, E567DN, E89UP, E89DN — carry real current in every discharge, exist in the independent netCDF machine description, and have no current channel in the competition dataset. Everything downstream inherits that hole.

Every piece needed to fill it by measurement rather than assumption is already landed:

§4 — Known, knowable, unknown: the discipline that makes the model honest

The point of discovery is to shrink the prescribed set to what is defensible and to make the rest visible rather than assumed. Three tiers, and a quantity may only be promoted upward on evidence:

  • Known. A shipped channel, or a prescribed input of the same class — conductor currents, and total plasma current, which is in the competition input set and can also come from a partner transport solve. Prescribed directly.
  • Knowable. A quantity predicted from known channels by a discovered static relation that survived §3's stability tests. Prescribed through the relation, with the relation's residual carried as a stated uncertainty rather than discarded.
  • Unknown. Everything else. These become explicit free parameters solved alongside the equilibrium unknowns — not silently defaulted to zero, and not quietly filled with a label-recovered value as the current DIII-D route does.

The third tier is the substantive change. Today the five unshipped currents are prescribed from label-recovered values that §2 shows are probably not currents — which is worse than admitting they are unknown, because it hides the uncertainty inside an input that looks prescribed.

Done-when: the forward seam accepts a per-conductor tier declaration; unknown-tier currents enter the solve as free parameters with their prior and their recovered posterior both reported; and a receipt states, for any given run, which currents were prescribed, which were predicted through a relation, and which were solved for.

§5 — Boundaries and admissibility

The fit-once versus fit-per-frame distinction is load-bearing and is a decision below, not an implementation detail. Static circuit constants fitted once over a training corpus and then applied deterministically are calibration, in the same class as the turn counts this campaign already reads from a description. Currents fitted per frame against a label are fitting, and are not reproducible at inference. A relation discovered here is only useful if it is the former.

  • This plan does not own the constrained map. Prescribing total plasma current by amplitude elimination belongs to current-constrained-forward-solve; this plan supplies the conductor currents that solve consumes.
  • It does not own basin entry or topology pinning — dual-basin-solve does. The §10 overlay evidence showed a current constraint selects the branch class while leaving the X point 45 cm displaced, so both mechanisms are needed and neither substitutes.
  • Passive structure remains out of scope as a fitted model. The DIII-D onboarding measured that no passive signal rises above the label noise in that dataset. Passive current may appear here only as a competing term in §2's adjudication, never as a vessel model fitted against a solve that does not close.
  • Corpus reading conventions stay where they are pinned. This plan consumes the landed COCOS pin and the polarity screen; it does not re-derive them.

§ Decisions

Are discovered circuit relations fitted once over a training corpus and applied deterministically, or re-fitted per shot or per frame?

Locked on the plan's own admissibility argument and the two adjudication rounds: a per-frame refit against label flux is exactly the label-absorption failure measured twice (recovered spread 1.95-31.6x, ohmic-shape match 0/60), while the ohmic relation — the only surviving relation — is fitted once over 480,256 independent trace samples and reproduces at inference from shipped ECOILA.

What test promotes a recovered current from unknown to knowable?

Locked conservative, with the measured consequence stated: both label-referenced tests fail for every label-recovered quantity (held-out R-squared up to 0.998 with ohmic-shape match 0/60 confirms the counter-example; closure is unattainable for external conductors — interior Delta-star is insensitive by construction and the flux-space circuit arm closes 0/60). Promotion therefore rests on independent deterministic measurement: E567UP (scale 0.9929, residual 0.570%) and E89DN (1.0165, 0.706%) pass the preregistered 1% bound of the ohmic retest and are knowable through that fit-once relation with its residual carried as uncertainty and the different-pulse transfer caveat stated; ECOILB, E567DN, E89UP fail it and stay unknown.

How do unknown-tier currents enter the solve as free parameters?

Locked on conditioning risk: the inner solve is landed on a map measured at Picard spectral radius 1.15-1.40, and augmenting the Newton system changes Jacobian structure and conditioning on exactly that map. The outer loop preserves the inner solve unchanged, its nested cost is affordable within the measured 470.561 slices/s budget, and the choice is reversible through the dissent flow if the nested convergence proves too slow.

§ Followups

Adjudicate the 2.20x versus 1.04x current disagreement before any discovery run (§2)

This is the gate on the whole plan and it must run first. Two independent estimates of the same five conductor currents disagree by far more than either's error budget: label-flux recovery gives a 2.20× spread across the five (30,468 → 66,907 A) while the netCDF ohmic circuit measurement puts every one of them within 2 % of ECOILA, a ~1.04× spread. At design condition number 10.83 and a 0.484 % tare floor the recovery's coefficient-error budget is about 5 %, so conditioning cannot reconcile a factor of 2.2.

The decisive measurement: take the residual remaining after five-column recovery and compare it against the 0.484 % exact-tare floor. If the five columns explain the residual down to that floor, the recovered values are trustworthy and the circuit measurement needs re-examination. If structure survives, the recovered coefficients are absorbing something other than these five currents — the candidates being passive-structure current, the labels' own 4.3 % non-GS content, and the Δ* boundary-stencil artefacts already localised at the grid edges, all of which are smooth or edge-concentrated over a grid the five conductors sit largely outside and therefore partially degenerate with the five columns.

Report: post-recovery residual against the tare floor per frame; the spatial structure of what survives, since edge-concentrated and smooth residuals implicate different competing terms; and an explicit verdict naming which of the two estimates is to be believed. A live diagnostic already dispatched under current-constrained-forward-solve §5 tests the circuit-derived currents against the tared vacuum map directly and should be read alongside this rather than duplicated.

Do not proceed to §3's corpus discovery on an unresolved disagreement — a corpus-scale least squares over a subspace that is absorbing the wrong physics would produce stable, precise, wrong circuit constants, which is the most expensive possible outcome.
