§1 — The substrate is verified; the problem is now an optimisation

Kirk (arXiv:1312.6507) names coil positioning as MAST's dominant intrinsic error-field source and records that the copper inside the P4/P5 cases was never surveyed. The misfit programme localised a surviving vacuum residual beside the P4 coils. Between those two facts sits an inverse problem: solve for the coil geometry the vacuum measurements actually imply.

The substrate check ran on 2026-08-07 (~/.cache/nova-mast/misfit-localization/autograd_check.py). The biot operators were written JAX-compliant for accelerator throughput, and that same property now delivers derivatives: the traced polygon quadrature kernel (tiledassembly._traced_psi_gradient, statically unrolled, branch-free) reproduces the reference numpy kernel to 8×10⁻¹² and returns exact geometry Jacobians — jax.jacfwd of (ψ, B_R, B_Z) with respect to rigid section shifts and individual polygon vertices agrees with central finite differences at the FD truncation floor (~10⁻⁶), second derivatives are finite (Gauss–Newton and Newton both available), and jacrev agrees with jacfwd to 10⁻¹². Two conditions attach, and they are §2.1's work: the vertices → (edge, weight, norm) pack must run inside the trace (the numpy pack_section needs a traceable twin — a 15-line transcription matched to machine precision once the orientation convention was honoured: the counter-clockwise edge sum yields −f(φ), so the kernel's sign is minus the shoelace sign), and only the polygon quadrature path is verified — the other kernel classes need the same treatment. For the record: the ambix gs/polygon.py kernel is an independent numpy implementation using complex-step derivatives (exact, but target-side only and not traceable); nova's dual-namespace stack is the substrate here.

§2 — Implementation

  1. ✓ landed 2026-08-07 Kernel differentiability audit and refactor — the whole coupling family is geometry-traceable. Eight kernel classes brought to the traced pattern with three-part autograd regressions (value / jacfwd-vs-FD / jacrev-vs-jacfwd), merged 42dd1816: traced section pack (static horizontal-edge mask; edges/weights exact, norm 1e-15), polygon quadrature driven from the traced pack (vertex-level Jacobians, value 1e-10, jacfwd-FD 1e-6), ring filament (complement-native complete pair replacing opaque Cephes; on-axis Jacobians finite), rectangular finite section, arc filament (position + both azimuths), traced zeta (tanh-sinh unconditionally, 1e-13 vs routed host), and both closed-form polygon paths (full turn + finite arc). Two elliptic derivative repairs along the way: the third kind's forward mode no longer NaNs on the unreflected side, and the Carlson degenerate form's slope takes an even series in v² below ratio 0.1 — slope error 3e-9 → 3e-15 near small poles, values unchanged at 8e-16, +5.4% cost on the arc path only. Fast autograd lane 30 s; three closed-form section traces behind the slow marker (finite-arc trace is op-count-bound at ~284 s — measured, not reducible by grid size). Recorded limitation: at pole == complement exactly the slope is finite but 1.2e-02 relative (pre-existing, test-documented). Full default suite green.
  2. ✓ landed 2026-08-08 Geometry parametrisation — landed with the identifiability spectrum §2.3 demanded first. nova/biot/geometryparameters.py: per-coil blocks (rigid ΔR/ΔZ, affine scale/stretch/shear/tilt/dilation, per-vertex, discrete chamfer branch), Jacobians pinned jacfwd-vs-FD and jacrev-vs-jacfwd (30 tests). Spectrum at the real array (merge b4521803, figures docs/figures/geometry-identifiability/): the resolvable set is rigid+affine — rigid conditioning O(1), affine O(10³), per-vertex hopeless (free corners 10⁸, free turns 10¹³ — freeze them). Leading modes are rigid_z/rigid_r at 54 µm (P5 lower) to 2.2 mm (P4 upper) marginalised, tilt/dilation unresolvable (15–180 mm). The near-field probe cut costs ×3.2–17.3 in best-mode resolution — the geometry information lives in the near probes, so the solve keeps them and drives the 23-cell lattice rather than excluding them for the hull-smear trap — any spectrum quoted without that cut is not evidence. The 18 admitted flux loops carry 20–290× less geometry signal per channel than a probe (flux is a smoother functional of pack shape than field); they buy two extra affine modes inside 10 mm and lift the degenerate tilt/dilation directions up to 40× by breaking the geometry/current degeneracy — worth having, changes no verdict. Marginalising the 13 described current freedoms costs roughly half the resolved modes and is the honest bound a solve may claim. The discrete chamfer branch is decisively discriminable on every member (57–621 noise units) — settled, not a freedom to solve. Standing kernel constraint for any solve: a zero-weight flat edge carries no derivative, so tilt/shear Jacobians are taken at mirrored small tilts and averaged (FLAT_EDGE_TILT, regression-pinned) — bypassing it silently corrupts tilt/shear gradients 5–26×. Banked: ~/.cache/nova-mast/geometry-identifiability/.
  3. Problem class A — sensor-space (the MAST case). Recorded currents fixed; minimise the weighted misfit between measured vacuum couplings and modelled couplings over geometry parameters, per coil and jointly. The banked signed couplings (couplings.npz, 41 rows) are the immediate targets; calibrated flux loops join when mast-flux-loop-calibration lands. Identifiability first: the geometry-Jacobian singular spectrum at the real sensor set decides how many geometry modes the array resolves (the current-space answer was rank 10 of 13 — the geometry-space answer bounds what any fit may claim), and regularisation follows from it.
  4. Problem class B — flux-map-space. Given fixed coil currents and a target flux map with the plasma contribution subtracted (an equilibrium-reconstruction by-product, or the toroidal-harmonic misfit maps), solve the coil set's geometry to match the map on its grid. Same machinery, different data functional — the form any machine can consume without raw sensor access. The named application is the Sophelio Fusion Equilibrium Challenge (development phase closes 2026-10-18), and its premise is corrected here: verified against the challenge data page on 2026-08-11, the dataset ships per-shot conductor geometry (coil positions, section width/height, skew angles on six DIII-D F-coils) alongside coil currents, Thomson profiles and 65×65 EFIT flux maps for 7,915 DIII-D shots (CC BY 4.0, magnetics withheld; blind cross-machine target MAST, 1,206 shots, zero training) — see sophelio-challenge-geometry-sourcing. The challenge-facing deliverable therefore re-anchors from recovering the coil description to (a) validating the shipped DIII-D geometry against label-derived fields through the identifiability spectrum, and (b) inferring what does not ship — wall contour and passive/vessel structure — from labeled ramp frames where eddy fields dominate the mismatch, feeding diiid-forward-onboarding. The MAST-side science deliverable — recover a machine's coil positions from its published flux maps and currents alone, developed and scored on MAST as the oracle where our registry geometry is the known truth — is unchanged and keeps its full validation ladder; a machine that carries real geometry retains its structural edge on the cross-machine metric.
  5. Validation ladder, then MAST. Synthetic recovery throughout: perturb a known geometry, generate exact data (with realistic noise from the measured floors), recover, report bias and resolution limits per parameter class. Then the MAST application: P4/P5 members first (seeded from Kirk's offsets), scored against the surviving-misfit patterns, cross-checked against the provenance plan's linear symmetry scan (which this generalises), with the verdict — geometry correction found and quantified, or bounded as unresolvable at this array — recorded in a research note and, if found, delivered as a machine-description correction through the established registry route.

Done-when: kernel family audited and refactored with autograd regression tests green; identifiability spectrum for MAST geometry modes published; synthetic recovery ladder banked; MAST P4/P5 solve completed on both problem classes with confidence bounds; verdict recorded and any geometry correction handed to the registry.

§ Decisions

Optimiser for the geometry solve?

Owner selection 2026-08-12. The identifiability spectrum settles it on conditioning: after freezing per-vertex the resolvable set is rigid+affine only, so the parameter count is small, jacfwd already returns exact Jacobians, and conditioning is benign (rigid O(1), affine O(1e3)). Gauss-Newton/LM consumes that Jacobian directly and its normal-equation curvature is what the per-mode resolution and confidence bounds are read from; L-BFGS would discard an exact Jacobian already in hand and leave the error bars without a curvature source.
