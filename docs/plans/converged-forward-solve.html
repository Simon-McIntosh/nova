Why this plan exists

Lead directive, 2026-10-03: the Newton-Krylov, cut-cell, diverted and topology defects are to be identified and fixed once, with a deep implementation behind a narrow interface. The present code is sprawl. nova/equilibrium is 54,102 lines in 54 files. The axis and saddle are found in 17 files and the plasma region is decided in 23. newton_krylov takes 30 parameters, _active_set_newton_krylov takes 24 and runs 590 lines, and forward.py defines solve three times. The route carries four solvers, two clip modes, five promotion and recovery rungs and a frozen/live split, and 33 commits in 15 days to fixed_point.py alone, most of them guards, fallbacks and reverts.

The outcome of that route, measured on 2026-10-03: at the one certificate rung where the map is qualified (weak exact, 300 cells) the production solve stops at residual 0.0374 against a bound of 1e-12. From the production seed every certificate row at 110, 300 and 1000 cells accepts zero Newton steps, so each terminal state is its seed, while the same weak-110 row from a seed displaced 20 mm accepts four steps and reaches 2.7e-14. The strong-1000 row reaches 1.6e-15 on a state that misses the map and boundary clauses. On the DIII-D gate frames the current source is 10× better than the artifact and converges on none. The route the sprint set out to restore does not converge, and each layer added to it made a non-converging route look better rather than fix why it does not converge.

Three defects, in one vocabulary. The Grad-Shafranov fixed point is ψ = ψ_ext + G·J(ψ), G is the coupling matrix: built once per mesh, unchanged during a solve, and passed into the compiled program as an operand rather than baked in as a constant. J depends on ψ through exactly two channels: the topology read (axis flux, boundary flux, membership) and the profile.

  1. Membership. Private-flux cells are booked as plasma because the sub-cell booking tests the flux level without connectivity to the axis. The decomposition at 6cb5b3e25 proved it: analytic membership cuts the 132-cell exact error from 0.181 to 0.071 and the X-point offset from 115 mm to 6 mm, while the contour-tree region and the X-point wedge change nothing.
  2. Topology. Axis and saddle are chosen in 17 places by different rules, so the typed selector has no containment, the vertex-ring census was inert, and a finer grid admits different saddles.
  3. Newton. The Newton direction is computed for the frozen map while the step is scored against the live map. Newton is solving the wrong function; the ladders compensate; the zero accepted steps from the production seed are what that looks like.

§1 — The design: four pure functions and the inventory they replace

One research document, written by the design node, that fixes the interface below in signatures and invariants, inventories the 17 null-finding and 23 region-finding implementations and, for each, names which of the four functions absorbs it or why it is deleted (the 17 all collapse into topology.read; the 23 into topology.read's membership or current.book), explains the three defects with the measured evidence, and states the acceptance in §6 as a test list. It is written on codex-astra and re-reviewed on claude-opus before any implementation node dispatches.

  • topology.read(ψ) → axis, saddle or limiter contact, ψ_axis, ψ_b, membership, qualification. The contour-tree authority the lead ruled: hex cells, no raster, no normalised flux, σ from COCOS. The saddle is admitted by connectivity. This is the only place any of these is decided, and it returns a qualification verdict the solve honours.
  • current.book(ψ, read) → cell moments. The exact clip is the one booking; chord becomes a quadrature degree, not a route. Membership is sub-cell and axis-connected. A traced-capacity refusal stops the solve with a reason; an over-capacity cell never becomes an empty support.
  • gs.apply(ψ) = ψ_ext + G @ book(ψ, read(ψ)), with its JVP. The implicit Picard derivative, the moment-map derivative and the boundary-flux envelope derivative, all of which pass their contracts today, carry over.
  • solve.fixed_point(ψ0, constraints) → ψ*, receipt. Newton-GMRES with the operator applied at one call site inside a lax.scan, one line search, termination on the global residual and every constraint row (|row| ≤ 1e-12), or a stop with a reason. The receipt carries a history with one record per accepted Newton step: global residual, each row's residual and the accepted state. No host conversion of a traced target anywhere.

Done when the research document exists at docs/research/converged-forward-solve-design.html with the signatures, invariants, inventory table, defect mechanisms with evidence anchors, the per-resolution numeric tolerances that §2 and §3 use, and the §6 acceptance as a test list; a read-back review of that document on the local lane finds no sentence with two readings; and a separate claude-opus re-review of it passes, which means its stored review record scores every dimension at or above the project floor and lists no blocking finding of any kind, wherever in the document it falls.

§2 — The topology read

Implement topology.read as a single module against the analytic Solov'ev oracles: the weak-rotation reactor-static case (limited, no X-point) and the diverted single-null case, at the five analytic-certificate resolutions: 132, 300, 550, 1074 and 2616 realised cells. The oracle for each is the analytic state's own critical points and its exact plasma region.

Done when: on both analytic states at 132, 300, 550, 1074 and 2616 realised cells, the read places the axis and every admitted X-point within one pitch of the analytic position (one pitch is the mesh cell pitch at that resolution, the certificate's existing acceptance unit), returns, for every cell, the plasma area fraction in [0, 1] from the exact clip of the axis-connected region, matching the analytic fraction to within the numeric tolerance the §1 design document states per resolution, and reports the limited case as limited and the diverted case as diverted; the read is pure, traced, and differentiable in the null positions through the implicit function theorem on ∇ψ = 0; and a negative control fails the position clause: given the analytic flux translated vertically by three pitches against the fixed wall and mesh, the read reports nulls at least three pitches from the unshifted analytic positions. The qualification verdict is a separate output, not the position tolerance: it states whether the saddle is admitted (its component reaches the axis before the wall) and whether the null census matches the case.

§3 — The current booking

Implement current.book over the read's membership, with the exact clip as the one booking. Chord is a quadrature degree of the same function. The private-flux region is excluded by construction, because membership is axis-connected before any sub-cell polygon is clipped.

Done when: at the analytic diverted state, the booked current moments reproduce the analytic cell moments to within the numeric tolerance the §1 design document states per resolution, the private-flux cells book zero plasma current, the net current matches the analytic total to 1e-12 relative, and the booking is differentiable in ψ with its JVP matching central differences to the moment-map contract's existing stages (smooth control, axis flux, boundary flux, support partition).

§4 — The map and its derivative

Implement gs.apply as ψ_ext + G @ book(ψ, read(ψ)) with geometry (mesh, coupling blocks, stencils) as traced operands and nothing captured as closure constants. Carry the implicit Picard derivative, the moment-map derivative and the boundary-flux envelope derivative into it.

Done when: evaluated once at the analytic state with no solve, the map reproduces it within 1e-2 relative sup at every certificate rung in exact mode for both cases (the map-fidelity gate); the JVP matches central differences at the existing contract's tolerances; one compiled program serves two geometries of the same shape, counted as compilations; and the program's compile wall, executable size and host memory during a cold compile are reported.

§5 — The fixed-point solve

Implement solve.fixed_point as Newton-GMRES on the map's own JVP, applied at one call site served from a lax.scan, with one line search. It terminates when the global residual and every constraint row are at or below 1e-12, or stops with a stated reason. It emits the per-step history, one record per accepted Newton step. It traces under jax.jit and jax.vmap. The promotion ladders, the frozen/live split, the live-read fallback and the active-set recovery are not carried; they are retired in §6.

Done when: from the production seed, every row of §6's acceptance table accepts Newton steps and reaches the joint acceptance; the solve traces under jax.jit and jax.vmap, and on one device the traced terminal state is bit-identical to the eager one; the per-step history is emitted and its last record agrees with the terminal receipt on the fields they share (terminal residual, each row's residual, the accepted state); and the compile-cost bounds of §6 hold.
