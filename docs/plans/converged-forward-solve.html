Why this plan exists

Lead directive, 2026-10-03: the Newton-Krylov, cut-cell, diverted and topology defects are to be identified and fixed once, with a deep implementation behind a narrow interface. The present code is sprawl. nova/equilibrium is 54,102 lines in 54 files. The axis and saddle are found in 17 files and the plasma region is decided in 23. newton_krylov takes 30 parameters, _active_set_newton_krylov takes 24 and runs 590 lines, and forward.py defines solve three times. The route carries four solvers, two clip modes, five promotion and recovery rungs and a frozen/live split, and 33 commits in 15 days to fixed_point.py alone, most of them guards, fallbacks and reverts.

The outcome of that route, measured on 2026-10-03: at the one certificate rung where the map is qualified (weak exact, 300 cells) the production solve stops at residual 0.0374 against a bound of 1e-12. From the production seed every certificate row at 110, 300 and 1000 cells accepts zero Newton steps, so each terminal state is its seed, while the same weak-110 row from a seed displaced 20 mm accepts four steps and reaches 2.7e-14. The strong-1000 row reaches 1.6e-15 on a state that misses the map and boundary clauses. On the DIII-D gate frames the current source is 10× better than the artifact and converges on none. The route the sprint set out to restore does not converge, and each layer added to it made a non-converging route look better rather than fix why it does not converge.

Three defects, in one vocabulary. The Grad-Shafranov fixed point is ψ = ψ_ext + G·J(ψ), G is the coupling matrix: built once per mesh, unchanged during a solve, and passed into the compiled program as an operand rather than baked in as a constant. J depends on ψ through exactly two channels: the topology read (axis flux, boundary flux, membership) and the profile.

  1. Membership. Private-flux cells are booked as plasma because the sub-cell booking tests the flux level without connectivity to the axis. The decomposition at 6cb5b3e25 proved it: analytic membership cuts the 132-cell exact error from 0.181 to 0.071 and the X-point offset from 115 mm to 6 mm, while the contour-tree region and the X-point wedge change nothing.
  2. Topology. Axis and saddle are chosen in 17 places by different rules, so the typed selector has no containment, the vertex-ring census was inert, and a finer grid admits different saddles.
  3. Newton. The Newton direction is computed for the frozen map while the step is scored against the live map. Newton is solving the wrong function; the ladders compensate; the zero accepted steps from the production seed are what that looks like.

§ Decisions

Are the Newton-Krylov promotion ladders and the frozen/live split patched or retired?

Lead, 2026-10-03: fixed once with a deep implementation behind a narrow interface; no building on refuted work and no further workarounds. The ladders and the split are retired when §5 passes the acceptance table.

Which authority does the topology read implement?

Lead ruling, relayed by the plan session: the contour-tree authority on hex cells, no raster, no normalised flux, σ from COCOS. The field representation the read evaluates is the open decision below.

What is plasma membership?

Lead, 2026-10-03, on the diverted map decomposition at 6cb5b3e25: membership is decided sub-cell, on the exact clip, and only for the component connected to the axis. The flux level alone books private-flux cells as plasma.

What is the acceptance for the new solve?

Lead, 2026-10-03: the joint acceptance of §6. A solve can reach 1e-12 on a wrong map; the strong-1000 row did.

Which lanes carry the design and the implementation?

Lead, 2026-10-04: codex-astra (gpt-6-astra, the model the account offers) for the design (§1), the four implementation nodes (§2 to §5) and the acceptance and retirement node (§6); claude-opus re-review of the design once its window reopens; the local lane for tests, instruments, evidence and read-back reviews. codex-terra is retired.

Which field representation does the topology read evaluate on the hex cells?

Lead, 2026-10-05: the own-node quadratic is the field everywhere except the saddle cell, whose support comes from the null's normal form (position and Hessian eigen-directions with curvature corrections), never from level-set roots. The null position, gradient and Hessian are evaluated from the Biot coupling kernels' own analytic derivatives of the booked current, never by differentiating the mesh flux. Reason: the null converges at the field's order with any representation; the separatrix level set at the null does not (a flux error e moves it by sqrt(2e/|H|), measured as half-order support in the saddle cell), so the saddle cell must be built from regular quantities. A global spline would improve the saddle support by one order only.

Do large receipts leave git for the GPFS evidence store?

Lead, 2026-10-05: npz, gz and json receipts above a few MB live under /work/projects/imas_gpu with a path pointer in the evidence fragment; PNG and SVG stay in git; pre-commit enforces a size ceiling; existing large receipts migrate in a separate cleanup node. Reason: 1,234 of nova's 1,312 tracked MB is docs/figures receipts, paid by every clone, worktree and archive.

How the differentiated Biot harmonic kernel loop is represented so the traced read meets the compile clause at 1e-13

Lead decision 2026-10-06. The read's cost is the Biot harmonic kernel expression, not the mesh: the kernels' point jet compiles cold in 83.6 s (fresh process, cache disabled before JAX initialises, b6f1efd95), and the kernel-backed read times out at 900 s at any cell count, with about 1.25 to 1.5 million expanded equations. harmonic_pole_moments and harmonic_multiply unroll their order recurrences in Python, and autodiff then differentiates every unrolled step, so the derivative graph compounds with derivative order. A 4.5x cut in unique equations bought no compile time, so the equation count is not the lever. Writing each recurrence's derivative as its own recurrence (custom_jvp on the kernel point jet) stops autodiff expanding the differentiated loop while leaving the primal arithmetic untouched, so the 1e-13 identity against the base kernels holds for values by construction. Fixed-capacity scans were tried by the kernel node and moved the differentiated arithmetic beyond 1e-13. Judging a scan against an extended-precision reference instead would change the identity reference, and relaxing the clause is forbidden by the locked acceptance. Accepted cost: hand-derived derivative recurrences for each kernel family, each proven against autodiff to 1e-13 at 10,000 points; the Hessian requires the rule itself to be differentiable (jvp of jvp); the largest of the three nodes.

Order ruling, 2026-10-05, coordinator's pending the lead's confirmation. Under the median-area normalisation with every sliver retained, the §2 run measured five-rung smooth orders of 1.976 (limited) and 1.094 (diverted) and a saddle-neighbourhood order of 1.366, with limited smooth maxima 8.4e-4 down to 3.8e-5 and diverted 1.6e-3 down to 2.9e-4 across 132 to 2616 cells (fragment anchor #cfs-topology-read-regular-rule). The limited figure is second order within the noise of a five-point fit; the assertion is fitted order at least p minus 0.1 with the raw order reported. The diverted figures share one mechanism: the saddle neighbourhood was defined as two pitches, so as cells shrink the smooth set reaches into the region where the gradient grows linearly with distance from the X-point and a third-order flux error is a first-order position error, while the cells inside the neighbourhood not covered by the normal-form branches keep the half-order level set. Ruling: the saddle neighbourhood is a physical radius r_nf, measured per case as the distance within which the curvature-corrected normal-form branches match the analytic separatrix to the budget, and every cell inside r_nf takes its support from those branches; outside it the quadratic level set has a gradient bounded below by |H|·r_nf and converges at second order with a constant proportional to 1/r_nf. Both orders are asserted at least 1.9 under this definition; a saddle neighbourhood still below that is a representation question for the lead.
