§1 — The exchange surface is under-specified, and the proof is measured

The transport–equilibrium window exchanges a dictionary that grew one field at a time, and nobody can state its contract. The convergence-composition study proved this the hard way: which exchanged fields participate in the stopping gate and which are merely receipted was decided post hoc, by measuring three candidate gate compositions against landed traces (all-field at 14 iterations, shape down-weighted at 11, shape-excluded at 9) and asking the owner to rule. A surface whose gate membership has to be discovered by experiment after the fact is a surface without a contract. The exchange today carries geometry face waveforms, source drives, boundary values and representation metadata with their roles, directions, time bases and differentiability classes implicit in solve_window's internals.

Four consumers now need the same surface, which is why the contract is worth a plan rather than a refactor commit. The window exchange is the first consumer. The time-parallel experiment showed the second: the Parareal coarse/fine handoff state is the window-boundary exchange state, and its qualified negative concluded that a useful coarse propagator must advance exactly this state without an equilibrium solve. The ensemble-filter direction (owner, 2026-08-22) makes it the third: an ensemble filter's state vector and its analysis-update injection point are this same window-boundary state. And the ambix learned surrogate makes it the fourth: a model trained to advance the coupled state must consume and emit a schema, and that schema must be this contract or the surrogate trains against a moving target. Defining the surface once, before the ambix assimilator hardens its interface, is the cheap ordering.

§2 — The contract

A typed CouplingState in nova/transport declaring, per field: physical meaning and units; exchange direction (equilibrium→transport, transport→equilibrium, or bidirectional); the time base it is sampled on (equilibrium coarse samples versus transport steps) and the interpolation rule between them; its stopping-gate role (gating, receipted, or excluded-with-reason — the shape channels' exclusion becomes a declared property instead of a solver constant); its differentiability class (traced, host-boundary, or static); and its fail-closed serialization. The window exchange in solve_window is refactored to construct, exchange and receipt this type, with the existing receipts (per-field exit residuals, gating and all-field norms, damping events, ledger closures) unchanged in content and provenance-stamped by the contract version. No physics changes; the gate composition and adaptive damping stay exactly as the two locked owner decisions state them.

The boundary the cross-repo survey fixed (2026-08-22): the per-slice, per-member state at the seam is ambix's FluxFunctionState — tested, immutable, COCOS-provenanced — and nova consumes it rather than recreating it. What ambix does NOT have, in code or contract, is the time-dependent piece: its interface plan specifies "time-varying coordinate maps, interpolation policy and convergence receipts containing iterations, contraction estimate, exit residual and damping" in prose, with no matching type anywhere in imas_ambix. The CouplingState is exactly that missing piece, defined on nova's side of the litmus (deterministic, geometry-bearing, no sigma), and its exported schema is the adapter contract the ambix consumer binds.

Done when the coupled-window suite passes unchanged on the refactored exchange, the gentle demonstration window reproduces its banked receipt (4 iterations, gating contraction 0.236, flux closure exactly 0) bit-for-bit on the gating trajectory, and a schema artifact (versioned field table: name, meaning, units, direction, time base, gate role, differentiability) is exported under scripts/coupling_surface/ for the ambix consumers to read.

§3 — Consumer bindings

Three thin bindings prove the contract carries its consumers, each a measure rather than a promise: (a) the window exchange consumes it (the §2 gate); (b) a window-boundary extract/restore round-trip — serialize the state at a window boundary, restore it, continue the solve, and show the continued trajectory matches the uninterrupted one to receipt precision, which is exactly the property both a time-parallel corrector and an ensemble-filter analysis update require at that boundary; (c) the exported schema is read back and validated field-for-field against the live exchange by a test, so schema drift fails visibly. The ensemble and filter consumers themselves are separate plans; this plan owes them only the surface.

§ Decisions

How is the transport–equilibrium coupling surface defined?

Owner direction 2026-08-22: the coupling surface is not well defined and must be, before the ambix assimilator hardens its interface. The gate-composition study is the measured evidence that implicit surfaces cost real iterations and post-hoc rulings.
