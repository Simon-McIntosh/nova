§1 — The exchange surface is under-specified, and the proof is measured

The transport–equilibrium window exchanges a dictionary that grew one field at a time, and nobody can state its contract. The convergence-composition study proved this the hard way: which exchanged fields participate in the stopping gate and which are merely receipted was decided post hoc, by measuring three candidate gate compositions against landed traces (all-field at 14 iterations, shape down-weighted at 11, shape-excluded at 9) and asking the owner to rule. A surface whose gate membership has to be discovered by experiment after the fact is a surface without a contract. The exchange today carries geometry face waveforms, source drives, boundary values and representation metadata with their roles, directions, time bases and differentiability classes implicit in solve_window's internals.

Four consumers now need the same surface, which is why the contract is worth a plan rather than a refactor commit. The window exchange is the first consumer. The time-parallel experiment showed the second: the Parareal coarse/fine handoff state is the window-boundary exchange state, and its qualified negative concluded that a useful coarse propagator must advance exactly this state without an equilibrium solve. The ensemble-filter direction (owner, 2026-08-22) makes it the third: an ensemble filter's state vector and its analysis-update injection point are this same window-boundary state. And the ambix learned surrogate makes it the fourth: a model trained to advance the coupled state must consume and emit a schema, and that schema must be this contract or the surrogate trains against a moving target. Defining the surface once, before the ambix assimilator hardens its interface, is the cheap ordering.
