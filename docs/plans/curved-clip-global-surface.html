§1 — Why this is a plan and not a section on the plan that owns the clip

plasma-edge-current-representation owns separatrix_clip and it is shipped at implementation 1.0 in a closed sprint. A finished plan is a record, not a container: work appended to it renders on the page and appears in no open path, so it would be hidden rather than tracked. The curved-clip question is live, has a named precondition that has just been satisfied, and needs write authority over a module a completed plan owns — which is three reasons it needs a plan of its own rather than a footnote on someone else's.

It is also not a section of coefficient-space-newton. That plan supplies the global surface and inherits the standing fence that the clip module is not written from the transport or solver side. A plan that specifies clip work while forbidding itself to do it is incoherent, and an earlier draft of its §16 was exactly that. This plan takes the write authority; that plan owes it the surface.

§2 — What is already built, and what refuted it twice

The machinery exists and is validated. A chord-to-hyperbola closed form agreeing with a subcell raster to 1.57e-5; a later C¹ bicubic route with oriented Green-theorem corrections for area and the first and mixed moments, the 12-node Gauss–Legendre _arc_rule along in-cell arcs, fixed bisection for edge roots and constrained Newton solves for extrema (commit 47808904). The sagitta survives as the arc-fit leverage measure in nova/biot/arc.py. Nothing here needs inventing.

The original argument was sound and remains so. A chord confines a surface top to an edge crossing while the surface is locally parabolic about its turning point, so the radial error scales as the square root of the cell height and no amount of area accuracy touches it. Clipping against the arc moves the extremum to second order while improving area only from second to third — which matched the observed split between large triangularity errors and moderate moment errors. Extremum error and area error are different classes and only one responds to better moments.

A monotonicity proof then corrected the specification. The section had specified an arc turning point that cannot exist: for bilinear flux the level-set derivative has a constant numerator, so every branch is monotone and its extrema are its endpoints. The square-root extremum error therefore belongs to the bilinear reconstruction rather than to the chord. The exact hyperbola correction was still validated to 1.6e-5 against a subcell raster — and measured worse on every decisive spherical-tokamak field, moving away from both reference routes.

The bicubic escalation was excellent and lost anyway, and that is the finding.

The mechanism is architectural, not a question of order. A local centred-tangent Hermite bicubic cannot recover the global surface information that both reference readers use. Fourth order on a smooth analytic surface and twenty per cent on a real EQDSK are perfectly consistent once the reason is seen: the two constructions converge to different surfaces, and both reference routes use the global one. A global spline solves a system in which every cell's polynomial is informed by the whole grid and is C²; a local Hermite stencil is C¹ and blind beyond its neighbours, and STEP's low aspect ratio amplifies the missing within-cell gradient structure. The corroborating reading is “two against one”: on STEP, TORAX and Nova's own contour extraction agree with each other markedly better than either agrees with the clipped route, so the clipped route is the outlier rather than the arbiter.

§3 — The precondition is now met

The prescription left behind is explicit and is recorded on the owning plan: if edge-current accuracy ever wants curvature corrections, take the curve from the global C² tensor B-spline and not from per-cell interpolants. At the time that spline was in flight. nova/linalg/tensor_spline.py landed on 2026-08-21 at commit 75e33479, and fit_tensor_spline fits a global cubic tensor spline whose evaluation already returns both second derivatives and the mixed term. The named condition is satisfied, so the refutation is retestable rather than final.

Two strengths of surface authority are available and they are not the same. The weaker one is available today: fit the global spline to the flux map and take the clip curve from it. The stronger one arrives if coefficient-space-newton makes that spline the carried solution object, because then the curve the clip cuts against and the surface the solver converges on are the same object and cannot diverge at all. This plan does not wait for the stronger form — the weaker one directly tests the prescription, and a negative there would close the question without the larger programme.

Stronger than met: the composition already ships, and nobody has re-scored it. The reuse map found that the production extraction path already does exactly what the prescription asked. extract_flux_surface_geometry reaches _surface_clips, which fits the complete flux map with fit_tensor_spline and passes each fixed 4×4 Bernstein block from TensorBSpline.cell_coefficients into the landed bicubic root, arc, Green-moment and constrained-extremum machinery. The rejected centred-tangent producer, _tensor_bicubic_coefficients, survives only behind the isolated legacy _clipped_surface_geometry route. So this plan builds nothing: it re-scores a curve source that is already in production against banks taken from two superseded ones. The banked local-bicubic figures at 47808904 were measured on the local producer, and the characterisation constants have moved with later extraction changes, so the historical numbers must be quoted independently rather than read off the current constants.

§6 — A gate closed in a benchmark receipt is not regression-protected

The re-score closed both STEP gates through a one-off driver, and nothing in the test suite holds them closed. The characterisation harness that produced the historical banks lives in tests/test_transport_geometry_reference.py, and the reuse map recorded that its constants "have moved with later extraction-service changes" — which is why the historical figures had to be quoted from 47808904 and its parent rather than read off the tree. That leaves an open question the re-score did not answer: whether those constants currently agree with what the production path measures. If they do not, the suite is either failing or characterising something other than the shipped route, and in both cases a future regression in the curve source would land silently.

This is deliberately not a re-derivation. The measured values are the ones already banked by §4 through the production path, so the work is to confirm the suite sees the same numbers and to make the closed gates a standing assertion rather than a receipt someone has to remember.

Done when the characterisation tests are executed and their outcome reported with the command and count; every constant that disagrees with the §4 production measurement is reconciled to the measured value with the previous constant and the difference quoted per field; and the STEP clipped-to-contour gates are asserted as bounds — vpr_face below 3.92 % and g1_face below 6.20 % — so a regression in the curve source fails a test rather than surviving until someone re-reads a receipt. A suite that already agrees with the measurement is the good outcome and is reported as such, with the assertion still added.

§5 — Method requirements

An analytic-surface result never stands alone here. Every claim carries its real-equilibrium counterpart, because the gap between them is the entire history of this question.

Name the production diff between compared trees and decline attribution when more than one candidate mechanism sits in it.

Never derive an acceptance criterion from an achieved value of the quantity it scores, and a criterion must be passable by something.

Respect the fixed-shape traced contract. The clip traces once over a moving separatrix with padded fixed capacities, and a hypothesis that those capacities were silently truncating STEP cells was already tested and falsified — required vertex count 5 against capacity 8, three spare slots. Any change here keeps that property and does not quietly widen a capacity constant, which is precisely the fix that would have reappeared on the next machine.

§ Decisions

Does the re-measurement take the weaker surface authority available today — a global spline fitted to the flux map — or wait for the stronger one, where the spline is the carried solution object and cannot diverge from the solve? The weaker form tests the prescription immediately and a negative would close the question outright; the stronger form removes a residual difference between the fitted surface and the converged one, but couples this plan's schedule to a larger programme.

Deduced from §3, which already states that this plan does not wait for the stronger form, and from §4, where a negative closes the curvature question outright. Waiting would couple an eight-hour re-measurement to a larger programme in order to remove a residual difference between a fitted surface and a converged one — a refinement that only matters if the fitted form first shows the global surface closing the gap the two local reconstructions opened. fit_tensor_spline supplies a global cubic tensor spline today whose evaluation returns both second derivatives and the mixed term, which is the whole content of the prescription the prior refutation left behind. The carried-surface form is deliberately NOT committed here: it becomes a question only on a non-negative fitted result, and a reportable negative retires it along with the rest of the curvature question.

§ Followups

Do not rebuild the curved clip, and do not gate on the analytic oracle

THE MACHINERY EXISTS AND REBUILDING IT WOULD WASTE THE PLAN. A chord-to-hyperbola closed form validated to 1.57e-5 against a subcell raster, a C1 bicubic route with oriented Green-theorem area and moment corrections, the 12-node Gauss-Legendre _arc_rule along in-cell arcs, fixed bisection for edge roots and constrained Newton extrema all landed at commit 47808904. Read that work and the evidence anchors bicubic-negative and clipped-cell-moments in docs/evidence/archive/flux-function-forward-transport-landed.html before writing anything. The single change this plan makes is WHERE THE CURVE COMES FROM.

GATE ON STEP, NOT ON THE ORACLE. This is the trap that caught the previous programme twice. The bicubic reached fourth order to six figures on a smooth analytic surface and then moved STEP vpr from 16.46 to 18.14 per cent against a 3.92 per cent gate and g1 from 9.78 to 22.31 against 6.20. An analytic-surface result is evidence about the primitive and about nothing else, because a local and a global construction converge to different surfaces and only the difference between them shows up on a low-aspect-ratio grid.

THE MONOTONICITY PROOF CONSTRAINS WHAT MAY BE CLAIMED. For bilinear flux the level-set derivative has a constant numerator, so every branch is monotone and its extrema are its endpoints - an arc turning point inside a cell cannot exist under that reconstruction, and the square-root extremum error belongs to the reconstruction rather than to the chord. Under a global C2 surface the reconstruction changes and that error class may not arise at all, but that is a PREDICTION. Measure it; do not assume the proof transfers unchanged to a different surface.

ANSWER THE OPEN DECISION BEFORE DISPATCH. The fitted global surface is available today and tests the prescription immediately; the carried surface from coefficient-space-newton removes a residual difference but couples this schedule to a larger programme. A negative on the fitted form would close the curvature question without needing the stronger one, which is an argument for taking it first.
