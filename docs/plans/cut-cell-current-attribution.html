Why this plan exists

Three measurements made on 2026-09-09 and 2026-09-10 do not fit together, and the misfit is the plan. First, the production chord clip attributes current to only 16 of the 43 cells the analytic separatrix cuts on the weak-rotation Solov'ev case at 110 cells, and every participating cut cell's zeroth, first and second moments are wrong against the exact density over the exact clipped region; substituting exact cut-cell moments into the linear flux image removes 98.6 percent of the squared flux error (uniform-cell-clip-and-coupling, cut-cell moment discriminator). Second, putting that exact participation into the production solve does not improve it: the weak and moderate static certificate rows terminate 63 and 11 times further from their fixed point, and when the two cells that resisted the general clip are placed back on chord moments the solve converges to 6.2e-13 but to a different equilibrium, its magnetic axis 0.533 m outboard of the analytic axis, while the never-converged chord baseline sits within 1.8 mm (the exact-placement discriminator, receipts under docs/figures/uniform-cell-clip-and-coupling/exact-participation/discriminator). Third, on the single-null case the solved flux already matches the analytic field to 1e-17 at 300 cells, yet the topology read admits no X-point at 110 or 300 cells, so the row fails joint qualification while its gradient and Hessian error concentrate around the X-point and along the legs.

The lead's reading, 2026-09-10, is the rule this plan is built on: an improved component that reduces acceptance is evidence that the model is not correct, not evidence against the component. A discretisation that lands near the reference only because its clip error cancels an error elsewhere is compensating, and what the certificate has been scoring is the compensation. The acceptance of this plan is therefore convergence to the reference with exact inputs, never a residual or a pass count on its own. The X-point cell mechanism that sol-current-demonstration recorded as a prerequisite and deferred while confined current made it a 0.2 A effect (the saddle cell is crossed four times by the analytic separatrix and production draws one chord with up to 15 mm endpoint displacement; evidence anchor saddle-gap-current-check) is now first-order for the read and is carried here as §2. The time is now.

§1 — Root cause: where the attribution error lives and why exact placement changes the basin

Two questions, both answerable with the machinery already in the held stacks. Where, cell by cell, does the chord clip misattribute current, and is the X-point cell of the single-null case the four-crossing cell the saddle-gap audit predicted, handled by one chord? And what does exact placement change in the solve such that its fixed point moves half a metre: the total current normalisation, the boundary flux level the read returns, the profile's placement in normalised flux, or the coupling image itself. The node in flight (ucc-xpoint-cell-allocation-rca, run r-20260910T035754118776, base 28a49bcb) delivers the first question with three figures per row on diverted -110 and -300 and weak -110: a close-up of the cell with the atomic hex, the analytic separatrix branches, the chord polygon, the exact spline-chain polygon and the analytic core-side region; a whole-domain line-contour panel of the per-cell attribution error with every cut cell outlined; and a ranked bar chart of absolute per-cell error, chord beside exact. Its receipt tabulates crossing count, analytic core current, chord and exact attribution and the three moment errors for every cut cell, and for the X-point cell the analytic X-point position inside it, the chord endpoints, the exact chain vertices and the nearest topology-read saddle candidate with its containment verdict and distance.

The second question is a paired measurement on arm b's terminal state against the analytic equilibrium: plasma current and its centroid, the boundary flux value the read returns against the analytic separatrix level, the normalisation anchor (axis flux) used for psi_N, and the flux image of the analytic current through the exact operator versus through the chord operator, each reported as a difference from the reference with its own contour panel. §2 — The X-point cell: saddle vertex as a clip vertex, branch-paired chords, per-branch supports

The single-chord clip cannot represent a cell the separatrix enters and leaves twice. The mechanism recorded on the boundary-ring audit and the SOL demonstration is carried here unchanged in shape and made concrete: the fitted saddle position is inserted as a vertex of the clipped polygon; the four edge roots are paired by branch (the two roots each branch connects through the saddle) so the clip yields the core-side wedge, the private-flux wedge and the two common-SOL wedges as separate polygons of fixed vertex capacity; and the current moments integrate per wedge with the profile of that wedge's region (the confined profile on the core wedge, zero or the SOL closure elsewhere). The complement-clip machinery already exists (common_support), and the general ordered spline-chain clip on the held stack (0372d3fc) already inserts every edge root in polygon order, so the X-point cell is the case where two chains meet at one interior vertex. Fixed shapes are kept by padding: every cut cell carries capacity for four roots and one interior vertex, with unused slots exact zero, so the traced program does not branch on the cell's crossing count.

The read follows the clip. With the saddle carried as a vertex of the cut, the cell partition on the plasma cells (the saddle-aware flood of plasma-cell-topology-authority) sees the X-point cell split into its four regions, and the null census has a cell whose geometry states where the saddle is instead of one that straddles it; the no-admitted-X-point verdicts at 110 and 300 cells on the single-null case are the first test of whether that is sufficient, and if the read still refuses, the refusal names its reason per candidate.

Done-when. On the single-null certificate case at 110, 300 and 500 cells, one all_debug oracle job reports the X-point cell's core-side current within 1e-6 relative of the analytic wedge integral and exactly zero private-flux and common-SOL current for the confined profile; one H200 job (one card, rows sequential, receipts and contour panels persisted per row) re-solves the four diverted certificate rows with the treatment on and reports the admitted X-point at 110 and 300 cells with position error inside one characteristic pitch, the joint-qualified diverted count against the committed 1 of 4, and every terminal residual beside its committed baseline with no regression. The four limited rows are re-run in the same job as the negative control and must be bit-identical, since they have no saddle to cut.

§3 — One discretisation: the flux read and the current placement from the same clipped geometry

The chord clip and the exact clip both take their edge roots and density coordinates from the same flux interpolant (shared_node_flux, support_flux_coefficients), and the current moments feed the polygon coupling (coupling_current_moments into current_moment_image) with zeroth, first and second moments. Exact moments under a boundary located by a linear edge interpolation, or a boundary located exactly under moments computed on a chord, are two halves of two different discretisations, and the solve finds the fixed point of whichever pair it is given. This section makes the pair one: the boundary the read returns, the region the current integrates over and the polynomial space the density is evaluated in are the same object per cell, so that with the analytic flux supplied the operator returns the analytic current image and the analytic boundary, and with the analytic current supplied the solve converges to the analytic equilibrium. The unified clip-and-moment path on uniform-cell-clip-and-coupling is where this is built; this section owns its acceptance and the design constraint, which the S19 discriminator established: the clipped geometry enters the flux read and the current placement together or exact moments alone move the answer away from the reference.

The design question the lead decides after §1 is which representation carries the read: the six-point ring quadratic extended to the clipped support, the global tensor spline the extraction route already fits, or the cellwise polynomial the moments are evaluated in. Each is a candidate for one reason and disqualified for one reason today, and §1's paired differences are what makes the choice an evidence question rather than a preference.

Done-when. The certificate is re-run with exact participation and the consistent read in one H200 job, and on every row at 300 cells and finer the solve converges to the analytic axis within one characteristic pitch with terminal residual at or below 1e-12, the diverted rows additionally admitting the X-point inside one pitch; arm b of the exact-placement discriminator is re-run as a preregistered test and lands on the analytic axis; the joint-qualified count is reported before and after; and every remaining error term is recorded by cell class with its magnitude, none retained because it improves a score. The chord clip is retired as the production default only when this done-when holds on all sixteen rows; until then it stays the default and the exact path stays behind the benchmark flag.

§4 — Acceptance rule and the held stacks

Acceptance for this plan and for every plan it informs is convergence to the reference with exact inputs: position of the axis and of every admitted X-point inside one characteristic pitch, terminal residual inside the locked recovery bound, and the joint qualification rule of null-identification-authority, reported per row. A pass count is a summary of that, never the gate. A component that is closer to the analytic answer on its own oracle and regresses the certificate is retained as evidence of a remaining inconsistency and is never reverted to recover the score. Two held stacks are inputs to §3 and are neither merged nor discarded until it lands: the exact participation stack at 0372d3fc (nineteen commits: general ordered spline-chain clip, cell confinement, memory-safe scan) and the discriminator stack at 28a49bcb (five commits: the arm driver, the support-splice padding fix, the relaxation instrumentation that showed the accelerated Krylov route never reads a step relaxation). The clipped-moment stack at 1c4daf51 on null-identification-authority (boundary-support trace, converges weak to 6.6e-13, breaks moderate to 0.105) is a third witness of the same inconsistency and its lead question stays open until §1 reports.

§ Decisions

What is the acceptance for cut-cell attribution work: a terminal residual, a pass count, or convergence to the analytic equilibrium?

Lead, 2026-09-10: a 100 percent pass rate is not a hard gate, and an improved model that reduces acceptance is an indication that the model is not correct. Exact inputs must give the exact answer: axis and admitted X-points inside one pitch, residual inside the locked bound, jointly. A component that regresses the certificate while agreeing better with its own oracle is evidence about the remaining components and is kept, not reverted.

Does the deferred X-point cell mechanism (saddle vertex as a clip vertex, branch-paired chords, per-branch supports) get built now or stay a prerequisite for the SOL closure?

Lead, 2026-09-10: we developed a plan to treat this correctly when the time comes, and the time is now. The single-chord X-point cell is first-order for the topology read on the single-null certificate rows (no admitted X-point at 110 and 300 cells while the flux is exact) and a lot of the visible error is driven from the placement of the primary X-point.

Which representation carries the flux read that the current placement must be consistent with: the six-point ring quadratic extended over the clipped support, the global tensor spline, or the cellwise polynomial the moments are evaluated in?

Lead, 2026-09-10: the global tensor spline, unless an alternative capable of the same accuracy and shared fidelity shows a significant performance benefit. A key feature of the global spline is its shared state, which makes downstream processes such as flux-surface averages much easier. Any challenger must match accuracy and shared fidelity and beat it materially on speed before it is considered.

§ Open questions

The lead asked for the plasma current scaling multiplier (lambda), which should sit near unity. The banked lambda amplitude history answers: the chord clip needs amplitude 0.930 at the seed and 1.050 at the terminal state on weak -110 (discriminator control arm), while the exact support on the SAME seed state needs 2.201 and reaches 5.561 at its terminal state (c-r100), 2.275 and 4.048 on arm b, and 2.036 and 4.658 on the moderate exact row. At unit amplitude the exact support therefore carries less than half the current the chord support carries on an identical state, and the solve rescales the profile two to five times to meet the current constraint, which is why it finds a different equilibrium. Cut cells carry only a few percent of the plasma current, so a factor of two cannot come from them: the exact mode is dropping or double-clipping current in cells it should not touch. Suspects, from nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py at 28a49bcb: _profile_support in exact mode traces every cell whose vertices straddle curved_level (participation = masks.profile_participation | vertex_participation) and then applies .qualify(participation), so interior cells outside participation may be zeroed; and curved_level switches between the split-spline level set and a local quadratic on surface.fit_executed, whose sign convention against inside_boundary has not been asserted. This revises the discriminator's verdict: before the flux-read consistency is blamed, the exact support must first carry the right total current at unit amplitude. The unit-amplitude current census node (cca-unit-amplitude-current-census, run r-20260910T052734465931) measures it cell by cell on the seed and terminal states.

Lead direction 2026-09-10 adopted: merge the robust work and then RCA the required fixes. The held stacks are merged only with production defaults unchanged, which at 28a49bcb they are not: _SUPPORT_CLIP_MODE defaults to 'exact' in nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py, so the merge node flips the default to 'chord', proves the committed certificate rows bit-identical at the merged head (the control arm already reproduces weak -110 exactly under the chord flag), and lands the exact path behind the flag for the RCA to work from main. The boundary-support trace stack (1c4daf51) carries retain_boundary_cut_current defaulting False, but the trace itself has no switch, so landing it means adding one that defaults off (the lead's option b) or holding it. ROOT CAUSE FOUND, 2026-09-10, from two census nodes reading the same code. The production current moments have not clipped any cell at the separatrix since 2026-08-27: commit c9a89e58 (fix(equilibrium): use profile-owned residual support) replaced traced_clip(signed_flux) and traced_clip(-signed_flux), a real chord clip against the signed flux, with traced_clip(jnp.ones(...)) qualified by the profile participation label, so every cell is booked whole or not at all by which side of the separatrix its centroid falls on (nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py _profile_support, main; observation.py:558 current_ledger; domain.py open_field_line). Everything measured since is downstream of that: the 16-of-43 participation on weak -110 is the count of cut cells whose centroids sit inside; the dominated error cells on the single-null 110 row are two-crossing cells booked whole (840.6 A against 85.6 A analytic, 717.5 against 34.9, 654.9 against 21.2); the four-crossing X-point cell is booked whole (459.7 A against a 10.5 A sliver); and the 0.196 percent scrape-off-layer current on the transport machine is 844 A in sixteen separatrix-crossing cells with outside centroids plus 228 A in fifteen open cells carrying the edge-extrapolated profile, none of it geometric clipping. The exact-support mode on the held stack is the first real clip since then, which is why it carries less than half the current at unit amplitude that the whole-cell booking carries and needs 2.2 to 5.6 times the amplitude: it is being compared against a baseline that was never clipping. Consequences for this plan: §1's second question (why exact placement moves the fixed point) is reframed as which of the two is right at unit amplitude against the analytic total, the census node cca-unit-amplitude-current-census measures exactly that; and the chord clip as a baseline is retired as a concept, the committed behaviour is whole-cell booking and must be named so in every table.
