Why this plan exists

nova ingests plasma data from the IMAS Data Dictionary. The lead ruled on 21 October 2026 (decision d-ippc-dd-cocos on iter-passive-plasma-circuit): nova reads DD3 data as COCOS 11 and DD4 data as COCOS 17. It uses COCOS 17 internally everywhere, and it treats data that does not conform to its own DD version's COCOS as invalid. DD3 data enters through simple, reusable translators.

The ruling followed a measured defect. nova/imas/plasma_source.py::PlasmaSlice.prescribe hard-codes one flux orientation: it refuses whenever ψ_boundary − ψ_axis has the same sign as the plasma current, which is the σ_Bp = −1 convention of COCOS 13 and 17. That guard was calibrated on DINA 105014/1. Measured over every slice above 1 MA, that case is stored as DD 3.38.1 yet carries σ_Bp = −1 in all 732 slices, so it does not conform to DD3. The guard therefore refused every conforming ITER source surveyed: CORSICA 130506, spiderInverse 131000 and 121000, and JINTRAC 134000, all COCOS 11 as DD3 requires. Of the DINA cases, 105052/1, 105069/3 and 105013/1 conform.

nova already owns one COCOS algebra: the stdlib-only nova_cocos package (packages/nova-cocos, plan cocos-standalone-distribution), with identify_convention, convention_transform(source=, target=) and its single factor table. This plan builds on it and adds no second table. imas-ambix owns its own COCOS conventions for the challenge corpus, and it consumes nova, so nova must not import ambix. The design review's prior-art scout reads ambix's COCOS handling only for reuse of ideas, not of code.

§1 — The census: every IDS reader and the convention it assumes

The census lists every place nova reads a COCOS-dependent quantity from an IDS: ψ and its derivatives, the plasma current, B0 and F, q, p′ and FF′, and the flux-loop and pick-up signals whose sign follows ψ or B_p. For each, it records the file and function, the quantities read, the convention the code assumes (explicitly, or implicitly through a sign test such as the plasma-source guard), and the DD versions its tests exercise. Each assumption is checked against nova_cocos.identify_convention applied to a fixture of the data that reader is tested on.

Done when, recorded in docs/research/dd-cocos-census.html: (1) the census table covers every reader found by a search for the IDS paths above under nova/ and scripts/, with the search command and its hit count stated so the table's completeness can be checked; (2) each row names the convention it assumes and whether that matches the DD version of its test data, with a fixture-backed verdict; (3) the internal consumers (solve, topology read, transport, circuit) are listed with the convention each now works in, which is the migration list for §4. Negative control: a reader whose guard is inverted in a scratch copy must change its census verdict.

§2 — One reusable DD3 translator, with DD4 passthrough and a conformance refusal

One module translates an equilibrium-bearing IDS read at DD3 into COCOS 17, and passes DD4 through unchanged. It decides conformance from the data's own sign tuple (the plasma current, B0, the ψ ordering from axis to boundary, and the sign of q) through nova_cocos.identify_convention. It refuses data whose tuple does not match its DD version's COCOS with a named error that states the DD version, the expected COCOS and the measured sign tuple. The translation applies nova_cocos.convention_transform(source=11, target=17) to every quantity in the census, so the factors come from the one table.

Done when: (1) CORSICA 130506/6 (DD 3.41.0) translated by the module matches, quantity by quantity, the analytic COCOS 11 → 17 mapping of the same slice, and a DD4 fixture passes bit-identically; (2) over every slice above 1 MA, DINA 105014/1 is refused by name, while DINA 105052/1, 105069/3, 105013/1 and CORSICA 130506/6 are accepted; (3) the module has one public entry point that readers call, documented in its docstring with the two DD conventions. Literal negative control: negate ψ on a conforming DD3 slice and the module refuses it.

§3 — The readers go through the translator

Every reader in the census takes its data through the §2 module, starting with nova/imas/plasma_source.py, whose fixed-orientation guard is removed in favour of the module's conformance refusal. The plasma source then works in COCOS 17 whatever DD version it was read from.

Done when: (1) PlasmaSource.from_imas prescribes CORSICA 130506/6 and DINA 105069/3, and refuses DINA 105014/1 by name; (2) every census reader calls the §2 entry point, and a search for a remaining fixed-orientation sign test returns none; (3) the readers' test files pass by delta against the base, each in its own fresh process, with every changed expectation named by the reader it belongs to. This section unblocks the reconstruction check of iter-passive-plasma-circuit §6, whose reference is CORSICA 130506/6.

§4 — nova's internals run in COCOS 17

The internal consumers the census lists (the forward solve, the topology read, transport, the circuit tier and their caches) work in COCOS 17. A consumer still in another convention is converted at its own boundary, never by a sign flip at a call site.

Done when: (1) every internal consumer in the census works in COCOS 17, each shown by a test that fixes a known COCOS 17 state and checks the consumer's sign-sensitive output; (2) the certified forward-solve rows and the analytic oracles keep their published values, by delta against the base; (3) cached artefacts whose stored fields change convention carry the convention in their key, and an entry written before the change is refused or translated, never served silently.

§5 — A valid measure of edge consistency between flux functions and flux map

The lead's criterion for a reference equilibrium is that its flux functions agree with its flux map at the edge. The survey that ranked the ITER sources could not measure that: its map-implied current carried a uniform factor of 225 to 300 in every case, DINA included, which is an operator artefact. This section builds the measure properly. It takes the Grad-Shafranov residual of the stored map against the current density from the stored p′ and FF′, in COCOS 17 after translation, in a band inside the boundary, and reports it as an absolute current density and against the local profile current.

Done when: (1) on an analytic Solov'ev state the residual is zero to the discretisation's stated order, and a 1 % FF′ perturbation is detected; (2) the measure is reported for DINA 105052/1, 105069/3 and 105013/1 and for CORSICA 130506/6, in a table in docs/research/dd-cocos-census.html; (3) the reference choice of iter-passive-plasma-circuit §6 is confirmed or revised on that table.
