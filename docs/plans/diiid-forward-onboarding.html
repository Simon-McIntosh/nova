§ Decisions

Which source is authoritative for the DIII-D machine description?

The scorer's ground truth was produced against the machine the dataset describes; the shipped geometry is the challenge's own contract. External files fill the two holes (wall, passive) and validate the rest.

How is vertical placement handled without magnetics?

The recorded coil currents are the closed-loop currents — the information that placed the plasma is already in the inputs. A constraint hook would smuggle a fitting term into the forward solve and break the seam authority; the receipt gives the consumer the conditioning information a hook would have hidden.

How many vessel eigenmodes per machine does vertical placement and ramp fidelity require?

What replaces the inapplicable starter-kit bar as the section-2 kill gate?

The measured diagnosis established that the published 0.94 is reached by fitting exterior psi to Green functions, while this plan forbids fitting anywhere in a forward. The two tests are not comparable, so the gate as written cannot pass or fail the entry premise. Our geometry and kernels were independently validated by the same diagnosis, so this is a question about what to measure, not about whether the physics is right.

Owner 2026-08-17: the answer is sitting in plain sight — we know the GS operator and can separate the flux functions, hence we can solve for plasma current, fit our nova filaments to it and thus subtract it from the field; with this differencing trick we have all the vacuum shots we need (we subtract the EFIT plasma) and can therefore verify our coil model in the absence of a plasma. The method is already de-risked: it is the rearrangement of the corrected composition the audit proved at R-squared 0.9967 on construction-exact synthetic truth, but the differenced field is the vacuum field itself, usable across the whole grid rather than an exterior band, and the transient-frame remainder minus the coil model is exactly the passive-model fitting corpus. Corpus convention handling enters through the imas-ambix challenge package's pinned authority, never a nova-side adapter.

How are the challenge corpus's flux, current and sign conventions handled across every consumer?

Owner step-back directive 2026-08-17: the campaign dived into the DIII-D flux maps without a systematic COCOS evaluation, while nova runs COCOS 17 internally. The per-benchmark ad-hoc factors that accumulated (a 2-pi per-radian conversion here, a fixed global sign there) produced exactly the predicted failure class: the composition audit caught the global flux sign applied twice, once to the coil term and once to a plasma term that already carried label convention, worth 8.5 points of exterior R-squared on synthetic truth (1.0 correct versus -3.20 through the unmodified gate).

Owner directive 2026-08-17, amended same day with the boundary correction: the pinned convention authority is imas-ambix work and must be kept separate from nova — the corpus reader owns its conventions, nova stays COCOS 17 and receives convention-clean quantities. The nova-side adapter node was stopped mid-run before any commit. The policy content is unchanged: one measured, pinned authority; ad-hoc per-consumer factors banned (a convention living in one imported home cannot be applied twice, which is the failure the composition audit measured at 8.5 points of exterior R-squared).
