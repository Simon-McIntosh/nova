§1 — Purpose and authority

Nova's side of the magnetics-free challenge entry (grounded facts: sophelio-challenge-geometry-sourcing and imas-ambix:sophelio-fusion-equilibrium-challenge): every deterministic capability the actuator-only inference problem needs, delivered as receipts-bearing forwards with no measurement fitting anywhere. The authority split follows the standing seam rule sharpened to produce, don't use: nova produces every response kernel — coupling operators, chord sampling, label-map extraction — exactly as it produces the magnetics design matrix it does not fit with; everything statistical about a signal stays in ambix. Beyond the competition, this plan is the first true second-machine onboarding, the direct test of the machine-portability claim: a repeatable recipe from shipped machine description to converged free-boundary forwards.

§2 — DIII-D machine description through the registry route

Ingest the dataset-shipped conductor geometry (18 F-coils with section dimensions and six skew angles — parallelogram sections routed through the polygonal-section analytic kernels — plus ECOILA and bcoil with declared turns-uncertainty fields) and per-shot Thomson chord coordinates as diagnostic-geometry records. Source the wall contour and passive-structure geometry externally (published DIII-D descriptions; disclosed), with provenance receipts on every element. The registry recipe is written down as it is executed — machine onboarding is the deliverable, DIII-D is its first instance.

Done-when: vacuum ψ on the shipped efit_grid from coil currents reproduces the challenge starter-kit vacuum check (reported R²≈0.94 class — exact bar pre-registered from the starter kit itself); geometry provenance receipts complete. This is a kill gate for the entry: failure means the shipped geometry or our kernels are wrong, and everything downstream waits.

§3 — Limiter-topology forward mode

A wall-contact boundary mode beside the diverted mode in the forward solve: LCFS by limiter contact, topology class and transition handling in the convergence receipts. Never scored by the challenge (limited frames are deleted corpus-wide) but load-bearing twice over: the reduced ramp traversal hands over its state at the diverted transition, and mid-pulse topology churn must be traversed without solver failure. Validation leans on assets the challenge deleted: synthetic limited equilibria round-trips, plus agreement of the topology classifier against our own MAST solves on limited frames (in-house data, no MAST EFIT involved).

Done-when: limited/diverted mode switch is receipt-visible; synthetic round-trips green; classifier agreement on in-house MAST limited frames banked.

§4 — Passive-conductor circuit forwards

Eigenmode-reduced vessel circuit models driven by coil-current and plasma-current time derivatives, per machine: DIII-D geometry from the external description with resistances/eigenmode amplitudes fitted by ambix on labeled ramp frames (competition data); MAST from the in-house vessel circuit fit, pre-existing and challenge-untouched. With no solver-side vertical pin (the owner decision: NK stabilisation plus state-vector attribution, never a constraint hook), the passive model sets the field decay index during every transient — it is promoted from ramp-frame nicety to the entry's highest-risk physics component and gets hostile attention first.

Done-when: passive forward operators are exposed through the seam with conservation receipts; the eigenmode-order study on labeled DIII-D ramp frames is banked (order decision locked); MAST vessel model wired from the registry.

§5 — Response-kernel library: sampling and label-map extraction

The kernels ambix's measurement models consume: chord→ψN sampling against a solved or labeled map; the Δ* operator applied to gridded maps giving jφ; per-surface radial separation recovering p′(ψ) and FF′(ψ) with conditioning-aware uncertainty (axis degeneracy, X-point ∇ψ→0); and the vacuum-region Δ*ψ≈0 receipt that doubles as a label-sanity flag. Mostly a hardening pass over the FSA-extraction and p′/FF′-projection machinery built for the transport seam.

Done-when: extraction round-trips prescribed p′/FF′ on nova-solved equilibria within pre-registered tolerance; on challenge train labels, extracted flux functions re-solve to the labeled map at the grid-resolution floor. Second kill gate: if the labels are not self-consistent under our own GS operator, the extraction premise — and with it the entry's training signal — is broken.

§6 — Batched throughput and vertical-conditioning receipts

The measured compute budget: batched ensemble×frame NK solves with warm-start branch continuity along each shot, and a per-solve vertical-mode conditioning receipt (n=0 stability margin / decay index) so the consumer can widen ensemble spread where vertical placement is ill-conditioned — receipts, not rails. Rides the accelerated boundary-push and catalog-batching machinery; publishes a slices/s budget table on the H200 node from which ambix sizes ensembles and windows, replacing the assumed compute envelope with a measured one.

Done-when: budget table published (device, batch, precision, convergence policy); conditioning receipt wired through the seam; branch-continuity behaviour pinned by tests.

§ Decisions

Which source is authoritative for the DIII-D machine description?

The scorer's ground truth was produced against the machine the dataset describes; the shipped geometry is the challenge's own contract. External files fill the two holes (wall, passive) and validate the rest.

How is vertical placement handled without magnetics?

The recorded coil currents are the closed-loop currents — the information that placed the plasma is already in the inputs. A constraint hook would smuggle a fitting term into the forward solve and break the seam authority; the receipt gives the consumer the conditioning information a hook would have hidden.

How many vessel eigenmodes per machine does vertical placement and ramp fidelity require?
