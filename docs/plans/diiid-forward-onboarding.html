§10 — Exact taring, and reading the unclaimed current

Owner-specified pipeline, 2026-08-21. §9 established that the missing boundary field does not live in the five-conductor subspace, and left the right question unanswered: which degrees of freedom does it need? This section answers that empirically rather than by proposing conductors — read the current the labels actually contain, subtract what is claimed, and look at what is left.

The accuracy upgrade that makes the rest possible. The landed differencing gate represents plasma current by node-centred point filaments: each interior grid node carries jφ × cell_area at its centroid, giving a 2.0 % median fractional RMS against the direct operator. That 2.0 % is the floor on everything downstream — a residual patch smaller than it cannot be distinguished from tare error. Replacing the lumped filament with the exact clipped cell — zeroth, radial and vertical moments over the true clipped polygon, from TracedClippedSupports and the moment stencils already in nova/equilibrium/stencil_mesh.py — removes both the whole-cell lumping at the boundary and the centroid collapse in the interior.

The self-validating closure test. Re-apply Δ* to the tared map interior to the LCFS. Every ampere there was ours to remove, so whatever remains is tare error, measured directly rather than inferred. This is a stronger check than the flux-space R² the differencing gate used, because it is local and it has a known correct answer of zero.

Then the unclaimed current. With the plasma removed to a demonstrated floor, Δ* on the tared map outside the LCFS returns current that no plasma cell accounts for. Attributing it splits the §9 question in two: current that is vessel-shaped — distributed, following the wall, decaying with the drive — is passive structure, and its geometry can be recovered from where it sits; current that is conductor-shaped — compact, localised, persistent — is a missing coil. Either answer is progress, and the second would name the degrees of freedom §9 could not.

Closing the loop. If passive structure is recoverable, re-tare to remove it and what remains should be coil field alone. Regularised least squares against the known conductor geometry then recovers coil currents that can be compared against the given channels — an end-to-end validation of the whole chain, using the shipped currents as the check rather than as an input.

Relation to the §4 deferral, which this does not override. §4's passive work was deferred because "a passive model fitted against a forward solve that does not yet close would absorb that solve's errors into vessel parameters". That hazard is specific to a solver in the loop. This route has none: passive current is read from the labels through Δ*, an operator that round-trips at 2.29e-8. The deferral's stated reason therefore does not apply to this method, and §4's fitted-vessel scope stays deferred regardless.

Two costs to measure before spending them. The dataset holds 1,559,340 labelled frames over 7,041 shots; at 65×65 float64 a full tared-map set is order 50 GB, so per-slice cost and total storage are reported from a bounded cohort before any full-dataset production run. And the 603 polarity-affected shots must be screened or flagged in anything that regresses currents.

Done-when: the moment-based tare's interior Δ* residual is reported against the landed 2.0 % filament floor; the hex-meshed vessel and its self-interaction operator are banked with conditioning receipts; unclaimed current outside the LCFS is localised and classified vessel-shaped or conductor-shaped; and, if passive geometry is recoverable, the re-tared coil-current regression is compared against the given channels. No recovered quantity may become an inference-time input that the entry cannot reproduce from actuator channels alone — the §9 lesson stands.

What replaces the inapplicable starter-kit bar as the section-2 kill gate?

The measured diagnosis established that the published 0.94 is reached by fitting exterior psi to Green functions, while this plan forbids fitting anywhere in a forward. How are the challenge corpus's flux, current and sign conventions handled across every consumer?

Owner step-back directive 2026-08-17: the campaign dived into the DIII-D flux maps without a systematic COCOS evaluation, while nova runs COCOS 17 internally.
