DIII-D vertical force balance

The EFIT reproduction gate reports all five DIII-D frames nonconverged with a whole-plasma vertical displacement of about 0.3 of span. The triage of 2026-09-03 (~/.config/reckon/crew/reports/nova/triage/diiid-vertical.md) read the receipts and the driver and found three separable facts. The seed is not the problem: the benchmark seeds the axis at EFIT's own (rmaxis, zmaxis) and the diverted branch at the boundary contour's lowest point, yet the terminal axis lands 0.29 to 1.29 m away on every frame. The total plasma current is not the problem: achieved_current_a matches the target to float precision on all five. The leading mechanism is the external field: the PF circuit current model records that its post-fit flux closure passes 1 of 60 frames against the 54-of-60 rule and that individual conductors carry fit residuals up to 7.75 percent, and an elongated diverted DIII-D plasma is marginally stable vertically, so a few-percent field error is amplified into exactly this kind of whole-plasma vertical excursion. Two gate definitions confound the reading: the branch selector's persistence threshold of three observations can never be satisfied on a single static solve, so converged is structurally false even on frame 179 whose residual reaches 1.3e-10, and the DIII-D error column is a fraction of centred EFIT RMS over the label LCFS interior while the MAST column is a fraction of peak-to-peak span over the whole grid.

§1 — Decompose the displacement

Bank Nova's post-solve magnetic axis for every DIII-D frame beside the EFIT axis (today only the scalar distance is written, at benchmarks/diiid_forward_gs_match.py near line 1403) and decompose the offset into radial and vertical components; add the same for the X-point and the geometric centre of the terminal boundary so the displacement is characterised as a rigid shift, a tilt or a shape change. Done-when: a five-frame receipt states ΔR and ΔZ of axis, X-point and boundary centroid per frame with the sign convention recorded, confirming or refuting from banked numbers that the displacement is dominantly vertical.

§2 — Is the external field complete? Tare the label and read the residual

The lead's framing (2026-09-03): before any solver-side explanation, entertain the possibility that Nova does not carry the full external field on DIII-D. Nova can tare the plasma out of the EFIT flux map (the exact clipped-cell current-moment tare, benchmarks/diiid_exact_clipped_tare.py, with its Delta-star closure and known zero target) and tare the coils out with the registered polygon-section response driven by the recorded currents (benchmarks/diiid_vacuum_against_exact_tare.py, which also removes the arbitrary additive gauge and reports the first-order projection on normalised R and Z without ever adding it to the prediction). What remains is the flux the model does not carry. Examine that residual on the five gate frames and on the wider tare cohort for structure: distributed current following the vessel contour (passive structure the model omits or mis-weights), localised patches at conductor positions (external coils or coil halves missing from the description, the five absent ohmic channels of diiid_circuit_driven_vacuum.py and diiid_solenoid_inclusion_ladder.py being the first candidates), or a smooth vertical-field or dipole component whose magnitude is compared against what would move a marginally stable elongated plasma by the observed 0.29 to 1.29 m. The landed vessel-versus-conductor geometry discriminator and exterior-patch detection (benchmarks/diiid_unclaimed_current_patches.py, diiid_unclaimed_current_origin.py, receipts under docs/figures/diiid-forward-onboarding/exact-tare/, ohmic-circuit/, diverted-root/) are the instruments; they are reused, not rebuilt. Binding rule (decision no-unphysical-external-constraints): the residual is diagnosed and, where it names a physical source, that source is added to the machine description with its measured or recorded current; it is never absorbed by fitting a coefficient, adding a constraint pin, or imposing an external condition the machine does not have. Done-when: a receipt per frame states the tared residual's gauge-free norm, its decomposition into vessel-following, conductor-localised and smooth-multipole parts with the fraction of the residual each carries, the vertical-field magnitude and the axis displacement it would produce, and a verdict naming the physical source of the missing field or stating that the residual is inside the label's own Grad-Shafranov inconsistency; figures show the residual map beside the vessel and conductor geometry for each frame.

§ Decisions

no-unphysical-external-constraints

The five DIII-D gate frames seeded at the EFIT axis end 0.29 to 1.29 m away. The PF circuit model's own receipts record flux closure passing 1 of 60 frames and conductor fit residuals to 7.75 percent, five ohmic channels are absent from the shipped description, and the exact clipped-cell tare has already found current outside the labelled LCFS on the wider cohort.

Lead ruling 2026-09-03: entertain the possibility that Nova has not captured the full external field. Tare the plasma current out of the EFIT flux map and the coils out with the recorded currents, then examine the residual for passive vessel structure or missing external coils. Where the residual names a physical source, that source enters the machine description with its measured or recorded current. Unphysical external constraints are never enforced: no fitted coefficient, no constraint pin standing in for a conductor, no imposed boundary condition the machine does not have.
