§1 — The measurement, and why it outranks the plan that found it

A node measuring something else found this and could not carry it. Two forward arms — the shipped exact-value route and the second-order support variant — converge on their own discrete fixed points to 1.221e-15 to 2.494e-15, and sit 0.258 to 0.451 in relative-sup error against a closed-form solution on identical carriers, supports, masks and denominator. A solve that has converged to machine precision and is still tens of per cent from the analytic answer is either solving a different problem or being compared against one.

Two explanations are already excluded by measurement rather than by argument. The comparison lane is not at fault: all four fresh rows reproduced the banked scores exactly, maximum delta 0.0 against a 5e-12 tolerance, so the figures are reproducible on the lane that produced them. And the flux gauge — the leading suspect, on the standing rule that ψN is gauge-safe only when its anchors come from the field being normalised — is measured and only partly responsible.

§2 — Operator or setup, and the distinction the receipt did not draw

The landed verdict reads genuine_discrete_operator_error, and that is correct as far as it was measured: it distinguishes the operator from the comparison. It does not distinguish the operator from the posing — how the closed-form case is handed to the solve. Those are different defects with different repairs and the evidence so far cannot separate them, so this plan does not inherit the stronger reading.

  • Posing. The analytic case may be fed with an external field, a boundary condition or a source normalisation that does not correspond to the closed form. This predicts an offset that survives refinement and that shows most strongly at the boundary — which is what is observed.
  • Operator. The discrete map may genuinely fail to reproduce the analytic solution. This predicts an error that falls under refinement, and the coarse-to-fine rows show almost no movement, so it is the weaker hypothesis on present evidence.

The cheapest discriminator is not a code audit. Refine further and watch: a posing error is a constant, an operator error converges. A second, nearly free check is to feed the discrete operator the analytic solution itself and apply the map once — if the one-application residual is at machine precision the operator reproduces the analytic fixed point and the fault is in how the solve was posed or seeded; if it is large, the operator does not admit that solution at all.

Done when the residue is attributed to posing or to the operator, reporting: a refinement ladder of at least four resolutions with the fitted convergence order of the analytic error and its uncertainty, so a constant offset is distinguished from a converging one; the one-application residual of the exact map at the analytic solution, in absolute flux and by region, against the terminal residual the solve reaches on its own fixed point; the external, boundary and source terms the analytic case is posed with, each quoted against what the closed form specifies; and a named verdict. A verdict of posing is the better outcome and must not be presented as the lesser one — it would mean the operator is sound and one setup is wrong, which is a bounded repair. No repair is authored under this section.

§3 — What is downstream, and what may not be claimed until this closes

This is recorded so the blast radius is stated once rather than rediscovered. Every parity, accuracy and reproduction claim that scores Nova's forward map against an analytic or reference solution inherits this residue, including the forward-parity lane and any accuracy axis of the coefficient-carrier matrix. Claims about self-consistency are untouched — a converged fixed point is still converged, a batched route still matches a scalar one, and a deterministic route is still deterministic. The distinction to hold is between agreeing with yourself, which remains demonstrated, and agreeing with the physics, which this plan is about.

Method requirements carried from the lane that found this. Report every error with its regional split, never as a single global norm. Name the production diff between compared trees and decline attribution when more than one mechanism sits in it. Never derive an acceptance criterion from an achieved value of the quantity it scores. And do not let a third plausible attribution stand without measurement: two attributions on the originating lane were argued from plausibility and both were refuted, which is why the verdict here waits for the ladder.
