§4 — The map admits the solution the solve does not find

§2 closed both branches it posed, and what survives is a third thing it did not. The operator applied once to the closed form returns machine precision, so the map has the analytic solution as a fixed point. Every posed term matches the closed form to rounding, so the case is not mis-fed. Yet the solve does not arrive there: where it was attempted on this ladder it stalled unqualified at relative residual 1.255 and 1.300 against a 1e−10 criterion, and where it does converge — the banked roots — it lands 19 to 38 per cent away under a common gauge. So the residue is not in the operator, not in the inputs, but in which fixed point the solver reaches.

That is a materially better position than either branch of §2, and it should be said plainly rather than buried: a map that admits the right answer and a solver that misses it is a bounded, local problem, whereas an inconsistent operator would have invalidated every forward result in the repository. It also explains two results on adjacent lanes that were otherwise loose. The plateau lane's four documented input substitutions converged on nothing — no wall, wiring, profile or discretisation candidate accounts for the residual — which is exactly what one expects if no input is wrong and the solve is landing on the wrong root. And the frame that once reached 2.9e−16 no longer reproduces, with 32 production paths differing and attribution declined, which is the signature of basin selection rather than of a named regression.

Three mechanisms produce a solver that misses a fixed point its map admits, and they are distinguishable. The discrete problem may have multiple fixed points, with the seed selecting a different one — the dual-basin work on this repository establishes that more than one branch exists. The iteration may be unable to reach it even from nearby, which is a contraction property of the map about that root rather than a property of the root. Or the convergence criterion may be satisfied away from it, which the plateau lane's sup-norm-over-global-peak residual makes plausible. The first is settled by seeding at the analytic solution and asking whether the iteration stays; the second by perturbing it and asking whether it returns; the third by evaluating the criterion at the analytic solution directly.

Banked evidence already bears on the first mechanism, and it is asymmetric between the two branches — recorded 2026-08-25 in answer to a question from the dual-branch lane. Seeding at the diverted EFIT labels is not the same experiment as seeding at the analytic solution, and the plateau lane already ran it. Of the six frozen MAST references, five hold the diverted branch — achieved_branch = diverted, topology_consistent = true — at terminal residuals of 4.585e-3, 6.624e-3, 5.168e-3, 7.425e-3 and 5.049e-3, bounded non-convergent. One does not: 21989/55 ends limited with topology_consistent = false at 5.024e-3, so the branch is not uniformly held even from a seed that starts on it.

The two branches are therefore not symmetric in the evidence, and a design that assumes they are would be building on sand. A limited root has a machine-precision demonstration: §6 of the plateau plan reached 2.409e-16 from the production moment seed once the class refusal was relaxed. A diverted root has none — the best diverted evidence anywhere is bounded non-convergence around 5e-3. So the existence of a discrete diverted root is still open, while the limited one is settled. What the label-seeded result does establish is that the diverted neighbourhood is mostly not repulsive: the iteration stays there and fails to converge, which is the signature of a contraction failure on that branch rather than of the root's absence. The single defector is the sharpest available discriminator and should be treated as such rather than averaged away.

Done when the solver's failure to reach the admitted fixed point is attributed, reporting: the terminal state and residual of a solve seeded at the analytic solution, so whether the iteration stays at a fixed point it starts on is answered directly; the same from seeds perturbed by a declared ladder of magnitudes, with the largest perturbation that still returns quoted as a basin radius or its absence stated; the same seeded and perturbed measurement repeated from the six diverted EFIT labels rather than only from the analytic solution, since those are the seeds that start on the requested branch, reporting per reference whether the branch is held and why 21989/55 alone leaves it; the convergence criterion evaluated at the analytic solution itself, since a criterion that is not satisfied there cannot select it; and a named verdict among multiplicity, contraction and criterion, or a statement of which were excluded and by what figure. A verdict of multiplicity makes this a seeding problem and is the cheapest outcome; a verdict of criterion makes it a scoring problem and is cheaper still.

§3 — What is downstream, and what may not be claimed until this closes

This is recorded so the blast radius is stated once rather than rediscovered. Every parity, accuracy and reproduction claim that scores Nova's forward map against an analytic or reference solution inherits this residue, including the forward-parity lane and any accuracy axis of the coefficient-carrier matrix. Claims about self-consistency are untouched — a converged fixed point is still converged, a batched route still matches a scalar one, and a deterministic route is still deterministic. The distinction to hold is between agreeing with yourself, which remains demonstrated, and agreeing with the physics, which this plan is about.

Method requirements carried from the lane that found this. Report every error with its regional split, never as a single global norm. Name the production diff between compared trees and decline attribution when more than one mechanism sits in it. Never derive an acceptance criterion from an achieved value of the quantity it scores. And do not let a third plausible attribution stand without measurement: two attributions on the originating lane were argued from plausibility and both were refuted, which is why the verdict here waits for the ladder.

The machinery exists - a one-application residual field driver with regional norms landed on the plateau lane at commit fac85ead and is worth reading before writing anything.

SELF-CONSISTENCY IS NOT AT STAKE AND SAYING SO MATTERS. A converged fixed point is still converged, batched still matches scalar at 1e-10, the route is deterministic under its control, and the clip gates are closed. What is at stake is agreement with the physics. Do not let this plan be read as invalidating results it does not touch, and do not let those results be cited as if they answered it.
