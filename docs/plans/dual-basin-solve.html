§1 — The finding and the owner's strategy

The free-boundary map has multiple genuine roots. On the closed-form oracle fixtures the same external field supports two machine-precision fixed points (residuals 2.5e-15 and 2.2e-15, axis separation ~74.8 mm), h-independent — measured by the recovery re-baseline (commit 0bed363d). Corrected by measurement 2026-08-21 (commit 46c55d1d): both banked roots are limited — the closed-form read binds the outboard wall, the alternate root the inboard wall, with exact wall-contact equality in all four production reads, and the analytic formula has no stationary saddle for R>0 (its only mathematical saddle sits at the excluded coordinate axis). The bank proves two fixed points, not two topology classes; the genuinely diverted in-domain X-point fixture is constructed and banked under this plan. Production's cold current-centroid seed converges to the alternate root; source-strength continuation collapses to the vacuum branch at intermediate amplitudes. Accuracy is settled — the recovery gates hold each root to round-off — but which root a cold solve reaches is unmanaged.

Owner strategy 2026-08-21 (the imas-efit precedent): where both basins exist, solve both — a diverted solve and a limited solve side by side — and make the selection afterwards, from history (hysteresis). The GPU route makes the second solve nearly free: the batched fixed point already amortizes to 30 ms/state at batch sixteen, so a two-branch portfolio rides the same batch. Solving each branch with its topology pinned also removes the failure mode where a solve switches topology class mid-iteration and crashes convergence — the branch never has to decide its own topology, only converge within it.

§2 — The two-branch solve

Each branch is a topology-pinned fixed point: the diverted branch seeds and iterates with saddle-anchored normalization and the clip against its separatrix; the limited branch seeds and iterates with wall-contact normalization and its own boundary read. Neither branch may change class mid-solve — a branch whose topology read contradicts its pin reports that fact in its receipts rather than switching. Branch construction reuses the production machinery end to end (one code path per quantity; the pin is a mask/anchor selection, never a parallel solver).

Done-when (restated 2026-08-21 on the classification evidence, commit 46c55d1d): the two-branch solve recovers from cold production seeds the limited banked roots at the recovery-gate floors and the diverted in-domain X-point fixture banked under this plan — with per-branch convergence receipts (residual, iterations, topology-consistency flag) and fixed shapes under jit/vmap so both branches batch together; a branch that cannot converge in its class reports non-convergence explicitly and never silently degrades.

§3 — Hysteresis selection

Selection between converged branches is a declared policy over history, not an in-solve heuristic: a shot evolving diverted stays on the diverted branch while that branch converges and its class is topologically admissible, switching only on a declared criterion (branch disappearance, or a persistent admissibility change), with the switch recorded in the receipts. Cold starts with no history select by a stated rule (e.g., the physically admissible branch of larger confined current, or the declared scenario class).

Done-when: the selection policy is a pure function of branch receipts plus declared history state; a synthetic sequence crossing a limited-to-diverted transition selects correctly with hysteresis (no chattering across the transition), pinned by test; the policy and its criteria are recorded as data in the result provenance.

The ladder measured the map at 1.12 ms and one Newton step at 20.6 ms — the 30-iteration Krylov loop owns the solve. The route to the locked ~1 ms target is iteration economy times batch amortization: adjacent-state warm starts (the catalog's regime) should cut Newton steps from ten toward one-to-two, and the two-branch portfolio batches with everything else. This section measures that economy on real sequences: iterations and wall time per state as a function of seed distance, batch size, and branch, against the banked 198.5/56.3/30.2 ms ladder points.

Done-when: a warm-start ladder reports converged wall time per state versus seed distance and batch size on the H200 for both branches, with the profiled phase decomposition (the slow-benchmark profiling mandate applies); the measured trajectory toward the 1 ms target is stated with the remaining gap attributed by phase; and the catalog-regime projection (adjacent-slice seeding at production batch sizes) is a number beside the target, not a belief.

§ Decisions

How does the solver handle the coexistence of diverted and limited roots?

Owner ruling 2026-08-21, from the strategy proven in imas-efit: solve a diverted AND a limited version wherever both exist and make the selection from history — the GPU route makes the second solve cheap (the batched fixed point already amortizes to 30 ms/state at batch sixteen), and pinning each branch to its topology class blocks the in-solve topology switches that crash convergence. Selection becomes a recorded policy over converged receipts instead of a property of the seed.
