§1 — The measured problem: every road leads to the limiter

Unconstrained or penalty-guided, the forward solve finds limiter-class roots wherever a diverted equilibrium is requested. The margin re-measurement graded proposals by relative_residual + max(−class_margin, 0) and reached terminal residuals down to 2.409e-16 — with 10 of 10 terminals limited where diverted was requested and 7 of 10 wrong-class proposals reducing the residual while closing nothing. The branch-pure arm is no better: the label-seeded lane pins the class into the flux map itself (operator.read(flux, requested_class), nova/equilibrium/forward.py:936-937; route ForwardProfile.solve_branch newton_krylov, benchmarks/label_seed_residual_field.py:441,468,492) and stalls at 4.585e-3 to 7.425e-3 on five of six diverted references, contracting only weakly (§4), while one (21989/55) defects to limited even from a diverted seed.

Three explanations are already excluded by measurement. The operator is exonerated: one application of the exact forward map to a closed-form field holds 2.69e-15 to 3.06e-15 across four resolutions (the operator refinement ladder), so the map admits the analytic fixed point. The inputs are exonerated: all four documented input substitutions were closed or screened without moving the plateau. And the root asymmetry is measured rather than assumed: a limited root has a machine-precision demonstration at 2.4e-16, while on the diverted side five of six references stall near 5e-3 — the §4 contrast has since produced exactly one machine-precision diverted terminal (22086/43, mixed arm, 3.58e-16), so a diverted fixed point exists somewhere in the family and is still unreached on five of six. Any design that assumes both branches converge everywhere is refuted before it starts.

The two iterations fail differently, and the difference carries the diagnosis. The mixed iteration — class participating as a penalty — reproduces exactly the failure a mature implementation of this ambiguity measured before separating branches: verdict-flipping non-convergence cured only by branch purity. Nova's pure diverted branch instead stalls without flipping, which relocates the open question from selection to contraction: why does the pinned diverted map fail to contract about its root where the reference implementation's pure branches contracted geometrically at ρ ≈ 0.33? That question is owned by discrete-operator-analytic-error §4 (seeded and perturbed at the analytic solution and at the six diverted labels); this plan consumes its answer and does not duplicate the experiment.

§2 — Prior art is adopted as ideas, never as code

imas-efit solved this ambiguity in production, and its record was mapped end-to-end (prior-art map, run r-20260825T070435806649; fifteen commits, thirteen symbol groups). Five concepts port; two things are banned outright.

The reference selector itself is also rejected on its merits: it is a hard precedence of booleans, a chi-squared tie band, and a categorical pulse anchor — "referentially pure, but not continuous in either branch flux map" — and its per-pulse temporal hysteresis presumes a slice ordering a batch-parallel catalog does not have. The continuous selector is the one genuinely novel object in this plan.

§3 — What already exists, graded for trace and batch

The capability map (run r-20260825T070446317634) graded every piece. The substrate is in better shape than the symptom suggests: the axis-connected flood fill, the connectivity boundary read, primary-X-point selection, the exact signed class margin (topology_margin = u_wall − u_xpoint, reconciled at 9cc2d0f3), the smooth class observable, and the two-branch pinned portfolio are all jax.jit-traceable and vmap-batchable today (17/18 focused tests; the one failure an isolated-process x64-configuration artifact, green on recheck). Primary-X identification agrees with the exact Boolean comparator on 10/10 banked terminals and 53/53 classified fixtures, with one honest gap: terminal receipts do not serialize the selected X coordinate, so spatial correctness is proven against the comparator, not independently (§7 closes this).

The differentiable transition quantity the design needs already ships. p_diverted — a softmax saddle weight inside traced_smooth_boundary_read (nova/equilibrium/connectivity_boundary.py:877-886) — is jitted, batched, produced on the production smooth read, and consumed by the profile basis; it has simply never been promoted to a branch-selection API. Three host-Python islands block a compiled catalog verdict: cold_seed_portfolio builds seeds with host NumPy and cannot sit under an outer vmap; select_forward_branch is a host history machine (bool()/float()/enums); and the admitted-state bootstrap loops in Python. Closing those three islands is implementation, not research.

§4 — The diverted arm: contraction measured, existence demonstrated

The paired contrast node runs the pinned portfolio and the mixed margin-penalty iteration on identical seeds over the six diverted references, records per-iterate residual sequences, and fits a contraction ratio per arm. The comparison target is the reference implementation's ρ ≈ 0.33 on pure branches. The contrast is landed (commit 991d5d6e) and the finding is sharp: the pure diverted arm is weakly contracting but stalled — fitted ρ 0.9503 to 0.9835, below one on all six references, residuals 4.6e-3 to 7.4e-3, converged 0/6 — an order of magnitude slower contraction than the reference implementation's pure branches. The mixed arm converged 22086/43 to 3.58e-16 achieved-DIVERTED (ρ 0.078, topology consistent, p_diverted 1.0, quadratic: 1.243e-3 → 1.357e-4 → 1.730e-7 → 7.078e-14), the repository's first machine-precision diverted terminal, on one of the two references whose exact-margin operand is absent. That operand absence (+∞ margin on 2/6 references) is the connectivity-localisation defect measured live on the production cohort. Batch-two jit and vmap parity holds at 2.2e-16. So existence is demonstrated, not inferred from weak contraction — why ρ sits at 0.95–0.98 rather than 0.33 on the pure arm stays with the basin probe.

Three refinements from re-reading the artifact, which the receipt summary does not carry. First, the converged arm was unpenalised: 22086/43's margin is absent, so max(−margin, 0) was identically zero at all twelve promotions and the "mixed" arm there was an unsteered residual-ranked Newton. That strengthens existence — nothing pushed the solve toward the diverted class — and means the run says nothing about whether margin grading helps. Absence is not sufficient either: 21978/35 ran at zero penalty too and settled into a period-2 cycle, alternating 1.076e-2 / 1.050e-2 to the last promotion. Second, 21989/55 is characterised: it holds the only negative class margin (−0.00340) and the only unsaturated observable (p_diverted 0.41 against 1.0 on all five others), so it is not a diverted configuration at its own label and its defection in both arms is the comparator reporting a boundary case rather than an anomaly. Third, a measurement-quality correction to this section's own earlier claim: "expands on three references (ρ up to 1.039)" is not supportable, because the mixed fits carry r² of 0.21, 0.00, 0.48, 0.08, 0.06 and 0.72 — three are unresolved noise and ρ 1.0386 sits at r² 0.08. Only 22086/43's mixed ratio is resolved. The pure column carries r² ≥ 0.99 on five of six and is the measurement here. Full record: §4 contrast landed.

The existence half of that dependency has now landed and is consumed here, not re-measured. discrete-operator-analytic-error §5 records the same 22086/43 terminal with its mechanism — converged, topology consistent, and reached under an inactive class penalty — so the published-label decision below is no longer gated on whether a discrete diverted root exists. It does exist. What remains from that plan is the rate question: why the pure map contracts at 0.95–0.98 rather than 0.33, and specifically why the same map converges quadratically on 22086/43 yet cycles with period two on 21978/35 from equivalent unpenalised starts. Those two references differ in no recorded quantity except shot identity — both carry absent margins, saturated p_diverted, and qualifying pure arms — so that pair is the cheapest available discriminator and needs no new runs.

Consequence for the selection-observable decision, which its own gating receipt has also now supplied. The exact class_margin is undefined on two of six production references, while p_diverted is defined on all six and is informative exactly where the margin is negative (0.41 against a margin of −0.00340 on 21989/55, saturated at 1.0 elsewhere). An observable that is absent on a third of the cohort cannot carry selection; one that is defined everywhere and agrees in sign where both exist can. That is evidence for explicit split roles rather than either observable alone, and it is recorded here for the lead to lock rather than assumed by this section.

§5 — The traced selection seam

Promote the class decision to one public, traced, batched seam consuming two qualified branch receipts: p_diverted carries the smooth selection weight (gradients flow through it), class_margin carries the exact signed comparator for reporting and gates, and neither substitutes for the other silently. Selection semantics must be explicit about the measured asymmetry: when exactly one branch qualifies, the selector degrades to the qualified branch — and the receipt counts degrade-path firings against genuine two-qualified selections, because on present evidence the degrade path fires on every experimental frame and an all-degrade cohort tests nothing about selection. Continuity is measured, not asserted: the selection weight must be continuous across the classified transition rail where both branches qualify. The prototype is landed and passes (commit 632a4396): jit-traceable with a 30-equation jaxpr, vmap-batchable, 53/53 exact-class agreement where both arms qualify, continuous on both dense 257-point persistent-saddle rails (max adjacent weight step 3.5255e-4, inside the Lipschitz bound), degrade semantics exact, and p_diverted equal to sigmoid(class_margin/0.01) to 2.2e-16. One discontinuity is retained honestly: the weight jumps at saddle-operand emergence (growing-saddle family), so production promotion consumes the connectivity-localization repair before claiming continuity there. Full record: §5 seam landed.

Implementation follows the capability map's composition list: seed construction becomes an array-only kernel (or precomputed batch tensors) so the portfolio sits under an outer catalog vmap; the selector state (selected class, pending class and count, availability, admissibility) becomes integer and boolean arrays advanced by lax.scan where a time axis exists, with the host SelectionReceipt retained as the egress and provenance boundary; the admitted-state bootstrap becomes a fixed ladder with device selection. The existing host selector's tests remain the semantic oracle the traced port must reproduce.

§6 — Fork policy under catalog economics

A two-branch portfolio doubles solve cost wherever it runs. The reference implementation measured fork-by-default as over-deployed and confined the fork to terminal marginality — but its escape mechanism is banned here, so nova's arming rule must come from its own margin: fork where |class_margin| is small or the smooth weight is uncertain, solve one pinned branch elsewhere, and measure the armed fraction on real catalog frames before locking the policy. The 1,341,435-slice budget (372.6 slices/s aggregate for the one-hour goal) is the denominator every candidate policy is priced against.

§7 — What this plan does not carry

The primary-X spatial-evidence gap (batched coordinate parity, terminal X-candidate serialization, the isolated test's x64 self-configuration) is independently shippable verification hardening on shipped machinery and is carried by the sibling plan primary-xpoint-evidence. Whether a discrete diverted root exists, and why the pure diverted map fails to contract, is carried by discrete-operator-analytic-error §4. This plan consumes both answers and duplicates neither experiment.

§ Decisions

Which observable carries branch selection: the smooth softmax weight p_diverted, the exact signed class_margin, or both with explicit roles (smooth weight for selection and gradients, exact margin for reporting and gates)? The capability map warns against silent substitution; the traced-selection receipt measures whether the smooth weight is continuous across the transition rail and agrees with the exact comparator where both branches qualify. Decide from that receipt, not in advance.

Does the catalog fork both branches on every slice (simple, uniform shapes, twice the solve cost on 1,341,435 slices) or arm the fork from the class margin (near-tangency slices only, single pinned solve elsewhere)? The reference implementation measured always-fork as over-deployed, but its arming trigger is not portable. Answer after the armed fraction is measured on real catalog frames and the two-branch batch cost is measured on the accelerator lane — never from either number alone.

What does the published label carry when the diverted arm never qualifies — degrade to the limited root with the diverted stall recorded, publish a two-state receipt with no single class, or withhold the label? On present evidence this is the common case, so the answer defines the catalog product. Answer only after discrete-operator-analytic-error §4 reports whether a discrete diverted root exists and why the pure map does not contract; deciding earlier would encode today's stall as a permanent semantic.
