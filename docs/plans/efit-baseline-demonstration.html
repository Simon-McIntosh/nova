§ Decisions

How should the free-boundary solve treat the state-dependent domain so Newton stops overshooting the mask switching surface? The Jacobian currently linearises the flood-fill Boolean cell masks as fixed; the evidenced consequence is a 2-cycle on 21978/35 (5 of 1,089 confined cells alternating), and mask switching is directly present on 2 of the 4 stalled tails.

Lead decision superseding all three inherited treatment options: the Newton solve should not use the boundary definition at all. The flood fill supplies the primary X-point and the shadow regions; the O/X pair defines psi_norm; shadows exclude disconnected psi_norm < 1 regions; every other cell participates and true exterior cells get j = 0 from the profile, which handles the scrape-off region.
