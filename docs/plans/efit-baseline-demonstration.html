The deliverable is non-negotiable: stable, accurate, and precise GS forward modelling demonstrated against the MAST and DIII-D EFIT baselines. Everything downstream rides on the topology extraction; where it is wrong or imprecise the whole pipeline is broken. This plan owns the demonstration and the scoping that decides how to reach it; the implementation work it identifies will be locked here as design decisions before any production change is dispatched.

§3 — Design (locked after §2 and lead review)

Two implementation thrusts and one demonstration gate, to be specified from the §2 reports and the decisions below, then dispatched as fenced implementation nodes — either as sections here or as dedicated plans if the write scopes demand it (open decision).

Thrust A — spline-precise extraction. Subgrid stationary-point extraction by Newton on the global C2 tensor spline (gradient and Hessian are already exposed), replacing local quadratic refinement as the position authority; separatrix and LCFS polylines traced from the spline level set at extraction resolution decoupled from solve resolution; the separatrix split at the X-point into the closed boundary and open legs, with the legs retained as separate branches; the comparison metrics computed on the closed boundary only. Prior art binds this thrust: local per-cell reconstruction is refuted twice (curved-clip-global-surface), the global spline is the sanctioned curve source, and fixed-shape traced contracts must be preserved.

Thrust B — limited-basin convergence. Treatment of the state-dependent domain per the mask-switch decision below, instrumented with mask-change-per-outer-iteration as a first-class receipt field; the isolated wall-resolution ladder (hold plasma carrier, source treatment, and seed fixed; vary wall element count and order; report limited-branch boundary flux, contact location, terminal residual per rung); and re-solve of the four disagreeing arms with the chosen treatment. GPU-compatible fixed shapes are non-negotiable: fixed trip counts, no data-dependent compaction, no host callbacks. Any smoothed membership design must re-run the conservation acceptance rescore, because divergence identities live on the commuting production route with failable envelopes that must not be widened to fit.

Demonstration gate. Preregistered geometric comparison against both EFIT baselines — the twelve frozen MAST arms and the DIII-D bank — with thresholds stated before the runs: topology-class agreement, closed-boundary sup/RMS, and saddle-to-X distance. Comparison stays geometric (efm is magnetics-fitted; COCOS 3 to 17 with unit geometric factor; EFIT treated as an independent reconstruction, not truth). Class labels read the achieved classification through the saddle-aware partition, never ForwardTopologyState.diverted.

§ Decisions

How should the free-boundary solve treat the state-dependent domain so Newton stops overshooting the mask switching surface? The Jacobian currently linearises the flood-fill Boolean cell masks as fixed; the evidenced consequence is a 2-cycle on 21978/35 (5 of 1,089 confined cells alternating) and the suspected consequence is the stalled limited arms. Constraints already set: GPU fixed shapes (fixed trip counts, no data-dependent compaction, no host callbacks); a smoothed membership entering the residual domain must re-run the conservation acceptance rescore without widening any envelope.

Do the two implementation thrusts live as sections on this plan or as two dedicated plans linked under this demonstration endpoint? Sections keep one lifecycle and one evidence record; dedicated plans give each thrust its own write authority, sprint item, and gate, with this plan holding the demonstration gate and the dependency links.

Preregistered gate thresholds for the demonstration: topology-class agreement count on the twelve MAST arms, closed-boundary RMS bound on converged arms, and saddle-to-EFIT-X distance bound. To be proposed from the §2 corroboration numbers and locked before any implementation node is credited against them — never derived from achieved values afterwards.
