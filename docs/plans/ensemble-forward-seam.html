§1 — The seam, surveyed, and what actually falls to nova

The authority split is locked and quoted: "Nova produces every deterministic response kernel and fits with none of them"; "everything statistical about a signal stays in Ambix"; differentiable geometry-bearing physics is nova's, anything carrying a sigma, a prior, or a decision is ambix's (ambix flux-function-state-interface, the seam litmus). The cross-repo survey (scripts/assimilation_seam_survey/report.md, 20 capabilities, 12 tested / 8 absent) then settled the feasibility question the owner raised — is a nova-side ensemble-Kalman demonstration useful before the ambix learned assimilator? — by inventory rather than doctrine: ambix already has the estimators. Its NovaEnsembleEstimator is a tested twin/assimilation harness (46 tests, receipts on one, five and eight H200s) and sequential_da a tested rank-6 localized ψ(ρ) filter; the challenge entry specifies a windowed iterated ensemble smoother over ~12–16 parameters per window admitting only converged members. What every one of those runs on today is at most one-dimensional radial current diffusion — the harness's own plan says its equilibrium product is a radial flux history, not a two-dimensional Grad–Shafranov map. The feasibility demonstration is therefore not a third estimator; it is promoting the physics under the existing estimators to nova's real coupled forwards. Nova ships the forward, the kernels, and the truth; ambix runs the filter it already owns.
