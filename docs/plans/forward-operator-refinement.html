§0 — The owner directive and the measured asymmetry

The owner directed (2026-08-23) that plasma-cell-resolution refinement and operator scaling be sequenced in the solver lane and landed, with the DIII-D forward-gate re-evaluation held behind it. The numbers the parity campaign measured and this plan carries: nova resolves the plasma interior with 248 cells against the reference's ~2,141 over the same region — 8.6× coarser where the physics is — and matching it costs 0.44 GB of coupling against a single-device dense ceiling near N=76,600, so the resolution half is nearly free and lands first. The cost asymmetry that decides the two axes against each other: mesh refinement is quadratic in interaction-matrix build compute, about 8.8 CPU-hours per nine-block build at reference-native resolution against roughly 2.4 minutes on device, while raising operator order changes no mesh, leaves the interaction matrices identical and rebuilds nothing.

Two carried cautions gate the reading. The observed mesh orders span 0.97–3.39 in two strata, so any order claim is per-stratum. And the imposed-boundary diagnostic showed refinement improving the bulk while worsening the extreme by a factor 31.8 — a refinement verdict quoted only in the bulk norm would repeat that mistake. Every verdict in this plan is judged against a criterion from scoring-criteria-derivation (declared prerequisite), never against the inherited 1e-8 bound.

§6 — The passive-closure defect

Convicted by the §5 mesh ladder: with passive source currents zeroed against retained, the solved flux sup-norm reproduction moves ~0.61–0.64 points at every resolution from 566 to 2,214 cells, against a 0.15-point ceiling and a documented 0.098-point direct passive drive — a six-fold amplification the mesh cannot explain. The reference assertion (tests/test_equilibrium_forward_reference.py:2182) is correct and stays; the closure path is wrong somewhere between the passive-current contribution and the solved reproduction. First the trace: on one fixed dense carrier, follow the passive-closure route and name the mechanism that amplifies a ~0.1-point drive into a ~0.64-point move — candidate classes worth separating are the passive contribution entering the source normalisation (amplifying through the span), a closure re-solve that moves the seed or basin rather than only the source, and a support/mask difference between the two arms. Repair follows in its own node once the mechanism is named, scoped at file level against the concurrent solver-defect lane in nova/equilibrium/ (three named repairs run there; the shared stability survey of §5 is the pre-read).

The trace reports the move per iteration, not only terminally, because the map's measured non-contractivity (spectral radius 1.1456 pinned) supplies a gain mechanism of its own: a ~6.2× amplification of a correct small drive by a converged expanding map wants a route/screening repair (the solver lane's action-qualification work), while a term wrong at iteration one, before amplification, wants a closure-path repair. The verdict states which top-level class holds: EXPANDING_MAP_AMPLIFICATION (geometric per-iteration growth at a rate consistent with the measured spectral radius, every closure term arithmetically correct) or CLOSURE_PATH_ERROR (a specific wrong term at iteration one, file:line named; the source-normalisation, seed/basin and support/mask candidates sit under this class).

Done when (trace): the two-class verdict is banked with iteration-resolved evidence, the mechanism or amplification rate is quoted with its code path or spectral-rate comparison, the arithmetic decomposition of the 0.64-point move is shown, and the repair node's file-level scope is stated in the same receipt.

Repair routing, pre-agreed with the catalog lane: on EXPANDING_MAP_AMPLIFICATION no repair node is cut here — the closure-move screening becomes a fifth refusal condition on that lane's in-flight action-qualification node (refusing a non-finite action, non-successful status, non-finite achieved linear residual, zero step at material residual), landing as one commit; this plan then records the screening spec it handed over and the verdict receipt. The closure-move screen enters as the FIFTH condition, LAST in precedence: the four existing conditions detect an unusable action, the fifth a usable action producing a suspect result, and a value screen ahead of a validity screen would complain about the magnitude of a state that was never numerically admissible. Screened is not fixed: on that branch the screening only refuses to report an amplified state as converged — it hardens the failure into a visible non-convergence and does NOT restore the passive-closure move to its documented 0.098-point response, which waits on the solver lane's topology-admission and expanding-map work. The reference assertion stays visibly red until restoration, by the test-visibility policy, and any suite-green clause elsewhere in this plan closes on that branch only as “green except the classified closure defect, screened and owned by the solver lane” with this section cited. On CLOSURE_PATH_ERROR the repair is this plan's node, file-scoped against that lane's occupancy (forward_operator.py and fixed_point.py held; source, sol_closure, conductor_current, conservation, domain and wall_mask free).

The classification is challenged on physics grounds and the challenge is testable. The trace's own budget contains no error term — and on a strongly elongated, vertically unstable configuration the passive conductors ARE the vertical stabiliser on the resistive timescale, so zeroing them and measuring a large coupled response may be measuring expected physics: the 6.60× gain corresponds to a dominant resolvent eigenvalue near 0.85, a stiff-but-stable response of a marginally-stabilised vertical mode. On that reading the 0.15 ceiling was calibrated on the DIRECT response (the documented 0.098 matches the measured 0.0972 direct peak) and is asserted on the CONVERGED COUPLED response, which differs by the resolvent gain by construction — no correct implementation could satisfy it. The narrow statement, kept narrow deliberately: vertical instability explains why the Picard iteration has ρ > 1 and stalls while Newton converges on the same expanding map; the equilibrium is stiff near a marginal vertical mode, Newton computes it correctly, and the large passive-closure response would be a genuine sensitivity of that equilibrium — nothing here implies the solver needs stabilising, and no lane acquires a stabilisation mechanism on the strength of it. The discriminator is the low-elongation contrast case (ledger node id vertical-stability-control — a scientific control, not a feedback controller): the same passive-closure measurement on a deliberately near-circular configuration. Gain collapsing toward the direct figure ⇒ PHYSICS — the repair is re-deriving the reference bound on the coupled response, and the amplification screen must NOT ship as a refusal, or it flags healthy elongated equilibria catalog-wide. Gain surviving at low elongation ⇒ the benign explanation is excluded and the anomalous-gain investigation reopens. Until the control reports, the class-A verdict stands as a MECHANISM finding (the gain is real and located) with its defect-versus-physics reading explicitly open. The screen shipped as observation, not refusal: it reports through its own result field (not-applicable / contracting / sustained-growth), distinct from the qualification field whose contract is refusal, its advisory-ness is pinned by a test asserting a sustained-growth trajectory still promotes, and its promotion to a fifth refusal awaits this control's verdict — on the physics branch nothing unwinds, the observation correctly describes elongated equilibria and keys against the derived coupled bound.

Physics-branch follow-on, one derivation pass: if the control says PHYSICS, the re-derived coupled ceiling and the DIII-D gate criterion are the same kind of object — the gate benchmark hard-codes 1e-6 against its own registered 1e-05 and neither carries a derivation from reference accuracy or discretisation error — so one node derives the criterion family for both (stability-aware coupled bound here, the gate bound traced or re-derived beside it), leaving the two lanes one criterion family instead of two unrelated numbers. Deriving both in one pass is the scoring-criteria pattern; inheriting either is what that plan exists to forbid. The coupled bound is necessarily configuration-dependent — the gain tracks how close the vertical mode sits to marginal, so a single scalar ceiling would be right for one elongation and wrong for the rest, repeating the 0.15 mistake; the contrast case supplies the first two points on that gain-versus-elongation curve for free.

The §3 floor receipts cite the route node's structured fields verbatim: differencing_step, step_selection_rule, step_invariance.second_step, step_invariance.floor_delta, step_invariance.bound, step_invariance.verdict — the neither-order-nor-mesh attribution case is a lookup of that verdict field, never an inference.

§3 — The adjudication: does order move what mesh moved

State whether raising operator order moves the convergence floors that mesh refinement moved — on the derived criterion, per stratum, in both the bulk and the extreme norm (the 31.8× caution). A measured constraint bounds what any resolution result can claim: the coupled fixed-point map is not contractive — Picard spectral radius 1.1456 with current-pinning against 1.2577 without, both above one, while Newton converges in 4 iterations to 1.59e-9 on the same expanding map — so no resolution bump rehabilitates damped fixed-point routes, and the adjudication separates FLOOR movement (discretisation) from route CONDITIONING (solver), crediting resolution only with the first. Baseline route, as amended: solver-default-route now names the REPAIRED EXACT AUTODIFF TANGENT as the default (the finite-difference arm was cancelled unbuilt when the zero-gap SVD-JVP repair inverted its premise), and the baseline floor already exists — 7.9305e-5 on the 4,225-node carrier, measured on that route with the mesh receipt. The step-invariance receipt fields (differencing_step, step_selection_rule, step_invariance.*) are dropped as not-applicable-on-exact-route, NOT as unmet: the failure they guarded — a floor that is really the differencing step's truncation/round-off minimum — is structurally impossible on a route with no step parameter; the names stay pinned for any future differenced arm. The transposed control that replaces them on the exact route: a latent non-differentiable branch can poison the tangent the way a bad step poisons a difference, so every floor receipt cited here must show krylov_action_qualification reading ACCEPTED throughout the run whose floor is quoted — a field lookup, already satisfied by both mesh rungs (89/89 ACCEPTED). The deliverable is a decision-grade statement for the solver lane: refine, raise order, or both, each with its measured cost.
