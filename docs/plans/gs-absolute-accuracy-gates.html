GS absolute accuracy gates

The north star's first half (forward-accuracy-northstar): absolute, validated forward-model accuracy, as a machine-checkable certificate rather than scattered receipts. The instruments exist — the shipped split basis reproduces the static Solov'ev references below 2e-8 normalized Hessian RMS, the jump-bearing diverted target carries a closed-form curvature step, and the mask-replay gate proved the topology machinery exactly — but no single preregistered artifact states the solve's absolute error with its convergence order, and no gate separates our error from EFIT's own input inconsistency. Dependency: the backend-margin forensics on solver-convergence-regression must land first, because a certificate that averages over an unexplained backend-sensitive arm is not a certificate.

§1 — The Solov'ev certificate

Preregister then measure absolute error norms for full converged forward solves (not just basis fits) against the exact Solov'ev family — the three static references plus the diverted jump-bearing target: ψ, ∇ψ and Hessian norms over the domain and in the two-pitch boundary band, axis and X-point position errors, and boundary flux error, each at no fewer than three resolutions with the measured convergence order stated beside the theoretical one. Reuse, never rebuild: tests/rotating_equilibrium_references.py, benchmarks/split_fit_jump_field.py's target construction, and the solve machinery the S18 receipts exercised. Done-when: docs/figures/gs-absolute-accuracy/solovev-certificate.json banks every norm with its convergence-order fit and figures plotting error fields at each resolution — plotted solutions make errors visible.

§2 — The EFIT reproduction gate

Reproduce measured EFIT solves — MAST efm and DIII-D efit_psirz at the committed reference slices — with norms that separate the two error sources: our solve against EFIT's ψ, and EFIT's ψ against its own GS consistency (the standing DIII-D core-coherent inconsistency scores 0.083 under our operator at stride 1 and is input-side; the MAST zero-iteration edge residual is 0.0116 at stride 1). The gate passes when our residual against the label sits at or below the label's own GS inconsistency in the applicable band — we cannot beat the label's noise floor and must not be scored as if we could. Marginal diverted arms inherit the forensics verdict from solver-convergence-regression; a backend-sensitive arm is scored on both backends with the spread stated. Done-when: per-machine reproduction receipts with the two-source error split plotted per frame.

§3 — The certificate artifact and its pin

One evidence document assembles §1 and §2 into the per-machine accuracy certificate — figure-heavy per the standing mandate, one page a reader can trust without re-running anything — and a focused regression lane pins the certified numbers so drift fires a test rather than a memory. Done-when: the certificate lives under docs/evidence/, the pinning tests are green in the default lane, and the plan records what an accuracy re-certification costs (wall time and lanes) so it can be repeated per release.
