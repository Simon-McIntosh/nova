Hex single-grid de-risk gates

The feasibility prototype (benchmarks/hex_cell_field_feasibility.py, 4/4 criteria) names three conditions that overturn its 80–140 worker-hour cutover estimate. This plan measures all three. Any one firing halts the cutover plans and returns the programme to the lead with the number.

§1 Cache regeneration and independent mask replay

The committed topology operand caches carry cell centres, domain labels and walls but lack per_cell_flux_values, per_candidate_domain_labels, current_cell_polygons and atomic_node_signed_flux — the four fields two figure campaigns and the prototype each named as missing. Regenerate the caches with those fields through the production authorities, then replay saddle-edge admissibility and the MAST 22086/43 private mask independently from the cached flux, on the plasma-cell adjacency.

Falsifier: any differing private-mask cell against the committed operand. The prototype measured zero of 42 on the mixed arm; the replay must reproduce that from cache alone, including the X-candidate selection — the step the current caches cannot support.

§2 Split-fit advantage on a jump-bearing forward-solve field

Re-specified 2026-09-01 under the locked s2-firing-adjudication decision. The original measured-map arm fired on both committed EFIT maps at matched 16-dof fits (improvement factors 0.626 MAST 22086/43, 0.612 DIII-D frame 179; receipt docs/figures/hex-cell-single-grid/measured-map-advantage.json) and the firing was ruled confounded: an EFIT ψ map is itself a globally smooth solution representation, so its finite-difference curvature carries no physical interface jump for the split basis to represent. Those numbers stay on the page as data.

The re-specified target provably carries the jump. Solov'ev profiles have constant p′ and FF′, so the current density is finite at the separatrix and the truncated source — plasma current supported on plasma cells only, exactly nova's forward-solve construction — creates a closed-form curvature step, ⟦Δ*ψ⟧ = μ₀R·j_φ(LCFS) with j_φ = Rp′ + FF′/(μ₀R). Fit split against global C2 at matched degrees of freedom on nova's converged forward-solve ψ for the diverted Solov'ev reference (the solovev-exact-recovery machinery and tests/rotating_equilibrium_references.py are the reuse anchors), interface on the solve's own separatrix, band of two cell pitches, on the production lattices. The curvature reference is analytic — Solov'ev Hessian inside, vacuum field outside — never finite differences of a smoothed representation and never any function containing the split basis's own exterior term.

Falsifier: the split fit loses its boundary-band second-derivative advantage (factor ≤ 1) on the jump-bearing forward-solve field. A finite advantage factor is expected, not the prototype's circular e-15.

§3 Accelerator batch behaviour

Measure the fixed-slot split carrier at production batch widths on one reserved H200 (betelgeuse, --reservation=gpu_0003_grpA), against the committed throughput ladder (26.797× saturation at width 1024, out-of-memory at 2048, receipt docs/figures/diiid-forward-onboarding/throughput/batched_throughput_budget.json). CPU reference from the prototype: split costs 12–17% over global, 25.0M active point evaluations per second at width 32.

Falsifier: a material batch regression — the split carrier failing to sustain the committed ladder's saturation shape within the stated overhead band.

§ Decisions

What clears the solver cutover?

The feasibility record docs/research/hex-cell-single-grid-feasibility.html states its own falsifiers: the estimate is overturned if an independent replay with cached per-cell flux produces any MAST mask difference, if the split fit loses its boundary-band advantage on a non-manufactured map, or if accelerator measurement shows a material batch regression.

Lead decision 2026-09-01, encoding the feasibility record's own recommendation: build the production-grade basis, but do not cut the solver over until all three checks pass. Any one falsifier firing halts the cutover plans and returns the programme to the lead with the measured number.

s2-firing-adjudication

The s2 falsifier fired on both committed EFIT maps at matched 16-dof fits (improvement factors 0.626 MAST, 0.612 DIII-D; receipt measured-map-advantage.json). An EFIT psi map is itself a globally smooth solution representation, so its finite-difference curvature carries no physical interface jump for the split basis to represent - the measurement was confounded, not the representation refuted. The measured numbers remain on the page as data.

Lead adjudication 2026-09-01 (option B): re-specify the s2 gate against a forward-solve psi that provably carries the jump. Solov'ev is the ideal carrier because its constant p-prime and FF-prime leave a finite current density at the separatrix, so truncating the source at the LCFS - which is what the forward solve does, plasma current supported on plasma cells only - creates an exact closed-form curvature step, jump of Delta-star psi equal to mu0 R j_phi at the LCFS with j_phi = R p-prime + FF-prime/(mu0 R). The reference curvature must be analytic (Solov'ev Hessian inside, vacuum field outside), never finite differences of a smoothed representation and never any function containing the split basis's own exterior term - the circularity that inflated the prototype's e-15 advantage.

s1-scoring-route

The telemetry-bank regeneration showed current HEAD no longer reproduces the committed bank: 10/12 arms changed selected primaries and 22086/43 pure flipped converged to active_set_stagnated (saddle delta 5.2 mm), independently corroborated by the settlement census (zero-mask suffix from trip 3 without qualification). The CPU regeneration also failed to finish the same arm in 3 h. A HEAD-scored mask difference would therefore indict the solver drift, not the flood-on-cells question the gate exists to answer.

Lead decision 2026-09-01 (option C): score the gate from operands produced at the committed bank's recorded source revision - a pinned worktree with any serializer extension applied as never-merged shadow scaffolding, the solve on the H200 with JAX_PLATFORMS=cuda,cpu - so the falsifier compares like against like, AND activate a regression plan (solver-convergence-regression) owning the HEAD drift as first-class work rather than a walked-past followup. The pinned-revision GPU run doubles as the discriminating experiment: if it reproduces the committed convergence, the drift is code, not backend.

§ Followups

Run the three de-risk gates in parallel nodes

Three independent measurements, one node each: the cache-regeneration-and-replay gate (adds per-cell flux, per-candidate domain labels, current_cell_polygons and atomic_node_signed_flux to the topology operand caches, and independently replays saddle-edge admissibility and the MAST 22086/43 private mask, where ANY differing cell fires the falsifier); the measured-map gate (fits the boundary-split representation on committed MAST and DIII-D EFIT psi maps at matched degrees of freedom and reports the boundary-band second-derivative advantage against a single global C2 fit, where losing the advantage fires the falsifier); and the accelerator gate (measures the fixed-slot split carrier at production batch widths on one reserved H200, where a material regression against the committed 26.797x saturation ladder fires the falsifier). Each gate reports its number to this plan whether it passes or fires.
