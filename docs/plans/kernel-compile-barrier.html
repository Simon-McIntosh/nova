Why this plan exists

Lead directive, 2026-10-20: the compile barrier is critical and recurrent, and it gets a plan of its own rather than living as rulings and side notes inside converged-forward-solve. Every program that evaluates a Biot coupling kernel and its derivatives traces the same expression: the harmonic recurrences in nova/biot/elliptic.py and nova/biot/rangefunction.py, and the packed polygon moments in nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py. XLA compiles that expression cold, and the cost does not depend on the mesh. Nothing in today's code bounds it, so each new kernel-backed program pays it again, in the solve, in test fixtures and in a test suite's memory.

Relaxing the identity tolerance or the compile clause is forbidden without the lead. The 15 elliptic helpers the point jet reaches already carry private tangents inside three times their primal, so the remaining cost lies in terms that work did not reach; §1 finds them before anything is rewritten.

§ Decisions

Which tangent forms may bound a kernel term's graph?

Inherited from converged-forward-solve's kernel-loop-representation and tangent-identity-tolerance rulings of 2026-10-06.

A fixed-capacity scan tangent of harmonic_moments compiled in 1.43 s and agreed with jax.jvp of the base to 1.04e-10, where the exact tangent took 54.1 s. The Hessian only steers Newton and the implicit null tangent, so 1e-9 sits far below every solve tolerance. Relaxing either bound is forbidden without the lead.
