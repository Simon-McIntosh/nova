Why this plan exists

Playable-forward-solve reached 0.995 and is a record: the turning-point and position controls, the warm-started reduced Newton and the H200 lane all landed. What remained on it as followups is the hard tail the lead asked for on 2026-09-06 and since: a labeller at about 300 slices a second against 12 to 14 today, a shape inverse whose admitted half step achieves twice the commanded motion, a constrained iteration proven only within one trip cap, a cold first iterate with no shadow, and the decoder corpus the imas-ambix video decoder needs. Each is a section here with effort, depth, risk and attempts. §1 — The whole slice solve inside one compiled program

Lead target: about 300 slices a second, against 12 to 14 warm on one H200 today (48 to 50 ms a trip, one to two trips a slice). A dense solve on about 1,100 plasma-cell amplitudes is well under a millisecond on the device, so the trip is latency-bound: the Newton ladder as a host loop with data-dependent backtracking and the trips of the topology read each pay about 7.6 microseconds of launch latency per step plus the host round trips between them. Put the whole slice solve inside one compiled program with a fixed trip budget and masked convergence instead of the host ladder, so one call is one slice and the launch count per slice drops to a handful.

Done when. One MAST shot (22086) is labelled through the single program with terminal states bit-identical to the host-ladder route on the converged rows and the per-slice wall recorded on the H200; the receipt tables launches per slice before and after.

§2 — The topology read of a batch of slices on device

The vmap probe over the fused trip ran slower per element because the fused close is a write-then-read topology pass whose per-element memory dominates. Re-cast the pass so a batch of slices reads in one fixed-shape program (the saddle-aware flood is already fixed-shape per slice) and measure the slices per second against batch size on one card.

Done when. A batch of 32 slices reads in one program with labels identical to the per-slice read, and the throughput curve against batch size is banked; this is the critical-risk section because it changes the read every consumer depends on.

§3 — The admitted half step on the elongation command achieves twice the commanded motion

From the bounded-step receipts: the elongation plus 5 percent command asks 54.95 mm at the upper and lower extrema; the full inverse step is refused by the axis referee and the half step is admitted, yet the half step achieves 112.4 mm upper and 119.0 mm lower with 30.0 mm uncommanded drift, so the linearised turning-point response under-states the true response by about four on this command while the upper-point command over-achieves by 1.6. Add a Newton outer loop on the achieved shape: re-linearise at the admitted state and iterate until commanded and achieved motions agree to a stated tolerance, still under the admission line search and the 20 kA ceiling.

Done when. The elongation and upper-point commands achieve their motion within 5 percent with the iteration count and uncommanded drift banked, and the figure shows commanded against achieved per iteration.

§4 — The constrained iteration converges over several capped trips

The globalisation acceptance on 27079 row 96 landed inside a single 1000 A trip cap, so the multi-trip path the cap exists for is only weakly exercised. Command the same row to a displacement needing three to four caps and require convergence within 1 mm, a monotone decrease of the augmented constraint merit across trips, no trip above the cap, no overshoot past the target at any accepted step, and the total compensating current within 10 percent of the free-solve value; record the trip count.

Done when. The receipt tables the trips with merit, step and current per trip, or the refusal names which requirement failed and at which trip.

§5 — Give the cold first iterate a shadow by reading twice

The wall-anchor repair excludes the first iterate of a run because it has no previous shadow. On the corpus census limited cold-start frames sit at a 0.066 median span ratio against 0.285 for warm ones over 1,228 frames, and the consumer's decoder is seeded at the starts of sequences, so the frames this leaves worst are the ones its short-horizon case depends on most. Read the first iterate twice, the second read shadowed by the first, and measure the cold-start span ratio against the warm one.

Done when. The cold-start median span ratio on the census moves toward the warm value with the delta banked, and the containment rule of the topology plan holds on the shadowed first read.

§6 — The decoder corpus

Conditional on §1 and §2 landing: the labeller over the decoder's shot list as one sharded job writing every shot's session as it lands with the per-slice conditioning flag and target source and a digest manifest, resumable by shot, with a completion receipt counting shots, slices, converged and qualified fractions and GPU-hours. Whether the conditioned slices may train is the imas-ambix lead's decision, which the flag makes enforceable on data.

Done when. The corpus is written and its completion receipt is banked; the job shape is one job sharded inside its allocation, never one job per shot.
