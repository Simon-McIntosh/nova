§1 — Why the loops, and why now

The flux loops were excluded from every MAST vacuum fit because their absolute gain is not established: a factor of 2π between the store convention (Wb versus Wb/rad) and the kernel convention could not be ruled out, and one unknown row gain corrupts every inverted current. Meanwhile the probe array's identifiability cliff — rank 10 over 13 coil directions, coil and case inseparable — is the binding constraint on the whole misfit programme: P2/P3/P6 wiring is unanswerable, the P4 pair shots are unusable (their drives run collinear with P3 and the solenoid), and the source-localization maps of the localization record rest on probes alone.

The exclusion is fixable by the same logic the vacuum programme runs on: for plasma-free shots the exact flux at every loop position follows from the described Green's kernel and the recorded currents. The 2026-08-07 data survey found the ground already half-prepared. The store publishes 29 fl_* channels in genuine total webers (0.15–1.5 Wb on plasma; silop_dphi = 2π on all 46 reconstruction loops), the 2π convention was already settled by measurement (MEASURED_FLUX_CONVENTION = 13, nova/imas/mast_solve_inputs.py:98-109), and a per-loop scale fit already exists at nova/imas/mast_flux_loop_adjudication.py:588-672 — it fits each channel against a Biot–Savart loop response and currently discards the result. This plan is therefore consumption and validation, not invention. Known bounds: 26 of the 29 data-carrying channels land on a described loop position; the description's 80 loops carry generic names, four duplicated P3 positions, and empty calibration fields; at P4 the described (upper) and measured (lower) loop sets are almost disjoint, and fl_p4u_1..3 never carry data — so the loop-position reconciliation at P4 is in scope, not an afterthought. And the archive's own EFIT gave every loop zero weight in every sampled shot, so a fitted gain has no upstream cross-check: its evidence must stand alone.

§2 — Implementation

  1. Loop inventory and position reconciliation. Join the 29 store channels to the description's 80 loops; resolve the 7 unreachable reconstruction-loop positions (fl_p4l_1..4, fl_p5l_3, fl_p6u_1..2) against the archive EFIT loop table, and decide per loop: adopt the archive position, add the missing described loop, or exclude with cause. P4 first — the misfit localises there and the sets are almost disjoint there.
  2. Forward flux columns. Extend the vacuum response model to flux targets: per-family flux per ampere-turn at each loop position from the polygon Green's kernel (the flux maps already exist gridded; loops need exact point evaluations).
  3. Per-loop gain fits. Re-run and this time consume the adjudication fit (mast_flux_loop_adjudication.py:588-672): through-origin regression of each loop's measured signal on the described flux over the sustained cohort, with the pre-excitation offset removed and loop channels swept for acquisition block-scale steps (the probe channels stepped by 1/2, 1/√2, √2, 2 across shot blocks) before any static gain is promoted.
  4. Convention confirmation and promotion. Confirm the measured 2π convention holds per loop (a residual global cluster off unity reads a store-wide factor); promote stable per-loop gains with the probe-scale evidence discipline; loops whose gain will not stabilise across shots or blocks stay excluded, with cause recorded. Note the absence of any upstream cross-check — the archive EFIT never weighted a loop.
  5. Inclusion and re-measurement. Admit calibrated loops to the free current inversion and recompute the identifiability spectrum — report the new rank and whether P2/P3/P6 wiring becomes answerable. Recompute the P4/P5 surviving-misfit patterns with loop constraints and hand them to mast-misfit-toroidal-harmonics.
  6. Gate decision packet. One document for the gate owner covering both calibration-consumption questions: scoring obr17 through its fitted 0.5011 gain, and scoring the calibrated loops at all. Both are physically correct and both move the artifact-vs-incumbent comparison; the decision is the owner's, the evidence is ours.

Done-when: loop gains fitted and banked with per-loop verdicts; convention factor named with evidence; inversion re-run with loops and the identifiability delta reported; localization constraint sets regenerated; gate decision packet delivered. All numbers reproducible from banked scripts under ~/.cache/nova-mast/.
