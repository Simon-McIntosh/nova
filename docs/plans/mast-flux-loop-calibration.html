§1 — Why the loops, and why now

The flux loops were excluded from every MAST vacuum fit because their absolute gain is not established: a factor of 2π between the store convention (Wb versus Wb/rad) and the kernel convention could not be ruled out, and one unknown row gain corrupts every inverted current. Meanwhile the probe array's identifiability cliff — rank 10 over 13 coil directions, coil and case inseparable — is the binding constraint on the whole misfit programme: P2/P3/P6 wiring is unanswerable, the P4 pair shots are unusable (their drives run collinear with P3 and the solenoid), and the source-localization maps of the localization record rest on probes alone.

The exclusion is fixable by the same logic the vacuum programme runs on: for plasma-free shots the exact flux at every loop position follows from the described Green's kernel and the recorded currents. The 2026-08-07 data survey found the ground already half-prepared. The store publishes 29 fl_* channels in genuine total webers (0.15–1.5 Wb on plasma; silop_dphi = 2π on all 46 reconstruction loops), the 2π convention was already settled by measurement (MEASURED_FLUX_CONVENTION = 13, nova/imas/mast_solve_inputs.py:98-109), and a per-loop scale fit already exists at nova/imas/mast_flux_loop_adjudication.py:588-672 — it fits each channel against a Biot–Savart loop response and currently discards the result. This plan is therefore consumption and validation, not invention. Known bounds: 26 of the 29 data-carrying channels land on a described loop position; the description's 80 loops carry generic names, four duplicated P3 positions, and empty calibration fields; at P4 the described (upper) and measured (lower) loop sets are almost disjoint, and fl_p4u_1..3 never carry data — so the loop-position reconciliation at P4 is in scope, not an afterthought. And the archive's own EFIT gave every loop zero weight in every sampled shot, so no upstream fit exists to check a gain against — but the archive still computes an independent forward prediction at every loop (efm/silop_c, 46 loops per time slice from a 938-filament, 101-circuit model), the same route that corroborated obr17's gain at the probes to 0.25%. Every promoted loop gain should carry that cross-check.

§ Decisions

Where is the flux-loop convention factor corrected — divided out on the nova read path, or fixed in the machine-description artifact so every consumer inherits it?

Closed by measurement rather than by choosing a site: the store and the kernel already share one convention - both are the total flux through the loop contour in weber (fitted ratio 0.96 on all 28 data-carrying channels, not 6.28 or 0.159) - so there is no factor for either site to apply.
