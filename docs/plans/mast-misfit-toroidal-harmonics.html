§1 — From projections to honest maps

The localization record produced the first misfit maps, but they are source-model projections: a single filament explains 43–60% of the surviving power and its best location disagrees between drive groups. The honest map is source-free: in the vacuum region the misfit field satisfies the homogeneous Grad–Shafranov equation, so the misfit flux admits an exact expansion in toroidal harmonics (half-integer Legendre functions about the machine's focal circle). Fitting interior and exterior expansions to the measured misfit — probe projections plus calibrated loop fluxes — yields contour maps of the misfit flux valid wherever no unmodelled current flows, and the places where the two expansions cannot be reconciled are precisely where the unmodelled current is.

Three products, per drive group (P4 lower first — its pattern reproduces at split-half cosine +0.92): the misfit flux contour map with confidence bounds, the enclosed-current localization (expansion-mismatch regions plus multi-filament and extended-path fits scored against the harmonic representation), and the non-axisymmetric residue — the fraction of the misfit no axisymmetric expansion reaches, which bounds what a 3D busbar run must explain and feeds mast-nonaxisymmetric-physics.

§2 — Implementation

  1. Basis. Toroidal-harmonic flux basis (P and Q half-integer Legendre) with the conditioning discipline the polynomial work established — orthogonalised on the fit window, degree chosen by held-out probes, never raw monomial-style contraction. Verify against the polygon Green's kernel on synthetic filaments (place a known filament, fit the expansion, recover flux to stated tolerance and location by mismatch).
  2. Constraint sets. Per drive group: the banked signed couplings (~/.cache/nova-mast/misfit-localization/couplings.npz) as B-projections at 77 probes, plus calibrated loop fluxes from mast-flux-loop-calibration, with per-channel noise weights from the sensor-floor record replacing the uniform pooling of the first pass.
  3. Maps. Interior expansion (regular at the axis) fitted to the centre-column band, exterior expansion to the outboard band; contour the reconciled flux and its disagreement. Bootstrap over shots for confidence bands.
  4. Source fits. Multi-filament (2–4 rings, symmetric pairs allowed but not imposed) and extended-path (line-current segments closing through the machine) models scored against the harmonic maps; report explained fraction, implied currents in supply-current units, and the residue after the best source.
  5. Toroidal sensitivity accounting. The n=0 fits above must carry an explicit statement of what each sensor class can see. Healthy MAST pickup channels are 180°-summed pairs, so all odd-n content cancels by construction (the error-field correction coils, odd-n by design, are doubly excluded — parity plus the measured R² < 0.0005) while even n (2, 4, …) from any toroidally-localised conductor leaks into the "axisymmetric" reading; toroidally-closed flux loops see exactly n=0 and nothing else; the 36 saddle loops are the only n-resolving set. Deliver the leakage bound: for plausible localised sources (a feeder run, the P4 coil tail), the even-n fraction that a summed pair reports against the true n=0 — and screen the pair-failure channels (obr17 class), which are single coils and regain full odd-n sensitivity, against the correction-coil and feeder odd-n content specifically. Cross-machine flux-loop versus probe disagreement per drive group is the practical asymmetry detector the user hypothesis predicts.
  6. Verdict. Either a localised source region with implied current and candidate identity (handed to mast-p4p5-circuit-provenance for engineering confirmation), or a measured statement that the survivor is non-axisymmetric or distributed beyond the array's resolving power — with the resolution limit quantified by the synthetic recovery tests. The reconciled misfit flux maps are also the natural plasma-subtracted targets for coil-geometry-inversion's flux-map problem class: publish them on a grid with uncertainty so the geometry solve can consume them directly.

Done-when: synthetic recovery validated; per-group misfit flux maps with confidence bands published as figures; source fits scored and ranked; non-axisymmetric residue quantified; a research record collapses the findings and hands named candidates onward.
