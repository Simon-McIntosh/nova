§1 — Outcome

Nova provides the production MAST model for non-axisymmetric drive fields, passive structures and circuits. It represents correction/RMP/ELM coils and leads, era-keyed sensor geometry, circuit semantics, passive screening and vacuum taring. Plasma response remains outside this first scope; exposed plasma-on channels carry flags and uncertainty inflation.

§2 — Research-first build

  1. Produce a geometry and circuit provenance research document covering engineering drawings, literature, IMAS availability, channel eras and uncertainty.
  2. Land versioned MAST 3D geometry and drive semantics using Nova's native 3D kernels and machine-description identities.
  3. Model open/shorted/era-dependent coil boundary conditions and passive screening with identifiable vacuum-shot gates.
  4. Expose vacuum taring through Nova's standard-name IO and machine API, then rewire Ambix to consume it.

§3 — Evidence gates

Evidence must include source provenance, per-era channel ledgers, vacuum/off-on contrasts, coil and passive Green-function cross-checks, held-out ramp residuals, uncertainty calibration, CPU/JAX parity and commit/environment identities. Negative results and unresolved plasma-response contamination are recorded explicitly.

§4 — Passive circuit identifiability from driven transients

The free decays are spent. nova/imas/mast_fitted_parameters.py records that twelve free decays support one effective passive time constant (0.023–0.072 s, 86.4 % of the post-pulse probe signal) with decisive attribution on 2 of 12 shots, not a resistance per passive family. This section asks what coil-driven transients add. Extend nova/imas/mast_passive_decay_modes.py, the mode fit that carries exact geometric inductance from nova/imas/mast_passive_inductance.py, to fit grouped passive resistances against the driven ramps and switch-offs of the vacuum cohort (nova/imas/mast_vacuum_cohort.py). Seed and bound each group with the nominal resistivity interval in nova/imas/mast_seed_parameters.py. Group by component family, never one free value per element.

The drive must be grounded before it enters the fit. The free-decay method fitted each shot's initial condition and so never needed the drive; a driven fit does. The published ampere-turn semantics are still unconfirmed (VERTICAL_PAIR_EXPLAINED in mast_fitted_parameters.py is a negative result), so carry each coil's drive scale as a nuisance parameter with an uncertainty interval in the Jacobian. A passive group counts as identifiable only if it stays identifiable with every drive scale free within its interval; otherwise a held-out improvement could come from a mis-scaled drive rather than a resistance.

Done when: the singular spectrum of the grouped-parameter Jacobian on the driven cohort is reported, naming how many groups are identifiable above the sensor floor already recorded in mast_fitted_parameters.py. Each promoted group improves held-out transient residuals on shots excluded from the fit, stays inside its nominal resistivity interval, and keeps the R/L system passive: positive dissipation, and every eigenvalue of L-1R positive. A group that is not identifiable stays at its seed with its interval and is recorded as a negative result. A negative control that permutes the drive columns across shots must remove the held-out improvement.

§5 — The 36 saddle loops as the n-resolving constraint set

No constraint bank consumes the 36 saddle loops today. Their geometry is written into the magnetics IDS by nova/imas/mast_geometry.py, and nothing in nova/calibrate/ reads it. They are MAST's only n-resolving sensor set. The harmonic source fits leave a whitened residue of 36–43 % on the P4/P5 upper and pair groups, which cannot be split between a non-axisymmetric source, pooled-shot systematics and spatial under-resolution without them. Read their geometry from the magnetics IDS through nova/imas/io_magnetics.py, never through nova.catalog.mast_geometry, because machine-description-retirement deletes that registry. Extend the owners rather than adding a parallel path. nova/imas/mast_misfit_harmonics.py already assembles mixed probe and flux-loop constraint sets (ConstraintSet, assemble, bank_shots) and owns the P4/P5 harmonic fit this section re-measures, so the saddle loops join that constraint set. A traversal sign is promoted only through the existing sensor-adjudication seam: sensor_adjudication_records in mast_fitted_parameters.py, with TurnDisposition and aggregate_turns in mast_vacuum_response.py. mast_fitted_parameters.py already records magnetics/flux_loop(saddle)/traversal_sign as unresolved and names the blocker: the store's published saddle signals have the poloidal-field pickup removed, so the sign is measurable only from an uncorrected saddle voltage. The first deliverable is therefore whether an uncorrected saddle voltage is reachable for the vacuum cohort. If it is not, the sign stays unresolved, and the harmonic re-measure runs on the corrected signals with the sign carried as a two-valued uncertainty.

Four nodes, each closing on its own verdict, in order: (a) whether an uncorrected saddle voltage is reachable for the vacuum cohort; (b) admission of the loops into the constraint set, judged on held-out residual against a control fit with the saddle loops removed and a control with the per-loop geometry assignment permuted, either of which must remove the improvement; (c) each loop's traversal sign, promoted or listed undecided; (d) the n = 1–6 re-measure of the P4/P5 residue with the localised-tail width.

Done when: saddle-loop predictions from the coil set reproduce vacuum shots held out of the fit, with the residual stated against the sensor floor. Each loop's traversal sign is either promoted with its held-out evidence or listed as undecided. The n = 1–6 harmonic content of the P4/P5 residue is re-measured with the saddle loops in the bank. The localised-tail width owed to mast-misfit-toroidal-harmonics is reported with its uncertainty; with a 20-degree footprint the even-n leakage bound for healthy summed pairs would be n2 0.9798, n4 0.9207, n6 0.8270. The pair-failure channels obv03, obr05, obr18 and obv10 stay out of this section until the per-era gains put before the owner are locked.

§6 — Toroidal-field fidelity: the average, then the ripple

Validate the toroidal-field model in two layers that are never fitted together. First the axisymmetric average against the measured R·Bφ product, then the ripple and n ≥ 1 error-field structure against the n-resolving set from §5. Do not absorb plasma response or a passive mismatch into a TF geometry correction. Start from the intrinsic error-field screen in nova/imas/mast_error_field_screen.py.

Done when: the axisymmetric TF average reproduces R·Bφ on vacuum shots within a tolerance stated before scoring. The predicted ripple and error-field amplitudes are compared with the saddle-loop and probe data on held-out vacuum shots, and each is promoted, bounded or recorded as unresolved. An ablation shows the TF block does not absorb residual that the passive (§4) or diagnostic (§5) blocks should explain.

§7 — Versioned parameters and parity with the imas-ambix 3-D path

Publish the identified passive, diagnostic and TF blocks through the existing evidence seam, not a new API. fitted_evidence() and refined_evidence() in nova/imas/mast_fitted_parameters.py already emit EvidenceRecords into the EvidenceLedger of nova/imas/machine_evidence.py, which requires an Uncertainty interval on every fitted field. Each promoted parameter enters there and retires the seed record it replaces through SUPERSEDED_SEED_PATHS; no public definition is added. Compare the vacuum-field and subtraction results against the retained imas-ambix 3-D path before any ambix donor is retired. The machine description itself is produced by the imas-ambix map-driven producer once machine-description-retirement lands, so this section publishes parameters and their evidence, not a second description registry.

Done when: Nova matches or improves the retained ambix 3-D vacuum-field and subtraction evidence at recorded tolerances; ablations show the passive, diagnostic and TF blocks are identifiable rather than compensating for each other; and the ambix donor cutover is agreed with the imas-ambix coordinator before any ambix file is removed.
