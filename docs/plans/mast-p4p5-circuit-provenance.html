§1 — What provenance must decide

The magnetics have said everything they can: a P4/P5-local, up-down symmetric, level-following field survives every current freedom the described geometry allows, and the localization maps put its preferred sources beside the P4 coils at feeder-scale currents. The 2026-08-07 data survey turned up four concrete provenance facts that now need engineering resolution rather than more regression:

  1. The description's asymmetry — premise corrected and adjudicated (2026-08-07). This item's original figures (P4 lower 17.3 mm further out, z = −1.1268 vs +1.1095) were an arithmetic artifact: the mean of the outline WKB's closed exterior ring, whose double-counted start vertex biases the two members in opposite directions. The area centroids of the same registry polygons give the honest reading: P4 upper 4.6 mm further from the midplane, P5 upper 8.7 mm. Adjudication (provenance note): placement asymmetry is consistent with as-built — Kirk's Table 1 Hall-probe fit (which senses the copper, unlike the case-only metrology) moves both members of each pair +z, upper further out in both pairs, at 9.6/13.6 mm — the description if anything understates it; no correction warranted. The +5.1% P4-upper footprint reduces to one described parameter (27 mm vs 26 mm main-row column pitch) — a described-geometry artifact, immaterial to the misfit (§2.1 measured footprint equalisation at +0.1% of the survivor).
  2. The P4-lower flux-loop positions are a copy — mechanism named (2026-08-07). Level-2 magnetics/flux_loop entries 20–23 (FL_P4L_1..4) are bit-identical to entries 12–15 (FL_P3L_1..4) on every shot sampled — the described P4-lower positions are a copied block, 0.37–0.44 m from where efm's silop rows 36–39 put the real loops (near-mirrors of the described P4-upper set). The archive's fl_p4l_1/fl_p4l_4 publish on 383/383 readable shots and are correctly refused by the 15 mm join. Every other loop family agrees to ≤ 6.0 mm. The correction belongs in the level-2 read of the registry recovery; details and the per-channel join accounting feed mast-flux-loop-calibration (provenance note).
  3. No supply, feeder, busbar or lead exists in any description on this host. pf_active.supply is empty (blocking the store's efps_current); EFIT's own 101-circuit model has nothing near P4/P5 beyond six vessel elements at r = 2.01 m flagged obsolete; a prior search for power-supply documents returned nothing. External sources (UKAEA publications, MAST design papers, FAIR-MAST metadata) are the remaining route.
  4. The case-current identity closes a solve-input gap. pXY_current = pXY_coil_current + pXY_case_current holds exactly (worst relative deviation 6.5×10⁻⁶ across 336 family-shot pairs), while nova/imas/mast_solve_inputs.py:815-825 still blocks the six pack-total channels as unexplained. Unblocking them with the measured identity adds six validated channels to the drive map.

  3. Supply and feeder topology — the 2026-08-07 web survey's answers and remainders. Settled: every upper/lower pair is series on one supply except P6 (anti-series, the vertical-control actuator — never differential P4/P5); five coil-voltage channels exist (one each for P1/P2/P4/P5 plus the P6 fast amplifier); the published P4 member current difference is 1.5% (transducers say 0.06%). Named but unrouted: the "P3/4/5 link board" (the only feeder-hardware fact in the public record), the toroidally-localised P4 "coil tail", and joggles — coil feeds are an explicitly named error-field source. Edlington, Martin & Pinfold, Rev. Sci. Instrum. 72, 421 (2001) is now RETRIEVED (local working tree at research/edlington-2001-mast-magnetic-diagnostics.pdf; kept untracked by the existing ignore rule — the repository is public and the paper is paywalled) and adds four hardware facts: pickup signals from coils 180° apart are summed before integration and digitisation (the hardware basis of the pair-failure gain class); the PF coil cans are electrically connected to the vessel both through their mechanical supports and through the vacuum-bellows connections of the feeder bars at vessel ports — a named galvanic network joining cases, vessel and feeders; MAST's own commissioning calibrated from designed vacuum shots (P2-plate compensation coefficients; the plasma-Rogowski matrix solve over eight vacuum shots, data taken at the end of long flat-tops so induced currents decay); and the P2 coil pack carries an eight-turn compensating winding that early operation left unused. Remaining action: test any establishable conductor geometry against the localization maps and obr10's differential signature (+290 nT/At on P5 pair member-difference). Sensor-physics bound on feeder testability: the 180° pair summing cancels a localised feeder's odd-n field at every healthy pickup channel, so paired probes see only its n=0 and even-n content — the discriminating sensors for a feeder hunt are the flux loops (exactly n=0), the 36 saddle loops (n-resolving), and the pair-failure single-member channels (full n sensitivity). Full 3D feeder-route inversion (toroidal location, pair separation, height) is deliberately OUT of this plan's scope: quantify the n=0/even-n leakage bound here (via the harmonics plan's sensitivity accounting) and hand the 3D route question to mast-nonaxisymmetric-physics (S3), where the saddle-loop data and the 3D coupling substrate live. Note for any MAST-U cross-check: P4 was physically repositioned during MAST-U assembly (−2.2 mm x, +3.2 mm y, sub-mrad tilts), so MAST-era positions are the pre-adjustment ones.

Identity re-verified at scale: 4,578 coil-set/shot pairs over 763 shots, worst residual 1.09e-07 of the summed terms (float32 eps); P2 set-level identity also holds (1,526 pairs, worst 8.8e-06). mast_solve_inputs.py now states the mechanism (the pack total is the coil plus its case), names both terms per row, derives qualifying sets from the drive map, and adds pack_total_channels/pack_total_residuals with six tests (two store-gated). Deliberate deviation from the original done-when: the six pack-total channels stay blocked rather than served — the total spans two containers (pf_active coil + pf_passive case) and the induced case term makes it no fixed multiple of either (ratio ±10–74%; the best fixed factor leaves 1.0–12.1% of peak), so a drive-map weight would fabricate a column. No excitation channel is now refused as unexplained; serving the six needs a two-channel source-map row (owner call). Suite 4,176/0 at base. Commit 81928526, merged da95fdc7.
