§1 — What provenance must decide

The magnetics have said everything they can: a P4/P5-local, up-down symmetric, level-following field survives every current freedom the described geometry allows, and the localization maps put its preferred sources beside the P4 coils at feeder-scale currents. The 2026-08-07 data survey turned up four concrete provenance facts that now need engineering resolution rather than more regression:

  1. The description is not up-down symmetric where the machine should be. P4 lower's centroid sits at z = −1.1268 m against P4 upper's +1.1095 m — 17.3 mm apart in |z| — and P4 upper's footprint is 3% larger (0.159 × 0.158 m vs 0.154 × 0.154 m); P5 differs by 12.9 mm. A symmetric residual proportional to P4/P5 drives is exactly what an erroneous asymmetry in the description of a symmetric machine would produce, and it would also explain the 26.5% P4 pair split in the free inversion. This is the cheapest candidate to eliminate and must go first.
  2. The described and measured P4 flux loops are almost disjoint. The description carries four P4-upper loop positions; the archive publishes data for P4-lower channels (fl_p4l_1, fl_p4l_4) whose positions the description has nothing near, and fl_p4u_1..3 never appear in 400 sampled shots. The one coil the misfit localises to is the one coil whose diagnostic description disagrees with the archive.
  3. No supply, feeder, busbar or lead exists in any description on this host. pf_active.supply is empty (blocking the store's efps_current); EFIT's own 101-circuit model has nothing near P4/P5 beyond six vessel elements at r = 2.01 m flagged obsolete; a prior search for power-supply documents returned nothing. External sources (UKAEA publications, MAST design papers, FAIR-MAST metadata) are the remaining route.
  4. The case-current identity closes a solve-input gap. pXY_current = pXY_coil_current + pXY_case_current holds exactly (worst relative deviation 6.5×10⁻⁶ across 336 family-shot pairs), while nova/imas/mast_solve_inputs.py:815-825 still blocks the six pack-total channels as unexplained. Unblocking them with the measured identity adds six validated channels to the drive map.

§2 — Implementation

  1. Symmetry elimination test (first, decisive, cheap). Forward-model the sustained P4/P5 cohort under controlled geometry variants — mirrored members (upper := reflected lower), symmetrised centroids, equalised footprints, and per-member (dz, dr, scale) scans — and score each variant against the banked surviving-misfit patterns (~/.cache/nova-mast/misfit-localization/couplings.npz). Either a geometry correction removes the survivor (fault found: description geometry) or the explained fraction caps well below one (the survivor needs current the coils do not carry). The difference field of a displaced member is not a filament, so this closes the gap the single-filament scan left open.
  2. P4 winding provenance. Trace where the described P4/P5 geometry came from (registry provenance, catalog-geometry records) and reconcile against the as-operated evidence: the archive's loop positions, EFIT's coil table (938-element fcoil set), and any published as-built drawings. Adjudicate the 17.3 mm / 3% asymmetry: real machine feature, described-geometry error, or digitisation artefact.
  3. Supply and feeder topology from external sources. Collect published MAST engineering descriptions of the P4/P5 supplies, feeder routing and earthing (design papers, SOFT/SOFE proceedings, UKAEA reports, FAIR-MAST channel metadata); deliver whatever conductor geometry can be established as candidate machine-description entries, and test each candidate's field pattern against the localization maps and obr10's differential signature (+290 nT/At on P5 pair member-difference).
  4. Case-current identity fix. Replace the block at mast_solve_inputs.py:815-825 with the measured identity, documented as mechanism (the pack total is the coil plus its case), with the 336-pair evidence banked beside the change and validation re-run.

Done-when: the symmetry test has a quantitative verdict (explained fraction per variant with confidence bounds); the P4 geometry provenance is adjudicated with sources named; supply topology findings are recorded with citations and candidate conductors tested in the forward model; the pack-total channels are unblocked with validation green; a research record collapses the outcome.
