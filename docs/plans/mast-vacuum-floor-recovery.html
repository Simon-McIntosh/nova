§1 — Objective: the floor is calibration error, and it is ours to remove

Both machine descriptions misfit the measured magnetics at the same level: on the frozen six-shot gate the efm path scores a whitened sensor residual of 0.740 and the artifact path 0.764 — and the metric's own definition places that floor at static coil/sensor calibration error, because the frozen solve never fits the magnetics channels. The S2 cutover passed by scoring the source change against a measured cross-source budget; this plan makes that budget moot. The objective is to complete and calibrate the machine description until the artifact path beats the incumbent on absolute misfit and approaches the quantified sensor-noise envelope on vacuum-dominated data. Every improvement lands as evidence-typed description (measured / published / generated / fitted / unresolved), never as a benchmark-side correction — the incumbent's SOLENOID_RESPONSE_SCALE is the cautionary example of a machine parameter hidden in a scoring path.

§2 — What is already in hand

The S2 execution left a complete measurement substrate; this plan consumes it rather than rebuilding it:

  • The gate: imas_ambix/spine_bench carries the frozen six-shot manifest, 15 registered tolerances including the raw-magnetics residual metric (schema 1.4), genuine banked before/after stamps, both budget classes, and honest stamp labelling. A 2-arm stamp costs ~20 minutes on sun_debug.
  • The attribution: per-channel and per-column residual decomposition, re-solved swap experiments and the instrumented drivers (~/.cache/ambix-after-path/attribution/; narrative in the cutover parity evidence). The excess concentrates on obv06/obv14 (61%), outboard at R = 1.845 m beside P4/P5, and is 88% vacuum-term.
  • The fitting machinery: nova/imas/mast_vacuum_cohort.py (17,111-shot survey, per-shot refusal reasons, train/held-out discipline), mast_vacuum_response.py (signed response fits with near-probe exclusion), mast_fitted_parameters.py, and the evidence-typed artifact with its channel drive map (semantic identity 68f64209…f351).
  • The literature leads: the MAST magnetic-diagnostics paper (Edlington, Martin and Pinfold, RSI 72, 421) documents in-situ calibration from individual PF-coil pulses and an eight-vacuum-shot matrix solve that removed pickup and induced-current contributions — direct evidence that the archive contains experiments designed to identify exactly what this plan fits. Battaglia et al. (PR1934) describe the same iterative description-refinement workflow on NSTX/MAST.
  • Known measured facts to honour: only sustained (~0.62 s) single-coil experiments measure turn counts (in ~6 ms pulsed shots the coil case carries up to half the feed current at probe-read time); probes closer than two winding-pack widths to an excited coil bias turn fits and must be excluded on that scale.

§3 — The calibration ladder

Five stages, strictly evidence-first, each with its own held-out challenge and each followed by a frozen-gate re-stamp (§4). Stages B–E are fits into the description; nothing in this plan touches a benchmark tolerance.

Stage A — dedicated calibration cohort and the noise envelope

The S2 vacuum cohort was generic no-plasma selection (400 shots, 22 excitation families). This stage mines the archive metadata for the designed calibration experiments: the individual-PF-coil sustained pulses per coil, the eight-vacuum-shot matrix family, TF-only and commissioning shots, and any toroidally-varying error-field-coil shots (the only class that can separate the 150°/330° bank candidates). Deliverables: a provenance-pinned calibration cohort keyed by experiment class; per-channel sensor noise measured from quiescent windows and repeat-pulse scatter (the noise envelope the ladder targets); and the train/held-out split declared before any stage-B fit. The cohort record states, per shot, which parameters it can identify — the identifiability map that orders stages B–E.

Stage B — close the turns and circuits

Three items, in identifiability order:

  1. Solenoid weight reconciliation. Two measurement routes disagree: the six-shot cohort fit gives 344.66 ± 11.7 A·turn/A while the incumbent's plasma-free vacuum stratum (85 shots, ~111k slices, no solve in the loop) gives [348.5, 361.1] at 95%. Reproduce the stratum measurement nova-side from level-1 data, run the ablation that explains why the cohort fit lands low (shot selection, near-probe exclusion radius, case-current contamination), and promote one weight with a defensible interval covering both routes — or record the visible disagreement with its cause.
  2. P4/P5 integer turn counts. Currently bounded at ~22 ± 5 with the archive claiming 23. Fit on the stage-A sustained single-coil shots with the pack-width exclusion; require the interval to shrink below 0.5 turn so the count is counted, not assumed. If the archive's own exact-integer ampere-turn ratio channels (23, constant across 17,111 shots) are adopted instead, they enter as published evidence with the fit as corroboration — the record must state which route promoted the value.
  3. P6 disposition. The store publishes P6 excitation as ampere-turns and no shot isolates P6 above 0.6% of predicted signal power. Author the explicitly-effective channel semantics as published evidence, search stage-A metadata for any dedicated P6 commissioning shot, and either pin the physical count or record the structural non-identifiability with the evidence that closes the search.
  4. Circuit gaps. The P2 inner/outer pack interconnection and the supply inventory stay unset unless stage-A documents surface; record the search.

Stage D — winding lattices as authored geometry

The artifact states uniform current density over each winding-pack polygon; efm resolves P4/P5 into a 6×4 filament lattice with a vacant top-outboard cross-over position, and the two differ by 2–3 mm in current centroid — exactly what the nearest probes see. Stage E — passive R/L calibrated on decay

The generic cohort separated exactly one passive decay pattern (23–72 ms), so per-family resistance stayed nominal. This stage combines three things the S2 pass did not: (i) exact inductance — L per enclosure group computed from nova.biot on the corrected case geometry (the transposed-extents fix lands upstream in mast-catalog-geometry-recovery), so R = L/τ converts every fitted time constant directly; (ii) the dedicated decay experiments from stage A — per-coil pulses excite different case groups preferentially, and the eight-shot matrix was designed to separate induced contributions; (iii) mode-resolved fitting — fit the two or three dominant spatial decay modes with the group L matrix in the loop rather than one lumped pattern. Promotion per the standing contract: positive, physically plausible, cross-shot stable, held-out improving; otherwise the nominal value stays with the negative result visible. The 2 unchannelled case groups (P6 upper/lower) stay induced.

§4 — Acceptance gates

  • Monotone ladder: the frozen-gate absolute residual never increases when a stage lands; a stage that increases it is reverted or its parameters stay provisional with the negative result recorded.
  • Held-out discipline everywhere: every promoted parameter improves held-out prediction on the stage-A cohort and is stable across excitation families; splits are declared before fitting; ablations prove identifiability before one block absorbs another's residual.
  • Description owns calibration: no fitted constant lands in a scoring or operator path; everything enters the artifact's evidence ledger with source, assumptions, uncertainty and supported shot range.
  • Deliberate digests: the physical digest moves only in the upstream registry-corrections node with an old→new mapping; artifact revisions compare by semantic identity; the ambix pin bumps with the dependency-contract test in the same commit.
  • Closure: artifact ≤ incumbent absolute residual on both arms; noise envelope quantified with the remaining gap decomposed per channel; all six S2 physical gaps re-dispositioned against the new evidence; negative results as visible as promotions.

§5 — Execution definition

Coordinator-only orchestration per reckon-ship sprint mode; Opus-class background workers, maximum two concurrent, each shut down after its manifest is audited and integrated. One detached worktree per worker on shared storage (git worktree add --detach beside the checkout — never under /run/user, which SLURM compute nodes cannot see). Environment rules embedded in every dispatch: uv sync before anything; uv run python -m pytest, never bare pytest; export JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu on login nodes; heavy fits and every gate stamp on srun --partition=sun_debug --cpus-per-task=8 --mem=16G --time=01:00:00 with OMP_NUM_THREADS=1, one stamp per job, file-redirect with exit sentinels, and no tracked-file edits while a stamp runs. Artifact publication is node-local only (GPFS rejects RENAME_NOREPLACE); revisions pin by semantic identity. Workers commit locally by explicit path list and never push; the coordinator audits (git show --stat, trailer sweep, scope check), merges, pushes, records plan state after every landing, and removes each worktree only when clean and reachable. Stage order A → B → (C ∥ D where scopes are disjoint: C is sensor records, D is winding geometry) → E → F re-stamps interleave after each landing. Cross-repo authority: nova owns all fitting and authoring; imas-ambix changes are limited to the pin bump and reader consumption of new evidence records, under the same authorization as the S2 cutover.

§ Decisions

What is the closure criterion for the vacuum floor — relative budgets, beating the incumbent absolutely, or a fixed numeric residual?

Owner directive 2026-08-04: vacuum shots must be recoverable to a very high level of accuracy. The incumbent's 0.740 is a measured, meaningful bar — it is what a description calibrated against this machine's own data achieves — so beating it absolutely proves the artifact description is at least as good as the incumbent everywhere the gate looks, and retires the qualified cross-source pass. A fixed numeric target ahead of the stage-A noise-envelope measurement would be arbitrary; the envelope becomes the stretch floor once measured. Changes if: stage A shows the envelope sits above 0.740 (then the incumbent bar is already noise-limited and the target reduces to statistical equivalence).

Does interior winding-turn placement (the lattice inside a pack outline) enter the physical configuration digest, or is it drive-level representation carried as evidence? The outline, turns and polarity are already identity; the question is the interior layout that stage D authors.

Locked on the mechanism precedent this sprint exercised twice: fitted/derived values move the artifact's SEMANTIC identity while the physical digest holds (the stage-A+B republish changed ten records fitted→published at unmoved ca06c8f64481114f). A stage-D lattice is a FITTED estimate with stated assumptions — S2 measured that copying efm's lattice HURT the residual, so lattices will be refit as evidence improves; if they entered the physical digest every refit would move machine identity and churn every pin for a drive-level detail. Identity keeps what hardware states: outline, turns, polarity. Reopen path per §07 if as-built winding drawings ever surface (a MEASURED lattice would be a physical fact and the decision deserves re-examination).
