§1 — What is measured, and what is not yet understood

The exclusion chain is complete and the obstruction is continuous-but-kinked. On the held moment-representation lineage, the free-boundary continuation rejects reproducibly at a bracket where: σ_min of the residual Jacobian stays finite along the accepted branch (no smooth fold); a 4× solver budget reproduces the stall residual to ten digits (no starvation); the pointwise tangent verifies against finite differences; and — after the participation repair — discrete events account for exactly zero of the stall (zero crossing-set changes, zero topology-selector changes, controlled aggregate jump 0.0 Wb). The map is value-continuous by measurement; what remains are derivative kinks — surfaces where the map is C0 but not C1 — with a deterministic Newton cycle across them fitting every observation.

What is not yet known is which kink class dominates, and that decides everything downstream. At the bracket endpoints the topology class stayed diverted with identical selectors, and the only discrete differences were three cells entering or leaving clip support — but endpoints are not trajectories. The kink the solver actually cycles on lives on the path between Newton iterates, where nobody has yet looked. Candidate classes, from local-and-frequent to global-and-rare:

  • clip support entry/exit — a cell's clipped support appearing or vanishing: value-continuous (measured, ε-ratios ≈ 2), derivative-kinked, one surface per near-boundary cell — the frequent, low-amplitude class;
  • atomic-edge crossing flips — an edge's crossing existence switching with node flux signs: the same class one level finer;
  • ring-completeness × participation — a cell entering participation whose interior stencil cannot form its gradient (35 such cells on the accepted root, −7.8 kA, 5.2e-4 of |I_p|): small in amplitude, but a distinct surface family;
  • x-point candidate selector switches — the selected saddle changing identity while the class stays diverted: rare, but it moves the boundary flux;
  • boundary-flux normalisation kinks — anything discrete in the axis or boundary flux feeds ψ_N globally, kinking every cell's source at once: the rare, high-amplitude class, including the limited↔diverted switch as its extreme case (not observed at the endpoints, unmeasured along trajectories).

The owner's directive is the plan's spine: understand what we are fixing before we fix it. No treatment is implemented until the census names the dominant class.

§2 — The kink census: name the surface Newton cycles on

Instrument the solver trajectory itself on the coarse reference fixture, at the barrier bracket and from the analytic seed. Along every Newton iterate path (and along the line segment between consecutive iterates, sampled densely enough to catch surface crossings): log each kink-surface crossing by class — clip support entry/exit (which cell), atomic-edge crossing flips (which edge), ring-completeness participation changes, x-point selector identity, axis/boundary flux sources, and topology class. For each crossing record the derivative jump it induces in the composed map (directional derivative from both sides) and its amplitude relative to the local Newton step. Then correlate: which class does the cycling iterate pair actually straddle, at what amplitude, how many times per iteration?

Done-when: a banked census reports per-class crossing counts and derivative-jump amplitudes along the stalled trajectories; the dominant class is named with the measured margin over the next class; the limited↔diverted question is answered explicitly (observed or excluded along trajectories, not only at endpoints); and the census states whether the dominant surface is local (per-cell) or global (normalisation-chain) — the fact that selects between §3 and §4 emphases.

§3 — Treatment 1: kink-aware root finding on the unchanged map

Gated on §2. The map's semantics do not change; the solver learns the map's true smoothness class. Candidates to evaluate against the convicted kink class, on the fixture, with the unchanged gate set: semismooth Newton variants (Clarke-generalised Jacobian elements at kink straddles); nonmonotone line search or trust-region globalisation that tolerates crossing a kink without cycling; damped Picard–Newton hybrids exploiting the measured map eigenvalue; and step-restriction at detected surface crossings (the census instrument doubles as the detector). The winner is selected by measurement: residual to the unchanged 1e-6 gate on the coarse fixture, then the fine continuation to t=1, then the full unchanged gate set at the reached root.

Done-when: either a route configuration converges the coarse analytic-seed solve to the unchanged 1e-6 residual gate with the unchanged physics gates scored at the root and the fine t=1 continuation scored likewise — or every candidate's failure is banked with its mechanism, which is the honest input §4 needs.

§4 — Treatment 2: C1 smoothing of the convicted surface (owner-gated)

Gated on §2's verdict and on §3's outcome; opening it is an owner decision because it changes representation semantics. Only the convicted surface is smoothed — a C1 transition over a measured band (for a support-entry kink: the clipped moments blended smoothly from zero over an ε-band of the entering cell's inside-fraction; for a normalisation kink: a smoothed boundary-flux blend), with the smoothing scale a reported parameter, never a hidden default. The physics price is re-measured, not assumed: patch-area conservation, the continuity pins, the steep-edge demonstration gates and the single-cell equivalence rungs all re-run under the smoothed map, with any degradation stated beside the convergence gain.

§5 — Adjudication and the held lineage's merge

Whichever treatment closes the residual gate: the coarse reference lane runs once, the fine continuation to t=1 is scored, and the analytic-validation verdict is written. That verdict is the owner's gate for merging the held lineage (routing + canonical centres + participation pin and repair) onto the primary branch and thereby the gate the sprint's forward chain — the SOL demonstration, the fcoil_c decomposition primary, and efit-forward-parity — stands on.

§6 — The remaining program: two named residues

Owner direction 2026-08-17: the science comes first — the smoothing is a root-convergence fix that is part done, failing on fine grids and for the far seed. The measured decomposition so far: the cell-level clip kink is solved to round-off; the coarse topology margin is real and deliberately unsmoothed; edge-crossing births are exonerated. Two residues remain, each a concrete thread:

  • The fine-scale class. A budget-invariant cycle at ~5–9e-6 survives complete clip-family smoothing on the fine fixture, and smoothing shortens the fine continuation (bracket 0.4375/0.45 vs 0.5875/0.6 unsmoothed) — an unexplained inversion. The census instrument, which has only ever run on the coarse fixture, runs on the fine stalled trajectories under both maps, with the smoothing bands themselves censused as a surface class. Its verdict gates the fine-side treatment.
  • The far-seed globalisation. No treatment globalises the analytic seed on the full map (best: damped hybrid at 0.0126 on the smoothed map). Continuation from the legacy root is the working globaliser; whether the production solve adopts continuation seeding, a semismooth globalisation informed by the fine census, or another design is decided on that census's verdict.

All treatment code lives as committed experimental lineage in the session worktrees (kink-aware route options; cell-level and complete clip-family smoothing), uncommitted to the primary branch; zero-width and default-route behaviour reproduce production bitwise throughout.

THE SEAM RESTATED CORRECTLY: the two evaluation paths were never two implementations of one integral - the interior path integrates the degree-3 nodal interpolant of the true j(psi_N) over the full polygon (that is what the stencil weights ARE), while the clipped path integrates a locally LINEARISED j over the clipped polygon. On a steep-gradient cell the linear model's own truncation is 23 percent, so the paths disagree at that order and no blend width can reconcile two different functions; the pin measured the error scaling 23.1/17.3/6.0/0.65 percent at missing fractions 2.8e-4/2.0e-4/1.0e-4/3.0e-5, which is the linear model's error, not a geometry artifact. THE TRUE FIX, dispatched as consistent-support-quadrature: one formula for every clip fraction - exact moments of the SAME degree-3 nodal interpolant over the ACTUAL support polygon, via closed Green/shoelace polygon moments contracted with the interpolant coefficients. Three properties by construction rather than tuning: (1) unclipped reduces to the interior stencil as an IDENTITY, so there is no hand-off to blend; (2) by Reynolds transport the moments' derivative carries a boundary term over the separatrix arc inside the cell which vanishes continuously as the cell fills, so the kink DISAPPEARS rather than being smoothed - the smoothing branch may become unnecessary and the node tests that at width zero; (3) boundary cells gain degree-3 accuracy instead of the linear model's 23 percent error. The first-moment truncation of the COUPLING blocks is separate and intentional (the rung-2 envelope) and is untouched by this.
