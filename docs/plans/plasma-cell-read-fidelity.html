§2 The wall-node private shadow is read from the nearest cell, not from the node

What the magenta circles are. The parity harness (benchmarks/topology_parity_harness.py) compares, for each of the 37 wall carrier nodes, the private-shadow flag the cell authority publishes (ForwardFluxOperator._carrier_shadow_read, field private_wall_node_mask) against the flag the retained raster read publishes for the same node, and draws a hollow magenta circle where they disagree. The twelve-row replay at 2b228af7a records 2 to 7 differing nodes on every row. Nodes 25 and 26 (R 0.565 m, Z -1.73 and -1.83 m) differ on all twelve rows and nodes 9 and 10 (their mirror at Z +1.73 and +1.83) on eight; on those the raster says private and the cell authority says not private, with the nearest cell carrying label 0. On the 21985 and 21989 pure rows five further nodes (11 to 15, Z 1.55 to 1.73 m) differ the other way, flagged private by the cells and not by the raster, with the nearest cell carrying label 3. The parity pass criterion counts differing cells only, so the wall-node disagreement rode through the twelve-row gate unadjudicated.

Mechanism. _wall_carrier_index is the index of the nearest cell centre to each wall node by Euclidean distance, and the node's private flag is that cell's private_flux label. In a narrow private leg, and wherever the nearest cell centre lies across the separatrix leg or in an unflooded exterior cell, the nearest centre is not the cell the node's flux belongs to, so the node inherits a neighbour's label. The raster read evaluates the flux at the node itself, which is why it and the visible geometry agree and the cell authority does not.

Where it is used. The flag is live, not decorative: nova/equilibrium/topology.py excludes private wall nodes from the wall-anchor selection when the requested class is LIMITED, so that a limited read cannot pick its contact point inside the private flux, and forward_operator.py feeds the previous trip's wall shadow into the next trip's read. On a diverted read the mask is inert, which is why the twelve MAST rows agree on cells while disagreeing on wall nodes. On a limited or marginal read a node wrongly flagged not-private is eligible as the wall anchor while sitting in the private region, and a node wrongly flagged private is excluded from a contact it may be making; both are the class of error the plan plasma-cell-topology-authority exists to remove.

Node pcrf-wall-node-shadow-from-the-node. Why: a private flag taken from the nearest cell centre is a proxy that fails exactly in the narrow private legs where the limited read's exclusion matters. Deliverable: the wall node's private flag derived from the flood at the node's own position, by the cell that contains the node (point-in-polygon over the committed cell polygons, or the flood evaluated at the node's flux against the admitted saddle flux and the X-point height band, whichever the topology read already carries), with the nearest-centre lookup retired; a harness gate that counts differing wall nodes beside differing cells and fails the row on either; the twelve-row replay rerun on one GPU job with the wall-node census at zero differing nodes on every row or each residual disagreement adjudicated in the receipt with the node's flux, the saddle flux and the raster's verdict; a regression test on a synthetic narrow private leg where the nearest cell centre lies across the leg; and the reduced-read-parity figure regenerated without magenta circles. §3 The saddle merge radius is a roundoff floor, not a discretisation radius

Measured by the §1 node on the analytic 132-cell state: three qualified saddle candidates 2.0 to 2.8 cm apart, 0.13 to 0.18 of the 0.151 m pitch, each with a _root_uncertainty radius of 1.7e-13 m and an active merge radius of 8.6e-15 m. The representative selection merges two roots only when their separation is within the sum of their radii, so roots of one physical saddle found by three neighbouring cells can never fold: the radius measures roundoff in the polished position, while the spread between neighbouring cells' roots is the discretisation error the ladder already bounds at a tenth of the pitch. The census then reports one admitted saddle and two "other qualified" saddles that are the same point, which is the cluster drawn on every analytic panel.

§ Decisions

wall-node-private-shadow-is-flux-at-the-node

Measured by pcrf-wall-node-shadow-from-the-node on the twelve-row bank: the containing cell and the nearest cell centre agree on all 46 disagreeing wall nodes, so cell ownership is not the disagreement; both comparators read a proxy (a cell label on the cell side, the nearest in-material raster node on the raster side) for a flag that means 'this wall node lies in the private flux of the previous read and is ineligible as a limited-read contact'. The flag is therefore defined by the flux at the node itself: private when the node flux lies on the private side of the admitted saddle flux and the node lies within the X-point height band the topology read carries, for every wall node without a cell-label exception; the raster comparator evaluates the same rule on the raster map at the node. This follows the lead's rule that a limited boundary touches the wall and a diverted one passes through the X-point: a wall node beyond the saddle cannot be a contact whatever cell contains it.
