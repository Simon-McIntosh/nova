§1 — The measured basis and the goal

Every design choice in this plan was selected by measurement, not preference. The edge-current attribution ladder (evidence: efit-flux-decomposition landed record) established: the centroid-inside source rule is 4–8× the whole composition error, with boundary cells whose centroids fall outside assigned zero current at 100% per-cell error while net current closes to ±1%; separatrix clipping repairs cell totals at order 2.03 (R² 0.99977) once the clip is conservative; the surviving current-weighted centroid offset is 0.24–0.65 cell widths and scale-invariant; the fixed linear-block arm wins the method comparison at 1.239e-4 of span — 78× below production, 61.9× below a moving filament that pays up to 266 per-step Green evaluations; Urankar Part V admits the linear extension with one more harmonic order plus two weighted arsinh companions at a 1.0× moment-build multiplier; and the interior degree-3 stencil sits four orders below the centroid rule at 2–3 shared evaluations per cell.

The goal: nova's plasma current close to the separatrix must agree with analytic solutions well enough that H-mode equilibria — where the current gradient concentrates in exactly the cells the separatrix crosses — can be solved with confidence. Coarse meshes must remain usable: the demonstration gate in §6 is deliberately posed at coarse pitch with a steep edge gradient.

§2 — The representation contract

Each plasma cell carries current density as a linear field about its fixed geometric centroid: j(R,Z) = j₀ + j_R·(R−R_c) + j_Z·(Z−Z_c). Every target quantity is then three matrix-vector products:

ψ = G₀·I + G_R·M_R + G_Z·M_Z — and identically for B_R and B_Z with their own kernel blocks.

Inclusion is by intersection, never by centroid: any cell the separatrix enters participates, with its clipped support integrated. Conservation is exact: the sum of patch areas equals the shoelace area of the piecewise-linear LCFS built from the same edge crossings at machine precision — the property whose violation the area gate caught at 4e-5 and whose repair took it to 6.6e-16. The matrices never move: per-iteration work is node evaluations of j plus moment assembly plus matvecs; per-step Green evaluations are zero by construction.

§8 — The equivalence ladder: small validated problems up to the barrier

Owner directive 2026-08-16. Step back from full equilibrium solves and validate the representation through toy problems the solver cannot confound, building up to the mechanism blocking the reference lane. Each rung is a unit test with a convergence claim, built with nova's own tools.

  • Rung 1 — single-cell equivalence: one large hexagonal cell carrying first a uniform then a linear current density through the nine moment blocks, against the same cell defined as a plasma boundary and meshed by nova into many finer low-order elements. Flux and both field components from the two routes must converge to each other under submesh refinement, at stated orders, on near and far targets.
  • Rung 2 — clipped-cell equivalence: the same cell cut by a straight then a curved boundary; the clipped moment representation against the finely meshed clipped region, including continuity as the boundary sweeps the cell.
  • Rung 3 — the hand-off jump: a small patch with a moving boundary crossing a cell-label transition; measure the numerical jump in the composed field where evaluation hands off between the interior-stencil and clipped-support routes — the suspected barrier mechanism, measured in isolation.
  • Rung 4 — toy fixed point: the smallest free-boundary iteration exhibiting the barrier, small enough that every discrete event is enumerable.

Done-when, per rung: a red-then-green unit test pinning the equivalence or the measured jump, a fitted convergence order where the rung claims one, and the rung's numbers recorded in the cumulative evidence before the next rung opens.

§ Decisions

Which boundary-cell current method does production adopt?

Owner selection 2026-08-15, on the measured comparison: fixed blocks reach 1.239e-4 of span at order 1.92 with zero per-step Green evaluations and three matvecs; the moving filament pays 72–266 Green evaluations per step and still loses by 61.9× because a point filament at zero standoff drops every section moment where coupling is strongest; the triangle hierarchy's discard rule is first-order in both area and flux and forfeits conservation by construction. The hierarchy's one-trace execution pattern is retained as the tracing model.

When does a cell participate in the plasma current calculation?

Owner direction 2026-08-15: any plasma cell that enters the LCFS is included. Measured basis: the centroid rule assigned zero current to 32/70/93/128 boundary cells whose true current is finite — a 100% per-cell error invisible to net-current closure, which stayed within ±1.05% throughout. It also made the current a discontinuous function of the flux map.

What conservation property must the patch calculation satisfy?

Owner direction 2026-08-15: exact, no leading-order disagreement. The assertion is against the piecewise-linear LCFS built from the same edge crossings — a partition property that holds at machine precision or the clip is defective (the T-junction defect it caught measured 4e-5). The polygon-versus-analytic-curve area difference is a separate geometric O(h²) term, reported beside the assertion and never inside it.

Which target kernels receive the moment companions?

Owner direction 2026-08-15: capture the full plasma interaction stack. Field consistency matters wherever the boundary read, force balance or sensor rows consume the same representation; nine blocks total. Coil and conductor operators are untouched — they are never clipped and carry declared currents.

What development discipline governs the implementation?

Owner direction 2026-08-15. Every node's manifest states the red-then-green order for its named tests; a node whose tests never ran red has not demonstrated they test anything.
