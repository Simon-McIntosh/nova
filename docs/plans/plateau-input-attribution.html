§1 — Existence is settled, twice, and that changes the question

An earlier framing of this work asked whether a diverted-class root exists below the measured plateau. That question is already answered and asking it again would waste the plan. It is answered from two independent directions.

From the physics. These frames are EFIT reconstructions of real DIII-D discharges — the corpus ships efit_psirz, efit_q95, efit_r_axis and efit_z_axis, and the solve's own control surface is derived from the released EFIT grid. A physical diverted plasma existed at each of these time slices. So the continuum free-boundary problem, with the machine's true wall, true conductor currents and true flux functions, has a diverted solution. There is no question of the physics admitting no answer.

From the solver. Nova's discrete operator is not incapable of finding one either. Frame 22086/43 in this same corpus family solved to a terminal relative residual of 2.911868346631881e-16 with cumulative growth 3.0. Machine precision, on the shipped operator.

So the plateau is not evidence about existence. It is evidence about inputs. If a root exists physically and the operator can reach machine precision elsewhere, then a solve that stalls at 0.087–0.155 while holding topology is solving a different problem from the one the physical plasma satisfied. The productive question is which of the differences between those two problems is responsible — and the differences are not hypothetical. Four of them are documented in the driver and its receipts.

§2 — Evaluate the residual as a field, at the labelled state

What the solve currently reports is not a field residual. nova/equilibrium/fixed_point.py::_relative_residual is max|g − x| / max(|g|) — the sup norm of F(ψ) − ψ over the whole flux state, divided by the global peak of the mapped state, reduced to one scalar per iteration. So the field residual is computed and then discarded. Three consequences follow and all three matter here: every trace of where the residual lives is thrown away; a sup norm is set by a single worst cell, so “one bad cell at the X point” and “broadly wrong everywhere” are indistinguishable; and normalising by the global peak makes a residual concentrated where ψ is small invisible.

Nobody has looked at that field, and it is the cheapest measurement on this lane. One map evaluation, no solve: apply the forward map once to the labelled state and keep F(ψlabel) − ψlabel as a map rather than a number. Its spatial structure separates the candidates that prose cannot:

  • concentrated at the boundary or the X point → boundary representation, or the wall
  • proportional to a single coil's Green's function → a current wiring error
  • smooth in ψ across the core → a profile or convention scale error
  • oscillating on the stencil scale → discretisation

The prior evidence on this question is stale and must be re-run, not cited. docs/figures/efit-forward-parity/reference-seeded-forward-slice.json reports a label-seeded solve on MAST 21985/51 converging in 5 Newton iterations to fixed_point_defect = 0.0 against a 1e-8 criterion — and landing on the vacuum branch, plasma current below 1% of efm/plasma_current_c, flux map 0.6479 of the reference span away. That receipt was authored at 57a1bfb9 on 2026-08-21. The current-normalisation amplitude that pins the declared plasma current arrived at cf812416 on 2026-08-22, one day later. With a(x, I) = I / Σ unscaled_cell_current(x) renormalising every current moment, a collapse to a current-free root is unreachable by construction. So we do not currently know what a label-seeded solve does on the shipped map. The same staleness applies to the eight-route reference-seeded survey recorded in efit-forward-parity's landed prose, whose “collapse to vacuum” conclusion is quoted in this plan's own history as though it were current.

Done when a receipt carries, for each of the six frozen MAST references on the current-pinned map: the one-application residual F(ψlabel) − ψlabel banked as a field with its sup, L2 and per-region norms rather than a sup alone; the fraction of that residual falling inside the closed-flux region, in the scrape-off layer, and within one stencil width of the boundary; the terminal branch classification and plasma-current fraction of a label-seeded Newton solve run on the same tree, quoted against the stale 2026-08-21 vacuum reading and against the pinning commit that supersedes it; and a statement of which of the four candidates in §1 the residual's structure implicates. A residual whose structure implicates none of them is a reportable negative that closes this section, provided the norms and the regional split are stated.

§3 — Explain the frame that converges

Frame 22086/43 reaching 2.911868346631881e-16 is the plan's control group of one, and it is the cheapest available cross-check on §2's verdict. If §2 names the pseudo-wall, then that frame should differ in wall treatment or in how far its boundary sits from the grid edge. If §2 names the unknown currents, that frame's conductor set should be complete or its free members should matter less. A substitution that explains the plateau on five frames but cannot explain why the sixth converges has not been established.

Done when the converged frame is placed against the five plateauing ones on every substitution §2 measured, with the differing values quoted, and the receipt either names the property that separates them or states which properties were compared and found not to separate them. A null result carries its sample count, because the predecessor plan's alignment negative was dominated by power at n = 8 and that lesson transfers directly.

§4 — Method requirements carried forward, unchanged

These bind every comparative node here. Each was learned from a measured failure on the predecessor lane rather than invented.

Name the production diff between compared trees, and decline attribution when more than one candidate mechanism sits in it. The predecessor's control declined historical attribution because seven production paths differed between the banked and current trees.

Never derive an acceptance criterion from an achieved value of the quantity it scores, and a criterion must be passable by something.

Report an admission or intervention count only beside achieved traversal. A conditioning rule was measured admitting 445 of 445 trials — an apparently perfect record — while collapsing traversal to 1.15 step-equivalents, because it shrank the step before the predicate saw it.

Change one input per arm. This section's whole method is attribution, and an arm that moves two substitutions at once cannot attribute anything.

§5 — Correct the assertions that route readers back down navigated paths

This plan was drafted twice from landed prose and code comments that asserted conditions the tree no longer holds, and both drafts were wrong in the same way. That is a defect in the record, not in the reading. Three classes of false assertion are already identified and each one cost a wrong turn.

Superseded stage descriptions presented as current state. coil-circuit-discovery's landed prose describes ECOILB, E567DN and E89UP as unknown-tier free parameters. That describes its tier-resolver stage; the same plan then shipped a closed series circuit driving all five from ECOILA at integer-snapped gains. A reader who stops at the prose concludes there are three free currents, which is what happened.

Dataset-scoped refusals read as physical absences. diiid_description.py raises “the competition dataset does not ship a wall contour”, which is true of the dataset and false of Nova, whose machine description carries a deterministic physical wall ring. The forward driver's docstring then justifies a pseudo-wall on that basis. The naming compounds it: OMITTED_POLOIDAL_CONDUCTORS and UNKNOWN_POLOIDAL_CONDUCTORS both describe conductors that are now wired.

Conclusions banked before the mechanism that invalidates them. The reference-seeded vacuum-escape survey predates current pinning by one day. Nothing in its receipt or in the landed prose quoting it says so, because a receipt records the tree it measured and not the trees that came after.

Done when every false assertion in the DIII-D description, current and forward driver paths is replaced by the measured reality with a report naming each one and the evidence that it was false; the real wall ring is the default topology surface for the DIII-D forward path with the pseudo-wall retained only as an explicitly selected fallback; the affected landed prose carries a dated supersession note naming the commit that invalidated it; and nova/imas/AGENTS.md states how to find and load a machine-description IDS through the machine class, and how to check whether a receipt predates a mechanism, in terms generic to IMAS rather than specific to this corpus.

§6 — A smooth transition between diverted and limited

The admissibility predicate is Boolean: a candidate is in the requested topology class or it is refused. Measured consequence — it refuses roughly 85 of every 89 Newton updates, and the two mechanisms built to step around that refusal were both refuted. A hard classifier is the wrong object here for a reason that is physical rather than numerical: diverted and limited are not two disjoint species but the two sides of one continuous configuration, separated by whether the critical flux surface touches a material surface, and a plasma passes smoothly between them as the X point moves toward and away from the wall.

A discontinuous predicate over a continuous transition creates its own difficulty. It has no derivative for a continuation method to follow, so a step that would land marginally on the wrong side is refused entirely rather than reduced; and near the transition an arbitrarily small state change flips the verdict, which is precisely where a free-boundary solve spends its time. Making the transition smooth — a signed margin between the critical surface and the material surface, or a continuous penalty on that margin, in place of a Boolean — gives the step something to descend and makes admission a matter of degree.

Done when a continuous topology margin exists whose sign reproduces the existing Boolean classification on every case the current predicate classifies, its zero set is demonstrated to lie where the classifier flips, and the five score-blind frames are re-measured with the margin in place quoting admitted-advance count out of 89, achieved Newton-step-equivalents and terminal residual against the banked values — with the requested class still held at the terminal state, since a smooth margin that reaches a lower residual by ending in the wrong class closes nothing.

§ Decisions

How is the pseudo-wall arm run — by expanding and contracting the existing rectangle over a stated range, or by sourcing DIII-D's real wall geometry and using it as the topology surface? The rectangle sweep is available immediately and bounds the sensitivity; the real wall answers the physical question but introduces a geometry the competition corpus deliberately does not ship, so its provenance must be stated and it may cross a corpus boundary the challenge intends to hold.

If freeing the three unknown-tier currents lowers the residual, is the result a solve or a fit? Three free conductor currents are three fitted parameters, and a residual improvement bought with them is not evidence that the forward map reproduces the reference. Decide in advance what the arm is allowed to claim.
