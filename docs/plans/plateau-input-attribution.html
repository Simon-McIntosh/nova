§1 — Existence is settled, twice, and that changes the question

An earlier framing of this work asked whether a diverted-class root exists below the measured plateau. That question is already answered and asking it again would waste the plan. It is answered from two independent directions.

From the physics. These frames are EFIT reconstructions of real DIII-D discharges — the corpus ships efit_psirz, efit_q95, efit_r_axis and efit_z_axis, and the solve's own control surface is derived from the released EFIT grid. A physical diverted plasma existed at each of these time slices. So the continuum free-boundary problem, with the machine's true wall, true conductor currents and true flux functions, has a diverted solution. There is no question of the physics admitting no answer.

From the solver. Nova's discrete operator is not incapable of finding one either. Frame 22086/43 in this same corpus family solved to a terminal relative residual of 2.911868346631881e-16 with cumulative growth 3.0. Machine precision, on the shipped operator.

So the plateau is not evidence about existence. It is evidence about inputs. If a root exists physically and the operator can reach machine precision elsewhere, then a solve that stalls at 0.087–0.155 while holding topology is solving a different problem from the one the physical plasma satisfied. The productive question is which of the differences between those two problems is responsible — and the differences are not hypothetical. Four of them are documented in the driver and its receipts.

And one measured number says the answer is far away, not nearby. The forward solve places the magnetic axis 118.5 to 251.1 mm from the reconstruction's axis, mean 164.6 mm and median 133.4 mm. On DIII-D that is tens of percent of a minor radius. A solve a few iterations short of convergence does not sit 165 mm off the axis it is reproducing; a solve of a different problem does. This is the single strongest argument that the plateau is an input defect rather than an iteration budget.

§2 — Attribute the plateau to a named substitution

Each of the four substitutions has a control that can be applied and measured, and each control is a change of input rather than of solver mechanism — which matters, because three solver mechanisms have already been measured on this lane and all three moved residual by noise or made it worse. The experiment is a paired same-tree measurement per substitution, of exactly the shape the predecessor's control established: change one input, hold everything else, and quote the plateau against the unchanged arm.

The pseudo-wall is the natural first target, for three reasons: it is the surface the refusing predicate reads, its sensitivity was already flagged as worth exposing but has only ever been probed on one frame at two expansions, and a wall is the one substitution whose correct value might simply be obtainable — DIII-D's wall is a known machine geometry even though the competition corpus does not ship it. The three unknown-tier currents are the natural second: they are already guarded as free parameters, so freeing them into an outer loop and reporting the residual reached is a measurement the existing machinery supports.

Done when each of the four substitutions carries a per-frame verdict on the five score-blind frames — MOVES_THE_PLATEAU with the residual reached quoted against that frame's value in docs/figures/topology-preserving-continuation/conditioning-repair.json, or NOT_IMPLICATED with the range of the substitution that was explored — and the receipt states which single substitution accounts for the largest share of the gap. A result in which no single substitution moves the plateau is a reportable negative that closes this section, provided the explored range of each is stated, because a bound nothing can clear is diagnostic about the bound.

§3 — Explain the frame that converges

Frame 22086/43 reaching 2.911868346631881e-16 is the plan's control group of one, and it is the cheapest available cross-check on §2's verdict. If §2 names the pseudo-wall, then that frame should differ in wall treatment or in how far its boundary sits from the grid edge. If §2 names the unknown currents, that frame's conductor set should be complete or its free members should matter less. A substitution that explains the plateau on five frames but cannot explain why the sixth converges has not been established.

Done when the converged frame is placed against the five plateauing ones on every substitution §2 measured, with the differing values quoted, and the receipt either names the property that separates them or states which properties were compared and found not to separate them. A null result carries its sample count, because the predecessor plan's alignment negative was dominated by power at n = 8 and that lesson transfers directly.

§4 — Method requirements carried forward, unchanged

These bind every comparative node here. Each was learned from a measured failure on the predecessor lane rather than invented.

Name the production diff between compared trees, and decline attribution when more than one candidate mechanism sits in it. The predecessor's control declined historical attribution because seven production paths differed between the banked and current trees.

Never derive an acceptance criterion from an achieved value of the quantity it scores, and a criterion must be passable by something.

Report an admission or intervention count only beside achieved traversal. A conditioning rule was measured admitting 445 of 445 trials — an apparently perfect record — while collapsing traversal to 1.15 step-equivalents, because it shrank the step before the predicate saw it.

Change one input per arm. This section's whole method is attribution, and an arm that moves two substitutions at once cannot attribute anything.

§ Decisions

How is the pseudo-wall arm run — by expanding and contracting the existing rectangle over a stated range, or by sourcing DIII-D's real wall geometry and using it as the topology surface? The rectangle sweep is available immediately and bounds the sensitivity; the real wall answers the physical question but introduces a geometry the competition corpus deliberately does not ship, so its provenance must be stated and it may cross a corpus boundary the challenge intends to hold.

If freeing the three unknown-tier currents lowers the residual, is the result a solve or a fit? Three free conductor currents are three fitted parameters, and a residual improvement bought with them is not evidence that the forward map reproduces the reference. Decide in advance what the arm is allowed to claim.
