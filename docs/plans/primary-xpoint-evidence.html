§1 — The gap: agreement is banked, the coordinate is not

The topology capability audit (run r-20260825T070446317634) confirmed the primary X-point rule — the first reachable saddle obstruction from the magnetic axis, with the private-flux wall shadow discarded — agrees with the exact Boolean comparator on 10 of 10 margin-remeasure terminals and 53 of 53 classified fixtures, across both surface selectors, with the crossing bracketed to 6.1e-8 Wb. It also found what the agreement cannot prove: no terminal receipt serializes the selected X-point coordinate, its flux operand, the candidate table, or a Hessian/saddle check, so the 10/10 result demonstrates comparator consistency rather than independent spatial correctness. The batched test suite has the same shape of gap: test_batched_primary_points_match_per_slice directly asserts only the O-point, while the X-dependent read is exercised without a dedicated batched X-coordinate parity assertion. A selection mechanism about to route catalog-scale class decisions through this identification (sibling plan dual-branch-selection) deserves the independent audit trail.
