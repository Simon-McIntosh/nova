§1 — The gap: agreement is banked, the coordinate is not

The topology capability audit (run r-20260825T070446317634) confirmed the primary X-point rule — the first reachable saddle obstruction from the magnetic axis, with the private-flux wall shadow discarded — agrees with the exact Boolean comparator on 10 of 10 margin-remeasure terminals and 53 of 53 classified fixtures, across both surface selectors, with the crossing bracketed to 6.1e-8 Wb. It also found what the agreement cannot prove: no terminal receipt serializes the selected X-point coordinate, its flux operand, the candidate table, or a Hessian/saddle check, so the 10/10 result demonstrates comparator consistency rather than independent spatial correctness. The batched test suite has the same shape of gap: test_batched_primary_points_match_per_slice directly asserts only the O-point, while the X-dependent read is exercised without a dedicated batched X-coordinate parity assertion. A selection mechanism about to route catalog-scale class decisions through this identification (sibling plan dual-branch-selection) deserves the independent audit trail.

The diagnostics must be discriminating, not merely present. A receipt that records an absent margin as absent explains nothing. The admitted-candidate diagnostics have to separate no typed saddle candidate was found from candidates were found and all were rejected — by the wall-shadow rule or by reachability — and record the operand values that drove the rejection. That is what converts an absent operand from a mystery into a named rejection, and it is the reason this item is worth more than its size suggests.

Where the absent operands actually are, and why that is a second cohort. The exact margin is absent (+∞) on two of the six frozen MAST references, 21978/35 and 22086/43 — and those are not the terminals above. They are written by benchmarks/pinned_branch_contrast.py into docs/figures/dual-branch-selection/pinned-branch-contrast.json, which serializes the margin's sign (class_margin_nonfinite) but never its operands. So the same discriminating-diagnostics requirement applies there, against a different driver, and this plan does not carry it: regenerating the DIII-D receipt cannot touch a MAST reference. Those two references matter beyond either plan — they are the pair whose contrasting behaviour under an identically inactive class penalty, quadratic convergence against a period-2 cycle, is the open discriminator on discrete-operator-analytic-error §5.
