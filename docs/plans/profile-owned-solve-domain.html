Ruled out by banked negative results, not to be re-run: inner Krylov accuracy (dense Newton itself cycles), fixed-point map contraction as a discriminator (spectral radius exceeds one at both cycling and converged references), and scalar homotopies for cold basin reachability. §2 — The design: the residual never sees a boundary

Lead design, locked 2026-08-26. The Newton solve stops consuming the boundary definition altogether. The flood fill's product is exactly two things: the identification of the primary X-point (the first saddle obstruction reached from the axis) and the definition of the shadow regions. The O-point and primary X-point pair — both polished on the global spline by separatrix-spline-extraction §2 — define psi_norm. The only mask applied to the residual domain excludes disconnected psi_norm < 1 regions (the shadows). Every other in-vessel cell participates in the solve, and true exterior cells receive j = 0 from the profile itself, because the current density is a function of psi_norm that closes beyond the boundary — which handles the scrape-off region without any geometric clip.

What this removes: the binding-level chord clip inside the residual (_traced_clip consumed at nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py:620-640 via signed flux against boundary_flux), the limiter operand's participation in the solve, and with them the bulk of the state-dependent Boolean domain — the discrete dependence shrinks to the shadow mask and the primary-X selection, both far better conditioned than a per-cell boundary clip. What this keeps: the static material mask (state-independent), the quadratic in-cell flux reconstruction and current-moment quadrature (now over full cell supports except shadowed cells), and the reachable-wall limiter as a post-hoc classification and diagnostic quantity only. The seam is ForwardFluxOperator._support_partition plus the topology-domain selection at nova/equilibrium/source.py:608-679, per the extraction seam map.

The O/X pair is tracked state, refined in step with the solve. Lead design, 2026-08-26: the null positions that define psi_norm are carried as solve state and refined each outer iteration by the warm-started spline polish from separatrix-spline-extraction §2a (tolerance exit under a fixed cap), so the nulls co-converge with the flux map instead of being re-detected cold or polished to machine precision on an unconverged field. Because the tracked polish is a smooth function of the field — with reverse-mode sensitivity supplied by implicit differentiation at grad psi(x*) = 0 — the Jacobian sees the null's dependence on the state, avoiding at the null exactly the linearised-as-fixed pathology this plan retires at the masks. Warm tracking also stabilises primary-X selection: a tracked point cannot flap between near-degenerate candidates the way a per-iteration argmin can. The guard: the winding-degree census keeps running as the discrete detector, and the tracked null re-seeds from it when its Hessian type degrades, its gradient norm stops contracting, or the census's primary candidate moves cells (a mid-solve topology change or a primary-X exchange in a near-double-null field).

The flood fill runs on the hex plasma cells. Lead directive, 2026-08-26: topology labelling operates on the production hexagonal plasma mesh, consuming the flux already evaluated at hex cell centroids and nodes by the solve — never on a separate rectangular lattice, and never re-evaluating flux for a topology pass. The machinery exists: centre-first six-neighbour rings from hex_stencil, physical shared-edge segments from the existing tensor axes, and hex_edge_admissibility evaluated from the global tensor spline are the production classification partition (commit 80706f89) — plain six-neighbour adjacency bridges the saddle and must not return. What this section retires is any remaining rectangular-proxy component labelling on the primary-X/shadow path, including the 4-connected rect kernel, so the solve's residual mask and the classification read share one hex partition fed by one flux evaluation.

Method requirements. GPU fixed shapes throughout: fixed trip counts, no data-dependent compaction, no host callbacks. The conservation acceptance registry must be rescored — divergence identities live on the commuting production route with failable envelopes that must not be widened to fit. Mask-change-per-outer-iteration (shadow mask, under this design) is instrumented as a first-class receipt field regardless of how small the residual domain dependence becomes. The DIII-D reference — currently converging with a stable margin and one-ulp partition sensitivity — is a protected baseline: it re-solves under the new residual and its geometric agreement must not degrade.

§ Decisions

How should the free-boundary solve treat the state-dependent domain — freeze it per outer iteration, smooth cell membership, damp mask-crossing steps, or remove the boundary definition from the residual entirely?

Lead decision, superseding the three treatment options inherited from discrete-operator-analytic-error. The solve should not use the boundary definition at all: the flood fill supplies the primary X-point and the shadow regions; the O/X pair defines psi_norm; shadows exclude disconnected psi_norm < 1 regions; every other cell participates and true exterior cells get j = 0 from the profile, which handles the scrape-off region. Treating the domain is unnecessary once the domain discontinuity itself is removed from the residual.

What geometry does the topology flood fill operate on?

Lead directive: flux is already computed on the hex cell centroids and nodes; repeating that computation on a rectangular lattice for a topology pass is wasted effort, and the rect kernel's 4-connectivity was only ever accidentally correct at the saddle. The flood fill uses the hex mesh with shared-edge admissibility from the global spline, sharing one partition between the residual mask and the classification read.

Under the profile-owned domain, how does a limited configuration normalize flux? The locked design defines psi_norm from the O/X pair. On the DIII-D limited reference the wall binds well before the X-surface (margin about −0.335), so an O/X normalization admits profile current in the in-vessel region between the wall-contact surface and the X-surface unless the profile decays there.

Lead decision: normalize by the binding level — wall-contact where the wall binds first, X otherwise — with the selection expressed convention-safely . Nova is COCOS 17, so the sense of increasing flux from axis to edge depends on the sign of the plasma current: a naive min() on raw psi picks the wrong operand on one current sign. Select the level nearest the axis flux along the confinement direction (equivalently, compare in axis-anchored normalized flux), never by raw-psi ordering. The binding level is a post-selection scalar; the residual domain itself stays boundary-free per the locked profile-owned-domain decision.

How does the Newton ladder stop accepting residual-amplifying promotions?

The extended-budget diagnostic proved the stall is rate-degrading: accepted promotions amplify the residual late in the solve (block-q exceeds 1 with recurrent excursions), so no promotion budget reaches 1e-8. The profile-owned support and the 1e-8 criterion are locked and not in question. The diagnostic's ranked guidance: residual-aware globalization first, then preconditioning; a matched-mesh ladder is secondary; a larger production budget is rejected by the evidence.

Instrument before committing to a method: focused traces at promotions 26 and 259/260 discriminate line-search from conditioning causes at the cost of one diagnostic cycle, so the globalization-vs-preconditioning choice is made on direct evidence rather than ranked inference. The profile-owned support and the 1e-8 criterion stay locked; a larger production budget remains rejected by the extended-budget evidence.

How is the recovery direction rebuilt when backtracking exhausts on smooth ground?

With the height augmentation eliminating mask-flip excursions (923→49 flips, 69→0 excursions), the solve plateaus at 8.0e4x criterion because the recovery direction exhausts while conditioning stays stable (~43). Non-amplification, the 1e-8 criterion, the composed wall-height mask and the profile-owned support are locked and unaffected.

The observed signature is a betrayed model under stable conditioning: rebuild the local linearization at the current iterate and take a Levenberg-damped step on the fresh model rather than re-scaling the stale direction. Measurable by the same 650-ceiling ladder; pseudo-arclength stays the fallback if the rebuilt model also exhausts.
