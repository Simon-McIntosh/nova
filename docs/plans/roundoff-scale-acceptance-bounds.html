§1 — The second fault, now that the first is closed

The determinism lane separated two faults deliberately and closed one. Fixed persistent executable reuse now makes three fresh production processes return bitwise-identical terminal flux and bitwise-identical values for all 69 registered observables, at a steady-state cost of 0.087 per cent. And acceptance still fails at 67 of 69 bounds. That was predicted rather than discovered: making a verdict reproducible cannot make a criterion passable.

The two bounds that fail are the two that were never passable. Every one of the 11 case verdicts that moved under the cache-off arm belonged to conservation.divergence_b or conservation.divergence_j. Those are divergence residuals: in exact arithmetic they are identically zero, so their computed value is the rounding error of the computation that produced them. A relative bound on such a quantity is a bound on roundoff. The receipt's own figures show what that means — maximum absolute differences of 1.3877787807814457e-16 and 2.7755575615628914e-16, one and two units in the last place of a binary64 number near unity, carrying maximum relative differences of 2.5 and 4.5 and maximum bound ratios of 2.967 and 5.341. A one-ULP change crosses the bound by a factor of three.

This plan's own standing rule already forbids it. A criterion must be passable by something. A relative criterion on a quantity whose exact value is zero is passable only when the last bit falls the right way, which is not a property of the solve. Determinism made that luck repeatable; it did not make it a measurement.

§2 — Classify every bound before repairing any

The temptation is to loosen the two failing bounds, and it must be refused: a criterion widened until the current value passes is derived from an achieved value of the quantity it scores, which is the one thing the standing rule prohibits. The distinction that makes a repair legitimate is whether the scored quantity has a nonzero exact value.

  • Nonzero exact value. A relative bound is meaningful, because there is something to be relative to. Volume, major radius, plasma current and the geometric moments are in this class.
  • Exactly zero. Only an absolute bound is meaningful, and it must come from the arithmetic — the number of operations, the magnitudes entering them and the working precision — rather than from any observed residual. A floor of the form n·ε·scale is derived; a floor chosen because the current value sits below it is not.

The classification is not a judgement call and should not be recorded as one: for each registered observable it is a fact about the quantity's definition, checkable by reading how it is formed. Recording it once, per bound, is most of the work — and it is the part that stops this recurring on the next observable someone registers.

Done when every one of the 69 registered bounds carries a recorded classification of whether its scored quantity has a nonzero exact value, with the reason stated per bound rather than in aggregate; every bound classified exactly-zero carries an absolute floor whose derivation from operation count, entering magnitudes and working precision is shown, and explicitly not from any achieved residual; the acceptance route is re-scored under the corrected criteria on the frozen six-case cohort at both registered widths, reporting the new pass count against the banked 67 of 69 and naming every bound whose verdict changed and why; and a test asserts that a bound on an exactly-zero quantity cannot be registered with a relative criterion, so the next observable cannot reintroduce this. A re-score that still fails is a legitimate outcome provided the surviving failures are on quantities with nonzero exact values, where a relative bound means something and a failure is a real disagreement.

§3 — What this does and does not unblock

The throughput product is a store of boundaries, moments and conservation diagnostics whose value rests on a consumer trusting a published label. Reproducibility is now established and priced. What remains is whether the acceptance check that certifies a label is measuring anything: two of its bounds currently are not, and until they are, a passing acceptance run and a failing one differ by the last bit of a quantity that should be zero.

This plan does not touch convergence, and must not be read as doing so. The plateau lane established that none of four documented input substitutions moves the residual, and the operator lane established that the forward map admits an analytic fixed point at machine precision while the solver reaches a different one. Those are separate and larger questions. Repairing a criterion changes what the acceptance route can certify; it changes nothing about which equilibrium the solver finds.
