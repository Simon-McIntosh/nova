§0 — Provenance: what the trace found

The forward-parity campaign closed with verdicts scored against five carried bounds. Tracing them (efit-forward-parity §13, commits 8609d06d, 354ec88d) classified the fixed-point 1e-8, magnetic-axis and LCFS bounds as INHERITED: fixed_point.py carries the residual and no argument for 1e-8, and its registry basis reads “the profile accelerator's existing strict convergence criterion”. The x-point and topology bounds are physically motivated with reachable evidence. The axis and LCFS bounds cite a stamp pinned to an artifact digest that has since moved, so neither bound can be reproduced from its own citation. The first derived replacement was algebraically circular: a Richardson estimate with p fitted from the same two residuals reduces to R_fine exactly (E_f/R_f = 0.99994–1.00029), so its 6-of-6 pass asserted only that a number is at most itself. Both halves hold: the gate is unjustified AND the 1-of-6 convergence count is not rescued. A units comparison across scales was refused for want of a banked conversion; any cross-scale claim here needs that conversion banked first.

Existing machinery this plan extends rather than rebuilds: benchmarks/efit_parity_criterion_provenance.py (the registered criterion, the discretisation-consistent estimate, per-reference pass columns) and its receipt docs/figures/efit-forward-parity/convergence-criterion-provenance.json. Carried measurements that constrain any derivation: observed mesh orders span 0.97–3.39 in two separate strata, and extrapolating a fixed absolute criterion to convergence needs between 1.2e3 and 6.3e11 times the cells. Downstream stakes: the GPU catalog hold (mast-catalog-gpu-solve) stands on a reliability count scored against this criterion; forward-operator-refinement declares this plan its prerequisite so no refinement node can target the inherited bound; and the DIII-D forward-gate re-evaluation is sequenced behind a non-circular criterion.
