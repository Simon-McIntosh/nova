§0 — Provenance: what the trace found

The forward-parity campaign closed with verdicts scored against five carried bounds. Tracing them (efit-forward-parity §13, commits 8609d06d, 354ec88d) classified the fixed-point 1e-8, magnetic-axis and LCFS bounds as INHERITED: fixed_point.py carries the residual and no argument for 1e-8, and its registry basis reads “the profile accelerator's existing strict convergence criterion”. The x-point and topology bounds are physically motivated with reachable evidence. The axis and LCFS bounds cite a stamp pinned to an artifact digest that has since moved, so neither bound can be reproduced from its own citation. The first derived replacement was algebraically circular: a Richardson estimate with p fitted from the same two residuals reduces to R_fine exactly (E_f/R_f = 0.99994–1.00029), so its 6-of-6 pass asserted only that a number is at most itself. Both halves hold: the gate is unjustified AND the 1-of-6 convergence count is not rescued. A units comparison across scales was refused for want of a banked conversion; any cross-scale claim here needs that conversion banked first.

Existing machinery this plan extends rather than rebuilds: benchmarks/efit_parity_criterion_provenance.py (the registered criterion, the discretisation-consistent estimate, per-reference pass columns) and its receipt docs/figures/efit-forward-parity/convergence-criterion-provenance.json. Carried measurements that constrain any derivation: observed mesh orders span 0.97–3.39 in two separate strata, and extrapolating a fixed absolute criterion to convergence needs between 1.2e3 and 6.3e11 times the cells. Downstream stakes: the GPU catalog hold (mast-catalog-gpu-solve) stands on a reliability count scored against this criterion; forward-operator-refinement declares this plan its prerequisite so no refinement node can target the inherited bound; and the DIII-D forward-gate re-evaluation is sequenced behind a non-circular criterion.

§1 — Derive a criterion independent of the quantity it tests

Derive and state, as a formula with per-reference values, a convergence criterion whose inputs are quantities other than the residual it gates. The circular estimator is excluded with its algebra shown: fitting the order from the same two residuals that form the estimate collapses it to the fine residual identically. Admissible routes include an order measured from three or more independent resolutions per stratum (the two observed order strata, 0.97–3.39, must be handled separately, never pooled), or a discretisation-consistent bound derived from the mesh scale and the measured order, extending the estimate already in benchmarks/efit_parity_criterion_provenance.py. The derivation must state its own domain of validity per stratum.

Done when: the formula is stated with its independence argument (no input is the gated residual), per-reference values are computed for the six frozen MAST references, the circular estimator's collapse is shown algebraically in the same receipt, and the receipt is banked under docs/figures/scoring-criteria-derivation/.

§2 — Re-score the frozen six beside the retained 1e-8 count

Apply the derived criterion to the six frozen MAST references and report the new convergence count beside the retained registered-1e-8 count — both counts stay on the page; neither replaces the other silently. The banked verdict figures this reads: 0/6 converge at the registered 1e-8; the closest bounded residuals sit at 1.97e-3 to 1.50e-2 with one vacuum collapse (21989). The re-score changes what the count MEANS, not what was measured.

Done when: a per-reference table (reference, closest residual, registered verdict, derived-criterion verdict) is banked with the receipt, and the catalog-hold implication is stated in one sentence either way.

§3 — Replace the digest pin and classify every bound

The axis and LCFS bounds cite a stamp pinned to an artifact digest that moved; an artifact digest is write-metadata, not an identity. Re-pin the citation to the artifact's semantic identity so the bound is reproducible from its citation, then classify each of the five carried bounds as supported (derivation reached), merely contained (holds numerically but no derivation), or reclassified (cannot be derived; stated plainly with what it is instead).

Done when: the two re-pinned citations resolve to reproducible artifacts, the five-bound classification table is banked, and every bound marked reclassified carries its replacement reading in one sentence.
