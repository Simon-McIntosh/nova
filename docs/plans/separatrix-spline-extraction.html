§2a — The polish runs in step with the converging flux map

Lead design, 2026-08-26. The isolated eight-trip form is a cold-start and extraction budget, never an architectural requirement. One kernel serves three call sites: the winding-degree census detects (discrete, guarded); a warm-started tracked polish refines in step with the solve, seeded from the previous outer iteration's null, so the null error stays slaved to the field error and everything co-converges — Newton on a stationary point contracts quadratically, so the tracked null converges faster than the map once in the basin and the terminal state carries a machine-precision null at no extra cost; and the full-budget polish serves cold seeds and post-solve extraction on the frozen field. Polishing to 1e-14 on an iterate whose field error is 1e-3 buys nothing, so precision is spent only where the field can support it.

Termination is a tolerance exit with a fixed upper cap. The fixed trip count is not required by the hardware or by JAX — lax.while_loop under a convergence predicate is jit-safe and shape-fixed — so the loop exits at tolerance and the count survives only as the safety cap and cold-start default. Two known costs shape the implementation. Reverse-mode AD does not pass through while_loop: where the in-step tracked use must be differentiable so the Jacobian sees the null's dependence on the field, differentiate the solution via the implicit function theorem at grad psi(x*) = 0 (custom VJP), which makes the iteration count irrelevant to the derivative; a single fixed trip per outer iteration is the differentiable fallback. And a vmapped while_loop runs every lane to the slowest, so the batched saving is set by the batch maximum — expected to be noise here because the per-trip body is microseconds, but that expectation is measured, not asserted, by the rung below.

GPU cost: estimated, then measured. No device measurement of this kernel exists yet (the landing node's GPU parity test skipped on a CPU lane). From banked H200 measurements — device loop overhead near 7.6 µs per iteration regardless of body, against the 42.77 ms slice cost — a cold eight-trip polish estimates at roughly 60 µs and an in-step single trip at roughly 8 µs per outer iteration, about 0.1–0.2 % of a slice either way. A reserved-H200 micro-benchmark rung (isolated cold polish; in-step tracked polish over a representative warm sequence; batched tolerance-exit tail against the fixed-cap form) replaces this estimate with numbers before any always-on default is set, under the same preregistered discipline as the dual-sweep lock.

§ Decisions

Which surface supplies every extracted curve, null position, and clip?

The global not-a-knot C2 tensor spline fitted to the complete flux map — locked by the lead's direction to use the global spline fit for precise stationary-point and contour extraction, and by the standing twice-refuted status of local per-cell reconstruction. Positions may be refined locally; the field never is.

Does the stationary polish run a fixed trip count or exit at tolerance?

Lead decision: once the tolerance is reached the loop exits; the fixed count is only the safety cap and cold-start budget, not a requirement. Constraints carried in §2a: reverse-mode differentiability of the in-step tracked use comes from implicit differentiation at grad psi(x*) = 0 (or a one-trip fixed fallback), never by differentiating through the while loop; the vmapped run-to-slowest-lane cost is measured on the reserved-H200 rung before any always-on default.

Does the wall-height exclusion stay, given its guard is mis-calibrated and the rule is inert on spherical-tokamak geometry?

The lead proposed augmenting the topological private-flux shadow with a vertical rule excluding wall nodes above an upper or below a lower X-point, on the hypothesis that fewer binary mask changes would stabilise convergence. Three measurements now bound the answer. FIRST, assessment over five geometries found the ungated rule physically wrong on two: a snowflake under-shadowing by 6 cells, a far coil-null primary by 15, with an alternate-primary identity flipping 68 of 96 wall bits. SECOND, an eligibility guard on two wall-span-relative fractions repaired all of that, and a paired production run showed the guarded rule removes zero cells in all 25 evaluations of MAST 22086/43, both arms tracing identical mask differences 38,23,0,1,22,0,0,0,0,0,0,0 to an identical terminal residual of 6.08e-12. THIRD, and decisively, the eligibility census explains WHY it removes nothing, and the reason is a calibration defect rather than redundancy: of 50 side decisions, 0 were admitted and 50 were disqualified, every one of them by the minimum-separatrix-radius condition and none by same-side ambiguity. With a wall span of 3.650000095 m the radial threshold is 0.912500024 m, while MAST's genuine divertor X-points sit at 0.5725-0.6008 m, missing by 0.31 to 0.34 m. The threshold was chosen to reject synthetic coil nulls at R=0.35 m, and on a spherical tokamak it rejects the real X-points too. So the guarded rule is dead by construction on this machine, and the radius criterion is discriminating on the wrong quantity: a null should be disqualified for not lying on the plasma separatrix, not for having a small major radius, which is a machine-size proxy that cannot carry from MAST to DIII-D. Retaining the rule therefore implies replacing the discriminator, not merely keeping the code.

What sets the EFIT-match boundary gates?

Until 2026-09-01 the corroboration gates were authored metre literals (worst eligible RMS at 0.55 m), loose enough that a 2.9 cm RMS read as PASS on numbers the lead rejects. The lead's physics rationale: EFIT is a magnetics fit, but with its inferred flux functions and corrected coil currents held fixed it is a converged Grad-Shafranov solution with fixed boundary conditions, so a forward solve fed the same inputs must reproduce it to the resolution the source EFIT grid affords. Gates therefore derive from the source reconstruction grid, per machine, read from the data at scoring time - never an authored metre literal.

Lead decision 2026-09-01: set the gates to a declared fraction of the minimum grid width of the SOURCE EFIT reconstruction grid, computed from the equilibrium data each bank actually read. With flux functions and coil currents fixed, discretisation of the source grid is the only legitimate mismatch budget; anything looser hides solver or input defects, as the 0.55 m gate did.

Which fraction of the source-grid minimum width do the gates use?

The mechanism is locked in boundary-gate-authority. The remaining free parameter is the fraction. On a 65-by-65 MAST efm grid the minimum width is roughly 3 cm, so one half gives about 1.5 cm, one quarter about 0.8 cm, one tenth about 3 mm. The lead's earlier statement that boundary RMS should be sub-centimetre if not tighter is satisfied by one quarter and below on both machines. The efit-fixed-input reproduction node now dispatched measures what the discretisation actually affords, so the fraction can be locked against a measured floor rather than a guess.

Lead decision 2026-09-01: gates lock at one quarter of the source EFIT reconstruction grid's minimum width, derived from the data per machine. On a 65-by-65 efm grid this is sub-centimetre (~0.8 cm), consistent with the standing sub-cm bar. Companion directive recorded with the lock: the validation solve is COLD-START - seeding the solve with EFIT's psi is rejected as the validation path, because the GS problem must be solvable from a cold start and a failure there must surface, not be masked by a warm seed. The zero-iteration residual evaluation GS(psi_EFIT) under Nova's discretised operator stays, endorsed as the formulation-error detector and the measured floor the quarter-width gate must respect.

Does the flood fill move from the flux raster onto the plasma current cells?

The lead's question of 2026-09-01: why does the flood-fill topology diagnostic run on a regular grid at all, and should flood fill and solve both use the plasma cells? The measured state: the tensor flux raster is NOT dead code - it is the live production authority carrying psi, the global spline, X/O extraction and the saddle-aware flood that produces the private mask (connectivity_boundary.py, this sprint's shadow work). The hexagonal plasma mesh (PolyGrid.grid_coordinates, polytrim, AtomicCellMesh.from_cells, traced_clip) separately carries current, with per-cell current resolved by flux functions plus the private mask. The SEAM between the two representations is real and has produced two measured symptoms: the panel census found centre membership disagreeing with cached labels on one cell of each retained-failure 21985/51 panel, and the hex-clip defect map recorded 96 of 447 nonempty plasma supports receiving exactly zero scattered current (-0.689 MA, 4.592 percent) at the mesh rim, now under mandated repair. Consolidating flood plus source onto the plasma cells would remove the seam, and cells carry psi_norm at centroids already; the flux raster would remain the PDE and spline authority. The cost is a refactor through connectivity_boundary, domain, forward_operator and the mask/telemetry machinery this sprint just stabilised, and the flood's saddle-aware cut logic would need re-deriving on the unstructured Delaunay adjacency.

Lead decision 2026-09-01: a feasibility and prototype worker investigates hex plasma-cell support EVERYWHERE - one grid for a GPU-batchable 2D spline representation and for the flood fill - before any commitment to consolidation. Design constraints stated with the decision: the fast null identifier is the existing six-neighbour sign-change count around each cell centroid (nova/biot/fieldnull.py categorize_2d) and remains primary, with the global 2D spline fit reserved for polish; and the spline solution is split in two at the plasma boundary with a discontinuous C2 - an interior spline separated from an exterior spline - because the current-density jump at the separatrix makes the second derivative discontinuous there, and a single global C2 fit smooths across that jump. The measured motivation stands beside it: MAST's zero-iteration residual is edge-concentrated (peak 0.0665 m from the LCFS) and halves with resolution, exactly the error class a boundary-split representation targets.
