§4 — Where the toroidal-field volume term comes from

The assembled functional W = -2 μ0 Sv - 6 μ0 P - Z carries the volume integrals P = ∫ p dV and Z = ∫ Bφ2 dV, so βp + li/2 is not a functional of the boundary field alone; the integral node's derivation shows a single virial field cannot remove both. The lead ruled on 2026-10-01 (decision toroidal-volume-term below): Z comes from a diamagnetic-loop measurement where the machine carries one, which makes the observed side fully external at the cost of a new input, and from the solved state's own integral only where no loop exists; every receipt names which source each row used. The diamagnetic flux enters through the magnetics reader the tree already carries, nova/imas/io_magnetics.py, whose diagnostic table names the diamagnetic_internal, diamagnetic_external, diamagnetic_compensation and diamagnetic_differential flux-loop channels; the store row is sourced through that reader, never a parallel one.

Done when. The decision below is locked, the receipt provenance field names the source of Z, and, if the diamagnetic route is chosen, the MAST diamagnetic signal is read through the store with its calibration stated.

§ Decisions

Where does the toroidal-field volume term Z of the Shafranov functional come from?

Lead decision 2026-10-01. The plan exists to make the Shafranov constraint's observed side external, and the diamagnetic flux is the standard measurement that separates beta_p from l_i/2. Taking Z from the solved state's own integral of B_phi squared leaves the observed side partly self-referential: the constraint would partly check the solve against itself. Where a machine carries a diamagnetic loop (MAST does), Z comes from it; elsewhere it falls back to the solved state, and every receipt names which source it used. Accepted cost: a new calibrated input whose sign and calibration must be stated.
