§4 — Where the toroidal-field volume term comes from

The assembled functional W = -2 μ0 Sv - 6 μ0 P - Z carries the volume integrals P = ∫ p dV and Z = ∫ Bφ2 dV, so βp + li/2 is not a functional of the boundary field alone; the integral node's derivation shows a single virial field cannot remove both. Until the lead rules, Z is the solved state's own integral and every receipt says so. The alternative is a diamagnetic-loop measurement where the machine carries one, which makes the observed side fully external at the cost of a new input.

Done when. The decision below is locked, the receipt provenance field names the source of Z, and, if the diamagnetic route is chosen, the MAST diamagnetic signal is read through the store with its calibration stated.
