§1 — What the demonstration must show

Owner commission 2026-08-16. A short proof of principle that SOL currents operate in the forward solve and can be maintained all the way to the divertor target, while the private-flux region carries no current. The target is a finite and continuous j crossing the separatrix and extending into the SOL — not a sheet, not a jump — with no artificial normalised-flux truncation: the exponential closure itself drives the current to zero, and the support is the whole common-SOL mask, which the material boundary already bounds. A cutoff at 1.05 or 1.10 would reintroduce exactly the support-edge discontinuity class the edge representation was built to remove.

The machinery this rides on is landed: the linear edge-current representation carries clipped boundary supports and exterior moments in the same three per-iteration vectors with no new coupling blocks; the topology read supplies common-SOL and private-flux masks; and the forward solve's SOL continuation contract (value and first-derivative continuity at the separatrix, independent private-flux policy) is in place from the forward-equilibrium plan. This plan composes them into a physical demonstration; it does not add solver machinery.

§2 — The SOL current closure

The SOL width and fall-off come from the Eich scaling: the heat-flux width λ_q evaluated from the equilibrium's poloidal field sets the physical decay length at the outboard midplane, mapped into normalised flux to give the e-folding of j(ψ_N) for ψ_N > 1. The decay is exponential; a dual e-folding variant (a narrow λ_q channel plus a broader spreading length) is measured beside the single-length form rather than assumed. Continuity at the separatrix is by construction: the SOL profile starts from the confined profile's separatrix value, so j is finite and continuous across ψ_N = 1.

Domain discipline: the SOL source lives on the common-SOL mask only, following field lines to their strike points on the material boundary — the current is maintained to the divertor target because the support extends to where the common-SOL flux surfaces meet the wall. The private-flux region is excluded by its topology label: it receives zero current however far its flux values sit below the support limit. This is the connectivity distinction the domain machinery already draws; the demonstration asserts it live.

§3 — Two solutions, one net current

Run two forward solutions on the same machine at identical net plasma current and p′/FF′ profiles as close as the closure permits: one with all current inside the LCFS (the confined control), one with part of the current flowing in the SOL under the §2 closure. Report:

  • ⟨j⟩ flux-surface-average profiles for both solutions across the full ψ_N range including the SOL leg, showing the finite continuous crossing at ψ_N = 1 and the Eich-scaled decay;
  • discrete-contour flux-map comparisons of the two solutions (matched levels), localising where the SOL current moves the equilibrium — boundary shape, x-point, strike-point geometry;
  • the measured support extent per width variant: the ψ_N at which j falls below a stated insignificance floor (e.g. 1e-6 of its separatrix value) — an outcome of the closure, never an imposed limit;
  • live assertions: zero current on the private-flux mask in every case; net-current identity between the two solutions to solver precision; current present on common-SOL cells adjacent to the strike points (maintained to the target).

Done-when: both solutions converge on the same machine with the net-current identity and private-flux-zero assertions green; the ⟨j⟩ profiles and matched-level contour overlays are banked as figures with the measured support extent per width variant; and the divertor-target continuity is asserted on the strike-point cells rather than claimed.

§ Decisions

What sets the SOL current width and fall-off?

Owner direction 2026-08-16: use the Eich scaling to set the SOL width and the fall-off of current in the SOL — exponential decay, possibly with dual e-folding lengths. The dual-length variant is measured beside the single length rather than assumed.

Does the private-flux region carry SOL current?

Owner direction 2026-08-16: maintain SOL current all the way to the divertor target whilst not solving currents in the private region. The topology label, not the flux value, excludes the private region — matching the locked domain discipline of the forward-equilibrium plan.

Where does the SOL current support end?

Owner refinement 2026-08-16 (superseding the same-day ladder direction): with an exponential fall-off there is no need to truncate — the closure definition itself drives the current to zero, the common-SOL mask is already bounded by the material boundary, and an artificial ψ_N cutoff would reintroduce the support-edge discontinuity class the edge representation removed. The support extent is reported as a measurement — the ψ_N where j falls below a stated insignificance floor per width variant — never imposed.
