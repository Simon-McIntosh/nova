§ Decisions

Which section kernel is the production default across all coupling paths?

Owner ruling 2026-08-20: there were never significant runtime advantages to the far-field filament truncation — the exact kernel costs 4 primitive evaluations against the quadrupole's ~5 and is more accurate, and the banked filament-truncation measurement already showed the exact section as the better buy at production's own far-field fraction. Exact is the default; speed is recovered at the ladder (§5), never by kernel truncation.

May a coupling substitute a simpler section shape for the authored cell geometry?

Owner ruling 2026-08-20: if we select hex then we use hex — no fallback to rectangle sections, ever, and the absence of the fallback is verified by construction and by test, not by convention. Measured basis: the substitution carries a stable ~3.5% share of the composition error with +20%/−10% second-moment distortion.

What is the accuracy authority for the forward model?

Owner ruling 2026-08-20: the Solov'ev solution is the fixed oracle and there is no confusion here — any disagreement between the discrete forward model and the analytic solution is nova's defect to fix. No gate is relaxed to accommodate a disagreement; an h-independent deviation is a defect whatever its size. Further forward progress gates on the §4 agreement set.

What is the runtime target for the production forward solve?

Owner ruling 2026-08-20: a converged Newton-Krylov or Newton-only free-boundary fixed point in about one millisecond on GPU, with design-matrix build time reasonable on both CPU and GPU. Accuracy is bought at build time through exact precomputed matrices; runtime stays fixed-shape matvecs. The §5 ladder prices every accuracy increment so the operating point is chosen on numbers.
