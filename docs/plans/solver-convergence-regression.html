§3 — Root cause and repair

With §1's revision verdict and §2's mechanism, identify the responsible change by targeted reading of the named subsystem's history — never a git-bisect workflow — and repair it or, if the new behaviour is judged correct (the old convergence was spurious), re-adjudicate the committed bank with the lead. A repair that touches solver semantics returns to the lead before merging.
