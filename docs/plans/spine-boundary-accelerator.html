§1 — Objective

Nova owns all physics solves; this plan makes the differentiable boundary push its pinned, accelerated core. The smooth LCFS discovery developed in ambix — identify the o-point, push a contour outward until it binds at the first x-point or the wall, one code path with no limited/diverted branch, continuous through the marginal transition by construction — already lives in nova code-identically (nova/equilibrium/connectivity_boundary.py + stencil_nulls.py + flux_surface_connectivity.py; audit: boundary-push-port-audit). What did not port is what makes it trustworthy and fast: the behaviour tests that pin x-vs-wall termination and switch continuity, the exact-tangent Newton–Krylov that converts 6× more slices than production Picard, the safeguarded Anderson mixer, the profile-damping guard, and the topology-read cost removals that the batched corpus engine was stalled on. This plan lands all of it in nova and retires the ambix accelerator plan (supersedes imas-ambix:batched-newton-accelerator).

§ Decisions

Which convergence accelerator is adopted as the batched inner solver? Carried from the superseded ambix plan. Evidence so far: exact-tangent NK converts 62% of cold slices at a 40-evaluation budget vs 19% smooth-Picard and 10% production-Picard, with every operation a Green's-GEMM column or tiny dense solve; Anderson ties Picard exactly on both maps in both studies. Lock after the §2 item-2 re-measurement on the fast read.

Re-measured on nova's reconstruction sweep map (benchmarks/fixed_point_ladder.py: 18 cold-seed slices with self-consistent synthetic magnetics, shared 40-evaluation budget). Every well-posed slice converts under all three schemes, with exact-tangent NK and safeguarded Anderson reaching a median residual near 6e-14 - four decades under relaxed Picard's 7e-10 - and NK converging the consistent rich-profile slices deepest (7e-7 at 40 evaluations, 7e-11 as the bootstrap driver, where Picard stalls near 2e-4). Combined with the carried ambix cold-seed evidence (62% conversion vs 19% smooth-Picard and 10% production-Picard on real slices; Anderson tied Picard there), the exact-tangent NK is the batched inner solver; the safeguarded Anderson mixer and relaxed Picard stand as the fallback ladder. All three are nova.jax.fixed_point package symbols sharing one per-evaluation residual trace.

How do the rich-profile coefficients enter the Newton iteration — lifted into a coupled KKT state (nonneg + prior rows inside the Jacobian), or held as an inner implicit fit with damping only?

The exact-tangent NK converges the implicit-fit map everywhere the data is self-consistent, including the rich (2,2)-degree family (6.9e-7 at 40 evaluations; 7.2e-11 driving the bootstrap), so the profile coefficients never need to enter the Newton state for convergence. The guards are the profile_relaxation damping parameter (independent coefficient morphing) plus the donor fixed-shape degradation branch (a sweep whose read fails keeps the previous coefficients and flux). The two failing ladder slices carry bootstrap data that never became self-consistent and defeat all three schemes equally - a data-consistency problem, not a state-lift problem. The coupled-KKT lift (section 2 item 5) is retired unless MAST catalog solves surface coefficient thrash the damping cannot hold.
