§1 — Objective

Before the ML-assisted step and before the GPU catalog run, the spine must prove it reconstructs equilibria in a way comparable to the existing EFIT solutions. The solver family is ported (ReconstructMoment / ReconstructProfile / topology labels / CurrentDiffusion with its TORAX cross-check at 0.4% RMS), the boundary push is being pinned and accelerated (spine-boundary-accelerator), and the corrected read layer guarantees the measured inputs (mast-corrected-read-layer). What has never been demonstrated is the assembled chain: measured magnetics in, GS-balanced flux maps out, scored against the firewalled EFIT referee on real MAST shots. This plan is that demonstration — the match-then-exceed gate the GPU catalog's throughput ladder stands on. A catalog of fast wrong equilibria is worthless; parity comes first.

§2 — Design

  • Shot set: the frozen six-shot benchmark set {21978, 21983, 21985, 21986, 21989, 22086} with all prescribed slices as the registered gate, plus a held-out extension stratified across campaigns and both field polarities (drawn once, pre-registered, never re-drawn) to show the result is not tuned to the six.
  • Referee discipline: EFIT (the efm catalog) is firewalled — scored against, never fitted to, never used to seed. Tolerances are pre-registered from measured spreads before the first scored run (the spine_bench precedent: 15 registered tolerances; reproduction metrics on change budgets, solve-health at exact fractions, the raw-magnetics residual measured from both ends). The cross-source budget mechanism from the cutover adjudication applies where the comparison crosses geometry sources.
  • Inputs: solve inputs load through the corrected read layer only — acquisition scales, promoted gains, exclusions and dispositions applied by construction; machine geometry from the content-addressed MAST artifact.
  • Transport consistency: CurrentDiffusion runs the shot's flux ledger on the reconstructed equilibria — the 1D ψ-transport spine and the GS solve must tell one story (flux consumption, li/βp separation) within registered bands; this is the temporal leg the per-slice metrics cannot see.
  • Batch form: the scored solve runs the same fixed-shape, vmap-safe path the GPU catalog will batch — parity proven on the eager path alone would not transfer.

§3 — Work items

  1. Assemble the chain behind one entry point: corrected reads → moment seed → accelerated profile solve → topology labels, with the per-slice scorecard emitted beside the solve.
  2. Register the tolerances from measured spreads (committed stamps, substrate-swap floors, sensor-displacement pricing) before the first scored run; document each bound's basis.
  3. Run the gate: the frozen six + the held-out extension; bank the per-shot, per-slice scorecard with figures (flux-map overlays, boundary distance distributions, residual decompositions per channel group).
  4. Transport leg: CurrentDiffusion flux-ledger consistency on the gated shots; verdict recorded per shot.
  5. Adjudicate: pass/fail per registered tolerance; a qualified verdict states exactly which metric failed, by how much, and what mechanism explains it — the honest-negative discipline is in force.

Done-when: the assembled spine chain scores the full gate with every metric adjudicated against its pre-registered tolerance; the scorecard record lands with figures; the verdict (parity / qualified / fail with mechanism) is written into this plan and the GPU catalog plan is unblocked or explicitly held.

§ Decisions

What bar unblocks the GPU catalog: parity on every registered metric, or parity on geometry/solve-health with a qualified allowance on the raw-magnetics residual (where the referee's own fitted calibration gives it a structural edge)?

Owner selection 2026-08-12: geometry, solve-health, profile-residual and the CurrentDiffusion temporal leg are scored strictly; the whitened raw-magnetics residual is judged under the cross-source budget class, with its margin recorded either way. The referee is not neutral on that one metric: efm carries a magnetics-fitted solenoid calibration, so reproducing the measured magnetics grades nova against EFIT's own fit rather than against the machine. The S5 vacuum-floor ladder is the measured precedent — it failed its same-source clause by +0.023 sigma while passing source-cutover 15 of 15, and the gap decomposed onto channels sitting 91-253x above their own noise floors. Scoring magnetics strictly would therefore risk holding the GPU catalog on evidence that cannot separate a spine defect from the incumbent's calibration advantage. Everything else stays strict, so the allowance buys no slack on geometry or convergence.
