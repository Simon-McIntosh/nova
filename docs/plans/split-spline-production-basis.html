Split-spline production basis

Harden the prototype's boundary-split representation into a production module. The physics: the current-density step at the separatrix makes ψ's curvature discontinuous there, so the field is represented as an interior patch and an exterior patch, each C2 within its region, ψ and ∇ψ continuous at the interface, second derivative free to jump. A single global C2 fit smooths across that jump — the error class MAST's edge-concentrated zero-iteration residual exhibits (0.0116 at stride 1, peak 6.7 cm from the LCFS, halving with resolution).

§ Decisions

The locked design constraints for the single-grid representation

Stated by the lead on 2026-09-01 with the feasibility order, and proven by the prototype benchmarks/hex_cell_field_feasibility.py at 4 of 4 criteria: boundary-band second-derivative RMS 0.267 for a single global C2 fit against 1.87e-15 for the split fit at equal 16-coefficient budgets; axis polish 5.0 mm to 0.101 mm in one step; saddle to 6.06e-16 m; zero private-mask cell differences against the committed MAST operand.

Lead decision: the six-neighbour sign-change census around each cell centroid (nova/biot/fieldnull.py categorize_2d) is the primary null identifier and the spline exists for polish only; the field representation is split in two at the plasma boundary with an interior spline separated from an exterior spline, each C2 within its region, psi and gradient continuous at the interface, and the second derivative free to jump, because the current-density step at the separatrix makes the curvature discontinuous and a single global C2 fit smooths across it — the error class MAST's edge-concentrated zero-iteration residual exhibits.
