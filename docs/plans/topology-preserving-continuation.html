§1 — The frontier, measured

The forward free-boundary solve no longer fails for unknown reasons. Over 2026-08-23 the failure was decomposed until what remains is a single, quantified, well-posed obstacle, and this plan exists to close it. Every number below is measured and banked; none is an estimate.

What was fixed, so the frontier is not confused with the debris in front of it. The exact residual-Jacobian action was non-finite on the first Arnoldi vector — root-caused to division by a zero singular-value gap inside the JAX SVD JVP, reached through jnp.linalg.lstsq in the Null2D quadratic magnetic-null fit, because symmetric fixed-grid stencils carry repeated singular values (commit 06b09f5b). A finite but exactly zero Krylov step was being reported as success and bypassing the existing non-finite rail; it is now one of four named refusal conditions (6143221d). Trial admission now refuses any candidate leaving the requested topology class, and on the banked case all 89 promoted iterations hold diverted with a finite terminal X point where the unqualified route crossed at iterations 31, 50 and 88 and terminated limited (257af9dc). Branch selection was already closed by construction through amplitude elimination. None of that is the frontier.

The frontier is that the admissible step is too small to matter. Of 1,671 rejected trials across the five score-blind frames, 1,669 were rejected for leaving the topology class and only 2 for a non-finite state — zero Krylov-action refusals, zero non-finite candidates. Probing below the production ladder found the largest admissible fraction to be exactly 0.03125 on all 85 probed refusal iterations, with 0.0625 inadmissible. Admission runs at 4 to 9 of 89 trials. An 89-iteration budget taking steps capped near one thirty-second would traverse roughly 2.8 Newton-step-equivalents — but that figure is 89 × 1/32, a hypothetical for a budget in which every iteration admits at the probed cap, and it is not what the route achieves. See the measured correction below.

Extending the backtracking ladder to reach that step was measured and is null. Six fixed factors down to 0.03125, demonstrably exercised with the smallest rung selected on three of five frames, moved admissions only to 7, 7, 10, 5 and 9 of 89 and moved residuals by +4.5%, −1.4%, −7.5%, +0.14% and +2.8% — three worse, two better, indistinguishable from noise. The frames sit at terminal residuals 0.0854 to 0.1644 against unqualified plateaus of 0.0259 to 0.0847, so the topology guard currently converts a confidently wrong answer into a correct-but-stalled one. That is a better failure and it is not convergence.

Measured correction to this section, 2026-08-23 — the diagnosis is admission rate, not step length. A paired same-tree control now measures what this section inferred, and two of its readings must be restated. First, the admitted steps are not small. The 0.03125 figure is the largest fraction admissible on the 85 probed refusal iterations; on the iterations that do admit, the largest admitted factor is 1.0 on all five frames — full Newton steps. Second, the achieved traversal is not 2.8. With step conditioning disabled on the current tree the five frames traverse 18.88 Newton-step-equivalents in total (2.92 to 5.19 per frame), against a banked selected-factor proxy of 22.47. So the obstacle is that only 4 to 10 of 89 iterations admit anything, not that what they admit is capped small. A predictor-corrector that advances along the manifold still targets exactly that quantity — how often a material advance is available — which is why §2 stands unchanged. What changes is the comparator: the honest baseline is the conditioning-disabled same-tree control, never the 2.8 hypothetical. Full record: §2 control.

And a production regression sits in front of this work. The same paired control isolates dimension-general step conditioning by moving only its condition-ratio limit from e to infinity. Enabled, it fires on 89 of 89 iterations of every frame and admits 445 of 445 factor-one advances — an admission record that looks perfect — while collapsing effective traversal to 1.15 Newton-step-equivalents and worsening terminal residuals from the 0.087–0.155 range to 3.75–6.35, roughly two orders. Topology stays formally admissible throughout because the mechanism shrinks the step before the predicate sees it, so full admission is what shrinkage looks like from the admission counter. §4's own requirement was dimension-generality and that is met; this is the cost of meeting it, measured for the first time, and it must be repaired before any convergence claim rests on the production default.

Why finer backtracking cannot be the answer, stated as mechanism rather than as a measurement. Backtracking searches into the admissible region from outside it. It can only ever return the largest scaled copy of a step that already points out of the region, so where the admissible fraction is small every admissible step is small by construction. Halving the factor again buys a smaller step, not a better direction. Closing this needs a route that advances along the admissible set.

§5 — Method requirements binding on every comparative node here

Two failures of measurement discipline occurred while this frontier was being characterised, both caught, both cheap to prevent and expensive to miss. They are requirements on this plan's nodes, not advice.

State the production diff between the two trees, and refuse attribution where more than one mechanism changed. A re-measurement against banked comparators was read as evidence for the mechanism under test when a different production change had landed in between; the true mover was an unrelated operator-order commit, confirmed by an independent harness agreeing to four significant figures. Tree-pinning records what was measured, which is necessary and not sufficient — it does not say whether a delta is attributable. Every comparative receipt here names the production paths that changed between the trees it compares, and declines attribution when more than one candidate mechanism sits in that diff.

Never derive an acceptance criterion from an achieved value of the quantity it scores. A convergence criterion was derived below a floor that was itself the best residual the solve had ever reached, making it unpassable by construction including by the measurement it came from; it was withdrawn. An earlier estimate on the same lane had collapsed the same way when a Richardson term reduced algebraically to the residual it was fitted from. A criterion must come from a quantity independent of the score — a discretisation-error estimate from solution differences, an independently confirmed model, or a physical scale — and must be passable by something. A bound nothing can clear is diagnostic about the bound.

A verdict string must not encode a causal claim its measurement cannot support. A node returned a cost verdict from a bare ratio threshold on frames where the mechanism under test had fired zero times. The rule was under-specified, not the worker wrong. Any verdict here that names a cause states the evidence that the cause was active.

§ Decisions

What parameterises advance along the topology-preserving manifold — arc length in state space, the source amplitude already used by the banked source-domain continuation, or the constrained plasma current? The banked negative matters here: scalar source-strength homotopy converges to the dominating limited root, so a parameter that reintroduces that path would repeat a measured failure.

State-space arclength is the only candidate whose predictor direction is built from states already admitted on the requested branch while the physical source stays fixed, so the coordinate cannot retrace the banked source-strength curve under another name. Source amplitude is directly refuted: two bounded composed-map campaigns (21 rungs; 41 rungs at 5 Newton / 30 GMRES per rung) both terminated CONVERGED on the dominating limited root at terminal parity error 5.06e-2 against a 1e-10 recovery floor, so doubling the rung count moved traversal and not the basin endpoint. Constrained plasma current is refuted because production eliminates it: ForwardFluxOperator.current_normalisation_amplitude computes a(x, I) = I / sum(unscaled_cell_current(x)) and scaled_current_moments multiplies every zeroth and first current moment by that one common amplitude, so target current cannot move source shape, centroid or topology independently, and where the normalisation is irregular it goes non-finite and is refused rather than selecting a branch. The arclength is a numerical gauge only; the Boolean topology class is never differentiated and no second classifier is added. Adjudicated by continuation-parameter-evidence, commit f1adace7, report scripts/continuation_parameter_choice/report.md; no production solve ran, so the report carries no performance attribution.

What observable measures seed alignment without a converged solve? It must be computable from the seed and the operator alone, since a criterion needing the answer cannot choose the starting point.

Preregistered as q(s,F) = ||F(s)-s||_2 / ||(I - J_F(s))(F(s)-s)||_2, computed from the cold moment seed, one fixed-point map evaluation and one tangent action, with no converged solve anywhere in the path - which was the section's hard constraint, since a criterion needing the answer cannot choose the starting point. Larger q means the seed residual occupies a direction on which the local fixed-point action is weaker. The definition is LOCKED AS THE CANDIDATE, not promoted as a selector: on the five-frame banked-outcome corpus it scores Spearman 0.60 against cumulative growth at p = 0.28475697986529386 and Spearman 0.00 against terminal residual at p = 1.0, so it fails the section's requirement of consistency against both outcomes and the verdict is HOLD_AS_SEED_SELECTOR. The definition is locked precisely so it cannot be tuned after seeing those outcomes; the defect is statistical power at n = 5, and the next node enlarges the outcome bank and re-tests this same definition unchanged.
