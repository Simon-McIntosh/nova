§1 — The frontier, measured

The forward free-boundary solve no longer fails for unknown reasons. Over 2026-08-23 the failure was decomposed until what remains is a single, quantified, well-posed obstacle, and this plan exists to close it. Every number below is measured and banked; none is an estimate.

What was fixed, so the frontier is not confused with the debris in front of it. The exact residual-Jacobian action was non-finite on the first Arnoldi vector — root-caused to division by a zero singular-value gap inside the JAX SVD JVP, reached through jnp.linalg.lstsq in the Null2D quadratic magnetic-null fit, because symmetric fixed-grid stencils carry repeated singular values (commit 06b09f5b). A finite but exactly zero Krylov step was being reported as success and bypassing the existing non-finite rail; it is now one of four named refusal conditions (6143221d). Trial admission now refuses any candidate leaving the requested topology class, and on the banked case all 89 promoted iterations hold diverted with a finite terminal X point where the unqualified route crossed at iterations 31, 50 and 88 and terminated limited (257af9dc). Branch selection was already closed by construction through amplitude elimination. None of that is the frontier.

The frontier is that the admissible step is too small to matter. Of 1,671 rejected trials across the five score-blind frames, 1,669 were rejected for leaving the topology class and only 2 for a non-finite state — zero Krylov-action refusals, zero non-finite candidates. Probing below the production ladder found the largest admissible fraction to be exactly 0.03125 on all 85 probed refusal iterations, with 0.0625 inadmissible. Admission runs at 4 to 9 of 89 trials. An 89-iteration budget taking steps capped near one thirty-second traverses roughly 2.8 Newton-step-equivalents in total — about an order of magnitude short of what convergence needs.

Extending the backtracking ladder to reach that step was measured and is null. Six fixed factors down to 0.03125, demonstrably exercised with the smallest rung selected on three of five frames, moved admissions only to 7, 7, 10, 5 and 9 of 89 and moved residuals by +4.5%, −1.4%, −7.5%, +0.14% and +2.8% — three worse, two better, indistinguishable from noise. The frames sit at terminal residuals 0.0854 to 0.1644 against unqualified plateaus of 0.0259 to 0.0847, so the topology guard currently converts a confidently wrong answer into a correct-but-stalled one. That is a better failure and it is not convergence.

Why finer backtracking cannot be the answer, stated as mechanism rather than as a measurement. Backtracking searches into the admissible region from outside it. It can only ever return the largest scaled copy of a step that already points out of the region, so where the admissible fraction is small every admissible step is small by construction. Halving the factor again buys a smaller step, not a better direction. Closing this needs a route that advances along the admissible set.

§2 — Advance along the manifold, do not backtrack into it

The topology-preserving set is a manifold in state space: the states whose emergent topology matches the requested class. The Newton step is computed without reference to it and generally leaves it. Backtracking then retreats along that same departing direction until it lands back inside — which is why the admissible fraction, not the Newton direction, sets the step length. A predictor-corrector instead moves along the manifold's tangent and projects back, so step length is limited by the manifold's curvature rather than by how quickly the Newton direction exits it.

Why the step length differs. Both routes respect the same admissibility test. Backtracking is limited by how fast the Newton direction leaves the manifold; a predictor-corrector is limited by the manifold's curvature, which the measured 1/32 cap says nothing about.

Deliverable. A topology-preserving predictor-corrector in the production forward path, fixed-shape and safe under jit and vmap with no data-dependent control flow, taking the admissibility predicate that already exists rather than a second copy of it. It must reuse the landed machinery — the class predicate, the action qualification, the terminal branch receipt — and any new component needs a stated reason the existing one does not fit.

Done when the five score-blind frames are re-measured with per-frame terminal residual, admitted-advance count against the banked 7, 7, 10, 5 and 9 of 89, mean advance length as a fraction of the Newton step against the banked 0.03125 cap, terminal topology class and X-point finiteness; and the receipt states the achieved Newton-step-equivalents traversed against the banked 2.8. A route that holds topology and still stalls is a reportable negative and closes this section honestly; a route that loses topology to gain residual does not close it at all.

§3 — Seed alignment as a selection criterion

Seed direction is a measured lever on two things at once, and nothing currently chooses it deliberately. An alternate same-shot seed on an identical case moved amplification bursts from three to none and cut cumulative divergence growth from 5.379792627282051e9 to 0.0619 — eleven orders. Independently, frames whose residuals improved under warm-neighbour seeding show 136× less cumulative growth than those that worsened (medians 2.16e6 against 2.94e8), with one qualified outlier at shot 21985 slice 51 that is also the most-instrumented case and must be resolved as most-informative versus instrumentation-perturbed before that split is cited as law.

This retro-explains a result that had been banked as noise: warm-neighbour seeding lifted none of the five MAST stalls while improving three residuals and worsening two. On a strongly non-normal map, transient growth depends on the direction of the initial error rather than its magnitude, so a seeding change that helps some frames and hurts others by comparable amounts is the predicted behaviour rather than an absent effect.

The design consequence is that pooling by adjacency selects on the wrong variable. Warm-starting has been treated throughout as distance reduction — start nearer, converge in fewer steps. On this map a seed that is further away but aligned away from the transient-growth directions can beat a nearer one. Because the same seed change also governs whether a batched solve reproduces a per-slice solve, a catalog that seeds from neighbours is choosing both its iteration count and its reproducibility on one variable it does not know it is choosing.

Done when a seed-alignment observable exists that can be computed without a converged solve — otherwise it cannot select a seed — and is shown to rank candidate seeds consistently with measured outcome on the frames where outcome is already banked, quoting rank correlation against both cumulative growth and terminal residual. The 21985/51 outlier is adjudicated in the same receipt.

§4 — Step conditioning independent of Krylov dimension

Conditioning ill-conditioned Krylov steps at the iteration they occur closed the reproducibility half of the problem on the route it was calibrated for: bursts 3 to 0, cumulative growth 5.379792627282051e9 to 2.217948717948718, six-case maximum flux difference 1.1646900802908533e-05 to 3.5787553476218648e-12, with the one-map separation unchanged at 2.220446049250313e-16 so single-application agreement was not traded away (commit c710c3f1).

But it is a fitted rule, not a method, and this is the weakness to remove. Three constants come from one dataset: a trigger of 44.5 placed between one burst-local minimum and one preceding quiet event, a baseline of 27.7817 taken as one quiet median, and an exponent of 3 that is unexplained. A projected operator's condition scales with the projection dimension, so an absolute threshold cannot transfer — and it did not: all seven DIII-D carriers recorded zero conditioning interventions, because they run a 24-vector route while the discriminator was calibrated at 12. The mechanism was inert exactly where the gate lives.

Done when the trigger is a ratio against a baseline the solve estimates online from its own recent projected conditions, the damping scales from measured condition relative to that baseline, the exponent is derived from a stated principle rather than inherited, and engagement is demonstrated at three projection dimensions including 12 and 24 — with a nonzero intervention count at 24 against the banked zero, and no dimension-specific or dataset-fitted constant remaining anywhere in the path.

§5 — Method requirements binding on every comparative node here

Two failures of measurement discipline occurred while this frontier was being characterised, both caught, both cheap to prevent and expensive to miss. They are requirements on this plan's nodes, not advice.

State the production diff between the two trees, and refuse attribution where more than one mechanism changed. A re-measurement against banked comparators was read as evidence for the mechanism under test when a different production change had landed in between; the true mover was an unrelated operator-order commit, confirmed by an independent harness agreeing to four significant figures. Tree-pinning records what was measured, which is necessary and not sufficient — it does not say whether a delta is attributable. Every comparative receipt here names the production paths that changed between the trees it compares, and declines attribution when more than one candidate mechanism sits in that diff.

Never derive an acceptance criterion from an achieved value of the quantity it scores. A convergence criterion was derived below a floor that was itself the best residual the solve had ever reached, making it unpassable by construction including by the measurement it came from; it was withdrawn. An earlier estimate on the same lane had collapsed the same way when a Richardson term reduced algebraically to the residual it was fitted from. A criterion must come from a quantity independent of the score — a discretisation-error estimate from solution differences, an independently confirmed model, or a physical scale — and must be passable by something. A bound nothing can clear is diagnostic about the bound.

A verdict string must not encode a causal claim its measurement cannot support. A node returned a cost verdict from a bare ratio threshold on frames where the mechanism under test had fired zero times. The rule was under-specified, not the worker wrong. Any verdict here that names a cause states the evidence that the cause was active.

§ Decisions

What parameterises advance along the topology-preserving manifold — arc length in state space, the source amplitude already used by the banked source-domain continuation, or the constrained plasma current? The banked negative matters here: scalar source-strength homotopy converges to the dominating limited root, so a parameter that reintroduces that path would repeat a measured failure.

What observable measures seed alignment without a converged solve? It must be computable from the seed and the operator alone, since a criterion needing the answer cannot choose the starting point.
