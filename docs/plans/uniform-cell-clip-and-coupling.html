both-tilings-one-operator

Lead direction 2026-09-08, and it supersedes an earlier coordinator summary that wrongly said hex should be the default with a rectangular path retained only where needed. Four parts, all binding. BOTH ROUTES STAY OPEN: hexagonal and rectangular are both to remain possible, hex is the default carrier, and rectangular is a first-class path rather than a fallback tolerated where hex fails. EQUAL ON THREE AXES: same order, same accuracy, same speed. That rules out adapting one tiling into the other's representation at run time, because the adapter costs speed on whichever side pays for it, so both routes could produce answers and still fail the requirement. TRACEABILITY AND BATCHABILITY ARE NOT NEGOTIABLE: fixed shapes, no data-dependent branching or data-dependent shapes inside the transformed region, and no host-side dispatch on cell geometry. A method that unifies the tilings by branching on mesh type fails this even with correct arithmetic, because it breaks the compiled batch. ONE INTERACTION, ONE MATMUL, which is the crux: in the lead's words an optimum run should only need to matmul a single interaction, and carrying two grids means two operators when one would suffice. The cost of the present split is therefore OPERATOR duplication rather than code duplication. WHAT THIS REJECTS: two kernels behind a dispatch, two operators summed, and any per-tiling specialisation surviving into the traced program. WHAT IT ADMITS: a single kernel whose cell geometry enters as DATA rather than as STRUCTURE, which is the same discipline already applied when profile tables moved out of static operator identity into stacked member leaves, so one compiled program serves every member with the shape fixed and the values varying. That precedent is the closest design template in the tree.

WHY THIS PLAN EXISTS: THREE NODES HIT ONE SEAM IN A SINGLE DAY, and two questions the lead asked are unanswerable because of it rather than incidentally. THE MEASURED FACTS, all from 2026-09-08 and all traced rather than asserted. The current-moment path runs profile solve, into the operator's normalised current moments and observation, into the support partition and profile support, into the atomic mesh traced clip, into the partitioned current moments and the coupling conversion, and it NEVER reaches the bicubic arc moment correction or the twelve-node arc rule at any point. The curved path runs flux-surface geometry extraction, into the surface clips, the tensor spline fit, the cell coefficients and the bicubic arc correction, takes rectangular regular-cell inputs, and is consumed by flux-surface geometry alone. They are not merely separate but UNBRIDGEABLE as built: no callable or data conversion connects atomic polygon cells to the tensor-cell arc correction while preserving same-cell identity, so the chord-versus-curve accuracy comparison and the two-route cost profile could not be produced at all. The affine round trip met the same wall from the other side: the EFIT extraction route requires a rectangular flux lattice while our forward receipts are unstructured hex maps flagged unavailable-non-tensor-product, so the route we apply to source data cannot be applied to our own solutions, and every profile extracted from EFIT by it has never been validated against a case whose answer we know. WHAT THE MOMENT PATH DOES TODAY, which is the asymmetry the plan must remove. The separatrix crossing on each atomic edge is a LINEAR interpolation of flux along that edge; the clipped support is the polygon through those crossings; its area and first and second moments are then computed ANALYTICALLY for that polygon, exact for a cellwise-linear current density; and the clipped cell is fanned into triangles under DEGREE-FIFTEEN Duffy quadrature. So we integrate to degree fifteen over a boundary that is first order. The shipped curvature work measured the trade once and found that clipping against the arc moves the EXTREMUM to second order while improving AREA only from second to third, so the gain for current moments may be modest; nobody has measured it on this path because nobody can.

§ Open questions

Why does exact cut-cell current placement, which removes 98.6 percent of the linear flux-image error against the analytic Solov'ev field, leave the static fixed-point iteration 63x (weak -110) and 11x (moderate -110) further from its root than the chord clip? The solved weak field (docs/figures/uniform-cell-clip-and-coupling/exact-participation/certificate-figures/weak-rotation-reactor-static-production-route-reduced.png) has its axis displaced outboard and a spurious closed structure at the outboard midplane where cells 101 and 102 sit. Worker hypothesis: the chord clip and the spline clip both take their boundary roots and density coordinates from the same shared_node_flux and support_flux_coefficients interpolant, so the chord supplied a compensating placement bias between that flux read and the moment-image couplings (cell_current, radial_moment, vertical_moment through coupling_current_moments into current_moment_image); exact placement without an exact flux read steepens the geometry-to-current-to-flux feedback. The lead's standing hypothesis that the second-order compatibility terms (the curvature moments added by quadrature) are not compatible with steep edge gradients points at the same seam from the other side. Candidates to discriminate: (a) the flux read at the clipped cells is the inconsistency and the remedy is a paired change where the boundary flux read and the current placement use the same clipped geometry, which is what the unified clip section builds; (b) the two residual cells (first-moment error 8.9e-3) are enough to destabilise the iteration, testable by zeroing their moment correction; (c) the iteration's damping was tuned to the chord's smoother map and needs re-tuning, testable by a relaxation sweep on weak -110 in one H200 job.

Candidate (a), by the discriminator: exact current placement converges (6.2e-13, once the two contaminated cells are excluded) to a self-consistent equilibrium that is not the Solov'ev one (axis 0.533 m outboard), while the chord clip never converges yet sits within 1.8 mm of the analytic axis. The flux read and the current placement are inconsistent with each other: the discrete Green's coupling and the boundary flux interpolant were balanced against chord moments. Two-cell contamination is a secondary effect (it prevents even the wrong fixed point); damping is unmeasured because the accelerated Krylov route never reads the relaxation. Consequence for the unified clip section: the clipped geometry must enter the flux read and the current placement together, as one operator, or exact moments alone move the answer away from the reference. The lead's standing hypothesis that the quadrature-built second-order compatibility terms conflict with steep edge gradients is the same seam seen from the coupling side and is the next thing to test.
