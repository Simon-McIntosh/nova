§1 — Objective

Give nova a polygon cross-section arc element. The element hierarchy today pairs a filament with a thick rectangular section at each geometry: Arc (nova/biot/arc.py) is the 3D circular arc filament and Bow (nova/biot/bow.py) is its rectangular-section thickening through the zeta quadrature; Line/Beam do the same for a straight segment. The polygon section exists only for the full turn — the axisymmetric ring landed under biot-operator-assembly. There is no polygon-section arc, so any 3D geometry built from conductors of non-rectangular cross-section has to approximate them.

This plan closes that gap with the general Urankar Part V case: a polygon cross-section swept through an arc of sweep below 2π. The full turn nova already has is precisely the specialisation this plan undoes — which is what makes the work substantial rather than a parameter change, and also what gives it the strongest possible regression test.

§2 — Why this is not a parameter change

Five things change when the sweep stops being a full turn, and each is a body of work:

  • The elliptic integrals stop being complete. The full turn is the paper's case C, in which every integral collapses to its value at α = π/2 — every K, E and Π complete, and cn K = 0 annihilating the radial vector potential as a structural check. A finite arc reinstates incomplete F(α,k), E(α,k) and π(α,η²,k).
  • Every moment recursion regains a boundary term — expected of all of them, and measured to be true of only one. The shipped reduction contracts against three families, and the root and pole families fold their own factor back under the integral, which is an identity of the INTEGRAND rather than of the limit: both are correct at an interior amplitude exactly as they stand, now pinned against partial-range quadrature. The plain harmonic moments are the one that moves, and there the change is structural: the source term costs them the minimality the complete family's downward recursion exploits, so they are solved as a system paired with an upward recursion instead. Landed below.
  • The amplitudes become per-target arrays. α_i = (π + φ − φ_i)/2 depends on the target's azimuth relative to each arc end, so what was a constant is now a field. That is what makes the evaluation batchable on the tiled path — and the α-case selection, which looked like a per-target branch that a trace could not take, turns out not to be one at all: the paper's three cases are one formula in the half-turn count. Landed below.
  • Bφ becomes a real component. Its vanishing for the full turn is a parity argument — eq 11b's azimuthal row is the radial bracket weighted by sin φ instead of cos φ, the bracket is even about φ = π, and the odd weight integrates to nothing. On an arc there is no such cancellation, so the row has to be implemented and tested rather than asserted away.
  • Conditioning gets harder, not easier. The landed work cured a loss that grew as the fourth power of the section's aspect ratio by taking each pole factor in the range variable that vanishes at its own end. An arc end can place a pole arbitrarily close to an interior limit, so the same treatment needs a third case that the full turn never presents.

§3 — Prerequisites and inherited corrections

This plan starts from the landed full-turn reduction and inherits both its machinery and its known limitations. Read before starting:

  • nova/biot/polygonanalytic.py — the full-turn reduction, its dual range bases, and the module docstring's account of the conditioning.
  • nova/biot/elliptic.py — complete sn and cn moment families and the shifted-pole recursions, all of which need interior-limit boundary terms here.
  • tests/test_biotpolygonanalytic.py — the acceptance-gate pattern (converged oracle, contour-distance bands, a candidates registry, aspect and per-section envelopes asserted in both directions) to be reproduced for the arc.
  • nova/biot/arc.py, nova/biot/bow.py, nova/biot/zeta.py — the element-class pattern to follow, and Bow itself as the zero-edge-slope oracle.
  • The coupling-schemes findings — the measured conditioning account and the verified paper erratum below.

Inherited erratum (verified, must be carried). Eq 11b's vertical row prints its rational term as −b₁²D/a₀²; it is −b₁D/a₀², linear in the edge slope. The two forms agree only at slope 0 and slope 1, so a rectangular section and a 45° edge both hide the error, and the printed form makes assembled BZ wrong by 286% on a trapezium. Everything derived from that row inherits the correction — the eq 19a boundary term, the field families, and here also the azimuthal row that the full turn never formed.

Inherited gate shortfall — resolved before this plan started. The full-turn flux was 3–20× outside its 1e-10 acceptance gate for reasons that were post-reduction round-off rather than the reduction itself. The parent plan's harmonic-numerator rung closed it (all four gate sections now hold 1e-10 on all three components) and its corner-organisation rung followed, so the arc is built on the repaired basis and inherits no ceiling. gate-first is locked harmonic-first on that record rather than on a schedule judgement.

What the full turn had to change about the paper, and therefore what the arc inherits. The shipped reduction is not a transcription of eqs 21–22: those are packaged in powers of a range variable, and a reduced numerator of degree six written that way reaches coefficients ten thousand times its own size, so contracting it against a same-signed moment family forms the answer out of terms that exceed it by as much. The shipped route carries every numerator in the HARMONIC basis instead, holds each range function as its two exact end values plus a bulk, and splits each denominator between its two factors rather than its two roots. The arc extends THAT machinery; the paper is the guide to the case bookkeeping, the field rows and the interior-limit recursions, not the packaging.

§4 — Deliverables

  • A finite-arc polygon-section evaluation of the vector potential and all three field components, with the α-case branch selection under test.
  • An acceptance gate built like the full turn's: a converged numerical oracle over the arc, per-edge and per-limit pins, an aspect-ratio sweep, and per-section envelopes asserted in both directions.
  • Element classes following the Arc/Bow pattern (Constants + Matrix dataclass, ClassVar name, axisymmetric = False) so the frame and groupset machinery can consume them rather than only a bare function.
  • Cost per pair measured on a compute node, fresh process, median of 3, beside the full-turn and Bow figures.

§5 — The four free oracles

This plan is unusually well supplied with independent checks, and all four are tests rather than afterthoughts. Each isolates a different failure mode:

  1. Sweep → 2π reproduces the landed full-turn evaluation, to the accuracy the full turn itself achieves. Catches errors in the α-case bookkeeping and the interior-limit boundary terms, which is where most of the new risk lives.
  2. Zero edge slope reproduces Bow within Bow's own tolerance — the arc analogue of the full turn's reduction to cylinder_greens. Catches errors in the degenerate branch the printed packaging cannot express.
  3. A converged direct quadrature over the arc, built the way the full turn's oracle was (raise the rule until it stops moving, and assert that it has).
  4. Horizontal edges contribute nothing, exactly, as in the full turn.

Bφ has no such free oracle — it is the one component with no rectangular counterpart to check against, because Bow carries it too but through a different formulation. It therefore needs the direct quadrature and an integral identity of its own; treat it as the highest-risk row.

§ Decisions

Land the parent plan's harmonic-numerator work before starting the arc, or build the arc on the present basis and inherit its 3–20× gate shortfall?

Settled by the record rather than by a schedule judgement: the parent plan's harmonic-numerator rung landed 2026-07-25 (develop 7e5b49b0) and its corner-organisation rung followed (56254283), both before any arc work started. All four gate sections now hold the 1e-10 far band on all three components -- hexagon 6.2e-12, rectangle 1.6e-12, trapezium 6.5e-12, thin plate 6.0e-11 -- so the 3-20x shortfall this decision was hedging against no longer exists and the arc inherits no ceiling. The arc-first option is therefore not a live alternative, and the arc is built on the repaired basis: harmonic numerators, range functions held as two exact end values plus a bulk, and denominators split between their two FACTORS rather than their two roots. Note the consequence for how the paper is used: the shipped route is a re-derivation and not a transcription of eqs 21-22, whose power-basis packaging is what the harmonic rung replaced, so the paper guides the case bookkeeping, the field rows and the interior-limit recursions -- not the packaging.

Where does the incomplete third-kind integral come from — a Carlson symmetric-form evaluation written for this purpose, or the existing complete routine extended with an amplitude argument?

Sharpened by the partial-range rung (2026-07-26, develop 7fece719 + 2959d45c + 0e33f622). The FIRST and SECOND kinds are done and took the extend-complete route -- one arithmetic-geometric mean with the amplitude carried alongside the modulus, fixed at fourteen trips, complement-native, reproducing the shipped complete routine at a quarter turn to 2.3e-16 across the whole double range. The third kind did not follow, and the evidence says NEITHER printed option is right as posed. (1) carlson-incomplete reproduces the cancellation the complete case rejected Carlson for. The seed is wanted in two orientations: a root just past the FAR end of the range, where R_F + (n/3) R_J is a sum of positives and holds; and a root just past the NEAR end, where the characteristic is hugely negative, the two terms are of opposite sign and nearly equal, and the arrangement loses about sqrt(p) -- five digits at a shift of 1e-10, which is what a slender section produces. The seed multiplies a numerator end value that is small by the same amount, so it needs full RELATIVE accuracy and the loss reaches the answer undiminished. (2) extend-complete does not carry over from the first two kinds: Bartky's rearrangement, which is what makes cel one fixed-trip descent over eighteen decades of pole, has no amplitude in it at all, so the incomplete counterpart (Bulirsch el3) is a different algorithm rather than the same one with an argument added -- and its published form is branch-heavy where this codebase needs a fixed trip count. A THIRD route came out of the analysis and should be costed first: the ill-conditioning is entirely in an ELEMENTARY factor. With the pole written as cos^2 a + p sin^2 a the whole growth is arctan(sqrt(p) tan alpha)/sqrt(p), exact, and the remainder carries a factor k^2/(p - 1) that is small exactly where the direct evaluation is worst -- so the remainder needs only eps sqrt(p) relative accuracy, which the Carlson arrangement already delivers. Paired with Carlson taken directly in the far-end orientation, where it is a sum of positives, the two orientations are each covered by the means that suits them. Add elementary-split-plus-carlson as a third option when this is locked.

§ Followups

Reinstate the alpha-case bookkeeping and the interior integration limit, then build the arc gate

First rung, and it is deliberately the risky half rather than the coefficient algebra: get the branch selection and the interior-limit boundary terms right, verified by the sweep-to-2pi oracle against the landed full-turn evaluation, before any new field row is attempted. Seed the pole families at an interior amplitude — the incomplete third kind, and the elementary split that conditions it

The one primitive the arc still lacks, and the only thing between the landed machinery and an assembled arc evaluation. The pole families' recursions are already verified at a partial limit; they are handed a seed by quadrature in the tests, and this rung replaces it. The decision incomplete-third-kind carries the measured reason neither of its two printed options works and the third route to cost first: the seed's whole ill-conditioning sits in an elementary arctangent that can be taken out exactly, leaving a remainder that needs only eps sqrt(p) relative accuracy — which is what the Carlson arrangement already delivers.
