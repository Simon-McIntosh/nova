One MAST arm settles in 12 active-set trips

The production-resolution MAST 22086/43 pure-diverted arm executed 12 of the 16 allowed outer trips. Its mask difference first reached zero at trip 2, changed again at trips 3 and 4, then stayed zero from trip 5 through the terminal trip 11. The reported live residual on trip 11 was 6.079771712859503×10−12.

Cost implication. Straight multiplication by the recorded pre-active-set DIII-D single-frame solve time gives 5 frames × 12 trips/frame × 1455.959 s/trip = 87,357.540 s, or 24.265983 h (24 h 15 min 57.540 s). This is the requested direct scaling, not a new DIII-D timing measurement. The MAST run shows that treating every trip as cold is conservative: its later compiled and warm-started trips were much cheaper than its first two.

Settling and timing interpretation

  • First zero mask difference: trip 2. This was transient, because trips 3 and 4 changed 1 and 22 cells respectively.
  • Stable zero mask difference: trip 5 onward. The solve completed on trip 11, consuming 12 trips against the limit of 16.
  • Mean and maximum: 3.738124 s mean over all trips; 18.303108 s maximum at trip 1.
  • Warm-cost observation: trips 0–1 averaged 17.013821 s, while trips 2–11 averaged 1.082984 s. The later group was 6.37% of the early mean, or 15.7 times cheaper. This run combines warm starting with one-time compilation, so it establishes the observed cost drop but does not separate those two causes.

The mask sequence does not oscillate indefinitely and does not drift: after the transient 0, 1, 22 excursion, it becomes the fixed value zero. The residual values above are reported exactly as printed by the run; this record makes no additional claim about solution quality.

Five-frame DIII-D arithmetic

Using the measured 12 trips-to-settle as the multiplier and the recorded pre-active-set DIII-D time as one full-solve unit: 

single frame: 12 trips × 1455.959 s = 17,471.508 s = 4.853197 h
five frames:  5 × 17,471.508 s = 87,357.540 s = 24.265983 h

This checkable projection is the implied cost if every DIII-D trip costs the full recorded 1455.959 s. The MAST callback timings show that later trips can be much cheaper once compiled and warm-started, so 24.265983 h should not be presented as a measured five-frame runtime. A defensible warm-adjusted DIII-D estimate requires per-trip DIII-D timestamps; this one-arm MAST measurement does not provide them.
