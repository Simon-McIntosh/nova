Calibration foundations — what the session built, and what it measured

Three strands landed on 2026-08-07, and they are one thing: the MAST misfit programme's findings stop being prose and in-code constants, and become a validated document, a tested package, and a differentiable forward model. Each strand is recorded against its own plan — diagnostic-correction-schema (shipped), nova-calibrate-package (first slice, 55%), and coil-geometry-inversion §2.1 — and this page is the single place where all three are visible at once.

Strand 1 — corrections become a document

Commits 798d9438 + c479abeb (schema and instance, merged e3c551cb); 84719e86, c6bd321a, 4c1e32f4, aaee7705 (read-path cutover, merged 4f37803a).

The design driver is a measurement, not a preference: the MAST store carries 19 channels whose acquisition scale steps between shot blocks, obr05 flips gain state shot-to-shot, and obv03 steps mid-life. A correction is a function of pulse range. An in-code dictionary cannot express that, cannot version it, and cannot validate it. The schema is authored as LinkML at nova/calibrate/schema/diagnostic_correction.yaml; the Pydantic models (nova/calibrate/correction_model.py) and the JSON-Schema export are generated and committed, so reading a document needs only pydantic and pyyaml — the LinkML toolchain sits in an optional schema extra and is pinned by a byte-for-byte regeneration test that fires whenever the extra is installed.

Eight correction kinds (gain, acquisition_scale, pair_state, offset, drift_rate, exclusion, quality, convention) and five statuses (promoted, recorded, withheld, refused, superseded). The application order is not documentation — it is an enum with ranks the engine sorts on: offset (1) → drift (2) → acquisition scale (3) → pair state (4) → gain (5), with convention (6) applied at the unit boundary and never inside the per-channel chain. Validation rejects two faults a well-shaped document can still carry: intervals overlapping within one channel-kind-status, and a quantised value landing off its declared ladder.

The MAST instance

nova/calibrate/corrections/mast/magnetics.yaml, 2,588 lines, 128 records over 78 channels. Counted from the file itself: 113 acquisition blocks over 76 channels (108 promoted, 5 refused), 5 promoted gains with their unrounded pooled ratios beside the four-decimal constants the read path uses, 1 withheld gain (obv11) carrying the gate that refused it, 3 pair states, 2 exclusions, 3 quality states, and 1 measured flux-loop convention. The declared ladder is {0.25, 1/2, 1/√2, 1, √2, 2, 4} at tolerance 0.08 — declared as a hypothesis before any step was classified, and confirmed by the histogram: of 37 steps found, 29 land on a rung.

obr05 is the fourth shape worth naming: it flips between 1.25 and 0.50 pulse to pulse, so it is stored as state: indeterminate with candidate_values: [1.25, 0.5] and no value. A mean of the two describes no pulse; a consumer needs the size of what is unresolved, not an average of it. The application engine refuses to serve a channel in that state rather than guessing.

The cutover, and the order it happened in

Equivalence came before deletion, not after. The document-served read path was compared against the in-code constants by 3,395 float-equal (==) reads over all 76 channels at every block endpoint, interior, boundary and gap: zero mismatches, including the five refused blocks reading byte-identically to prior behaviour. Only then were PROMOTED_CHANNEL_SCALES and three sibling constants deleted (served now by nova/imas/mast_corrections.py) and the 4,394-line JSON block table removed, frozen as tests/calibrate/data/banked_block_scales.json so the comparison stays runnable. Engine read cost is 1.2 ms/shot for 76 channels against 0.1 ms for the raw table — a 12× multiple on a number small enough that no caching was added.

Two behavioural edges held, not decided

This is the most important honesty in the record, and it is easy to misread the cutover without it. The five promoted gains were never applied on the read path — not before this work and not after it. They existed as constants that fed an evidence string; they now exist as document records with far better provenance, and they are still not divided out. The reader is deliberately scoped to acquisition scales, and that scope is pinned by test_the_read_path_divides_by_no_promoted_gain in tests/calibrate/test_read_path_equivalence.py. Promoting them into the arithmetic rescales every downstream fit and is an owner decision with its own evidence, queued as an open followup on the schema plan. Likewise the flux-loop 2π stays a convention digit applied at the unit boundary rather than entering the per-channel chain, so it cannot be applied twice. A cutover that had quietly changed behaviour while claiming equivalence would have been worse than no cutover.

Strand 3 — the forward model becomes differentiable

Commits bf8f9bcb + dcc3a62a, merged 42dd1816; regression suite at tests/test_biotgeometryautograd.py. The biot operators were written JAX-compliant for accelerator throughput. That property now pays a second time: it yields exact derivatives with respect to source geometry. Only the polygon quadrature path was verified before this work; the audit brought the whole family, and each class carries a three-part regression — value against the host numpy kernel, jacfwd against central finite differences, and jacrev against jacfwd.

Two elliptic derivative repairs — and why one of them was nearly missed

The third kind's forward mode returned NaN on the unreflected side; the fix holds the root argument at one where no reflection is wanted.

The second is the instructive one. Carlson's degenerate form lost the eps/pole ratio in its slope on the direct side: both autodiff modes were wrong by ~3e-9 while the value kept every digit. The jacfwd–jacrev cross-check agreed to 4e-10 — and that agreement was mutual error, because both modes differentiate the same defective expression. Two exact modes agreeing is not evidence of correctness when they share an implementation. The reference had to be differentiated under the integral sign rather than differenced. The repair is an even series in v² below ratio 0.1: slope error 3e-9 → 3e-15 uniformly over poles from 1e-12 to 1e12, value unchanged at 8e-16, cost +5.4% and only on the arc path. One limitation is recorded rather than hidden: where the pole equals the complement exactly, the slope is finite but 1.2e-2 relative — pre-existing, and documented by its own test.

Cost of the lane: the fast autograd lane is 30 s over 8 tests. Three closed-form section traces carry the slow marker and are deselected by default; the finite-arc trace is op-count-bound at ~284 s, profiled rather than assumed — the reverse pass alone is 63 s with a single cotangent, so grid size is not the lever.

What this unlocks. Every nova coupling operator is now differentiable end-to-end with respect to source geometry. That is the substrate the geometry inversion needs (coil-geometry-inversion §2.2 onward: parameter blocks, then the identifiability spectrum at the real MAST sensor set), and it is the route into the Sophelio Fusion Equilibrium Challenge's flux-map problem class — recovering a machine's coil positions from published flux maps and currents alone, developed against MAST where our registry geometry is the known truth.
