Which input the recorded refusal measured

The predecessor probe logged valid=false, qualified=false, and a NaN axis at 607 and 2,097 realised cells with (6.2, 0.0) as its only root. Its script paired fixture.analytic_case(), a rotating carrier, with the _analytic_inputs("limited") static field and loaded the older resolution-named Zarr cache. That pair is not the published static certificate. Reconstructing the rotating carrier geometry without its expensive coupling blocks reproduces the realised counts exactly. Its wall spans R = 0.97794 to 2.15 m, while the static field's declared axis is at R = 6.2 m. No cell contains that point at either rung: the closest cell centre is 4.05697 m away at 607 cells and 4.05071 m away at 2,097 cells. The gate that refuses is inside = any(_inside_cells(geometry, root.position)) inside kernel.read; extrema then has no eligible root. The Hessian type is correct and the no-saddle persistence branch passes, but vessel membership is false. The mixed wall's largest sampled static-field ψ is −63.66830290168743 Wb, below the axis's +49.94072179477446 Wb by 113.60902469646189 Wb, so flux polarity is not the refusing clause.

The content-addressed store under certificate-fixtures instead resolves the static weak-rotation-reactor-static case at requested 550, 2,000, and 5,000 cells to 553, 2,105, and 5,167 realised cells. The earlier refusal is a real observation on the mixed pair, but does not establish a refusal of the certified analytic carrier.

The field's own orientation and the gate

For the limited static field, the definition is ψ(R,Z) = 2π[A − ½P(R² − R₀²)² − VZ²]. Its coefficients P = 0.031620221879352824 and V = 0.6132957357419372 are positive, so (R₀,Z) = (6.2,0) is a strict maximum, with Hessian diagonal entries −8πPR₀² and −4πV. COCOS 17 with positive current gives σ = +1 here. The eigenvalues of σ times the Hessian are −30.54837771115935 and −7.706901511539268. ψ at the axis is 49.94072179477446 Wb; the largest sampled wall ψ is −0.04915504379573112 Wb. Thus the signed axis level is above the wall, as the read requires.

The axis lies inside a carrier cell on each published rung (553, 2,105, and 5,167 cells). The one-root census has no saddle, so the near-fold persistence condition passes by its explicit no-saddle branch; there is no finite axis-to-saddle persistence to estimate. The signed Hessian is negative definite, the root is valid and finite, and the ranking gate's extrema and eligible masks admit it. The read reports valid=true, qualified=true, and axis (6.2,0.0) on all three rungs. Reversing the current sign makes σ = −1 and signed eigenvalues +7.706901511539268 and +30.54837771115935; that negative control returns valid=false and a NaN axis. The polarity gate therefore fires on the known wrong orientation and accepts the field's actual orientation.

The read from axis ranking's base revision da4c4eb98 was also run on those same published 553- and 2,105-cell inputs. It returned valid=true, qualified=true, and (6.2,0.0) at both. The gate added by ranking therefore did not change the certified carrier's acceptance; the earlier NaN came from the mixed fixture pair.
