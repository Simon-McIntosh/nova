What the prototype census can retain

The end-to-end compiled prototype census nevertheless returns only one saddle on the right. The warm caller passes a two-position prior (axis and primary X-point), so the wall-contact saddle relies on the cell proposal every time. Passing the last valid wall-contact position as a third prior at the right offset restores two saddles and three valid roots in the same scripts.prototypes.read_robustness.census.census function. The direct carry test is a positive control: the census can represent and classify the root when its identity is maintained. It does not by itself identify which fused proposal, polishing, containment, or deduplication expression changes in the original two-prior program.
