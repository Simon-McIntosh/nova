The dropping line, with its inputs on both sides

The rejecting step is the cell-membership test in scripts/prototypes/read_robustness/census.py. Each cell proposes its quadratic seed, and a seed is admitted only when its cross product against every cell edge is non-negative: inside = all(where(slot < vertex_count, cross >= 0, True)). The threshold is exact zero, and the quantity it tests is a difference of two nearly equal products, so on a seed that lands on an edge its computed sign is set by rounding rather than by the field.

Cell 263 is the only proposal of the wall-contact saddle, so when it leaves the candidate set the saddle leaves the census while still hyperbolic. In the traced census at 282 cells, across the adjacent pair:

The repair, and the counts it restores

The admission threshold now carries the rounding of the value it compares, from two computed sources and with no tuned constant. The first is the forward error of the two-term determinant, 4 · eps · (|edge|1) · (|relative|1). The second is the seed's own uncertainty propagated from the quadratic solve, eps · |pitch| · ||H−1||1 · (||H||1 · |offset|1 + |gradient|1), which is unbounded in the conditioning of that 2×2 matrix and dominates at the saddle. A seed is admitted when its cross product is negative only within the sum of the two, the second term scaled by the edge length as the cross product scales the displacement. Both terms are computed from the operands at the state being classified, so the decision is continuous in the field and no switch was introduced.

The determinant term alone was measured first and is not sufficient: at 2.86e-18 it does not cover a 5.77e-17 departure, and the pair still read 3 → 2. It is retained as one addend rather than replaced, because it is a real part of the error and dropping it would make the sum a fit rather than a bound.

Neither departure is a fold

A saddle-node pair merges when the determinant and the smaller eigenvalue both reach the rounding of the Hessian. At 282 the last state holding the wall-contact saddle is 0.1732, where its position is (1.543228, 0.882895) and its determinant is −2.9608e-05, with eigenvalues −4.4544e-02 and +6.6469e-04. The determinant's own forward error at these entries is of order 1e-20, so |det H| is fifteen orders above roundoff. Its nearest neighbour in that state is 6.06e-02 away and 5.68e-02 in the state after, so nothing merged with it. At 550 the same measurement gives −5.8060e-05 for the saddle, nearest neighbour 1.2473e-01 before and 2.0958e+00 after. Neither root folds; both leave the census while hyperbolic.

The stage that refuses them is the proposal, not any comparison. Printing the census masks in order shows candidate cells [3, 247, 263] at 0.1732 and [3, 247] at 0.17325: cell 263 stops proposing a seed at all, and it is the only cell that ever proposed the wall-contact saddle. No other cell proposes one near it either, so the root is not displaced to a neighbouring proposal — it is simply unseen. At 0.17335 cell 249 proposes a seed again, but at a different point, (1.550246, 0.869961) against the vanished root's (1.543228, 0.882895).

The reason is a coverage hole in the proposal, not a threshold. A cell proposes its own quadratic stationary point only when that point falls inside the cell's own polygon; at these states only 3 of the 282 cells satisfy that test at all, and the cell that contains the saddle is not among them. Admitting a seed anywhere in the domain instead would raise the candidate count to 153 against a seed capacity of 64, where selection is by index and would silently truncate — a worse defect than the one it fixes. Repairing this needs the proposal itself reworked, with the capacity and the selection order settled together, and that is a design change this node did not make.

The repair as a test, and the coverage hole handed on

Done-when (5) is met by two tests in tests/equilibrium/test_topology_read.py, both marked slow. The first builds the 282-cell carrier, warms its census prior to 0.16, and bisects the adjacent binary64 pair on the census whose cell-membership step is the strict exact-zero comparison; across the pair it asserts three roots and two saddles on each side, then confirms the retained saddle against values computed independently of the census — a seed displaced a quarter pitch from the census reading is polished with topology.stationary_read and must return to the same point, hyperbolic, carrying the same flux value to 1e-9 absolute — and finally requires that point to persist from the other side of the pair. The second test asserts that the reinstated strict exact-zero admission loses a saddle at that same pair while the repaired census keeps both, so the declared control is a test rather than a script's exit code.

The rounding enclosure is now used rather than carried: kernel.psi_rounding_enclosure is what the saddle-admission probe calls to bound the fit's rounding, so the probe and the invariant it declares read the same quantity from one place.

What this round leaves open

Done-when (3) is therefore only half met and is reported as unmet rather than argued closed: the admission departure at the bisected pair is removed and the root now persists through 0.16 to 0.173 at 282, but the 0.1735 and 0.1225 departures remain, and the measurement above shows they are neither folds nor consequences of the repaired step. They are a second and distinct defect in the same census: a stationary point that no cell can propose because the cell's quadratic model places its own stationary point outside its own polygon, which is a floor on the method's resolution at a wall-contact saddle and not an error in a comparison.

The declared negative control is satisfied: the reinstated dropping step loses the hyperbolic root again at the 282-B adjacent pair, and the control exits nonzero recording exactly that. The read-level test at the tie states that done-when (5) also asks for was still not written in this round.
