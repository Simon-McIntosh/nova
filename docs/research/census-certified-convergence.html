The dropping line, with its inputs on both sides

The rejecting step is the cell-membership test in scripts/prototypes/read_robustness/census.py. Each cell proposes its quadratic seed, and a seed is admitted only when its cross product against every cell edge is non-negative: inside = all(where(slot < vertex_count, cross >= 0, True)). The threshold is exact zero, and the quantity it tests is a difference of two nearly equal products, so on a seed that lands on an edge its computed sign is set by rounding rather than by the field.

Cell 263 is the only proposal of the wall-contact saddle, so when it leaves the candidate set the saddle leaves the census while still hyperbolic. In the traced census at 282 cells, across the adjacent pair:

Three controls fix the cause. The same computation executed eagerly, and the same census compiled with the field passed as an argument, both keep three roots on both sides. Only the field built inside the compiled sampler of fast_map_production/tie_probe.py departs, which is where the varied jets are constructed within the trace and fused multiply-add contraction leaves the last bits different from the eager construction. That is why the predecessor's isolated stationary_read, containment and deduplication checks all passed while the end-to-end census still lost the root: each of those stages was measured outside the trace whose arithmetic sets the sign.

The repair, and the counts it restores

The admission threshold now carries the rounding of the value it compares, from two computed sources and with no tuned constant. The first is the forward error of the two-term determinant, 4 · eps · (|edge|1) · (|relative|1). The second is the seed's own uncertainty propagated from the quadratic solve, eps · |pitch| · ||H−1||1 · (||H||1 · |offset|1 + |gradient|1), which is unbounded in the conditioning of that 2×2 matrix and dominates at the saddle. A seed is admitted when its cross product is negative only within the sum of the two, the second term scaled by the edge length as the cross product scales the displacement. Both terms are computed from the operands at the state being classified, so the decision is continuous in the field and no switch was introduced.

The determinant term alone was measured first and is not sufficient: at 2.86e-18 it does not cover a 5.77e-17 departure, and the pair still read 3 → 2. It is retained as one addend rather than replaced, because it is a real part of the error and dropping it would make the sum a fit rather than a bound.
