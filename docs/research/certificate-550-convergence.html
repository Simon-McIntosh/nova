The direct root control in Titan job 1289639 identifies the derivative gap. At the exact seed the solved axis is (6.2, 0) m and its JVP along the rejected correction is exactly (0, 0). At h = 1/4096, a fresh finite solve places it at approximately (6.1999999816, 2.36027718e-5) m: a displacement of (−1.83721891e-8, 2.36027718e-5) m and a finite vertical derivative of 0.0966769534 m per unit step. Inspection of fixed_points._adaptive explains the mismatch: its while loop takes no iterations when the seed is already converged, so differentiation through that branch gives the constant seed's zero tangent; a perturbed field requires iterations and moves the root.

The owner repair now applies the implicit-function equation to every converged root: Hψ · droot = −d(∇ψ), with the gradient parameter derivative evaluated at the fixed converged position. The returned jet is then evaluated at that position, so its tangent includes the implicit root displacement. No map, acceptance, pin, or limit compensation was added. The committed exact-seed test constructs a cached quadratic field, resolves its root, asserts zero adaptive iterations, and differentiates a linear vertical perturbation. The base route reports JVP (0, 0) against central difference (approximately 0, 0.1) and fails; the changed route reports vertical JVP 0.1 and passes at the same 1/4096 step.

The full carrier falsifies the inference that this root tangent alone causes its Newton stall. The changed route's Taylor remainder is 1.32062218497e-8 at h = 1e-6 and 1.32059496526e-9 at h = 1e-7, a slope of approximately 1.000009, and its centered derivative error tends to 0.00660. Its first 550-cell solve still rejects the step at residual 0.0012319931459852727. Its 12-iteration GMRES correction and relative linear residual are identical to the base values; the correction array differs by zero. Thus the implicit root tangent is repaired, while the map still has an independent first-order directional mismatch. The head root probe and tangent-removal solve are running to isolate this distinction further.

Vertical-position control

Job 1289627 augmented this same row with the existing uniform radial-field basis and a current-centroid row, targeted at the analytic booked height 9.276322181002429e-17 m with a 1e-3 T source scale. GMRES again solved its linear system, to relative residual 7.666895564035161e-11, but the correction was rejected at every measured scale. Its merit values and first-order Taylor remainders match the free run to the shown precision; the terminal field amplitude is 0 T and the residual remains 0.0012319931459852727. A zero-height centroid pin therefore does not lift this carrier's stall, unlike the earlier diverted lobe carrier. The exploratory augmentation was removed from the product route after this measurement.
