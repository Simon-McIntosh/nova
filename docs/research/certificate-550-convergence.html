The direct root control in Titan job 1289639 identifies the derivative gap. At the exact seed the solved axis is (6.2, 0) m and its JVP along the rejected correction is exactly (0, 0). At h = 1/4096, a fresh finite solve places it at approximately (6.1999999816, 2.36027718e-5) m: a displacement of (−1.83721891e-8, 2.36027718e-5) m and a finite vertical derivative of 0.0966769534 m per unit step. Inspection of fixed_points._adaptive explains the mismatch: its while loop takes no iterations when the seed is already converged, so differentiation through that branch gives the constant seed's zero tangent; a perturbed field requires iterations and moves the root.

The owner repair now applies the implicit-function equation to every converged root: Hψ · droot = −d(∇ψ), with the gradient parameter derivative evaluated at the fixed converged position. The returned jet is then evaluated at that position, so its tangent includes the implicit root displacement. No map, acceptance, pin, or limit compensation was added. The committed exact-seed test constructs a cached quadratic field, resolves its root, asserts zero adaptive iterations, and differentiates a linear vertical perturbation. The base route reports JVP (0, 0) against central difference (approximately 0, 0.1) and fails; the changed route reports vertical JVP 0.1 and passes at the same 1/4096 step.

The full carrier falsifies the inference that this root tangent alone causes its Newton stall. The changed route's Taylor remainder is 1.32062218497e-8 at h = 1e-6 and 1.32059496526e-9 at h = 1e-7, a slope of approximately 1.000009, and its centered derivative error tends to 0.00660. Its first 550-cell solve still rejects the step at residual 0.0012319931459852727. Its 12-iteration GMRES correction and relative linear residual are identical to the base values; the correction array differs by zero. The head carrier root probe (job 1289763) measured the corrected vertical JVP as 0.0968274219 m per step against a 1/4096 finite derivative of 0.0966769534 m per step; its radial components were −7.20962e-5 and −7.52525e-5. Removing the tangent at head (job 1289760) restores the same 0.0012319931459852727 stall. Thus the root derivative is repaired, while the map still has an independent first-order directional mismatch.

Remaining full-map mismatch

The implicit stationary-root tangent is the delivered fix. It does not make the 550-cell map smooth or its Newton solve converge: the nearest after residual is 0.0012319931459852727, converged=false, with Taylor slope approximately 1.000009 rather than 2. The after Newton correction is identical to base. The remaining first-order mismatch is localized to cut-cell current residuals around paired cells 61 and 508, with unchanged support and a smoothly moving wall contact. scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production/kernel.py owns the read and membership handoff; its imported Bell polygon and split-polygon implementations in scripts/prototypes/sub_millisecond/measure_bell_cut.py and scripts/prototypes/fast_read/ordinary_cut.py are candidates, not established causes. The completed root-probe and tangent-removal receipts are inputs for that next investigation. The acceptance test and 1e-12 limit remain unchanged.
