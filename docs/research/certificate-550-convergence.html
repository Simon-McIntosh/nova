The mechanism is the width of the level ramp. _continuous_path_weight in scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production/kernel.py builds flux_width = jnp.asarray(1e-8) and then flux_weight = jnp.clip(0.5 + (passage - level) / flux_width, 0, 1). A ramp 1e-8 wide is below any step a central difference or a Newton correction can take and below the working scale of the carrier, so at the scale the solve operates on the clip saturates on both sides and the weight is a step, 0 below level and 1 above it. Downstream the value is carried by a maximum-propagated connectivity, so the forward-mode derivative is exactly zero while the value jumps by one. Neither reading is a usable Newton derivative: autodiff reports the locally flat branch and the finite difference reports the jump. The map's own columns show it reaching the solve — cell 50's three blocks are wrong by four to five orders of magnitude, and where the jump happens to cancel against the smooth part of geometric_membership the column agrees (cell 40 block 0), which is exactly why a probe along the residual direction alone did not expose it.

The diverted 550-cell row does not show the same signature. At its stalled iterate (scaled residual 0.00155033572741) every probed column of cells 8, 80, 227, 242 and 522 — three blocks each, at h = 1e-5 and 1e-6 — agrees to at most 1.96e-5 relative (cell 8 block 2, h = 1e-5, 0.15873097 against 0.15873408), every other column agreeing to 3.2e-7 or better, and the worst read stage is 6.90e-5 relative, root_position at cell 522 block 2, and no derivative is non-finite; path_weight at cell 8 block 0 reads base 1.0 with derivative 0.0 against a central difference of 0.0, so the connectivity does not flip in any probed basis direction. Two conclusions follow. First, the rejected tenth correction on the diverted 550 row is not caused by a map whose derivative disagrees with its values, which refutes for that row the hypothesis the 2,000-cell row confirms. Second, the remaining live candidate there is the same value discontinuity, which a basis-column probe at a non-flipping iterate cannot see: the next instrument must probe along a direction that flips the connectivity mask rather than along basis columns, and no fix was landed against this row. The nearest figure reached remains 0.00155033572741 against the unchanged 1e-12 limit.

A width from the cell's own excursion, and what it does and does not repair

The constant ramp was replaced by a width the map carries. _flux_past_level_weight(peak, trough, level) weights a cell by the share of its own flux excursion past level — one where the whole excursion is past, zero where none of it is, the linear share between — with peak - trough taken from the same edge quadratic the read already evaluates, so the ramp is as wide as the cell's own resolution of the level and narrows as the mesh refines. A degenerate span falls back to the sign of the excursion. _continuous_path_weight now takes peak and trough from the endpoints and turning value of that quadratic and reduces the two cells of an edge by the minimum of their shares, so an edge connects only where its colder end has passed the level; both boundary candidates are built from the same weight, so the value is never branched on topology and the limited-to-diverted transition stays continuous, moving smoothly through the interval at any cell whose excursion straddles the level. Committed at d03faa230.

The rerun of the basis-direction column probe at the two stalled iterates separates the two rows. At the 550-cell iterate every stage now agrees: five probed cells (82, 110, 228, 523, 524), three blocks, two step sizes, worst stage disagreement 8.27e-5 relative (root_position, cell 524 block 1, h = 1e-6), with path_weight and membership agreeing to roundoff everywhere. No basis direction at that iterate flips the connectivity, and the row's stall does not share the step-function cause. At the 2,000-cell iterate the disagreement survives the fix. Cell 47 block 0 reads path_weight forward mode 4.379 against a central difference of 5615.57 at h = 1e-5 and 3.446 against 56135.5 at h = 1e-6, with membership at 0.168 against 1559.45. The central difference grows tenfold as the step shrinks tenfold, so the value jumps by about 0.056 across any step: the 1e-8 ramp is gone and the discontinuity is in the connectivity mask itself, at a cell whose excursion exceeds the level at one end and not at the other. No width drawn from that excursion can remove it, because the excursion is the width — the jump is in admissible-set membership, not in the gate's resolution.
