Forward-solve compile-cost evidence

Compilation is currently a first-order forward-solve cost. This record gathers the measured compile and lowering walls that are otherwise scattered across the millisecond solve, cut-cell, batched-operator and playable records. The table keeps the instrument's distinctions: a lower-only probe is not an executable compile, an unrecorded byte count is not inferred from HLO, and a cache refusal is not reported as a cache hit.

§1 — The measured ledger

The compile wall is the recorded cold or warm compilation interval when the receipt exposes one. “Lower-only” names a StableHLO probe that did not produce an executable. “Not recorded” is retained where the source receipt did not expose a value; “not serialisable” is the measured outcome when XLA or protobuf refused to materialise the executable. For the memory-ladder rows, the instruction count is StableHLO and the executable-size field is the serialized executable or proto described by the source comment. For the census rows, the count is optimized-HLO instructions and the byte field is the captured executable context reported by the census.

The table contains more than the minimum twelve rows because a cache hit is not a cold compile, a constrained lower is not an executable, and a serialization refusal is itself an engineering result. §2 — Mechanisms already measured

Captured mesh literals. The closure-owned whole-cell program carries mesh-sized arrays as literals. At 300 cells, the constant census finds 856 literals above 1 KiB and 372.55 MiB of captured bytes, with 359.06 MiB in interaction matrices; at 1,000 cells it finds 889 literals and 2.94 GiB, with 2.89 GiB in interaction matrices. The host memory ladder sees the corresponding executable context grow from 462 MB at 300 cells to a 3.74 GiB proto at 1,000 and 19.2 GiB at 2,500. The evidence rows are the whole-cell closure rows and the two census rows above; the detailed literal inventory is docs/figures/millisecond-converged-solve/program-census/report.md.

Repeated traced copies. A known-present source sentinel, rather than an empty search, finds 120 traced copies of ForwardFluxOperator.normalised_current_moments and 36 copies of ForwardFluxOperator._fixed_design_read in both solve rungs. The older 83-copy premise does not reproduce at this revision. The Python Newton and trip loops are host-only and contribute zero optimized-HLO copies; the compiled budgets are already jax.lax.fori_loop bodies. This is the measured reason the follow-on targets one evaluator state machine and the two repeated bodies rather than rewriting host loops that are not in the executable. Evidence: docs/figures/millisecond-converged-solve/program-census/report.md and docs/plans/millisecond-converged-solve.html#c-run-r-20260915T124717561490-msc-program-constant-census.

Per-host cache identity. The persistent cache is explicitly selected per recipe, and its key includes the serialized computation, JAX/jaxlib and backend versions, compiler flags and options, and accelerator topology. The runtime identity also records the device topology, which is why a compiled program from one host or accelerator environment cannot be assumed to serve every all_debug node. The 300-cell size receipt names the CPU runtime and its cache directory; the compiled-slice receipt names the H200 runtime and records 12 of 12 same-program reuse with zero backend compile after the retained program. Evidence: /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/reports/nova/s19-codex/mesh-arguments/receipts/size-300.json and docs/figures/millisecond-converged-solve/compiled-slice-cache/receipt.json.

Constraint-row lowering multiplier. The centroid probe quantifies the extra traced work from one centroid constraint: the unconstrained lower is 423,061 StableHLO instructions and 45.246 seconds, while the constrained lower is 1,036,327 instructions and 147.213 seconds. That is 2.45 times the instruction count and 3.25 times the lower wall before an executable or solve is produced. The receipt preserves both arms as lower-only, so no executable-size claim is attached to this comparison. Evidence: /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/reports/nova/s19-codex/centroid-row/compile-unconstrained.json and compile-constrained.json.

Two-gibibyte serialisation limit. The 1,000-cell whole-cell proto (3.74 GiB), the 2,500-cell proto (19.2 GiB), and the exact-clip 1,000-cell serialized form (4.34 GB) cross the 2 GiB serialization boundary. They therefore cannot contribute a normal persistent-cache hit even when the cache is configured. The exact-clip receipt is explicit that its in-process cold compile and warm executions remain valid while no cross-process cache-hit claim is made. Evidence: the memory-ladder comment above and docs/evidence/archive/cut-cell-current-attribution-landed.html#exact-clip-warm-cost-and-workspace.

§3 — What the cost means for the millisecond solve

The measured walls are not one homogeneous benchmark. The 45.246- and 147.213-second centroid figures stop at lowering; the 74-second 300-cell cache retrieval is a warm backend retrieval; the 77.2-second MAST width-twelve row pays one warm batch compile; and the 1,029–1,074-second exact-clip rows are in-process cold compiles with warm fixed-point solves, not cache hits. The 1,579.189-second DIII-D head row is a real cold width-five compile, while its paired archived base is 169.030 seconds from a cache hit. Keeping these states side by side is the useful result: cache policy changes the wall, but it does not erase the graph and serialization mechanisms that set the cold cost.

The lead's directive on 2026-09-16 is therefore explicit: an hour per compile is too much and must be addressed as the codebase stabilises. The present record already shows the repair direction: pass mesh-sized arrays and profile values as traced arguments, eliminate duplicated current-moment and topology bodies through one evaluator state machine, and preserve a cache key whose host and accelerator identity are stated rather than assumed. The codebase should be judged against the measured rows above, including the negative rows, until the same receipts show a material reduction in cold wall, executable size and host working set.
