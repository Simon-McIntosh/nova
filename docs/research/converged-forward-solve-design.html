Converged forward solve: four pure functions

The forward solve has one meaning of plasma membership and one nonlinear function. topology.read determines the axis-connected sub-cell region; current.book integrates that region; gs.apply forms its magnetic image; solve.fixed_point differentiates and scores that same image. A small residual cannot substitute for correct topology, a faithful map, satisfied constraint rows or a bounded compiled program.

Authority, scope and open choices

This research document specifies the implementation boundary for the live design section. Nova owns the equilibrium topology, current booking, GS map and solver; transport, inverse control and labelling consume the resulting receipt. The existing ForwardSolveRequest → ForwardProfile.solve → ForwardSolveReceipt seam remains the host-facing adapter. It performs validation, cache selection and serialization; it cannot choose a second numerical route.

The source inventory is bound to 6598321adb5312ea9420c143d7f9de9cf59f8f00. Acceptance follows the live plan version 2 read on 2026-10-04 at 12:51 UTC, including its 64 GB cold-compile ceiling, bit-identity requirement and three-pitch topology control. The numerical observations below are dated, linked receipts at their stated revisions, not fresh numerical reruns at this source revision. This node changes documentation only. Review and implementation acceptance remain separate work.

The plan leaves field representation open. The global tensor spline and six-point ring quadratic remain alternatives until the coordinator records the choice on the plan. Large-receipt storage is also open in the plan; this node follows its explicit dispatch limit: data above 300,000 bytes stays in the run directory. Neither choice is silently converted into plan state here.

Topology: one field and one axis-connected component

Let u = σψ, with the magnetic axis a maximum under the convention contract. Build the contour-tree authority on the wall-clipped hex carrier, with deterministic tie ordering and explicit outside attachments. Critical events, within-cell stationary points and edge intersections must describe the same continuous field used to clip support. A join tree of centre samples alone cannot certify the topology of a higher-order polynomial between them.

  1. Recover coefficients and enumerate within-cell stationary points and shared-edge critical/intersection events using fixed-capacity buffers. Publish required counts before compaction. Refuse singular/under-resolved representations, unresolved branch ties or any capacity excess; do not truncate candidates into a plausible read.
  2. Choose the confined axis branch from the tree's component/containment evidence, not the global flux maximum or proximity to a requested analytic answer. An optional declared seed identifies a branch; it is not a returned null when no branch qualifies. Exclude the virtual outside node from physical critical-point output.
  3. Descend that branch in signed flux to the first limiting event: an admitted saddle connecting its branch to an open/private branch, or a reachable wall contact of the same component. Emit every admitted X-point, not only the boundary owner. Handle equal events deterministically and expose ambiguity when class cannot be qualified.
  4. Represent the open interior immediately on the confined side of the limiting level as connected sub-cell fragments. Join fragments across actual shared-edge intervals of nonzero extent; touching only at a saddle does not join interiors. Close the selected axis component only after its identity is known. Intersect it with the occupiable wall units, preserving each unit's own closure and material exclusion.
  5. Polish the admitted stationary coordinates in that field and recheck their ownership/containment. Return all derived scalars, labels and validity from this evaluation. Downstream consumers may inspect qualification; they cannot recategorize the boundary or select different nulls.

Field-representation requirement. Independent per-cell fits are not automatically continuous across a shared edge. The proposed polynomial representation must supply a single-valued edge trace and matching intersection ownership, or a measured, explicit reconciliation that does not change the current-booking field. That is part of choosing the representation, not a post hoc tolerance. A tensor spline alternative must be evaluated on the hex cells without a topology raster and must also drive clipping. A ring quadratic cannot be accepted solely because its stationary point is cheap: the seven-sample fit and its truncation/continuity must satisfy the same field, membership and map-fidelity contracts. No alternative is a runtime fallback.

Within a qualified, nondegenerate branch, differentiate a stationary position x by Hψ(x)·δx = −∇δψ(x). Axis and saddle flux derivatives are the field perturbation at the stationary point; the position term vanishes there. A smooth selected wall extremum uses the constrained envelope derivative, and a fixed corner uses its fixed point. Singular Hessians, changing component identities and class transitions are not smooth derivatives to disguise. Their qualification is explicit, and the line search must reread every candidate; no arbitrary derivative through integer branch labels is claimed.

Booking: connectivity precedes integration

current.book is supplied the read from the same psi. gs.apply constructs this pairing lexically in one evaluation; no cached read from a previous Newton state is admissible. Topology owns fragment identity; booking intersects the selected fragments with the exact level geometry and integrates the profile on them. Several disconnected pieces can occupy one hex cell. A centre label, an X-point height band, or a signed-flux inequality cannot stand in for fragment membership.

Return Iᵢ = ∫ jφ dA, Mᴿᵢ = ∫ (R−Rᵢ)jφ dA and Mᶻᵢ = ∫ (Z−Zᵢ)jφ dA, in the coupling operator's established units and basis. Zero current in private flux means zero plasma current; it does not relabel an independently prescribed conductor. Any explicitly supported open-domain source must have its own ledger entry and cannot be folded into the core normalization.

Exact clipping is the sole booking. Chord accuracy is a declared lower quadrature degree in that function, with the same connectivity, domain labels, error accounting and refusal rules. A prescribed total-current normalization differentiates through the same booked denominator and refuses zero/nonfinite denominators. It must meet |ΣIᵢ−Ianalytic|/|Ianalytic| ≤ 1e-12 while each cell moment still meets its analytic quadrature-error contract. Global renormalization cannot conceal a wrong distribution.

Differentiate coefficients, axis/boundary fluxes, profile evaluation, moving clip intersections and current normalization. Preserve the existing moment-map stages: smooth control, topology levels, axis flux, boundary envelope, support partition/clip weights, cell moments, cell integral measure, normalized observations and target-residual normalization. The moment-map instrument uses central steps 1e-3, 3e-5, 1e-5; its staged report is diagnostic, not itself a pass/fail tolerance definition. The boundary contract fixes 1e-8 scaled discrepancy and 1e-10 smooth-control error at step 1e-5. Retain these bounds and the existing end-to-end 1e-2 contract. The moment Jacobian assertion requires the coarse 1e-3 error to exceed 1e-2, with the 3e-5 and 1e-5 errors successively smaller and below it. This selection-crossing fixture remains a control; it is not a demand that a smoother replacement manufacture a coarse-step failure on every field.

Per-resolution membership and moment budgets

These are design acceptance caps, not measurements of the present implementation. The caps are equal across resolutions because dimensionless local accuracy, not a resolution-dependent permission to book private flux, is required. The existing curved-clip area tests use relative 5e-7 with absolute 1e-12; the sampled-arc first-moment derivative tests use 2e-6. Those are numerical precedents for the selected scales, not proof of analytic-state accuracy. No existing tighter primitive assertion is relaxed.

For each wall-clipped hex cell with area Aᵢ > 0 and pitch hᵢ, compare the selected geometric support Sᵢ with the independent analytic region Sᵢ*. Membership error is area(Sᵢ △ Sᵢ*) / Aᵢ; equal areas with different locations cannot pass. Define Dᵢ = max(∫Sᵢ* |jφ*| dA, |I*| Aᵢ / ΣAᵢ). Current error is |Iᵢ−Iᵢ*|/Dᵢ; first-moment error is the maximum of |Mᴿᵢ−Mᴿᵢ*|/(hᵢDᵢ) and |Mᶻᵢ−Mᶻᵢ*|/(hᵢDᵢ). The cells and fixed centroids are identical in the two arms. The nonzero analytic total supplies a stable denominator for empty and near-zero-current cells. Zero-area padded cells are masked, counted and never used to normalize an error.

The composed map and its tangent

read = topology.read(psi, geometry, convention, topology_policy)
booking = current.book(psi, read, geometry, profile, quadrature)
mapped = external_flux + coupling @ booking.moments
return MapEvaluation(mapped, read, booking, read.valid & booking.valid)

The coupling is fixed during a solve but remains a traced operand. External flux contains the explicitly declared conductor supply and any paired compensating fields. Two geometries with identical leaf shapes/dtypes must reuse one executable when their coordinate, coupling, profile or target values change. For each mesh shape, use one declared maximum constraint/telemetry/quadrature layout: inactive rows and lower quadrature degrees are masked operands, not fresh specializations per active row count or clip mode. Field representation and evaluator structure are static program identity; source values are not. Geometry itself is not a differentiation target.

Dgs(ψ)[v] = G·Dbook(ψ, read(ψ))[v] includes the read and boundary-envelope response on a qualified branch. The fixed-point residual is Fψ = ψ − gs.apply(ψ).flux; its action is v − Dgs(ψ)[v]. No tangent may call a frozen-partition map while the nonlinear score calls a different map. Preserve the implicit fixed-point derivative contract: only a converged, qualified root admits (I−Dgs)·dψ/dp = ∂gs/∂p, with matrix-free transpose action for reverse mode. Retaining that mathematical derivative does not retain Picard as a competing production solver.

Newton on the same function that is scored

For m constraint rows, pair exactly m compensating unknowns a with their declared flux images and bounds. Evaluate the augmented residual F(ψ,a) = ((ψ−M(ψ,a))/sψ, c(ψ,a;target)/srow). The positive scales are explicit, fixed within a solve and recorded; raw and scaled rows are both retained. Targets, amplitudes and row scales remain traced arrays throughout. The centroid uses the current first moments, not a separately reconstructed plasma centre.

GMRES solves DF·δ = −F at the current state. Use one monotone backtracking line search on φ = ½‖F‖², with explicit finite candidate budget, initial factor 1, shrink factor ½ and Armijo fraction 1e-4. The directional derivative comes from this same DF; a non-descent/nonfinite or inadequately solved direction is refused with a reason. Trial topology and capacity must qualify. A trial that crosses a combinatorial event is scored by a fresh read of its own field; it is never scored on the incumbent support. Exhaustion stops. It does not invoke a recovery ladder, Anderson, reduced Newton, a live-read fallback or a different clip route.

One call site is a program property. Implement a fixed-capacity lax.scan state machine carrying the incumbent, Krylov basis, trial factor, iteration counters and history. Each active microstep submits a state and direction to one jax.jvp evaluation of the augmented residual, whose body calls gs.apply once. Nonlinear-only requests use a zero direction. Krylov requests use the incumbent and the next basis direction; trial requests use the trial state. All geometry/targets are explicit operands to that call. This avoids a separately traced nonlinear-score body and tangent body; it may do redundant tangent arithmetic on score requests, which the compile and execution receipts must measure. Bounded Newton/Krylov/line-search transitions update the carry without another map call site. Verify optimized HLO, not merely the presence of a scan in Python.

The stopping metric retains the source contract ρ = max|M−ψ| / max(max|M|, 1e-30) over the whole flux carrier, including private and vacuum targets. No residual mask removes difficult cells. Success requires ρ ≤ 1e-12, every |scaled row| ≤ 1e-12, finite quantities and valid topology/booking. Report the exact metric and gauge alongside the values. An already qualifying seed returns already_converged; the required production-seed fixtures are nonconverged witnesses and must show accepted Newton steps.

Named stop categories cover invalid input, no qualified axis/boundary, ambiguous topology, unsupported/singular tangent, topology/clip capacity, nonfinite value, Krylov failure, compensator bound, line-search exhaustion and iteration budget. No stop sets numerical convergence true. Keep the last qualified accepted state; if none exists, return the input with an explicit invalid-state receipt. Under vmap, completed lanes remain inert while others proceed, retaining each lane's own reason.

The receipt stores the initial state separately and one history record per accepted Newton step: accepted state, global residual, every raw/scaled row residual, compensating unknowns, topology class/qualification, step factor, iteration and accepted count. Store trial refusal summaries separately. A validity mask separates padding from observations. The terminal receipt must equal its final accepted history entry, independently recomputed through the map. With zero accepted steps, the history is empty and the separately recorded input/stop state supplies that comparison. Host serialization adds revision, input hashes, requested/realised cells, shape/dtype signature, resolved policy, device/cache identity, cold compile seconds, executable bytes and peak host RSS. No JSON/file writing or host conversion happens inside traced functions.

Implementation and retirement order

The implementation sequence is topology, booking, composed map and derivative, then fixed-point solve, each against its independent oracle and an observed failing control. First verify which recorded defect still reproduces on the implementation base; retain a working guard and add its missing test if the behaviour is already correct. Existing contour-tree brute-force, compact-node endpoint, outside-node and COCOS sign tests are retained: their passing receipts establish reusable mechanisms, not proof that the composed topology read already exists.

Only after all acceptance rows are qualified at one integrated head may the production ladders and alternate routes be deleted. Reduce forward.py to its request/receipt facade and forward_operator.py/fixed_point.py to surviving numeric helpers or remove them when unused; they cannot keep the retired solver reachable. Run the public default-wiring and consumer tests when their call sites change. No source or tests are changed by this document.

The review handoff must settle the field representation and DIII-D oracle applicability and approve the explicitly proposed per-resolution and derivative-stage caps before those gates are armed. It must also reconcile the reconstructed inventory with any original census retained outside the plan. This document can be reviewed with those gaps explicit; it does not authorize choosing them implicitly during implementation. Numerical runs will bank their own full receipts and shared-level, wall-masked poloidal panels on the prescribed compute lane. This design contains only its two required explanatory SVGs.
