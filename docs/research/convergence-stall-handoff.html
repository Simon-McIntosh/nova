Working hypothesis for the stalls

The stall is an acceptance-policy consequence, not a discretisation or backend defect. Commit a2e65fda ("evaluate candidates on induced masks") scores a Newton candidate and the incumbent each on the active-set mask they themselves induce, and refuses the promotion unless the residual strictly decreases under that scoring. On marginal arms the candidate that the old code accepted at trip 2 is refused (3.13e-3 versus 3.49e-3 on the induced masks), the mask settles from trip 3 and never re-opens, and the fixed-mask Newton–Krylov map then contracts along a near-neutral mode too slowly to reach tolerance before the stagnation stop fires. The old code took that promotion, re-opened one cell then twenty-two at trips 4 and 5, rose transiently, and converged to 6e-12 in twelve trips.

Evidence for the hypothesis, in order of strength:

  • Per-trip trajectory capture with each candidate commit re-enacted locates the first divergence at exactly that hunk (root cause).
  • Re-solving at the bank's true revision reproduces the committed rows to 2e-16 (true pin).
  • Every step-side remedy that leaves the policy in place produces a bit-identical trajectory, because its candidate is refused by the same guard (remedy ladder).
  • The corrected Solov'ev certificate shows the same signature on exact solutions with no noise and no seed ambiguity: fourteen of sixteen rows settle at 1e-3 to 3e-2 after ten Newton steps, while the two rows whose loop converges reach 2e-16 and 5e-16 (corrected certificate).
  • Paired CPU and GPU solves stay inside float64 noise, so the backend is excluded (backend forensics).

What remains unproven: that re-admitting the pre-settlement promotion recovers convergence on the Solov'ev rows as well as on the MAST arm. The bank reproduction proves it for 22086/43 pure at the old revision; the Solov'ev production route has never been run with the old acceptance.

  • Mixed arms still do. 21985/51 mixed 2.8e-16, 22086/43 mixed 1.7e-14, 21978/35 mixed 7.5e-12 at HEAD. These arms are unaffected by the policy because their mask never sits on the margin.

  • The pure marginal arm reached tolerance, not ULP. The committed 22086/43 pure row was 6.08e-12 in twelve trips at revision d47f7cd1; HEAD stagnates at 4.80e-4 after seven. That is the regression, and it is located.

The decision the next session needs

All five remedies that respect the locked acceptance policy are negative. The plan's remaining move is to test the policy itself, scoped so the referees stay green:

  1. Score pre-settlement candidates on the incumbent partition (recommended). Keep induced-mask scoring once the mask has settled; before settlement, compare candidate and incumbent on the incumbent's mask, which is what the banked code did. Test: 22086/43 pure must return to 6e-12, the Solov'ev fourteen must move, and the seven referees plus the hex-reference gates must stay green. This is the only remedy with a positive precedent (the committed bank).
  2. Accept honest stagnation as final and re-baseline the banks under it, treating the marginal-basin rows as a known limitation. The north star's accuracy half then stands at two Solov'ev rows and three MAST mixed arms.
  3. Reopen a2e65fda wholesale. Not recommended: the induced-mask scoring fixed a real defect on non-marginal arms and the referees encode it.

Whichever branch is chosen should be measured first on the Solov'ev fourteen (exact answer known, no seed ambiguity, 150 to 850 s per row on the H200) and only then on the MAST bank.
