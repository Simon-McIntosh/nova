Research input · measured ruling proposal

Which region a cut cell books

Recommend one shared normal-form support for the X-point owner and saddle neighbourhood, ordinary quadratic cuts outside it, and a spatially partitioned boundary cell so the handoff exchanges vanishing area. Independent references cover 64 cells and five states. The geometry mismatch reproduces, but the reported pinned runtime switch does not; neither geometry uniformly equals the Bell-field region within its finest quadrature error. This proposal preserves that distinction and awaits the lead’s lock.

The decision and its provenance

The decision is which support geometry owns each class of cut cell, and how a moving saddle or boundary hands a cell between classes without a finite current jump. The consumer is the converged forward-solve plan, especially the topology read and forward-solve booking interfaces. The lead owns the corresponding ruling lock. The requested artifact belongs in nova because the field reconstruction, read and current integration are nova functions.

The tie state is pinned to c09e138e5. The analytic comparison uses base 5e75d3019f65d272998a7e732e32fb073767ee06. These are different executable reads: the pinned tie tree selects the first contour-tree event and also carries a closed-lobe branch; the base selects a saddle from the axis merge path. The comparison must not substitute one read for the other without naming the change. Each measurement log prints its revision, imported nova.__file__, module path and resolved working directory.

The predecessor's pinned cell-term record reports conic area 0.0003694767222 m² and normal-form area 0.0004130564771 m² in cell 266. At identical pointwise density the current difference is 0.8930978222 A; the ordinary density approximation contributes another 0.0677978031 A. Their sum is 0.9608956253 A before the connectivity weight. The normal-form area differs from membership × full_area / path_weight by 2.54 × 10−9 m². The reviewed comparison below independently reproduces this decomposition.

The same predecessor says the previously reported adjacent-double switch does not reproduce at its integration revision: both offsets 0.16436667480678155 and 0.16436667480678158 select saddle booking. The reproduction therefore records the actual selector operands on both sides; it does not label a hypothetical forced geometry exchange as an observed runtime switch.

Ruling proposal for the read and booking owners

Use one support object per cell: the selected normal-form lobe in the saddle neighbourhood, the quadratic cut outside it, and a spatially partitioned cut for a cell intersecting the neighbourhood boundary. Read area, booked current and both first moments must integrate that same object with the same density and quadrature points. This is a recommendation for the lead to lock, not a change to the live plan or product source. A whole-cell replacement selected by centre distance, crossing count or ordinary validity is not a continuous geometric handoff.

Write D = disk(X, r_eff), where r_eff = max(r_nf, 1.5 × median pitch), and let N and Q be the selected normal-form and ordinary supports with the same connectivity convention. The proposed region of cell C is (C ∩ D ∩ N) ∪ (C ∩ complement(D) ∩ Q). This is one binary support with two disjoint pieces. When the disk enters or leaves a cell, the exchanged area tends to zero; bounded density therefore makes the cell current and both first moments continuous. Any circle arcs needed to close mismatched cuts are explicit support edges and must be included in the fidelity check. This continuity argument does not prove Newton convergence or differentiability through every topological event.

The measured owner polygons lie wholly inside the current effective disks: their farthest vertices are 0.8242, 0.5992, 0.5574 and 0.6257 times r_eff for tie, 282, 550 and 2,098 respectively. Thus the proposed spatial partition preserves the complete owner lobe on these carriers. Runtime qualification must check containment on other meshes; it must not silently switch the owner to an ordinary cut. Owner transfer must repartition the same lobe rather than create a different support, and selected-saddle changes remain separately qualified events.

The radius policy must be explicit. These measurements use the actual policy default r_nf = 0 and its 1.5-pitch floor; they do not claim to have run the calibrated 0.0548 m policy. The radius is a case-calibrated runtime policy operand: 0.0548 m belongs to this analytic case, not to every machine. The proposal uses that calibrated physical radius with its moving floor, retains the amplitude acceptance on that arm, and retains the fixed-radius arm for the 1.9-or-better order check. No order is inferred from this three-carrier local comparison.

The one wording clarification proposed to the lead is every cell inside r_nf takes its support from those branches: replace centre-based whole-cell membership by every portion of a cell inside the effective normal-form neighbourhood takes support from those branches; portions outside use the ordinary cut, with one shared integral over the disjoint partition. The owner-lobe choice, connectivity exclusion, physical-radius calibration and two-arm acceptance remain intact. This is an explicit proposed revision if the existing sentence is intended to require whole-cell replacement; it is not silently treated as permission to change that ruling.

The changes belong in scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production/kernel.py::read for support selection and its receipt; nova/equilibrium/topology.py::_normal_form_cell_fragments for the branch portions; and the common ring construction beside nova/equilibrium/current.py::_normal_rings and _conic_rings for the disk intersection. scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production/program.py::_saddle_moments and scripts/prototypes/fast_read/ordinary_cut.py::bell_moments must consume that common support and density contract. Reuse nova/equilibrium/clip_quadrature.py::_quadrature_from_arrays; do not introduce another copied fan reduction. The lobe-share implementation already uses its eight-point Gauss product rule, which superseded the truncated local degree-five constants. The fast prototype measured here still uses a separate three-point triangle rule at four segments.

Evaluate the same profile on the same field representation at the common quadrature points on both sides. This removes the ordinary-path density approximation as a second source of handoff jumps. The present analytic profiles reduce to aR + b/R and are independent of normalised flux; their controls do not qualify a general nonlinear profile. The implementation must cover such a profile explicitly. Neither a shared fan nor a higher quadrature order removes the normal-form-versus-Bell representation error.

Continuity test to pin the change: in the read/booking regression module, move a single cell across the disk-entry, disk-exit, owner-transfer and active discrete-selector thresholds. Require the saved flags or intersection measures to prove that the intended switch actually occurred. Compare weighted cell current and both first moments on the two sides against the sum of their independently refined booking errors plus the independently measured physical field/weight change. Refine the perturbation until that physical change is below the booking error. Run the literal mutation --force-conic-right, replacing the right support by the whole ordinary cut with the same density and weight, and require the same continuity assertion to fail. Include the pinned pair below as a stable-state control; it does not exercise a runtime switch and is not passed off as that test.

The cost is disk/curve intersections and two piece integrals in a boundary band about one cell wide, common pointwise density evaluation, and the shared higher-order rule in the prototype saddle path. For regular cells of pitch h the additional band scales with circumference, O(r_eff/h), rather than all cells. This is an operation-count estimate, not a timing claim. The existing eight-by-eight product rule uses 64 points per live triangle versus three in the fast saddle path, a 21.3-fold point-count increase at fixed triangle count; four-to-256 chord refinement separately increases curved fan segments 64-fold. Those reference settings are not prescribed as production defaults: choose the shared rule and boundary refinement by the error budget, and measure their cost. The existing fixed saddle capacity of 16 also needs qualification when r_nf is fixed while the mesh refines; an overflow must refuse rather than truncate the active cells. A chord fan at 256 segments still differs from the curved-sector reference, so 1e-12 agreement with the curved region would require additional boundary refinement or curved-edge quadrature. Agreement at 1e-12 with the same finite chord fan is a different, already demonstrated, contract.

The counter-evidence prevents unconditional adoption: selected normal-form booking raised historical map error to 0.0869; at 550 cells the lobe map error 4.87 × 10−4 was worse than the retired half-plane's 4.10 × 10−4; and the free 550-cell lobe solve stalled at 1.54987 × 10−3. The later vertical pin reached 2.09 × 10−13 on the same booked map, so that stall is not evidence that switching owner geometry is the correct repair. Separately, certificate convergence reports a connectivity-width repair at 550 while the finer diverted row remains open. Keep both controls: map fidelity across the carrier ladder and pinned cold solves with Taylor slopes, alongside the moving-floor amplitude and fixed-radius order gates. None was rerun or claimed by this local measurement node.

Independent references and refinement

The row matrix covers tie cell 266; analytic cell 71; the X-point owner at 282, 550 and 2,098 cells; and at least twelve ordinary cut cells on each of a diverted and a limited case. Selection spans the nearest available cuts, the radius neighbourhood and the distant boundary. An absent saddle on the limited control is reported as no applicable normal-form geometry, not as a measured zero-area lobe.

For analytic rows, Bell elements are constructed directly from the independent oracle's nodal jets on the cached carrier. This avoids a redundant source-matrix build while preserving the Bell representation being sampled. It is an analytic-state reconstruction measurement, not a terminal forward solve. The tie state uses the pinned executable fixture and warmed prior without importing its archived tree into the worker's own source tree.

The independent evaluator exports the Bell polynomial coefficients and evaluates them with NumPy and a separate nearest-generator lookup. Known points are compared with the executable Bell evaluator before any absence is reported. The oracle is evaluated separately in NumPy and checked against its executable field. Every result carries area [m²], unnormalised current [A], and first current moments about the cell centre [A m]; the connectivity multiplier is reported separately.

Fine Cartesian midpoint sampling uses widths 128, 256, 512 and 1,024, with 2,048 added for the owner and named cells. It samples the quadratic, Bell and analytic fields against the boundary level selected by the read. It also samples the normal-form sector directly from its sidedness functions, without calling the ring builder. Both the entire positive local region and the axis-facing portion are retained: a local half-plane is an explicit diagnostic, not a substitute for a global connectivity proof. Independent connected-component floods from the magnetic axis provide a resolution-qualified topology check and print known core and polygon counts. Their observed limitations are reported below; they do not certify exact saddle connectivity.

An adaptive vertical-slice reference complements the raster's boundary noise. NumPy polynomial roots locate each field's slice crossings, without the production clipping kernels. The source's radial density form aR + b/R is used only after comparison with the actual profile at 17 radii and three normalised flux values. Height integrals and their first moments are then exact; the outer adaptive rule is refined through normalised tolerances 10−6, 10−8 and 10−10. Its reported error estimate and refinement movement remain visible beside the grid convergence.

The booking geometries are separately refined through 4, 16, 64 and 256 segments. An independent signed product rule of orders four and eight integrates the same pointwise density over each fan, alongside the production three-point triangle rule. This distinguishes chord error, density error and integration error. A known-region instrument control already recovers area 2, current 5 and radial first moment 8/3, and independently recovers area 1 for a saddle wedge. This control validates the measurement tool; it does not establish any physical-cell result.

The continuity check to accompany the ruling

The proposed test records a single cell's booked current and both first moments on opposite sides of each class switch while keeping the field, selected boundary and connectivity accounting explicit. The allowed difference is the independently measured booking error plus the physical change between the two field states; an adjacent-double perturbation makes the latter directly measurable. Record radius membership, owner containment, crossing count, ordinary validity, selected saddle and closed-loop status so a passing test identifies the mechanism it exercised.

The literal negative control in the measurement probe is --force-conic-right: leave the two pinned field states unchanged, but replace the right-hand cell 266 current by its ordinary conic booking times its actual connectivity weight. The check must refuse that exchange if its jump exceeds the ring-refinement error budget. The unmodified pair is a positive control only for the state and revision it actually selects. Neither arm is a product-source repair.
