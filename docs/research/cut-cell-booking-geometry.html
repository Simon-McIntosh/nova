Which region a cut cell books

A geometry ruling needs three distinct comparisons: the region represented by the read, the region integrated by each booking path, and the independent analytic region. This record reports the reviewed tie, 282-cell and 2,098-cell measurements and a provisional per-class proposal. The 550-cell and limited controls remain outstanding; no completed measurement matrix or locked ruling is claimed.

The decision and its provenance

The decision is which support geometry owns each class of cut cell, and how a moving saddle or boundary hands a cell between classes without a finite current jump. The consumer is the converged forward-solve plan, especially its read and booking interfaces. The requested artifact belongs in nova because the field reconstruction, read and current integration are nova functions.

The tie state is pinned to c09e138e5. The analytic comparison uses base 5e75d3019f65d272998a7e732e32fb073767ee06. These are different executable reads: the pinned tie tree selects the first contour-tree event and also carries a closed-lobe branch; the base selects a saddle from the axis merge path. The comparison must not substitute one read for the other without naming the change. Each measurement log will print its revision, imported nova.__file__, module path and resolved working directory.

The predecessor's pinned cell-term record reports conic area 0.0003694767222 m² and normal-form area 0.0004130564771 m² in cell 266. At identical pointwise density the current difference is 0.8930978222 A; the ordinary density approximation contributes another 0.0677978031 A. Their sum is 0.9608956253 A before the connectivity weight. The normal-form area differs from membership × full_area / path_weight by 2.54 × 10−9 m². The reviewed comparison below independently reproduces this decomposition.

The same predecessor says the previously reported adjacent-double switch does not reproduce at its integration revision: both offsets 0.16436667480678155 and 0.16436667480678158 select saddle booking. The reproduction therefore records the actual selector operands on both sides; it does not label a hypothetical forced geometry exchange as an observed runtime switch.

Independent references and refinement

The row matrix covers tie cell 266; analytic cell 71; the X-point owner at 282, 550 and 2,098 cells; and at least twelve ordinary cut cells on each of a diverted and a limited case. Selection spans the nearest available cuts, the radius neighbourhood and the distant boundary. An absent saddle on the limited control is reported as no applicable normal-form geometry, not as a measured zero-area lobe.

For analytic rows, Bell elements are constructed directly from the independent oracle's nodal jets on the cached carrier. This avoids a redundant source-matrix build while preserving the Bell representation being sampled. It is an analytic-state reconstruction measurement, not a terminal forward solve. The tie state uses the pinned executable fixture and warmed prior without importing its archived tree into the worker's own source tree.

The independent evaluator exports the Bell polynomial coefficients and evaluates them with NumPy and a separate nearest-generator lookup. Known points are compared with the executable Bell evaluator before any absence is reported. The oracle is evaluated separately in NumPy and checked against its executable field. Every result carries area [m²], unnormalised current [A], and first current moments about the cell centre [A m]; the connectivity multiplier is reported separately.

Fine Cartesian midpoint sampling uses widths 128, 256, 512 and 1,024, with 2,048 added for the owner and named cells. It samples the quadratic, Bell and analytic fields against the boundary level selected by the read. It also samples the normal-form sector directly from its sidedness functions, without calling the ring builder. Both the entire positive local region and the axis-facing portion are retained: a local half-plane is an explicit diagnostic, not a substitute for a global connectivity proof. Independent connected-component floods from the magnetic axis provide that additional topology check and print a positive core count before reporting an empty cell.

An adaptive vertical-slice reference complements the raster's boundary noise. NumPy polynomial roots locate each field's slice crossings, without the production clipping kernels. The source's radial density form aR + b/R is used only after comparison with the actual profile at 17 radii and three normalised flux values. Height integrals and their first moments are then exact; the outer adaptive rule is refined through normalised tolerances 10−6, 10−8 and 10−10. Its reported error estimate and refinement movement remain visible beside the grid convergence.

The booking geometries are separately refined through 4, 16, 64 and 256 segments. An independent signed product rule of orders four and eight integrates the same pointwise density over each fan, alongside the production three-point triangle rule. This distinguishes chord error, density error and integration error. A known-region instrument control already recovers area 2, current 5 and radial first moment 8/3, and independently recovers area 1 for a saddle wedge. This control validates the measurement tool; it does not establish any physical-cell result.

The continuity check to accompany the ruling

The proposed test records a single cell's booked current and both first moments on opposite sides of each class switch while keeping the field, selected boundary and connectivity accounting explicit. The allowed difference is the independently measured booking error plus the physical change between the two field states; an adjacent-double perturbation makes the latter directly measurable. Record radius membership, owner containment, crossing count, ordinary validity, selected saddle and closed-loop status so a passing test identifies the mechanism it exercised.

The literal negative control in the measurement probe is --force-conic-right: leave the two pinned field states unchanged, but replace the right-hand cell 266 current by its ordinary conic booking times its actual connectivity weight. The check must refuse that exchange if its jump exceeds the ring-refinement error budget. The unmodified pair is a positive control only for the state and revision it actually selects. Neither arm is a product-source repair.

Reviewed measurements and their limits

The geometry disagreement reproduces; the runtime switch does not at the requested pin. The pinned cell 266 has the same selected saddle, owner status, normal-form membership and saddle booking on both adjacent-double states. Its centre stays 0.0618872 m from the saddle, well inside the effective 0.150695 m radius. Both boundary levels are 0.00816869754691945 Wb to the shown precision; their actual difference is 1.73472 × 10−18 Wb. The weighted current changes by 4.99600 × 10−14 A. There is no radius crossing, tie-induced selector flip or saddle replacement in this reproduction. The historical 5.2 × 10−18 Wb / 0.961 A switching claim therefore cannot be attributed to this executable pair.

At 64 segments, the independently integrated conic and normal-form areas recover 0.0003694767222 and 0.0004130564771 m². With the same pointwise density their currents are 7.8801663546 and 8.7732641768 A: a geometry difference of 0.8930978222 A. The ordinary density approximation gives 7.8123685515 A, another 0.0677978031 A. The total hypothetical exchange is 0.9608956253 A, before connectivity weighting. These measurements reproduce the predecessor's decomposition, without inventing a runtime switch.

The finest independent adaptive reference for the normal-form sector gives area 0.000413053937684236 m², matching the read's membership × full_area / path_weight, 0.000413053937684239 m². Its current is 8.7732104935 A. The Bell field's axis-facing region at the same selected boundary carries 8.7216779547 A over 0.000410663617634496 m². Thus normal-form region error relative to this field reference remains 0.05153254 A (0.591%), although it is much smaller than the conic's −0.84120216 A (−9.645%). This representation difference is not quadrature error.

Independent field thresholding still needs an ownership convention at the saddle. The adaptive references below retain both the full positive region and its axis-facing part. At 2,098 cells the full fitted conic contains 13.92895186 A while its facing part contains 0.93322409 A; the Bell facing lobe contains 0.78450391 A and the normal-form lobe 0.78448567 A. The unqualified conic ring helper reports invalid on both measured analytic owner cells. Its all-positive integral is a diagnostic, not an accepted production owner rule. Global raster flooding from the axis joins the other saddle lobe at the sampled resolution and reaches the wall. This does not certify the intended owner lobe; no global-connectivity claim is inferred from the local half-plane diagnostic.

The accepted rows currently comprise cell 266, all 16 selected cells on the 282-cell carrier (including owner 7 and cell 71), and all 16 on the 2,098-cell carrier (including owner 47). The 551-cell measurement is excluded: a same-name cache entry had a different wall and no admitted saddle. The limited request failed before a state was built because no legacy cache group matched. A read-only content-addressed carrier selection will fill these two missing cases. The tie flood's zero count is also excluded: its box omitted cell 266 and touched the selected component. Corrected flooding must contain every measured polygon and see its known interior before an empty connected region is interpretable.
