Is EFIT’s reference a fixed point of the registered forward map?

EFIT’s DIII-D references are not fixed points of the measured registered map. Relative defects are 0.0481222, 0.0482373 and 0.0300333 for frames 137, 146 and 179. Reference-seeded Newton fails to converge on all three, whereas MAST converges to 2.2 × 10⁻¹⁶. Frames 137 and 146 have appreciable upward reference forces; 179 is nearly vertically balanced. The evidence supports map inconsistency with an additional dependence on the seed and branch path, rather than a universal downward-force explanation.

Instrument and provenance

The DIII-D rows are frames 137, 146 and 179 selected through the registered benchmark’s select_frames. Each uses _build_profile, its EFIT-flux seed and physical wall, the completed circuit-current adapter and the frame’s recorded plasma-current target. The MAST row is shot 21978, slice 35, at 33 × 33 grid points, using the parity benchmark’s passive-inclusive fitted-circuit builder. Both builders are the same ones used by the finite-admission measurement. This is a measurement of those specific composed maps; no source, coil current, mask, tolerance or product code is repaired.

The primary residual is exactly max|g(ψref) − ψref| / max|g(ψref)|, over the full grid-plus-wall state, cross-checked against fixed_point._relative_residual. The largest grid defect’s location is reported separately because a wall entry can set the full-state maximum. A companion centred defect subtracts the median additive constant from the difference, using the same denominator. It diagnoses gauge sensitivity and does not replace the primary raw measurement.

The vertical-force quadrature is Fz = −Σ Icell BR 2πR, in newtons: the operator’s own clipped cell-current integrals supply Icell = Jφ dA, after its declared-current amplitude is applied. BR comes from the map image’s grid flux through Nova’s poloidal_field, which divides the total-flux derivative by 2πR. A second force uses the reference flux’s gradient instead. This distinguishes the field the composed model supplies from the one the label carries. The force fraction divides by μ0 Ip²/2, the characteristic thin-ring hoop-force scale before the aspect-ratio/internal-inductance geometry factor. It is a specified comparison scale, not a measured net radial force. Cell-centre force quadrature and finite-difference field gradients carry discretization error, including a possible discrete plasma self-force.

The negative controls translate the total reference grid and wall flux by rigid ±2 cm in Z while retaining the wall, external currents and prescribed profiles. Cubic interpolation evaluates ψ(R,Z−ΔZ); the spline extrapolates the outermost grid strip where necessary. The signed force offset and its displacement slope are reported, so a biased force need not cross zero for the displacement response to be visible.

Newton uses the benchmark’s registered ForwardSolveRequest and ExplicitSolveSeed, with only warmup=0 changed. No topology class is imposed. A finite saddle is not called an admitted X-point on a limited state, and no cross-branch saddle distance is reported as X-point displacement. Terminal residual convergence and public receipt qualification are kept separately.

The diagnostic plasma tare applies Delta-star to the reference on the benchmark grid, retains the density inside EFIT’s LCFS, and images its node-area zeroth current moments through the map’s own Green operator. The difference splits algebraically into the prescribed plasma image minus this label-current tare, and the supplied external field minus the remaining label flux. Their sum is checked against the measured grid defect. This node-area tare has a different discretization from the older native-grid exact clipped-profile tare, so it is identified explicitly and does not silently inherit that instrument’s closure claims.

The target-current switch removes only the common amplitude applied to the reference-state current moments. The coil switch zeros the ordinary conductor-current vector; any prescribed current-field response remains and is reported separately. DIII-D’s absent driven passive state is not replaced by an invented current. MAST’s prescribed field contains both active and passive fitted circuits, so that switch alone cannot separate its passive circuits from its active ones.

The plasma/exterior pair is an additive decomposition of the grid defect. The target-current switch overlaps the plasma term and is a sensitivity experiment, not a third additive term. The largest contributor is ranked by centred grid RMS, with its flux amplitude reported separately. A large coil-only flux is not itself a coil error: its appropriate comparison is the reference remainder after the independently reconstructed plasma tare. These switches locate a closure mismatch; they do not uniquely identify a missing coil current or establish that an unrecorded passive current exists.

What the measurements discriminate

Frame 179 supports map inconsistency without the same force-imbalance signature. Its raw/centred reference residuals are 0.0300333/0.0228092, but its net vertical force is only −297.638 N, or −0.0289285% of the hoop scale. Newton moves the axis outward 0.521957 cm and upward 1.12165 cm (distance 1.23715 cm), retains a diverted terminal state, and stops with ACTIVE_SET_STEP_REFUSED at solver/direct residuals 0.00793785/0.00793845. Its selected upper saddle does not match the continued lower reference saddle, so an X-point displacement is not reported. This frame does not support a large reference vertical-force deficit as the common cause of failure.

Frames 137 and 146 support reference-map inconsistency and also retain solver-path sensitivity. Their reference residuals are 0.0481222 and 0.0482373, compared with 0.00669902 for MAST; their median-centred residuals remain 0.0410921 and 0.0433227, so an additive gauge offset does not account for the mismatch. The corresponding upward forces are 17429.7 and 11641.8 N, or 1.04524% and 0.702914% of the specified hoop scale. The discrete plasma self-force is only about 6 and 4 N. With the exterior fixed, both frames change from downward to upward force across the −2/+2 cm controls, a destabilizing vertical response around this reference neighbourhood.

Reference-seeded Newton does not reproduce the large downward excursion of the synthetic cold-seed runs. Frame 137 moves inward 3.44491 cm and down 0.228484 cm, retaining a diverted state but stopping with ACTIVE_SET_STEP_REFUSED; its direct terminal map residual is 0.00393316. Frame 146 moves inward 8.20425 cm and up 9.92959 cm, also diverted and step-refused, with direct residual 0.00826240. Thus the downward cold-start path is not an inevitable direction of Newton from EFIT, and reaching a smaller self-consistency residual does not establish recovery of the reference equilibrium.

The independently continued lower saddles verify admitted X-point displacements of 7.33492 cm for 137 and 4.29763 cm for 146. MAST’s selected terminal X-point is upper while its stored reference saddle is lower, so it also has no admitted same-branch gap. The solver’s terminal residual and the direct full-state map residual are deliberately both printed: their support/topology reads can differ, most visibly on 146 (0.00209043 versus 0.00826240). The smaller solver number does not replace the map diagnostic used at the reference.

The source-image and exterior-remainder discrepancies are comparable. The exterior remainder is the larger centred component for 137 (0.0246954 versus 0.0202767 Wb) and 146 (0.0215780 versus 0.0178416 Wb). The declared-current amplitude improves closure: removing it raises their reference residuals to 0.121473 and 0.117388. Reversing that normalization is therefore not supported as a repair. For 179 the plasma-versus-tare term is larger (0.0235099 versus 0.0157473 Wb).

The displacement controls demonstrate sensitivity, with two limits. MAST’s residual increases by factors 8.10917 and 9.65841, and the same instrument reads its converged terminal root at 2.20420 × 10⁻¹⁶. Its shifted forces are asymmetric and both downward, so no symmetric restoring law is inferred. Frame 146 at −2 cm instead reduces the residual slightly, from 0.0482373 to 0.0475090. That prediction fails for an already off-root state; the exception is retained. Frame 179’s class-free reference read is diverted, whereas the brief designates the archive frame as the limited control; both shifted reads are limited. Its displacement response therefore also crosses a topology/support change.

The repair indicated by the reference experiment is to restore consistency between the reference flux, its plasma-source image and the supplied exterior field before treating EFIT as a root of this map. The owning composition points are benchmarks/diiid_forward_gs_match.py:_label_state and _build_profile; the current-data boundary is nova/imas/diiid_current.py. This measurement does not uniquely determine a physical coil or passive-current correction, especially with the stated node-area tare discretization, and does not reopen the rejected missing-external-field hypothesis as a proved cause. Separately, seed admission and continuation belong to nova/equilibrium/forward.py:cold_seed_portfolio and nova/equilibrium/fixed_point.py: a requested branch must be checked against its actual admitted topology. The adjacent diverted-branch continuation work owns product repair; this record supplies its reference-state evidence. No product source is changed here.
