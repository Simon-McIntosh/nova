The outer-trip search reset discards contracting Newton work

The settled-mask inner solve is neither stationary nor limited by its Krylov direction. It contracts locally, but every outer trip first runs eight warmup sweeps that raise the residual above the best state already carried by the solve. The Newton endpoint remains worse than that carried best, so the outer solve returns exactly zero improvement and repeats.

Adjudication

Acceptance mismatch is ruled out in both frames. Exactly zero candidates lower the live relative-sup residual while failing the merit threshold. The candidates production selects lower both quantities. A local barrier is also ruled out in both frames. The Newton directional derivatives are materially negative, and 20 of 23 frame-179 candidates plus 9 of 23 frame-44 candidates lower the live residual. Basin or model error is present but is not the terminal mechanism. Three frame-179 and fourteen frame-44 candidates predict a merit decrease that the actual nonlinear evaluation does not deliver; nevertheless the backtracking ladder finds accepted descent in both. The frames agree on the operational verdict while differing sharply in model fidelity: frame 44 needs four refusals before its first accepted Newton factor, whereas frame 179 accepts the full step.

Thus none of the three explanations, taken as a terminal cause, fits the observed stall. The deciding fact is that a production-valid promotion exists at each retained state but the outer algorithm never executes a trip from that state without first replacing its search trajectory. If the selected one-step ratios persisted, they would imply approximately 194 further promotions for frame 179 and 10,620 for frame 44 to reach the unchanged 1×10−6 tolerance; the best diagnostic Levenberg ratios imply 19 and 1,154. These are conditional projections, not convergence claims from one step. What the census establishes is reachability: the tolerance is not excluded by a stationary retained state or by the acceptance gate. Consequence for the demonstration gate

The existing production and damping-search chains cannot reach 1×10−6; that negative result remains exact. The new evidence does not show that the tolerance is unreachable by every method within the current residual formulation, discretisation, and fixed-shape execution model. Quite the opposite: every halt retains admissible, materially useful descent. The evidence therefore points to globalization model and candidate-selection coverage, not to a positive residual floor requiring a changed formulation, grid, or tolerance. Reaching the gate remains unproven, but relaxing it is not supported by this census.

Two observations would overturn that conclusion. A fixed-shape chain that follows the available accepted directions to 1×10−6 would positively establish reachability. In the other direction, an exhaustive self-consistent-mask optimization showing gradient collapse and a positive minimum above tolerance would establish a formulation or discretisation floor. Neither observation is present here.

Evaluated remedies and remaining engineering direction

  1. Evaluate candidates on their own promoted masks. Implemented and retained as a correctness rule: it refuses the measured 2.05594× regression and preserves bit-identical numerics when the mask does not change. As a reachability remedy it is negative: the final chains halt at 0.0231962682 and 0.0810029190 rather than 1×10−6.
  2. Carry the best-observed iterate across outer trips and do not degrade it during warmup. Implemented as a fixed-shape promoted- merit guard and refuted as the stall remedy: both frames remain at outer q = 1.0000000000000000 for four guarded trips. Direct settled-mask re-evaluation now proves the protected residuals are reachable under the current domains with exactly zero difference. Keep the guard as a no-regression invariant, but do not project convergence from it.
  3. Resume the frozen-mask Newton trajectory across outer trips. Implemented by carrying the terminal state and masking warmup, and refuted as the stall remedy: both frames remain at outer q = 1.0000000000000000 for six trips. The local endpoints remain worse than the retained states, so neither frame can reach tolerance at any finite trip budget.
  4. Carry the full globalization state across unchanged masks. Implemented for the merit window, adaptive radius, damping, Krylov baseline, amplification state, and best fallback, and refuted as the stall remedy. Both frames retain zero mask differences and outer q = 1.0000000000000000 across six measured trips. Do not pursue another variation of cross-trip state carry.
  5. Replace the bounded candidate ladder with an own-mask-aware adaptive descent model. This is the single remaining direction most directly supported by the evidence: both final arms consume the steepest scale floor and then refuse every offered candidate. Cost is high: a fresh endpoint census, fixed-shape adaptive-scale design, full residual/JVP and conservation revalidation, and another production two-frame chain. The present two-frame measurement alone cost 360.325 s to compile and 2378.544 s to execute. It is falsified if a census at both final endpoints finds no own-mask descent direction, or if an adaptive chain again reaches terminal contraction one above 1×10−6.
  6. Increase the Newton budget only after the reset is removed. At the measured local rates, reaching 1×10−6 from the carried residual would take approximately 528 and 999 uninterrupted accepted iterations. Cost: very high compute. Falsifying evidence: measured contraction degrades before crossing the carried best. Merely adding outer trips is already falsified because their measured rate is exactly one.

Krylov retuning is not ranked as a primary remedy: reductions already sit seven to nine orders of magnitude inside the requested tolerance. A preconditioner change would become relevant only if a guarded, uninterrupted nonlinear trajectory exposed a materially worse linear reduction or a direction-quality failure not present here.

What this investigation establishes

The final repair makes acceptance honest: a candidate cannot win using a residual measured on a mask it will not own. That removes the known 2.05594× regression and lowers frame 179 substantially, but neither production frame reaches 1×10−6; both exhaust the bounded candidate ladder with terminal contraction one. On the evidence gathered, the unchanged tolerance is therefore not reachable by this formulation’s current fixed candidate family. The evidence does not prove that no fixed-shape method can reach it. The one remaining direction worth measuring is an own-mask-aware adaptive descent model, beginning with a fresh census at the two new halt states and carrying the high validation and compute cost stated above. No conclusion here depends on relaxing the tolerance; that policy choice remains open for the lead.
