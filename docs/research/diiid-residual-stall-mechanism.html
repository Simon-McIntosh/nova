Research record · measured solver mechanism

The outer-trip search reset discards contracting Newton work

The settled-mask inner solve is neither stationary nor limited by its Krylov direction. It contracts locally, but every outer trip first runs eight warmup sweeps that raise the residual above the best state already carried by the solve. The Newton endpoint remains worse than that carried best, so the outer solve returns exactly zero improvement and repeats.

Deciding result. Frame 179 accepts all 24 settled-trip steps at local q = 0.9799177, yet warmup raises 0.0448512 to 0.327281 and the Newton endpoint 0.201127 is still 4.48432× worse. Frame 44 accepts 22 steps and refuses two for sufficient decrease at local q = 0.9887442, yet warmup raises 0.0811700 to 0.146068 and the endpoint 0.113868 is still 1.40283× worse. Both outer trips return exactly zero improvement, making the settled outer contraction q = 1.0.

Remedy falsifier. Retaining the incoming state whenever an unchanged active set proposes a worse promotion merit leaves both persisted stalls unchanged over four resumed trips. Every measured per-trip q is 1.0000000000000000. Retention prevents regression, but it does not preserve the locally contracting endpoint needed to make progress.

Continuation falsifier. Resuming each unchanged-mask trip from the preceding terminal Newton state, with warmup masked off, also leaves both persisted stalls unchanged for six trips. Every measured outer q remains 1.0000000000000000, so terminal-state continuation is refuted as a route to the unchanged 1×10−6 tolerance.

Domain-comparability verdict. Re-evaluating each carried state on its reconstructed settled mask reproduces both stored residuals exactly: 0.04485116727666494 and 0.08117000452284782, with zero absolute difference. Both masks are fixed under the promoted-mask update. The carried targets are genuinely reachable on the settled domains; the flat outer residual is not an artefact of comparing different masks.

Measurement method and callback qualification

The analysis used the complete log and both terminal checkpoints, not a tail sample. The fixed-shape solve emits both mapped branch lanes and JAX stages callbacks from both sides of a transformed conditional. This yields 1,536 raw inner-newton lines per frame, including 768 speculative refusal lines whose own Krylov qualification says accepted. Those impossible combinations are callback staging artifacts, not selected nonlinear decisions. Semantic records were selected from the actual diverted lane and its selected conditional branch: 144 records across six executed trips for frame 179 and 72 across three trips for frame 44. The last executed trip is the settled-mask trip reported below.

Residual localization re-evaluated the pinned driver at the checkpoint state, taking the governed wall from ProfileBuild.wall. The reconstructed relative-sup residual equals the stored scalar bit for bit for both frames. Grid locations came from FluxLattice.coordinate; wall distances used the built wall coordinates; X-point, axis, boundary flux, and normalized flux came from the terminal topology.

The mechanism is therefore the solve-level search reset: the inner Newton model finds usable directions and contracts, but the next outer trip replays a degrading warmup from a state above the retained optimum. Newton does not recover the carried best within its 24 iterations, so best-observed selection correctly returns the old state. The settled mask then launches the same ineffective search again.

The local rate and settled outer rate reconcile the prior views

The previously recorded tail-contraction estimate of approximately q = 0.970 per promotion is broadly consistent with the local Newton rates. Relative to a contraction deficit of 0.030, frame 179's deficit of 0.0200823 is 1.49× smaller (0.17 orders of magnitude) and frame 44's deficit of 0.0112558 is 2.67× smaller (0.43 orders). These are slower, but not orders-of-magnitude contradictory.

The committed bank sees a different level of the algorithm. Its settled outer residual is bit-identical because the warmup reset discards the carried best at the start of every trip and the local Newton endpoint never earns promotion over it. The actual outer contraction is therefore exactly q = 1.0. The recorded projection of about 425 further promotions to 1×10−8 consequently does not hold at any budget: repeating an outer action with q = 1.0 never reaches the criterion. Even the counterfactual local rates would require 755 and 1,406 uninterrupted Newton iterations from the carried residuals to reach 1×10−8; the present outer loop interrupts that contraction every 24 iterations.

Best-iterate retention is correct but refuted as the stall remedy

The outer solver now compares an unchanged-active-set candidate with its incoming state in the same smooth relative-sup promotion merit. A worse candidate cannot replace the incoming state; a changed active set still advances because the two live objectives are not directly comparable. The comparison is scalar and masked inside the existing fixed-trip device loop. A monotone affine solve is bit-identical with the guard on and off in eager and compiled execution, while a constructed warmup that improves the exact sup residual but worsens the promoted merit correctly retains the incoming state.

The public branch-selector rejection is a consequence

For both measured frames, the committed receipt reports finite results, diverted achieved topology, topology-class agreement, current agreement at floating-point precision, and no seed-identity rejection. Its public qualification additionally requires the fixed-point relative residual to be at or below 1×10−6. The measured values 0.0448512 and 0.0811700 fail that condition, so public branch selector found no convergence-qualified diverted root is a consequence of the residual stall rather than an independent topology-class refusal.

Ranked remedies to evaluate

  1. Carry the best-observed iterate across outer trips and do not degrade it during warmup. Implemented as a fixed-shape promoted- merit guard and refuted as the stall remedy: both frames remain at outer q = 1.0000000000000000 for four guarded trips. Direct settled-mask re-evaluation now proves the protected residuals are reachable under the current domains with exactly zero difference. Keep the guard as a no-regression invariant, but do not project convergence from it.
  2. Resume the frozen-mask Newton trajectory across outer trips. Implemented by carrying the terminal state and masking warmup, and refuted as the stall remedy: both frames remain at outer q = 1.0000000000000000 for six trips. The local endpoints remain worse than the retained states, so neither frame can reach tolerance at any finite trip budget.
  3. Carry the full globalization state across unchanged masks. Preserve the merit window, adaptive radius, damping, and current local model with the terminal iterate instead of constructing a fresh inner solve. Cost: high because these fixed-shape carries must cross the outer boundary and enter checkpoint identity. Falsifying evidence: a full-carry continuation still returns outer q = 1 or its live mask changes.
  4. Rebuild the warmup objective so it shares the promoted merit and mask. Cost: high, requiring residual/JVP and conservation revalidation. Falsifying evidence: the warmup increase is required to satisfy a distinct constraint whose violation makes the carried best inadmissible.
  5. Increase the Newton budget only after the reset is removed. At the measured local rates, reaching 1×10−6 from the carried residual would take approximately 528 and 999 uninterrupted accepted iterations. Cost: very high compute. Falsifying evidence: measured contraction degrades before crossing the carried best. Merely adding outer trips is already falsified because their measured rate is exactly one.

Krylov retuning is not ranked as a primary remedy: reductions already sit seven to nine orders of magnitude inside the requested tolerance. A preconditioner change would become relevant only if a guarded, uninterrupted nonlinear trajectory exposed a materially worse linear reduction or a direction-quality failure not present here.
