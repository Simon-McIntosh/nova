The outer-trip search reset discards contracting Newton work

The settled-mask inner solve is neither stationary nor limited by its Krylov direction. It contracts locally, but every outer trip first runs eight warmup sweeps that raise the residual above the best state already carried by the solve. The Newton endpoint remains worse than that carried best, so the outer solve returns exactly zero improvement and repeats.

Adjudication

Acceptance mismatch is ruled out in both frames. Exactly zero candidates lower the live relative-sup residual while failing the merit threshold. The candidates production selects lower both quantities. A local barrier is also ruled out in both frames. The Newton directional derivatives are materially negative, and 20 of 23 frame-179 candidates plus 9 of 23 frame-44 candidates lower the live residual. Basin or model error is present but is not the terminal mechanism. Three frame-179 and fourteen frame-44 candidates predict a merit decrease that the actual nonlinear evaluation does not deliver; nevertheless the backtracking ladder finds accepted descent in both. The frames agree on the operational verdict while differing sharply in model fidelity: frame 44 needs four refusals before its first accepted Newton factor, whereas frame 179 accepts the full step.

Thus none of the three explanations, taken as a terminal cause, fits the observed stall. The deciding fact is that a production-valid promotion exists at each retained state but the outer algorithm never executes a trip from that state without first replacing its search trajectory. If the selected one-step ratios persisted, they would imply approximately 194 further promotions for frame 179 and 10,620 for frame 44 to reach the unchanged 1×10−6 tolerance; the best diagnostic Levenberg ratios imply 19 and 1,154. These are conditional projections, not convergence claims from one step. What the census establishes is reachability: the tolerance is not excluded by a stationary retained state or by the acceptance gate. The next distinguishing measurement is whether repeated no-warmup promotions launched from each newly accepted retained state preserve descent or enter a new basin barrier.

The observed terminal contraction is exactly one after the stated halt in all four arms. The census proved that one useful promotion exists; this chain proves that neither production selection nor the tested per-link damping family supplies a sustained route to tolerance. The next measurement must identify a direction after the link-4/link-5 barrier rather than extend the existing promotion budget.

Ranked remedies to evaluate

  1. Carry the best-observed iterate across outer trips and do not degrade it during warmup. Implemented as a fixed-shape promoted- merit guard and refuted as the stall remedy: both frames remain at outer q = 1.0000000000000000 for four guarded trips. Direct settled-mask re-evaluation now proves the protected residuals are reachable under the current domains with exactly zero difference. Keep the guard as a no-regression invariant, but do not project convergence from it.
  2. Resume the frozen-mask Newton trajectory across outer trips. Implemented by carrying the terminal state and masking warmup, and refuted as the stall remedy: both frames remain at outer q = 1.0000000000000000 for six trips. The local endpoints remain worse than the retained states, so neither frame can reach tolerance at any finite trip budget.
  3. Carry the full globalization state across unchanged masks. Implemented for the merit window, adaptive radius, damping, Krylov baseline, amplification state, and best fallback, and refuted as the stall remedy. Both frames retain zero mask differences and outer q = 1.0000000000000000 across six measured trips. Do not pursue another variation of cross-trip state carry.
  4. Rebuild the warmup objective so it shares the promoted merit and mask. Cost: high, requiring residual/JVP and conservation revalidation. Falsifying evidence: the warmup increase is required to satisfy a distinct constraint whose violation makes the carried best inadmissible.
  5. Increase the Newton budget only after the reset is removed. At the measured local rates, reaching 1×10−6 from the carried residual would take approximately 528 and 999 uninterrupted accepted iterations. Cost: very high compute. Falsifying evidence: measured contraction degrades before crossing the carried best. Merely adding outer trips is already falsified because their measured rate is exactly one.

Krylov retuning is not ranked as a primary remedy: reductions already sit seven to nine orders of magnitude inside the requested tolerance. A preconditioner change would become relevant only if a guarded, uninterrupted nonlinear trajectory exposed a materially worse linear reduction or a direction-quality failure not present here.
