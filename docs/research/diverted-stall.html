Mechanism and repair under measurement

The imported read previously formed a wall candidate only for wall cells in a boolean provisional connected set, setting it to negative infinity otherwise. One graph-edge eligibility change therefore injected a finite wall candidate above the saddle flux and changed the support discontinuously. The isolated prototype now computes the maximum reachable flux level to each wall cell from continuous face capacities and caps a path through the private saddle frame at the saddle level. It keeps the saddle-region geometry available on both sides of the boundary verdict. This copy replaces scripts/prototypes/fast_read/kernel.py::read only inside the diverted-stall measurement process; product code and the imported prototype are unchanged. Its H200 convergence and validity results are pending, so this is a tested diagnosis and a candidate mechanism repair, not yet a qualified two-carrier solve.
