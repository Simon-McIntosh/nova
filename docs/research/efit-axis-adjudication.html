Effect axis adjudication

Evidence for the lead to adjudicate the imas-efit and nova fixed-point disagreements on imas-efit's boundary-level trace records. Every record is rebuilt from its own 65×65 grid with a scipy not-a-knot bicubic spline, checked at imas-efit's recorded axis, and censused exhaustively for stationary points. Both codes' axes and saddles, both boundary contours and every census point are drawn. No verdict on which code is right is stated here.

Method

  • Field. The record's own spline: scipy.interpolate.RectBivariateSpline of its 65×65 grid at k=3, s=0, evaluated through a coefficient-matrix form for the census. Nothing here uses either nova field-swap adapter.
  • Rebuild check. At imas-efit's recorded axis the spline value must equal the record's axis ψ and |∇ψ| must sit at rounding level; both are tabulated per record.
  • Census. Newton on ∇ψ = 0 from a 120×120 seed lattice over the grid box, deduplicated at 10−4 m; points outside the grid box are dropped, where the spline extrapolates. Each point's Hessian eigenvalues give its type. A point lying on one side of a code's boundary contour is nearer that code's axis in ψ than the boundary level.
  • Adapter A axis rule. The nova axis comes from nova's own census: adapter A calls nova.equilibrium.topology.read on the spline field (crosscheck.py), so its axis is nova's topology read, unanchored to the record.
  • Adapter B axis rule. The record axis is a seed only (adapter.py): adapter B seeds a bracket census with (record.axis_r, record.axis_z), polishes it, and picks the stationary point nearest the record axis, reporting reading.axis_flux. Its exact match with imas-efit's axis level is therefore by construction. Adapter B records no axis position, so its axis is identified among the census points as the one whose ψ equals its recorded axis_level; that match is stated in each panel's table.
  • Absent fixed points. Adapter A reports NaN geometry where nova admits no stationary point; those records carry no adapter A mark, and their geometry columns read –. Every one of them carries adapter A's own note in the per-record table.
