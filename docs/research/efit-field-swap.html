Reading imas-efit boundary trace records with nova's boundary read

Measured landing fragment

Question and authority

The lead chose a cross-check, not a convergence of rules: nova reads each of imas-efit's fields with its own topology rule, and neither code's rule is changed to match the other. The source is imas-efit's 12 paired BOUNDARY_LEVEL_TRACE 2 records in pairs.tsv, plus four crossing records. Their stored verdicts came from imas-efit's discontinuous saddle rule, which that project is replacing with an escape-level rule. This table reports the stored verdicts as recorded; it does not claim they are that project's eventual replacement decisions.

Field and geometry adapter

The trace codec follows imas-efit's read_boundary_trace_record in src/EFIT/boundary_level_trace.f90. The field adapter fits the same not-a-knot bicubic spline through nova's owned nova.linalg.tensor_spline.fit_tensor_spline; it books no plasma current and calls no Biot-Savart operator. An independent scipy fit reproduced the record grid to at most 6.7e−16 in ψ, including the disputed JT-60SA record. The wrapper's value, gradient and Hessian are tested against scipy at 64 interior points, and the exported file test checks the raw trace lines independently of the codec.

Nova's read receives a square lattice of 16, 24 or 32 samples per axis inside the record wall. This substituted geometry is an approximation: it is not nova's production hex or Delaunay carrier. For wide walls, the adapter retries the contour mesh with a fitted vertex capacity when nova's own capacity produces no valid vertex; the core defect belongs to the separate contour-capacity repair. Crossing records have no wall-segment array but do contain a four-point polygon, which the adapter uses as their wall and closes at its first point. Every row is parsed and attempted. A row with no admitted saddle reports the census refusal and no invented boundary decision. The trace wall_index splits the wall into separate loops. The unique loop containing the stored axis defines the vessel; contained loops are obstacles, and loops outside that vessel are excluded. The square lattice omits cell centres inside obstacles and passes the vessel and obstacle boundaries as separate wall units, with no connecting edge. Nova's current TopologyGeometry.from_cells calls every supplied unit a vessel, so the wall-contact type at an obstacle is an explicit approximation of this exchange geometry.

All 28 input decisions

The two decision cells name their source: efit is the record's stored decision; nova is the spline-and-lattice read. Both include boundary and wall-contact levels. Each saddle cell gives a level and position where the source provides them; the trace format stores imas-efit's saddle level but no saddle position. The absolute difference is |ψb,efit − ψb,nova|; the relative column divides it by imas-efit's |ψb − ψaxis| span. A dash means nova refused a boundary decision, so no boundary difference is asserted. On refused rows the nova wall-contact level is separately evaluated with scipy on the same spline and selected wall units; on qualified rows that independent evaluation agrees with the sweep's wall level within 2e-14.

What this comparison can and cannot settle

The 28 rows separate a field representation issue from a decision issue. The independent spline and wall controls show that the source field is rebuilt; qualified disagreements are differences in axis or boundary selection on that field under the sampled geometry. The 5 refusals are limitations of the substituted square-lattice census, not evidence that the two codes agree. The four crossing rows use the stored polygon as wall because the segment array is empty. The stored imas-efit rule is discontinuous and being replaced, so this table is a cross-check of its recorded decisions, not a prescription for nova's read or a forecast of the new rule.

Core contour-mesh vertex capacity remains a separate repair. The receipt's root census and this field-swap adapter are measurement instruments; no nova equilibrium source or imas-efit source is changed here.
