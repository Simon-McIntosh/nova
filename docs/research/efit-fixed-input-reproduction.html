Convergence quality and topology localization move inversely

On MAST 22086/43, Nova’s stride-1 cold solve with EFIT’s own p-prime, FF-prime and 101 fitted circuit currents converges to 9.678145996015393 × 10−12 in six active-set trips. Yet that converged state has no RMS-eligible closed boundary and selects a saddle 0.15065971085750432 m from EFIT’s X-point. With Nova’s inference inputs on the same stride-1 grid, the solve stalls at 9.001840977909396 × 10−4 while placing the saddle only 0.008463463498677284 m from EFIT’s X-point. Convergence quality tracks the input family; topology localization quality tracks it inversely.

Measured tension, not a class flip. The converged fixed-input state and EFIT are both classified diverted, and label agreement is true. The coarse class therefore does not explain the absent closed branch or 15.1 cm saddle displacement. The inference-input saddle is much closer, but at 8.463 mm it still exceeds the locked 7.578 mm quarter-width gate by 0.885 mm.

The fixed point is displaced; the cause is not yet separated

EFIT’s own MAST map scores a zero-iteration relative residual of 0.011614703393987796 under Nova’s stride-1 operator. Holding EFIT’s fitted currents and extracted flux functions fixed, Nova’s cold solve reaches 9.678145996015393 × 10−12. Because the solved residual lies far below the EFIT-map residual, Nova’s fixed point under those transported EFIT inputs is genuinely displaced from the published EFIT solution; this is not merely failure to converge back to the source map.

The record does not assign that displacement to one cause. Three candidates remain unseparated: Nova’s discretisation, transport or representation of the EFIT inputs, and slack in EFIT’s own published self-consistency. A discriminating measurement must evaluate the operator terms on one identical native grid in a three-way ledger: EFIT’s own discretisation with its raw fitted sources, Nova’s operator with those same raw sources, and Nova’s operator after the current extraction and transport path. Term-by-term differences in the elliptic, pressure and toroidal-field contributions would then separate operator error, input transport and source self-consistency.

DIII-D cold starts were lost to cancellation

Job 1260050 ran for 1-02:57:29 on rigel. sacct records CANCELLED by 39486, and UID 39486 resolves to job owner mcintos; the lead identifies this as an administrative cancellation at 2026-09-02 11:49:30. The log shows COLD_SOLVE_START machine=DIII-D stride=1, inner-Newton iterations 0 and 1, then cancellation. It contains no DIII-D completion marker, no DIII-D terminal receipt and no final cohort receipt. The shell trap wrote EXIT_MARKER=0, but scheduler cancellation is the terminal truth.

No DIII-D cold-start convergence, residual, topology or boundary claim is made. Any future DIII-D run still carries the shipped 24-conductor inference currents, not EFIT’s fitted-current set, which is absent from the declared parquet schema. Its boundary distance would therefore remain a bounded-below indicator rather than a clean fixed-input reproduction.

Lead decision required: whether to fund a DIII-D relaunch. The cost basis is already material: the completed MAST stride-1 solve alone took 75,791.539 s, and its one-time stride-1 prescribed-response carrier build took 6,448.064 s, for 82,239.603 s (22.844 h) before the DIII-D cold start could finish. This document records the gap; it does not silently schedule or impute the missing result.

Root-cause input to the north-star convergence work

This research record informs the S19 north-star solver-convergence-regression plan. That plan tracks the same MAST 22086/43 converged-to-stagnated flip across solver and backend conditions. The fixed-input inversion here is a root-cause input: it demonstrates independently that residual convergence and EFIT topology localization can move in opposite directions even on one machine, one arm and one declared grid.
