Escape-level maximin certificates

These three same-axis comparisons test the boundary rule on each trace record's own field. The strict full-precision claims are different from their rounded display values. Nova's MAST-U contact is blocked at its claimed level. On both synthetic rows nova understates the reach to the specified wall point, but that point's scipy-spline value is slightly below imas-efit's full-precision claim. A path cannot attain a level higher than its own endpoint. The delivered imas-efit paths also stop at different wall points and each dips below its claimed level. These facts settle which exact claims fail without selecting a winner by visual judgement.

Independent spline and bounds

The BOUNDARY_LEVEL_TRACE 2 grid is rebuilt with scipy RectBivariateSpline(kx=ky=3,s=0), as in the axis-adjudication rebuild. Every reported path and cut value is polarity*psi on that spline. A sampled 800-by-800 four-neighbour graph inside the recorded wall ring proposes paths; its Kruskal merge level is only a sampled estimate. Exact axes and exact targets are joined to the proposed cell sequence by evaluated straight segments. Every path segment is split at spline knots. A bicubic restricted to one such segment is degree at most six; seven evaluations yield Bernstein coefficients whose minimum and maximum enclose the segment. The numerical enclosure includes an absolute 1e-12 arithmetic pad. Independent midpoint residuals were at most 1.4e-15. Text exports preserve 17-digit coordinates and every cell-path vertex, with intermediate samples at no more than half the graph diagonal step. The 800-grid step is about 5.5 mm on MAST-U and 2.6 mm on the synthetic records; it is not an error bound on the continuous maximin.

A sublevel cut is verified continuously by the same Bernstein upper bound. On MAST-U, downhill branches from the spline saddle reach the vessel wall on both sides. Splitting the recorded wall polygon by that wall-to-wall curve puts the axis and nova's contact in different pieces. On each synthetic row the exact wall endpoint is below imas-efit's full-precision level; a certified closed square around it separates the endpoint from the axis. Those squares are only about 4.7e-12 m in radius, so they expose a full-precision endpoint mismatch rather than a macroscopic change of boundary topology.

Nova's merged read and the admission step

The field-swap adapter presents the same trace grid through TraceSplineField; the merged fast_map_production.kernel.read is called with roots polished from that field. The MAST-U result reproduces the recorded limited decision and exact contact: boundary flux 0.020530973894263525 at (0.3327971398830414,1.3030896186828613); its saddle candidate is 0.019652442525099224. The read's cell-membership step is kernel.read's wall_cell = argmax(_inside_cells(...)), followed by _wall_path_ceiling and _wall_effective_level. The graph treats an admitted cell as connected through its edge peaks. The exact contact maps to cell 0, whose computed path ceiling is 0.02192981135271742 and whose path weight is 1.0; the wall score therefore remains its tangency 0.020530973894263525. That cell-level passage does not establish a continuous path through the bicubic field inside a saddle-bearing cell; the independent wall-to-wall cut shows the contact is disconnected at the chosen level. The adapter uses a local compatibility wrapper for the census's sampled jet interface; the imported field-swap and merged read implementations are not modified.

Both synthetic records are sampled from the same grid used by the adapter. At 963 common points per row, including the exact axis and target, nova's imported tensor spline and the independent scipy rebuild differ by at most 2.45e-15; at the two exact synthetic targets the differences are below 2.5e-17. At all 22,001 delivered imas-efit path samples per synthetic row, the path file values and independently rebuilt scipy values differ by at most 8.9e-16. The axis-adjudication gradient warning at the recorded synthetic axes is an axis metadata issue, not evidence that the two readers used different input grids. The remaining endpoint gaps are stated as measured gaps; no cause is assigned from their size alone.
