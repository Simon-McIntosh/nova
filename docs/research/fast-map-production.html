Fast fixed-shape map on the diverted carrier

The corrected fixed-shape read and single-owner booking descend the diverted carrier's scaled moment residual monotonically over ten dense corrections but do not converge it: the residual moves from 1.7989 to 1.0209 and the eleventh correction is rejected, so no accept state is reached at 282 nodes. The stall is therefore not the boolean wall eligibility or the mixed cell booking alone, and the unfixed-pair control shows the reverse: the uncorrected pair stalls 380 times lower, at 2.6846e-03, and books the X-point cell the corrected pair does not.

Method and provenance

The corrected pair lives in scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production. read.py carries a corrected copy of scripts.prototypes.fast_read.kernel.read: the wall candidate keeps the same continuous edge-peak wall flux and maximises it over every wall edge with no eligibility gate. program.py carries a corrected copy of scripts.prototypes.fast_read.program.map_moments: one owner selects each cell's representation and takes both its membership and its booked moments from that choice. Nothing under nova/ is changed.

Carriers are opened from the analytic oracle machine store by (analytic_case, requested_cells, builder). The certified store holds only the 5000-cell carrier, so build_machines.py writes each ladder carrier and its machine-keyed exterior into the store the lane is pointed at; a carrier requested for k cells has k+32 hex nodes on the diverted case. Every row runs on one H200 under the gpu_0003_grpA reservation as a single job with the rungs as rows inside the allocation, binary64 enabled before any array is constructed, and one temporary directory per job removed on exit.

Each carrier is built once and re-read from the store by later rows, which is what makes a ladder row cheap to repeat. The build wall is the measured cost of the carrier itself, not of the map.

What the run establishes, and what it does not

The boundary class stays 0 and the booked saddle-cell count stays 0 through every accepted correction, so at this carrier and resolution the map never enters the X-point booking branch the single-owner change governs. The descent is slow for a different reason than the two mechanisms this node corrected, and the scale of the correction needed is large: the residual starts near 1.8 where the uncorrected pair starts at 4.696e-03, and the uncorrected pair stalls at the 0.0027 the earlier fused-convergence study reported.

Consequently this node does not claim the diverted carrier converges. The corrected pair removes a specific discontinuity and makes each cell's membership and moments consistent; it does not by itself produce a converged diverted state, and that is a separate and larger defect than the one the spec named. The unfixed-pair control re-runs the original read and booking at the same carrier and resolution, and it is the sharper result. It stalls after ten corrections exactly as the corrected pair does, but at a scaled sup residual of 2.684581554642e-03 against the corrected pair's 1.0208587667876892 — 380 times lower — with boundary class 1 and one booked saddle cell at every accepted correction, against class 0 and no saddle cell for the corrected pair. So the uncorrected pair does enter the X-point booking branch at this carrier, and it reproduces the 0.0027 stall the earlier fused-convergence study reported.

Restoring the unfixed pair therefore lowers the residual rather than raising it. On this carrier the two mechanisms this node corrected are a regression, not an improvement: the branchless wall candidate and the single-owner booking together suppress the saddle booking that the uncorrected pair performs, and the map stalls an order of magnitude and a half higher without it. The premise that the boolean wall eligibility was what held the diverted carrier at 0.0027 is not supported by this measurement. The likely mechanism is that maximising the continuous wall level over every wall edge admits a wall level the eligibility gate excluded, which moves the booked current and the representation away from the state that converges; isolating that is the next measurement, and the corrected pair must not be treated as an improvement on this evidence.

Step-scale scan at the stalled iterate

The probe re-runs the program at the last accepted iterate with a dense Jacobian. The linear solve is exact — its relative residual is 8.9e-14 — and JTr is not small: the gradient-to-(‖J‖‖r‖) ratio is 0.407, so the iterate is not a stationary point of the map. A finite step along the Newton direction nevertheless raises the scaled residual from 1.020859 to 27.8865, and the largest residual of the whole scan sits at step 0.178 with the X-point cell's membership at 1.

The Taylor rows at this iterate are not a clean power law: the two-norm remainder is nan at step 1e-2 on the Newton direction and at step 1e-3 on the random direction, and only reaches 2.96e-10 and 6.34e-14 at step 1e-7. A remainder that is undefined at 1e-2 and finite at 1e-7 is a further sign that the map is not differentiable over that interval, which is why the spec's Taylor requirement is stated at a converged state.

Limited cold solve at 289 nodes

The same corrected pair converges the limited carrier in three dense corrections to a scaled sup residual of 3.996e-15, from a starting residual of 2.315e-03, with the boundary class at 0 and 254 support cells at the terminal state. The map executes in 1.18 ms there. This reproduces the earlier fused-convergence measurement on the same rung in both count and order (three cold corrections, below 4e-15), so the corrected read and booking do not disturb the limited carrier's convergence.

A step-scale scan from the converged limited state (scaled sup residual 3.996e-15, relative gradient 0.032) walks 82 steps along the Newton direction from step 0 to step 1 without leaving roundoff: the residual spans 3.996e-15 to 4.898e-15, the largest excursion is 9.0e-16, and the X-point cell's membership stays at 1 across every step. This is the continuity the divergence at the diverted carrier lacks, and it is measured at a converged state as the spec requires. Only the Newton direction was scanned; the two random directions the spec names are unmeasured.

Which mechanism causes the regression

The corrected read carries three deviations from the original, not the two the spec names: the wall candidate taken over every wall edge with no eligibility gate, the separated ordinary and normal-form fractions, and a normal-form cell set conditioned on a live saddle rather than on a diverted state. Two independent switches measure each separately: --wall-gate restores the boolean eligibility of the wall candidate and leaves the booking corrected; --saddle-gate restores the diverted-state condition on the normal-form cell set and leaves the wall candidate branchless.

Restoring the wall eligibility alone moves the reference residual from 1.7989604 to 4.696461790766224e-03, agreeing with the uncorrected pair to ten significant figures, and moves the stall from 1.0209 to 2.6898e-03 in the same boundary class with the same booked saddle cell. The single-owner booking is then worth the remaining 0.2 per cent of the stall, 2.6898e-03 against 2.6846e-03.

The saddle condition is measured the same way and is not the mechanism. With the wall candidate left branchless and only the normal-form cell set restored to the diverted condition, the reference residual is unchanged at 1.798960437380231 and the stall moves from 1.0209 to 9.3501e-01 — 8.4 per cent, two orders of magnitude short of the wall gate's effect — and the trajectory never leaves boundary class 0 and never books a saddle cell. So the normal-form set condition is a second-order term here; it cannot re-enter the X-point booking branch while the wall level is wrong. The wall candidate is the regression: maximising the edge-peak wall flux over every wall edge admits a wall level the eligibility gate excluded, and that level reads the diverted carrier as limiter-limited from the reference state onward. The two changes the spec asked for are not equally implicated, and the eligibility is load-bearing on this carrier rather than the defect it was taken for.
