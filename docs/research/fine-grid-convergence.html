Method and provenance

The fixed-shape continuous map is imported from scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production without editing it. The derivative instrument in scripts/prototypes/fine_grid_convergence builds the same certified diverted carrier, reference moments, coupling rows and moment scales as that route. It reads the prior accepted terminal moments, evaluates the current map again, linearizes that map, solves for a Newton direction, and measures Taylor remainders in the current, radial-moment and vertical-moment blocks along the Newton direction and three independently seeded random directions. The saddle probe compares the normal-form and ordinary-cut area fractions and moments at and next to the X-point, with one-sided finite differences. Binary64 is configured before any JAX array is constructed, the probe asserts the GPU backend, and each H200 log prints the imported module path, worktree, revision, command and resolved working directory.

This finite-step change is the bracket for the next live booking scan.

H200 job 1286884 then measured both factors on every graph edge at the same live states and completed with EXIT=0. The cell 50–52 edge is the only edge whose strength falls by more than 0.5. Its flux margin stays positive at about 4.2167 × 10−5, and its flux weight stays 1. Its saddle-sector weight, and therefore its frame weight, changes from 1 to 0; the near-saddle factor stays 1. The original frame ramp used a fixed signed-distance width of the larger of 10−7 and 10−4 times the median cell pitch around an edge-midpoint sector test. A small saddle displacement crosses that narrow ramp and removes two connected cells from the map. This is a continuous but geometrically unresolved handoff, rather than a change of X-point booking or flux passage. component-scan.json contains the edge rows and component-scan.log records the successful run.

The prototype correction replaces that frame width with the sampled change of the saddle-sector signed distances across the same edge. This gives the edge strength a physical geometric scale as the sector boundary moves across it; the flux factor, wall candidate, booking rule and boundary-candidate computation retain their accepted forms. The corrected cold solves and original-map control are running in H200 job 1286885.
