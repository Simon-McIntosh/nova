Fine-grid diverted convergence

The accepted continuous map converges the 282-cell diverted carrier to 1.25 × 10−13, while its 550- and 2,098-cell cold solves stop at 1.55 × 10−3 and 3.63 × 10−3. A live scan traced the 2,098-cell finite-step change to the cell 50–52 saddle-sector frame. A one-sided geometric ramp then converged the 2,098-cell cold solve to 6.14 × 10−13, but left the 550-cell solve stalled and failed the 282-cell analytic-map guard.

Method and provenance

The fixed-shape continuous map is imported from scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production without editing it. The derivative instrument in scripts/prototypes/fine_grid_convergence builds the same certified diverted carrier, reference moments, coupling rows and moment scales as that route. It reads the prior accepted terminal moments, evaluates the current map again, linearizes that map, solves for a Newton direction, and measures Taylor remainders in the current, radial-moment and vertical-moment blocks along the Newton direction and three independently seeded random directions. The saddle probe compares the normal-form and ordinary-cut area fractions and moments at and next to the X-point, with one-sided finite differences. Binary64 is configured before any JAX array is constructed, the probe asserts the GPU backend, and each H200 log prints the imported module path, worktree, revision, command and resolved working directory.

The read-only 550- and 2,098-cell machine cache used by the accepted fast-map run is /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261014T174345284407-lean-map/cache. The earlier support cache named in the node brief contains a 5,000-cell machine but no 550- or 2,098-cell machine; a fresh attempt against it failed during input loading and supplies no numerical evidence. The predecessor terminal receipts and their full H200 logs are under /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261015T160647356198-fast-map-production.
