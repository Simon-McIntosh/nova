Fine-grid diverted convergence

The accepted continuous map converges the 282-cell diverted carrier to 1.25 × 10−13, while its 550- and 2,098-cell cold solves stop at 1.55 × 10−3 and 3.63 × 10−3. A live scan traced the 2,098-cell finite-step change to the cell 50–52 saddle-sector frame. A one-sided geometric frame ramp preserves analytic-map fidelity at both fine sizes but fails it badly at 282 cells; its fine cold solves are running.

Method and provenance

The fixed-shape continuous map is imported from scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production without editing it. The derivative instrument in scripts/prototypes/fine_grid_convergence builds the same certified diverted carrier, reference moments, coupling rows and moment scales as that route. It reads the prior accepted terminal moments, evaluates the current map again, linearizes that map, solves for a Newton direction, and measures Taylor remainders in the current, radial-moment and vertical-moment blocks along the Newton direction and three independently seeded random directions. The saddle probe compares the normal-form and ordinary-cut area fractions and moments at and next to the X-point, with one-sided finite differences. Binary64 is configured before any JAX array is constructed, the probe asserts the GPU backend, and each H200 log prints the imported module path, worktree, revision, command and resolved working directory.

The read-only 550- and 2,098-cell machine cache used by the accepted fast-map run is /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261014T174345284407-lean-map/cache. The earlier support cache named in the node brief contains a 5,000-cell machine but no 550- or 2,098-cell machine; a fresh attempt against it failed during input loading and supplies no numerical evidence. The predecessor terminal receipts and their full H200 logs are under /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261015T160647356198-fast-map-production. At 550 cells, reducing the moment step from 10−4 to 10−5 reduces the Newton-direction remainder by approximately 100 in all three moment blocks: slopes 2.000, 2.000, and 2.000. The three seeded random directions give block slopes between 1.998 and 2.006 over the same decade. The local JVP therefore matches the map in these measured directions. At 2,098 cells, all three seeded random directions give slopes 2.001–2.011. The Newton direction has a small-step current-block remainder of 1.46 × 10−10 at 10−4, close to the approximately 10−11 evaluation floor at smaller steps; its slope over that decade alone is not evidence of a first-order derivative. At step 10−3, however, the current-block remainder is 1.30945 and its largest term is cell 50, while the remainder at 10−4 is 1.46 × 10−10. This finite-step change is the bracket for the next live booking scan.
