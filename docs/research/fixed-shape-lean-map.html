Point-booking bias and correction

The earlier forward-solve options measurement reported a diverted hard-centroid map error near 0.42 from about 2,000 to 10,000 cells and a limited plateau near 0.0036. The independent replay here reproduced 0.418807 at 2,098 diverted cells and 0.409206 at 5,158 cells, using the same analytic field and exact-support exterior. Its exact booked control was 1.77 × 10−5 and 4.36 × 10−6, respectively, so the comparison resolves a current-image signal rather than a uniform or zero field.

The point mask counts a selected cut cell as a whole source and can select detached private-flux cells below the saddle. At 2,098 diverted cells it selected 419 partial cells and 21 cells with zero connected membership; the latter held 1.22% of its unnormalised point current. The correction books only connected full cells at their centroid and reuses exact first moments for every partial cell, then normalises once to the same current. It reduces diverted error by factors of 226 and 716 at the two measured sizes. The separate controls show which mistake dominates: retaining the private cells while correcting cuts leaves errors 0.41436 and 0.41439, whereas connected point booking leaves 0.08785 and 0.03572. Thus detached private flux causes the persistent plateau, while cut area and moments account for the remaining refinement error. H200 job 1285581 carries these controls.

Fixed-shape representation and analytic-state map ladder

Each regular interior cell has a fixed 18-point triangle-fan Gauss rule. Irregular wall cells and connected partial cells go through one fixed-capacity exact booking batch. A cell source contributes its current and two first moments to a dense precomputed values map at every interior Gauss point. The full six-component nodal jet map is stored, while a machine-sized row gather supplies the active boundary band, wall events and fixed-point windows. A newly cut cell without its nodal rows or a cut count above capacity returns a typed refusal. The 2,105-cell limited quadrature probe reserved 1,075 cut slots for 882 selected cells and returned the refusal code when capacity was deliberately set to one; the compact batch agreed cellwise with the full exact booking.

One current per full interior cell is less accurate than carrying first moments. At 2,098 diverted cells the one-current analytic-state error was 1.824 × 10−3, versus 1.766 × 10−5 with two first moments; at 5,158 it was 5.633 × 10−4 versus 4.364 × 10−6. Thus one current alone can clear the basic 10−2 map ceiling at this density, but misses the tenfold margin at 2,098 and is about two orders of magnitude less faithful. Warm fixed-capacity cut booking took 26.6–98.6 ms on these four coarse and certified-size rows, so it is already far above the 1 ms full-solve target. H200 job 1285582 supplies these current and first-moment comparisons and deliberate over-capacity refusals.

The support read and exact cut booking dominate. Their isolated walls sum to 10.44 ms, close to the 10.18 ms fused call; the two matvec walls sum to 0.743 ms and their combined physical byte floor is 0.0172 ms. Even a perfect matrix reader would leave a multi-millisecond support-and-booking path at this cell count. These measured stage calls use the same nonzero analytic-state moments, fixed row capacities and double precision as the terminal solve.

The full-column dense jacfwd allocated 132.73 GiB and failed on the first 289-cell row (job 1285579). Linearising once and mapping eight tangent columns at a time completed both cold solves, but its cold compile walls exceed the 60 s clause and its solve walls exceed the 1 ms goal by four orders of magnitude. The warm cached factor is faster yet neither neighbouring slice reaches the strict 10−12 residual; the third diverted correction regressed from 1.55 × 10−12 to 2.21 × 10−11. No tested cell count has passed the joint §6 acceptance. The 282/289 rung is the smallest tested count to clear the tenfold analytic-state map margin and the cold residual/root/flux clauses; constraint rows and executable size have not been qualified in this prototype.

Support-route continuity and residual floor

A fixed-shape vertical-field sweep on the 282-cell diverted carrier computed both X-point and wall-event candidate levels on every call (H200 job 1285605). Its unperturbed analytic state was valid, but only 1 of 41 samples over ±0.2 flux-span-per-pitch slope was valid. The apparent limited-to-diverted class change occurred between refused states: both sides had invalid roots or booking status 2. Their boundary levels differed by 0.614 of the analytic flux span. This is a failure to exhibit an accepted continuous transition, rather than evidence for the size of a jump on an accepted path.

One cell switched between full-cell Gauss and exact-band routes in the same refused region. Its raw current differed by 0.006998 of total current across a tightly bisected bracket; one side lacked required band rows despite the cell lying in a frozen two-neighbour ring of 240 cells. Central differences of the full mapped moments at normalized steps 10−3, 10−4 and 10−5 grew from 1.50 × 105 to 1.64 × 107 in relative-sup response, with at least one refused side at every step. The current implementation therefore has not shown a below-10−12 route jump or a differentiable crossing. A wall-localized smooth perturbation is queued to seek a qualified transition on the same fixed carrier.

At the saved diverted terminal, a separate compiled mixed-booking read gave residual 1.55 × 10−12; repeating it was bit-identical, and a width-one vmap changed it by less than 9.71 × 10−16. Replacing the mixed interior/cut booking with full exact booking changed the scaled equation by 5.33 × 10−8. The earlier 1.34 × 10−12 plateau was not an immutable arithmetic floor: a scalar-consistent line search reached 7.56 × 10−13 in job 1285598. These controls rule out repeated-call noise and width-one batching as the immediate floor, while leaving reconstruction and active-set sensitivity as measured risks; they do not isolate roundoff in cut booking as the cause.

Method and provenance

The prototype lives under scripts/prototypes/lean_map and changes no product module. It configures double precision before constructing JAX arrays, records nova.__file__ and its worktree in each job log, and runs rows in fresh processes on one reserved H200 per measurement. Coarse certificate carriers and exteriors are built with NOVA_ANALYTIC_ORACLE_BUILDER=device inside this run's cache, leaving the certified caches untouched. The bias replay compares a known nonuniform analytic field to an exact booked control before interpreting a small error as fidelity. Map-only walls are synchronised warm calls and are never added to separately measured solve walls.

The analytic carriers, point-bias controls, quadrature rows and dense-map ladder are written under /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261014T174345284407-lean-map. The limited and diverted 250-cell carriers are device-built and realised as 289 and 282 cells.
