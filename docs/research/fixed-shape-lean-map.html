The hard-centroid bias comes from booking support as a binary point decision. Restricting the bulk to connected full cells and retaining exact cut-cell moments restores refinement. A fixed-shape values and jet map is being measured against the joint acceptance.

Point-booking bias and correction

The earlier forward-solve options measurement reported a diverted hard-centroid map error near 0.42 from about 2,000 to 10,000 cells and a limited plateau near 0.0036. The independent replay here reproduced 0.418807 at 2,098 diverted cells and 0.409206 at 5,158 cells, using the same analytic field and exact-support exterior. Its exact booked control was 1.77 × 10−5 and 4.36 × 10−6, respectively, so the comparison resolves a current-image signal rather than a uniform or zero field.

The point mask counts a selected cut cell as a whole source and can select detached private-flux cells below the saddle. At 2,098 diverted cells it selected 419 partial cells and 21 cells with zero connected membership; the latter held 1.22% of its unnormalised point current. The correction books only connected full cells at their centroid and reuses exact first moments for every partial cell, then normalises once to the same current. It reduces diverted error by factors of 226 and 716 at the two measured sizes. This is a diagnosis of the combined area, component and first-moment error. A separate control is queued to isolate the private-cell contribution from the cut-cell contribution.

Fixed-shape representation and early map rows

Each regular interior cell has a fixed 18-point triangle-fan Gauss rule. Irregular wall cells and connected partial cells go through one fixed-capacity exact booking batch. A cell source contributes its current and two first moments to a dense precomputed values map at every interior Gauss point. The full six-component nodal jet map is stored, while a machine-sized row gather supplies the active boundary band, wall events and fixed-point windows. A newly cut cell without its nodal rows or a cut count above capacity returns a typed refusal. The 2,105-cell limited quadrature probe reserved 1,075 cut slots for 882 selected cells and returned the refusal code when capacity was deliberately set to one; the compact batch agreed cellwise with the full exact booking.

Method and provenance

The prototype lives under scripts/prototypes/lean_map and changes no product module. It configures double precision before constructing JAX arrays, records nova.__file__ and its worktree in each job log, and runs rows in fresh processes on one reserved H200 per measurement. Coarse certificate carriers and exteriors are built with NOVA_ANALYTIC_ORACLE_BUILDER=device inside this run's cache, leaving the certified caches untouched. The bias replay compares a known nonuniform analytic field to an exact booked control before interpreting a small error as fidelity. Map-only walls are synchronised warm calls and are never added to separately measured solve walls.

The analytic carriers, point-bias controls, quadrature rows and dense-map ladder are written under /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261014T174345284407-lean-map.
