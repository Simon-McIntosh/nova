Forward accuracy reuse map

The four forward-accuracy plans do not start from an empty tree. This inventory is shaped by the capability each plan needs, not by filenames. Every row names the callable machinery, its proof surface or receipt, and whether it can be reused whole, reused only as a component, or must be rejected because it measures a different quantity. The central seam is deliberate: exact-reference accuracy, EFIT label inconsistency, nonlinear convergence, and active-set trip cost are four different claims.

Absolute norms and convergence order against exact references

The production certificate needs full converged solves, absolute and normalized field errors for ψ, ∇ψ, and Hessian, boundary-band and whole-domain partitions, geometric critical-point errors, and a fitted order over at least three resolutions. Existing basis-fit and one-map receipts are instruments, not substitutes for that full-solve certificate.

Fixed-point stall remedies near a unit outer-map eigenvalue

A near-unit eigenvalue calls for acceleration or a change in constrained map, not merely more relaxed Picard steps. The current solver already contains most remedies. The important negative finding is that no separate deflation implementation was found; introducing one would be new machinery and must justify why the existing exact-tangent and continuation routes are inadequate.

Reuse decisions

  • Extend, do not replace: use analytic_operator_ladder.py for resolution/order aggregation, but feed it full-solve ψ/gradient/Hessian and geometry errors from the exact references.
  • Compose two independent scores: retain the existing DIII-D and MAST solve-versus-label comparators and place the observed EFIT self-consistency floor beside each; never subtract the floor into a single opaque number.
  • Route remedies through one solver authority: compare Anderson depth, exact-tangent GMRES depth/cap, pins and continuation through ForwardProfile.solve. Deflation is absent and therefore requires a demonstrated gap before implementation.
  • Extend the continuation seam for trip reuse: mask identity already defines when Newton/globalization state can persist. Keep fixed compiled extent, own-mask acceptance, best-iterate retention, and all seven named referees as non-negotiable gates.
