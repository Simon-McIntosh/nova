Fused moment correction convergence

On the 289-cell limited carrier, a dense moment Jacobian reaches a scaled residual of 2.98 × 10−15 in three corrections; four-vector Krylov needs fourteen. The 282-cell diverted carrier retains a saddle-region stall: a damped dense step lowers the residual from 0.004696 to 0.002685, then finds no decreasing trial. This is an accepted-iterate result, not a converged forward state.

Method and provenance

The prototype in scripts/prototypes/fused_convergence imports the committed fixed-shape read and moment map. It does not change the product solver. Both carriers use the stored 250-rung certificate machines, binary64 enabled before array construction, and their original analytic field and current profile. The limited carrier has 289 cells and the diverted carrier 282. The one-step probes form the residual and jax.linearize factor once at a starting state; short Krylov, GMRES and dense LU apply that same linearized map. The dense matrix consists of the factor applied to identity columns in batches, so its linear residual can check the complete Jacobian rather than only a Krylov subspace.

All numerical rows ran on the reserved H200 with XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_PREALLOCATE=false. Each log prints the imported nova and measurement-module paths, the worktree, revision and command. The run directory is /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261015T115843362666-fused-convergence; its JSON receipts hold every accepted residual, factor wall and terminal moments. The root derivative experiment changes the prototype's imported cubic-ray tangent during that run only; it does not modify nova/.

Linear correction and cold histories

The prior four-vector outcome was reproduced by running the map again. Its limited predecessor reaches 6.715 × 10−13 in fourteen corrections and the diverted cold state stops at 0.00342278 after the first accepted correction. The dense limited step reaches 6.673 × 10−6, 5.704 × 10−12 and 2.981 × 10−15 on its three accepted corrections. Dense linear residuals are between 9.55 × 10−16 and 2.23 × 10−15; the Krylov linear residual stays near 0.03 to 0.32, identifying the limited cold iteration count as an incomplete linear solve.

At the first dense limited correction, Python-side linearization and factor construction took 84.47 s, batched factor compilation 10.52 s, matrix construction and LU 10.75 s, and LU application 0.679 ms. These are separate host-observed components, not the wall of one compiled correction. Subsequent dense factor builds took about 7.1 s each. The compiled map was 1,343,052 B; its 1.73 s compile here reused a persistent cache and is not a cold-compile claim.

Diverted derivative and residual carrier

The diverted centre has scaled residual 0.004696. The one-sided current-block Taylor remainder falls from 2.553 × 10−5 at perturbation 10−3 to 2.566 × 10−7 at 10−4 and 2.341 × 10−9 at 10−5, approximately the quadratic slope expected of a valid tangent. Below that, binary64 cancellation contaminates the finite difference. Replacing the cubic-ray closed-form tangent with the simple-root implicit derivative changes the central-difference current-block discrepancy by less than 0.1% at 10−4 and 10−5; those discrepancies are about 1.27 × 10−5. This experiment does not identify the ray-root tangent as the cause of the diverted stall.

Damped dense corrections reduce the diverted residual to 0.002685 after nine accepted steps, with line-search scales falling from 0.25 to 0.000488. The dominant entry remains cell 71's current block, with a radial contribution about 0.000776 and a vertical contribution about 0.000241 at that stage. It is one of the selected normal-form saddle cells, together with cells 7, 8 and 9. The first nine accepted states retain the same 179 support-cell identities, the same saddle-cell identities and diverted boundary class; successive maximum membership changes decrease with the accepted step size. The tenth dense step finds no decreasing trial within twelve halvings. A rejected trial changes class and support, so the terminal state must remain the ninth accepted state.

Warm neighbour and timing qualification

The live four-vector control starts from the converged limited predecessor at 6.715 × 10−13, applies the prescribed vertical-field perturbation and reruns the map. Its first correction ends at 3.920 × 10−9; a second reaches 1.837 × 10−10. Both are above 10−12, reproducing the warm stall. On this Python-side diagnostic the first warm linearization took 79.74 s, factor compilation 1.77 s and Krylov application 3.11 s, so its wall cannot qualify the 0.90 ms compiled warm-slice condition.

Dense LU from the same converged predecessor reduces the perturbed neighbour to 1.875 × 10−11 after one correction and 4.447 × 10−15 after two. Reusing the predecessor's dense factor as a chord gives 8.660 × 10−12 and 3.544 × 10−15. Thus two corrections meet the residual target in both forms, while one does not. Dense refactorization took 7.63 and 6.95 s on the two warm corrections; LU application took 0.627 and 7.58 ms in this host-ledger run. Building the predecessor factor for the chord took 10.33 s; its compiled factor-apply program was 7.362 MB, compiled in 10.82 s, and its first and second observed applications took 146 and 3.08 ms. The timings include host dispatch, and neither single-state application meets the 0.90 ms condition.
