Fused moment correction convergence

On the 289-cell limited carrier, GMRES and dense LU each reach a scaled moment residual below 4 × 10−15 in three cold corrections; four-vector Krylov needs fourteen. On the 282-cell diverted carrier, accurate linear solves still stall near 0.002685 after nine damped corrections. The diverted state is not converged, so the fused warm-slice and batch walls remain unqualified as a two-carrier forward solve.

Method and provenance

The prototype in scripts/prototypes/fused_convergence imports the committed fixed-shape read and moment map. It does not change the product solver. Both carriers use the stored 250-rung certificate machines, binary64 enabled before array construction, and their original analytic field and current profile. The limited carrier has 289 cells and the diverted carrier 282. The one-step probes form the residual and jax.linearize factor once at a starting state; short Krylov, GMRES and dense LU apply that same linearized map. The dense matrix consists of the factor applied to identity columns in batches, so its linear residual can check the complete Jacobian rather than only a Krylov subspace.

All numerical rows ran on the reserved H200 with XLA_PYTHON_CLIENT_PREALLOCATE=false. Each log prints the imported nova and measurement-module paths, the worktree, revision and command. The run directory is /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261015T115843362666-fused-convergence; its JSON receipts hold every accepted residual, factor wall and terminal moments. The root derivative experiment changes the prototype's imported cubic-ray tangent during that run only; it does not modify nova/.

Diverted derivative and residual carrier

The diverted centre has scaled residual 0.004696. The one-sided current-block Taylor remainder falls from 2.553 × 10−5 at perturbation 10−3 to 2.566 × 10−7 at 10−4 and 2.341 × 10−9 at 10−5, approximately the quadratic slope expected of a valid local tangent. Radial and vertical blocks show the same order. The symmetric probe at 10−3 crosses a geometry change and cannot estimate the local derivative; at 10−6 and below, binary64 cancellation takes over. The central-difference current-block discrepancy near 1.27 × 10−5 at 10−4 and 10−5 therefore does not by itself identify a wrong JVP. Replacing the cubic-ray tangent with an implicit simple-root derivative changes that discrepancy by less than 0.1% and leaves the damped dense terminal residual at 0.00268458155. The ray-root tangent is not the cause of the measured stall.

Damped dense corrections reduce the diverted residual to 0.00268458155 after nine accepted steps, with line-search scales falling from 0.25 to 0.000488. The dominant entry is cell 71's current block at −0.00268458; its radial and vertical blocks are +0.00077641 and −0.00001465. Cells 250, 215, 251 and 104 carry the next largest current-block magnitudes, between 0.001067 and 0.001455; the largest vertical-block magnitude anywhere is 0.00024122. Cell 71 is one of the selected saddle-booking cells, with 7, 8 and 9. Every accepted iterate retains the same 179 support identities, saddle identities and diverted class. Maximum membership movement between accepted iterates shrinks from 0.02227 to 0.0000605, and the two selected null positions move smoothly. The tenth dense and GMRES corrections find no decreasing trial within twelve halvings. Rejected full trials can change class or support, so each terminal panel uses the last accepted state.

The saddle derivative ablation is a live rerun: stop_gradient removes the selected saddle moments from the Jacobian while the nonlinear map still books them. Its line search accepts four corrections and stalls at 0.00404421, worse than 0.00268458 with the term present. The converged count is 0/1 in both runs, so this control does not produce the stipulated lower converged count or a red converged-count row. It establishes that the booking derivative matters to progress, without establishing it as the entire cause of the stall. The live four-vector warm rerun is the independent negative control that fails the 10−12 target after both one and two corrections.

Warm neighbour and timing qualification

The live four-vector control starts from the converged limited predecessor at 6.715 × 10−13, applies the prescribed vertical-field perturbation and reruns the map. Its first correction ends at 3.920 × 10−9; a second reaches 1.837 × 10−10. Both are above 10−12, reproducing the warm stall. The compiled map evaluation is 1.15 ms in that row. Python-side linearization takes 79.74 s and four-vector application 3.11 s; this is a diagnostic path, not a qualified compiled warm-slice wall.

Dense LU from the same converged predecessor reduces the perturbed neighbour to 1.875 × 10−11 after one correction and 4.447 × 10−15 after two. Reusing the predecessor's dense factor as a chord gives 8.660 × 10−12 and 3.544 × 10−15. Thus two corrections meet the residual target in both forms, while one does not. The dense row's compiled map evaluation is 1.23 ms, factor builds take 7.63 and 6.95 s, and LU applications take 0.627 and 7.58 ms. Building the predecessor factor for the chord takes 10.33 s; its compiled factor-apply program is 7.362 MB, compiles in 10.82 s, and its first and second observed applications take 146 and 3.08 ms. These are host-observed components and do not qualify an individual 0.90 ms warm call.

Interpretation for the forward solve

The four-product linear step explains the limited warm failure: accurate GMRES and LU corrections agree and reach the target in two warm corrections. It does not explain the diverted failure. On that carrier the accurate GMRES and LU directions agree to the reported precision, but their full nonlinear trials increase the residual by more than two orders of magnitude. Damping keeps the accepted support, saddle selection and boundary class stable while the step size collapses; the residual remains concentrated at selected saddle cell 71. The Taylor and implicit-root experiments do not establish a wrong local ray-root derivative, and the ablation confirms that the saddle booking derivative affects progress. These observations localize the remaining defect to the nonlinear saddle-region correction and its global step acceptance, without identifying a unique faulty term.

The two-carrier 10−12 convergence condition is unmet. The best diverted cold residual is 0.00268458, about 2.7 × 109 times the target. The limited two-correction batch is a throughput demonstration only; its 289.8 MB executable and the unresolved diverted state prevent qualification against the complete warm-slice and batch conditions.
