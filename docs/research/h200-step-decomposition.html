The 300-slice target is an algorithm problem, not a configuration problem

The matched width-1024 arm measures 0.430199003 s per member-slice, about 15.09% slower than the stale ladder's exact 0.373804407 s. The additive fit assigns 409.533851 s of each batch to the conflated reconciliation, Newton setup and first Krylov action term. That single term consumes 119.98 times the entire 3.413333-second batch budget implied by 300 slices per second. Removing every fitted configurable contribution still leaves a ceiling of only 2.500404 slices per second.

Exact qualification. the operand is a limited_plasma branch under a fixed one-Newton-step budget with the pilot's maximum relative residual of 0.0011089898293892464 and zero stable conditioning receipts, so no figure here is a convergence claim and none of it exercises an X-point or shadow path.

Three decisions from the measurement

  1. Current matched cost regresses. Warmup 2, GMRES 4 at width 1024 measures 440.523780 s per batch, 0.430199003 s per member and 2.324505617 slices/s. The stale committed ladder is 0.373804407 s per member, so the matched current program is 15.09% slower, not faster.
  2. Configuration cannot reach 300 slices/s. At width 1024, 300 slices/s permits 1024 / 300 = 3.413333 s for the entire batch. The 409.533851-second intercept alone is 119.9806 times that budget. It requires algorithm or kernel work on the fixed per-step structure.
  3. The configuration-only ceiling is 2.500404 slices/s. Removing the two fitted warmup sweeps and all three additional GMRES actions leaves 1024 / 409.533851 = 2.500404 slices/s. This is an optimistic fitted counterfactual, not a separately executed program.

Explicit two-trip sensitivity

The replay does not identify a fixable non-firing exit: it shows material progress and continuation eligibility. Nevertheless, the requested upper-bound sensitivity is informative. If an algorithmic change made two trips scientifically sufficient and the entire fitted intercept scaled linearly with trip count, then 409.533851 × 2 / 16 = 51.191731 s. After also deleting the configurable terms, the ceiling would be 1024 / 51.191731 = 20.003230 slices/s. The remaining gap is 300 / 20.003230 = 14.9976×. Thus even the optimistic two-trip counterfactual still needs about a fifteen-fold kernel-level, algorithmic or precision reduction. Trip policy alone cannot close the target.

The vertical-conditioning diagnostic belongs outside throughput timing

At width 32 the diagnostic itself measures 0.003225–0.003352 s per batch, about 0.00010 s per member. The independently compiled inside-minus-outside median is −0.199866 s, which is process drift, not a negative diagnostic cost. The production benchmark already stops its solve timer before branch qualification and conditioning. That is the correct boundary: the receipt is diagnostic work, not part of ForwardProfile.solve_batch execution.
