Research record · analytic and committed-operand measurement

One fixed-shape hex-cell carrier is feasible as a prototype

All four prototype criteria pass. At equal 16-coefficient capacity, the boundary-split carrier lowers boundary-band curvature RMS from 0.267167 to 1.87e-15, the primary six-neighbour scan plus one polish step places the axis within 0.101 mm and the X-point within roundoff, fixed-shape CPU evaluation compiles and batches at widths 1 and 32, and the same-cell flood reproduces all 42 private cells in committed MAST 22086/43 mixed with zero differing cells.

Verdict: FEASIBLE for a production-grade prototype, not a cutover authorization. The result establishes that the representation, null scan, compiled evaluation and selected-saddle flood can share one structured half-offset carrier. It does not establish end-to-end solve speed or candidate-selection parity because the committed MAST cache has no per-cell flux or per-candidate domain labels.

Carrier and interface

The analytic target is a diverted polynomial Solov'ev manufactured field with exact axis at (1.0, 0.36) m, saddle at (1.0, 0.0) m, and a curved Cassini separatrix. It is sampled at 2,009 centroids in a 41 by 49 structured index array. Alternate rows are offset by half a radial cell, while the axial index ring remains the six-neighbour stencil used by nova/biot/fieldnull.py FieldNull.categorize_2d.

Both representations have 16 fitted coefficients. The global carrier is one C2 polynomial spline patch. The split carrier has a shared polynomial trace plus an exterior-only squared level-set term. Because that term and its first derivative vanish at the interface, psi and grad psi match exactly while curvature is free to jump. The zero level set follows the curved boundary continuously through cells; it is not snapped to a row, column, centroid, or straight cut.

The preregistered pass condition is improvement in the two-pitch boundary band. The curvature improvement is 1.43e14-fold. This very large factor is expected for the manufactured target: the split basis contains its exact C1 interface mechanism, while a matched-capacity global C2 patch must smooth the curvature jump. It is a feasibility result, not an expected production error ratio.

Null-identification criterion: pass

The primary identifier is unchanged in mechanism: compare each centroid's flux with the angle-ordered six-cell ring and count sign transitions. Zero transitions identifies extrema; four identifies saddles. The full interior lattice is scanned, so the scanned fraction is 1.0 per null. The scan finds two extrema and one saddle. The upper-axis lattice seed is 5.000 mm from truth and one Newton step on the split fit reduces that to 0.1009 mm. The X seed lies at the exact lattice coordinate and polish leaves a 6.06e-16 m error. The spline therefore polishes candidates; it does not replace the sign-change census.

Compiled batchability criterion: pass on CPU

No accelerator is used. JAX reports the CPU backend. Fit is one fixed 2,009 by 16 least-squares program vmapped over four fields; evaluation is one fixed 512-point program vmapped over a 32-member capacity. Width 1 masks 31 slots and width 32 admits all slots, so both measurements reuse one executable rather than recompiling a dynamic batch shape. There are no compacted gathers, data-dependent arrays, host callbacks, or operations excluded from batching. The durable JSON records compile-plus-first-run, warm timings, and the split/global warm-time ratio at widths 1 and 32. These are carrier timings, not a claim about a complete equilibrium solve.

Flood-fill criterion: pass with a named cache boundary

The real-geometry operand is committed MAST 22086/43 mixed, row 11 of mast-topology-operands.npz. Its 1,089 centres are interpreted as 33 by 33 structured hex indices, and the selected O-point seeds the existing saddle-aware label propagation. Links crossing the selected X-point's horizontal cut are closed. The committed mask contains 42 private cells; the single-grid result contains 42; the symmetric difference is 0 cells.

The cache carries centres, final domain labels and selected O/X points, but not per-cell flux or per-candidate domain labels. Consequently this replay proves that moving the selected-saddle flood onto the structured cell graph preserves this topology answer. It cannot independently prove the upstream X-candidate choice or reproduce spline-derived shared-edge admissibility. Those missing fields are production-cutover work, not silently reconstructed evidence.

Practicality and falsifiable cutover estimate

Against the matched-capacity incumbent global carrier, the split carrier's measured accuracy delta is the 1.43e14 boundary-curvature factor above; its exact value is manufacture-specific. The timing delta is recorded at both batch widths in metrics.json, rather than inferred from FLOPs. No end-to-end incumbent two-mesh solve was rerun, so this record makes no 300-slice/s or hardware claim.
