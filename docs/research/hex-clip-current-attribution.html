§1 — Localisation verdict

The straight-edge clip, closed polygon moments, affine-current integration, current-centroid convention and first-moment coupling transform are not the defect. An independent 247-case regular-hex chord sweep exercised the same AtomicCellMesh.traced_clip and InteriorCurrentMomentStencil.support_moments calls used by the forward solve. The smallest retained area was 2.673e-10 of a full cell. Maximum errors were 1.53e-16 m² in area, 1.16e-15 of full-cell current, and 5.74e-16 of full-cell first-moment scale. The complementary core/common partitions recombined to full-cell current within 8.10e-16 of scale and the uncut support was bitwise identical to the ordinary interior contraction.

The rim-current availability defect is repaired on current main. A fresh census against the same immutable 566-cell fixture finds 447 nonempty plasma supports and 447 own-node scatter destinations. Exactly 0 of 447 nonempty supports with nonzero exact clipped current receive zero scattered current. Missing current is therefore 0.000000 MA, or 0.000000% of the exact -15.005421582 MA total. The retired complete-ring restriction still reproduces its original 96-cell rim omission, summing to -0.689027210 MA or 4.591855%. Current attribution no longer uses that restriction: StencilMesh.current_moment_stencil receives every per-cell support_centre, and InteriorCurrentMomentStencil.support_flux_moments integrates the cell's own centroid plus direct pre-clip sampling vertices. No neighbour value is invented for a partial ring; complete rings remain necessary only for derivative and shared-node flux reconstruction.

The per-cell source participation change in 15fbcf2f did not by itself establish rim availability. The availability repair is the own-node representation in 6282acd5, now pinned by a production-path regression. Complementary material partitions recombine to full-cell current within 2.0e-15 of scale, and an uncut complete-ring cell is bitwise identical in current and both first moments to the direct interior contraction. The independent curved-boundary and density-accuracy questions remain separate from this closed scatter defect.

§5 — Closed verification ladder ready to implement

Rung 1 — uncut regular hex and affine density

Use fixed centroid c=(R0,Z0), circumradius a, and vertices v_k=c+a(cos(pi/6+k*pi/3), sin(pi/6+k*pi/3)) for k=0..5. Let x=X-c and j(X)=j0+g_R*x_R+g_Z*x_Z. Compute closed shoelace tensors A=integral dA, Q1=integral x dA=0, and Q2=integral x x^T dA. The oracle is I=j0*A+g·Q1 and m1=j0*Q1+Q2@g. Run at three radii and three translations near ITER major radius. Require each scaled current and first-moment error at or below 1e-13, and require the uncut support_moments result bitwise equal to __call__. This rung is already green in the supplied reproducer.

Rung 2 — straight chord, including slivers and vertices

For unit normal n=(cos(theta),sin(theta)), define the exact retained polygon by s(X)=d-n·(X-c)>0. Intersect each crossed edge at t=s(v_i)/(s(v_i)-s(v_{i+1})); then apply the same shoelace tensors and affine-current identities as rung 1. Sweep at least 19 angles over [0,pi) and retained chord fractions {1e-5,1e-4,.01,.05,.15,.30,.50,.70,.85,.95,.99,1-1e-4,1-1e-5}. Test s and -s together. Require scaled I and m1 errors at or below 1e-13, area through second moment at the corresponding binary64 closed-form floor, and complement sums equal to the uncut result at the same tolerance. Report current centroid only where |I| > 1e-12 |j0*A_full|. This rung is also already green.

Rung 3 — exact curved Solov'ev contour

Use the total-flux analytic field Phi=2*pi*q with q(R,Z)=((R²-R0²)²)/(8R0²)+Z²/2. It obeys DeltaStar(q)=R²/R0²+1. Choose R0=6.2 m and set q_b=((R0+a)²-R0²)²/(8R0²) with a=0.8 m; the exact LCFS is q=q_b and psi_norm=q/q_b. A compatible exact current is j_phi=-(R²/R0²+1)/(mu0*R), obtained from DeltaStar(Phi)=-2*pi*mu0*R*j_phi.

For each intersected hex, form an independent oracle without straightening the curve. At a given R, intersect the hex's horizontal interval with |Z| <= sqrt(2*(q_b-(R²-R0²)²/(8R0²))); integrate j_phi, (R-Rc)j_phi and (Z-Zc)j_phi by adaptive 21-point Gauss-Kronrod in Z and then R, splitting at every polygon vertex and analytic radial LCFS root. Tighten absolute and relative tolerances by factors of four until every reported moment changes by less than 1e-12 of its full-cell scale.

Predict the linear-contour error from the implicit-curve curvature kappa=|F_RR F_Z²-2 F_RZ F_R F_Z+F_ZZ F_R²|/(F_R²+F_Z²)^(3/2) for F=q-q_b. For each reconstructed chord of length L with kappa_max*L <= 0.25, use the conservative area envelope B_A=kappa_max*L³/6, twice the leading circular-segment term. Require |A_linear-A_curve| <= B_A, and bound induced current and first moment by sup|j|*B_A and sup(|X-c|*|j|)*B_A. Repeat at half and quarter pitch; both measured error and bound must converge with the predicted cubic-in-chord scale. Separately report the boundary displacement caused by shared-node flux reconstruction, so it cannot be mislabeled as polygon quadrature.

Rung 4 — full coarse boundary ring

Run the exact curved-support oracle from rung 3 on every nonempty cell of the immutable 566-cell fixture. Compare in priority order: m0, current centroid c+m1/m0, then physical m1. Availability is now closed: all 447 nonempty cells are attributed and the regression pins zero lost cells. Define the interior error scale exactly as the bank does: p95 of finite absolute relative m0 error on retained cells, currently 0.1607953241. The repaired ring p95 must be no larger; for |I_ref|<1 A, report absolute amperes separately rather than letting a near-zero denominator dominate the gate. Plot per-cell m0, centroid distance and |m1| error on R-Z maps, with the worst cells labelled.

Rung 5 — simultaneous field consequence

Use the same 731 banked targets, analytic flux, exact total current and legacy control. Build a source oracle directly from the curved supports: fan-triangulate each clipped support, integrate G_psi(R_t,Z_t;R,Z)j_phi(R,Z) adaptively, and use a Duffy transform for any target inside its source triangle so the logarithmic self-kernel is resolved rather than sampled. Tighten the quadrature until every target changes by less than 1e-4 Wb. Then require all three consequences in one run: total current within 0.5% of -15.005421582 MA; shift at the original argmax target no more than 0.5 Wb from the 10.935349449 Wb source-error baseline; and supremum over all targets strictly below the 0.826 Wb legacy control. Report the uniform, radial and vertical companion contributions separately. A source result does not pass by cancellation with a coupling error.

§6 — Consequence for the implementation order

The complete-ring availability zero is closed without a new production change. Relative to the retired restriction, restoring the rim changes the converged MAST fixture operand by -0.689027210 MA. Its current centroid moves from (6.18318439, 0.52028488) m to (6.21006817, 0.51025319) m: +26.883779 mm radially and -10.031682 mm vertically. These are operand-sizing numbers, not a regenerated bank score. Bank regeneration and gate re-scoring belong to a follow-on node.

Next validate net-current density attribution on the curved analytic oracle. Only after m0 is inside the interior scale should centroid and first-moment accuracy be promoted. The coupling basis and companion matrices remain unchanged unless the separated quadrant evidence grows from 0.01337 Wb to the source-error scale. Smoothing is not an alternative: it cannot create the missing 96 stencil centres or make a quadratic shared-flux reconstruction exact on a quartic field.
