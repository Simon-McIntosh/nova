Execution evidence

ITER plasma prescribed from DINA

The current prescription covers both the 2 MA and 15 MA DINA cases. Nova computes every conductor contribution from geometry and current, then compares the prescribed plasma flux with a signed, gauge-aligned reconstruction of DINA's recorded flux.

Prescription and boundary selection

Read ITER 105014/1 and 105052/1 through imas-python at their native Data Dictionary 3.38.1. At each slice above 0.1 MA, the stored 50-point flux-function derivatives are evaluated on the 65 by 129 poloidal grid, masked by an axis-enclosing closed boundary, and scaled to the declared plasma current. The original separatrix endpoint gap, selected boundary source, and any reconstructed-contour fraction are retained per slice. A diverted outline is first cut at its stored X-point; the closed DINA boundary outline is next; a closed ψ contour is a last resort.

Geometric plasma-flux reference

Nova evaluates coil and passive contributions with its finite-section and polygon Green kernels, using DINA's pf_active and pf_passive currents. The current is interpolated at each equilibrium time from its own IDS time base. This matters in 105052/1: equilibrium and passive IDSs each have 2,924 samples, while pf_active has 29,234. An index-based read at the 1.218 s equilibrium slice would select 0.122 s coil currents; interpolation removes that measured error. Both cases have homogeneous_time = 1, so the IDS-level time arrays are used.

The native ψ orientation is inferred from the sign of Ip and its axis-to-boundary flux span: 105014/1 needs sign +1, 105052/1 sign −1. Flux has an additive gauge. At each sampled slice, nova fixes that gauge at the magnetic axis by comparing the signed stored ψ with the sum of nova's conductor flux and nova's Green-kernel flux from DINA's independent profiles_2d.j_tor. The axis gauge has median 0.001 Wb in 105014/1 and −0.429 Wb in 105052/1 after the current-time correction. The reconstructed plasma reference at a covered conductor is signed DINA total ψ minus nova's coil and passive flux and this axis gauge.

The 2-D ψ map covers the two VS3 sections of 14 active-coil sections. For the other 12, nova applies its Green kernel directly to DINA's stored j_tor cell currents, independently of the flux-function prescription; the receipt labels those rows separately. On 105014/1, each of 24 recorded magnetics flux-loop readings replaces the map's total ψ at that loop before the same conductor subtraction and gauge correction. As a positive check on the total-flux instrument, map ψ minus the recorded loop reading has median −0.00152 Wb over 576 comparisons; its 0.487 Wb maximum occurs in early ramp-up.

Each flux check requires |predicted − reference| ≤ max(A, f × |reference|). The absolute floor A prevents a small plasma reference in ramp-up or ramp-down from turning a finite conductor-subtraction error into an unbounded percentage. Case 105014/1 uses A = 1 Wb and f = 10% for ψ-map coil and magnetics references. Case 105052/1 uses A = 3 Wb and f = 5% for ψ-map coils. Both use A = 0.1 Wb and f = 1% where the reference is DINA j_tor through nova's kernel. The final column below is the largest residual divided by its applicable bound; every value must be at most 1.

Controls and reproduction

Moving the source 1 cm vertically at 5.93 s in 105014/1 changed a magnetics loop's flux by −0.0183351 Wb against −0.0183045 Wb predicted from its local vertical derivative, a 0.167% difference. In 105052/1 at 14.72 s, the same displacement at a fixed target changed flux by −0.0379121 Wb against −0.0378468 Wb predicted, a 0.173% difference. A retained mutation that ignored the shift failed the focused test. A deliberately tightened 105014/1 limit of 0.1 Wb or 1% refused a VS3 residual of 1.01907 Wb against its 0.110186 Wb bound at 37.128 s; tightened-geometric-105014.log ends in EXIT=1. The normal-limit runs for both cases end in GATE_PASS and EXIT=0.

Current-profile conventions and internal inductance

The native DD 3.38.1 fields fix the flux/current part of the COCOS interpretation. For all 867 active slices of 105014/1, Ip, Bφ, ψaxis−ψboundary, median p′ and median FF′ are negative. For all 2,036 active slices of 105052/1, Ip and Bφ remain negative while the other three signs are positive. At representative slices, 105014/1 has Ip −1.967 MA, Bφ −2.65 T, ψ span −9.756 Wb, p′ −147.1, FF′ −0.2479; 105052/1 has Ip −8.451 MA, Bφ −5.30 T, ψ span +65.459 Wb, p′ +279.7, FF′ +0.9011. DINA's independently stored jtor is negative in both. With the IDS right-handed (R,φ,Z) orientation and total-flux units, nova_cocos maps these to COCOS 17-like and 11-like flux/current senses respectively: the physical Green-kernel ψ sign is +1 for 105014/1 and −1 for 105052/1. The latter factor flips both stored derivatives together, so its raw positive j becomes DINA's negative Ip without reshaping the profile.

The final COCOS poloidal-angle digit is not independently identifiable for 105014/1: every active q array is empty or nonfinite, and the IDS stores no usable 2-D BR/BZ arrays. Its measured quantities are compatible with COCOS 17 and 13; the 17 label uses the standard positive-q angle choice. The finite positive q at the 105052/1 representative slice (median 1.506) supports COCOS 11 under that same orientation. The profile conclusion is invariant to the 17/13 ambiguity because those conventions have identical Ip, Bφ, ψ and derivative transforms. A per-radian ψ reading is rejected by li(3): its median error is 58.6 (105014/1) or 44.1 (105052/1), against 0.034 and 0.044 for total Wb. Current scale also picks both derivatives per Wb: the medians are +1.0426 and −0.99979; treating both as per radian leaves the Ip-scaled shape identical but moves them to +6.5511 and −6.2819.

For each active slice, the selected closed boundary defines the IMAS support and geometric major radius Rgeo. The field comparison uses Bp from DINA's total ψ map, with native flux sign and 1/(2πR), plus nova's greens_bz_br correction for the candidate current minus DINA's stored 2-D current. The correction is evaluated on a 33 × 65 grid; j-map and centre comparisons use the full 65 × 129 grid. The metric is li(3) = 2∫LCFSBp²dV/(μ₀²Ip²Rgeo). Nova's plasma-only Green field was also computed independently: its median li(3) minus DINA is +0.159 and +0.290 in the two cases, because DINA's total equilibrium field includes external conductors. The table below uses the total field with nova's current-change correction to compare profile conventions on like support. DD 3.38.1 has no global_quantities.li field, so li(1) cannot be compared.

A positive check on the field instrument integrates DINA's own ψ map on the same boundary before any candidate-current correction. At full 65 × 129 resolution its median li(3) residual is +0.0059 (range −0.0162 to +0.0750) on 105014/1 and +0.0486 (−0.0031 to +3.0434) on 105052/1. Coarsening the energy integral to 33 × 65 shifts those medians by only +0.0012 and −0.0028. Most of the large late 105052/1 residual therefore exists in the DINA-map integration before changing the current profile; it is amplified by the falling Ip normaliser and cannot be assigned to the derivative convention.
