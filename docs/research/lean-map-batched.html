Research input and execution evidence

Lean-map batched chord measurements

The lean map is an accuracy reference, not a qualifying batched solver. Its analytic error falls from 5.49 × 10−4 at 282 diverted cells to 1.67 × 10−4 at 550 cells; the earlier independent 2,098-cell diverted certificate measured 1.77 × 10−5. On the H200, the current width-128 chord takes 44.0 ms per state at 553 limited cells and 34.4 ms at 550 diverted cells, about 49 and 38 times the 0.90 ms target. Only 13/128 diverted states converge. Production rows were stopped after this decisive miss.

Method and acceptance

The candidate is the fixed-shape lean map: connected full cells use an 18-point interior Gauss rule, while connected partial cells and irregular wall cells use exact cuts in a fixed-capacity batch. A dense values map serves interior Gauss points and a gathered six-jet map serves the boundary and fixed-point windows. The analytic certificate supplies the exact-support exterior. The same scaled moment equation is used for cold Newton, a fresh dense Jacobian factor, and two cached-factor chord corrections. All program calls take state, geometry, map operands and factor as runtime arguments. Double precision is enabled before any JAX arrays are constructed.

The program requested limited and diverted carriers at each resolution, each twice with the device builder in separate caches; the stop ruling left only the 282–553-cell pairs complete. A row reports the independently checked analytic-state map error; cold residual history, terminal gradient ratio and repeatability; and synchronised width-1, width-16 and width-128 walls divided by distinct states. Map-only, factor-build and chord-correction walls are separate measurements. Compilation runs with the persistent compilation cache disabled. The strict pass requires each scaled moment residual to be at most 10−12, a per-state chord wall below 0.90 ms, executable code below 50 MB and cold compilation below 60 s.

The hard-centroid control recomputes point booking on the same analytic field and exact-support exterior. A perturbed LU factor is compared with the unperturbed factor on the same chord state, so a refusal counts as a negative control only when the unperturbed state passes. The 550-cell continuity sweep bisects a limited-to-diverted bracket and records boundary flux, booked current and refusal flags. The planned production stage profile would have timed support read, exact cuts, fixed-point roots, interior Gauss and matrix reads; it was stopped before a receipt, so the independently measured 282-cell reference profile is cited instead.

The largest width-128 chord residual is 1.84922 × 10−12 at 289 limited cells, 2.20649 × 10−11 at 282 diverted cells, 4.77834 × 10−13 at 553 limited cells, and 7.87471 × 10−12 at 550 diverted cells. All width-1/16/128 map, factor and chord executables on the 282–553-cell rows occupy 1.37–8.56 MB, below 50 MB. Their cache-disabled compilations take 21.4–56.3 s, below 60 s. Cold Newton compilation instead takes 110.1–119.6 s at these sizes and 124.7 s at 2,105 limited cells, above 60 s. The 2,105-cell width-one chord executable is 25.46 MB and compiled in 47.71 s.

At width 128 the 550-cell map alone costs 11.74–14.44 ms per state, 13–16 times the entire 0.90 ms target; the measured fresh factor costs 10.7–21.6 s per state, and 251 s at 2,105 limited cells. Factor-build work alone cannot close the two-correction chord wall. The most likely map change is replacing connected-support label propagation with a bounded read: the independent 282-cell H200 stage profile measured 6.120 ms for that read out of a 10.176 ms fused map, versus 4.322 ms for exact cut booking, 0.488 ms for dense Gauss values, 0.255 ms for band jets, 0.317 ms for fixed points and 0.244 ms for interior Gauss. These isolated stages overlap and are not additive. The 2,098-cell stage receipt was never produced, so dominance at that resolution remains unmeasured.

The hard-centroid control re-ran point booking: diverted analytic error rises from 5.48822 × 10−4 to 0.513469 at 282 cells and from 1.67399 × 10−4 to 0.430255 at 550 cells, turning both current rows red. The independent earlier 2,098-cell certificate measured 0.418807 for hard centroids versus 1.766 × 10−5 for exact cuts; that value is contextual, not a rerun in this batched job. The LU perturbation raises residuals, but none of its unperturbed offset-1.0 comparators passes 10−12, so no factor mutation is counted as a qualifying refusal.

The 550-cell continuity probe reports a 0.5926-span boundary-flux jump across a bisected topology-class change. It is a probe artefact, not a demonstrated discontinuity of valid lean-map states: both bracket endpoints have valid=false, exact cut booking status 2 (invalid), and raw booked current zero; only 1 of 41 sampled states has a valid booking. The positive control at the terminal state has status 0, valid exact booking and identical repeat output, so the instrument can see a valid state. The invalid bracket cannot establish the requested roundoff continuity or a physical jump. A valid transition bracket would be needed to decide that question.

Ruling and limits

The width-128 two-correction chord misses the 0.90 ms per-state target by 49 times at 553 limited cells and 38 times at 550 diverted cells, with only 13/128 diverted states converged. The lead stopped the remaining production rows after that result; no further H200 job was submitted. The lean map remains an analytic-accuracy reference because its error falls with resolution and its exact cuts decisively beat hard centroids.

No first or second 2,098-cell diverted carrier or batch receipt exists in these jobs. The second 2,105-cell limited carrier is also absent; the first has only a cold solution and width-one batch plus the width-16 map wall. Its width-16 factor/chord and all width-128 rows lack numeric receipts, and no 2,098-cell stage profile was produced. The earlier independent 2,098-cell diverted map error and 282-cell stage profile are labelled as such in this page and its figure; neither is presented as a production batch measurement. The 550-cell continuity bracket is invalid, so the reported 0.5926-span jump cannot decide valid-state continuity. The factor mutation likewise has no passing comparator. These limits remain explicit in the delivered result.
