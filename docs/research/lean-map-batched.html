Research input and execution evidence

Lean-map batched chord measurements

The completed 550-cell H200 batches preserve analytic accuracy but miss the 0.90 ms per-state chord target: 44.0 ms for 553 limited cells and 34.4 ms for 550 diverted cells at width 128. Only 13 of 128 diverted chord states meet 10−12. An eight-hour production job stopped before all 2,098-cell and 282-cell rows; its numeric receipts, rather than its misleading wrapper exit line, define the findings below.

Method and acceptance

The candidate is the fixed-shape lean map: connected full cells use an 18-point interior Gauss rule, while connected partial cells and irregular wall cells use exact cuts in a fixed-capacity batch. A dense values map serves interior Gauss points and a gathered six-jet map serves the boundary and fixed-point windows. The analytic certificate supplies the exact-support exterior. The same scaled moment equation is used for cold Newton, a fresh dense Jacobian factor, and two cached-factor chord corrections. All program calls take state, geometry, map operands and factor as runtime arguments. Double precision is enabled before any JAX arrays are constructed.

Each requested resolution has both limited and diverted carriers, and each carrier is built twice with the device builder in separate caches. A row reports the independently checked analytic-state map error; cold residual history, terminal gradient ratio and repeatability; and synchronised width-1, width-16 and width-128 walls divided by distinct states. Map-only, factor-build and chord-correction walls are separate measurements. Compilation runs with the persistent compilation cache disabled. The strict pass requires each scaled moment residual to be at most 10−12, a per-state chord wall below 0.90 ms, executable code below 50 MB and cold compilation below 60 s.

The hard-centroid control recomputes point booking on the same analytic field and exact-support exterior; the expected diverted error is about 0.42 at 2,098 cells. A perturbed LU factor is compared with the unperturbed factor on the same chord state, so a refusal counts as a negative control only when the unperturbed state passes. The 550-cell continuity sweep bisects a limited-to-diverted bracket and records boundary flux, booked current and refusal flags. The 2,098-cell stage profile times the support read, exact cut booking, fixed-point roots, interior Gauss booking and matrix reads separately.

At width 128 the lean map alone already costs 11.74–14.44 ms per state, 13–16 times the entire 0.90 ms target. The measured fresh factor costs 10.7–21.6 s per state at 550 cells and 251 s at 2,105 limited cells. The 550-cell width-128 map, factor and chord executables are below 9 MB; their cold compilation takes 28.6–56.3 s. By contrast, cold Newton compilation is 119.5 s at 550 cells and 124.7 s at 2,105 cells, above the 60 s limit. The width-one 2,105-cell chord executable is 25.46 MB and compiled in 47.71 s. Since the map alone misses the target, factor-build work cannot by itself close the chord wall. The first candidate is replacing connected-support label propagation: the earlier 282-cell stage profile measured 6.1 ms for that read out of a 10.2 ms map, against 4.3 ms for exact cuts. This is an inference from the earlier profile; the outstanding 2,098-cell stage profile must establish whether it remains dominant at production resolution.

The hard-centroid control re-ran point booking: at 550 diverted cells its analytic error rises from 1.67399 × 10−4 to 0.430255, which turns the map-accuracy row red. The factor mutation is not yet a qualifying refusal: the 550-cell offset-1.0 comparator becomes nonfinite, and the 2,105-cell limited comparator ends at 2.02921 × 10−10 before its 1% perturbation ends at 7.38937 × 10−8. Neither comparator passes the strict target.

The 550-cell continuity probe found a bisected topology-class change, but both bracket endpoints report invalid exact cut booking (status 2). The boundary flux jumps by 0.593 of the analytic flux span while booked raw current is zero on both sides. This is a measured refusal and discontinuity, not evidence of the required smooth transition. The probe's terminal booking floor is valid and repeats identically, so the invalid bracket is not inferred from an empty instrument. The cause remains to be diagnosed in the lean-map booking or bracket construction.
