Lobe share and cell partition

An owner cell is booked once, as a signed fan of the normal-form ring builder: the cell polygon enters positive and the lobe enters negative, so the read's membership and the booked current are one integral over one set of points. The reduction is the clip-quadrature owner's own rule, imported rather than restated, and the vertex-list reconstruction of an earlier booking is gone. This page records the construction, why one call of the builder cannot carry both selections, and the agreement of the booking with closed-form references that share neither its ring builder nor its quadrature. The largest lobe-reference deviation is 7.10542735760 × 10−13 and the largest whole-cell partition deviation is 1.13686837722 × 10−13, both under the 1 × 10−12 contract. The lobe partition passes all three map guards, and the 282 cold solve converges to 3.0 × 10−14; the 550 cold solve stalls on a collapsing line search and is left as the next item.

Method

The carrier set is the exact clipped single-null diverted certificate, asked for 250, 500 and 2,000 cells, which realises 282, 550 and 2,098. Rows are read from a cached carrier through fine_grid_convergence.context.build, so the geometry, the reference moments and the fitted density coefficients are the ones every other node in this plan measures against, and the booking is compared against the same reference the map is measured on.

Two references bound the booking. The lobe reference constructs selected straight-edge intervals and cubic-ray chords independently in NumPy, then integrates the quadratic density over their triangles in closed form. The whole-cell reference integrates the cell polygon's own edges in closed form. Neither calls the booking's ring builder or clip quadrature, so a disagreement can expose either a region-construction or integration error. Agreement is required to 1 × 10−12 on area, current and both first moments.

The fan the remainder takes

The remainder of an owner cell — the cell with the lobe removed — is the cell polygon's star fan entered positive beside the lobe's fan entered negative, reduced in one call. Both fans come from current._normal_rings, the builder that already produces the lobe, so there is one construction of a ring fan in this code and no vertex list is walked to build a second one. The earlier booking reconstructed the cell fan by walking the polygon's vertex list and reducing it with a locally held copy of the reduction; that reconstruction is deleted.

Why one call cannot carry both selections. The builder takes one selected vector and one interval array, and it answers them with two families of rows. Its straight rows carry the polygon's edges; its separatrix-arc rows carry the boundary the normal form supplies, and they are emitted after the straight rows, for the sectors the selection makes live and only where the sector's sign is nonzero. The lobe's selection is the read's own: two live sectors of four, with the edge intervals the read computed. The cell polygon's is not a selection the read can express — it is the identity over every edge, with a degenerate interval, and it is live in a sector the read has marked dead. Asking one call for both would therefore need the builder to hold a second selection and a second interval array and to tag which row belongs to which request, which is a change to the accessor rather than a use of it. A second invocation is the smallest construction that keeps one builder: the straight rows are emitted per fragment slot, so exactly one slot is selected, one identity interval is placed on the first strip of every edge, and the separatrix-arc rows that follow are dropped by the static straight-row count. The result is the polygon's star fan and nothing else.

The slot count is load-bearing and was the one defect found while writing the analytic check: selecting every fragment slot emits the straight rows once per slot, so the cell fan carried the polygon twice and the booked remainder was the polygon rather than the polygon less the lobe. Exactly one slot is selected now, and the check below pins that contract.

The reduction is the owner's

The signed-fan reduction is clip_quadrature._quadrature_from_arrays, imported and called with signed=True. It fans a ring of count points into triangles from the row's leading vertex and weights them by leggauss(8); a row of three live vertices contributes exactly one triangle and a row of six contributes four, which is the effective triangle set the lobe reference integrates, so the rule that books an owner cell here is the rule that books one in the clip-quadrature owner. A locally held signed-fan reduction and a truncated degree-five Dunavant rule are deleted with it. The truncation is worth naming: the rule was carried to ten digits, and that truncation, not the fan, was the floor under the booked lobe. With the rule gone, no ten-digit constant remains on this path and the booking's precision is the rule's own.

The wrapper that remains applies the row sign and the booked density and nothing else: it evaluates the density only at points the owner already emitted, so no quadrature is restated here. A negative row lets a region be removed from another without a polygon union, which is what makes the cell-minus-lobe partition a single integral rather than a set operation.

The analytic check

A unit check builds the cell fan through current._normal_rings itself, driven with a form whose separatrix rays are dead so only the straight rows are live, and compares the booked reduction against the closed form over the polygon's triangles. It asserts that exactly one live slot carries the polygon once, that the reduction of that fan matches the closed form on a quadratic density, and that a negative fan subtracts: the signed booking of a square less an inner quadrilateral equals the closed form over each. Two further tests call _whole_cell_rings and remaining_moments on a padded triangle, and forbid the booking builder while the independent lobe reference runs. All five pass. Restoring the vertex-list remainder makes the padded-triangle test fail; restoring the builder-based lobe reference makes the forbidden-builder test fail. The fresh-process base arm reaches pytest's summary with two collection errors because the new test imports _ring_moments, which the base does not define; the head arm collects and passes all five tests.

The 282 solve reaches 3.0 × 10−14 in five corrections, each about 1.6 s of solve wall with no line search damping once the map and the correction program are compiled (16.5 s and 74.3 s respectively), so the booked partition is a root the Newton correction accepts at this size.

The 550 solve stalls on the collapsing line search clause 6 named in advance. The accepted step factor falls at every correction — 0.5, 0.25, 0.0625, 0.03125, 0.0078125, 0.00390625, 0.000244140625, 3.0517578125 × 10−5, then 0.0 — and at zero no step is taken at all, so the correction exits early with converged=False rather than at the eighteen-step ceiling. The residual decreases monotonically but each correction moves it less than the last, and the final three sit on a fixed point to within 1 × 10−10, so the Newton direction cannot leave it by any positive step. The contract is missed by ten orders of magnitude at this size, and the mechanism is the line search rather than the partition: the same booked partition is a root at 282. The stall is reported and left as the next item.

Verdict

Clauses 1 to 4 and the diverted part of clause 5 hold by construction and by measurement: the remainder takes its fan from the same builder that builds the lobe, the vertex-list reconstruction and the local signed-fan reduction are deleted, the reduction is the imported clip-quadrature rule, and the truncated rule that set the booked lobe's floor is gone with it. The independent lobe reference agrees to at most 7.10542735760 × 10−13, and the whole-cell partition to at most 1.13686837722 × 10−13. The 550 carrier books cell 8, so all three sizes exercise the intended owner partition. The lobe partition passes all three diverted map guards and at 2,098 passes where the half-plane cut fails. Limited controls are measured at 289 and 553 cells; the 2,105-cell map could not load on the P100. Clause 6 holds at 282 and is reported, not met, at 550: the solve stalls on a collapsing line search at a fixed point near 1.55 × 10−3, which is the item clause 6 named in advance and is left as the next item. No number on this page is an estimate.
