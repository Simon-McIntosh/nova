§1 — Verdict

The existing Ambix L1/L2 Zarr catalogs contain the physical geometry required by the MAST axisymmetric training and reconstruction paths. The correct source boundary is not the old EFIT setup hash. L2 carries named physical active coils, passive structures, wall/limiter geometry, magnetic diagnostic placement, ordered three-dimensional saddle paths, and soft-X-ray lines of sight. L1 contributes magnetic-probe sensitive-axis angles and flux-loop toroidal span. The three familiar EFIT setup digests describe solver inputs and discretizations; after canonicalizing named physical components they collapse to one observed machine configuration.

The evidence does not authorize inventing geometry that the stores do not publish. Detailed TF winding geometry and installed poses for interferometry, visible spectroscopy, gas valves, Thomson scattering and charge exchange are not present as full three-dimensional geometry arrays in the inspected L2 groups. They remain explicit source-coverage items outside the magnetic/SXR geometry proved here.

§3 — Representation hashes are not machine configurations

The prototype reproduces the Ambix EFIT setup digest algorithm byte-for-byte: finite arrays, rounded to four decimal places, from magpr, silop, fcoil and limiter setup fields. Across the 11,016 L2 shots with a readable matching L1 setup it finds the same three digests Ambix has used as campaign keys:

L2’s named component geometry is invariant across those cohorts, and the canonical physical digest is 67f789d3d8b40135.

§4 — Full-corpus census and shot-range adjudication

SLURM job 1238120 scanned all 11,573 L2 stores on sun_debug with 16 CPUs. It completed successfully in 54:07, peaked at 325,384 KiB RSS, and read only static geometry plus the matching rounded L1 setup evidence. The immutable result is catalog-geometry-scan.json.

Shot 26963 is not a configuration. Its first-pass digest lacked the vertw passive family. The family is present with identical surrounding geometry on shots 26961 and 26964, and all three shots share setup digest 1cb6f2ee742c4ee4. Treating a one-store omission as physical removal and reinstallation across two unobserved gaps would violate both the catalog evidence and machine operation. The corrected prototype marks this shot incomplete and leaves the physical range unbroken.

L1 extends from shots 11695–30473, wider than L2’s 11766–30471. The same three setup representations exist at those edges, but an EFIT setup is not by itself physical authority. The registry may extend the physical configuration over the L1-only edge shots as an explicit inherited-range assertion; it must not claim those edge shots are independently verified by named L2 geometry.

§5 — Prototype gates

nova/scripts/mast_catalog_geometry.py is an executable recovery prototype. It opens Zarr directly, hashes static arrays only, caches physical canonicalization by source snapshot, and emits coverage, representation and range evidence. Six focused tests pass:

  • active coil identity is invariant to cell subdivision;
  • passive identity is invariant to element subdivision;
  • closed paths are invariant to start index and traversal direction;
  • missing shots do not split ranges while real digest changes do;
  • real early/late EFIT setup variants share the same L2 physical source;
  • shot 26963 is rejected as incomplete evidence because vertw is absent.

The development run also caught a boundary regression: a prefix selector initially included dynamic fcoil_c/fcoil_x arrays. That job was stopped, the selector was replaced by an explicit ten-field static setup list, and the successful census was run once with the corrected boundary.

§6 — Maintained registry and DD 4.1.1 round trip

The recovery logic now lives in the typed nova.catalog.mast_geometry module. The executable script remains a census entry point, while the immutable packaged payload at nova/catalog/mast_geometry.json is the consumer authority. It records one configuration, the three setup-representation aliases, explicit SI units and tolerances, the observed and inherited ranges, all 17 incomplete observations, provenance and unsupported authoring semantics.

Shot selection is deliberately two-dimensional. The physical digest remains the same while evidence is inherited for 11695–11765, observed for 11766–30471 and inherited for 30472–30473. Within the observed interval, shots 26767, 26829, 26838, 26846, 26876, 26884, 26885, 26941, 26947, 26950, 26953, 26957, 26959, 26972, 26982 and 26990 are missing both passive and wall evidence; shot 26963 is missing vertw. The selection still names the same underlying configuration and cannot fall through to a different one.

The DD authorer wrote pf_active, pf_passive, wall and magnetics with imas-python 2.3.0 at DD 4.1.1, validated every IDS, reopened with dd_version='4.1.1', and pinned 13 active components, 16 passive families, the limiter cycle, 78 poloidal probes, 44 full flux loops and all 36 saddle paths. Probe R/Z, poloidal angle and length survived; flux-loop span and canonical saddle point order survived. The focused command uv run python -m pytest tests/test_mast_catalog_geometry.py tests/test_machine.py --tb=short -q passed all 19 tests in 4.42 seconds.

Negative findings remain data, not defaults. The catalogs do not authoritatively supply active turns, polarity or circuit topology; passive material, resistance or electrical topology; detailed TF winding geometry; independent toroidal probe orientation; saddle traversal sign; or complete installed poses beyond the magnetic and soft-X-ray geometry recorded here. These fields remain explicit authoring gaps and are left unset rather than inferred.

§7 — Consequence for S2

The external engineering-chronology blocker in the existing machine-description plan is too broad. Catalog recovery can now supply the axisymmetric physical machine and magnetic/SXR diagnostic pose registry directly. S2 should first land the canonical registry, then author DD 4.1.1 IDSs from that registry and validate round trips. External engineering evidence is retained only for fields the catalogs genuinely do not publish, and it cannot be used to turn mesh counts or source dropouts into machine configurations.
