Decision: score obr17 through its fitted gain?

owner decision open

obr17 is not an unexplained description residual. Its promoted gain is 0.5011 (unrounded fit 0.5011125727424951; supported interval [0.5011, 0.5037]), a third independent route gives 0.5023 ± 0.0007, and pulse-by-pulse values remain 0.488–0.514 from pulse 15000 to 30420. The mechanism is published: a MAST equilibrium pickup channel averages two coils 180° apart toroidally, so losing one member produces a half-amplitude channel. One factor removes 88.3% of this channel's residual amplitude on the artifact arm and 88.5% on the incumbent.

Decision: does a pulse-scoped model replace one static gain?

owner decision open

The evidence settles one scientific boundary: a lifetime-static scale is not a faithful model for the pair-failure class. It can represent the lifetime-stable obr17, but not obv03's mid-life step and not obr05's pulse-to-pulse flips. The remaining owner decision is whether the gate consumes validity-scoped corrections or refuses the affected measurements.

What is fixed whichever options the owner selects

  1. Both arms consume exactly the same measurement policy. No correction is artifact-only or incumbent-only.
  2. No tolerance changes. A scoring-policy discontinuity is recorded by one paired artifact/incumbent re-stamp at a single commit, not absorbed into the budget.
  3. Coverage is part of the result. Every stamp reports included channels, included shots, refused states, and whether loops were fit-only or scored.
  4. Gain means divide the recorded waveform by the measured multiplier. The record retains raw values, applied values, validity intervals, provenance, and set identity.
  5. No unsupported interpolation. The unmeasured obv03 transition remains bracketed, and an unresolved obr05 state remains unresolved.
