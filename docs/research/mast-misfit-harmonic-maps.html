Harmonic maps of the vacuum misfit: what the array determines, and where it stops

The localization record scored one filament at a time against the surviving P4/P5 misfit. This record replaces that scan with a representation: in the vacuum region the misfit flux solves the homogeneous Grad–Shafranov operator exactly, so it expands in toroidal harmonics, and the expansion is a map everywhere rather than a score at candidate points. Two things had to be built first — constraint sets that mix probe field projections with calibrated flux loops under per-channel noise, and a fit that carries the described coil currents as unknowns instead of projecting them out.

The headline is not the map. It is that two of the three localisers the section set out to use do not work, and both fail for reasons the data itself shows — while the third does work and puts the strongest survivor below the P4 lower winding, with a resolution that independently reproduces a bound the conductor hunt reached by a different route.

What this session established

Does the misfit look like enclosed current or like far-field leakage? Enclosed, on the lower groups. Undecided on the upper ones. Fitted about the driven winding, the first-kind family explains 0.863 of the whitened power on P4 lower and 0.584 on P5 lower against 0.412 and 0.402 for the second kind — over 0.342 and 0.202 for the thirteen described coil columns alone. P4 upper 0.661 against 0.565, P5 upper 0.783 against 0.776: no separation.
What does projecting the coil span out first cost on real data? Between a tenth and everything. Explained power falls 0.863→0.792 (P4 lower), 0.584→0.479 (P5 lower), 0.661→0.378 (P4 upper) and 0.783→0.033 (P5 upper). The basis section measured the shortcut's position bias at zero noise; this is its price in reach, at every group.
What do the calibrated flux loops buy? Not an improvement — a requirement. An expansion fitted to the 76 probes alone, evaluated at the 17 loop rows it never saw, misses by 3× to 40 195× what predicting zero there would have cost. The probe array does not determine the misfit flux where the loops sit.
Can either sensor band alone carry the machine? No, in both directions. A centre-column-fitted expansion predicts the outboard band 42–12 649× worse than silence; an outboard-fitted one predicts the centre column 8–23× worse. The current sits between the bands, which is what makes neither able to extrapolate across.
Does the interior/exterior map difference localise the source? No. It localises the focal circle. Swept over six placements spanning the machine, the peak of the difference follows the circle it was given to a median of 0.10–0.23 m. It reaches 13.9 resampling sigma wherever it is put.
What does localise? The family contrast, scanned over placement. Peaks 0.52 at (1.90, −1.40) for P4 lower, 0.42 at (1.95, −0.30) for P5 lower, 0.20 at (1.30, +1.20) for P4 upper, 0.08 at (1.45, +1.00) for P5 upper — each beside or below its own driven winding.
How well is it localised? In height, not in radius. P4 lower's half-maximum region is bounded above at Z = −0.95, immediately at the P4 lower winding, and runs to the bottom of the scan; in radius it spans 0.60–2.40 m, the whole scan. Shot resampling gives R 1.89 ± 0.21, Z −0.88 ± 0.89.
Is the survivor one conductor? No. The single-source read off the fitted coefficients does not settle: modulus residual 0.026–0.665 and positions that walk out of the array. One filament does not carry the coefficient sequence.

The constraint sets

Each drive group carries 91–93 rows: 74–76 pickup probes as signed field projections in tesla per ampere-turn (obr17 excluded as an established instrument fault), and 17 flux loops as linked flux in weber per ampere-turn. The loop couplings are new here and are built by the probe reduction channel for channel — each loop's flux residual against the described windings regressed jointly on every substantially active ampere-turn waveform on the shot, the coefficient on the P4/P5 regressor kept — over the same 41 (shot, group) rows the probe bank holds, so the two blocks align and a resampling draw can take a whole shot.

Only the 18 loop channels the gain adjudication admits as published are used, and 17 of them join a described position; no per-channel gain and no convention factor is applied, because that record measured there is none to apply. Seven sit on the centre column and ten on the outer coils, including both P4-lower loops recovered by the position repair — which is why the loop block reaches exactly where the probe array is weakest.

The two classes arrive in different units and are made commensurable by whitening each row with its own noise: the robust scatter of that channel's per-shot couplings about their median, divided by the root of the shot count and floored at the channel's own quiet-time scatter from the sensor-floor record propagated through the drive span. Median signal-to-noise per row is 10.7 (P4 lower), 15.4 (P5 lower), 5.4 and 5.2 on the upper groups. Degree is chosen on held-out channels and lands at 6, 7, 4 and 1.

Two departures the row set forced. The held-out score is a median absolute whitened error, not a root-mean-square: fl_p2l_4 is mounted on the P2 lower coil and alone pins that coil's described column, so the fold that holds it out leaves its own prediction free to run to twenty times the data and a squared score then measures which fold got the leverage row rather than which degree fits. And the design is whitened before the selection rather than weighted inside it, because an unweighted score over a set mixing tesla with weber answers a question about unit choice.

The maps

The reconciled fit reaches 0.930 of the whitened power on P4 lower, 0.771 on P5 lower and 0.766 on P4 upper. What it does not reach matters as much: the whitened residual stays at 7.5–28.8 standard deviations, so a great deal of structure remains that no axisymmetric expansion of this degree represents. Some of that is the non-axisymmetric residue the plan expects to bound; some is the shot-to-shot systematics the pooled standard error does not cover.

The largest described-coil corrections the joint fit takes up are on the P2 pair — +0.222 and −0.192 ampere-turns per ampere-turn of P4 lower drive, and −0.736 and +0.494 on P5 lower. These are the coil columns absorbing what they can of the misfit, not a claim about P2; they are reported because a fit that was not allowed to move them would have pushed the same content into the harmonic block.

Why the map difference is not a localiser

Contouring the disagreement between the two expansions and reading its peak was the section's stated route to the enclosed current, and taken at face value it gives a confident answer: for P4 lower the peak sits at (1.540, −0.784) at 13.9 resampling sigma, with 34% of the supported plane above three sigma — a spot between the P4 and P5 lower windings that would have read as a find. It is an artefact. The two families differ most next to their own singular sets, and the inner family's singular set is the focal circle, so the peak is a property of where the expansion was centred. Moving the circle moves the peak with it, and no placement anywhere in the machine fails to produce a double-digit-sigma “detection”.

This is worth recording rather than quietly dropping. The failure mode is not that the measurement was too noisy; it is that a quantity with an obvious physical reading — where two expansions cannot be reconciled — is dominated by basis behaviour, and its confidence band does not warn about it, because every resampling draw reproduces the same artefact.

What does localise, and how well

The working localiser compares the two families fitted about the same circle and scans the circle. It is a comparison of two source hypotheses differing only in which side of the circle the current is on, and unlike the map difference it is a statement about the data's reach rather than about the basis: at a placement far from any current, both families fit equally and the contrast is zero. It is also strictly more general than the earlier filament scan — at degree n the inner family represents any current distribution near the circle, not one ring.

For P4 lower the half-maximum region is sharply bounded above at Z = −0.95 — hard against the P4 lower winding, whose centroid is (1.498, −1.099) — and open below. In radius it is not bounded at all: the region spans the entire 0.60–2.40 m of the scan. So the measurement says the strongest survivor is produced by current below the P4 lower coil and not by anything in the upper half or on the centre column, and says nothing about its radius. Shot resampling puts the peak at R 1.89 ± 0.21, Z −0.88 ± 0.89.

That extent is a resolution limit, and it agrees with a bound reached independently: the conductor hunt concluded the array cannot localise a ring to better than 1.5–2.3 m. Two different estimators, one scanning conductor candidates and one scanning the expansion's focal circle, return the same scale. The P4/P5 misfit is not a localisation problem this array can close.

The upper groups behave differently and consistently with their weaker patterns: P4 upper's contrast peaks at 0.20 in a compact region on its own winding, and P5 upper's at 0.08 with the half-maximum region covering a quarter of the scanned plane — which is to say, not localised.

The five reductions side by side

The right panel is the one that decides the loop question. The probes-alone expansion looks better on the left — it fits 0.913 of its own rows against the joint set's 0.930 — and is catastrophic on the right. Withholding the loops does not degrade the fit; it removes the constraint that stops the expansion running away where nothing else holds it. The same reading applies to the bands: each is comfortable on itself and useless across.

What is banked, and what is left open

  • ~/.cache/nova-mast/toroidal-harmonics/loop_couplings.npz — 41 (shot, group) rows of signed loop couplings, aligned to the probe bank, with the loop positions, the described response and the per-shot spans.
  • ~/.cache/nova-mast/toroidal-harmonics/maps.json and maps.npz — every headline number above, the five flux maps and the resampling band per group on the published grid, the contrast scan and its resampled peaks, and the focus-tracking sweep.
  • Scripts beside them: loop_couplings.py (store read), maps.py (fits and scans), map_figures.py (figures from the bank, recomputing nothing).
  • Code: nova/imas/mast_misfit_harmonics.py with 33 tests in tests/test_mast_misfit_harmonics.py, pinned against a synthetic filament hidden under a coil set sized to the same share of whitened power the real groups show.

Both maps were fitted on probes and loops; the plan's open question of whether to start on probes alone is answered by the right-hand panel above rather than by a schedule, and every number in this record uses the joint probes-plus-loops set with the coil currents free.

Left open for the sections that follow: the residual is 7.5–28.8 sigma after the best axisymmetric expansion, which bounds from below what a non-axisymmetric account must carry; the single-source read does not converge, so the multi-filament and extended-path fits are still to be scored; and the reconciled maps are published on a grid with a band, which is the form the flux-map problem class of coil-geometry-inversion can consume directly — with the caveat, now measured, that for three of four drive groups the band covers most of the plane.
