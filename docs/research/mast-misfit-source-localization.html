Mapping the misfit: where the missing conductor can and cannot be

The separation record ended with two named faults and three named targets. This session turns the second fault — the P4/P5-local field the description cannot produce — into maps: the signed misfit at every probe per ampere-turn of each P4/P5 drive, the poloidal-plane score of every candidate single-conductor location against that misfit, and the first contour maps of the misfit flux itself. It also puts two of the user-raised hypotheses to direct measurement: the differential-circuit reading of the P4 pair asymmetry, and the recoverability of the discarded flux loops.

Every number below is reproducible from the banked scripts and arrays under ~/.cache/nova-mast/misfit-localization/; the per-shot couplings were recomputed from the level-1 store with the acquisition block scales divided out on the read path, and the known obr17 gain fault is excluded from every source fit.

Can the flux loops be recovered? Yes — and the 2π question is already settled. Is the description itself symmetric where the machine should be? No — a new, cheap-to-test candidate. The described P4 members differ by 17.3 mm in |z| centroid and 3% in footprint; P5 by 12.9 mm. p4_lower centroid z = −1.1268 m vs p4_upper +1.1095 m; footprints 0.154² vs 0.159 × 0.158 m; both published at 23 turns. An erroneous asymmetry in the description of a symmetric machine produces exactly a symmetric, P4/P5-linear residual — and would feed the 26.5% P4 inversion split.

Method — clean signed couplings, not reused coefficients

The separation record's attribution coefficients were fitted jointly per family, which splits unstably when two members are driven in proportion. Here each shot's residual is regressed jointly on every substantially active ampere-turn waveform (span above 2000 ampere-turns and 5% of the largest span), with the two members of a driven pair merged into one pair-sum regressor whenever their waveforms correlate above 0.98, and the whole shot discarded when the regressor matrix conditions worse than 30. That guard matters: every P4 symmetric-pair shot in the archive drives P3 and the solenoid in near-proportion to P4 and fails it (conditions 100–211), which is why the P4 pair column is absent below and why flux loops and pulsed shots are needed to close that gap. The result is 41 (shot, group) coupling rows over 40 shots: 13 for P4 lower, 12 for P5 lower, 7 each for P4 upper and P5 upper, 2 for the P5 pair.

For each group the per-channel couplings pool as medians, and the pooled vector splits into the part the described thirteen-coil column space can absorb and the surviving part orthogonal to every described column — the free-inversion survivor of the separation record, now carried as a signed spatial pattern. The surviving share is 55–84% of coupling power depending on group, and obr17 is excluded throughout as an established instrument fault.

The surviving pattern is coherent across the whole array

Split-half stability separates signal from pooling noise: P4 lower's surviving pattern reproduces at cosine +0.92 between disjoint shot halves, P5 lower at +0.54, the upper groups at +0.32 to +0.42 on half the rows. The strongest cross-group agreements are P4 lower with P5 lower (+0.60) and with P5 upper (+0.63) — consistent with one source region coupled to several drives — while P4 upper's weaker pattern correlates with nothing, a caution against over-reading it.

Localization: the maps, and what they exclude

Four readings, in decreasing order of confidence:

  1. The P4 coil region is implicated twice, independently. P4 upper's survivor localises at (1.57, +0.71), immediately inboard-below the P4 upper winding; P5 lower's at (1.45, −1.20), immediately below the P4 lower winding. Neither drive is a P4 drive — a conductor near the P4 coils that responds to P5 currents cannot be inductive (the residual follows level, not rate) but is exactly what a shared supply return, a feeder loop, or a resistive structural path near P4 would produce.
  2. The implied currents are feeder-scale. Every rank-converged peak sits between 0.10× and 1.00× the supply current — far above any turn-count error (one turn is 0.043×… of the coil's own drive only) and far below the pair-sum edge artefact. The P5 upper fit wants precisely 1.00× the supply current at the centre column, where a supply conductor run would sit.
  3. No single axisymmetric filament explains everything. Peaks explain 43–60% of surviving power and disagree on location between groups. Either the source is extended (a current path, not a ring), several sources exist, or part of the survivor is non-axisymmetric (a busbar run) — which only the planned toroidal-harmonic and non-axisymmetric fits can separate.
  4. The P5 pair map runs off the grid. Its 79% peak sits at the scan boundary (R = 2.4 m, outside the vessel) at an unphysical −5.8× supply current: the two-shot pair pattern prefers a broad far field over any interior ring. More pair statistics (or loops) are required before reading it.

The differential-circuit hypothesis, tested

The hypothesis: P4/P5 serve vertical-field and position control, so shape- and position-controller demands could superpose and the two members of a pair could carry different currents than the record states — a differential circuit. Three measurements bound it:

  1. The member currents are independently measured. On every symmetric-pair shot the two recorded member traces correlate at 0.99995–0.999999 but are not copies: their difference spans 0.25–0.62% of the pair sum. The data survey confirmed the metrology end-to-end: separate transducers per member agreeing to 0.06% on P4 and 0.09% on P5 (least-squares upper/lower ratios 0.999463 and 1.000163 on shot 21978), against the P6 anti-series pair reading −0.27 — proof the measurement resolves a genuine differential when one exists. The described circuits are series pairs on a single supply (connections −1/+1, no extra nodes), and pf_active.supply is empty. Any controller superposition that reaches the coils is already in the recorded member currents the forward model consumes.
  2. The recorded difference does not drive the misfit. Partialling the pair sum and every other active drive out of both sides (Frisch–Waugh) and regressing each channel's residual on the member difference explains 0.2–1.2% of residual power on average per shot. The separation record's free inversion says the same thing more strongly: member currents solved freely per time sample still cannot remove the surviving field.
  3. Except obr10. On both fully-scored P5 pair shots the member difference explains 16% and 40% of obr10's partialled residual at a consistent +279/+293 nT/At (and 3–6% with the same sign on the P4 pair shots for obr04/obr10). obr10 is one of the six misfit carriers. A small genuinely-differential current path — or one member's slight misplacement making the pair's near-cancellation imperfect exactly at obr10's position — survives as a secondary, localised signature.

Verdict: as a driver of the main misfit, recorded-current superposition is excluded; as engineering context it stands. What remains open is precisely the part the record cannot see: current taking a path that is not the described winding — which is the missing- conductor lead again, now with obr10's differential signature as an additional constraint any candidate circuit must reproduce.

The flux loops are recoverable, and they are the missing rank

The separation record excluded flux loops because their absolute gain was not established. The data survey narrowed that worry considerably. The store publishes 29 fl_* channels in genuine total webers (0.15–1.5 Wb on a plasma shot; the reconstruction's own silop_dphi is 2π for all 46 loops, so every loop is toroidally complete), and the 2π convention question was already settled by measurement — MEASURED_FLUX_CONVENTION = 13 was set because the ratio of a loop's fitted flux to the reconstructed flux at its own position measures 2π (nova/imas/mast_solve_inputs.py:98-109). What is genuinely unestablished is a per-loop gain — and the fitting machinery already exists at nova/imas/mast_flux_loop_adjudication.py:588-672, fitting each channel's scale against a Biot–Savart loop response and currently discarding the result. The calibration plan is therefore consumption, not invention: promote stable fitted gains with the probe-scale evidence discipline, sweep loop channels for the acquisition block-scale steps first, and keep unstable loops excluded with cause.

Three structural facts bound the fit. The description carries 80 loops (44 closed, 36 saddle) with generic index names, no store channel names, and every calibration field empty; 26 of the 29 data-carrying channels land on a described position, and the archive's reconstruction validation names 7 loop positions the description cannot reach — fl_p4l_1..4, fl_p5l_3, fl_p6u_1..2. At P4 the two sets are almost disjoint: the description covers four P4-upper positions (three of which never carry data in 400 sampled shots) while the archive publishes P4-lower channels the description has nothing near — at exactly the coil the misfit localises to. And the archive's own EFIT gave every flux loop zero weight in every sampled shot (and zero weight to all 38 outboard probes), so no upstream reconstruction ever cross-checked these channels — obr17 and obv14 were never used by the machine's own fits, which is how a P4/P5-local description error could survive unnoticed for the machine's whole life.

The payoff is rank. The probe array resolves ten of thirteen coil directions and cannot separate a coil from its own case; flux loops sample the poloidal flux — a different functional of the source distribution with different geometric leverage — and directly constrain the misfit flux map that the contour programme needs. Loop inclusion upgrades three things at once: the current inversion (P2/P3/P6 wiring may become answerable), the source localization (ψ constraints between the probe bands), and the gate (more channels scoring the description). Whether it also changes the artifact-vs-incumbent comparison is a gate-owner question, same as obr17.

Caveats that bound every number above

  • Channels pool with uniform weight; no per-channel noise whitening is applied. The surviving shares and split-half cosines are robust to this, the exact peak locations less so.
  • The upper groups pool 7 rows each and the P5 pair only 2 — their maps are indicative, not evidential. P4 lower (13 rows, +0.92 split-half) is the only pattern to build on alone.
  • The filament scan removes the described-span component of each candidate column, so a conductor whose field pattern the coil set can nearly reproduce scores near zero everywhere — the maps localise only what is visible in the surviving misfit.
  • A single filament is the simplest source model; 40–57% unexplained surviving power says the truth is extended, multiple, or non-axisymmetric.
  • All statements about "the supply current" use the published 23 turns; if the turns are wrong the mA/At numbers stand but their supply-current multiples rescale.
  • Consumers joining the archive EFIT tables to the description: efm's outboard probe blocks run in the opposite order to the description's (efm indices 40–58 are obv, 59–77 are obr; the description names 40–58 obr and 59–77 obv). Positions pair correctly, so nothing moves in space — but raw index-order joins silently swap radial for axial across all 38 outboard channels. Join through the level-2 sensor_map, never by index.
