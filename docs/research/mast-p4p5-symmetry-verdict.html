The symmetry elimination test: the description's asymmetry is not the fault

Verdict: geometry error EXCLUDED as the source of the surviving misfit. Correcting the described P4/P5 member asymmetry — by mirroring one member from the other, symmetrising the pair centroids, or equalising the footprints — explains at most 3% of the stable surviving patterns and makes most of them worse (refit reductions −1% to −64%). The full per-member translation scan to ±25 mm finds nothing a coil position can fix: the best apparent explanations (0.29–0.36 explained fraction) sit at the scan boundary or collapse to 0.01–0.03 with sign-flipped gains when the probes within two pack widths of the coil are removed — they are near-field artifacts of the uniform-density section model, not evidence of a displaced coil. The one geometry component that survives every check is turn discreteness: driving the described 23-cell winding lattice instead of the uniform hull smear removes 15.0% of the P5 lower surviving power at fitted gain 1.28 (bootstrap [0.8%, 15.3%], far-field robust) and 11.4% on P4 upper (gain 1.14) — and it resolves the disputed 5-turn partial row to the described side of the pack (max |z|): flipping the row to the midplane side costs −24.7% on P5 lower. Everything else — at least 85% of the surviving power, and ≥95% of the only evidential pattern (P4 lower, split-half cosine +0.92) — needs current the coils do not carry, which hands the programme back to the feeder/conductor hunt with the geometry lead closed.

A premise correction: what asymmetry the model actually carries

The plan quotes P4 lower at z = −1.1268 m against P4 upper +1.1095 m — 17.3 mm with the lower member further from the midplane. The geometry the misfit was measured against says otherwise: the registry outlines (physical digest ca06c8f64481114f, built from the level-2 winding cells at shot 21978) put the P4 lower hull centroid at (1.4983, −1.0987) m and P4 upper at (1.4981, +1.1033) m — the upper member 4.6 mm further out, with the 0.159×0.158 m vs 0.154×0.154 m footprint split (area +5.1%) on the upper member; the source cell lattices give the same reading (∓1.0971/+1.1009, 3.8 mm). P5 differs by 8.7 mm (upper further out) with identical footprints. The 17.3/12.9 mm figures evidently came from a different source reading during the survey; the scan below tests the asymmetry the forward model actually consumed, and its translation grids (±25 mm) cover both readings' magnitudes with margin. The description's cells also already place the 23-turn winding's 5-turn partial row at max |z| on all four members — mirror-symmetrically — so the description is not asymmetric in the chamfer side, only in centroid and footprint.

Method

A geometry error in coil c produces a residual coupling on drive c equal to the variant's difference field: the field of the modified section minus the described section, per ampere-turn, computed with the same polygon-section Green's kernel that built the banked response (2510 kernel calls; the lattice variants drive the 23 described cells individually and average). Each variant is scored against its own drive group's surviving pattern three ways:

  1. Explained fraction f — squared cosine between the span-removed difference field and the survivor (the filament-scan metric): does the variant have the right shape.
  2. Fitted gain α — least-squares multiplier on the difference field. A real geometry error must fit at α ≈ 1 (its amplitude is not a free parameter); for translation grids the implied offset is α × the scanned offset while the response stays linear.
  3. Refit reduction — the variant's column replaces the described column, the measured coupling is corrected by the difference field, and the thirteen-column span removal reruns: the fraction of surviving power removed after the variant survives the same current freedoms that defined the target. This is the decisive metric — a variant that aligns but does not survive the refit has explained nothing.

Pooling and span removal reproduce the localization record exactly: per-channel medians over each group's (shot, group) coupling rows, channels with fewer than two finite rows dropped, obr17 excluded, QR projector of the described response on the kept channels. Bootstrap: the resampling unit is the (shot, group) coupling row — 13 rows P4 lower, 7 P4 upper, 12 P5 lower, 7 P5 upper, 2 P5 pair — resampled with replacement within group, the full pool-and-project pipeline rerun per replicate; B = 2000 for shape and gain, B = 1000 for refit reductions, seed 20260807, bounds are 2.5/97.5 percentiles. The two-row P5 pair bounds are indicative only. Far-field robustness: every leading variant is rescored with probes standing closer than two pack widths to the varied coil removed — the standoff below which the winding-lattice study measured the uniform-density section reading to break down, and the cut the response fits themselves apply.

A displaced coil moves its near field far more than its far field, so a translation can always buy alignment on the handful of probes beside the pack — the same probes on which the winding-lattice study measured a campaign-dependent misfit no static geometry can carry. When those probes are removed the P4 lower matches collapse two orders and flip sign; P4 upper's gain ≈ 1 candidates split into opposite-sign near and far fits, which no rigid displacement can produce. The 2D corner peak's bootstrap interval [−0.114, +0.722] spans zero, and its split-half explained fractions (0.293 / 0.537) disagree — against the survivor's own +0.92 stability. The P5 lower dr = −2.9 mm point estimate is instead the translation echo of the lattice component: the described cells sit 1.6 mm inboard of the hull centroid, and once the lattice is driven directly (α = 1.28, far-field robust) the translation adds nothing.

What this hands to the rest of the programme

  • To the provenance plan: the geometry-error candidate is closed as the survivor's source. The surviving misfit — 79% of P4 lower's coupling power, stable at +0.92 — needs current on a path that is not the described winding, at the feeder scale the localization maps already implied. The conductor hunt (supply returns, the P3/4/5 link board, the coil tail) is the remaining explanation standing.
  • To coil-geometry-inversion: (1) the banked difference-field grids in symmetry_scan.npz are ready-made linearisation points; (2) any geometry inversion over these couplings must exclude or separately weight the near-field probes — the ±25 mm basin is dominated by five channels whose misfit is known to be campaign-dependent, and chasing it buys sign-inconsistent geometry; (3) the forward model should drive the described cell lattice, not the hull smear — it is a strict, evidenced improvement worth 11–15% of surviving power on two groups at the correct amplitude; (4) the P4 lower survivor is not reachable by any P4 lower geometry freedom scanned here — a gradient solve that "finds" one will have found the near-field trap.
  • A discrete description fact settled: EFIT-MAST and FreeGSNKE disagree on which |z| side the 5-turn partial row sits; the archive's own cells and the magnetics agree it sits at max |z| (the described side), and the flipped branch is refused at −24.7% on the only group able to discriminate.
  • Not settled here (measurement-only): whether the lattice refinement is promoted into the forward model, and where any geometry correction would land (artifact revision vs fitted overlay) — the plan's open decision, untouched.

Caveats

  • The scan is linear and enumerated: translations, isotropic/anisotropic scales, mirror exchanges, and the two lattice branches. Tilts, ellipticity, and higher Kirk moments are not scanned; they are continuous freedoms for the inversion plan. Nothing here bounds a non-axisymmetric geometry fault (that path stays with the harmonics plan).
  • The upper groups pool 7 rows each (split-half +0.32/+0.42) and the P5 pair only 2 — their numbers are indicative. The verdict rests on P4 lower (13 rows, +0.92) and P5 lower (12 rows, +0.54).
  • Channels pool with uniform weight, no per-channel noise whitening — same convention as the localization record the targets come from.
  • The lattice difference field includes both turn discreteness and the 1.6 mm cell-vs-hull centroid offset; this scan does not separate them, only bounds their joint contribution.
  • Explained fractions are shape cosines against a pooled median pattern; a variant explaining 15% of surviving power removes 8% of surviving rms.
