Correction 1 — the obr probe axis was always in the source

The plan's premise ("the L1 source carries π/2 for all 78 probes; the correct axis has to be decided by prediction") turned out to be false: efm/magpr_ang carries exactly 19 angles of 0° and 59 of 90°, and the 19 radial ones sit exactly on the 19 obr positions (19/19 matched within 3 cm; ccbv never has a radial neighbour). 19 of the 78 source positions carry two co-located probes — one radial, one axial — and the recovery's _nearest_values broke that positional tie with np.argmin, i.e. by array order, which always returned the axial partner. The fix is a family-aware angle selection; the axis evidence class upgrades from fitted → measured (fitted count 13 → 12), with the vacuum refit's 1.35× held-out margin (6.78e-3 vs 9.15e-3 T) retained as confirmation rather than basis.

Correction 2 — coil_cases extents un-transposed

Every coil_cases plate had its radial and vertical extents transposed about its own centre; every other passive family is self-consistent under the builder's width-across-radius convention. Quantitative proof independent of efm: as built, 33.7% of total plate area (0.006484 of 0.019217 m²) sat inside the winding packs; corrected, 0.4%. Total area is identical either way — which is why area checks never caught it. All eight 4-plate groups now close into exactly one ring with the winding pack inside the hole (zero closed as built); the two 8-plate chamfered octagons (p3l/p3u) improve 26.9%→3.3% and 22.4%→2.6%, their residual being a box-representation artifact, not a transposition. Side effect: loop_sections coil_cases part count 32 → 24 as plates that now close an enclosure meet along shared faces (still multi-part, so the resistance disposition is unchanged). The registry half cuts case-plate bounding-box overstatement from 56.2× to 14.2× (p4: 62.3× → 16.1×); the residual 14.2× is the ambix reader's bounding-box collapse — the other half of that finding.
