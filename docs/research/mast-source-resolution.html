MAST source-resolution programme

The remaining gaps are now split by what they actually prevent. Parameters with a physically defensible prior are generated, versioned and refined against vacuum data. Discrete topology and sign choices are inferred only when the experiment identifies them. Public sources are admissible evidence when their machine, revision, units and coordinate conventions are recorded.

What the external evidence changes

A UKAEA study of NSTX, MAST and their upgrades describes the same workflow now adopted here: create an axisymmetric machine description from drawings, estimate conductor resistance from bulk-material properties, then use dedicated vacuum shots to refine passive resistance and location as well as magnetic-sensor position and orientation. It explicitly calls this an iterative process. See Battaglia et al., time-dependent vacuum-field calculations, pp. 6–8.

The original MAST diagnostic paper reports stainless-steel vessel and coil cases, an Inconel centre tube, in-situ calibration from individual PF-coil pulses, and an eight-vacuum-shot matrix solve that removed pickup and induced-current contributions. This is direct evidence that the archive contains experiments capable of identifying circuit, passive and diagnostic response: Edlington, Martin and Pinfold, MAST magnetic diagnostics.

For equilibrium specifically, UKAEA's MAST reconstruction work uses three-dimensional vessel calculations on vacuum coil-test shots and maps them to effective resistances in the two-dimensional EFIT vessel groupings. That demonstrates a legitimate reduced axisymmetric representation rather than a requirement for complete three-dimensional conductor detail at the start: Berkery et al., MAST equilibrium reconstruction.

Source hierarchy and promotion contract

  1. Existing physical evidence: the FAIR-MAST L1/L2 geometry and signal stores, the Nova registry and the recorded representation aliases.
  2. Authoritative public material: UKAEA papers, machine drawings, diagnostic schematics, technical reports and public shot metadata. MAST and MAST-U evidence must never be conflated. The source record stores URL, title, page/figure/table, publication revision, units, coordinate frame and uncertainty.
  3. Generated seed: nominal material properties, documented circuit structure, measured component geometry and bounded discrete candidates. Every seeded field is marked provisional and carries the assumptions used to produce it.
  4. Experimental refinement: vacuum shots with known coil excitations fit only identifiable parameter groups. Training shots and held-out shots are separated before fitting.
  5. Promotion: a value becomes authoritative for a supported shot range only when it predicts held-out responses, respects physical constraints and is stable across excitation families. Otherwise the seed remains provisional with uncertainty or the field stays unset.

The public FAIR-MAST service exposes a JSON metadata API and Zarr diagnostic data for campaigns M05–M09 under an open-data licence, while the UKAEA open-data portal exposes programme objectives including commissioning and TF test shots. These are discovery and calibration inputs, not proof by themselves: FAIR MAST and UKAEA MAST Data.

Active turns, polarity and circuit topology L1/L2 publish conductor geometry and measured circuit currents, but not a complete physical winding-to-supply contract. Solver filament multiplicity is not a turn count. Build a circuit manifest from public schematics and papers, then reconcile it with the EFIT current channels. Public evidence already states that MAST upper/lower coil pairs are normally series-connected, P6 is anti-series, and the solenoid uses two parallel circuits of alternating layers. Fit the signed coil-to-diagnostic vacuum response matrix. Passive material, resistance and electrical topology The outlines are present, but material grade, connection details and effective electromagnetic grouping are not encoded in the catalog. Assign component-family priors from documented stainless steel, Inconel or other sourced material and compute nominal resistance from resistivity, path length and section. Preserve a range for temperature and material uncertainty. Fit grouped effective R/L parameters to vacuum-current decay and loop/probe response. Hold out complete shots and at least one excitation family. Promote only positive, physically plausible, cross-shot-stable parameters; compare both transient waveforms and reconstructed passive current. Dynamic eddy-current fidelity. It is not a blocker: static equilibrium work starts with the versioned nominal model and later consumes calibrated parameters.
Detailed TF winding geometry The catalog carries the central-field quantity but not a complete installed 3-D winding path. MAST's dismantlable copper TF topology is documented publicly, but not yet reduced to a revision-pinned conductor model. Use UKAEA machine papers, figures, PROCESS's MAST topology description, public technical drawings and photographs to construct a bounded nominal winding path. Keep digitized dimensions and inferred symmetry visibly uncertain. Validate axisymmetric average against measured R Bφ; validate the detailed model separately against toroidal probe variation, ripple and known intrinsic error-field measurements. Do not tune a vacuum-only model to plasma-response residuals. 3-D ripple, error fields and structural loads. It does not gate the axisymmetric Grad–Shafranov reconstruction or the catalog GPU-solve census/parity ladder.
Independent toroidal-probe orientation The 36 catalog entries carry position only. Reusing a poloidal-probe orientation or a coordinate angle would silently change the measured field component. Extract diagnostic construction/orientation from the MAST magnetic-diagnostics paper and later schematics; initialize unresolved sensors with a normalized axis and a bounded installation-angle prior, never a fabricated exact value. Regress each sensor against known PF and TF vacuum excitations, jointly estimating gain and orientation only when identifiable. Require a unit vector, stable orientation across shots, correct sign, and held-out voltage/field residual below the pre-registered noise envelope. Toroidal-probe use in 3-D validation; it does not block poloidal magnetics-only equilibrium.
Poloidal channel-to-bank assignment The 78 primary probes have sourced sensitive-axis angles but their toroidal position is a discrete 150° or 330° candidate. A midpoint would be false precision. Form a binary assignment problem using channel names, wiring documents, array order and the two physical bank locations. Preserve both candidates when evidence is insufficient. Score candidates on vacuum-shot response and toroidally varying calibration/error-field shots, subject to one-to-one array-order constraints. Accept only assignments that are stable across held-out shots and decisively outperform the alternative; otherwise retain an explicit ambiguity. Correct 3-D diagnostic pose and shot-range identity; axisymmetric solvers may use the common R-Z pose without inventing φ.
Saddle traversal sign All 36 ordered 3-D paths and 972 vertices are present, but geometry alone is invariant under path reversal and therefore cannot determine Faraday-law signal sign. Keep canonical geometry direction-neutral and add a binary traversal/polarity candidate from wiring or acquisition metadata. Compare predicted signed flux derivative with known coil-pulse voltage. Require one sign to win on held-out shots, remain consistent with the acquisition polarity chain, and leave the geometric digest unchanged. Signed saddle measurements and 3-D validation; not static geometry or equilibrium.

For the TF source ladder, UKAEA's PROCESS documentation records the specific MAST topology in which copper TF inboard legs pass through the solenoid and the coils can be dismantled. It is a valuable topology source, not an installed-dimension authority: PROCESS TF-coil model. Likewise, measured MAST intrinsic error fields show that a vacuum approximation can differ substantially from the empirical correction, so vacuum geometry and plasma response must be validated as separate models: Kirk et al., MAST intrinsic error fields.

Execution order and artifacts

  1. Freeze the evidence manifest. Record every candidate source, supported shot range, revision, coordinate convention, uncertainty and licence. Reject MAST-U-only evidence unless it explicitly documents unchanged original-MAST hardware.
  2. Generate one provisional registry revision. Add nominal passive parameters, documented circuit relations and bounded diagnostic/TF candidates. The artifact must distinguish measured, published, generated, fitted and unresolved evidence states.
  3. Assemble the vacuum cohort. Use metadata to select no-plasma commissioning, individual-coil and mixed-excitation shots. Pin signal provenance and exclude contaminated time windows before any fitting.
  4. Fit by identifiable blocks. Circuit signs/turns first; passive R/L second; diagnostic orientation/bank/sign third; detailed TF geometry on a separate 3-D lane. Never let one block absorb another block's residual without an ablation proving identifiability.
  5. Challenge on held-out data. Report waveform residuals, flux/field residuals, parameter intervals, cross-shot drift and the nominal-versus-refined improvement. Negative results remain visible.
  6. Promote and cut over. Publish a content-addressed machine artifact. Run the frozen six-shot parity gate and explicit zero-import census before retiring only the superseded axisymmetric Ambix donors. Retain all 3-D donors until native Nova parity exists.
