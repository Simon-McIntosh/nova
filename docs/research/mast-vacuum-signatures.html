MAST archive vacuum signatures

The raw level-1 archive was swept pulse by pulse. Quiet windows identify additive offset and integrator drift; plasma-free driven windows compare the recorded coil currents with the exact Green-response columns to make opportunistic gain measurements. The bank is evidence, not calibration authority: every emitted correction has recorded status.

Transition catalogue

Measured bound — adjacent-pulse precision is unobtainable from the banked vacuum-window scalar observables. The raw series contains 1,445 apparent changes (1,522 blocks over 77 channels) against 37 established acquisition switches. A robust simultaneous-channel observable requires at least three accepted channels in one pulse and removes the alternating-median pulse factor in log gain. This retains 27,438 measurements over 3,462 pulses. On that same cohort, common-response cancellation removes 390 of 1,296 apparent changes (30.1%), but 906 remain — 869 more than the falsification set permits. The reduction is real, but it is not a discriminating acquisition observable.

For obv03, all five driven measurements survive the ratio cohort, but the states remain reversed in time: changed-state evidence appears at pulse 19,337 while old-state evidence persists through pulse 19,857. The bank contains zero independently classified pickup-response states and zero shot-resolved link-board states. That absence is decisive rather than clerical: after common-response cancellation, a channel-local pickup multiplier and a channel-local acquisition multiplier enter the scalar gain as the same product. Synthetic controls recover an isolated injected acquisition step exactly while also demonstrating that those two local causes are algebraically identical without an external shape or configuration label. The topology record independently states that no public shot-by-shot link-board inventory was recovered. The later shared-link P2 inversion is fully stable, but that is a cohort-level circuit-basis verdict rather than a per-shot configuration coordinate and therefore cannot label these scalar transitions.

Portability proof on TCV

The machine-agnostic claim passed on a non-MAST source. TCV pulse 77,549, run 1 provides pf_active, magnetics, and summary in its recorded DD 3.41.0. It carries 60,968 common time samples, 29 active-coil current traces, 38 poloidal-field probes, 38 flux loops, and a plasma-current trace. The adapter reads the IDSs with imas-python, first obtains the stored version from ids_properties/version_put/data_dictionary, reopens at that exact version, and converts only IDS nodes to arrays. No HDF5 dataset is read directly.

The executable path imports only the two generic calibration modules; every source-specific operation is confined to the test adapter that selects IDS nodes, aligns the recorded plasma trace, and states thresholds. Git blame pins both public kernel definitions to adb7cb70f, and this portability commit changes neither kernel. Artifacts and interpretation boundary

The durable bank contains signature_series.npz for dense channel time series, signature_store.json for chunk and refusal provenance, transition_catalogue.json for measured and established-boundary transitions, transition_observable.json for the common-response falsification and precision bound, and recorded_corrections.yaml with 1,676 schema-validated records. It was produced from the raw read path so an already-promoted block correction could not erase the transition being measured. Gain fitting is admitted only for probes at least two winding-pack widths from every energised coil; near-field channels still contribute their quiet-window instrument terms. No correction in this research record is promoted to the diagnostic read path.
