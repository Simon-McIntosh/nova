MAST archive vacuum signatures

The raw level-1 archive was swept pulse by pulse. Quiet windows identify additive offset and integrator drift; plasma-free driven windows compare the recorded coil currents with the exact Green-response columns to make opportunistic gain measurements. The bank is evidence, not calibration authority: every emitted correction has recorded status.

Artifacts and interpretation boundary

The durable bank contains signature_series.npz for dense channel time series, signature_store.json for chunk and refusal provenance, transition_catalogue.json for measured and established-boundary transitions, and recorded_corrections.yaml with 1,676 schema-validated records. It was produced from the raw read path so an already-promoted block correction could not erase the transition being measured. Gain fitting is admitted only for probes at least two winding-pack widths from every energised coil; near-field channels still contribute their quiet-window instrument terms. No correction in this research record is promoted to the diagnostic read path.
