Owner-cell current: attribution of the half-plane booking error

Node owner-cell-current. The axis-facing half-plane owner region was introduced to book the current over the cells its cut passes through. It improves the coarse carriers and fails the fine one: at 2,098 cells the diverted map error stalls at 2.4930e-4 against a guard of 1e-4, while the booking it replaces reaches 9.4726e-5 and the reconstruction floor is 1.7664e-5. This record attributes the excess to a term, tests the two candidate repairs, and reports which of the six done-when clauses the measurement supports.

The comparison reported a gap of 71 % on the current of cell 47 at 2,098 cells, 9.2 % on cell  7 at 282 cells, and 10.6 % on cell 8 at 550 cells, against a representation term of at most 3.5e-6.

Where the excess actually is

With the integral's accuracy and its density weighting both excluded, the remaining difference between the half-plane owner and the booking it replaces is the region share itself. At 2,098 cells the owner books 11.76 % of cell 47's area; the same cell's contribution was measured by the predecessor node at relative_moment_residual 3.3346e-3, and removing that one cell's residual takes the map error from 2.4930e-4 to 1.4740e-4 — 59 % of the excess over the floor. The owner's presence accounts for the whole of the remaining excess: booked 9.4726e-5 → half-plane 2.4930e-4 is a change of 1.5457e-4, and 9.4726e-5 is under the guard the half-plane misses.

So the failing term is the region-share arithmetic — which part of the cell is booked through the owner integral and which through the ordinary booking, and the fraction that reconciles them. That is within the scope of the lead ruling of 2026-10-17 ("keep the half-plane geometry, whose area is already right, and fix how current is integrated over the owner region"): the geometry and the area stay, and the share assignment changes. It is not the two candidate repairs this node set out to test, both of which are refuted above.

The traced and dense readings cut the same region — they now agree to 1.50e-5, 3.30e-5 and 3.07e-5 relative, so the 71 % gap this node reported earlier was entirely a defect in the dense reference and not in the booked integral. The reference solved its slice intercepts from the field's polynomial coefficients as though the slice basis were the coordinate axes while evaluating the field in the rotated basis the traced integral uses, so the two cut different regions. That is fixed: the slice quadratic is now composed from the field gradient and curvature tensor with the rotation applied.

The region both cut is not the intended one. The intended region is the normal-form lobe, and the lobe agrees with the independent analytic grid at every size: to 4.2e-4 at 282 cells, 2.3e-4 at 550 and 1.1e-4 at 2,098, against the submitted region's +0.2114 %, -1.0774 % and +18.99 % respectively. The submitted region is therefore too large by 19 % at 2,098 cells, too small by 1.08 % at 550, and too large by 0.21 % at 282 — a non-monotone error, which is why it helps two carriers and fails the third.

The cause is the conic, not the quadrature. The submitted integral bounds its region by the zero level of the fitted cell quadratic measured from the boundary, and that fit's stationary value sits 5.80e-8 above the boundary at cell 47, so its positive region joins through its own stationary point and reaches past the separatrix. The lobe is bounded by the separatrix itself. Raising the owner rule's order sixfold, which this node measured earlier as a 7.62e-9 move in the map error, cannot touch a 19 % region error: the two integrals agree with each other and disagree with the region.

The share defect

Which current is booked for a cell the owner region touches, and which is lost. At 2,098 cells, cell 47's ordinary cut books 0.460443 of the cell carrying 12.641941 A, and the normal-form lobe over that cell carries 0.784530 A. The submitted owner region books 0.933259 A over the 19 %-too-large region.

The share is not partitioned. map_moments replaces an owner cell's booking outright — ordinary_cut = cut & ~saddle_cut (owner_map.py:299-305) gives such a cell the owner integral and nothing else, and the cell is excluded from the full-interior booking because its membership is a fraction and not 1 (owner_map.py:288-291). So the part of the cell outside the owner region is booked by nothing, and the target-current rescale (owner_map.py:318) spreads that deficit across every other cell.

The rule. For every cell the owner region touches, the booked moments are the owner integral over the axis-facing lobe plus the ordinary cut integration over that same cell with the lobe removed, so the two parts tile the cell's own cut region exactly once and their sum equals the cell the ordinary route books alone. Neither the 19 % region error nor the dropped share can be repaired by a rescaling fraction: a fraction that reconciles two overlapping regions double-counts wherever they overlap, which is what the blend arms measured on this carrier already showed.

The ordinary route cannot supply the remainder. The partition's limiting case — hand the owner cells back to the ordinary whole-cell booking, which integrates the cell's own polygon with a fitted quadratic density — was measured on the profile representation (job 1288163, CPU): 2,098 reaches 1.61932398342e-2, 550 reaches 9.97172971635e-4 and 282 reaches 1.49396091282e-2, against 9.47260772731e-5, 4.87161187281e-4 and 1.0866014244493266e-3 on the lobe route. Every guard fails, by 162x, 2.0x and 13.7x. The ordinary fit mis-books these cells rather than merely overlapping the lobe, so ordinary = lobe + (ordinary − lobe) is not the partition: the remainder must be integrated by the owner machinery over the same ring fan with the lobe removed, then checked against a dense whole-cell reference.

No construction satisfies all three guards, and the two that pass at one end pass for opposite reasons. The submitted fitted quadratic is a whole-cell fit, so it tracks the flux best where the cell is large and its region is set by the cell's own shape; it is 19 % too large at 2,098 cells, where the cell is small and the fit's stationary value sits 5.80e-8 above the boundary. The exact-Hessian conic is a tangent cone at the saddle, so it is exact where the cell is tiny and progressively wrong as the cell grows — 9.3x and 18.8x over guard at 282 cells. That trade-off is the signature of a local tangent model against a global cell fit, and neither is the separatrix. The intended region is the separatrix-bounded lobe, whose curvature and cubic corrections the saddle normal form supplies: it matches the independent analytic sample at all three sizes (4.2e-4 / 2.3e-4 / 1.1e-4), which no conic above does.

So the repair is a region substitution, not a coefficient choice: bound the owner region by the normal-form rays the read already carries (fragment_selected, edge_interval) rather than by any conic's zero level, then book the density over that region with a rule accurate enough that a dense reference over the same region agrees to 1e-12 on area, current and first moments. The rays are already in the read, so this is bookkeeping inside the owner, not new geometry. The likely reason the coarse carriers preferred the fitted quadratic is that the conic error and the unpartitioned share partly cancelled there; once the share is partitioned there is no reason to expect that trade-off to remain.

What the lobe does not yet do. The share is still unpartitioned in the sense the ruling names: an owner cell is booked with the lobe integral instead of its ordinary cut booking (ordinary_cut = cut & ~saddle_cut, owner_map.py), and the lobe is a fragment of that cut region — 2.85 % of cell 47 against the cut region's 46 % — so the remainder is booked by nothing and the target-current rescale spreads it. At 2,098 cells that deficit is one cell in 2,098 and the rescale absorbs it, which is why the gate passes there; the partition the ruling asks for is not implemented. Neither is the independent dense reference at 1e-12 (the agreement above is against the accessor's own construction, not an independent one), nor the cold solve, the transition bracket, or the panels.

Declared negative control

The declared control was: "restoring the fitted Bell flux inside the owner region brings the 2,098 map error back above 1e-4". The control fails. Restoring the fitted representation moves the 2,098 map error to 2.49303535497e-4 — from 2.49299183493e-4, a change of 4.35e-9 — so the measure never fell, and the control cannot be demonstrated. That is the measurement's verdict on the repair, not a defect in the control: the representation is not a term of the error, so no mutation of it can move the measure across the guard. A control that tracks a term which does move the measure would have to mutate the region share, not the density representation.

Next action

The two repairs the lead's ruling of 2026-10-17 orders, in order, and where this node stands on each:

  1. Region. Established here: the traced region is the submitted half-plane bounded by the fitted quadratic's level-shifted zero set, it is 19 % too large at 2,098 cells, and the intended region is the normal-form lobe, which matches an independent analytic sample at all three sizes. The repair is to bound the owner region by the separatrix (the saddle normal form's ray pair, whose cells and intervals the read already carries as fragment_selected and edge_interval) rather than by the fitted quadratic, and then to require the traced integral and a dense reference over that same region to agree to 1e-12. That 1e-12 comparison is not yet made: the current agreement, 3.07e-5 relative in area at 2,098 cells, is quadrature-order limited by the owner rule's two-node inner rule.
  2. Share. The rule is stated above; the partition is not yet implemented. It needs the ordinary route to integrate the cell with the lobe removed, which today's bell_moments cannot express: it books the cell's cut region whole.

Until both land the gates cannot move: the map error is a region error of 19 % on one owner cell and a share error that the target-current rescale converts into a global distortion. Neither is a tolerance.
