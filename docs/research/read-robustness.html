Research input

Robust topology read on moving fields

The frozen reference lookup reproduces one valid read in 41 states on each topology. Reading all stored jets and censusing roots raises the valid share to 36/41 limited and 32/41 diverted with the moving analytic-axis check. The bisected class change has both sides valid and a raw-current jump of 7.781e-06 of target current. The measured read remains above its 0.12 ms target.

Method and positive controls

Each 41-state test uses the same 289-cell limited or 282-cell diverted analytic certificate carrier and a prescribed vertical-field slope from −0.2 to +0.2 reference flux span per pitch. The baseline is the retained Bell-row lookup and its original fixed seeds. The candidate contracts every stored six-component nodal jet, proposes extrema and saddles from each cell’s quadratic on a fixed lattice, polishes them on the Bell field, removes duplicate roots, continues accepted roots between neighbouring states, and sends every distinct saddle to the connected-support read. It has no saddle-size floor. A separate Newton solve on the closed-form analytic field gives the moving axis used for identity; its residual is recorded on every row. The final diverted arm books all selected support exactly while retaining split interior/cut current as a control. The analytic-moments arm and an actual newly cut node known missing from the frozen lookup are positive controls.

The complete coarse jet maps hold 1217 limited rows (50.65 MB) and 1164 diverted rows (47.27 MB). The frozen references populate only 916/1161 and 937/1188 row slots. Storage slack is therefore not lookup coverage. Every H200 payload used binary64, reported the imported nova path in its log header, and cleaned a per-job local temporary directory.

Limits and next implementation step

This is a prototype in scripts; it changes no product read or production seed. The per-cell quadratic proposes stationary candidates, while the full Bell field polishes them; a carrier with more candidates than the fixed slots returns overflow. The fixed-capacity gather follows tested new cuts but is not itself a fast connected-support read. Whole-cell exact booking is an accuracy control and adds cost; it does not establish the intended fused fast booking path. The measured full contraction, gather, census and complete read determine whether the 0.12 ms allowance is credible. Any remaining cold-seed or transition failure requires a separate mechanism repair before this route can replace the production read.
