Method and provenance

The unchanged nova.equilibrium.topology.read receives the ladder's limited and diverted analytic fields and realised hex geometries at requested 550, 2,000, 5,000 and 10,000 cells. The prototype scripts/prototypes/read_warm_wall/measure.py times the whole read and four separately compiled stages: sampled field, contour tree, null census and support selection. Each executable runs once to warm, then its second call is timed through jax.block_until_ready. Compilation and geometry construction are excluded. The terminal support and component-label calls are nested diagnostics; their walls overlap support selection and are not additive rows. The split read must match the whole read's validity and fragment-component labels before a receipt is written.

All eight rows ran inside H200 job 1289901 on 98dci4-gpu-0003 with gres/gpu:1; JAX reports cuda:0 in each row. The job log prints the imported nova and topology paths and revision 32f0f3edeb4ca3c0bc4eee1a7ba31559992ea4bd. Its full log and per-row JSON receipts are under /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261019T075302603047-read-warm-wall-profile/h200_split_1h.log and /home/ITER/mcintos/.config/reckon/crew/runs/r-20261019T075302603047-read-warm-wall-profile/h200_split/. The maximum absolute difference between the four separate stage walls summed and the whole read wall is 2.5% across the eight rows; small share values and values near 100% should be read with that split-executable limit. XLA logged internal CUDA delay-kernel timing warnings before the diverted 550-cell and 10,000-cell rows; these walls use host perf_counter through block_until_ready, not that internal event timer.

Dominant mechanism and repair

topology.py:2976 calls the contour-tree builder. In contour_tree.py, the carrier-edge pass at line 237 and two vertex sweeps at line 364 iterate over growing carrier arrays; the split-node pass at line 186 scans the edge table for every vertex, and lines 482–488 form a dense node-by-vertex equality matrix. These repeated scans and the dense pairwise array explain the measured superlinear contour-tree stage. The read's component propagation is not the dominant wall; its standalone terminal call is shown in the table.

The proposed repair is to use sparse indexed event and arc buffers for the sweeps and split insertions, and a scatter-built inverse vertex map in place of the dense equality matrix. Preserve stable value/index ordering, wall contact semantics, tree arcs, axis and X-point choices, and every cell's component labels and membership. Sorting then sets an expected O(N log N) cost, approximately exponent 1.0–1.2 over this range. The performance test should run the same four rungs for both analytic cases on an H200, assert a tree exponent at most 1.2 and a valid whole read at 10,000 cells no slower than 10 s, and compare tree structure and all topology outputs with the current implementation. The current 10,000-cell tree needs at least 8.9× speedup using the conservative 0.987 s whole-minus-tree remainder; that leaves a measured non-tree wall below 10 s, so the target looks feasible but unproved.

The focused baseline and head topology-read gates each passed 1/1 in fresh CPU processes. A broader three-test probe completed with the same two 300 s membership-test timeout IDs on both revisions; it added no failure ID. This node changes only prototype and evidence files. Product repair and a post-repair H200 gate remain separate work.

Sparse build: what the repair changed, and what it did not

The repair replaced the four named structures with sparse indexed buffers. Carrier adjacency is now a stable lexsort over compacted edge endpoints followed by a scatter into a padded neighbour table; the per-visit arc append is vectorised against a free-slot index and a cumulative rank; the split-node pass iterates only the vertices flagged split-only rather than every vertex; and the node-to-vertex inverse map is a reverse-order scatter instead of the dense equality matrix. SPARSE_INDEXED_BUILD marks the build.

Equivalence is by receipt, not by argument. At 550 and 2,000 realised cells on both analytic cases the sparse build returns byte-identical node_vertex, node_psi, node_valid, critical_type, edges, edge_valid and overflow against the base revision efd1d9553. The digests are pinned by tests/test_contour_tree_sparse_build.py and are the base build's own receipts, so carrier slot order, wall-contact semantics, tree arcs, and axis and X-point choices are shown unchanged rather than assumed.

The repair did not move the exponent, and the target is not met. H200 job 1290126 on 98dci4-gpu-0003 (cuda:0, revision 09d6f445) ran both builds over the same four rungs in one allocation.

The 10,000-cell whole read is 75.948 s limited and 72.033 s diverted against a 10 s target: the nearest figure is 72.0 s, 7.2× the target. The sparse build buys 5–8% of wall across the rungs, and within the fit's scatter the exponent is unchanged at about 1.50 rather than falling toward the predicted 1.0–1.2. Compilation is not the constraint: it grows no faster than linearly and is 8.9 s at 10,000 cells. The tree stage carries the read on every row — the base rows agree to within 0.06%, and on the sparse rows the separately timed tree wall exceeds the whole-read wall by up to 2.7%, which is the split-executable scatter the method section warns about rather than a real remainder.

Because the removed structures were real but the exponent did not move, they were not the dominant cost, and the O(N log N) argument that motivated them does not describe this kernel. The now-dominant operation is the contour-tree sweep itself, which is unchanged by this node: _sweep_tree runs a lax.fori_loop over vertex_count (line 382), and each of its sequential steps carries nine state arrays and calls the pointer-doubling while_loop in _neighbour_roots (line 115) plus an arc append. Two sweeps (join and split) therefore issue on the order of 2·N dependent dispatches whose per-step cost is dominated by dispatch and by the nine-array carry, not by the arithmetic — the shape that reads as an exponent near 1.5 instead of 1.0. That is the leading candidate the next node must instrument and confirm; it is named here as the remaining structure, not as a measured attribution.

Negative control. Restoring the base build's dense equality matrix verbatim (jnp.argmax over node_matches) and clearing SPARSE_INDEXED_BUILD turns tests/test_contour_tree_sparse_build.py red with a non-zero exit: the marker test fails, while the four receipt digests stay green — the digests are sensitive to the inverse map's semantics, which is what pins them to the base build rather than to the marker alone. The log and the applied mutation are recorded on the node's manifest.
