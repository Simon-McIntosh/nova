Reconstructed plasma source

A measured MAST equilibrium reaches the same current-prescription path as DINA, with a separately clocked coil-current series and an explicit flux-sign check.

Method

The MAST example is EFIT shot 21978 at 0.200000 s, read by nova's existing mast_efit reader. Its source COCOS is 3. That reader converts flux from Wb/rad to total Wb under nova's COCOS 17 convention; the EFIT p′ and FF′ remain derivatives on the reconstruction's own Wb/rad coordinate, so the prescription uses a derivative scale of one. EFIT's closed last-closed-flux-surface outline is selected as a stored boundary outline, without calling it a separatrix. That boundary and the 65 by 65 flux map prescribe 840,505.375 A with a current scale of 1.000039891. Thirteen measured coil-current channels have 7,500 samples on a clock independent of the equilibrium slice.

The comparison evaluates plasma flux using nova's Green kernel. EFIT's fitted conductor currents and filament geometry supply the conductor contribution to EFIT's own total ψ map, and the common arbitrary flux offset is fixed at the magnetic axis. These fitted currents serve only to separate the stored reference map; the generic source carries the separate measured coil series. A nonempty-reference control found 118 held points with nonzero plasma and conductor responses and a 0.398628031 Wb axis-to-boundary flux span.

Identity and negative control

On ITER DINA 105014/1 at 5.927598 s, the generic source gives the same prescribed cell-current array, boundary, plasma current and scale factor as the existing native-IDS path. The source interpolates twelve coil-current channels at the equilibrium time from their own 2,027-row IDS clock. The focused gate also runs the original prescription tests.

The prior current-density function accepted a MAST reconstruction whose ψ map, profile coordinate, axis ψ and boundary ψ were all sign-flipped while the plasma current was held fixed; it still prescribed 840,505.375 A. The generic source checks the plasma-current sign against the axis-to-boundary ψ ordering before prescription and refuses that mutation. The retained red mutation log and the passing head-arm test make this a fired guard rather than a suite-only claim.

Reconstruction route into the coupled plant

The narrow coupled_plant call now takes a generic plasma source wherever it took a DINA case, so a reconstruction or a simulation reaches the plant through the same interface the native path uses. Measured at 42dc8ef43 on DINA 105014/1: through the generic source the call returns today's plant exactly — coil inductance and resistance, section and modal plasma flux, plasma times, the section block and the modal coil coupling are bit-for-bit equal to the plant built from a minimal source carrying DINA's own prescription (maximum absolute flux difference 0.000000000 Wb).

The same slice was then presented the way a reconstruction arrives rather than the way an IDS export does: EFIT-style p′ and FF′ on DINA's own ψ normalisation, the stored last closed flux surface named as the boundary outline, and the coil currents resampled to every fourth row of their own clock. The tolerance was fixed before the comparison, at a relative 1e-3 against the DINA-path plant. What bounds it is where the two presentations draw the boundary: the native route hands over the stored separatrix outline, which closes within the polygon tolerance and is therefore the polygon prescribe_current_density resolves first, while the reconstruction route hands over the stored boundary outline with no separatrix to prefer. Both enclose the same plasma; their discretisation differs, and that difference propagates into the flux the plant links at each winding. The coarser coil cadence does not enter the prescription at all — at the slice time both cadences interpolate the same currents — so it changes the series the plant keys on and nothing the plant derives from the plasma. Measured: 1.163e-05 relative on the section flux and 1.163e-04 on the modal projection, both inside the declared tolerance, with the plant's provenance naming the reconstruction-style source.

Scope

This record measures one MAST reconstruction slice and one DINA slice. Positions outside the stored MAST ψ grid are not compared. The plant-return check awaits the narrow coupled-plant call; this record asserts only this source and its prescription/linkage checks. No JT-60SA data or figure is used.
