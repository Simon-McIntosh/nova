Reference sign guard

Every external field states its own signed-flux orientation - a trace record a polarity, an IDS equilibrium the COCOS of the Data Dictionary version it was written in together with the sign of its plasma current - and scripts/prototypes/efit_field_swap/orientation.py already turned that statement into the read convention. Nothing checked that the flux the reference stores agrees with the statement. This node adds that check and drives it over the measured case.

The criterion

A declared convention fixes the magnetic axis as a flux extremum: with sigma = +1 the axis is a psi maximum and psi_axis > psi_boundary, with sigma = -1 a minimum. sigma is computed by TopologyConvention.from_cocos from the written convention and the plasma-current sign, so DDv3 COCOS 11 with negative Ip and DDv4 COCOS 17 with positive Ip both require +1 and agree. The guard reads the sign of the grid psi at the stored axis minus the grid psi over the stored boundary. The difference cancels any additive gauge constant, so a reference cannot satisfy the check by re-choosing its zero. The stored scalars are checked with the same difference, so a grid that happens to agree cannot hide scalars that do not, and the reverse. A refusal names the slice, the declaration and both measured signs.

The guard is applied to all twenty-eight field-swap trace records, because a guard that refuses a good record is as wrong as one that reads a bad one. Negative control

The declared mutation removes the sign-consistency guard. With the two guard entry points replaced by no-ops, the negated-grid test fails: test_negated_grid_against_its_scalars_is_refused is in the red log with EXIT=1. The synthetic field is an IDS-shaped grid whose stored scalars put the axis at a psi maximum and whose grid is the negation, placing it at a minimum; the guard refuses it, and adding any constant to the grid leaves the verdict unchanged.

Suite arms

Both arms ran the same five test files - the efit_field_swap and tcv_liuqe_known_answer test modules - with the repository-root conftest, in fresh per-process invocations, the baseline from a base-revision scratch tree and the after arm from this worktree. The baseline arm reached its summary with one failure, test_limited_slice_is_read_only_when_coverage_is_certified (a pre-existing slow real-store case, unaffected by this node), and 32 passes. The after arm did not complete inside the bound that was applied: it reached thirty-one tests and was killed at 480 s before its own summary, so its exit status is 124 and no after-arm summary line exists. The suite delta is therefore not measured by this run; the node's own behaviour is instead evidenced by the nine passing unit tests, the twelve-of-twelve refusals over the TCV store and the zero-of-twenty-eight field-swap refusals, and the red negative control. Completing the delta - both arms under a longer bound, or with the one slow real-store test excluded by an explicit selection - is left as a follow-on.
