Reference sign guard

Every external field states its own signed-flux orientation - a trace record a polarity, an IDS equilibrium the COCOS of the Data Dictionary version it was written in together with the sign of its plasma current - and scripts/prototypes/efit_field_swap/orientation.py already turned that statement into the read convention. Nothing checked that the flux the reference stores agrees with the statement. This node adds that check and drives it over the measured case.

The criterion

A declared convention fixes the magnetic axis as a flux extremum: with sigma = +1 the axis is a psi maximum and psi_axis > psi_boundary, with sigma = -1 a minimum. sigma is computed by TopologyConvention.from_cocos from the written convention and the plasma-current sign, so DDv3 COCOS 11 with negative Ip and DDv4 COCOS 17 with positive Ip both require +1 and agree. The guard reads the sign of the grid psi at the stored axis minus the grid psi over the stored boundary. The difference cancels any additive gauge constant, and the tolerance is scaled by the grid's range rather than by its absolute value, so even a large change of zero cannot turn a contradictory sign into an undecided one. Grid values are sampled through the same tensor spline used by the comparison read. The stored scalars are checked with the same difference, so a grid that happens to agree cannot hide scalars that do not, and the reverse. Where a trace wall has no point inside its grid, the spline Hessian at the stored axis supplies an offset-independent maximum-or-minimum check. An IDS slice with no boundary is refused with that reason and its slice and declaration. A sign refusal names the slice, declaration and measured signs.

Negative control

The declared mutation removes the sign-consistency guard. With both guard entry points replaced by no-ops, pytest exits EXIT=1 with eleven failures. These include the negated-grid and TCV read-path tests, and both new trace read-site tests. The TCV test then reaches the topology read. The synthetic IDS-shaped field declares DD 3.41.0 (COCOS 11) and negative Ip, with stored scalars making the axis a psi maximum and a negated grid making it a minimum. The live guard refuses it, and adding a constant as large as 1e10 to the grid leaves the verdict unchanged.
