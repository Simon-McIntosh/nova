Merge-path saddle admission on the axis merge path

Measured evidence fragment

The defect

In scripts/prototypes/fast_map_production/kernel.py the read classified every valid root as a saddle by Hessian signature and took saddle_index = 1 + jnp.argmax(saddle_score_rows) over sigma * value. The roots come from nova.equilibrium.fixed_points.solve (or, in the prototype family, read_robustness.census), called at program.py:172. On the 41-state sweeps a second root enters the census at the wall contact. That root ties the wall level by construction: the wall candidate is _wall_effective_level(tangency, bottleneck) = minimum(tangency, bottleneck), the bottleneck of the private-flux contact is the just-admitted saddle's own level through _wall_path_ceiling, and the wall contact the census returns carries that level, so min and the saddle agree — at the tie, to the last bit. The root wins the argmax, then leaves the census between adjacent doubles.

Bisected at 282 and 550 cells, the booked current steps by +114.75 and +125.05 across the island's two edges against a boundary-flux move of at most 6 × 10−7; the prior tie-island record bisected both islands to adjacent binary64 and attributed the step to this selection rather than to the class comparison, which is label-only.

The repair

One owned function, _merge_path_saddle, replaces the argmax and the decision is taken before the wall-versus-saddle contest. The merge path runs from the magnetic axis out to the boundary, and the saddle that bounds it is the one the axis reaches at a level at least its own: the maximin passage from the axis to the saddle's own cell, taken from the ungated _wall_path_ceiling, meets or exceeds the saddle's level. A wall-contact root the axis reaches only below its own level is off that path even though its paired extremum ties the boundary level. Candidates are ranked by the passage margin axis_ceiling - level, so a tie between wall contact and saddle can move only the classification label and never the admitted saddle. The function is fixed-shape and traced, resolving its index by argmax over a masked margin with no host-side branch. psi_rounding_enclosure states the binary64 enclosure of the fitted psi — the machine epsilon times the fit's absolute row sum — so a saddle/extremum pair closer than it can be cancelled as one number before admission.

The census is not at fault at the observed tie

The departing wall-contact root's |det H| is 1.6 × 10−4 at 282 (offset 0.16133) shrinking to 5.4 × 10−5 before it leaves, four to two orders of magnitude off roundoff, so its exit is not a fold and nova/equilibrium/fixed_points.py is left unmodified. The root finder stops returning a genuine saddle with large curvature; the merge-path admission removes the step without touching the census. A census repair certifying Newton convergence against a computed rounding bound would be owed only if the departure coincided with the rounding floor, which it does not.

Result

At 282 cells the boundary class no longer switches anywhere in the window (base: two switches; head: zero) and the admitted saddle level is the primary X-point's continuous trace (−0.01040 → −0.01133) where base jumps onto the wall contact (+0.00799 … +0.00862) and back. At 550 cells the same: base carries six class switches, head carries none, and the admitted saddle is held on the primary X-point. The boundary flux is unchanged between adjacent samples at both arms. The gate — tests/test_fixed_points.py, tests/test_fixed_point.py and test_bottleneck_level.py, CPU, fresh process — reports 66 passed. The negative control (restoring the argmax inside _merge_path_saddle) turns test_path_admission_rejects_the_wall_contact_root red at EXIT=1.
