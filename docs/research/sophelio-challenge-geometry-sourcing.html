§1 — Premise correction: coil geometry ships

The research reference recorded on coil-geometry-inversion (2026-08-07) stated the challenge publishes coil currents and flux maps but no coil geometry. Verified against the challenge data page on 2026-08-11, that premise is wrong: the dataset ships per-shot conductor geometry — coil_R, coil_Z, coil_width, coil_height, plus skew angles for six DIII-D F-coils — and Thomson chord coordinates (thomson_chord_R, thomson_chord_Z), with the 65×65 efit_grid_R/Z in metres. Coil currents are ampere-turns (kA·turn), which dissolves most turns ambiguity by construction (declared exceptions: ECOILA, bcoil on DIII-D and the MAST solenoid/TF/EFPS channels, which keep uncertainty fields). The full verified dataset schema, scoring and rules live in the companion doc imas-ambix:sophelio-fusion-equilibrium-challenge.

Consequence for coil-geometry-inversion: its challenge-facing deliverable — recover a machine's coil description from published flux maps and currents — loses necessity for the entry, but the machinery gains two better-posed challenge applications: (a) validating the shipped DIII-D geometry against label-derived fields (a shipped description is a claim, not a truth; the identifiability spectrum says which of its parameters the data can check), and (b) inferring what does NOT ship — wall contour and passive/vessel structure — from labeled ramp frames, where eddy fields dominate the mismatch. The MAST-side science deliverable (registry-truth oracle development) is unchanged.

§3 — Reuse inventory for the DIII-D onboarding

Three landed nova capabilities map directly onto the onboarding plan's hardest objects. The polygonal-section coil kernels (Urankar-class analytic Green's functions, ~1e-13 accuracy) are exactly what the six skewed DIII-D F-coils need — parallelogram sections are the case the capability was built for. The FSA extraction and p′/FF′ projection machinery built for the transport seam already does most of what the label-extraction kernel needs (Δ* on a gridded map, per-surface separation); the new work is conditioning-aware uncertainty near axis and X-point plus the vacuum-region Δ*ψ≈0 receipt. The MAST vessel circuit fit supplies the zero-shot machine's passive model without touching challenge or FAIR-MAST signals. The accelerated boundary-push / NK solve work and the catalog-scale batching machinery carry the throughput requirement; the onboarding plan publishes the measured budget rather than assuming one.
