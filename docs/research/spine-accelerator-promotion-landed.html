The first-ever coverage of Topology.boundary caught a live defect. The selector's vertical shadow bounds were typo'd (nova/jax/topology.py assigned x_height_min twice, the second time from x_height_max with inverted values), so any finite x-point stole the binding from a confined-most wall tangency — a limited plasma beside an in-grid saddle could never bind at the wall. The shadow rule is restored (a wall contact vertically beyond the x-point band lies in a null's private-flux shadow; a side with no null beyond the axis casts no shadow) and pinned six ways in tests/test_topology_boundary.py, with the ionization mask and LCFS flux covered alongside.

§2 — The promoted accelerators

nova/jax/fixed_point.py carries the three batched inner solvers as package symbols with one result contract and one per-map-evaluation residual trace (NaN in Newton tangent slots), so budget ladders compare fairly. All are jit/vmap-safe with fixed shapes:

  • picard — relaxed iteration (the status-quo reference);
  • anderson — Walker–Ni type-II mixing: ridge-regularised history solve, warmup delay, restart on residual growth, the accepted move rescaled to a cap against the plain relaxed step (the library-grade mixer's semantics — the probe script's reject-on-cap degenerates to Picard on strong contractions), non-finite fallback;
  • newton_krylov — exact-tangent Jacobian-free NK: one jax.linearize per step, fixed-shape batched GMRES on (I−J)s=f with no early exit, relaxed-step fallback and cap; 2+m map evaluations per step.

ReconstructProfile gains the seam and the two donor guards: least_squares_map exposes the un-relaxed sweep map for external drivers (the axis seed pins the flood basin — the current-centroid pin); profile_relaxation independently damps the least-squares coefficient update; the scan carries the fixed-shape degradation branch (a sweep whose topology read fails keeps the previous coefficients and flux instead of absorbing into NaN); and ProfileResult.axis now reports the sub-grid O-point of the solved flux instead of the static seed.

§3 — The re-measured ladder and the two locks

At the shared 40-evaluation budget every well-posed slice converts under all three schemes — this synthetic family does not reproduce ambix's hard cold-seed conversion gap — but the residual depth separates decisively: Anderson 3.9e-14 and NK 5.9e-14 median versus Picard 6.7e-10, four decades at equal cost. On the rich-profile (2,2) slices with consistent data NK converges deepest (6.9e-7 at 40 evaluations; 7.2e-11 as the 72-evaluation bootstrap driver, where plain Picard limit-cycles). The two unconverted slices carry bootstrap data that never became self-consistent and defeat all three schemes identically — a data-consistency failure, not a scheme failure.

accelerator-primary → nk-jvp. The nova re-measurement shows NK at or beyond Anderson everywhere it matters and never worse than tolerance-relevant margins; the carried ambix evidence (62% vs 19%/10% cold-slice conversion on real diverted maps, where Anderson tied Picard) supplies the hard-map discrimination this synthetic family cannot. Anderson and Picard stand as the safeguarded fallback ladder.

profile-state → implicit-fit. NK converges the implicit-fit map everywhere the data is self-consistent, including the under-constrained rich family, so the coefficients never need to enter the Newton state; damping plus the degradation branch hold the confined branch. The §2 item-5 coupled-KKT lift is retired unless MAST catalog solves surface coefficient thrash the damping cannot hold. The damping comparison itself came out protective-only on this family (undamped runs converge faster and nothing escapes to the outboard corner synthetically) — the escape evidence remains ambix's held-out measurement, and the parameter defaults to 1.0 (off).
