The active-set stall is now a two-machine result

MAST 22086/43 pure terminated active-set stagnated at a relative-sup residual of 9.001840977909396×10−4 after 9 of 16 allowed trips. Trips 7 and 8 carried the same settled mask and the same residual bit for bit. This is the DIII-D stall signature reproduced on MAST at the native 65-by-65 resolution, through a source revision that includes own-mask acceptance. The own-mask-aware adaptive descent model is therefore the convergence blocker for the quarter-width programme on both machines, not a DIII-D oddity.

Scientific verdict. Resolution improves the selected null position by a factor of 2.83, to 8.463 mm, but this run does not measure boundary discretisation. Its 9.0018e-4 relative-sup residual is roughly five orders above the unchanged 1e-8 convergence criterion. The large boundary distances below are convergence artifacts and must not be used to accept or reject the quarter-width boundary gate.

The stall unifies MAST and DIII-D

The first two trips changed 970 and 1,055 active-set cells. Trip 2 then reached zero mask difference, and every remaining trip retained zero. Residual descent continued through trip 7, after which trip 8 repeated both the settled mask and 0.0009001840977909396 exactly. This is the first measured activation of the production active_set_stagnated exit; it avoided spending the seven remaining budgeted trips.

A zero mask difference is not itself evidence of settlement. The committed 33-by-33 solve reached a false zero at trip 2 before changing again, so three consecutive zero-difference trips are the minimum useful confirmation. This trajectory supplies seven consecutive zeros before the repeated-residual exit. The mask trajectory is 970, 1055, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0, 0.

The null improves with resolution

The selected Nova saddle moves from 0.02396426750340677 m from the nearest EFIT X-point on the 33-by-33 solve to 0.008463463498677284 m on the 65-by-65 solve. The distance falls by 0.015500804004729484 m, a 2.8315-fold improvement. The native-grid result is only 0.000885463498677285 m, or 0.885 mm, outside the locked 0.007578 m quarter-width gate. Null localisation therefore responds in the expected direction even though the equilibrium has not converged.

The boundary numbers are stall artifacts

Binding RMS rises from 0.02891128908081361 m to 0.18888046534076347 m, and sup from 0.16610684834559725 m to 0.46288669662867227 m. The lower-null sector carries the separation: the lower EFIT X-point lies 0.4630516093028299 m from the native-grid Nova boundary, against 0.16625530761395624 m on the converged 33-by-33 state.

Comparing a state stalled at roughly 9e-4 against a settled state at roughly 2.6e-16 measures convergence, not discretisation. The committed 33-by-33 bank row is independently marked converged against the same 1e-8 criterion. Consequently the native-grid 0.18888 m RMS is not a resolution verdict, and the discretisation question stays open until a converged 65-by-65 solve exists.

Native-grid operational cost

The response has shape 4262 by 101: 4,225 grid targets plus 37 wall targets, across all 101 fitted circuits. The solve then took 16848.899298 s. Its first three callbacks arrived 809, 3266, and 4486 s into the solve; individual callback intervals over all nine trips ranged from 808.683 to 2458.775 s. Unlike the compiled 33-by-33 arm, the full-grid trajectory shows no warm-trip speedup.
