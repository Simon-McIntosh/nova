Research input

Sub-millisecond converged forward design

The 282-cell diverted certificate already clears the analytic-state flux-map ceiling by a factor of sixteen, yet the current live map takes about 10.2 ms and a qualified warm correction takes hundreds of milliseconds. The connected read and exact cut booking dominate. This document distinguishes measured execution from a conditional latency budget for a redesigned solve.

Numerical contract and starting measurement

The consuming plan's joint acceptance requires a solved-equation residual and each scaled constraint row at most 10−12, nulls within one pitch, analytic-state flux-map error at most 10−2 on certificate rows, one executable per shape, a cold compile at most 60 s, an executable at most 50 MB, host compile memory at most 64 GB, and jit/vmap of the production solve. A low residual on an inaccurate map does not pass. The coarse 282/289-cell certificate is a design probe; the section's certified rows include 2,000, 5,000 and 10,000 cells, centroid-constrained rows, and DIII-D frames.

The H200 lean-map study measured one analytic-state map at 282 diverted cells: connected read 6.120 ms, exact cut batch 4.322 ms for 57 cuts, interior Gauss booking 0.244 ms, roots 0.317 ms, and two field-map contractions 0.743 ms in separately compiled stages. The fused map was 10.176 ms. Its analytic flux error was 6.19 × 10−4, against 1.43 × 10−4 at 289 limited cells. The stage timings overlap in the fused call and are not additive. The 34.4 MB values map and 48.2 MB jet gather have a combined ideal HBM read floor of 0.0172 ms, so the present latency is dominated by algorithms and launches, not by a proven memory-bandwidth floor.

The same study reported 40 refusals in 41 diverted vertical-field perturbations; the zero-current map had wrong-root or invalid-booking status on both topologies. Its earliest qualified warm correction took 291.7 ms limited, while a diverted five-correction run took 725.6 ms and still missed the strict residual. Cold compilation was about 80 s. No published row has demonstrated a sub-millisecond converged solve.

Candidate measurements

The H200 comparison uses the read-only 282/289-cell device-built analytic carriers from the lean-map run, binary64 JAX, seven synchronised warm calls per candidate, and one compiled call per row. The compact fragment graph independently reproduced the current read's selected component; its diverted carrier contains six excluded fragments, so an intentionally all-selected control disagrees on six, proving the agreement check can see private support. The limited carrier has no excluded active fragments and cannot serve that negative control. The graph is compacted on the host after the exact read; its dense timing is a lower bound on a dynamic-shape implementation, not a fixed-shape or vmap-safe production wall. The exact label and dense timings start with fragments and intervals already computed, so neither pays for the expensive per-cell strip construction.

H200 job 1285734 built adjacency on device at all 578/564 slots, performed ten dense Boolean squaring rounds and matched exact active-fragment selection on both carriers. Its vmap check compiled and ran two identical members with identical outputs. This closes the host-compaction timing gap for these two shapes, but does not test different topology states under one batched call, nor the cost of building the Bell fragments it consumes. The full-capacity wall is 0.547 ms limited and 0.341 ms diverted: a modest 0.115 ms win over exact label propagation on diverted and a slight loss on limited. A fresh fixed-capacity device join sweep in H200 job 1285746 sorted 289/282 cell-centre levels, merged component labels without dynamic root loops, and returned both wall and foreign-basin levels in one program. A four-node control returned wall 1 and saddle 2; moving the only wall node into the foreign basin delayed the wall result to 2, so the instrument observes private-wall exclusion. On the certificate it still took 37.09/47.92 ms. The diverted saddle level, 0.005697, lay near the reference 0.005710, but the limited case returned 0.331 against reference 49.941 in its outward coordinate, and the diverted 41-state sweep jumped by 1.119 reference spans between adjacent samples. Its identical-input width-two vmap passed. The measured formulation therefore fails latency, boundary parity and continuity; a sorted sweep over cell centres is not an accurate stand-in for EFIT's wall-constrained nodal join tree.

The fixed-shape sorted cell-graph union scan took 1,070 ms limited and 1,907 ms diverted, measured as one Python dispatch of a compiled executable. The limited arm found wall level 0.33095 in axis-relative outward flux and no saddle; the diverted arm found wall level 2.27 × 10−6 and saddle level 0.005697. The diverted wall event is inconsistent with the exact diverted classification. A private-wall false candidate is plausible because the scan lacks EFIT's PRIVATE classification, but the cell-graph discretisation is another possible cause; this probe does not separate them. Its cost and result reject this literal serial GPU translation, not the join-tree concept. A parallel event formulation must prove both correctness and latency. Kernel launch counts inside each executable were not captured by this Python call timer.

Nova's existing fixed-capacity join/split contour tree, timed independently in H200 job 1285731 on the same carriers, takes 457.2 ms limited and 406.1 ms diverted after its vertex flux is supplied. The receipts contained 289/282 valid carrier vertices, 818/805 edges and no overflow; the tree emitted 6/6/1/1 and 4/5/2/1 minimum/saddle/maximum/outside nodes. The nonzero census and differing node counts establish that this is a real tree computation, not an empty call. It is too slow for the warm map even before a boundary or booking, and it does not expose EFIT's two candidate levels directly.

The connected support read is not the Reeb graph. topology.read uses a contour tree to order its null census, then resolves support by Bell fragments and label propagation at a chosen level. The lean-map route calls read_at_stationary_points and skips tree construction because its roots are supplied. A join tree tracks component mergers across levels; the one-level fragment flood answers which pieces belong to the axis now. Marching squares traces an outline at a level and needs a separate inside and wall rule. It cannot replace the boundary decision merely by being faster at drawing a line.

The current exact graph propagation is under 0.8 ms, while the full connected read is 6.120 ms; replacing propagation alone cannot recover the full 6 ms. The remaining strip geometry and saddle-candidate work must be isolated and vectorised. A marching-squares trace from the X-point and a wall-domain inside test have not been implemented in this fixed-shape probe; EFIT's host contour is an outline renderer and its documented flat-tangent wall failure disqualifies it as a boundary decision without a new domain construction. Neither that candidate nor the local rule has a valid analytic-map error row.

quadratic_cell_fragments sorts a fixed list of conic/edge events, then executes a fori_loop of minimum-label propagation over every local strip before a 32-node transformed Gauss area rule. Those are concrete candidates for the unmeasured remainder of the read wall. The next stage probe must time event construction, local strip propagation, area quadrature, saddle forms and inter-cell graph separately on the same inputs; assigning the entire 5.6 ms remainder to one of them without that separation would repeat the instrument mistake this comparison is meant to prevent.

Refusal mechanism and cold seed

fixed_points.solve first follows the supplied root seeds through an adaptive local Hessian Newton. It retries a wrong Hessian type from the previous accepted point, then rejects a wrong type, nonconvergence or any root outside the retained Bell-row lookup. support_read.read combines those statuses with topology validity; current.book makes invalid moments nonfinite. Thus a zero-current input can fail before the outer moment solve obtains a usable residual or tangent. The reference-band lookup has storage slack but no rows for an unanticipated node, so padding its capacity alone cannot repair a moving band.

EFIT's measured false fixed point from a single wall-adjacent filament shows why a local seed can be a basin selection, not just an initial guess. H200 job 1285726 compared zero, uniform and centroid-circle physical current moments against the valid analytic state at equal nonzero total current for the distributed arms. This is a map-entry probe with the analytic root seeds and reference Bell-row lookup, not a converged production-seed solve. The analytic arm returned finite output and all zero statuses on both carriers, so the instrument can read a known valid state.

Root statuses are 0=CONVERGED, 1=NEEDS_ROWS, 2=WRONG_ROOT, 3=NOT_CONVERGED. Booking status 2 is invalid; band status 1 is a newly cut cell outside the retained lookup.

The design therefore needs a mobile row window and a root census or continuation robust to a large field change; distributed current alone is not the fix. The exact production-seed, perturbed-step and accepted-Newton tests remain required before claiming cold-start robustness.

A separate production limited-seed probe on the coarse bank captured the actual ReconstructMoment.uniform_disc output in the cold portfolio. At requested current 1,627,957.013 A it populated 161 cells and summed to 1,627,957.013 A; at requested zero current it populated zero cells and summed to zero. An ExplicitSolveSeed carrying the saved warm flux remained bitwise identical after constructing the zero-current cold portfolio. The seed builder zeroes only the two first-moment arrays beside a nonzero zeroth-current array; the explicit warm resolver does not traverse that builder. This closes the suspected shared-path current-erasure mechanism for the limited production entry point, while leaving its zero-current topology refusal and the diverted production path to be qualified separately.

A fresh H200 vertical-field sweep on the current source (job 1285732) reproduced only one valid read among 41 sampled states. At the bisected limited/diverted class switch, the two boundary flux readings differ by 0.61448 of the reference span, but both sides have cut status 2 and no valid booked current; that pair cannot establish a discontinuity of an accepted solve. The inspected X-point candidate is absent on one side. The retained jet table has capacity 1,188 but only 937 required reference rows; at an interior-band crossing both states are invalid and the fixed lookup is incomplete. These status and coverage checks explain why a formally differentiable local field interpolation cannot make the overall read usable across a perturbed trajectory.

The same job's wall-local perturbation again had only one valid read among 41 sampled states. Its bisected limited/diverted boundary level changed by just 7.34 × 10−10 of the reference span, supporting candidate-level continuity there, but one side was invalid and the route's booked current jumped by 1.285 times the reference current. The failure is not solved by adding unused capacity: the missing rows must be populated for the state that is actually visited.

Recommended architecture and falsification gates

The design target is one moment-space device executable for a fixed machine shape. Precompute exterior and Green/jet rows once per machine. Each scanned Newton step forms the field from moments, obtains both limited and diverted boundary candidates from one wall-aware connected-domain decision, locates qualified roots, books full and cut cells into three physical moments, and returns the moment residual and constraint rows. The selected support remains the axis-connected component; a private pocket can never enter merely because its scalar flux is inside the boundary level. A local saddle frame can refine the saddle cell, but it is not the Reeb graph: the Reeb/join tree records global component mergers. Marching squares remains an outline renderer after the boundary decision, with the wall embedded in its domain.

Both boundary levels must be computed on every step. Selecting which level is binding may be nonsmooth at a topological change, while the candidate levels themselves should move continuously under a small field perturbation. The merge sweep must admit every genuine saddle and classify private wall contacts separately; an arbitrary basin-size floor is prohibited. A fixed-shape GPU union-find or equivalent component event kernel is a candidate, not yet a measured win. If it costs more than a fused fragment read, the latter retains ownership.
