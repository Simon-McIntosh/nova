Independent topology of the census tie pair

The read's axis is the field maximum. Its lower saddle_form is not the first saddle on that maximum's merge path and is not an admitted X-point: the read says boundary_class=0. On the published source revision the four booked cells lie beyond the upper saddle, which is the first separator. The 128.416 A and 129.731 A booking is therefore an integral over the read's chosen region, not evidence that the region is confined plasma.

Repair and admissibility

At the read owner, select the first separator on the axis extremum's continuous or certified contour-tree merge path. Preserve the upper saddle as a barrier inside its own cell when computing wall escape and support connectivity; then form membership only from the axis-connected component above that first event. A lower saddle's positive coarse passage margin must not outrank an earlier, slightly negative-margin upper saddle. Re-run both offsets with the same physical field, assert the upper saddle is encountered first, the reported boundary class matches the chosen physical event, and each booked cut-cell fraction lies on the confined axis component. Check the 282-cell carrier before extrapolating to finer meshes.

The field is a smooth, well-resolved three-null perturbation of a certified carrier, not a solved equilibrium with a force-balance receipt. The published read plus booking is not an admissible current-bearing equilibrium representation: it puts current beyond the first axis-component separator. The independent field alone can support a small upper-axis island; this record cannot certify it as a self-consistent Grad–Shafranov equilibrium. A precise continuous wall-contact level is not certified by the raster, although the 320→640 path and contours identify the upper saddle as the first resolved separator.

The existing test_census_keeps_the_wall_contact_saddle_across_the_adjacent_pair is correct about three roots, two saddles, and the lower root's persistence across offsets. Its “primary X-point” assertion is not correct: it polishes the lower saddle from state["previous"][1] and requires reading.saddle_form to match it. That assertion must instead identify the first saddle on the maximum's merge path, require the read's actual boundary class and point to agree with that event, and reject booked cells outside the confined axis component. The 2% booked-versus-direct-current assertion may remain only as a quadrature check after topology is independently accepted; both integrals now cover the same wrong four cells.

Physics ruling for the lead: if the upper-saddle separatrix meets the upper vessel within the continuous wall-contact uncertainty, should this near-wall state be classified as diverted by that saddle or limited by the first contact? The choice affects the final boundary label. It does not make the lower saddle first or validate the four upper-wall booked cells.
