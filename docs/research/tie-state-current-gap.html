The residual is a booking approximation on the coarse map, not a region difference: the booked support and the direct integral cover the same four selected cut cells, so the two numbers are two ways of evaluating one region.

The direct integral's own grid dependence bounds how tightly the two can be compared. Between 16×16, 32×32 and 64×64 points per triangle the direct current moves at most 0.118% (at offset 0.1732, from 130.334347 to 130.488250), so the smaller grid puts the two booked values 1.59% and 1.32% from the physical integral with a quadrature floor of about 0.12%. A fourfold refinement to 64×64 does not close the gap — so the looser tolerance of rel=0.15 was hiding a real, coarse-map booking approximation, and the tighter bound is the honest one.

Booking or region?

The residual compares two integrals over the same chosen region; it does not certify that region as axis-connected confined plasma. The direct quadrature in _dense_cut_current selects the same cells the booking tags — reading.membership > 0, four cells in that historical measurement — and integrates the profile's current density over each cell's own fan with the read's path_weight. The booked value is the same cells evaluated through the read's fitted cut representation. The two integrals evaluate the field and profile inside identical cells, and that difference is 1.3–1.6% on the coarse 282-cell map. The gap was previously unmeasurable because the read refused the axis at both offsets under the pre-fix persistence gate; with the fix merged (PERSISTENCE_ULP_FLOOR, 8 ULP) the axis is admitted (persistence 4.189e-07 against a floor of 1.53e-17) and the comparison runs.

The booked-current bound is tightened from rel=0.15 to rel=0.02, the larger measured gap (1.59%) plus the measured quadrature floor (0.12%), derived from that historical quadrature measurement.
