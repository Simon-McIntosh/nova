Booked versus integrated current at the 282-cell tie pair

With the normal-form lobe owner and the axis-persistence fix on main, the booked current at the tie pair is within 1.6% of an independent cut-cell quadrature of the physical field, and the direct integral does not move more than 0.12% with a fourfold change in quadrature grid. The bound is tightened from rel=0.15 to rel=0.02.

The read is valid and qualified at both offsets. The gap of 13% recorded by the seed-census node does not survive the normal-form lobe owner: the booked current moved up to within 1.3–1.6% of the independent integral. The residual is a booking approximation on the coarse map, not a region difference: the booked support and the direct integral cover the same four selected cut cells, so the two numbers are two ways of evaluating one region.

The direct integral's own grid dependence bounds how tightly the two can be compared. Between 16×16, 32×32 and 64×64 points per triangle the direct current moves at most 0.118% (at offset 0.1732, from 130.334347 to 130.488250), so the smaller grid puts the two booked values 1.59% and 1.32% from the physical integral with a quadrature floor of about 0.12%. A fourfold refinement to 64×64 does not close the gap — so the looser tolerance of rel=0.15 was hiding a real, coarse-map booking approximation, and the tighter bound is the honest one.

Booking or region?

By measurement the residual is a booking approximation, not a different region. The direct quadrature in _dense_cut_current selects the same cells the booking tags — reading.membership > 0, four cells, asserted in the helper — and integrates the profile's current density over each cell's own fan with the read's path_weight. The booked value is the same cells evaluated through the read's fitted cut representation. There is no uncovered or double-counted area: the two integrals differ only in how the field and profile are evaluated inside identical cells, and that difference is 1.3–1.6% on the coarse 282-cell map. The gap was previously unmeasurable because the read refused the axis at both offsets under the pre-fix persistence gate; with the fix merged (PERSISTENCE_ULP_FLOOR, 8 ULP) the axis is admitted (persistence 4.189e-07 against a floor of 1.53e-17) and the comparison runs.

The assertion

The booked-current bound is tightened from rel=0.15 to rel=0.02, the larger measured gap (1.59%) plus the measured quadrature floor (0.12%), stated in the constant's comment. It is not widened to absorb a gap: the bound now sits just above the agreement the code achieves.
