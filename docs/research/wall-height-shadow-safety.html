What the shipped predicate does

The production call is ForwardFluxOperator.residual_shadow_components in nova/equilibrium/forward_operator.py. It passes the wall-node heights, the achieved magnetic-axis height, topology.x_point, the connectivity read's qualified xset, and private_wall_node_mask to wall_height_shadow_mask in nova/equilibrium/connectivity_boundary.py. The operative final predicate is:

height_shadow = hysteretic_height_half_planes & private_wall_node_mask

The helper first matches topology.x_point to the nearest finite row of the qualified X-point table and accepts that match only inside _x_qualification_distance. If the primary is below the axis it supplies the lower height reference; if it is above, it supplies the upper reference. At most one candidate on the opposite side supplies the other reference: the maximum height below the axis or the minimum height above it, respectively, so the chosen opposite bound is the one closest to the axis. Additional same-side nulls do not supply a bound. A finite side admits a new bit only beyond the reference plus the 0.02 m hysteresis band and retains a previously set bit until it crosses back through the opposite side of that band.

If no X-point qualifies, neither side is recomputed: the helper retains the previous wall shadow and intersects it with the current connectivity-private wall nodes. With the zero previous mask used on the initial read, this means no wall-height exclusion at all. This is why the limited fixture returns 0/0, while a transiently missing candidate after promotion preserves—not invents— the accepted window.

Important direction: despite the name “augmentation”, the height predicate cannot add a node outside the topological private-wall shadow because of the final conjunction. It can only retain or remove connectivity-proven private nodes. Every measured added_to_pure_shadow count is therefore zero by construction; safety depends on whether the removals are physically justified.

The shipped eligibility guard adds two vessel-scaled conditions in nova/equilibrium/connectivity_boundary.py. A contributing side is ambiguous when another qualified saddle on that side lies within 1% of the wall's poloidal span of the chosen reference. A reference is radially ineligible when its major radius is less than 25% of that same span, placing it in the symmetry-axis coil region rather than the plasma-separatrix neighbourhood. No device-specific metre threshold is embedded. When either condition holds, connectivity-private nodes on that poloidal side pass through unchanged instead of being cut by height. The final proposed & private_wall conjunction is unchanged, so the guarded result remains a subset of pure topology for every input.

Relationship to the hex-flood geometry authority

The pure-shadow baseline is not inferred from the height rule. The existing analytic geometry battery in tests/test_hex_flood_geometries.py verifies limited, lower-single-null, upper/lower double-null, and wall-notch partitions at 21, 29, and 37 grid points. The paired-null battery in tests/test_hex_flood_snowflake.py verifies snowflake-plus and snowflake-minus partitions at 0.08 m and 0.16 m null separation over the same three resolutions. This assessment executes those tests unchanged, then adds one focused test in tests/test_wall_height_exclusion.py that passes their connectivity-private result through the production height helper and pins all five difference and churn rows above. A separate all-geometry invariant check proves every baseline, perturbed, and alternate-primary guarded mask is a subset of its pure topological mask. The outboard-low-node assertion selects the largest R among lower private wall nodes and proves that it remains excluded from the residual.

Recommendation and falsifier

Retain with the shipped unambiguous plasma-separatrix guard. Apply the height reduction only when a contributing side has no second qualified saddle inside 1% of wall span and its reference radius is at least 25% of wall span. The measured snowflake and coil-null failures now fall back to pure topology, while the connected-double-null, limited, and single-null controls are unchanged. The post-guard battery has zero wrong removals and zero perturbation or alternate-primary churn over all five 96-node geometries.

This recommendation would be overturned by either of two evidence results: a broader manufactured or real-machine cohort showing a physically valid height removal in an ambiguous case that the named guard rejects, or active-set telemetry showing that pure-shadow fallback increases settled mask trips enough to outweigh the wrong-cell risk while the guarded height rule remains topology-correct. Conversely, any nonzero wrong-removal count in a guarded conventional single- or connected-double-null fixture is sufficient to remove the augmentation entirely. No production masking behaviour is changed by this research node.
